ATTACHMENT A - FACILITY PLANS FOR REVIEW (ITEM 6)

Under EPCRA section 303, the county keeps an off-site emergency response plan for each facility
that stores an extremely hazardous substance above the threshold planning quantity. Plans are
updated at least every 3 years, or sooner when the facility changes. Staff visited each site
with the local fire department this summer. Draft updates were emailed to members October 9.

1. Cold storage warehouse, Town of Manitowoc Rapids
   Substance: anhydrous ammonia (refrigeration), about 18,000 lb in the system.
   Changes: new engine room and second receiver added in 2025; vulnerable zone recalculated
   (now 0.9 mile); updated evacuation and shelter-in-place map; two nearby farm residences added
   to the notification list. Fire department: local volunteer department, with mutual aid.
   Last plan: 2023.

2. Municipal water treatment plant, City of Two Rivers
   Substance: chlorine gas, one-ton cylinders (up to 4 on site).
   Changes: plant is converting to liquid sodium hypochlorite in 2027; plan updated for the
   interim period. Contacts and call-out list refreshed. Last plan: 2023.

3. Agricultural cooperative fertilizer site, Town of Rockland
   Substance: anhydrous ammonia nurse tank storage, 30,000-gallon bulk tank.
   Changes: new site manager; spring and fall application season staffing; route for nurse
   tanks to fields reviewed with Highway (no change to county road restrictions). Last plan: 2022
   (overdue; update completed this cycle).

4. Food processing plant, City of Manitowoc
   Substance: sulfuric acid and sodium hydroxide (wastewater treatment); ammonia refrigeration
   below threshold.
   Changes: facility added a second wastewater line; acid storage moved indoors; new
   containment berm. Last plan: 2024 (updated early because of the change).

Recommended action: approve plans 1-4 as drafted, with any corrections from members by
October 30. Approved plans go to Wisconsin Emergency Management and the local fire departments.

ATTACHMENT B - TIER II SUMMARY, 2026 REPORTING YEAR (ITEM 5)

- Facilities filing Tier II reports for Manitowoc County: 187 (2025: 181).
- Facilities with an extremely hazardous substance above the planning threshold: 41.
- New filers this year: 9 (mostly propane retailers and two new agricultural sites).
- Facilities that filed in 2025 but not 2026: 4. Staff sent reminder letters September 18; two
  have since filed, two closed or no longer store reportable quantities.
- Most common substances reported: propane, diesel fuel, lead-acid batteries, sulfuric acid,
  anhydrous ammonia.
- Fees collected for the state program: passed through, per state formula.
