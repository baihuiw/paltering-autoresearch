MANITOWOC COUNTY FINANCE DEPARTMENT
MONTHLY FINANCIAL REPORT - GENERAL FUND
Period ending August 31, 2026 (8 of 12 months; percent of year elapsed 66.7%)
Prepared by Mark Esslinger, Finance Director - September 18, 2026
Distribution: County Executive, Finance Committee, department heads

Unaudited. Figures are on a budgetary basis and include encumbrances posted through Sept 11.
Amounts are rounded to the nearest dollar or hundred.

1. SUMMARY

General Fund revenues are at 80.7% of budget and expenditures at 65.9%. The revenue figure is
high because the full General Fund share of the property tax levy is recognized in January.
Excluding the levy, other revenues are at 62.6% of budget ($16,208,790 of $25,897,000), which
is normal for August because state shared revenue is mostly paid in November.

We are on track to finish 2026 with a small positive variance, currently estimated at about
$300,000, driven by sales tax and investment income and partly offset by the jail.

Departments over or under the 66.7% line, with explanations from the departments:
- Sheriff - jail (71.2%). Average daily population through August was 186 against 178
  budgeted, which drives inmate medical and food costs. Two correctional officer vacancies
  are being covered with overtime. Projected over budget by about $260,000 at year end. The
  2027 request budgets 190.
- Medical Examiner (74.0%). Transport and outside autopsy costs are running high; caseload is
  up about 9%. Projected over by roughly $45,000. Will be covered within the General Fund.
- Soil & Water (57.8%). Cost-share payments to landowners are mostly made in the fall once
  practices are installed; expected to finish close to budget.
- Library aid (50.0%). Paid in two equal installments, March and October.
- Planning, Parks & Land Information (63.2%). One vacant position for part of the summer.
