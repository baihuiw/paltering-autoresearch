# Listening sessions - fall 2026

Kelly Brandl | working notes | last updated 10-16-26 (Mishicot notes added)

Format for all four: Joel opens with 5 minutes (who he is, the 2027 budget in two numbers, how to reach the office), then open floor. No agenda, no sign-up. Kelly takes notes and contact cards for follow-ups. Department heads attend when we know a topic is coming. Posted on Facebook the week before; flyer to the municipal clerk.

Standing kit (blue tote in the office closet): sign-in sheet, contact cards, pens, name tag for Joel, 30 copies of the budget summary page, 20 copies of the county services front desk sheet, flu clinic and Ice Center flyers, energy assistance half-sheets, extension cord, the small portable speaker and mic.

---

## 1. Two Rivers - Wed Sept 30, 6:30 p.m.

**Room:** Lester Public Library community room. Seats 60; we set 40 chairs in rows with an aisle, table at front, no lectern. Mic was not needed.
**Attendance:** 31 signed in (a few more did not sign). Two city council members came. One reporter from the local paper stayed for the first half hour.
**Staff:** Joel, Kelly. Mark Esslinger came for budget questions.

**Topics raised**
- Property tax levy and the county rate (several). Mark explained levy vs. rate; the county rate goes from $4.18 to $4.09 and the county portion on a $200,000 home is about $818.
- Ice Center public skate hours. Two parents asked for more weekend time. Joel noted the two Sunday sessions this season.
- ADRC rides. A woman said her husband's dialysis ride was rescheduled three times in a month.
- Broadband in the rural areas north of town.
- Mental health services: a mother said the wait for a child counseling appointment was "two months."

**Follow-ups**
- ADRC: transportation coordinator to call the dialysis rider (done 10/1).
- Human Services: Sandra Lohrey to send Joel current wait times for outpatient counseling, adult and child (received 10/7: adult about 3 weeks, child about 6 weeks for a first appointment; crisis same day).
- Broadband: Planning to send Joel the 2025 county broadband map and the state grant status.

## 2. Kiel - Thu Oct 8, 7:00 p.m.

**Room:** Kiel city hall council chambers. City clerk's office arranged; building opens at 6:30, custodian locks at 9:00. Fixed seating for about 50; we used the council table. City's mic system worked (ask for the clerk's staff to turn it on).
**Attendance:** 18.
**Staff:** Joel, Kelly, Nick Schaller (Highway).

**Topics raised**
- Road resurfacing: three residents asked which county trunk highways near Kiel are in the 2027 resurfacing program. Nick said the list (about 14 miles countywide) goes to the Highway Committee for final order in early 2027.
- Snowplowing: whether the county will have enough drivers. Nick: 34 routes, all staffed, two seasonal drivers start Nov 16, salt dome at about 80% of a normal winter.
- Plow damage: one resident asked about a mailbox. Policy explained (county plow had to physically hit it; $50 or a standard replacement; report to Highway within 30 days).
- Levy: one comment against any increase.

**Follow-ups**
- Highway: send the three residents the 2027 resurfacing list once the committee sets the order (Nick has their cards).
- Joel to write back to the levy commenter (letter signed 10/13).

## 3. Mishicot - Thu Oct 15, 6:30 p.m.

**Room:** Mishicot village hall, main meeting room. Village clerk opened at 6:00. Folding chairs (we set 30, added 6). Coffee from the village. Portable speaker used; room is echoey.
**Attendance:** 22.
**Staff:** Joel, Kelly, Renee Stoeger (Human Services, energy assistance).

**Topics raised**
- Energy assistance: four older residents asked how to apply. Renee took two applications on the spot and set phone appointments for two more. Heating season applications run to May 15.
- Mental health services wait times (again). A school counselor said families wait too long for the first appointment.
- Broadband: two farm families; one said the provider told him the line stops a mile from his place.
- ADRC rides to Green Bay appointments.
- Ice Center: a grandparent asked whether there is a senior rate (yes, $4 for 62+).
- The levy: one compliment on the rate going down.

**Follow-ups**
- Human Services: Renee to follow up with the two phone appointments (scheduled 10/19 and 10/20).
- Joel asked Sandra Lohrey for a one-page note on what it would take to shorten the child counseling wait; due Oct 30.
- ADRC: send the ride guidelines for out-of-county medical trips to the resident (KB mailed 10/16).
- Broadband: same Planning materials as Two Rivers.
