# MEMORANDUM

**To:** Planning and Park Commission
**From:** Dan Pautz, Parks Manager
**Through:** Erin Salzwedel, Planning, Parks & Land Information Director
**Date:** October 12, 2026 (packet sent October 9)
**Re:** Fischer Creek Park master plan update - summary of public comments

## Background

The draft Fischer Creek Park master plan update was released for public comment on September 8, 2026, with comments due October 2. The draft proposes three projects over five years:

1. **North loop campground:** 60 campsites with electric hookups, a shower and restroom building, a camp host site and a gravel loop road on about 14 acres north of the main parking lot.
2. **Accessible kayak and canoe launch** at the creek mouth, with a hardened path from the lower lot.
3. **Trail resurfacing** of the bluff trail and the creek trail (about 2.1 miles), with two new overlooks.

The Commission is asked to recommend the plan to the County Board, with or without changes, at its October 26 meeting.

## Comments received

The county received 89 comments through the online comment form between September 8 and October 2.

| Position on the campground | Comments |
|---|---|
| Support | 48 |
| Oppose | 41 |
| **Total** | **89** |

Nearly every comment addressed the campground. The kayak launch and trail work drew broad support, including from most people who opposed the campground.

## Main themes

**In support of the campground**
- Family camping close to home; the nearest public campgrounds with electric sites are more than 30 minutes away.
- Visitors would spend money in Cleveland and nearby businesses.
- Camping fees would help pay for upkeep of the whole park.
- Several commenters asked for a few tent-only sites.

**Opposed to the campground**
- Traffic and turning movements on CTH LS at the park entrance, especially with trailers.
- Noise and lights near homes to the north.
- Loss of trees and the wet area along the proposed north loop.
- Keep Fischer Creek a quiet day-use park; fix existing facilities first.
- Load on the park's well and septic system.

## Staff response to common concerns

- **Traffic:** Highway's August count at the entrance was 1,450 vehicles per day. A right-turn taper on CTH LS is included in the cost estimate.
- **Wetland:** The north loop alignment avoids the delineated wetland with a 75-foot buffer; a delineation was done in July.
- **Cost:** The campground would be paid from camping fees and the parks fund, with no new levy. Projected revenue at 45% occupancy covers operating costs and repays the parks fund over 12 years.
- **Lights and noise:** Quiet hours 10 p.m. to 7 a.m.; downward lighting only; a 100-foot wooded buffer on the north edge.

## Next steps

- October 26: Commission recommendation (public comment taken at the start of the meeting).
- November or December: County Board action.
- If approved, design in 2027 and construction of the campground no earlier than 2028; the kayak launch and trail work could start in 2027.
