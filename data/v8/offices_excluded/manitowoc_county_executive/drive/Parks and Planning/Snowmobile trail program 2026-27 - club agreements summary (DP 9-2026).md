## The program in brief

Manitowoc County has about 270 miles of county-funded snowmobile trail, almost all of it on private land under landowner permission. The county does not groom trail itself. Fourteen local snowmobile clubs groom, sign, brush and patrol their segments under annual agreements with the county. State snowmobile trail aid from the DNR is paid to the county as project sponsor and passed through to the clubs as reimbursement for documented grooming and maintenance costs. The levy does not pay for grooming; Parks staff time to run the program is in the regular parks budget.

## What stays the same

- Routes and grooming standards are the same as 2025-26, except the Maribel reroute below.
- Grooming standard: groom within 48 hours after a snowfall of 4 inches or more once trails are open, and at least once a week during the open season, conditions permitting.
- Clubs keep signs to the state standard and renew landowner permissions each year.

## What changed in the 2026-27 agreement

1. **Monthly grooming logs.** Due by the 10th of each month for the month before, instead of one log at season's end, so Parks can spot a segment falling behind while it is still winter.
2. **Certificate of insurance due Nov 15.** Each club must send a certificate of liability insurance naming Manitowoc County as additional insured by Nov 15, 2026. A club's segments will not open until its certificate is on file.
3. **Reimbursement in two installments,** February and June, instead of one payment in June. The clubs asked for this; groomer fuel and repairs hit hardest in January.

Reimbursement is limited to documented eligible costs and to the state aid available for each club's mileage. For 2025-26, the county passed through $80,700 to the 14 clubs.

## Opening criteria

Trails open only when Parks declares them open, after checking with the clubs: enough frost to carry a groomer without rutting, enough snow to protect fields and crops, and never before the end of the nine-day gun deer season. Trails may open county-wide or segment by segment, and they close again in a thaw. Opening and closing are posted on the snowmobile trail status line, (920) 683-4188, and on the trails page of the county website. The CE office posts on the county Facebook page when Parks calls it.

## Maribel reroute (segments 31-32)

A landowner near Maribel withdrew permission over the summer for about 1.2 miles of trail on segments 31 and 32. Club M worked with two neighboring farms to move that stretch to the east along a field edge and a farm lane. The new route has written landowner permission, adds no road crossings, and was walked by Parks staff in September.

## Trail map

An updated county snowmobile trail map (PDF) showing the Maribel reroute goes on the county website by Nov 15, 2026. Parks will send each club the GIS file for its segments once the reroute is measured; Kyle Ostrowski (GIS) is updating the layer.
