Dear Ms. Hoffmann,

Thank you for your letter about ice time at the County Ice Center. You asked two things: whether there will be more ice for the younger kids this season, and when public skating starts and what it costs. I spent a lot of winters behind the bench at that rink, so this is a question close to my heart, and I was at the Expo grounds this afternoon walking through the building with our Public Works Director and the Expo facility manager.

On youth ice: the youth hockey association books its practice and game ice with the Expo office each season, and the association sets its own schedule for each age group from that block. For 2026-27 the association has the same weekday evening blocks as last year plus one added Saturday morning hour, which I understand is going to the mites and the learn-to-play group. If your son's team is still short on ice, the association's ice scheduler is the right first call, and the Expo office can tell you whether any open hours remain.

Public skating starts Friday, October 23. The schedule for the season is:

- Fridays, 7:00 to 8:30 p.m.
- Saturdays, 1:00 to 2:30 p.m.
- Sundays, 1:00 to 2:30 p.m. and 3:00 to 4:30 p.m.
- Adult skate, Wednesdays, 11:30 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.

There will be extra holiday sessions from December 26 through January 3, and a six-week learn-to-skate program on Saturday mornings, 10:00 to 10:45 a.m., starting November 7, for $60.

You also asked about the price. The Expo-Ice Center Board raised public skating fees in April, the first change since 2019. Admission is now $6 for adults (13 to 61) and $4 for children 12 and under, up from $5 and $3. Seniors 62 and older pay $4, and children 3 and under skate free with a paying adult. Skate rental is $4, and a 10-visit punch card is $50, which brings a child's visit down a bit if your family skates often. I know a dollar more per person adds up for a family of five, and I appreciate you saying so. The increase goes toward keeping the ice plant and the building running, and the fees still compare well with other rinks in the area.

The schedule can change on tournament weekends, so please check the Expo page on the county website before you head out, or call the Expo office at (920) 683-4378. The staff there are friendly and will know the latest.

I hope to see your family on the ice this winter. Thank you for writing.

Sincerely,

Joel Stahlberg
Manitowoc County Executive
