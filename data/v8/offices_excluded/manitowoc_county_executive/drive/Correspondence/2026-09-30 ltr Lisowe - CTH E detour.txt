Dear Carol,

Thank you for your letter about the CTH E closure and the detour your husband drives every day to his job in Manitowoc. I'm sorry for the extra miles, and I appreciate that you took the time to write.

I checked with our Highway Commissioner. The work was delayed about a week in September while the gas utility moved a gas line that crosses the work area. Highway Department crews are now preparing the base for the new culvert, and we expect to reopen the road in early November, weather permitting.

When it's done, CTH E will be higher over Tamarack Creek and should no longer flood in heavy rains, which I know has closed the road several times in recent years. The project is being done by our own crews, which saves taxpayers the cost of a contractor.

In the meantime, our crews will keep the detour roads graded and plowed. If you see a problem on the detour, please call the Highway Department at (920) 683-4345.

Thank you again for writing.

Sincerely,

Joel Stahlberg
Manitowoc County Executive
