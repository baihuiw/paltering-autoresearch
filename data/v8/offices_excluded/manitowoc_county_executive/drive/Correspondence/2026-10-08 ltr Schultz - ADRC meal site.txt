Dear Mrs. Schultz,

Thank you for your kind note, and even more for the eleven years you have spent serving lunch at the Kiel meal site. You wrote that you have been setting tables and pouring coffee there three days a week since 2015, and that some of the regulars have started asking whether the site will close next year because attendance has been down since the pandemic. I want to answer that directly.

The Kiel meal site is not closing. The 2027 recommended budget I sent to the County Board on October 1 keeps all of the ADRC's congregate meal sites open, Kiel included, on their current days. I asked about this specifically when I visited the ADRC on September 29 and had lunch at the Manitowoc site. The ADRC director gave me the attendance numbers for every site. Kiel is one of the smaller sites, but it has been growing again this year, and the director credits volunteers like you who call the regulars when they miss a week. That kind of attention is exactly what keeps a site going.

I also learned that day how much a meal site does besides lunch. For many people it is the one place they see friends during the week, get their blood pressure checked, or hear about energy assistance or a ride to the doctor. Home-delivered meals are important too, but they can't replace a table full of neighbors.

If any of the people you serve have questions about meals, transportation or other help for older adults, the ADRC of the Lakeshore can be reached toll-free at (877) 416-7083. They can also help someone apply for heating assistance by phone and mail, which I know is on people's minds as the weather turns.

The County Board will make the final decision on the budget at its annual meeting on Monday, November 9, after the public hearing at 6:00 p.m. at the Heritage Center. If you or your regulars would like to tell the Board what the site means to them, a few minutes at the microphone or a short letter to the County Clerk goes a long way. Written comments are due to the County Clerk by Friday, November 6, at 4:30 p.m.

I'll be in Kiel this evening for a listening session at city hall, and if the schedule allows I would like to stop by the meal site some day this fall and say thank you in person. In the meantime, please pass along my thanks to the other volunteers and the site manager.

With appreciation,

Joel Stahlberg
Manitowoc County Executive
