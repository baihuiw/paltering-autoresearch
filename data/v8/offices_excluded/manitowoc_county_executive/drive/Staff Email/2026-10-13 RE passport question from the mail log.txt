Hi Kelly,

Sorry for the slow reply, two of us were out this week and the counter has been busy. Yes, we do passports here, no need to go to Green Bay. Here's the short version you can use:

- Passport applications at the County Clerk's office in the Courthouse, by appointment, Mon-Fri 9:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m. Call (920) 683-4004 to set one up.
- Fees: the federal application fee is a check made out to the U.S. Department of State (amount depends on book/card, we go over it at the appointment), plus our $35 acceptance fee to the county. We can take the photo here for $15.
- Under 16: both parents and the child have to appear. If one parent can't come, the other brings the absent parent's notarized consent form (State Dept form, we can tell her which one). Bring the child's certified birth certificate (not the hospital one), and ID for each parent.
- Routine processing is about 4-6 weeks right now. Expedited is available for an extra fee. For January travel she has time, but I'd tell her not to wait until December.

If the husband can only do a late afternoon, we can usually fit a 3:00 or 3:30 appointment.
