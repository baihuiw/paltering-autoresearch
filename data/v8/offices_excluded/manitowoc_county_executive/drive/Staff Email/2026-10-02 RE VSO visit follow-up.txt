Joel,

Thanks for coming down this morning and for sitting in on the walk-in hour. I think you saw the whole range in 45 minutes: a widow with a burial benefit question, a Vietnam vet whose hearing claim finally came through, and the daughter with the assisted-living bills.

A few things I said I'd put in writing:

1. Where we are and when. We're staying in Courthouse Room 105, first floor. Office hours Mon-Fri 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Walk-ins Tue and Thu 8:30-11:30 a.m. Everything else is by appointment, and we recommend an appointment for a first visit because it runs about an hour. Phone (920) 683-4056. Veterans should bring their DD-214 if they have it. If they don't, we request it for them.

2. Home and facility visits. For veterans who can't come in, Tracy (our benefits specialist) or I go to them, by appointment. This year so far that's 41 home visits and 23 at assisted-living facilities or nursing homes, mostly in Manitowoc and Two Rivers, a few out by Kiel and Reedsville.

3. Aid and Attendance. This is the big one. VA pension with Aid and Attendance helps wartime veterans and surviving spouses who need help with daily living, and it's often the difference between affording assisted living or not. We filed 38 of these claims in all of 2025. We're at 47 through September this year, and I expect 60 or more by December. Adult children are finding us through the facilities' social workers, which is good, but each claim is a lot of paperwork (medical forms, care costs, income and assets) and they take 6 to 9 months at the VA. Spouses and adult children can come on the veteran's behalf with a signed authorization or a power of attorney.

4. Space. We have one private interview room. When Tracy and I are both meeting with people, one of us ends up at the front counter talking about someone's income and medical history with the next person waiting four feet away. That's not right for the veteran. Room 107 next door has been storage for Information Systems for years. If it could be cleared out and get a door that closes and a phone jack, that would solve it. I don't think this is a big-dollar thing.

Thanks again. It means a lot to the staff when the County Executive shows up.

Doug
