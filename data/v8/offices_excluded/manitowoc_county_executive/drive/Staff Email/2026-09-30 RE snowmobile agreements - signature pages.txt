Hi Tanya,

I'm walking over the 14 snowmobile club agreements for 2026-27 this afternoon. They need the County Executive's signature. The Planning and Park Commission approved them at its August meeting (same form for all 14 clubs, only the club name, the segment numbers and the mileage differ). Each club has already signed. Joel signs page 6 of each, two originals per club, so 28 signatures. Yellow flags on every page. One original stays with us, one goes back to the club.

So Joel knows what he's signing, the short version:

- About 270 miles total, same routes and grooming standards as 2025-26. The one exception is a 1.2-mile reroute near Maribel (segments 31 and 32). A landowner withdrew permission over the summer, and the club found a new route across two neighboring farms. Map attached.
- State snowmobile trail aid still passes through the county to the clubs. No change in how that works.
- What's new in the agreement:
  1. Grooming logs are due monthly (by the 10th of the following month) instead of in one pile at the end of the season. That's so we can catch a stretch that isn't being groomed while it's still winter, and it lines up better with what the DNR asks for.
  2. Each club must send a certificate of insurance naming Manitowoc County as additional insured by Nov 15. No certificate, no trail opening on that club's segments.
  3. Reimbursement is paid in two installments, February and June, instead of one payment in June. The clubs asked for this. Fuel for the groomers is their biggest cost and it hits in January.

Nothing in here changes what the county spends from the levy. Erin has read through it, and Corporation Counsel reviewed the form in July.

The updated trail map goes on the website by Nov 15. I'll send the GIS file to the clubs once the Maribel segments are final.

If Joel can sign by Friday that gives the clubs a month to get their insurance in before the deadline.
