Sites are closed on Saturdays of holiday weekends (Memorial Day, Labor Day) and from Dec 24 through Jan 1. Use is free for county residents of the towns and villages without curbside recycling; others need a $20 annual drop-off permit, sold at the sites and at the Recycling Center office.

## What goes in which bin

**Containers bin (blue roll-off):**
- Plastic bottles and jugs #1 and #2 (empty, caps on)
- Aluminum cans and clean foil
- Steel and tin cans (rinse; labels OK)
- Cartons (milk, juice, broth)

**Paper bin (green roll-off):**
- Cardboard, flattened (no waxed boxes)
- Newspaper, magazines, catalogs, office paper, junk mail, paperboard (cereal boxes)

**Glass bin (separate):**
- Glass bottles and jars, any color. Rinse; take off lids and corks.
- No window glass, mirrors, drinking glasses, ceramics, light bulbs or ovenware.

## Not accepted at the sites

Plastic bags and film, garbage, yard waste, tires, electronics (accepted at the Recycling Center only), batteries, appliances, household hazardous waste (bring to Clean Sweep).

## Where it goes

Everything collected at the drop-off sites is hauled to the county Recycling Center, sorted and baled, and sold to processors and mills. Glass is shipped to a glass processor and made into new bottles and fiberglass insulation. Sales of recyclables help pay for the sites, so clean, sorted material matters.
