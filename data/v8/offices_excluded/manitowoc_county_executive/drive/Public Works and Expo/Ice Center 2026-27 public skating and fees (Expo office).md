# Manitowoc County Ice Center - Public Skating 2026-27

Manitowoc County Expo grounds, 4921 Expo Dr., Manitowoc, WI 54220
Expo office: (920) 683-4378, Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m.
Prepared by the Expo office, September 2026. Posted on the Expo page of manitowoccountywi.gov and at the rink.

## Season opens Friday, October 23, 2026

The Ice Center opens for public skating on **Friday, October 23**. Opening night also includes a youth hockey game at 6:00 p.m.; public skate follows at 7:00 p.m. as usual.

## Regular public skating schedule (from Oct 23)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Friday | 7:00-8:30 p.m. |
| Saturday | 1:00-2:30 p.m. |
| Sunday | 1:00-2:30 p.m. and 3:00-4:30 p.m. |
| Wednesday (adult skate, 18 and older) | 11:30 a.m.-1:00 p.m. |

There is no public skating Monday, Tuesday or Thursday; the rink is booked for youth hockey, high school hockey, figure skating and adult league.

**The schedule can change for tournaments and special events.** Several weekends in November through February have youth hockey tournaments, and some public sessions are moved or canceled. Before you come, check the Expo page or call the Expo office.

## Learn to skate

- **Saturdays, 10:00-10:45 a.m., six weeks starting November 7** (Nov 7, 14, 21, 28, Dec 5 and 12).
- **$60** for the six-week session, which includes skate rental and free admission to the Saturday 1:00 p.m. public skate on class days.
- Ages 4 and up, including adults. Classes are grouped by age and level: beginner (never skated), intermediate (can skate forward without help) and advanced (stops, backward skating, crossovers).
- Helmets are required for everyone under 13 in class (bike or hockey helmets are fine). A limited number of loaner helmets are available.
- Register at the Expo office or by phone. Class size is limited to 60; last year's sessions filled by the end of October.
- A second session starts in January; dates will be posted in December.

## Holiday break sessions

Extra public skating **December 26 through January 3**, daily 1:00-2:30 p.m., plus the regular evening and weekend sessions. Closed Christmas Day and New Year's Day. Exact times will be posted by December 1.

## Rink rules

- Skate in the direction posted (counterclockwise unless announced). Staff may reverse direction partway through a session.
- No racing, tag, chains of skaters or carrying children while skating.
- No hockey sticks, pucks or figure skating jumps during public sessions.
- Helmets are strongly recommended for children and beginners.
- Walkers and "skate helpers" are available for beginners, free of charge, first come, first served.
- No food or drinks on the ice. Concession stand is open during Friday and weekend sessions.
- Strollers and wheelchairs are not allowed on the ice, but the warm viewing area is accessible.
- Children under 10 must be accompanied by someone 14 or older.
- The ice is resurfaced between sessions; please leave the ice promptly when asked.
- Staff may ask anyone not following the rules to leave without a refund.

## Skate rentals and sharpening

- Rental skates in children's size 8 through adult size 13 (hockey and figure styles).
- Skate sharpening at the pro shop window, $7 per pair, during Friday and weekend sessions. Allow 15 minutes.

## Private rentals and birthday parties

- Ice rental for private groups: $195 per hour (weekday daytime $150), subject to availability.
- Birthday party package for a public session: admission and rental for up to 12 skaters plus use of the party room for 90 minutes, $120. Book at least two weeks ahead with the Expo office.

## Parking and access

Use the main Expo entrance off Expo Dr. The main lot by the Ice Center entrance has accessible parking. During large events on the grounds (craft fairs, shows), allow extra time for parking.
