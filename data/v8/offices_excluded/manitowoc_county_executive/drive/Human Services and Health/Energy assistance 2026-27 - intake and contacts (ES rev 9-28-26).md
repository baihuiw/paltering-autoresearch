# Energy Assistance 2026-27 - Intake and Contacts

Manitowoc County Human Services, Economic Support Unit. Internal sheet for front desk, switchboard and ES staff. Rev. 9-28-26 (RS). Replaces the 2025-26 sheet; please recycle the old copies at the front window.

## The season

- **Heating season applications:** Thursday, October 1, 2026 through Friday, May 15, 2027.
- **Crisis help** (disconnect notice, already disconnected, or out of fuel / under a quarter tank of propane or fuel oil): **year-round**, not just during the season.
- Program: Wisconsin Home Energy Assistance Program (WHEAP). The county takes and processes applications; the state sets the rules and the income guidelines and pays the benefit.
- Benefit is a **one-time payment for the season, paid directly to the heating utility or fuel vendor**, not to the household. Households that get a heating benefit also get an electric benefit paid to the electric utility.

## How people can apply

1. **By phone appointment.** Call (920) 683-4237. Staff do the whole interview over the phone and tell the caller exactly what to send in. This is the easiest route for most people.
2. **In person.** Walk-in hours at Human Services are **Monday-Thursday, 8:30-11:30 a.m.** No walk-in intake on Fridays (phone appointments only). Anyone who shows up outside walk-in hours gets an appointment slip, not turned away at the window.
3. **Online** through the state's application site (link on the Human Services page of the county website). Online applications land in our queue; staff call back if anything is missing.
4. **By phone and mail, for older adults and homebound people.** The whole application can be done without coming in: phone interview, then the person mails copies of documents (or the ADRC helps gather them). We mail the signature page with a stamped return envelope.

Callers from the County Executive's office or other departments: transfer to (920) 683-4237 or give that number. Please don't try to answer eligibility questions at the switchboard.

## What to bring or mail

- Proof of income for **everyone in the household for the last 3 months** (pay stubs, unemployment, pension statements). For Social Security or SSI, the **award letter is enough**; no need for three months of bank statements.
- A **recent heating bill** and a **recent electric bill** (account numbers are what we need most). Propane or fuel oil customers: name of the vendor and the account number.
- **Social Security numbers** for everyone in the household.
- If there's a disconnect notice, bring or send that too.

## Income guidelines

Staff check eligibility by phone. **Do not quote dollar figures** at the front desk, on the switchboard or in emails; the state updates the guidelines and household size matters. Say: "Call (920) 683-4237 and staff will check it with you."

## Crisis calls

- Disconnect notice, already shut off, or nearly out of fuel: put the call through to the energy line right away; don't take a message. Same-day callback if the line is busy.
- Federal rules: assistance must be arranged within 48 hours, or 18 hours if the situation is life-threatening (no heat with an infant, an older adult or someone with a medical condition in the home).
- After 4:30 p.m. or on weekends: crisis line through the Behavioral Health after-hours number on the voicemail, and the utility's own emergency line.

## Reminders

- Renewals: households that got help last year get a reminder letter from the state in September. They still need to apply; nothing carries over automatically.
- Landlord-heated units: tenants whose heat is in the rent may still qualify; take the application.
