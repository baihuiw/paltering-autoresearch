## Children (18 and under)

- **No cost** for children on BadgerCare/Medicaid, uninsured, or underinsured, through the Vaccines for Children program.
- Other children: we bill the family's insurance. Bring the insurance card.
- **A parent or legal guardian must come with any child under 18.** We can't vaccinate a minor brought by a grandparent, sitter or older sibling unless that person is the legal guardian.
- **Children under 9 getting a flu shot for the first time need two doses, four weeks apart.** We give the first dose at the clinic and the nurse writes the date for the second dose on the record card. The second dose can be at any later clinic or a Wednesday appointment.
- **FluMist (nasal spray)** is available for ages 2-49 while supply lasts. Not for children under 2, pregnant people, or some people with asthma or weak immune systems; the nurse screens at the table.

## Adults

- Bring your insurance card (Medicare Part B, Medicaid, or private insurance). We bill insurance; there's nothing to pay at the clinic for most people.
- High-dose or adjuvanted vaccine for adults 65 and older, while supply lasts.
- **No one is turned away for inability to pay.**

## What to expect

- Fill out a short consent form at the door (forms also on the Public Health page to print at home).
- Wear a short-sleeved shirt or one that rolls up easily.
- Plan on about 15 minutes, longer at the start of an evening clinic.
- COVID-19 vaccine is not offered at the community clinics this fall; ask your clinic or pharmacy, or call us about the Wednesday appointments.
