6. BUILDING

The roof over the Human Services building (926 S. 8th St.) leaked again in the September 11 storm, this time over the second-floor records room. Public Works moved two file cabinets and put down tarps; no records were damaged. A new roof is in the 2027 recommended budget under Public Works buildings (estimate $485,000). If approved, Public Works plans to bid it over the winter and do the work in June and July 2027, with staff staying in the building.

7. BUDGET

The 2027 request was submitted August 28. The County Executive's recommended budget will be released October 1; Finance Committee review of Human Services is scheduled for the evening of Tuesday, October 6. The main increases are in children's long-term support and crisis services, offset in part by state and federal revenue.

8. OTHER

The County Executive toured the building and met the leadership team on Friday, September 18. He asked for a short summary of the outpatient waitlist each month, which starts with this report.
