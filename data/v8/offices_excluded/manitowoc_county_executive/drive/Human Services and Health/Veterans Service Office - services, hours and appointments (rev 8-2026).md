# Manitowoc County Veterans Service Office

**Services, hours and appointments.** Handout for veterans and families. Rev. August 2026 (DL).

Doug Liebl, County Veterans Service Officer

## Where and when

- **Location:** Manitowoc County Courthouse, 1010 S. 8th St., Manitowoc, **first floor, Room 105**. Use the 8th Street entrance; Room 105 is to the left past security.
- **Phone:** (920) 683-4056
- **Hours:** Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Closed on county holidays. Veterans Day is not a county holiday; we're open November 11.
- **Parking:** the Courthouse lot on 8th Street; two accessible spaces by the north door.

## Appointments and walk-ins

- **Appointments are recommended.** A first visit takes about an hour, so we set aside the time and have your file ready. Call (920) 683-4056.
- **Walk-ins:** Tuesday and Thursday, 8:30-11:30 a.m. Walk-ins are seen in order of arrival. For a new claim, we may start the paperwork and set a follow-up appointment.
- **Home or facility visits** for veterans who can't come to the Courthouse (homebound, in the hospital, in a nursing home or assisted living), **by appointment**. Call and tell us where the veteran lives and the best time to visit.

All of our services are **free**. You never have to pay anyone to file a VA claim.

## What we help with

- **VA pension and Aid and Attendance.** Monthly payments for wartime veterans and surviving spouses with limited income. Aid and Attendance adds to the pension for someone who needs help with daily activities, and it often helps pay for **assisted living** or in-home care. Medical expenses such as assisted living costs can count against income; bring the facility's monthly statement.
- **Disability compensation** for conditions connected to service, including increases and appeals.
- **VA health care enrollment** and help with VA clinic and travel questions.
- **Wisconsin state veterans benefits** (education grants, property tax credit for disabled veterans, state veterans homes, emergency assistance grants).
- **DD-214 and military records requests** if you don't have your discharge papers.
- **Burial benefits:** VA burial allowance, headstones and markers, burial flags, and the Presidential Memorial Certificate. Funeral homes can call us directly.
- Survivors' benefits (Dependency and Indemnity Compensation, survivors pension).
- Referrals for housing, employment and benefits through other agencies, including the ADRC for older veterans.

## What to bring

- **DD-214** (discharge papers), if you have it. If not, come anyway; we can request it.
- Photo ID.
- For pension or Aid and Attendance: income for the household (Social Security letter, pension statements), bank account balances, and medical or care expenses (assisted living or home care bills, insurance premiums).
- For a disability claim: any medical records you have and a list of your doctors.
- For a surviving spouse: the marriage certificate and death certificate.

## Family members helping a veteran

Spouses and adult children are welcome to come in on a veteran's behalf, call us, or attend with the veteran. To act on a claim we need either:

1. a **signed authorization** from the veteran (we have the VA form at the office and can mail it), or
2. a **power of attorney** that covers the veteran's affairs (bring a copy).

If the veteran can't sign and there's no power of attorney, call us; there are other routes and we'll walk you through them. We can talk generally with any family member about what benefits exist.
