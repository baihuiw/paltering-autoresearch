MANITOWOC COUNTY PUBLIC SAFETY COMMITTEE
MINUTES - MONDAY, OCTOBER 5, 2026, 1:00 P.M.
Courthouse, Room 316, 1010 S. 8th St., Manitowoc

Present: Supervisors Sue Kinjerski (Chair), Tom Rappel, Bill Rusch, Janet Duvall, Ken Wilda
Also present: Holly Wenzel, Emergency Services Director; a Sheriff's Office captain; the Medical Examiner's office; Tanya Gerlach, County Executive's office

1. Call to order at 1:00 p.m.

2. Minutes of September 14, 2026. Motion by Rappel, second by Duvall, to approve. Carried 5-0.

3. Sheriff's Office report. Jail average daily population for September was 186. Two correctional officer positions remain open; interviews this month. The state traffic safety grant accepted by the County Board on September 15 will fund overtime for impaired-driving patrols from November through March. Received and placed on file.

4. Emergency Services report. Wenzel presented the JDC's September operations report (distributed at the meeting; final numbers to follow). 911 calls answered within 15 seconds: 97.4%. One telecommunicator vacancy is posted. The CAD/NG911 upgrade kickoff was held September 15; telecommunicator training will begin in December and go-live is planned for January 2027. Outdoor warning sirens: monthly tests continue through November; the two Mishicot sirens were repaired September 16. Supervisor Wilda asked about the cost of the upgrade; Wenzel said $412,000, mostly from the 911 fund, as shown in the recommended budget. Received and placed on file.

5. Hazardous materials. The Local Emergency Planning Committee meets October 21. Updated plans for four facilities will be presented for approval.

6. Medical Examiner. Caseload report for the third quarter. Received and placed on file.

7. Winter storm exercise. Wenzel invited committee members to the November 12 tabletop exercise with Highway and the Sheriff's Office at the Emergency Operations Center.

8. Public comment. None.

9. Next meeting: Monday, November 2, 2026, 1:00 p.m.

10. Adjourn at 2:06 p.m. Motion by Rusch, second by Wilda. Carried 5-0.

Recorded by Emergency Services administrative staff.
