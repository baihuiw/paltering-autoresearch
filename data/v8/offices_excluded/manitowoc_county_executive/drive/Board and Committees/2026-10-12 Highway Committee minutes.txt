MANITOWOC COUNTY HIGHWAY COMMITTEE
MINUTES - MONDAY, OCTOBER 12, 2026, 8:00 A.M.
Highway Department Conference Room, 3500 STH 310, Manitowoc

Present: Supervisors Allen Brennecke (Chair), Mike Tesch, Dawn Kronforst, Gary Pritzl, Ann Leiterman
Also present: Tom Kracht, Highway Commissioner; Nick Schaller, Operations Manager; Joel Stahlberg, County Executive (items 1-5); Kelly Brandl, County Executive's office; one member of the public

1. Call to order at 8:00 a.m. by Chair Brennecke. Notice posted as required.

2. Minutes of September 14, 2026. Motion by Tesch, second by Leiterman, to approve. Carried 5-0.

3. Winter maintenance readiness. Kracht reported 9,200 tons of salt on hand, with the balance of the seasonal order through the state contract. All 31 plow routes are staffed; two LTE operators start November 16. The county will again plow the town roads under agreement for six towns. Received and placed on file.

4. CTH E / Tamarack Creek box culvert: Commissioner Kracht reported that the box culvert was set about 1.3 feet too high on October 2 because the department's survey crew tied the grades to an old control point instead of the project control point, so the culvert must be lifted and reset, and the road will remain closed until about May 2027. The permit requires the bottom of the box to be buried below the streambed for fish passage. The precast supplier's crane is not available until November and the base cannot be rebuilt properly once the ground freezes, so the site will be stabilized for winter and the work redone in spring. Kracht estimated the added cost at $146,000, to be paid from the highway fund balance, in addition to the $286,000 project budget. He said the control point should have been checked against the plans before the base was placed and that the department now requires two sign-offs before any structure is set. Supervisor Kronforst asked about farms and the school bus route on the detour. Schaller said the detour will be maintained and plowed as a priority route. Supervisor Pritzl asked whether the county could be reimbursed by anyone. Kracht said no; it was county work. Received and placed on file.

5. 2027 highway budget. The County Executive reviewed the recommended 2027 highway budget: $4.1 million for county trunk highway resurfacing (about 14 miles), the CTH Q bridge deck overlay, and replacement of two tandem plow trucks. Discussion.

6. Equipment purchase. Motion by Leiterman, second by Tesch, to approve the purchase of one tandem axle plow truck with wing and box from the low bidder under the state cooperative contract for $298,450, as budgeted. Carried 5-0.

7. Town road agreements. Kracht reported that the 2027 town road maintenance and paving agreements were sent to all towns on October 1, with returns requested by November 30.

8. Airport. Hangar row 3 roof repair completed September 29. The fuel farm inspection passed on October 6.

9. Driveway and right-of-way permits. 41 permits issued in September (driveways 17, utility 19, other 5).

10. Public comment. None.

11. Next meeting: Monday, November 9, 2026, 8:00 a.m.

12. Adjourn at 9:21 a.m. Motion by Pritzl, second by Kronforst. Carried 5-0.

Recorded by the Highway Department administrative assistant.
Minutes are not final until approved by the committee.
