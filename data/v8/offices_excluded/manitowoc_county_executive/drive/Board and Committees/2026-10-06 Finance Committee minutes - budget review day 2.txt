MANITOWOC COUNTY FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES - 2027 BUDGET REVIEW, DAY 2
TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6, 2026, 6:00 P.M.
Heritage Center, County Board Meeting Room, 1701 Michigan Ave., Manitowoc

Present: Supervisors Pat Rehrauer (Chair), Gary Pritzl, Ann Leiterman, Rick Gerds, Bill Rusch
Also present: Joel Stahlberg, County Executive; Mark Esslinger, Finance Director; Tanya Gerlach, County Executive's office; department heads and staff as listed under each item; Supervisors Duvall and Kinjerski (not members); two members of the public

1. Call to order at 6:00 p.m. by Chair Rehrauer. The chair reminded members that the committee is reviewing the County Executive's recommended budget department by department; amendments will be taken up together on day 3 (October 7) so they can be weighed against each other.

2. Public comment. One resident spoke in support of keeping the ADRC's Valders meal site open. One resident asked how the Human Services roof would be funded; the chair referred the question to the Public Works review on day 3.

3. Human Services. Sandra Lohrey, Director, and the department's fiscal manager.
   2026 adopted expenditures $38,920,000; 2027 recommended $40,127,000 (+3.1%). Levy $9,840,000 (2026) to $10,050,000 (2027), +2.1%. State and federal revenue covers the rest of the increase.
   Main increases: children's long-term support ($612,000, fully state and federal funded), crisis services ($240,000, including the contracted evening therapist hours and a 24-hour crisis line contract increase), wages and benefits under the pay plan. Out-of-home care is budgeted flat at $4.1 million, based on 72 children in care in September.
   Supervisor Pritzl asked why out-of-home care was not increased when it ran over in 2025. Lohrey said placements have come down for 14 months and relative placements cost less. Supervisor Leiterman asked about the seven vacancies; Lohrey said the budget assumes 3% vacancy savings, about $410,000, which is in line with this year. Supervisor Gerds asked whether energy assistance staffing costs the levy; Lohrey said the two LTEs are paid entirely from the state's administrative allocation. No amendments proposed.

4. Public Health. Nicole Fessenden, Health Officer.
   2026 adopted $3,462,000; 2027 recommended $3,540,000 (+2.3%). Levy $1,714,000 to $1,760,000. The department requested $3,551,000; the recommended budget is $11,000 lower in supplies.
   Supervisor Rusch asked whether the well water program covers its costs. Fessenden said sample fees cover about 70%; the state lab raised its fees in July. Supervisor Pritzl asked about grant risk; Fessenden said about 38% of the budget is grants, mostly WIC and immunization, and flat for 2027. No amendments proposed.

5. ADRC of the Lakeshore. The ADRC director and the nutrition program coordinator.
   Manitowoc County share: 2026 $5,880,000; 2027 recommended $6,021,000 (+2.4%). County levy $612,000, unchanged from 2026. All six congregate meal sites in Manitowoc County stay open on their current days. Home-delivered meals budgeted at 82,000 meals (2026: 78,000), now averaging 312 a weekday.
   Supervisor Leiterman asked about the Valders site, which serves about 12 people on its one day a week. The director said it stays open on Wednesdays as now. Supervisor Gerds asked about the volunteer driver shortage; mileage reimbursement rises to the IRS rate in January. No amendments proposed.

6. Veterans Service. Doug Liebl, Veterans Service Officer.
   2026 adopted $398,600; 2027 recommended $409,300 (+2.7%), wages and benefits. State grant $14,500, unchanged. Liebl reported 1,214 office visits and 386 home or facility visits through September, and that VA pension and Aid and Attendance claims for veterans in assisted living have grown. Chair Rehrauer thanked the office for its work with the county's assisted living facilities. No amendments proposed.

7. Child Support. The Child Support Director.
   2026 adopted $1,942,000; 2027 recommended $1,991,000 (+2.5%). The program is funded about two-thirds by federal reimbursement and state performance funding; county levy $318,000 (2026: $311,000). Collections through August were $11.8 million, with 72% of current support collected. Supervisor Rusch asked about the electronic filing project with the courts; the director said it will be in place in spring 2027. No amendments proposed.

8. County Clerk. The County Clerk.
   2026 adopted $968,000; 2027 recommended $941,000 (-2.8%). The decrease reflects two regular elections in 2027 against four in 2026. Passport acceptance fees are budgeted at $62,000 (about 1,770 applications). Supervisor Pritzl asked about the cost of the voting equipment maintenance contract; the Clerk said it rises 5% under the existing agreement. No amendments proposed.

9. Summary. Esslinger noted that the six departments reviewed tonight total $53.03 million of the $131.9 million recommended. Nothing reviewed tonight changes the recommended levy of $42.31 million.

10. Day 3 (Wednesday, October 7, 6:00 p.m.) will cover the remaining departments, capital projects and any amendments.

11. Recess at 8:14 p.m. Motion by Gerds, second by Leiterman. Carried 5-0.

Recorded by Finance Department staff.
