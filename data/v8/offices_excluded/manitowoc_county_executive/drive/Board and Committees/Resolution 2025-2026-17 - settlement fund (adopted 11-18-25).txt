MANITOWOC COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
RESOLUTION NO. 2025/2026-17

Creating a restricted fund for opioid settlement proceeds and setting rules for its use

Adopted November 18, 2025. Offered by the Human Services Board and the Finance Committee.

WHEREAS, Manitowoc County is receiving payments under the national and state opioid settlement agreements, to be paid over a number of years; and

WHEREAS, the settlement agreements require that the money be spent on approved opioid abatement and recovery purposes; and

WHEREAS, the County Board wishes to keep the proceeds apart from ordinary county operations so that residents can see how they are spent;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Manitowoc County Board of Supervisors as follows:

1. Fund 275 (Opioid Settlement Fund) is created. All settlement payments received by the county and the interest on them shall be deposited to Fund 275.

2. Money in Fund 275 shall be used only for purposes on the approved-uses list in the settlement agreements, as recommended by the Human Services Board and approved by the County Board, either by resolution or in the annual budget.

3. Fund 275 money shall add to, and shall not replace, spending the county would otherwise make from the levy or other funds.

4. The Finance Director shall report receipts, spending and the fund balance to the Finance Committee and the Human Services Board each quarter, and shall include a Fund 275 schedule with the recommended budget each year.

5. Transfers out of Fund 275 to any other fund require approval of the County Board.

6. The initial recommended uses, to be reviewed each year, are: (a) peer recovery coaches and follow-up for people treated for an overdose; (b) naloxone for the Health Department's distribution program; (c) prevention grants to schools and community groups; (d) treatment support for people leaving the jail.

7. The Human Services Director shall administer the contracts under this resolution.

Fiscal note: Receipts are estimated at $350,000 to $450,000 a year through 2038, depending on the payment schedule and the number of counties participating.

Passed: 22 aye, 2 no, 1 excused.

Attest: County Clerk
