MANITOWOC COUNTY PERSONNEL COMMITTEE
MINUTES - MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2026, 3:00 P.M.
Courthouse, Room 316, 1010 S. 8th St., Manitowoc

Present: Supervisors Carol Ziemer (Chair), Allen Brennecke, Lyle Brockman, Mike Tesch, Dennis Oswald
Also present: the Personnel Director; the Personnel benefits coordinator; Tom Kracht, Highway Commissioner; Nicole Fessenden, Health Officer; Sandra Lohrey, Human Services Director; Tanya Gerlach, County Executive's office

1. Call to order at 3:00 p.m. by Chair Ziemer.

2. Minutes of August 24, 2026. Motion by Oswald, second by Brockman, to approve. Carried 5-0.

3. Public comment. None.

4. Reclassification: Public Health sanitarians. The Personnel Director presented the request to reclassify the two Sanitarian positions in Public Health to Environmental Health Specialist II (pay grade 9 to pay grade 10). The positions now carry the full state agent inspection load for lodging, pools and body art in addition to food. The job classification committee scored both positions at grade 10 in August. Annual cost $5,820 including benefits, absorbed in the 2026 Public Health budget through vacancy savings and included in the 2027 request. Fessenden answered questions about the state agent contract. Motion by Tesch, second by Brennecke, to approve, effective October 4, 2026. Carried 5-0.

5. Reclassification: Economic Support lead worker. Request to reclassify one Economic Support Specialist to Economic Support Lead Worker (grade 8 to grade 9) to recognize the energy assistance coordination and training duties now done by that position. Lohrey said the lead worker has run energy assistance intake and trained new staff for three seasons without the title. Cost $2,940 annually. Motion by Brockman, second by Oswald, to approve, effective October 4, 2026. Carried 5-0.

6. Limited-term employees, Human Services. Request for two LTE energy assistance intake workers, 32 hours a week, September 28, 2026 through May 14, 2027, paid from the state energy assistance administrative allocation (no levy). Lohrey explained that the offers were made contingent on this approval so the workers could be trained before the October 1 opening. Chair Ziemer asked that future LTE requests come to the committee a month ahead. Motion by Tesch, second by Brennecke, to approve. Carried 5-0.

7. Limited-term employees, Highway. Kracht requested two LTE plow operators for the 2026-27 winter, starting Monday, November 16 and ending no later than March 31, 2027, at the LTE operator rate. Both need a current CDL; one is a returning operator from last winter. With the two LTEs, Highway can staff all 34 plow routes with a relief operator for every three routes. Supervisor Oswald asked about overtime; Kracht said the LTEs reduce the need to hold regular operators past 12 hours. Motion by Oswald, second by Brockman, to approve. Carried 5-0.

8. Employee wellness program. The benefits coordinator reported on the 2026 program: 512 employees and spouses completed the health assessment and biometric screening (68% of eligible), up from 61% in 2025. Employees who complete the screening keep the $25-per-month premium discount. 2027 screenings will be held at the Courthouse, Highway Department and Human Services on October 19-23, 2026 (for the 2027 plan year), with a make-up day November 5. The flu shot clinic for employees is October 6 and 13 at Public Health. The coordinator also reported that the walking challenge in July had 140 participants. Received and placed on file.

9. Vacancy report. The Personnel Director presented the vacancy report as of September 25: 23 open positions countywide, out of 712 authorized FTE.
   - Human Services: 7 (2 CPS initial assessment social workers, ES specialist, outpatient therapist, crisis worker, psychiatric nurse, APS social worker)
   - Sheriff's Office: 4 (2 correctional officers, 1 patrol deputy, 1 clerk)
   - Highway: 3 (2 patrol operators, 1 mechanic)
   - Public Health: 1 public health nurse
   - Joint Dispatch Center: 1 telecommunicator
   - Health Care Center: 4 (CNAs)
   - Finance: 1 accountant
   - Parks: 1 maintenance worker
   - Clerk of Courts: 1 deputy clerk
   Supervisor Brennecke asked about time to fill. Average time from posting to start date is 61 days, up from 48 in 2024; nursing and mental health positions take the longest. The Personnel Director said a proposal for a signing incentive for hard-to-fill clinical positions will come to the committee in October.

10. County Executive's office. Gerlach reported no changes in the office's positions with the transition. The County Executive asked that the 2027 pay plan review start in January as scheduled.

11. Next meeting: Tuesday, October 27, 2026, 3:00 p.m. (moved from Monday, October 26).

12. Adjourn at 4:02 p.m. Motion by Brennecke, second by Tesch. Carried 5-0.

Recorded by Personnel Department staff.
