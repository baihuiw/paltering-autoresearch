MANITOWOC COUNTY BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
REGULAR MEETING - TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 15, 2026, 6:00 P.M.
Heritage Center, County Board Meeting Room, 1701 Michigan Ave., Manitowoc

DRAFT - subject to approval at the October 20, 2026 meeting

1. Call to order. Chair Ron Zastrow, acting county executive, called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

2. Roll call. 24 present; Supervisor Ken Wilda excused.

3. Pledge of Allegiance; moment of silence.

4. Approval of the agenda. Motion by Supervisor Oswald, second by Supervisor Kinjerski. Carried by voice vote.

5. Minutes of August 18, 2026. Approved as printed.

6. Public input. Three speakers: two in support of the county's continued funding for the Two Rivers senior meal site, and one asking the Board to finish the Devil's River State Trail surface near Denmark.

7. Communications. The County Clerk reported that the special election to fill the vacancy in the office of County Executive was ordered on September 3 for Tuesday, November 17, 2026, with nomination papers available from the Clerk's office.

8. Confirmation of the appointment of County Executive. Chair Zastrow presented his appointment of Joel Stahlberg, Town of Manitowoc, as County Executive under Wis. Stat. 59.17(7), to serve until a successor is elected and qualified. Mr. Stahlberg addressed the Board and answered questions about his background and his plans for the budget. Supervisor Rehrauer asked whether he intended to run in the special election; he said he did. Motion by Supervisor Brennecke, second by Supervisor Tesch, to confirm. Roll call: 21 ayes, 3 noes (Supervisors Ziemer, Duvall, Kubiak), 1 excused. Confirmed.

9. Resolution 2026/2027-41, authorizing the Sheriff to accept a state traffic safety grant ($48,500). Adopted on a voice vote.

10. Resolution 2026/2027-42, approving the 2027 agreement with the county's stray animal shelter provider. Adopted 24-0.

11. Resolution 2026/2027-43, authorizing the Highway Department to purchase one motor grader under the state contract. Adopted 23-1.

12. Ordinance 2026/2027-44, amending the county shoreland zoning ordinance to update definitions to match state rules. First reading; referred to the Planning and Park Commission.

13. Resolution 2026/2027-45, recognizing Veterans Service Officer staff for 25 years of service. Adopted by acclamation.

14. Reports. Finance Director: second-quarter financial report, sales tax revenue up 3.4% over 2025. Human Services: caseload report. Emergency Services: county fair weekend went smoothly.

15. Supervisor reports. Several supervisors thanked Chair Zastrow for serving as acting county executive.

16. Next meeting: Tuesday, October 20, 2026, 6:00 p.m.

17. Adjourn at 7:48 p.m.

Respectfully submitted,
Deputy County Clerk
