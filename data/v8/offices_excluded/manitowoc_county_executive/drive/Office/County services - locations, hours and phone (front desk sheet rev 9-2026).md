**Passports.** Applications are taken at the County Clerk's office by appointment, Mon-Fri 9:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m.; call (920) 683-4004 to book. The federal fee is paid by check to the U.S. Department of State; the county's acceptance fee is $35. Photos can be taken at the Clerk's office for $15. Children under 16: both parents (or one parent with the other's notarized consent form) and the child must appear; bring the child's certified birth certificate and parent ID. Routine processing takes about 4-6 weeks; expedited service is available for an extra fee. The County Executive's office does not process passports.

**Veterans Service Office (Room 105).** Doug Liebl, Veterans Service Officer. Appointments recommended (allow about an hour for a first visit); walk-ins Tuesday and Thursday 8:30-11:30 a.m. Home or facility visits by appointment for veterans who can't come in. Free help with VA pension and Aid and Attendance, disability compensation, VA health care enrollment, state benefits, DD-214 requests and burial benefits. Bring the DD-214 if you have it. Spouses and adult children may come on a veteran's behalf with a signed authorization or power of attorney.
