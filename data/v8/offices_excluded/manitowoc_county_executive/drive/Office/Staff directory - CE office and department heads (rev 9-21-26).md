## Elected officials (not under the County Executive)

County Clerk (elections, marriage licenses, dog licenses), Treasurer (property tax payments), Register of Deeds, Sheriff, Clerk of Circuit Court, Coroner. Refer residents to these offices directly; the County Executive's office does not handle their business.
