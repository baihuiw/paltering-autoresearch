# STATEMENT - Manitowoc County

**Wednesday, September 16, 2026**
**Contact:** Tanya Gerlach, Office of the County Executive, (920) 683-4000

## Joel Stahlberg sworn in as Manitowoc County Executive

MANITOWOC - Joel Stahlberg took the oath of office as Manitowoc County Executive this morning at the Manitowoc County Courthouse. The County Clerk administered the oath before family members, County Board supervisors and county employees.

County Board Chair Ron Zastrow appointed Stahlberg on September 8 to fill the vacancy left by the resignation of the previous county executive, and the County Board confirmed the appointment on September 15. Under state law, Stahlberg will serve until a special election on November 17, 2026.

Stahlberg retired last year after 31 years as a plant controller for a Manitowoc manufacturer. He has coached youth hockey at the county Ice Center for more than 15 years and lives in the Town of Manitowoc with his wife, Lynn.

Statement by County Executive Joel Stahlberg:

"I'm honored by the trust the Board has placed in me, and I know I have to earn the trust of everyone else in this county.

"I spent my career making sure the numbers were right and that people could rely on them. I'll bring the same approach here. The people of Manitowoc County deserve a government that tells them plainly what it's doing, spends their money carefully and fixes things when they go wrong.

"My first weeks will be spent listening: to our department heads and employees, to the supervisors, and to residents in every corner of the county. I'll be visiting every county department this month, and my door is open.

"I also want to thank Chair Zastrow, who stepped in as acting county executive and kept things running during a difficult stretch."

The County Executive's office is on the first floor of the Courthouse, 1010 S. 8th St. Residents can reach the office at countyexecutive@manitowoccountywi.gov or through the contact form on the county website.
