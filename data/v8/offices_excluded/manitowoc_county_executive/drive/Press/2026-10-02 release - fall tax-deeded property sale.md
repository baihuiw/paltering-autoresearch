## County offers seven parcels in fall sealed-bid sale

MANITOWOC - Manitowoc County is accepting sealed bids through Monday, October 26 on seven county-owned parcels acquired for unpaid property taxes, Public Works Director Brian Hornacek announced today.

The parcels include four vacant residential lots in the City of Two Rivers, a former tavern building on County Road R in the Town of Cooperstown, a 7.8-acre wooded parcel in the Town of Rockland, and a small strip of land in the Town of Newton. Minimum bids range from $3,000 to $48,000 and are based on recent appraisals.

"Every parcel we sell goes back on the tax roll and back into productive use," said County Executive Joel Stahlberg. "That's good for the neighborhoods and good for every taxpayer in the county. Our sealed-bid process is open to everyone and gets the best price for the public."

Bids are due at 10:00 a.m. on Monday, October 26 at the Public Works office in the Administration Building, 1110 S. 9th St., Manitowoc, and will be opened in public at that time. The Public Works Committee will act on the bids at its November meeting. The bid packet, with maps, appraisal summaries and terms of sale, is available on the county website and at the Public Works office.

Properties are sold as-is by quit claim deed. Buyers are responsible for checking zoning, access and any building code requirements with the city or town.

The county sold 14 parcels in 2025 and five so far in 2026 through its spring and fall sealed-bid sales.
