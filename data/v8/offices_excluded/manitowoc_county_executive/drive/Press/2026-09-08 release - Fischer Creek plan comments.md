## County seeks public comments on Fischer Creek Park plan

MANITOWOC - Manitowoc County is asking residents to comment on a draft update of the master plan for Fischer Creek Park, the 109-acre county park on Lake Michigan near Cleveland.

The draft plan proposes a 60-site campground with electric hookups north of the main parking lot, an accessible kayak and canoe launch at the mouth of Fischer Creek, and resurfacing of about two miles of the bluff and creek trails, with two new scenic overlooks.

"Fischer Creek is one of the most beautiful places in the county, and we want to hear from the people who use it and live near it before anything moves forward," said Parks Manager Dan Pautz.

The draft plan, a site map and a cost summary are posted on the county website and are available at the Planning, Parks & Land Information office in the Administration Building, 1110 S. 9th St., Manitowoc.

### How to comment

Comments will be accepted through **Friday, October 2, 2026 at 4:30 p.m.**:
- Online, using the comment form on the Parks page of the county website;
- By email to parks@manitowoccountywi.gov; or
- By mail to Manitowoc County Parks, 1110 S. 9th St., Manitowoc, WI 54220.

All comments received by the deadline will be summarized for the Manitowoc County Planning and Park Commission, which is expected to make a recommendation to the County Board this fall. The Commission's meetings are open to the public.

Park visitors can also see the plan on display boards at the park shelter through September.
