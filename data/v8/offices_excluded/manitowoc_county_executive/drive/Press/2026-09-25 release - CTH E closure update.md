## CTH E culvert project update: reopening now expected in early November

MANITOWOC - County Trunk Highway E between Hillcrest Road and Kassel Road in the Town of Kossuth will remain closed into early November while Manitowoc County Highway Department crews finish replacing the culverts that carry Tamarack Creek under the road, Highway Commissioner Tom Kracht said today.

The road has been closed since August 24. Work was delayed about a week in September while the gas utility relocated a gas line that crosses the work area, which took longer than planned. The relocation was finished September 18, and county crews are now preparing the base for the new culvert.

"Our crews are working hard to get CTH E open before winter, and we appreciate everyone's patience with the detour," Kracht said. "Farmers, school buses and milk trucks use this road every day, and we know the detour adds miles."

The project replaces two failing 72-inch corrugated metal pipes with a single 12-foot by 6-foot precast concrete box culvert. The new culvert is designed to carry a 100-year flood, which should end the road flooding at Tamarack Creek that closed CTH E four times in the past ten years, and its buried bottom will let fish move up and down the creek.

The work is being done by Highway Department crews, which saves the county the cost of a contractor. The project budget is $286,000.

The posted detour remains in place. Reopening is weather permitting. Updates will be posted on the county website and the county's Facebook page.
