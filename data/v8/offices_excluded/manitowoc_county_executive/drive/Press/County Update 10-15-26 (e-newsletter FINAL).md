# County Update - Manitowoc County
**Thursday, October 15, 2026** | From the Office of the County Executive

---

## A note from Joel

Four weeks into this job, I've visited the Highway shop, the dispatch center, the Expo grounds, Human Services and the Recycling Center, and I've met hundreds of the people who keep this county running. My recommended 2027 budget is now in the County Board's hands. It holds the county's share of your property tax bill nearly flat while keeping roads plowed, parks open and services close to home. The public hearing is November 9. I hope you'll come.

-- Joel Stahlberg, County Executive

---

## 2027 budget hearing: Monday, November 9

The County Board will hold its public hearing on the 2027 budget at 6:00 p.m. on Monday, November 9 at the Heritage Center, 1701 Michigan Ave., Manitowoc, followed by the annual meeting. The recommended budget is posted on the county website. You can also send comments to your County Board supervisor.

## Ice Center opens October 23

Public skating starts Friday, October 23 at the Manitowoc County Ice Center at the Expo grounds. Open skate schedule, rental times and learn-to-skate sign-ups are on the Expo page.

## CTH E at Tamarack Creek

CTH E between Hillcrest Road and Kassel Road will stay closed through the winter. The culvert work will resume in spring when conditions allow. Please continue to follow the posted detour. Thank you for your patience.

## Flu shots

Manitowoc County Public Health is holding walk-in flu shot clinics for adults and children 6 months and older. Bring your insurance card; no one is turned away for inability to pay. Dates and locations are on the Public Health page and Facebook.

## Clean Sweep: thank you!

A record 612 cars came through the September 19 Clean Sweep at the Expo grounds, and we collected more than 40,000 pounds of household hazardous waste. The next Clean Sweep is in May.

## Winter is coming

Highway crews are ready, with salt on hand and all plow routes staffed. Please keep cars, basketball hoops and trash carts out of the road during storms, and mark your mailbox and driveway edges.

## Special election November 17

The County Clerk reminds voters that a special election for County Executive is on Tuesday, November 17. This is a separate election from the November 3 general election. For your polling place and absentee ballot information, visit myvote.wi.gov or contact your municipal clerk.
