## County 911 center answers 97 percent of calls within 15 seconds

MANITOWOC - The Manitowoc County Joint Dispatch Center answered 97 percent of 911 calls within 15 seconds in the first eight months of 2026, better than the national standard of 90 percent, County Executive Joel Stahlberg announced today.

The Joint Dispatch Center (JDC) answers every 911 call made in Manitowoc County and dispatches the Sheriff's Office, city and village police departments, fire departments and ambulance services across the county. Its 21 telecommunicators handled more than 24,000 911 calls and 55,000 non-emergency calls through August.

"When you call 911 in this county, a trained professional picks up fast, and that matters more than anything else in an emergency," Stahlberg said. "I spent an evening at the consoles last week, and I came away impressed by how calm and careful our dispatchers are."

The county's 2027 recommended budget, to be released October 1, includes an upgrade of the dispatch center's computer-aided dispatch (CAD) system to prepare for Next Generation 911, which will let 911 centers receive photos, video and more precise caller locations in the future.

### Tips for residents

- **Know your address.** Every home in the towns has a rural address sign at the end of the driveway. Make sure yours is visible from the road in both directions, and teach children their address.
- **Text if you can't talk.** Text-to-911 is available everywhere in Manitowoc County. Call if you can, text if you can't.
- **Stay on the line.** If you call 911 by mistake, stay on the line and tell the dispatcher, so they don't have to spend time calling you back.
- **Use the non-emergency line** for things that are not emergencies: (920) 683-4200 (Sheriff's Office) or your local police department.

The JDC is part of Manitowoc County Emergency Services, which also includes Emergency Management, the county's hazardous materials team coordination and the Emergency Operations Center.
