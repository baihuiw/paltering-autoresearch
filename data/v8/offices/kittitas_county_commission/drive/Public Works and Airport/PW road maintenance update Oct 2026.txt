KITTITAS COUNTY PUBLIC WORKS
Road Maintenance Update - for the Board study session, October 19, 2026
N. Ambrose, Director

1. FALL CHIP SEAL AND PATCHING
Chip seal season closed September 18. Completed 2026: 41.6 lane-miles (plan: 44). Not completed: Smithson Road (2.4 lane-miles), moved to the 2027 program because of an aggregate shortage in late August.
Pothole patching: crews working through the backlog on Number 81 Road, Hayward Road and Upper Peoh Point Road. 118 patch tickets closed since September 1.

2. GRAVEL ROADS
Fall grading on the lower county gravel system is about 70 percent complete. Dust complaints on Hayward Road (gravel trucks from a private pit): the board referred this on 9/15; staff applied a second round of dust palliative 9/23 and asked the pit operator to reduce speed.

3. GUARDRAIL AND SAFETY
Manastash Road guardrail (MP 3.2-3.6) awarded 10/6; contractor expects to install before November 15.
Thorp Highway at Clerf Road: speed study scheduled for the week of November 2; results to the board in December.
Sign replacement: 214 signs replaced so far this year under the retroreflectivity program.

4. WINTER READINESS
Sand and salt: 2,600 tons of salt ordered under the state contract; sand stockpiles at Ellensburg, Thorp, Cle Elum and Easton are full.
Plows: 22 trucks ready; two new operators hired; snow priority map unchanged from 2025-26 (priority 1: school bus routes and roads to Bowers Field and the hospital).

5. BRIDGES
Biennial inspections completed on 31 county bridges in August-September; no load restrictions added. Teanaway River bridge deck joint repair scheduled for spring 2027.

6. BOWERS FIELD
Runway 11/29 crack seal the week of October 19. Perimeter fence repairs done. Hangar door roller quotes for Row J due October 30.

7. EQUIPMENT
Two pickups surplused (auction October 24). New grader delivered for the upper county shop.

8. STAFFING
Equipment Operator II: two positions filled; one open (Cle Elum shop).
