# Snow and Ice Control Plan 2026-27 - Summary

*Kittitas County Public Works, Road Maintenance Division. Summary for the Board study session of October 19, 2026. The full plan and route maps are on file at the Public Works office.*

## What the county plows

The county maintains about 560 centerline miles of county road: about 410 miles paved and 150 miles gravel. City streets (Ellensburg, Cle Elum, Kittitas, Roslyn, South Cle Elum) are plowed by the cities. Interstate 90, US 97, SR 10, SR 821, SR 903 and SR 970 are state highways plowed by the state department of transportation. Private roads and driveways are not plowed by the county.

## Priorities

| Priority | Roads | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | School bus routes, roads to the hospital and Bowers Field, main arterials (Thorp Hwy, Vantage Hwy, Reecer Creek Rd, Number 81 Rd, Manastash Rd, Teanaway Rd) | Plowed and sanded within 4 hours after snowfall stops; started during storms |
| 2 | Collector roads and other paved roads with year-round residents | Within 12 hours after snowfall stops |
| 3 | Gravel roads and low-volume roads | Within 24-36 hours, after priorities 1 and 2 |
| 4 | Cul-de-sacs and dead ends | After priority 3 |

The priority map is unchanged from 2025-26.

## Crews and equipment

- Shops: Ellensburg, Thorp, Cle Elum, Easton.
- 22 plow trucks, 6 graders with wings, 3 loaders.
- Shifts: 2:00 a.m. starts during storms; 12-hour shifts maximum, with an 8-hour rest break.
- Staffing: two new Equipment Operator II hires this fall; one vacancy at the Cle Elum shop.

## Materials

| Material | Amount | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deicer salt (state contract) | 2,600 tons ordered | Pre-treating and spot treatment on priority 1 paved roads |
| Winter sand (county pits) | 9,000 cubic yards | All roads |
| Liquid anti-icer | 60,000 gallons | Pre-treating bridges and curves before frost events |

Sand stockpiles at Ellensburg, Thorp, Cle Elum and Easton are full.

## Rules residents should know

- Don't push snow from driveways onto or across the county road (county code). It creates ridges that are a hazard to traffic and plows.
- Vehicles parked in the right-of-way during a storm may be towed at the owner's cost.
- Mailbox damage: the county replaces mailboxes damaged by direct plow contact with a standard post and box; it does not replace boxes knocked down by thrown snow.
- Plow berms at driveways are unavoidable. Crews cannot clear them for individual driveways.
- Report hazards (drifting, ice, downed trees) to Public Works road dispatch, (509) 962-7523, 7:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m. weekdays; after hours, call 911 for anything blocking a road.

## Changes this year

1. **Vantage Hwy pullouts.** Crews will clear the three chain-up pullouts between MP 8 and MP 22 after the main lanes, in response to residents' requests. Not a guaranteed service level.
2. **Road condition map.** Public Works will update the road conditions page on the county website by 7:00 a.m. on storm days.
3. **Anti-icer.** Extended to the Teanaway Rd curves and the Number 81 Rd canal crossing approaches.

## Communication with residents

- **Road conditions page** updated by 7:00 a.m. on storm days, and again at noon if conditions change.
- **Closures** (drifting on Vantage Hwy, Parke Creek Rd, Naneum Rd and Look Rd are the usual ones) are posted on the website and the county's social media, and sent to local radio.
- **School districts** get a 5:00 a.m. call from the road supervisor on storm days with conditions on bus routes.
- **Board office and commissioners:** calls about a specific road go to road dispatch. Staff will call residents back the same day during business hours.

## Chains and heavy vehicles

- County roads do not have chain requirements, but the county may close a road to vehicles over 10,000 pounds during thaw if the road surface is breaking up (seasonal load limits, usually February-March). Notices are posted at least 3 days ahead, except in an emergency.
- Farm equipment and hay trucks may get permits during load limits for essential trips.

## Gravel roads in winter

- Gravel roads are plowed but not sanded except on hills and curves.
- Graders work gravel roads during thaw to keep ruts from setting.

## Budget

2026 snow and ice budget: $1.62 million (road fund). 2025-26 season actual: $1.71 million (a heavier January). The 2027 request holds at $1.62 million, with salt prices up 6 percent under the state contract.
