# Bowers Field Airport Rules and Regulations

Kittitas County Public Works - Airport Division
Adopted by Resolution 2019-044; Section 6 revised by Resolution 2023-102

*Excerpt: Section 6, Hangars and Tiedowns. Full rules at the Airport Manager's office.*

## 6.1 County-owned hangars

The County owns 24 T-hangar units in Rows H and J. Units are leased month to month at the rate set in the County fee schedule. Tenants must keep an airworthy or under-restoration aircraft in the unit; storage of vehicles, boats or household goods is not allowed.

## 6.2 Waiting list

The Airport Manager keeps a waiting list for county-owned hangars. Anyone may be added to the list by submitting a hangar application with the aircraft registration. There is no fee to join the list. Applicants must confirm their interest once a year or may be removed.

## 6.3 Offering vacant units

When a county-owned hangar becomes vacant, the Airport Manager shall offer it to applicants in waiting list order. An applicant may decline a unit once and keep their place; an applicant who declines twice moves to the bottom of the list. An applicant who does not respond within 10 days of an offer is treated as declining.

## 6.4 Leases

Hangar leases at the standard rate are signed by the Airport Manager under delegated authority. Leases at other than the standard rate, and ground leases for privately built hangars, require Board of County Commissioners approval.

## 6.5 Tiedowns

Tiedowns on the north apron are first come, first served at the posted monthly or nightly rate. Tiedown tenants are not given priority for hangars.

## 6.6 Fairness

Hangar space is a public resource. The Airport Manager shall apply this section the same way to every applicant.

## 6.7 Inspections

The Airport Manager may inspect hangars with 24 hours' notice to tenants, or without notice in an emergency.
