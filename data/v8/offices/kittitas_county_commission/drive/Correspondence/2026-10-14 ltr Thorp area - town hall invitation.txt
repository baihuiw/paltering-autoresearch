To: Thorp-area community groups, churches, the Thorp school PTO, the fire district and the Grange
(sent by email and posted at the Thorp post office, the Grange hall and the school)

Dear neighbors,

I'd like to invite you and anyone in your organization to a **community town hall on Thursday, October 22, from 6:30 to 8:00 p.m. at the Thorp Grange hall.**

This is an open meeting. **Everyone is welcome, and you don't need to sign up or RSVP.** Doors open at 6:00 p.m. There will be coffee and cookies, and the hall is wheelchair accessible. If you need another accommodation, please call my office at (509) 962-7508 by Monday, October 19.

I was appointed to the Board of Commissioners on September 15, and since then I've spent most of my time with county departments and in budget meetings. I want to spend an evening hearing from the people who live here. I'll start with a short update (about 15 minutes) on the topics I hear about most:

- Thorp Highway speeds near the Clerf Road bus stop, and the speed study Public Works will do the week of November 2
- The six-year road plan (the board's hearing is October 20)
- The 2027 county budget and how to comment before the December 7 hearing
- Wildfire readiness, including the October 31 chipping day for Thorp and Manastash and the Firewise neighborhood grants
- Anything else you bring

The rest of the evening is for your questions and ideas. Public Works Director Neil Ambrose will be there to answer road questions, and I've invited the Thorp fire chief.

Because two commissioners are a quorum, my colleagues on the board won't attend together. This is my district meeting; if a second commissioner comes, the meeting will be noticed as a special meeting so that it complies with the open public meetings law.

If you can't make it, you can always reach me by email at renee.gustafsen@co.kittitas.wa.us, through the county website contact form, or by calling my office. Our regular board meetings are on the first and third Tuesdays at 9:00 a.m. in the Commissioners' Auditorium, and you can join them online or by phone.

I hope to see you on the 22nd.

Sincerely,

Renee Gustafsen
Chair, Kittitas County Board of Commissioners
