Dear Martin,

Thank you for your note about recycling in the upper county. I toured both of the county's solid waste sites in my first two weeks on the board, and the recycling area at Cle Elum was one of the things I asked about.

You asked about three things.

**Glass.** You're right that the county doesn't take glass for recycling. Our Solid Waste staff tell me the nearest place that buys glass is on the west side of the mountains, and trucking it there costs more than the glass is worth, so for now it goes in the garbage. The Solid Waste Advisory Committee asked last month whether crushed glass could be used locally as road base or pipe bedding, and staff are asking Public Works about a small pilot. If that works, we'd have a use for it here in the county.

**Full cardboard bins on Saturdays.** I saw this myself. Saturday traffic at Cle Elum has grown with all the new homes in the upper county, to about 410 transactions on an average Saturday. The advisory committee has asked staff to price a cardboard compactor for Cle Elum, which would hold several times as much. That estimate goes to the committee in November. In the meantime, Solid Waste will swap the bins more often on summer Saturdays.

**Contamination.** The mixed paper bins at Cle Elum are still getting plastic bags and food mixed in, which can spoil a whole load. New signs went up in August. If you want to help, the best thing is what you're already doing: breaking down boxes, and reminding neighbors that plastic bags go in the garbage, not the paper bin.

The county is also starting the five-year update of its solid and hazardous waste plan, which will look at recycling, yard waste and the hours and layout at Cle Elum. There will be a survey in the spring and open houses next fall, and I've asked staff to send you notice of those meetings. The advisory committee also has an open seat for a Cle Elum representative; the board expects to fill it on October 20, but there will be other openings.

Thank you for caring about this. Please call my office at (509) 962-7508 anytime.

Sincerely,

Renee Gustafsen
Chair, Kittitas County Board of Commissioners
