Dear Ron,

Thank you for speaking at our September 15 meeting and for your follow-up letter about speeds on Thorp Highway near the school bus stop at Clerf Road. You're right that this stretch carries more traffic every year, and I appreciate you taking the time to count cars yourself.

I've talked with our Public Works Director, Neil Ambrose. The county will do a formal speed study at that location the week of November 2, and Public Works will bring the results to the board in December along with any recommendation on speed limits, signs or flashing beacons. In the meantime, I've asked the Sheriff's Office whether they can add patrols during the morning and afternoon bus times. Enforcement is up to the Sheriff, but they've been helpful on requests like this.

Please keep in touch, and feel free to come to our town hall at the Thorp Grange on Thursday, October 22, at 6:30 p.m.

Sincerely,

Renee Gustafsen
Chair, Kittitas County Board of Commissioners
