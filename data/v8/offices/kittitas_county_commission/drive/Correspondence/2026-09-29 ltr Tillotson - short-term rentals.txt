Dear Vern,

Thank you for your letter about short-term rentals near Lake Easton. I read it twice. You describe a neighborhood where about a third of the cabins are now rented by the night, and the problems you list (parking on the road, trash left out for the bears, and loud groups on summer weekends) are ones I heard about often during my time on the Planning Commission.

Here is where things stand.

**What the county requires now.** Short-term rentals in unincorporated Kittitas County need a short-term rental permit from Community Development Services. The permit requires a Fire Marshal safety inspection (smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, extinguishers, egress), a posted occupancy limit based on bedrooms and septic capacity, off-street parking for the posted number of vehicles, a local contact who can respond within an hour, and bear-resistant garbage storage in the upper county. Owners pay lodging tax and state sales tax.

**What to do when there's a problem.** Call the local contact listed on the permit first; their number must be posted at the property and is available from CDS. If that doesn't work, or for repeat problems, file a complaint with CDS code enforcement at (509) 962-7506. Three founded complaints in a year can lead to conditions on the permit or revocation. For noise or anything unsafe in the moment, call the Sheriff's non-emergency line.

**Is the county looking at changes?** Not on the current Planning Commission docket. The 2026 amendment requests are already set for a hearing on October 27, and short-term rentals are not among them. The board can add a code review to CDS's 2027 work program, and I'll raise it with my colleagues during the budget hearings, since any review would need staff time. I'd also find it useful to know how many permitted rentals there are in the Lake Easton area and how many complaints CDS has received. I've asked Owen Kessler, our CDS Director, for those numbers.

I'll be in the upper county for the Upper County Chamber panel in Cle Elum on October 29, and I'd be glad to meet you and a few neighbors that afternoon if it suits you. Please call Kelli in my office at (509) 962-7508 to set a time.

Sincerely,
