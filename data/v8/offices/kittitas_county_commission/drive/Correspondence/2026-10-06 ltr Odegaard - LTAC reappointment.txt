Dear Marcy,

On behalf of the Board of County Commissioners, thank you for agreeing to serve another term on the Kittitas County Lodging Tax Advisory Committee. The board approved your reappointment on today's consent agenda. Your new term runs from January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2028.

The committee's work matters more than most people realize. The lodging tax that visitors pay on hotel, motel and short-term rental stays can be spent only on tourism promotion and tourism-related facilities, and state law requires the county to hear from a committee of lodging businesses and tourism organizations before it spends it. Your committee's recommendations shape how about half a million dollars a year is spent marketing the valley and the upper county, supporting events that bring overnight visitors, and operating the visitor centers.

I read the committee's 2027 recommendations from your September 24 meeting with interest. The committee recommended $533,000 in awards from 14 applications, with careful notes on each, including the decision to hold back on the wayfinding sign request until the state approves the sign locations. The board will take up the recommendations with the 2027 budget in December. If the committee wants to present them at the December 7 budget hearing, please let our Clerk of the Board, Lisa Brannigan, know so she can put you on the agenda.

A few housekeeping items from Lisa:

- The Prosecutor's office is offering open public meetings and public records training for board and committee members on Friday, October 9, 9:00 a.m. to noon, at the courthouse. If you completed the training in the last four years, you don't need to repeat it, but you're welcome to come.
- The committee's 2027 meeting calendar will go out in December. Commissioner Rasch will continue as the board's liaison.
- Please keep your contact information current with Lisa so committee packets reach you.

Thank you again for your time and judgment. Volunteers like you are how a county our size gets good advice.

Sincerely,
