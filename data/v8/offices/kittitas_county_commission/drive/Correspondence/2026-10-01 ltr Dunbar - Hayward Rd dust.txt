Dear Gloria,

Thank you for your letter of September 22 and for the photos. I could see the dust cloud from the trucks in every one of them, and I understand why you keep your windows shut all summer.

Here is what I've learned from our Public Works Director, Neil Ambrose:

1. **Dust control this season.** After residents raised Hayward Road at the board's September 15 meeting, Public Works put down a second application of dust palliative on the gravel section on September 23. The palliative season ends September 30 because it doesn't bond once nights get cold and wet, so there won't be another treatment until late spring. Crews will grade the gravel section in the next week or so as part of fall grading.

2. **The trucks.** The gravel trucks come from a privately owned pit that has its permits. Public Works has asked the operator to have drivers slow down on Hayward, and the operator agreed to remind them. Speed enforcement is up to the Sheriff's Office, and I have passed your letter to them as well.

3. **Paving.** Hayward Road's gravel section (about 2.1 miles) is in the draft 2027-2032 Six-Year Transportation Improvement Program as a new project to convert it to a chip-sealed (bituminous) surface with dust control. It's listed in 2030 at an estimated $1.26 million, and Public Works plans to apply for a state grant for it in 2027. I want to be straight with you: projects in the last three years of the plan are planning projects, and they move up only when there's money to build them. The road fund is stretched, so the grant matters.

4. **How to weigh in.** The board's public hearing on the six-year plan is **Tuesday, October 20, at 9:30 a.m.** in the Commissioners' Auditorium at the courthouse, and you can also join online. Anyone can testify for about three minutes. If you can't come, you can send written comments to Public Works by noon on Monday, October 19, and they'll be in the record. Comments from neighbors about traffic, dust, and who uses the road help Public Works and the board decide where a project belongs on the list. A short petition or letter signed by several households on Hayward carries weight.

I've asked Public Works to send you a copy of the draft list. Please call my office at (509) 962-7508 if you have questions, or catch me at the town hall at the Thorp Grange on October 22.

Sincerely,
