Dear Tor,

Thank you for your letter about the weed notice you received for the whitetop along your east fence. I understand how frustrating it is to get a notice when the seed is blowing in from somewhere else.

Under state law, every landowner is responsible for controlling noxious weeds on their own property, and our Noxious Weed Control Board applies that rule the same way across the county. The good news is that there is help. Through the weed board's cost-share program, a county crew will treat the area and you pay only half the cost of the product; the county covers the crew's time. Luis Arreguin, our weed coordinator, can also come out and look at the site with you at no charge. You can reach him at (509) 962-7006.

You also mentioned the county road shoulder next to your property. Our crews treat county road right-of-way on Fairview Road on a regular schedule, and I've asked that your stretch be checked this fall.

I grew up pulling whitetop on our own place, so I know it's a long fight. Thank you for taking care of your land.

Sincerely,

Renee Gustafsen
Chair, Kittitas County Board of Commissioners
