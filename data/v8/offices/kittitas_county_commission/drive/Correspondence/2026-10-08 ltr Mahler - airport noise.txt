Dear Lyle,

Thank you for your email of September 29. I'm sorry the first week of September was so hard on your household. You're right that the helicopters were starting before 6:00 most mornings that week.

I asked our Airport Manager, Rick Tolliver, what happened and what the county can do. Here is what he told me.

**The September helicopter traffic.** The early-morning flights from September 4 through September 12 were firefighting helicopters working the Liberty fire from a temporary base on the north side of Bowers Field. Firefighting, medical and law enforcement aircraft are exempt from the airport's noise procedures, and I think most of us would want it that way. The fire base closed on September 12.

**What the airport's procedures say.** Bowers Field has written noise abatement and engine run-up procedures, revised in 2022 with input from neighbors. They ask pilots to use Runway 29 when winds are calm, to avoid low flying over the homes north and east of the field, and to avoid touch-and-go practice between 9:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. Maintenance engine run-ups (running an engine at high power for testing rather than just before takeoff) are allowed only from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. on weekdays and 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. on weekends and holidays, and long run-ups have to be done at the far end of the field. I've enclosed a copy.

One limit I should be honest about: the Federal Aviation Administration, not the county, controls aircraft in flight, so the flight procedures are voluntary. The run-up rules are on airport property, so the county can enforce those. Rick told me that a Saturday-morning run-up on September 19 that neighbors heard on Bowers Road was traced to a shop on the field running an engine before the allowed hour, and he spoke with the owner.

**How to report noise.** If you hear something that seems outside these rules, please call the airport office at (509) 962-7536 (voicemail after hours) with the date, time and what you heard. Staff log every call, try to identify the aircraft, and follow up when it involves a run-up or a repeat problem. The log is summarized at each Airport Advisory Committee meeting. The next meeting is Thursday, November 12, at 5:30 p.m. in the terminal conference room, and the public may speak.

**Looking ahead.** The airport's layout plan update, which starts this fall, includes a noise review. The committee will hold public meetings on it, and I've asked that neighbors on Bowers Road get notice of them. Commissioner Doug Rasch is the board's liaison to the airport and the committee, and I've copied him.

Thank you for raising this in such a constructive way. Please keep in touch.

Sincerely,

Renee Gustafsen
Chair, Kittitas County Board of Commissioners
