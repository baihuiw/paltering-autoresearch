Dear Joyce,

Thank you for writing about the pullouts on Vantage Highway, and for your kind words about my appointment. Your letter was one of the first to land on my desk, and it is a good example of something the county can actually fix.

You asked two things: whether the county will keep the chain-up pullouts clear this winter, and whether more pullouts could be added for slow farm equipment and trucks that need to pull over to turn.

On the first, I spoke with our Public Works Director, Neil Ambrose. Last winter the plows cleared the travel lanes but often left the pullouts buried, and you're right that drivers ended up chaining up in the lane. This winter's snow and ice plan will clear the three pullouts between milepost 8 and milepost 22 after the main lanes are done on each storm. Neil was careful to say that this comes after the priority roads, so it won't always happen the same morning, but it is now part of the plan rather than left to chance.

On the second, adding paved pullouts is already in the county's draft six-year transportation plan: six new paved pullouts between milepost 8 and milepost 22, planned for 2028 at an estimated $420,000. The board will hold a public hearing on the six-year plan on Tuesday, October 20, at 9:30 a.m. in the Commissioners' Auditorium, and you're welcome to testify in person, online, or in writing to Public Works by noon the day before. If you and your neighbors want it moved up, that hearing is the place to say so.

Finally, I've asked Public Works to look at the faded "Slow Vehicle Turnout" signs you mentioned near milepost 15. They'll be checked under the sign replacement program this fall.

Thank you again, and please don't hesitate to call my office at (509) 962-7508.

Sincerely,

Renee Gustafsen
Chair, Kittitas County Board of Commissioners
