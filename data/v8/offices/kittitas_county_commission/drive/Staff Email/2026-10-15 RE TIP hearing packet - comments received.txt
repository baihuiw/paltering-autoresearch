Nov 17 for adoption, per Neil (gives us time to respond to testimony).

The right answer on getting a road on the list:
1. Testify at the hearing Oct 20 at 9:30 (in person or online), or send written comments to Public Works by noon Mon Oct 19. Say which road, where, and what the problem is (dust, safety, traffic, condition, school bus, etc.).
2. Public Works looks at every request when it updates the list. We rank projects using traffic counts, pavement or surface condition, crash history, school bus routes, and resident comments. Gravel-to-paved requests also look at how many homes are on the road and truck traffic.
3. A road that doesn't make this year's draft can be added next year. The plan is updated every year.
4. Being on the list in years 4-6 (2030-2032) means planned, not funded. Those move up when we get grant money.
5. For maintenance problems in the meantime (potholes, washboarding, dust), call road dispatch at (509) 962-7523.

Hayward is a good example: it was added to this year's draft because of last year's comments. It's in 2030 and we'll apply for a state grant in 2027.

Ellen
