Hi Kelli,

You asked for a quick status for Renee's update and the town hall. Here's where the Firewise neighborhood grants stand two weeks in:

- Applications received: 2 (one road association near Easton, 22 homes, asking $5,000; one HOA in the Teanaway area, 41 homes, asking $4,600).
- Calls and emails from neighborhoods thinking about it: 11. Most are in the Ronald / Lake Cle Elum area and around Easton. A couple from Thorp Prairie and Manastash.
- Total available: $40,000. Max per neighborhood $5,000, minimum $500, 1:1 match (cash or volunteer hours at $32/hr, equipment and materials count too). Deadline Dec 1 at 4:00.
- We expect 10-14 applications by the deadline based on last year. Last round we had 9 applications and funded 8.

The questions I get most:
1. "Do we have to already be a Firewise USA site?" No. They need to be recognized OR have started the process (called us and scheduled or done a risk assessment). I can do a risk assessment visit in about 2 weeks right now; it gets harder after the snow.
2. "How do we count match?" Most groups use volunteer hours from work days. A $5,000 grant needs $5,000 in match, about 157 hours. Sign-in sheets with hours are enough documentation.
3. "Is it paid up front?" No, reimbursement after the work. Up to 25 percent advance if they need it for a contractor.
4. "Minimum size?" 8 homes. Cabins count.

If anyone writes to the commissioners, it's fine to send them straight to me: (509) 962-7506 or this email. The guidelines and application are on the CDS Fire Marshal page.

Holly

Individuals can get a free home wildfire assessment from us anytime, and the chipping days are open to everybody on the routes (Oct 17 Easton/Ronald, Oct 24 Cle Elum/Roslyn with a drop point at the Cle Elum PW shop for anyone who missed the 17th, Oct 31 Thorp/Manastash). The conservation district has a separate forest health cost-share for bigger forest parcels.

The grant itself has to go through a group of 8+ homes. If someone wants to start a group, I'm happy to come to a neighbor meeting.
