# Commenting on the 2027 Kittitas County Budget

*Public information sheet. Board of County Commissioners office with the Auditor's finance division. October 2026. Copies at the Board office counter, the Auditor's office and on the county website.*

The county adopts its budget once a year. Residents can comment at any stage, but some points in the calendar matter more than others.

## The key dates

| Date | What happens | Can the public take part? |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 13-15 | Department budget hearings (appointed departments) | Open to the public to watch, in person or online. These are work sessions between the Board and departments; there is no public testimony. |
| Oct 26-28 | Elected office and courts budget hearings | Same as above. |
| Nov 2 | **Preliminary budget** published | Read it online, at the Auditor's office, or at the Ellensburg and Cle Elum public libraries. |
| Nov 16 | Board sets 2027 property tax levies | Regular agenda item; general citizen comment at the start of the meeting. |
| **Dec 7, 10:00 a.m.** | **Public hearing on the 2027 budget and the 2026 supplemental budget** | **Yes - testimony in person or online, and written comments.** |
| Dec 14 | Adoption if not adopted Dec 7 | Board action. |

The public hearing is in the **Commissioners' Auditorium, Kittitas County Courthouse, 205 W. 5th Ave., Ellensburg**, and online (join link on the meeting notice and the Board's agenda page). Speakers sign in at the door or raise a hand online; the Chair usually allows 3 minutes per person.

## Written comments

You do not have to attend to be heard. Send written comments:

- **By email** to any commissioner (first.last@co.kittitas.wa.us) or to the Clerk of the Board, lisa.brannigan@co.kittitas.wa.us
- **By mail or in person** to the Board of County Commissioners, 205 W. 5th Ave., Suite 108, Ellensburg, WA 98926
- **Through the county website contact form** ("Budget / taxes")

Comments received by **4:00 p.m. Friday, December 4** are copied to all three commissioners and included in the hearing packet. Comments that arrive later, up until the Board votes, are still read and entered in the record, but may not be in the printed packet.

Tip: name the program, department or fund you're writing about (for example, "Extension - 4-H youth programs" or "Public Works - road fund") and say what you'd like the Board to do. Specific comments are easiest to act on.

## How the budget is organized

- **Current expense (general) fund**: most county services paid by property and sales tax, including the Sheriff, jail, courts, Prosecutor, Public Health's general fund share, Extension, Assessor, Treasurer, Auditor and the Board office.
- **Special revenue funds**: roads (road levy and gas tax), solid waste (site and hauler fees), the Event Center, Bowers Field, lodging tax, and others. These funds must balance on their own revenues.
- The Board can move money among current expense departments, but not freely between special revenue funds and the general fund.

## About Extension and 4-H

WSU Extension in Kittitas County (including 4-H youth development, master gardeners and agriculture programs) is a partnership between the county and the university. The county pays office space, support staff and program costs through the current expense fund. Extension's 2027 budget was reviewed at the department hearing on Wednesday, October 14 at 2:00 p.m. Comments about Extension funding are welcome in writing or at the December 7 hearing.
