KITTITAS COUNTY PUBLIC WORKS
MEMORANDUM

TO: Board of County Commissioners
CC: Auditor's Office, finance division
FROM: Neil Ambrose, Public Works Director
DATE: September 28, 2026
RE: County road fund (101) - 2027 budget request and five-year outlook

1. SUMMARY
The road fund is in sound shape for 2027 but is spending more than it takes in each year on preservation. Our 2027 request is $22,312,000, up 3.1 percent. It holds the chip seal program at about 44 lane-miles and funds the snow and ice program at the 2026 level. Without new grant money, the fund balance falls below our target by 2029.

2. REVENUE (2027 estimates)
  County road levy (unincorporated)            $9,583,400   (1 percent plus new construction; finance draft)
  Motor vehicle fuel tax (state distribution)  $3,410,000   (flat; fuel use steady)
  Federal forest receipts and PILT share        $640,000
  Grants (state and federal, project-specific) $5,820,000   (Manastash guardrail carryover; Number 81 RAP; Reecer Creek STBG)
  Charges to other funds and agencies           $1,280,000   (work for the airport, solid waste, cities)
  Interest and miscellaneous                      $310,000
  TOTAL REVENUE                               $21,043,400

  Maintenance (patching, grading, ditches, signs) $6,940,000

4. FUND BALANCE
  Projected 1/1/2027: $8,940,000
  Projected 12/31/2027: $7,671,400
  Policy target: 25 percent of operating expenditures (about $3.3 million) plus a $2 million capital reserve, total $5.3 million.

5. FIVE-YEAR OUTLOOK (no new revenue, no levy diversion)
                    2027        2028        2029        2030        2031
  Revenue         21.04       20.66       21.01       21.34       21.70   ($ millions)
  Expenditures    22.31       22.44       22.90       23.29       23.71
  Year-end bal.    7.67        5.89        4.00        2.05        0.04
The balance drops below the $5.3 million target in 2029. The main pressures are asphalt and aggregate prices (up 18 percent since 2023), salt, and the growing paved mileage in the upper county.

6. OPTIONS WE ARE PURSUING
- Grants: RAP applications for Number 81 Rd (pending) and Hayward Rd (2027 round), the federal safety program for Westside Rd guardrail, and bridge funding for Parke Creek.
- Six-year TIP: the draft for the October 20 hearing keeps years 4-6 as planning years; projects there do not move up without funding.
- Chip seal: hold at 44 lane-miles; deferring it costs more later.
- Gravel to paved conversions (Cove Rd, Hayward Rd) add long-term maintenance cost; we will bring a policy on which gravel roads qualify, based on traffic counts and dust complaints, with the 2028 TIP.

7. REQUEST
Accept the road fund request for the October 13 hearing and give direction on the levy (finance draft assumes the full 1 percent plus new construction).

8. STAFFING AND EQUIPMENT
- Road maintenance staff: 46 positions (no change requested). Two Equipment Operator II positions were filled this fall; one remains open at the Cle Elum shop. We are holding one engineering technician position vacant through mid-2027 to help the fund balance.
- Overtime: 2026 snow and ice overtime was about $310,000, mostly in January. The 2027 request holds overtime at the 2026 budget.
- Equipment: the equipment rental and revolving fund (501) will replace two plow trucks in 2027 (ordered in 2026 because of a 14-month build time). Rental charges to the road fund rise about 3 percent to cover replacement reserves.
- Facilities: the Cle Elum shop salt shed is in the TIP for 2031. Until then, salt for the upper county is stored under tarps at the Cle Elum shop and hauled from Ellensburg during long storms.

9. RISKS
- A hard winter. Each additional major storm costs about $90,000-$120,000 in labor, materials and equipment time.
- Grant timing. If the Number 81 Rd grant is not awarded in December, that overlay slips to 2028 and the 2027 capital line drops by about $1.1 million (spending and revenue both).
- Aggregate prices and supply. The 2026 shortage in late August cost us 2.4 lane-miles of chip seal. We will bid the 2027 crushed surfacing stockpile in October (Oct 20 agenda) to lock in quantity.

N. Ambrose
