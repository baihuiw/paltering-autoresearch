# Kittitas County 2027 Budget Calendar

Accepted by the Board of County Commissioners, September 15, 2026
Prepared by the Auditor's Office, finance division

Under RCW 36.40, the county budget is prepared by the Auditor's finance staff from department requests, reviewed by the Board in hearings, and adopted after a public hearing in December.

## Key dates

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| Aug 10 | Budget instructions and salary projections to departments |
| Sept 11 | Department and elected office requests due to finance |
| Oct 13-15 | Department budget hearings with the Board (appointed departments) |
| Oct 26-28 | Budget hearings with elected offices and courts; wrap-up |
| Nov 2 | Preliminary budget filed; available to the public |
| Nov 16 | Board sets the 2027 property tax levies (current expense and road) |
| Dec 7, 10:00 a.m. | Public hearing on the 2027 budget and 2026 supplemental budget amendments |
| Dec 14 | Budget adoption (if not adopted Dec 7) |

## Guidance from the Board

- Current expense requests should hold to 2026 levels except for contracted wage increases and insurance.
- New positions require a written justification and a funding source.
- Special revenue funds (roads, solid waste, Event Center, airport) must balance on their own revenues and fund balance.
- Supplemental amendments to the 2026 budget will be heard with the 2027 budget on December 7. Departments should submit 2026 amendment requests by November 13.
