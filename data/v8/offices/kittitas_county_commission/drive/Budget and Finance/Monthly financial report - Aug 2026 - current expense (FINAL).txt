Notes:
- Jail medical contract is over its pace (74 percent) because of two long hospital stays in May-June. Sheriff's office will request a supplemental appropriation of about $160,000 with the December supplemental budget.
- Overtime in the jail is at 81 percent of the annual budget. Two corrections officer vacancies were filled in August.
- Indigent defense is on pace. A state grant reimbursement of $112,000 for public defense is expected in October.
- Board of Commissioners office: 61.8 percent. Salary savings expected from the District 2 vacancy in September will be offset by the one-time cost of a new chair's equipment and training.
