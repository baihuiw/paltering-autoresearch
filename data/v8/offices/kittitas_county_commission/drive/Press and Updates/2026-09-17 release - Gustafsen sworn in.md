## Renee Gustafsen sworn in as District 2 Commissioner

ELLENSBURG - Renee Gustafsen took the oath of office as Kittitas County Commissioner for District 2 on Wednesday morning in the Commissioners' Auditorium, surrounded by family, neighbors and county staff.

Commissioners Gene Fortier and Doug Rasch appointed Gustafsen on September 15 from three nominees submitted by the county central committee, as the Washington Constitution requires when a partisan county office falls vacant. She will serve until the seat is filled at the November 2027 general election.

Gustafsen and her husband Mark farm hay on Tjossem Road east of Ellensburg. She retired in 2025 after 22 years as finance manager of a Yakima Valley irrigation district and served on the Kittitas County Planning Commission from 2021 until her appointment.

"I've spent my career making budgets balance and my weekends moving hand lines," Gustafsen said. "I know what it costs to run a farm and what it costs to run a county, and I'm going to work to make sure people get their money's worth from both. My door is open."

Her priorities for the coming months: the 2027 county budget, wildfire readiness in the upper county, county roads, and keeping the fair and Bowers Field strong.

Residents can reach Commissioner Gustafsen at renee.gustafsen@co.kittitas.wa.us, through the contact form at co.kittitas.wa.us, or by calling the Board office at (509) 962-7508.
