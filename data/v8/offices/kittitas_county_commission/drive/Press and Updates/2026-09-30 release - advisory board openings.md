# NEWS RELEASE - Kittitas County Board of Commissioners

**For immediate release: Wednesday, September 30, 2026**
**Contact: Lisa Brannigan, Clerk of the Board, (509) 962-7508**

## Volunteers wanted: county advisory boards have openings

ELLENSBURG - The Kittitas County Board of Commissioners is looking for residents to serve on several citizen advisory boards and committees. These volunteer groups advise the board on land use, roads and the airport, solid waste, tourism spending and the fair.

"I served on the Planning Commission for five years before I joined this board, and it was the best education in county government I could have had," said Chair Renee Gustafsen. "You don't need to be an expert. You need to be willing to read the packet, listen to your neighbors, and give honest advice."

### Current openings

| Board or committee | Openings | Meets | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Planning Commission** | 1 (unexpired term to Dec 31, 2028) | 4th Tuesday, 6:30 p.m., plus workshops | Holds hearings and recommends comprehensive plan and development code changes to the board |
| **Solid Waste Advisory Committee** | 1 (Cle Elum representative); 1 (recycling industry), term begins Jan 2027 | Quarterly, Thursday afternoons | Advises on transfer sites, recycling, household hazardous waste, and the solid waste plan update starting in 2027 |
| **Bowers Field Airport Advisory Committee** | 1 | Second Thursday, most months, 5:30 p.m. | Advises on airport operations, the layout plan update, noise, and capital projects |
| **Fair Advisory Committee** | 2 (terms begin Jan 2027) | Monthly, Wednesday evenings, 6:30 p.m. | Advises on the county fair and Event Center facilities |
| **Lodging Tax Advisory Committee** | 1 (tourism-funded activity representative) | As needed, about 4 times a year | Recommends how lodging tax revenue is spent on tourism |

### Who can apply

Most positions require residence in Kittitas County; some require residence in a particular area or a particular background (for example, a lodging business or a tourism organization for the Lodging Tax Advisory Committee). The Planning Commission position may be filled from any part of the county.

Members serve without pay. Planning Commission members receive mileage. All new members complete open public meetings and public records training, which the Prosecutor's office provides at the courthouse; the next session is Friday, October 9.

### How to apply

Applications are on the county website (Board of Commissioners - Boards and Committees) and at the Board office in the courthouse, 205 W. 5th Ave., Suite 108, Ellensburg. Submit by email, mail or in person.

- **Planning Commission and Solid Waste (Cle Elum):** applications reviewed as received; the board expects to appoint in October and November.
- **Terms beginning January 2027:** apply by **Friday, November 20, 2026**.

The board makes appointments at its regular meetings, usually on the consent agenda.

### About the board's advisory groups

Kittitas County has more than 20 citizen boards and committees. A full list, with meeting schedules, agendas and minutes, is on the county website. Most meetings are open to the public, and many can be joined online.
