## Record entries, big hearts at the 2026 Kittitas County Fair

ELLENSBURG - The 2026 Kittitas County Fair wrapped up Monday with a record 2,140 entries from 612 exhibitors, and the Board of County Commissioners is thanking the young people, families and volunteers who made it happen through smoke and triple-digit heat.

"The fair is where this county shows what it's made of," said Commissioner Gene Fortier, who served as acting chair during fair week. "Kids who raised animals and baked and built things all year came out in 100 degrees and smoke, and they shined."

Highlights:

- **Record entries.** 2,140 entries, up 9 percent from 2025, including new youth drone photography and FFA small engines classes.
- **Youth livestock sale.** 131 animals sold at the sale run by the Youth Livestock Sale Committee.
- **Volunteers.** About 340 volunteers gave more than 6,000 hours.
- **Keeping animals safe.** Event Center staff ran barn fans and misters around the clock on Sunday, and the barns passed the state veterinarian's walk-through.

The fair is run by Kittitas County through the Kittitas Valley Event Center with the advice of the Fair Advisory Committee. Ribbons and rosettes were awarded at the fair; exhibitors can find premium payment information in the premium book.

Mark your calendars: the 2027 Kittitas County Fair runs Labor Day weekend, September 2-6, 2027.
