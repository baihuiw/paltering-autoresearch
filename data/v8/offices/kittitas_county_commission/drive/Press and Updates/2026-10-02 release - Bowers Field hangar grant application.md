## County seeks state aid for new hangars at Bowers Field

ELLENSBURG - Kittitas County has applied for a $750,000 state airport aid grant to build a 10-unit T-hangar building at Bowers Field, the county-owned airport north of Ellensburg.

All 24 county-owned T-hangars at Bowers Field are leased, and pilots can wait two years or more for one to open up. The new building would sit on the north apron, near the existing Rows H and J.

"Bowers Field is one of the county's best economic assets, and right now we're turning people away," said Chair Renee Gustafsen. "More hangars means more based aircraft, more fuel sales and more people using our airport. I'm proud the county is going after this money."

The total cost is estimated at $1.42 million. The rest would come from the airport fund, which is supported by hangar rents, fuel sales and ground leases rather than property taxes. If the grant is awarded in early 2027, design would follow in 2027 and construction in 2028.

The county's hangars are leased month to month and offered to pilots in order from the airport's waiting list. Pilots interested in a hangar can join the list at no cost by contacting the Airport Manager's office at Bowers Field.

Bowers Field supports wildland firefighting aircraft each summer, flight training, agricultural aviation and medical flights, and hosts the annual fly-in breakfast each June.
