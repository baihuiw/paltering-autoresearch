# Notice: Permit Portal Temporarily Offline

*Kittitas County Community Development Services and Information Technology. Posted October 1, 2026 on co.kittitas.wa.us and at the CDS counters.*

The online Permit Portal will be offline from Thursday, October 1 through Wednesday, October 7, 2026, while we install additional security protections and reset sign-in credentials. We expect it back on Thursday, October 8.

## What to do in the meantime

- **New applications.** Bring paper plans to a CDS counter (Ellensburg, 411 N. Ruby St., Suite 2; upper county counter at the Cle Elum annex, Tuesdays and Thursdays 8:30-4:00) or email them to the CDS permit desk. Applications received this week will be entered with their original dates.
- **Applications already in review.** Plan reviewers will contact you directly if they need anything. Review times are not affected by the outage.
- **Inspections.** Call the inspection line before 3:00 p.m. the day before. Inspections continue as scheduled.
- **Fee payments.** Pay at the counter by check, cash or card. The portal does not store payment card numbers; online payments go through the county's separate payment processor.

## When the portal returns

Everyone with a portal account will need to choose a new password the first time they sign in. County staff will sign in with an additional verification step.

Questions: CDS, (509) 962-7506, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
