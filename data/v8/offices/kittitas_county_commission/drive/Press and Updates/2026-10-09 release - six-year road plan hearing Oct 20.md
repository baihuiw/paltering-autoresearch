## Tell the county which roads matter most: six-year road plan hearing is October 20

ELLENSBURG - The Kittitas County Board of Commissioners will hold a public hearing on the county's 2027-2032 Six-Year Transportation Improvement Program on **Tuesday, October 20, at 9:30 a.m.** in the Commissioners' Auditorium at the Kittitas County Courthouse, 205 W. 5th Ave., Ellensburg. Residents may also join online or by phone; connection details are on the meeting notice on the county website.

The six-year plan is the county's list of road, bridge and safety projects for the next six years. State law requires every county to update it each year after a public hearing. The first three years are the county's working program; the last three are planning years, and projects move up as money to build them is found, usually through state and federal grants.

"This is the most direct way residents can shape where road dollars go," said Chair Renee Gustafsen. "If there's a road in your neighborhood that needs work, tell us, and tell us why. Traffic, school buses, dust, farm equipment, safety: that's what helps Public Works and the board set priorities."

### What's in the draft

The draft plan lists 24 projects totaling about $23.2 million in 2026 dollars. Highlights include:

- **Manastash Road guardrail** (MP 3.2-3.6): awarded October 6; installation expected before November 15.
- **Thorp Highway safety improvements** near Clerf Road: a speed study the week of November 2, with signs or beacons to follow based on the results.
- **Smithson Road chip seal**: moved into the 2027 program after an aggregate shortage this summer.
- **Number 81 Road overlay** from the canal crossing to Vantage Highway, pending a state grant.
- **Vantage Highway pullouts**: six new paved pullouts between MP 8 and 22 (2028).
- **Umptanum Road culvert** replacement with a fish-passable structure (2028).
- **Parke Creek Road bridge** replacement (2029-30).
- **Hayward Road gravel section**: new to this year's draft, converting about 2.1 miles of gravel to a chip-sealed surface with dust control (2030; grant application planned for 2027).
- **Cove Road paving** (2029) and annual chip seal, sign and ADA ramp programs.

The full draft list is posted on the Public Works page of the county website and is available at the Public Works office and the Board office.

### How to comment

- **In person or online at the hearing** on October 20 at 9:30 a.m. Speakers usually have three minutes.
- **In writing** to Kittitas County Public Works, 411 N. Ruby St., Suite 1, Ellensburg, WA 98926, or by email to Public Works, **by noon on Monday, October 19**. Written comments are given to all three commissioners and become part of the hearing record.
- **Getting a road added.** Residents who want a road considered that isn't on the list should say so at the hearing or in writing and describe the problem. Public Works ranks projects each year using traffic counts, pavement condition, crash history, school bus routes and resident input. A road that doesn't make this year's list can be added in a later year.

The board expects to adopt the final plan at a regular meeting in November.
