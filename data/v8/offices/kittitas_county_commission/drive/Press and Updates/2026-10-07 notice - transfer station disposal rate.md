# PUBLIC NOTICE - Kittitas County Solid Waste

**Posted October 7, 2026 at both transfer stations, on the county website and in the Daily Record legal notices**

## Garbage disposal rate adjustment at county transfer stations

Effective **Monday, October 12, 2026**, the disposal rate for garbage (municipal solid waste) at the Ellensburg and Cle Elum transfer stations will be **$120.00 per ton**, and the minimum charge will be **$15.00** per visit. The Board of County Commissioners adopted the adjustment by Resolution 2026-118 on October 6.

**Why the change?** Kittitas County does not have its own landfill. All garbage brought to the transfer stations is hauled by contract to a regional landfill. The adjustment reflects higher regional hauling and disposal costs that took effect on October 1. The Solid Waste program is paid for by the people who use it, not by property taxes, and garbage rates had not changed since January 2023.

**What isn't changing:** rates for clean yard waste, appliances and tires; free scrap metal and recycling drop-off; and free household hazardous waste collection for residents on the first Saturday of each month in Ellensburg.

"No one likes paying more at the scale house," said Chair Renee Gustafsen. "We held these rates for nearly four years, and we'll be doing a full review of the system next year to keep costs as low as we can. Sorting out yard waste and metal is still the best way to save."
