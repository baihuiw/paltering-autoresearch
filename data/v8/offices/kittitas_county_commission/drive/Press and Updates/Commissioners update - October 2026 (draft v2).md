# Commissioners' Update - October 2026

*Draft v2 for the county website and email list. KM 10/9 - Renee to review.*

## From the chair

It's been a busy first month. I've sat down with every department head, toured Bowers Field and the transfer stations, and started the 2027 budget hearings. Thank you to everyone who has called, written or stopped me at the feed store. Keep it coming.

-- Renee Gustafsen, Chair

## 2027 budget

Department budget hearings ran October 13-15, and the elected offices come in October 26-28. The preliminary budget will be public on November 2, and the public hearing is Monday, December 7 at 10:00 a.m. Our direction to departments: hold the line in the general fund, and make sure roads, solid waste, the Event Center and the airport pay their own way.

## Roads

- Manastash Road guardrail replacement awarded October 6, under the engineer's estimate.
- Winter sand and salt are stocked.
- The six-year transportation plan hearing is October 20 at 9:30 a.m. This is your chance to tell us which roads matter most.

## Bowers Field

The county has applied for a $750,000 state grant toward a 10-unit hangar building. All 24 county hangars are leased and the waiting list is long; new hangars mean more based aircraft and more fuel sales for the airport fund.

## Fair

A record 2,140 entries this year from 612 exhibitors. Thank you to the families, 4-H leaders, FFA advisors and volunteers who made it through the smoke and the heat. The 2027 fair is September 2-6.

## Wildfire readiness

Free chipping days: October 17 (Easton and Ronald), October 24 (Cle Elum and Roslyn area), October 31 (Thorp and Manastash). Firewise neighborhood grant applications are due December 1.

## Noxious weeds

Our weed crews have treated more than 1,900 lane-miles of county road right-of-way this season, a record. If you have knapweed or whitetop on your own land, the weed board's cost-share program can help: a county crew treats it and you pay half the product cost. Call (509) 962-7006.

## Transfer stations

The garbage rate at both transfer stations is now $120 per ton, with a $15 minimum, reflecting higher regional hauling and disposal costs. Yard waste, appliance and tire rates did not change, and household hazardous waste drop-off is still free on the first Saturday of the month in Ellensburg.

## Meet with us

Town hall: Thursday, October 22, 6:30 p.m., Thorp Grange. Regular board meetings are the first and third Tuesdays at 9:00 a.m. in the Commissioners' Auditorium and online.
