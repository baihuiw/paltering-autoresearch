# NEWS RELEASE - Kittitas County

**For immediate release: Thursday, October 8, 2026**
**Contact: Kittitas Valley Event Center office, (509) 962-7639**

## Event Center 2027 rental rates set; summer dates booking now

ELLENSBURG - The Kittitas County Board of Commissioners has adopted the 2027 rental rate schedule for the Kittitas Valley Event Center, and the Event Center office is now taking bookings for all of 2027.

The new rates, adopted October 6 by Resolution 2026-116, take effect for events on or after January 1, 2027. They raise building, arena, barn, RV and equipment rates by an average of about 4 percent, the first change since January 2024. The reservation deposit, cleaning and damage deposits, the 20 percent weekday discount for nonprofits and youth groups, and the multi-day discount are unchanged.

"The Event Center is one of the county's real gathering places, from horse shows and quilt shows to weddings and family reunions," said Chair Renee Gustafsen, the board's liaison to the Event Center. "The rental income pays to keep the buildings and grounds in good repair, so families and groups can keep using them. Even with the new schedule, our rates are at or below most fairgrounds in central Washington."

| Indoor arena | $710 |
| Outdoor arena | $470 |
| RV site with power and water (per night) | $40 |

The complete rate sheet, rental requirements and a how-to-book guide are on the Event Center web page and at the Event Center office at the main gate.

### Book early for summer

Event Center Manager Megan Thiessen said summer Saturdays fill quickly. "We already have holds on most June and July Saturdays for the Picnic Grove and Exhibit Hall A," she said. "If you're planning a reunion, wedding or company picnic for next summer, call us now. We'll hold a date for a week while you decide."

Returning annual renters had first choice of their usual dates through September 30; all other 2027 dates opened to the public October 1. The grounds are closed to outside rentals from August 23 through September 12, 2027 for the county fair (September 2-6) and setup and teardown.

### How to book

Call or email Booking Coordinator Jolene Sauter at (509) 962-7639 or jolene.sauter@co.kittitas.wa.us, Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Tours are available on weekdays. A 25 percent deposit and a signed contract confirm the booking; the balance is due 30 days before the event.

### Improvements

The Event Center will repair the Pavilion's north roof this month, before the November craft show and holiday bazaar. Other 2027 capital requests, including ventilation in the swine barn, are under review in the county's budget process.
