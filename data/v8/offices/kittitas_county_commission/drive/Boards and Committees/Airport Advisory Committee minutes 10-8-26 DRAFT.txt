BOWERS FIELD AIRPORT ADVISORY COMMITTEE
Minutes - DRAFT
Thursday, October 8, 2026, 5:30 p.m.
Airport terminal conference room, Bowers Field

Members present: Hal Brekke (chair), Diane Sorrell, Jeff Amundson, Pat Nyberg. Absent: one vacancy.
Staff: Rick Tolliver, Airport Manager; Neil Ambrose, Public Works Director.
Board liaison: Commissioner Doug Rasch.
Public: 5.

1. Minutes of August 13 approved as written.

2. Manager's report (Tolliver)
- Fuel sales, July-September: 100LL 14,820 gallons (up 6 percent); Jet A 22,410 gallons (down 3 percent, fewer fire tanker days than 2025).
- Fuel farm meter calibration completed 9/25.
- J-7 hangar door came off the track in the 9/25 wind; rehung; quotes being sought for new rollers on the J row.
- Hangars: all 24 county T-hangars leased. H-12 turned over 10/1. Waiting list stands at 17 (list attached to the packet).
- Wildlife: two coyote sightings on the east side; fence crew checked the perimeter fence.
- Runway 11/29 crack seal scheduled for the week of Oct 19, weather permitting. NOTAM will be issued.

3. New T-hangar building (Ambrose)
The county applied on Sept 25 for a state airport aid grant toward a 10-unit T-hangar building on the north apron. Total estimated cost $1.42 million; grant request $750,000; the balance from the airport fund and a possible interfund loan. Award decisions expected in early 2027. If funded, design in 2027 and construction in 2028. Mr. Amundson said the 10 units would cut the waiting list substantially. Commissioner Rasch said the board supports the project.

4. Airport layout plan update
Consultant scoping meeting held Sept 17. The update is required before the hangar building can be sited. Draft scope to the committee in November.

5. Through-the-fence request
A property owner on Bowers Road has asked about taxiway access from a private lot. Staff explained that the county's grant obligations generally prohibit through-the-fence access. Committee recommended no further action.

6. Public comment
Ms. Pell asked when the waiting list would be updated online. Staff said the list in the packet is current and will be posted after the meeting.

7. Next meeting: Thursday, November 12, 5:30 p.m.

Adjourned 6:48 p.m.
Recorded by R. Tolliver
