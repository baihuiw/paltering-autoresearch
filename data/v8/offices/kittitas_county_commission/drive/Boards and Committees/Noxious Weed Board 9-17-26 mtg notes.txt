KITTITAS COUNTY NOXIOUS WEED CONTROL BOARD
Regular meeting - notes
Thursday, September 17, 2026, 7:00 p.m.
Extension office meeting room, Fairgrounds

Board members present: 4 of 5 (one district vacant). Coordinator Luis Arreguin. Commissioner liaison: Chair Gustafsen (attended her first meeting; introduced herself as "a hay farmer who's been pulling whitetop for thirty years").

1. Season report (Arreguin)
- County road right-of-way: about 1,700 lane-miles treated so far this season, on pace for a record year.
- Cost-share program: 38 sites so far. The program is open to any landowner: the county crew treats, the owner pays 50 percent of the product cost, and the county covers crew time. Applications through the website or the office.
- County-forced control: 5 sites this season, all billed to owners at the published rate (crew $68.00/hour plus product and a $36.00 truck charge). Two liens filed for unpaid bills from 2025.
- Biocontrol: knapweed root weevils released at 6 sites in the Teanaway.

2. Fall notices
About 15 notices will go out in September and early October, mainly for diffuse knapweed and whitetop. Standard deadline 30 days. The board reaffirmed that the notice letter should go out with the cost-share flyer so owners know there's help.

3. Policy reminder
Under state law and the board's policy, control of noxious weeds on private land is the owner's responsibility. County crews work on private land only (a) through the cost-share program, (b) as county-forced control after a notice deadline, billed to the owner, or (c) as a free demonstration plot approved by the board in advance. Mr. Lundgren asked that this be included in the new member packet.

4. 2027 budget
The board's 2027 request holds the per-parcel weed assessment level. Request goes to the commissioners in the October budget hearings.

5. Public comment
A Thorp resident asked about puncturevine on the Thorp Highway shoulder; added to the ROW list.

Next meeting: November 19, 2026.
