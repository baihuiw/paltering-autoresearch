KITTITAS COUNTY LODGING TAX ADVISORY COMMITTEE (LTAC)
MINUTES
Thursday, September 24, 2026, 2:00 p.m.
Commissioners' conference room, Kittitas County Courthouse, and online

MEMBERS PRESENT: Marcy Odegaard (lodging business), and four other members (two lodging businesses, two tourism-funded activity representatives). One position vacant.
BOARD LIAISON: Commissioner Doug Rasch (chair of the committee under state law).
STAFF: Lisa Brannigan, Clerk of the Board.
PUBLIC: 11 (applicants).

1. CALL TO ORDER. Commissioner Rasch opened the meeting at 2:00 p.m. and reviewed the committee's role: under RCW 67.28.1817 the county must submit proposed lodging tax uses to the committee for review and comment before the board acts. The committee's recommendation is advisory.

2. MINUTES of May 21, 2026 approved.

3. FUND STATUS (Brannigan, from finance)
- 2026 collections through August: $612,400 (2025 same period: $588,900, up 4.0 percent). Short-term rentals now account for about 38 percent of collections.
- Estimated amount available for 2027 awards: $540,000 after administration and the county's existing tourism facility commitments.
- 2025 award recipients have all filed their post-event reports. Two reports were incomplete (missing overnight stay estimates); staff followed up and both are now complete.

4. 2027 APPLICATIONS
Fourteen applications requesting $630,000. Each applicant had five minutes to present, followed by questions. Members scored applications individually before the meeting using the scoring sheet (visitor impact, overnight stays, readiness, partnership and leverage, and past performance).

Highlights of discussion:
- Visitor center operations (chamber of commerce): members praised the new website and mobile visitor guide. Recommended $175,000 of $185,000.
- Upper county visitor guide and winter marketing: recommended $85,000 of $92,000. Members asked that the winter campaign promote weekday stays, when lodging is not full.
- Event Center event marketing: Manager Megan Thiessen presented. The request supports marketing for horse shows and dog shows that bring multi-day overnight stays. Recommended $55,000 of $60,000.
- Gravel cycling event: members liked the concept but noted the route uses county gravel roads and needs a Public Works road-use permit. Recommended $12,000 of $24,000, with the balance available mid-year if the permit is issued.
- Winter western heritage weekend: new event. Recommended $28,000 under the committee's first-year cap.
- Trail maps and signage: capital portion reduced; recommended $30,000 of $38,000.
- Bowers Field fly-in day: moved from 2026 to May 2027. Recommended $6,000 of $8,000.
- Visitor wayfinding signs at I-90 exits (Board office request): not recommended this year. State approval of sign locations is required first. Members encouraged a resubmittal.

Scoring notes:
- Average scores ranged from 61 to 88. Members agreed that any application scoring below 65 would not be recommended this year.
- Two members recused from scoring applications from organizations they serve on; their scores were not counted for those applications and the average was taken of the remaining three.
- Members asked applicants to report actual overnight stays where possible (hotel block pickup, campground counts), not only estimates.

5. ACTION
Motion (Odegaard, second by a tourism member) to recommend the 2027 awards as shown on the summary table, totaling $533,000, leaving $7,000 for mid-year requests. Carried unanimously.

6. OTHER BUSINESS
- Ms. Odegaard's term expires December 31; she has agreed to serve again. Staff will place the reappointment on the board's October 6 consent agenda.
- The vacant tourism-funded activity seat will be advertised with the board's fall advisory board openings.
- Members asked staff to simplify the post-event report form for 2027 and to send reminders 30 days after each event.

7. PUBLIC COMMENT
An applicant asked whether awards could be paid in advance. Staff explained that lodging tax awards are paid as reimbursement after the event or project, with supporting invoices, under the county's agreement form.

8. ADJOURNED 4:12 p.m.

Next meeting: to be scheduled in spring 2027 for mid-year requests.

Recorded by L. Brannigan
Recommendations transmitted to the Board for consideration with the 2027 budget (Lodging Tax fund 110).
