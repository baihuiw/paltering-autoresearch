## Process reminders (for the Chair)

- Applications go to Lisa, who sends them to the commissioners and the committee's staff.
- For the Planning Commission, commissioners usually interview finalists in open session at a study session. The Chair should not discuss pending land use matters with applicants.
- New members receive the open public meetings and public records training within 90 days (state law). The Prosecutor's office session on October 9 counts.
- Appointment letters are signed by the Chair (log number OL-26-xxxx).
- Committees must post agendas at least 24 hours in advance of regular meetings; the Clerk posts them on the county website.
