## Things to know

- Two commissioners are a quorum. No board business between commissioners outside a noticed meeting, including email and text. Social events are fine; don't talk shop.
- The board sets policy; departments make day-to-day decisions. Requests from residents go to the department head with a copy to Tracy.
- Land use matters that might come to the board on appeal: don't discuss with applicants or neighbors (appearance of fairness).
- Election year: county resources (staff time, email lists, the website) can't be used for any campaign, including the vice chair's. Ask Tracy if unsure.
- Seat is on the November 2027 ballot for the rest of the term (to Dec 31, 2028).
