## Board of Commissioners meeting notes

- **Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (Election Day):** the regular meeting is held as scheduled at 9:00 a.m.
- **Tuesday, December 1 and 15, 2026:** regular meetings. The 2027 budget hearing is a separate special meeting on **Monday, December 7 at 10:00 a.m.**
- **Tuesday, January 5, 2027:** first meeting of the year; annual reorganization (chair and vice chair for 2027) and liaison assignments.

## Office hours reminder

Board office: Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m., open through the lunch hour. Letters can be left in the drop slot by the Suite 108 door at any time the Courthouse is open.

Courthouse building hours: 7:30 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Individual offices set their own counter hours within those times.

## Holiday-week reminders for the Board office

- Letters and contact-form messages that arrive during a holiday week are answered within five working days of the office reopening.
- The signature folder is worked on the last business day before a holiday and the first business day after.
- Legal notices for hearings must allow for the newspaper's holiday deadlines (usually one day earlier than normal). Lisa keeps the deadline list.
- Public meeting notices are still posted on the website and the Courthouse board during closures.

## Early closures

The county may close offices early for severe weather or smoke. Closures are announced on the county website and Facebook page and sent to local radio by 6:30 a.m. when possible. Employees should check with their supervisor.
