## Reminders

- If it's a threat, a claim or a reporter: Tracy, right away. Don't reply.
- If someone asks a commissioner to "help with" a department decision: get the details and permission, send to the department head with a copy to Tracy, and tell the person who will follow up. Same process for everyone.
- Answer within five working days.
