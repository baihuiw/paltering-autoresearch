# Board of Commissioners Office - Procedures

Mail, requests and routing. Revised September 2026 (T. Engebretson) for the new chair's staff.

## 1. What the board does and doesn't do

The Board of County Commissioners sets policy, adopts the budget, approves contracts and fee schedules, and hears appeals where the code says so. **Day-to-day decisions belong to the departments**: building permits (CDS), road work orders (Public Works), hangar leases and tiedowns (Airport Manager), transfer station accounts (Solid Waste), event bookings (Event Center), weed notices (Noxious Weed Control Board staff). The board office passes residents' questions and requests to the right department; it doesn't decide individual cases.

The Sheriff, Prosecutor, Auditor, Treasurer, Assessor, Clerk and the courts are independently elected or judicial. The board funds them but doesn't direct them. Questions about the jail, emergency alerts and evacuations, property taxes, elections, or court cases go to those offices.

## 2. Mail and the contact form

- Everything addressed to a commissioner gets a reply within five working days, from the commissioner's account or by letter.
- Number outgoing letters OL-26-#### (Kelli keeps the log).
- If a message asks about a department matter, check the drive and the department's latest report or memo before replying, and copy the department head on anything you promise.
- Threats, anything saying someone is suing or filing a claim against the county, and anything from a reporter go to Tracy right away. Don't reply.
- Public records requests go to the county's public records officer under the county's PRA policy; log the date received.

## 3. Requests for help

Residents often ask a commissioner to "help with" a permit, a notice or a bill. The office:
1. Gets the details and the resident's permission to share them.
2. Sends it to the department head with a copy to Tracy.
3. Tells the resident who will follow up and when.
Commissioners and staff don't ask departments to decide an individual case a particular way. Everyone gets the same process.

## 4. Signatures and deadlines

- The signature folder is worked every Tuesday and Friday. Anything with a legal deadline is flagged in red with the date on the cover sheet.
- Contract notices (renewals, extensions, terminations) are entered on the contract calendar by the Clerk of the Board when the contract is signed. (Second check added 10/2026: Tracy reviews the contract calendar every Monday.)

## 5. Meetings

Regular meetings are the first and third Tuesdays at 9:00 a.m. Study sessions with departments are scheduled most Mondays. Two commissioners are a quorum: commissioners don't discuss board business with each other outside a noticed meeting, including by email or text.
