# Kittitas Valley Event Center - 2027 Facility Rental Rates

**Adopted by the Board of County Commissioners October 6, 2026 (Resolution 2026-116). Effective for all event dates on or after January 1, 2027.**

Questions and bookings: Event Center office (Fair Office), (509) 962-7639, Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Booking coordinator: Jolene Sauter, jolene.sauter@co.kittitas.wa.us.

The 2027 schedule averages about 4 percent above 2026, the first change since January 2024. Dry camping, deposits and the nonprofit discount did not change. Bookings made now for 2027 dates are billed at 2027 rates.

## Deposits and discounts (unchanged)

- Reservation deposit: 25 percent of rental, due when the contract is signed. The balance is due 30 days before the event.
- Cleaning and damage deposit: $300 for events under 300 people; $500 for 300 or more or any event with alcohol. Refunded within 21 days after inspection.
- Nonprofit and youth organizations: 20 percent off building rent, Monday-Thursday only.
- Multi-day events of 4 or more consecutive days: 10 percent off building rent.

## Requirements

- Certificate of liability insurance, $1 million per occurrence, naming Kittitas County as additional insured. Renters without their own coverage may buy single-event coverage through the county's tenant user program; the office can send the link.
- Events serving alcohol need a state banquet or special occasion permit and a licensed server; no keg service in the Picnic Grove.
- Cancellation more than 90 days out: deposit refunded less a $50 fee. Within 90 days: deposit kept.

## Dates not available

The grounds are closed to outside rentals from August 23 through September 12, 2027 for fair setup, the fair (September 2-6, 2027) and teardown. The Pavilion is not available December 20-January 3.

## Family reunions and picnics

The Picnic Grove is the most popular choice for groups of 75-150 in summer. Exhibit Hall A works well for groups up to about 200 who want indoor space and a kitchen. Many reunions book the Grove and Hall A together for the same day ($790 in 2027) so there is a backup if it rains or gets hot. Summer Saturdays book early; June and July 2027 dates are already holding.
