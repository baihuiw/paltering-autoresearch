KITTITAS COUNTY FAIR 2026
Entries and Attendance Summary
Prepared by the Fair Office for the Fair Advisory Committee - September 21, 2026

FAIR DATES: Thursday, September 3 - Monday, September 7, 2026

ENTRIES (all departments)
Total entries: 2,140 (2025: 1,968; 2024: 1,905) - the most on record
Exhibitors: 612 (2025: 571)
  4-H: 238 members from 19 clubs
  FFA: 96 members from 5 chapters
  Youth open class: 104
  Adult open class: 174

Largest departments: Still exhibits - home arts and baking (412); Rabbits and poultry (301); Horticulture (268); Photography (207); Livestock - sheep, swine, beef, goats (244 head).

New this year: Drone photography class (youth), 14 entries; Small engines (FFA), 9 entries.

ATTENDANCE
Paid admissions: 38,210 (2025: 47,900)
  Thursday 6,140; Friday 8,820; Saturday 7,050; Sunday 6,980; Monday 9,220
Saturday: heavy smoke from the Liberty fire, air quality "unhealthy" from about noon; grandstand show went on.
Sunday: high of 100 degrees; barn fans and misters ran around the clock; two heat-related first aid calls, no transports.
Season passes sold: 1,180

YOUTH LIVESTOCK SALE (run by the Youth Livestock Sale Committee, not the fair)
Sale held Sunday, September 6: 131 animals sold; committee reports total sale proceeds of $318,600. Buyer checks distributed by the committee September 25.

VOLUNTEERS
About 340 volunteers logged 6,100 hours.

SAFETY
No significant incidents. One lost child reunited within 10 minutes. Livestock barns passed the state veterinarian's walk-through.

THANK YOU to our superintendents, 4-H leaders, FFA advisors, sponsors and the Event Center crew.
