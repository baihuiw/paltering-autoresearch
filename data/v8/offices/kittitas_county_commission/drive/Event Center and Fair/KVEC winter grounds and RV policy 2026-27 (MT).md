# Kittitas Valley Event Center - Winter Grounds and RV Policy, 2026-27

*M. Thiessen, Event Center and Fair Manager. Effective November 1, 2026 through March 31, 2027. For staff, renters and the Event Center web page.*

The Event Center stays open all winter for indoor events, horse and dog shows, auctions and meetings. Winter brings frozen water lines, snow on the lots and short days, so a few things change between November 1 and March 31.

## 1. RV sites

- **Power-and-water sites:** water is shut off to the RV loop from November 1 to March 31 to protect the lines. Sites stay available with **power only** at the regular nightly rate for 2026 ($38) and 2027 ($40). A frost-free hydrant at the north end of the loop is available for filling tanks.
- **Dump station:** closed November 1 - March 31. The nearest public dump stations are posted at the Fair Office.
- **Event-related camping only.** In winter, RV camping is limited to people attending or working an event on the grounds, for the nights of the event plus one night before and after. No long-term or monthly RV stays.
- **Snow:** RV sites are plowed after the main drive, parking lots and barn aisles. Renters should be ready to move for plowing when asked.

## 2. Barns and stalls

- Barns 1 and 2 have heated tack rooms; Barns 3 and 4 do not. Stall water is from the frost-free hydrants at the barn ends; hoses must be drained and stored after each use.
- No heaters of any kind in stalls. Heated water buckets are allowed only on the outlets marked for them in Barns 1 and 2.
- Stall cleanout: renters strip stalls to the mats before departure; the $25 per-stall cleaning charge applies if stalls are left bedded.

## 3. Buildings

- Thermostats in the Exhibit Halls and the meeting room are set to 55 degrees when the buildings are not rented, and brought up to 68 degrees two hours before a rental starts. Renters may not adjust thermostats above 70.
- Exterior doors must be closed during events; propped doors freeze the sprinkler riser rooms.
- **Pavilion:** the north half may be in use for the youth hockey ice sheet from early December through late February if the board approves the agreement on October 20. The south half stays available for rentals. North-side doors will be locked during the ice season.

## 4. Parking lots and walkways

- The main lot, Exhibit Hall A lot and accessible parking are plowed and sanded first, by 7:00 a.m. on event days when possible. The gravel east lot is plowed but not sanded.
- Renters with events before 8:00 a.m. after a snowfall should call the grounds on-call number the evening before so a crew can start early.
- Ice melt at building entrances is applied by staff. Renters may not use rock salt on concrete walkways (it damages the concrete).

## 5. Weather cancellations

- If the county closes offices for severe weather, the Event Center office is closed, but booked events may go on if the renter chooses and the grounds can be cleared safely.
- If the Manager closes the grounds for safety (ice storm, roof snow load, power outage), the renter may reschedule within 12 months at no charge, or receive a full refund of rent and deposits.
- Renters who cancel because of weather within 7 days of the event, while the grounds are open, may reschedule once within 6 months; the deposit transfers.

## 6. Roof snow load

The Pavilion and the barns have roof snow load limits. When roof snow depth reaches the trigger on the posted chart, staff will close the affected building until the roof is cleared. The Pavilion north-end repair (work order 26-0917) is scheduled to be done before winter.
