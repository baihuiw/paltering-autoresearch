# Renting the Kittitas Valley Event Center

*Handout for the front counter and the web page. Revised September 2026 (J. Sauter). Rates are on the separate 2027 rate sheet.*

The Kittitas Valley Event Center is the county fairgrounds in Ellensburg. Outside the fair season it hosts horse shows, dog shows, craft and quilt shows, auctions, weddings, family reunions, school events and company picnics. About 160 rental events used the grounds in 2025.

## How to book

1. **Check the date.** Call or email Jolene with the date, the number of guests and what you have in mind. She will tell you what is open and pencil in a 7-day hold.
2. **Tour (optional).** Tours are available weekdays.
3. **Sign the rental contract** and pay the 25 percent reservation deposit to confirm. The contract lists the spaces, hours, add-ons and the cleaning and damage deposit.
4. **Send the insurance certificate** at least 30 days before the event. If you do not have a policy, we can send the link for single-event coverage.
5. **Pay the balance** 30 days before the event.
6. **Walk-through** at the end of the event with the grounds lead. Deposits are refunded within 21 days after inspection.

## Good to know

- **Groups of 75-150.** The covered Picnic Grove shelter seats about 150 at the picnic tables and has power and a nearby restroom building. Exhibit Hall A seats 220 at tables and has a warming kitchen; the commercial kitchen is an add-on.
- **Parking** is free and included with every rental. The east lot is gravel; ADA parking is paved at Exhibit Hall A and the Pavilion.
- **Food.** You may bring your own food or caterer. Caterers must have a food establishment permit from Public Health. Barbecues are allowed in the Picnic Grove only, at least 10 feet from the shelter, and not during burn bans.
- **Alcohol** requires a state permit and a licensed server; see the rate sheet.
- **Decorations.** No nails, staples or tape on walls; use the hanging rails. No confetti, glitter or open flames indoors.
- **Pets** are allowed on the grounds on leash, but not in Exhibit Hall A or B unless the event is an animal event.
- **Noise.** Amplified music ends at 11:00 p.m. Friday and Saturday and 10:00 p.m. other nights. Neighbors are close on the north and west sides.
- **Accessibility.** All buildings except the upper grandstand are accessible. Tell us in advance if you need an accessible restroom trailer at the Grove.

## Nonprofits, schools and youth groups

Nonprofits and youth organizations get 20 percent off building rent Monday through Thursday. County departments and public agencies with an interlocal agreement may qualify for a fee waiver; ask the Manager.

## Common questions

- **Can we get in early to decorate?** Yes. Move-in and move-out days are half the daily rate when booked with the event. Same-day early access before 7:00 a.m. is staff labor at the after-hours rate.
- **Is there Wi-Fi?** Guest Wi-Fi in the Pavilion, Exhibit Hall A and the office building at the main gate; not in the barns or the Picnic Grove.
- **Can we have a bounce house?** Yes, with the vendor's insurance certificate. Blowers must be on a dedicated circuit; ask staff where.
- **Do you have tables and chairs at the Grove?** The 14 picnic tables stay at the Grove. Folding tables and chairs can be added from the rental list.
- **What if it rains?** Many summer renters book Exhibit Hall A with the Grove for a backup. Same-day indoor moves are possible only if a hall is open that day.
