# 2026 Kittitas County Fair - Premium Book

**September 3-7, 2026 - Kittitas County Fairgrounds, Ellensburg**

*Excerpt: General Rules and Regulations for All Exhibitors (pages 4-6). Department rules follow in each section of the full book.*

## Entries

1. Exhibitors in the open class must be residents of Kittitas County. 4-H and FFA exhibitors must be enrolled members in good standing in a Kittitas County club or chapter.
2. Entries close August 14, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. Online entry is open through the fair website; paper entry forms are accepted at the Fair Office.
3. There is no entry fee for 4-H, FFA or youth open class. Adult open class entries are $1.00 each.
4. Each exhibitor may make only one entry per lot.

## Judging and awards

5. Danish system: each entry is judged against a standard and receives a blue, red or white award. Champion and reserve champion rosettes are awarded where listed.
6. The judge's decision is final.
7. Premiums are paid per placing as listed in each department (for example, youth still exhibits: blue $4.00, red $3.00, white $2.00; youth livestock showmanship: blue $10.00, red $7.00, white $5.00).

## Premium payments

8. Premium checks will be mailed by October 15, 2026 to the address on the entry form. Please keep your address current with the Fair Office.
9. Checks not cashed within 90 days are void and will not be reissued.
10. Premiums are paid from fair funds. If funds are not sufficient to pay all premiums in full, premiums may be prorated.
11. Exhibitors are responsible for reporting premium income as required by law.

## Release of exhibits

12. Still exhibits are released Monday, September 7, from 6:00 to 9:00 p.m. only. Exhibits not picked up become the property of the fair.
13. Livestock may not be released before 6:00 p.m. Monday, September 7, except by permission of the barn superintendent.

## Youth livestock sale

14. The youth market livestock sale is conducted by the Kittitas County Youth Livestock Sale Committee, a separate nonprofit organization. Sale proceeds are paid by the committee, not by the fair.

## Conduct

15. Exhibitors, parents and leaders are expected to treat other exhibitors, judges, volunteers and staff with respect.

Questions: Fair Office, Kittitas Valley Event Center, (509) 962-7639.
