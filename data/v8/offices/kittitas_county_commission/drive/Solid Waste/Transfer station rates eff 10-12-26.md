# Kittitas County Transfer Stations - Rates and Hours

**Effective Monday, October 12, 2026 (Resolution 2026-118)**

Ellensburg Transfer Station and Cle Elum Transfer Station

## Garbage (municipal solid waste)

| | Rate |
|---|---|
| Per ton | $120.00 |
| Minimum charge (loads up to 250 lb) | $15.00 |
| Uncovered or unsecured load fee | $10.00 |

Loads are weighed in and out. A typical pickup load of household garbage weighs 300 to 700 pounds.

## Other materials (unchanged)

| Material | Rate |
|---|---|
| Clean yard waste (no bags, rocks or dirt) | $45.00 per ton, $8.00 minimum |
| Appliances | $20.00 each; $35.00 with refrigerant |
| Passenger tires | $5.00 each off rim; $8.00 on rim |
| Scrap metal (clean) | Free |
| Household hazardous waste (residents) | Free, first Saturday of each month, Ellensburg only |
| Cardboard, mixed paper, aluminum cans | Free in the recycling bins |

## Hours

| Site | Summer (through Oct 31) | Winter (Nov 1 - Mar 31) |
|---|---|---|
| Ellensburg | Mon-Sat 8:00-4:30 | Mon-Sat 8:00-4:00 |
| Cle Elum | Wed-Sat 8:00-4:00 | Wed-Sat 9:00-3:00 |

Closed Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

## Payment

Cash, check, debit and credit cards accepted. No card surcharge. Commercial accounts billed monthly.

## Tips to save money

- Separate clean yard waste and scrap metal; they cost less or nothing.
- Combine trips. The minimum charge applies to each visit.
- Cover and secure your load to avoid the unsecured load fee.
