KITTITAS COUNTY SOLID WASTE ADVISORY COMMITTEE (SWAC)
Meeting notes - DRAFT for approval at the next meeting
Thursday, September 10, 2026, 3:00 p.m.
Public Works conference room, Ellensburg

Members present: 7 of 9 (Ellensburg, Kittitas, Roslyn and South Cle Elum representatives; agriculture; commercial hauler; public member). Absent: recycling industry representative. Vacant: Cle Elum representative.
Staff: Paula Strassburg, Solid Waste Director; Amber Petrakis, Recycling and HHW Coordinator.
Board liaison: none present (District 2 vacancy; the liaison assignment will be made by the Board after the appointment).
Public: 2.

1. NOTES OF JUNE 11 approved as written.

2. CLE ELUM REPRESENTATIVE
The Cle Elum seat has been vacant since the June resignation. The Board office has one application, from a Cle Elum resident who worked 20 years for a regional hauler. The committee recommends the Board appoint (expected on an October consent agenda).

3. RECYCLING
Petrakis reported first-half 2026 recycling at the drop-off bins: cardboard 323 tons, mixed paper 102 tons, aluminum and tin 17 tons, scrap metal 397 tons. Cardboard markets recovered in the spring; the county received a small rebate on cardboard in May and June after paying to move it most of 2025.
- Contamination in the mixed paper bins at Cle Elum is still high (plastic bags, food). New signs went up in August. Staff will spot-check in October.
- Glass: there is still no glass recycling. The nearest market is on the west side and the hauling cost per load is more than the material is worth. A member asked if crushed glass could be used as road base or pipe bedding. Staff will ask Public Works about a pilot.
- The committee asked staff to bring the cost of an upper county cardboard compactor to the November meeting.

4. HOUSEHOLD HAZARDOUS WASTE
Through August: 1,962 household visits on the monthly first-Saturday collection days (same period 2025: 1,880). The July 4 holiday collection was moved to July 11 and drew 284 visits, the most of the year. The small-business appointment program served 14 businesses.
The spring upper county collection event (Cle Elum, May 16) drew 212 households. Staff plan to repeat it in May 2027.

5. SOLID AND HAZARDOUS WASTE PLAN UPDATE
The county's plan is due for its five-year review. Strassburg presented a draft scope: waste composition sort (spring 2027), review of transfer site capacity and hours, recycling and organics options, disposal options and contracts, HHW, illegal dumping, and financing. The state ecology department has asked for a draft by mid-2028. SWAC is the review body. The committee asked that the plan look at:
- Saturday and Sunday hours at the Cle Elum site in summer
- A yard waste compost option in the lower county rather than hauling it
- Illegal dumping on Forest Service and DNR roads in the upper county
Staff will bring a revised scope and a consultant cost estimate in November.

6. ILLEGAL DUMPING
Five cleanups on county roads since June (mattresses, furniture, drywall, a hot tub). The Sheriff's office issued one citation. The committee supports adding two trail cameras at the worst Vantage Hwy pullout.

7. CLE ELUM SITE
Saturday traffic has grown with new homes in the upper county. Average Saturday transactions at Cle Elum: 410 (2025: 365). Lines backed onto the access road three Saturdays in August. Staff are testing a second scale-house window on summer Saturdays and will report.

8. OTHER
- Fall cleanup: the cities of Ellensburg and Cle Elum are running their own fall cleanup weeks in October; county residents outside city limits use the transfer sites as usual.
- Christmas tree drop-off will again be free at both sites in January, trees only (no lights, tinsel or stands).
