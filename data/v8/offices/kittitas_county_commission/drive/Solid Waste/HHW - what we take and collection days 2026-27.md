# Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) - What We Take and When

*Kittitas County Solid Waste. Updated September 2026 (A. Petrakis). For the web page, the scale houses and the Board office.*

Household hazardous waste is anything from your home, garage, shop or garden that says **Caution, Warning, Danger, Poison, Flammable or Corrosive** on the label. It should never go in the garbage, down the drain or on the ground.

## Cost

**Free for Kittitas County households.** Bring a driver's license or utility bill showing a county address. Businesses, farms using commercial quantities, contractors and property managers are not covered: call (509) 962-7542 for the small-business program, which is by appointment and billed at cost.

## What we take

- Oil-based paint, stain, varnish, paint thinner and solvents
- Latex paint (or dry it out with kitty litter and put the dry can in the garbage with the lid off)
- Used motor oil, oil filters and antifreeze
- Gasoline and gas/oil mix (in sealed containers)
- Pesticides, herbicides and other yard and garden chemicals; rodent bait
- Pool and spa chemicals
- Household cleaners: drain openers, oven cleaners, bleach, ammonia
- Fluorescent tubes and compact fluorescent bulbs (unbroken)
- Household batteries (tape the ends of lithium batteries); car batteries
- Propane cylinders up to 20 pounds; small camping cylinders
- Mercury thermometers and thermostats
- Aerosol cans that are not empty

## What we don't take

- Ammunition, fireworks, flares or explosives (call the Sheriff's Office non-emergency line)
- Medical sharps and medicines (sharps containers can go in the garbage when sealed and labeled; medicine take-back kiosks are at local pharmacies)
- Radioactive material, including old smoke detectors (return to the manufacturer)
- Asbestos (call the regional clean air agency for disposal rules)
- Unlabeled 55-gallon drums

## Limits

- Up to **25 gallons of liquid or 220 pounds** per household per visit.
- Containers of 5 gallons or less, sealed, not leaking, and in the original container when possible.
- **Estate or shop cleanouts** with more than the limit: call ahead. Staff can set up an extra appointment so we can plan for the volume. Please don't mix products together.

## Any day of the week

These go in the drop-off area at **both** sites (Ellensburg and Cle Elum) during regular hours, free:
- Used motor oil (up to 5 gallons per visit) and oil filters
- Car batteries
- Household batteries in the collection box at the scale house
- Antifreeze (Ellensburg only)

## Tips for the trip

- Put containers in a sturdy box lined with plastic in your trunk or truck bed, upright and separated.
- Don't smoke or eat while handling.
- If a label is gone, write what you think it is on masking tape.
- Leaking containers: put the whole container in a bucket with a lid.
