KITTITAS COUNTY SOLID WASTE
MEMORANDUM

TO: Board of County Commissioners
FROM: Paula Strassburg, Solid Waste Director
DATE: September 21, 2026
RE: Transfer stations - third quarter report; long-haul agreement; disposal rate for October 6 agenda

1. THIRD QUARTER OPERATIONS (July 1 - September 20)

Tonnage received: Ellensburg transfer station 4,980 tons; Cle Elum transfer station 1,710 tons; total 6,690 tons, up 4 percent from Q3 2025. Self-haul transactions: 21,406 (Ellensburg 14,880; Cle Elum 6,526). About 38 percent of self-haul transactions paid the minimum charge.

Revenue: gate fees at the current rate of $98.00 per ton and $12.00 minimum came to $701,350 for the quarter. Card payments are now 81 percent of transactions; there has been no card surcharge since the new scale software went live in June.

Cle Elum Saturday hours (8:00-4:00) continue through October 31, then winter hours (9:00-3:00). Clean yard waste at both sites stays at $45.00 per ton. Household hazardous waste collection at the Ellensburg site is free for residents on the first Saturday of each month.

Staffing: one scale attendant vacancy at Cle Elum, covered by on-call staff.

2. LONG-HAUL AND DISPOSAL AGREEMENT

Ptarmigan Transport & Disposal hauled all 6,690 tons to the regional landfill in Q3 under the long-haul agreement, at the 2025-26 rate of $54.10 per ton for hauling and disposal combined. The agreement's current term ended September 30. Section 4.2 lets the County extend the term one year at the current rate plus CPI, capped at 3 percent, by written notice delivered on or before September 15; otherwise the agreement renews for one year at the contractor's published rate.

Because the County's extension notice was not delivered to Ptarmigan by the September 15 deadline, the agreement renews on October 1 at Ptarmigan's published 2026-27 rate of $79.60 per ton instead of the extension rate of $55.72 per ton. Ptarmigan confirmed this by letter on September 17. The notice was prepared on September 8 and placed in the board signature folder, but it was not signed and sent before the deadline. At about 26,500 tons a year, the difference is roughly $632,800 over the twelve months.

The solid waste fund cannot absorb that amount; the projected year-end fund balance is $1.1 million against a policy minimum of $900,000.

3. RECOMMENDATION

Adopt a disposal rate adjustment of $22.00 per ton for municipal solid waste at both transfer stations (from $98.00 to $120.00 per ton) and raise the minimum charge from $12.00 to $15.00, effective Monday, October 12, 2026. At current tonnage this recovers about $583,000 a year, most of the added contract cost. Yard waste, appliance and household hazardous waste rates would not change.

Staff will rebid the long-haul agreement in 2027 so a new contract can start October 1, 2027, and will bring a full rate review with the solid waste plan update.
