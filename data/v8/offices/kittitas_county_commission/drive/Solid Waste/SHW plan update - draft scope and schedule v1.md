# Kittitas County Solid and Hazardous Waste Management Plan - Five-Year Update

**Draft scope and schedule, version 1. P. Strassburg, September 2026. For SWAC review November 12, 2026.**

## Why

Counties in Washington must keep a comprehensive solid waste plan and a hazardous waste plan and review them at least every five years (RCW 70A.205). Kittitas County's current plan was adopted in 2021. The state ecology department has asked for a preliminary draft by mid-2028. The plan sets the county's direction on collection, transfer, recycling, organics, disposal, household hazardous waste and how the system is paid for. The cities take part through interlocal agreements and adopt the plan too.

## Who

- **Lead:** Kittitas County Solid Waste (director; recycling and HHW coordinator).
- **Review body:** Solid Waste Advisory Committee (SWAC), which meets quarterly and will meet monthly during drafting.
- **Consultant:** to be selected by qualifications in early 2027 (waste sort, financial model, plan drafting). Estimated $140,000-$180,000 over 2027-28; the state local solid waste financial assistance grant covers up to 75 percent of eligible costs.
- **Partners:** Cities of Ellensburg, Cle Elum, Kittitas, Roslyn and South Cle Elum; Public Health (solid waste handling permits); Public Works (roads, possible crushed glass pilot); Conservation District (agricultural plastics).

## Chapters and key questions

1. **Waste stream.** How much, from where, and what is in it? A spring 2027 composition sort at both sites, plus scale data since 2021. Population growth in the upper county is the main driver.
2. **Collection.** Curbside collection inside the cities and certificated hauler service in the unincorporated area; self-haul to the sites. Is there demand for a rural drop-box site in the Thorp or Kittitas area?
3. **Transfer system.** Capacity, queuing and hours at Ellensburg and Cle Elum; the Cle Elum site's summer Saturday lines; the need for weekend hours in the upper county.
4. **Waste reduction and recycling.** Drop-off recycling, cardboard markets, glass options, contamination, outreach to new residents and short-term rental owners.
5. **Organics.** Yard waste is ground and hauled now. Options: a lower county compost site, a partnership with a private composter, or backyard composting outreach.
6. **Disposal.** The county has no operating landfill and long-hauls waste to a regional landfill. The plan will describe disposal options and contracting approaches for the next 20 years.
7. **Household and small-business hazardous waste.** Monthly Ellensburg collection; spring upper county event; small-business appointments. Should there be a second fixed site?
8. **Illegal dumping and litter.** Upper county forest roads; Vantage Hwy pullouts; enforcement with the Sheriff and land managers.
9. **Agricultural wastes.** Bale wrap and twine, pesticide containers, used oil from farms.
10. **Financing.** The system is paid for by users through site fees and hauler fees, not property taxes. The plan will include a cost-of-service review and a 6-year capital list (scale replacement at Ellensburg, Cle Elum site expansion).

## Public involvement

- Online survey spring 2027 (also on paper at both sites and the libraries).
- Two open houses (Ellensburg, Cle Elum) in fall 2027.
- SWAC meetings are open to the public; comment at every meeting.
- Draft plan comment period of 30 days, then a Board public hearing.
