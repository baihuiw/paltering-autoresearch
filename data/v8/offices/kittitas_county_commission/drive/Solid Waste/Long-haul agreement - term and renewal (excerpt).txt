AGREEMENT FOR LONG-HAUL TRANSPORTATION AND DISPOSAL OF MUNICIPAL SOLID WASTE
Kittitas County and Ptarmigan Transport & Disposal
Contract No. SW-2023-04

EXCERPT - Sections 3 and 4 (term, rates and renewal). Full agreement on file with the Clerk of the Board.

SECTION 3. RATES

3.1 The County shall pay the Contractor a combined rate per ton for loading, transportation and disposal of municipal solid waste delivered from the Ellensburg and Cle Elum transfer stations. The rate for the first contract year is set in Exhibit B.

3.2 The rate shall be adjusted on October 1 of each year of the initial term by the change in the Consumer Price Index (CPI-U, West Region), not to exceed 3 percent in any year.

3.3 Fuel adjustment. If the average retail price of diesel fuel in Washington changes by more than 15 percent from the base price in Exhibit B, either party may request a fuel adjustment under Exhibit C.

SECTION 4. TERM AND RENEWAL

4.1 Initial term. The initial term of this Agreement is October 1, 2023 through September 30, 2026.

4.2 Extension at County option. The County may extend this Agreement for one additional year at the rate then in effect, adjusted under Section 3.2, by written notice to the Contractor delivered on or before September 15 of the final year of the term. The County may exercise this option up to two times.

4.3 Renewal absent notice. If the County does not give notice under Section 4.2, this Agreement shall renew for one year at the Contractor's published rate for the renewal year, which the Contractor shall provide to the County on request.

4.4 Termination for convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 180 days' written notice.

4.5 Notices. Notices under this Section shall be signed by the Chair of the Board of County Commissioners or the Chair's designee and delivered by certified mail or by email with confirmation of receipt.

SECTION 5. TONNAGE

5.1 The County estimates annual tonnage of 25,000 to 28,000 tons. The County does not guarantee minimum tonnage.
