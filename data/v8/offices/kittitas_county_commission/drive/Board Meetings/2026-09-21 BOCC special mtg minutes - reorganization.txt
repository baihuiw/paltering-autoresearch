KITTITAS COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
Monday, September 21, 2026, 1:30 p.m.
Commissioners' Auditorium, Kittitas County Courthouse

Notice of this special meeting was posted and sent to the news media on September 17, 2026.

PRESENT: Commissioners Gene Fortier, Doug Rasch and Renee Gustafsen.
STAFF: Tracy Engebretson; Lisa Brannigan (recording).
PUBLIC: 6.

1. CALL TO ORDER. Vice Chair Fortier called the meeting to order at 1:30 p.m.

2. REORGANIZATION OF THE BOARD FOR THE REMAINDER OF 2026.
Vice Chair Fortier said he had served as acting chair since the previous chair's resignation took effect on September 1, and that because he is on the November 3 ballot he would prefer not to serve as chair for the rest of the year. Commissioner Rasch said he would prefer to keep his current committee assignments and declined the chair.

Motion Rasch, second Fortier, to elect Commissioner Renee Gustafsen chair of the Board for the remainder of 2026, with Commissioner Fortier continuing as vice chair. Carried 3-0.

3. COMMISSIONER LIAISON ASSIGNMENTS (remainder of 2026).
Chair Gustafsen: Budget and Finance (with the Auditor), Event Center and Fair, Noxious Weed Control Board, Human Resources, Solid Waste Advisory Committee.
Vice Chair Fortier: Community Development Services, Public Health, Conference of Governments, Yakima Basin water issues.
Commissioner Rasch: Public Works (roads and Bowers Field), Airport Advisory Committee, Sheriff and law and justice, Lodging Tax Advisory Committee.
Motion Fortier, second Rasch. Carried 3-0.

4. SIGNATURE AUTHORITY. Resolution 2026-109 updating signature authority for warrants, contracts and notices to name Chair Gustafsen. Motion Rasch, second Fortier. Carried 3-0.

5. ADJOURNMENT. 1:52 p.m.

______________________________
Renee Gustafsen, Chair
