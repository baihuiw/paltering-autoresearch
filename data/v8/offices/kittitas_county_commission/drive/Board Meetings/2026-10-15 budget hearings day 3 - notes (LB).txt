KITTITAS COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
2027 BUDGET HEARINGS - DAY 3 (APPOINTED DEPARTMENTS)
Thursday, October 15, 2026, 9:00 a.m.
Commissioners' Auditorium, Kittitas County Courthouse, and online

Summary notes (not formal minutes; budget hearings are noticed as special meetings and the recording is the official record).

PRESENT: Chair Renee Gustafsen; Vice Chair Gene Fortier; Commissioner Doug Rasch.
STAFF: Auditor's office finance division; Tracy Engebretson; Lisa Brannigan (notes).
PUBLIC: 3 in person, 4 online.

9:00 PUBLIC DEFENSE
2027 request $2,064,300 (up 4.4 percent). Public defense administrator reported that caseloads are within the state standards for felonies but near the limit for misdemeanors. The state public defense grant (about $112,000) is expected again in 2027. Contracts with conflict counsel renew in January; rates up 3 percent. Commissioner Rasch asked whether a staffed public defender office would cost less than contracts in the long run. Administrator will bring a comparison in spring 2027. No change to request.

10:00 MISDEMEANANT PROBATION
Request $465,900 (up 2.4 percent). Active caseload 412 (down from 455). Electronic home monitoring now contracted. Chair Gustafsen asked about fees collected from probationers: about $38,000 a year, roughly flat. No change.

11:00 CORONER
Request $251,000 (up 11.1 percent). The difference is the autopsy contract: the pathologist's per-case fee rose from $2,400 to $2,950, and the office expects about 60 cases. Commissioner Fortier asked whether the county could share a contract with a neighboring county; finance will check with the counties association. Commissioners agreed the autopsy cost is not discretionary.

1:00 BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OFFICE
Request $589,100 (up 2.8 percent, wages and benefits per the salary schedule). Tracy Engebretson presented. Three staff positions, no change. Line items:
- Meetings and travel held flat at $14,500 (counties association, state meetings, town halls).
- Records software (agenda management) renewal up $1,200.
- Advertising (legal notices for hearings) $22,000, flat.
- Training $4,000, including open public meetings and records training for new board and committee members.
Chair Gustafsen asked staff to add a line for live captioning of board meetings (estimate $6,500 a year). Commissioner Rasch supported; Commissioner Fortier asked that it be considered with the other general fund requests at the Oct 28 wrap-up. Added to the wrap-up list.

2:00 NON-DEPARTMENTAL
Request $4,112,000 (up 2.0 percent).
- Risk pool assessment up 4 percent ($1.18 million).
- Transfers to the Event Center fund and the airport fund held flat.
- Contingency: $400,000 (unchanged).
- Debt service on the 2016 courthouse improvement bonds: $612,000 (final payment 2031).
Finance noted that the current expense requests total $44.17 million against estimated 2027 revenue of about $43.1 million, a gap of about $1.07 million before any restoration requests. Options will be presented Oct 28: holding vacancies, trimming some department requests, use of fund balance above the policy minimum, and timing of capital items.

Public comment opportunity: the Chair noted that budget hearings are work sessions without public testimony, and that residents may comment in writing at any time or at the public hearing on Monday, December 7, at 10:00 a.m. One person online asked where to find the preliminary budget; staff said it will be posted November 2 and available at the Auditor's office and the public libraries.

3:00 WRAP-UP FOR APPOINTED DEPARTMENTS
Chair Gustafsen summarized the items added to the Oct 28 list so far:
1. Extension - 4-H volunteer leader training, $6,000 (restore 2024 cut)
2. Live captioning of board meetings, $6,500
3. IT cybersecurity analyst (new position), about $118,000 with benefits
4. Prosecutor - one deputy prosecutor (elected office; hearing Oct 26)
5. Sheriff - two deputies (elected office; hearing Oct 26)
6. Maintenance - courthouse boiler controls, $84,000 (one-time)
7. Public Works - higher salt price (road fund, not general fund; for information)

Commissioner Fortier said he expects to support the boiler controls as one-time and wants to see the sheriff's and prosecutor's cases before deciding on new positions. Commissioner Rasch asked finance to show what each request would mean for the year-end fund balance. Chair Gustafsen said the board would not draw fund balance below the policy minimum.

Adjourned 3:38 p.m.

Next: elected offices and courts, Monday October 26, 9:00 a.m.
Notes: L. Brannigan
