KITTITAS COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
REGULAR MEETING MINUTES
Tuesday, September 15, 2026, 9:00 a.m.
Commissioners' Auditorium, Kittitas County Courthouse, 205 W. 5th Ave., Ellensburg

PRESENT: Vice Chair Gene Fortier (acting chair); Commissioner Doug Rasch. One seat vacant (District 2).
STAFF: Tracy Engebretson, Board Office Administrator; Lisa Brannigan, Clerk of the Board (recording).
PUBLIC: approximately 41 in person, 26 online.

1. CALL TO ORDER. Acting Chair Fortier called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

2. APPOINTMENT TO FILL VACANCY, COMMISSIONER DISTRICT 2.
Acting Chair Fortier stated that the District 2 position became vacant on September 1, 2026 on the resignation of the previous commissioner, and that under Article II, Section 15 of the Washington Constitution the remaining commissioners must appoint one of three persons nominated by the county central committee of the previous commissioner's party. The central committee's letter of September 10 nominated, in alphabetical order, Renee Gustafsen, Harold Mickelsen and Joanne Pruitt-Bell. The board interviewed all three in open session on September 14.

Public comment: eleven speakers. Seven spoke for Ms. Gustafsen, citing her Planning Commission service and finance background; two spoke for Mr. Mickelsen; two asked that the board consider which nominee would serve full-time.

Commissioner Rasch said all three were qualified and that Ms. Gustafsen's budget experience was what the board most needed going into the 2027 budget. Acting Chair Fortier agreed.

Motion Rasch, second Fortier, to appoint Renee Gustafsen as Kittitas County Commissioner, District 2, to serve until a successor is elected and qualified. Carried 2-0.

The oath of office will be administered Wednesday, September 16, at 8:30 a.m. in the Commissioners' Auditorium. Under RCW 42.12.040 the position will appear on the November 2027 general election ballot for the remainder of the term ending December 31, 2028.

3. CITIZEN COMMENT. Two speakers on Thorp Highway speeds and one on dust from gravel trucks on Hayward Road; referred to Public Works.

4. CONSENT AGENDA. Minutes of September 1, 2026; vouchers and payroll ($1,872,413.55 all funds); surplus of two Public Works pickups for auction. Motion Rasch, second Fortier. Carried 2-0.

5. PUBLIC WORKS - 2027-2032 SIX-YEAR TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT PROGRAM. Set public hearing for October 20, 2026 at 9:30 a.m. Motion Fortier, second Rasch. Carried 2-0.

6. BUDGET - 2027 BUDGET CALENDAR. The board accepted the 2027 budget calendar presented by the Auditor's finance staff: department hearings October 13-15 and 26-28, preliminary budget November 2, public hearing December 7. Motion Rasch, second Fortier. Carried 2-0.

7. ADJOURNMENT. 10:22 a.m.
