KITTITAS COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
REGULAR MEETING MINUTES - DRAFT (for approval 10/20/2026)
Tuesday, October 6, 2026, 9:00 a.m.
Commissioners' Auditorium, Kittitas County Courthouse, 205 W. 5th Ave., Ellensburg

PRESENT: Chair Renee Gustafsen; Vice Chair Gene Fortier; Commissioner Doug Rasch.
STAFF: Tracy Engebretson, Board Office Administrator; Lisa Brannigan, Clerk of the Board (recording); department staff as noted.
PUBLIC: approximately 14 in person, 9 online.

1. CALL TO ORDER. Chair Gustafsen called the meeting to order at 9:00 a.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

2. CITIZEN COMMENT. Ron Hatley (Thorp) asked the board to look at speeds on Thorp Highway near the school bus stop at Clerf Road; the Chair referred it to Public Works. Ann Mattila (Ellensburg) thanked the board for keeping the Courthouse open during the lunch hour.

3. CONSENT AGENDA. Minutes of September 15 and September 21, 2026; payroll and vouchers through 9/30/2026 ($2,416,882.07 all funds); reappointment of Marcy Odegaard to the Lodging Tax Advisory Committee; Interlocal agreement with the City of Cle Elum for 2027 animal control services. Motion Fortier, second Rasch, to approve the consent agenda. Carried 3-0.

4. PUBLIC WORKS - MANASTASH ROAD GUARDRAIL. Director Ambrose reported three bids for replacement of 1,140 feet of guardrail on Manastash Road (MP 3.2-3.6). Low bid $186,420.00; engineer's estimate $201,000.00. Motion Rasch, second Fortier, to award to the low bidder. Carried 3-0.

5. PUBLIC WORKS - 2027 WINTER SAND AND SALT. Authorization to purchase up to 2,600 tons of deicer salt through the state contract and 9,000 cubic yards of winter sand from county pits. Motion Fortier, second Rasch. Carried 3-0.

6. SOLID WASTE - RESOLUTION 2026-118, DISPOSAL RATE ADJUSTMENT. Director Strassburg presented a resolution adjusting the municipal solid waste rate at the Ellensburg and Cle Elum transfer stations from $98.00 to $120.00 per ton, and the minimum charge from $12.00 to $15.00, effective Monday, October 12, 2026, to cover increased long-haul and disposal contract costs. Clean yard waste, appliance and household hazardous waste rates are unchanged. Commissioner Rasch asked how long the adjustment would stay in place; Director Strassburg said staff will bring a full rate review with the 2027 solid waste plan update. Motion Rasch, second Fortier, to adopt Resolution 2026-118. Carried 3-0.

7. CDS - BLUEBIRD RIDGE AMENDMENT NO. 1

Fire Marshal Ostlund reported that the secondary emergency access road is not passable, so the 31 occupied homes in Bluebird Ridge have only one way in and out, Bluebird Ridge Drive. He said the culvert crossing washed out last spring and has not been rebuilt. Director Kessler presented Amendment No. 1 to the development agreement with Coalfield Land Company LLC, extending the deadline to complete the road from September 30, 2026 to August 31, 2027, keeping the $185,000 performance bond in place and requiring monthly progress reports. Commissioner Fortier asked whether the county could call the bond now; Director Kessler said the bond would not cover the full cost of the crossing and the developer is in the best position to finish the work. Motion Fortier, second Rasch, to approve Amendment No. 1. Carried 3-0.

8. EVENT CENTER - 2027 FACILITY RENTAL RATES. Manager Thiessen presented 2027 rates for the pavilion, barns, arena and RV sites, an average increase of 4 percent, the first since 2024. Motion Rasch, second Fortier. Carried 3-0.

9. HUMAN RESOURCES - JOB DESCRIPTIONS. Updated job descriptions for Equipment Operator II and Permit Technician. Motion Fortier, second Rasch. Carried 3-0.

10. COMMISSIONER REPORTS.
Commissioner Rasch: attended the Upper County Chamber luncheon and the Cle Elum Pioneer Days wrap-up meeting.
Commissioner Fortier: Yakima Basin water forum, Sept 30; Conference of Governments meeting.
Chair Gustafsen: first department head study sessions completed; attended the Washington counties association district meeting in Wenatchee on Oct 1; town hall at the Thorp Grange set for Oct 22.

11. ADJOURNMENT. There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 10:41 a.m.
