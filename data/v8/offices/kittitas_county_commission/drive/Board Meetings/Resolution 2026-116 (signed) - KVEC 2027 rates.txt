COUNTY OF KITTITAS

RESOLUTION NO. 2026-116

A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE 2027 FACILITY RENTAL RATE SCHEDULE FOR THE KITTITAS VALLEY EVENT CENTER

WHEREAS, the Kittitas Valley Event Center is operated by Kittitas County as an enterprise activity, and rental of its buildings, arenas and grounds is a principal source of revenue for the Event Center fund; and

WHEREAS, the facility rental rates were last adjusted effective January 1, 2024 by Resolution 2023-142; and

WHEREAS, since that time the Event Center's costs for electricity, water and sewer, propane, liability insurance and seasonal labor have gone up; and

WHEREAS, the Event Center Manager has compared the county's rates with those of comparable fairgrounds and event facilities in central Washington and found that the proposed 2027 rates remain at or below the regional median for most spaces; and

WHEREAS, the Fair Advisory Committee reviewed the proposed schedule at its meeting of September 21, 2026 and recommended adoption; and

WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners reviewed the proposed schedule in study session on October 5, 2026 and considered it at a regular meeting on October 6, 2026;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of Kittitas County, Washington:

Section 1. The 2027 Kittitas Valley Event Center Facility Rental Rate Schedule, attached as Exhibit A, is adopted. The schedule raises building, arena, grandstand, barn, stall, RV and equipment rates by an average of approximately four percent.

Section 2. The rates in Exhibit A apply to all events held on or after January 1, 2027, regardless of when the rental contract is signed. Contracts signed before the effective date for 2027 event dates shall be billed at the 2027 rates.

Section 3. The reservation deposit, cleaning and damage deposits, cancellation terms, nonprofit and youth organization discount and multi-day discount set out in Exhibit A are continued without change.

Section 4. The Event Center Manager is authorized to waive or reduce rental fees for county departments, for other public agencies under an interlocal agreement, and for events co-sponsored by the county, and shall report such waivers to the Board quarterly.

Section 5. The Event Center Manager is authorized to set rates for items not shown in Exhibit A at a level that recovers the county's cost, and shall bring any such rates to the Board for adoption in the next annual schedule.

Section 6. Resolution 2023-142 is superseded as of January 1, 2027.

ADOPTED this 6th day of October, 2026.
