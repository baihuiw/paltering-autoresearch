BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
KITTITAS COUNTY, WASHINGTON

RESOLUTION NO. 2026-118

A RESOLUTION ADJUSTING THE MUNICIPAL SOLID WASTE DISPOSAL RATE AND MINIMUM CHARGE AT THE KITTITAS COUNTY TRANSFER STATIONS

WHEREAS, Kittitas County operates the Ellensburg and Cle Elum transfer stations and sets disposal rates by resolution of the Board of County Commissioners; and

WHEREAS, the current municipal solid waste disposal rate of $98.00 per ton and minimum charge of $12.00 have been in effect since January 1, 2023; and

WHEREAS, the County's costs for long-haul transportation and disposal of municipal solid waste at a regional landfill increased on October 1, 2026; and

WHEREAS, the Solid Waste Fund must remain self-supporting and maintain the minimum fund balance set by County financial policy; and

WHEREAS, the Board considered the recommendation of the Solid Waste Director at its regular meeting of October 6, 2026;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of Kittitas County, Washington:

Section 1. Effective Monday, October 12, 2026, the disposal rate for municipal solid waste at the Ellensburg and Cle Elum transfer stations is $120.00 per ton, and the minimum charge per transaction is $15.00.

Section 2. The rates for clean yard waste ($45.00 per ton), appliances ($20.00 each, $35.00 with refrigerant), tires and household hazardous waste are unchanged.

Section 3. The Solid Waste Director shall return to the Board with a comprehensive rate review together with the update of the Kittitas County Solid Waste Management Plan.

Section 4. All other provisions of the solid waste fee schedule remain in effect.

ADOPTED this 6th day of October, 2026.
