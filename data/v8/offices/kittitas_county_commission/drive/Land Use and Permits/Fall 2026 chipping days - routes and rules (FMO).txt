KITTITAS COUNTY FIRE MARSHAL'S OFFICE / PUBLIC WORKS
FALL 2026 CURBSIDE CHIPPING DAYS - ROUTE SHEET AND RULES
Issued September 28, 2026 (H. Brandau). For crews, the Board office front desk and the web page.

Free for residents of unincorporated Kittitas County. No sign-up needed on the route roads below. Residents on other roads in the same area can call by the Wednesday before to be added if the crew can reach them.

SATURDAY, OCTOBER 17 - EASTON AND RONALD
Crew A (Easton): Sparks Rd, Railroad St, Lake Easton area roads, Cabin Creek Rd (county portion), Easton loop roads.
Crew B (Ronald): roads off SR 903 between Ronald and the Lake Cle Elum area (county roads only), Ronald townsite streets.
Start 7:00 a.m. Crews work until all piles on the route are done or 5:00 p.m.

SATURDAY, OCTOBER 24 - CLE ELUM AND ROSLYN AREA
Crew A: unincorporated roads around Cle Elum and South Cle Elum, Westside Rd, Upper Peoh Point Rd.
Crew B: unincorporated roads around Roslyn and the Ronald-Roslyn corridor not covered on 10/17.
Easton and Ronald residents who missed October 17 may bring piles to the drop point at the upper county Public Works shop in Cle Elum, 8:00 a.m.-noon on October 24 only. Pickup loads only; no trailers over 12 feet.

SATURDAY, OCTOBER 31 - THORP AND MANASTASH
Crew A: Thorp Hwy, Clerf Rd, Thorp Cemetery Rd, Thorp Prairie roads (county).
Crew B: Manastash Rd, Umptanum Rd, and roads off Manastash west of Ellensburg.

RULES FOR PILES
1. Piles at the edge of the road (not in the ditch or the road) by 7:00 a.m. Crews make one pass.
2. Branches up to 6 inches in diameter, at least 3 feet long, cut ends facing the road.
3. Maximum pile: about 10 feet long by 5 feet wide by 4 feet high. Up to 3 piles per address.
4. No stumps, roots, root balls, lumber, wire, rocks, dirt, grass clippings, leaves, pine needles in bags, or blackberry and other thorny vines.
5. No piles under power lines or within 5 feet of mailboxes, fences or hydrants.
6. Chips are left on site unless the resident asks crews to blow them back onto the property (if access allows) or haul them away (hauled chips go to the county's compost grinding).

AFTER THE FALL ROUND
- This is the last county chipping for 2026. Spring 2027 chipping days are planned for late April and May; dates will be announced in March, with Easton and Ronald again first on the schedule.
- Between rounds, residents can take clean yard waste and branches to either transfer site (Ellensburg or Cle Elum) at the yard waste rate, or have a Firewise neighborhood work day (neighborhood grants are open until December 1).
- Outdoor burning restrictions remain in effect in the unincorporated county. Do not burn slash piles until restrictions are lifted and you have checked with your fire district.
