TOP QUESTIONS AT THE COUNTER (September, lower county)
1. Can I put a second home or ADU on my land? Depends on zone and lot size; the ADU code update (PC hearing Oct 27) would allow detached ADUs on more rural lots.
2. Do I need a permit for an ag building? Ag buildings on qualifying farm land are exempt from building permits but need a site plan for setbacks and a fire access review if over 4,000 sq ft.
3. Can I live in an RV on my land while I build? Yes, with a temporary RV permit tied to an active building permit (up to 18 months).
4. Where are my property lines? CDS does not locate property lines; a licensed surveyor is needed.
5. How long does a new home permit take? Average first review 15 working days in the lower county in September (target 15).
