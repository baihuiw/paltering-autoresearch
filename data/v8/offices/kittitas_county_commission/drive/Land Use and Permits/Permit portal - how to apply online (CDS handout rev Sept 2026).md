# Applying Online: The Kittitas County Permit Portal

*Community Development Services handout, revised September 2026 for the new hosting. Ask for a paper copy at any CDS counter.*

The Permit Portal lets you apply for building, mechanical, septic and land use permits, pay fees, upload plans and track a review without a trip to the counter. In September 71 percent of upper county and 68 percent of lower county residential applications were submitted online.

## What you will need

| Permit | Usual uploads |
|---|---|
| New home, addition, shop or garage | Site plan, floor plans, elevations, energy code forms, septic approval or sewer letter |
| Owner-builder | The above, plus the signed owner-builder form and a photo ID |
| Septic | Designer's drawings and a parcel map |
| Land use / short plat | Deed or title report, parcel map, application narrative |
| Fee waiver or reduction request | Signed request form and supporting income documents |
| Contractor | Copy of state contractor registration and liability insurance |

Files can be PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 25 MB each. Name them so a reviewer can tell what they are: "Smith site plan.pdf" is better than "scan0043.pdf".

## Steps

1. Create an account with an email address you check. You will get a confirmation message.
2. Choose the permit type and enter the parcel number (it is on your tax statement).
3. Upload your documents and review the fee estimate.
4. Submit. You will get an application number. Plan reviewers email you with questions; reply through the portal so the file stays together.
5. Pay fees when the review is ready (the portal sends you to the county's payment processor).
