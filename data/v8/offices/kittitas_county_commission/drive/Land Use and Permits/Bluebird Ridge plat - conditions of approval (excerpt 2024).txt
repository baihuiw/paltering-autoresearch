KITTITAS COUNTY COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT SERVICES
Conditions of Approval - Preliminary Plat
File: LP-24-00007  Bluebird Ridge (52 lots, Phases 1 and 2)
Applicant: Coalfield Land Company LLC
Location: North side of Nelson Siding Road, approx. 3.5 miles west of Cle Elum
Approved by the Board of County Commissioners: Resolution 2024-071

Excerpt prepared for the board office binder (conditions 9 through 18 only; full findings and conditions on file with CDS).

9. Roads. Bluebird Ridge Drive and all interior roads (Kestrel Lane, Junco Court, Tanager Loop) shall be private roads built to the Kittitas County Road Standards for private roads serving 41 or more lots, and maintained by the owners association. A road maintenance agreement shall be recorded with the final plat.

10. Access to Nelson Siding Road. The plat's primary access is a single approach from Bluebird Ridge Drive onto Nelson Siding Road. The approach permit shall be obtained from Kittitas County Public Works before construction.

11. Addressing and signage. Road name signs and address markers shall be installed before any certificate of occupancy is issued for a lot on that road.

12. Water. Each lot shall be served by the Group B water system approved by Kittitas County Public Health. Fire flow storage of 30,000 gallons shall be provided at the tank site on Tanager Loop.

13. Fire hydrants and standpipes. Dry standpipes shall be installed at the locations shown on the approved fire protection plan.

14. Secondary emergency access. A secondary emergency access road connecting the north end of Kestrel Lane to the forest road north of the plat shall be constructed to the Fire Marshal's standards (20-foot all-weather gravel surface, maximum 12 percent grade, crossings and culverts rated for fire apparatus) and accepted in writing by the Fire Marshal no later than the date set in the development agreement. The route shall be gated with a Knox lock or breakaway chain only, never a padlock, and kept clear of vegetation.

15. Performance security. Until the Fire Marshal accepts the secondary emergency access road, the applicant shall maintain a performance bond or assignment of funds in favor of the County in an amount not less than 125 percent of the engineer's estimate for the remaining work.

16. Wildfire. All lots are within the wildland-urban interface. Each home shall meet the ignition-resistant construction standards of the International Wildland-Urban Interface Code as adopted by the County, and the owners association shall maintain a 30-foot fuel reduction zone along Bluebird Ridge Drive.

17. Covenants. The covenants shall include notice to purchasers of conditions 14 through 16.

18. Phasing. Phase 1 (lots 1-28) and Phase 2 (lots 29-52) may be recorded separately. Building permits for Phase 2 lots may be issued once Phase 2 is recorded.

Development agreement: the date for completion of condition 14 was set at December 31, 2025, and extended administratively by the CDS Director to September 30, 2026 (letter dated January 12, 2026).
