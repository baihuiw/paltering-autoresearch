AMENDMENT NO. 1
TO DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
LP-24-00007 (BLUEBIRD RIDGE)

Between Kittitas County, a political subdivision of the State of Washington ("County"), and Coalfield Land Company LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("Developer").

RECITALS

A. The County and Developer entered into a Development Agreement for the Bluebird Ridge plat, approved by Resolution 2024-071, which requires the Developer to construct a secondary emergency access road under Condition 14 of the plat approval.

B. The date for completion of the secondary emergency access road was extended administratively to September 30, 2026.

C. The secondary emergency access road has not been completed, and the Developer has asked for additional time to rebuild the culvert crossing and complete the road.

D. The Board of County Commissioners considered this request at its regular meeting of October 6, 2026.

AGREEMENT

1. Completion date. The Developer shall complete the secondary emergency access road and obtain the Fire Marshal's written acceptance no later than August 31, 2027.

2. Performance security. The Developer shall keep in force the existing performance bond in the amount of $185,000.00 until the Fire Marshal's written acceptance. The County may draw on the bond if the road is not accepted by the completion date.

3. Progress reports. The Developer shall submit a written progress report to the CDS Director by the 10th of each month, beginning November 10, 2026, describing work completed, the contractor's schedule and any permits pending.

4. Interim measures. Until acceptance, the Developer shall (a) keep the route free of padlocked gates and debris, (b) maintain the 30-foot fuel reduction zone along Bluebird Ridge Drive required by Condition 16, and (c) cooperate with the Fire Marshal on any interim measures the Fire Marshal requires.

5. No other changes. All other terms of the Development Agreement remain in full force.

APPROVED by the Board of County Commissioners on October 6, 2026.
