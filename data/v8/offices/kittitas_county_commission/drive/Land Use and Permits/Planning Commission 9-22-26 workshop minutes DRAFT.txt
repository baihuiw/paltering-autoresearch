KITTITAS COUNTY PLANNING COMMISSION
WORKSHOP MINUTES - DRAFT
Tuesday, September 22, 2026, 6:30 p.m.
Commissioners' Auditorium, Kittitas County Courthouse, and online

MEMBERS PRESENT: Corinne Lafleur (chair), and four other members. One position vacant (Commissioner Gustafsen's former seat, vacated on her appointment to the Board). One absent, excused.
STAFF: Jared Szabo, Planner II; Owen Kessler, CDS Director (items 3-4).
PUBLIC: 9 in person, 6 online.

1. CALL TO ORDER. Chair Lafleur opened the workshop at 6:30 p.m. She thanked former member Renee Gustafsen for five years on the commission and congratulated her on her appointment. The Board will advertise the vacancy.

2. MINUTES. Minutes of August 25, 2026 approved as corrected (item 4: "Cove Rd" for "Cave Rd").

3. 2026 COMPREHENSIVE PLAN DOCKET - WORKSHOP
Planner Szabo reviewed the seven amendment requests received by the June 30 deadline (summary in the packet). No action is taken at a workshop. The public hearing is Tuesday, October 27, 6:30 p.m.

Discussion by item:
- CPA-26-01 (rural residential to rural 5, near Thorp): two members asked about water availability and fire district response time. Staff will include the fire district's comment letter in the hearing packet.
- CPA-26-02 (commercial designation at a Vantage Hwy interchange parcel): members asked whether the request is site-specific or would allow any highway commercial use. Staff said any use allowed in the zone.
- CPA-26-03 (text amendment, farm worker housing): broad support. Members asked staff to coordinate with the agricultural community and Extension.
- CPA-26-04 and CPA-26-05 (mineral lands designation, two adjoining parcels south of Ellensburg): one member asked about truck routes and dust on county roads. Staff said haul routes are set at the permit stage, not the designation stage, and Public Works would comment. Two members of the public spoke about gravel truck dust on rural gravel roads generally.
- CPA-26-06 (urban growth area adjustment requested by a city): staff said the city's council has not yet acted on its own resolution; the item may be deferred to the 2027 docket.
- CPA-26-07 (text amendment, small-scale event venues in the agricultural zone): extensive discussion. Members want clear limits on number of events, hours, parking and noise. Staff will bring draft standards.

4. ACCESSORY DWELLING UNIT CODE UPDATE
Director Kessler summarized the remaining issues after the August workshop: maximum size (staff now proposes 1,000 sq ft or 50 percent of the main home, whichever is greater, up to 1,200 sq ft), whether a detached ADU may be on a separate septic system, and parking. Members agreed the draft is ready for the October 27 hearing along with the docket.

4a. WORK PROGRAM FOR 2027 (brief)
Director Kessler previewed likely 2027 items: the shoreline master program periodic review (state comments due back Nov 30), a sign code cleanup, the critical areas ordinance update required on the state's schedule, and possibly a short-term rental code review if the Board adds it. Members asked that the critical areas update include outreach to farmers early, and that staff schedule fewer evening workshops in December and January. Staff will bring a draft 2027 work program to the November meeting.

5. PUBLIC COMMENT
- A Thorp resident spoke against CPA-26-01, citing traffic on Thorp Highway.
- A Kittitas resident asked that the event venue standards protect neighbors from late-night noise.
- A builder supported the ADU update and asked that it take effect before the 2027 building season.

6. STAFF REPORT
- The Board of County Commissioners will hold the six-year transportation plan hearing October 20. Commission members may testify as individuals.
- Planning Commission vacancy: applications through the Board office; the Board appoints.
- Training: the Prosecutor's office will provide open meetings and public records training October 9 for new board and commission members; the session is open to planning commissioners.

7. NEXT MEETING
Tuesday, October 27, 2026, 6:30 p.m. - public hearing on the 2026 docket and the ADU code update.
