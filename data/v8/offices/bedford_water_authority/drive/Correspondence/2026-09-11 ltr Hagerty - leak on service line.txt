Thank you for your email of September 8 about the leak at your home and for your patience while our crew checked it.

Our crew visited on September 8 and found that the meter and the connections in the meter box were working properly and that the leak was on the line between the meter box and your house. Under the Authority's policy, the property owner owns and maintains the service line from the meter box to the house, and the Authority maintains the main, the line up to the meter and the meter box. I understand that you had a plumber repair the line on September 9.

I am glad to tell you that your account qualifies for a leak adjustment. Because your August usage was more than double your average, we have credited $65.08 to your account for the water lost to the leak. The credit will appear on your next statement. This is your first adjustment; the policy allows up to three in six years.

I know a repair like this is an unwelcome expense, and I appreciate how quickly you took care of it. If you have any other questions about your account, please call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, extension 1.
