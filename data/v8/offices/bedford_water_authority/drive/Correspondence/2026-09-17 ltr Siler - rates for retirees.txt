You raised a fair question about what the July rate increase means for retirees and others on fixed incomes, and I promised you a written answer. Board members do not respond during public comment, so I am writing on the Board's behalf.

Why rates went up in July

New water and sewer rates took effect July 1, 2026. The Board held a public hearing on them on April 21 and adopted them on May 19. For a typical household using 4,000 gallons a month with a standard 5/8-inch meter, the monthly bill went from $119.60 to $124.20, an increase of $4.60, or 3.8 percent.

The main reasons for the increase were:

- Payments on the revenue bonds the Authority issued in 2025. Those bonds are paying for new filters at the Central water treatment facility, the water main replacement on Oakwood and Edmund Streets in Bedford, the second phase of the Route 460 water main and the Sunset Ridge recoating project.
- Treatment chemicals, which now cost about 14 percent more than they did in 2025.
- Water we buy from the City of Lynchburg for part of the Forest area, which went up 4 percent.
- Electricity for our treatment facilities and pumps, up 6 percent.
- A 3 percent cost-of-living raise for our employees.

It may help to know that the Authority receives no tax money. Everything we spend on treatment, pipes, crews and debt payments comes from the bills our customers pay. To keep this year's increase as small as we could, the Board postponed a meter replacement project in the Lakes area to next year, two open positions have been left unfilled, and we re-bid our employee health insurance.

What the Finance Committee will look at

At the meeting, the Chair referred your request for a senior discount to the Board's Finance Committee. That committee will meet on Tuesday, October 6, at 5:30 p.m. at the Authority Annex. I expect it to ask staff what a discount for seniors or customers on fixed incomes could look like: who would qualify, how we would confirm eligibility, what it would cost, and how other utilities in Virginia handle it. Because rates for this year are already set, any change would most likely be considered with next year's budget, which begins July 1, 2027. The draft budget goes to the Finance Committee in March, and there will be a public hearing in April.

I do not want to promise a result the Board has not reached. There is no senior discount today, and I cannot say whether there will be one. But I can promise that your request is on the committee's list, and that we will let you know what the committee decides.

Help in the meantime

If a bill is ever hard to manage, please call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, ext. 1, and ask for Renee Dowdy or Brandi Coles. They can go over your bill with you, check whether your usage looks normal, and tell you about local assistance funds that help households with utility bills. A sudden jump in usage is often a leak, and we will check your meter at no charge. Bank draft AutoPay is also free and takes one more thing off your list.

How to speak at future meetings

You are welcome at any regular Board meeting. The Board meets the third Tuesday of each month at 7:00 p.m. at the Authority Annex, 1723 Falling Creek Road, and on Zoom. The next meetings are October 20 and November 17. To speak in person, sign up at the door between 6:30 and 6:55 p.m.; you only need to give your name and the area where you live. To speak by Zoom, send an email to board@bedfordwater.com by noon on the day of the meeting. Each speaker has three minutes. If you prefer to write, comments received by noon on the meeting day are given to every Board member and noted in the minutes. The Finance Committee's meetings are also open to the public.
