Is water and sewer available?

Water: yes. There is an Authority 6-inch water main along the north side of Cottontown Road in front of your lot, so no new main is needed.

Sewer: based on our maps, there is no public sewer main on that part of Cottontown Road. The nearest gravity sewer is a little under half a mile away. That means you would most likely need a septic system, which is permitted through the county health department, not the Authority. Bringing a sewer main to your lot is possible under the Authority's developer policy, but the owner pays for the new main, and for a single home that expense is usually far higher than a septic system. Engineering will confirm all of this in writing in your availability letter.

The steps, in order

1. Ask for an availability letter. It is free and usually takes about five business days. Send Engineering the lot address (or the tax map number if the address has not been assigned yet). The letter states whether a main is in front of the lot and whether sewer is available. Your builder, lender and the county will all ask for it.

2. Apply for the connection. Send Engineering the application with the lot address, tax map number and a site plan or plat showing where the house will sit and where you would like the meter.

3. Pay the connection charges. These must be paid before the county releases the building permit for water and sewer. As of July 1, 2026, for a standard 5/8-inch residential meter:
   - Water only: $7,190 (capital recovery $5,250, tap $1,650, meter $290)
   - Sewer, if it were available: $8,800 (capital recovery $6,200, tap $2,600)
   - Both water and sewer: $15,990
   For your lot, if the availability letter confirms water only, the amount would be $7,190. Many builders pay these charges on the owner's behalf, so check your contract.

4. We install the tap and meter box. An Authority crew makes the connection to the main and sets the meter box at the edge of your lot, usually within 15 business days of payment. It can take longer in winter, or if a bore under the road is needed. Your lot is on the same side of the road as the main, so we do not expect a bore.

5. Your contractor runs the line to the house. The water line from the meter box to the house belongs to you. Your contractor installs it, and the county building official checks it as part of your building permit.

6. Open your account before you move in. Stop by Customer Service at our office or call (540) 586-7210, ext. 1. Opening an account requires the $25 application charge and a $125 water deposit (plus $125 for sewer where we provide sewer).
