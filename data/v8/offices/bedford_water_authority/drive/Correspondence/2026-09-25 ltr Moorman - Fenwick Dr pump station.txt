Dear Mr. Moorman:

Thank you for your letter of September 18 and for welcoming me to the Authority. I am sorry the odor from the Mill Branch pump station has made evenings on your street so unpleasant for so long.

I have read the file, and I visited the station this week with our Director of Operations. The odor comes from gases released when sewage sits in the wet well and is pumped out, and it is worst on still, humid evenings when the air does not move. Our crews clean the wet well regularly, which helps for a time.

The longer-term fix is an odor control unit that treats the air leaving the station. The Board included it in this year's capital plan. We have ordered the unit, and installation is planned for later this fall. I will make sure our staff let you and your neighbors know when the work is scheduled.

In the meantime, if the odor is especially bad on a given evening, please call our 24-hour line at (540) 586-7210 and press 9, so an operator can check the station.

Thank you again for writing, and for your patience.

Sincerely,

Warren Kessinger, P.E.
Executive Director
Bedford Regional Water Authority
