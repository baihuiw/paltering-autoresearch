Dear Ms. Kowalczyk:

Thank you for your email of October 1 about low water pressure at your home in the mornings.

Our operators checked the pressure at the hydrant nearest your home on October 5 and found 52 pounds per square inch at 7:00 a.m., within the normal range for your area (40 to 80). Harbor Ridge sits near the top of its pressure zone, so morning pressure is lower when many households are using water at the same time. The September 29 main repair on your street did not change the pressure in the area.

A few things can cause low pressure inside a home even when pressure at the street is normal: a partly closed main shut-off valve, a pressure-reducing valve that needs adjusting, or sediment in faucet aerators or a water filter. A plumber can check these. If you would like, our crew will test the pressure at your meter so you can compare it with what you see inside; just call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, extension 1.

Thank you for letting us know, and please write again if it does not improve.
