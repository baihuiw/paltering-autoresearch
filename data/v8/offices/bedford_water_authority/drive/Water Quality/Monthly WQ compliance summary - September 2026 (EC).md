## Summary
- **Routine monitoring: all September compliance samples met standards.** Required reports were filed with the VDH field office on time.
- Turbidity at the Central WTP averaged **0.04 NTU**, well under the treatment technique limit.
- Third-quarter disinfection byproduct (DBP) results are in. The highest locational running annual average (LRAA) for total trihalomethanes was **58 ug/L** at one Lakes Central site, under the 80 ug/L limit.
- Lead line inventory: **2,180 of 3,400** unknown addresses verified. **No lead found.** 41 galvanized lines found and scheduled.
- 40 backflow test notices sent in September; 27 tests returned so far.

## 2. Disinfection byproducts - Q3 2026
Samples taken August 11 at the 10 Stage 2 monitoring sites (BC-, FC-, LC- and ST- sites); contract lab results received September 4. Full table: *Q3 2026 DBP results by site (EC).csv*.

| | Highest single result | Highest LRAA | Site with highest LRAA | MCL (LRAA) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total trihalomethanes (TTHM) | 76.4 ug/L | 58.0 ug/L | LC-8, Harbor Ridge station | 80 ug/L |
| Haloacetic acids (HAA5) | 40.2 ug/L | 33.9 ug/L | LC-8, Harbor Ridge station | 60 ug/L |

Every site met both limits. As usual, the third quarter is the highest of the year because warm water speeds up the reactions that form DBPs. LC-8 sits at the far end of Lakes Central, where water is oldest in late summer. Its LRAA has risen from 53.9 a year ago to 58.0. To keep it in check, we have added dead-end flushing at Harbor Ridge to the fall list and asked the SML WTP to review its operations for the coming summer. Q4 samples will be taken November 10.
