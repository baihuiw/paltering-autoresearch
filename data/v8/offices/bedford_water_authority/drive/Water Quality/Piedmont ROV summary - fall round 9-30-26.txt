PIEDMONT TANK INSPECTION SERVICES
Potable Water Storage Tank Inspection - Summary of Findings
Client: Bedford Regional Water Authority (attn: Erin Castellaw, Water Quality Manager)
Inspection dates: September 22-24, 2026
Method: Remotely operated vehicle (ROV) interior inspection while in service, with exterior and roof inspection by technician. Video and full report delivered on drive.
Report date: September 30, 2026

1. GRAVES KNOB (0.75 MG elevated) - inspected 9/22
Interior coating in good condition. Light, even sediment (under 1/2 inch). Vents and hatch secure and screened. Overflow flap valve functioning.
Recommendation: No action. Next inspection in five years.

2. ASHMOOR HILL (0.5 MG standpipe, built 1989) - inspected 9/23
Roof: Vent screen torn away on the north side; opening approximately 10 by 14 inches. Roof hatch gasket failed and hatch does not seat; daylight visible around the hatch from inside.
Interior: Sediment 3 to 5 inches across the floor. Remains of at least two birds and a large quantity of feathers on the floor near the center column; feathers floating at the water surface near the vent. Coating failure at the roof-to-shell joint with rust staining.
Exterior: Fair; chalking on the south face.
Recommendation: These are sanitary defects. We recommend that the tank be removed from service, drained, cleaned and disinfected, and that the vent screen and roof hatch be repaired, before the tank is returned to service. This should be done as soon as practicable. Coating repair at the roof joint can be scheduled with the next recoat.
Availability: Our earliest crew opening for drain, clean and disinfect is the week of November 16, 2026. We can do the vent and hatch repair the same week.

3. LONE PINE (1.0 MG ground storage) - inspected 9/24
Interior coating good. Moderate sediment (about 1 inch) in the northeast quadrant. Ladder cage: four loose bolts at the second platform (reported to staff on site).
Recommendation: Tighten ladder cage bolts. Washout at next scheduled cleaning. Next inspection in five years.
