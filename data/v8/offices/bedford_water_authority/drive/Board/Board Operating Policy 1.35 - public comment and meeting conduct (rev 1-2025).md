# Board Operating Policy 1.35
## Public Comment and Conduct of Meetings
Bedford Regional Water Authority, Board of Directors
Adopted March 2014. Revised January 21, 2025 (Zoom speakers, written comments, packet posting).

---

### 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how members of the public may speak at Board meetings, how meetings are run, and how agendas and packets are made available. Committee meetings follow it where practical.

### 2. Public comment period
1. Any person may speak during the public comment period at the start of each regular meeting, on any matter within the Authority's responsibilities, whether or not it is on the agenda.
2. **In-person sign-up** is at the door of the Authority Annex from 6:30 to 6:55 p.m. Speakers give their name and the area where they live or own property (for example, "Forest" or "Grove Street"). A street address is not required.
3. **Zoom speakers** register by noon on the meeting day by email to board@bedfordwater.com or through the contact form on bedfordwater.com (choose "Board / general question"). The Secretary confirms and gives the display name to use.
4. Each speaker has **three minutes**. Time may not be given to another speaker.
5. The public comment period is **30 minutes in total** unless the Chair extends it. Speakers are called in sign-up order.
6. Speakers address the Board as a whole, not individual members, staff or the audience.
7. The Board does not debate or answer questions during public comment. The Chair may ask staff to follow up. **Staff follow up with the speaker within five business days**, by phone, email or letter.
8. **Written comments** received by noon on the meeting day (by email, the contact form, mail or the drop box) are sent to all members before the meeting and noted in the minutes by the writer's name and topic.
9. **Public hearings** required by law or by Board policy (for example, on rates and fees) are separate agenda items with their own sign-up and are not part of the public comment period. Hearing rules are announced in the hearing notice.

### 3. Personnel and legal matters
Comments about named employees should be sent in writing to the Secretary rather than made in open session. Matters in active litigation may be raised, but the Board will not discuss them. Nothing in this section limits anyone's right to speak on Authority business.

### 4. Meeting decorum
1. The Chair presides and keeps order. Members are recognized by the Chair before speaking.
2. Members of the public do not speak from the audience except during public comment or a public hearing.
3. Personal attacks, threats and profanity are out of order. The Chair may warn a speaker once and then end that speaker's time. A person who continues to disrupt the meeting after a warning may be asked to leave.
4. Cell phones should be silenced.

### 5. Recording and broadcast
1. Meetings are recorded on audio and video by the Authority and streamed on Zoom. Recordings are posted on bedfordwater.com within three business days and kept under the Library of Virginia records schedule.
2. Any person may record an open meeting, from the back or sides of the room, without disrupting it.
3. The written minutes, once approved, are the official record. Recordings are not transcribed.

### 6. Zoom etiquette (members of the public)
- Attendees join muted with video off. The host unmutes registered speakers when called.
- Use the display name given at registration so the Secretary can find you.
- The chat function is turned off for attendees. Questions sent by chat are not read into the record.
- If the Zoom connection fails, the meeting continues in the room.

### 7. Agendas and packets
1. The Secretary prepares the agenda with the Executive Director and the Chair. Agenda items are due to the Secretary on the Wednesday about two weeks before the meeting, and staff memos on the Wednesday before the meeting at noon.
2. **The full packet is posted the Friday before the meeting on bedfordwater.com** (Board of Directors page) and sent to members the same day. Paper copies are available at the front counter on request.
3. Items added after the packet is posted are listed on an amended agenda and explained by the Chair at the start of the meeting.
4. Draft minutes are posted with the next packet and marked DRAFT until approved.

### 8. Closed meetings
The Board may meet in closed session only as allowed by the Virginia Freedom of Information Act, on a motion stating the subject and the code section. Closed sessions are held after public comment and before adjournment, and the Board certifies afterward that only the stated matters were discussed.

### 9. Review
The Policies and Projects Committee reviews this policy every two years, next in January 2027.
