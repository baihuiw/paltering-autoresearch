BEDFORD REGIONAL WATER AUTHORITY
REGULAR BOARD MEETING MINUTES - DRAFT (for approval October 20, 2026)
Tuesday, September 15, 2026, 7:00 p.m.
Authority Annex, 1723 Falling Creek Road, Bedford, Virginia, and by Zoom

1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair Lyle Harrelson called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Members present: Lyle Harrelson (Chair), Patricia Okafor (Vice Chair), Doug Ferrell, Margaret Thacker, Wendell Price, Sandra Quarles, Tom Hylton.
Staff present: Teresa Halvorsen, Acting Executive Director and Secretary; Gordon Ashworth, Director of Finance and Treasurer; Sanjay Mehra, Director of Engineering; Wade Lipscomb, Director of Operations; Erin Castellaw, Water Quality Manager; Kayla Brumfield, Communications Coordinator. Authority counsel present. Public: nine in the room, four on Zoom.

2. PUBLIC COMMENT
Ron Tolliver, Stewartsville, asked when the hydrant on Hardy Road near the church will be painted and flow-tested; staff will follow up.
Brenda Siler, Forest, said the July rate increase was hard on retirees and asked the Board to look at a senior discount. The Chair thanked her and referred the request to the Finance Committee.
Alan Mays, Sheffield Hollow Road, thanked staff for moving the water line extension forward and said several neighbors are still hauling water.

3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
Minutes of the August 18, 2026 regular meeting. Motion by Ms. Okafor, second by Mr. Hylton, to approve as presented. Carried 7-0.

4. FINANCIAL REPORT (JULY 2026)
Mr. Ashworth reported July operating revenue of $1.94 million, 3.1 percent above July 2025, reflecting the rate adjustment effective July 1. Operating expenses were within budget. Days of cash on hand: 214. Motion by Mr. Price, second by Ms. Quarles, to accept the report. Carried 7-0.

5. PERSONNEL COMMITTEE - EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR
Ms. Thacker reported for the Personnel Committee. The previous executive director retired effective September 4 after many years of service, and the Board thanked him again for his work. The committee advertised the position in June, interviewed four finalists in August, and recommends hiring Warren Kessinger, P.E., currently deputy director of operations at a regional water authority in the Shenandoah Valley, as Executive Director effective Monday, September 21, 2026, at an annual salary of $168,500, under the employment agreement reviewed by Authority counsel.

Mr. Price said he had no objection to Mr. Kessinger but would have preferred to keep Ms. Halvorsen as acting director through December and bring the decision to the full Board in executive session first.

Motion by Ms. Thacker, second by Mr. Ferrell, to hire Warren Kessinger as Executive Director effective September 21, 2026, and authorize the Chair to sign the employment agreement.
Roll call: Harrelson, yes; Okafor, yes; Ferrell, yes; Thacker, yes; Price, no; Quarles, yes; Hylton, yes. Carried 6-1.

The Chair thanked Ms. Halvorsen for serving as acting director.

6. ENGINEERING - SHEFFIELD HOLLOW WATER LINE EXTENSION (WS-26-01)
Mr. Mehra reported that four bids were opened September 3. The low bid was Dellwood Pipeline & Grading at $1,209,600.00, above the engineer's estimate of $1,030,000. The project is funded by a Drinking Water State Revolving Fund loan through the Virginia Department of Health (75 percent) and by the 38 participating owners (25 percent), and award requires the Department's concurrence.

Motion by Mr. Hylton, second by Ms. Okafor, to adopt Resolution 2026-19 authorizing the Executive Director to award the contract to Dellwood Pipeline & Grading in the amount of $1,209,600.00, contingent on concurrence from the Virginia Department of Health. Carried 7-0.

7. ENGINEERING - OTHER PROJECTS
Central WTP filters 3 and 4: contractor mobilizing September 28. Oakwood/Edmund 8-inch replacement: substantially complete by month end. Waterlick Road booster rehab: bid documents in review. SCADA radio upgrade: six of 19 sites cut over.

8. OPERATIONS REPORT
Mr. Lipscomb reported the September 10 break on the 12-inch transmission main along Route 221. Crews isolated the break within 90 minutes; about 140 customers were without water for up to nine hours and a precautionary boil water advisory was issued for 61 of them and lifted September 12 after two rounds of clean samples. He thanked the crews and the Forest Fire Department for traffic control.

9. WATER QUALITY
Ms. Castellaw reported all August compliance samples met standards. The fall tank inspection round (three tanks) is scheduled for the week of September 21. Lead service line inventory verification continues; no lead services have been found.

10. COMMITTEE REPORTS
Finance Committee will meet October 6 at 5:30 p.m. Policies and Projects Committee will meet October 13 at 5:30 p.m.

11. ADJOURNMENT
Motion by Mr. Ferrell, second by Ms. Quarles, to adjourn. Carried 7-0. Adjourned at 8:22 p.m.

Teresa Halvorsen, Secretary
