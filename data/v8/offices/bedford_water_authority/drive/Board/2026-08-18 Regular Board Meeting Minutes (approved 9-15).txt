BEDFORD REGIONAL WATER AUTHORITY
REGULAR BOARD MEETING MINUTES - APPROVED September 15, 2026
Tuesday, August 18, 2026, 7:00 p.m.
Authority Annex, 1723 Falling Creek Road, Bedford, Virginia, and by Zoom

1. CALL TO ORDER
Chair Lyle Harrelson called the meeting to order at 7:01 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

Members present: Lyle Harrelson (Chair), Patricia Okafor (Vice Chair), Doug Ferrell, Margaret Thacker, Wendell Price, Sandra Quarles, Tom Hylton.
Staff present: the Executive Director; Teresa Halvorsen, Assistant Executive Director and Secretary; Gordon Ashworth, Director of Finance and Treasurer; Sanjay Mehra, Director of Engineering; Wade Lipscomb, Director of Operations; Erin Castellaw, Water Quality Manager. Authority counsel present by Zoom. Public: six in the room, three on Zoom.

2. PUBLIC COMMENT
Gary Lintz, Montvale, said the street repair over his water line on Old Montvale Road had settled about two inches since the spring and asked when it would be patched. The Executive Director said Operations would look at it this week.
Carol Ennis, Bedford (Grove Street), thanked the Oakwood/Edmund crews for keeping driveways open during the work and for the advance door hangers. She asked whether the brick sidewalk at the corner of Grove and Oakwood would be relaid to match. Staff will follow up.
Phil Dorsey, Moneta, asked whether the Authority would put the board packet on the website earlier than the Friday before the meeting. The Chair said the Board would take that up when it reviews its operating policies.
No written comments were received.

3. APPROVAL OF MINUTES
Minutes of the July 21, 2026 regular meeting. Ms. Quarles noted a typo in item 6 (the Waterlick Road booster should read "rehab," not "rehib"). Motion by Mr. Hylton, second by Ms. Thacker, to approve as corrected. Carried 7-0.

4. FINANCIAL REPORT (JUNE 2026 AND FY2026 PRELIMINARY YEAR-END)
Mr. Ashworth presented the June report and preliminary, unaudited results for the fiscal year ended June 30, 2026. June operating revenue was $2.06 million. For the year, operating revenue was $26.1 million against a budget of $25.8 million, mainly because of a dry June and higher irrigation use in the Lakes area. Operating expenses were $23.9 million, about 1.6 percent under budget; treatment chemicals and electricity ran over budget and were offset by vacancy savings. Days of cash on hand at June 30: 218. Debt service coverage is estimated at 1.62 times, above the 1.20 required by the bond covenants.
Mr. Price asked that the audited figures be compared with these preliminary numbers when the audit is presented. Mr. Ashworth agreed.
Motion by Mr. Price, second by Ms. Okafor, to accept the report. Carried 7-0.

5. ENGINEERING - PROJECT UPDATES
Oakwood/Edmund 8-inch replacement (WS-26-12): about 2,100 of 2,600 feet of new main installed and in service. Service pipes on the Authority side of the meter are being renewed in copper as the work moves. The contractor expects substantial completion by the end of September, followed by paving and a punch list. A break on the old Oakwood Street main on August 28 is noted under Operations; that section is in the replacement area.
Sunset Ridge recoating: Mr. Mehra reported that the contractor's work was accepted on July 30 after the final walk-through. The closeout package (as-builts, warranty, and the contractor's final release) is complete. Staff recommends approval of the final pay application of $41,280, which includes release of retainage. Motion by Mr. Ferrell, second by Ms. Quarles, to approve final payment. Carried 7-0.
Central WTP filters 3 and 4 (WS-26-05): contract executed; media delivery set for late September.
Route 460 corridor phase 2 (WS-26-02): design at 75 percent; easement offers out to the remaining owners.

6. PERSONNEL COMMITTEE - EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR SEARCH
Ms. Thacker reported that the position closed July 31 with 23 applications. The committee screened the applicants with the help of Ms. Halvorsen and selected four finalists, who will be interviewed in person on August 26 and 27. The committee expects to bring a recommendation to the full Board at the September 15 meeting. The Executive Director confirmed that his retirement takes effect September 4 and offered to help with the transition. The Chair thanked him, and members joined in.
Mr. Price asked that the full Board be given the finalists' resumes before the September meeting. Ms. Thacker said the committee would share them in confidence through the Secretary.

7. OPERATIONS REPORT
Mr. Lipscomb reported four main breaks in July and two so far in August, including a 6-inch split on Cottontown Road on August 3 that left 31 customers out for six hours; a precautionary boil water advisory for those customers was lifted August 5. The hydrant replacement program has started for FY2027, with 6 of 40 hydrants replaced; the crew is working through Bedford Central first. He reported that summer demand peaked on July 28 and that all plants kept up.

8. WATER QUALITY
Ms. Castellaw reported that all July compliance samples met standards. Field verification for the lead service line inventory continues on weekdays; no lead has been found.

9. COMMITTEE REPORTS
The Finance Committee did not meet in August. The Policies and Projects Committee will meet September 8 at 5:30 p.m. if needed.

10. ADJOURNMENT
Motion by Ms. Okafor, second by Mr. Ferrell, to adjourn. Carried 7-0. Adjourned at 8:06 p.m.

Teresa Halvorsen, Secretary
