## Recommendation
The Personnel Committee (Ms. Thacker, chair, and Mr. Harrelson) reviewed the 2027 holiday schedule by phone on October 8 and recommends that the Board adopt it as shown below. The schedule follows Personnel Policy 4.10: eleven paid holidays plus one floating holiday for each full-time employee. No change to the number of holidays is proposed.

Suggested motion: "I move to adopt the 2027 holiday schedule as presented."

## Notes on the dates
1. **Weekend holidays.** Under Policy 4.10, a holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed the Friday before, and one that falls on a Sunday is observed the Monday after. In 2027 this moves Juneteenth to Friday, June 18 and Independence Day to Monday, July 5.
2. **Christmas.** Christmas Eve falls on a Friday and Christmas Day on a Saturday. The committee recommends closing Thursday, December 23 and Friday, December 24, which keeps the two-day Christmas closing staff have had for many years and avoids opening for a single day before the weekend. The committee preferred this to closing Monday, December 27, a heavy payment day at the counter.
3. **New Year's Day 2028** falls on a Saturday. Under the policy it would be observed Friday, December 31, 2027. Because it belongs to the 2028 calendar year, it will come to the Board with the 2028 schedule next fall and is listed here only so staff can schedule leave.
4. **Days the office stays open.** As in 2026, the office will be open on Presidents Day (February 15, 2027) and Columbus Day (October 11, 2027). Presidents Day was replaced by the floating holiday in the 2019 policy revision. Election Day is not a holiday for the Authority; in 2027 it falls on Tuesday, November 2, which is also the first Tuesday, so the Finance Committee will meet as usual if needed.
5. **Floating holiday.** Taken in a full day between January 1 and December 15, 2027, approved in advance by the supervisor. It does not carry over. Employees hired after September 30 do not receive a floating holiday for that year.
6. **Board meetings.** None of the 2027 regular meeting dates (third Tuesdays) falls on a holiday. Packet deadlines that fall near Veterans Day and Thanksgiving are shown on the board meeting and packet calendar.

## Operations coverage
Holidays apply to the office, Engineering and day crews. The treatment facilities, the on-call distribution crew and the emergency line operate every day of the year. Treatment operators who work a holiday are paid under the overtime and holiday pay sections of Policy 4.10, and the Director of Operations sets the holiday rotation by December 1 for the following year. The bulk water fill station at the Operations yard is closed on Authority holidays.

## Budget effect
Holiday pay is included in the FY2027 personnel budget and will be carried into the FY2028 request. Adopting the schedule has no added expense. The number of holidays is the same as in 2026.
