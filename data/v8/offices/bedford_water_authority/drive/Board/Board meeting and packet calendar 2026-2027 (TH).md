# Board meeting and packet calendar, October 2026 - December 2027
Working copy, T. Halvorsen. Rev. 10/9/26. Shared with Joanne Pettus, department directors and the Chair.

Regular meetings are the **third Tuesday of each month at 7:00 p.m.** in the Authority Annex, 1723 Falling Creek Road, and on Zoom (Board Operating Policy 1.35). Public comment sign-up 6:30-6:55 p.m.; Zoom speakers register by noon the day of the meeting.

**Deadlines (standard):**
- *Agenda items* to the Secretary: Wednesday, 13 days before the meeting, noon.
- *Staff memos and reports* to the Secretary: Wednesday before the meeting, noon.
- *Packet posted* on bedfordwater.com and emailed to members: Friday before the meeting, by 3:00 p.m.
- *Draft minutes* to the Chair for review: two weeks after the meeting.
