# Board of Directors - roster and committees (2026)

Seven members, appointed by the Bedford County Board of Supervisors and the Town of Bedford Council for four-year terms ending December 31. Officers elected each December; committees appointed by the Chair. Regular meetings the third Tuesday of each month, 7:00 p.m., Authority Annex. Board members are paid a monthly stipend and reimbursed for mileage.

**Staff officers (not members)**

- Treasurer: Gordon Ashworth, Director of Finance

**Committees**
- Finance: budget preparation, financial policies, rate recommendations. Meets first Tuesday as needed, 5:30 p.m.
- Personnel: employment policies, staff matters with the Executive Director, annual review of the Executive Director, staff appeals. Meets as needed.
- Policies and Projects: all other policies, proposed construction projects, appeals from the public. Meets second Tuesday as needed, 5:30 p.m.
