# Billing and Collections - Desk Guide for Customer Service Staff
Rev. July 2026 (R. Dowdy). Summarizes Board policy on billing, payments, disconnection and adjustments. The Board policy controls if anything here differs.

## Billing
- Meters are read monthly and each account gets one statement a month. Half the cycles bill mid-month, half at month end.
- Statements go by mail or email, as the customer chooses, and show readings, usage and amount due.
- The statement is the past-due notice. We do not mail separate past-due notices.

## Payments
- Payment is due by the due date on the statement. Payments count when we receive them, not when they are mailed.
- A **late charge of 10 percent** of the past-due balance is added after the due date.
- Not getting a bill does not excuse a late payment. The customer should call for a duplicate.
- Ways to pay: online portal, autopay, phone (card, $3 convenience fee), drop box, counter (cash, check, money order, card), mail.

## Disconnection for nonpayment
- Accounts with a past-due balance on the cycle's disconnect date may be disconnected. Disconnect runs are on Wednesdays.
- Before a list goes to the field, Customer Service checks it against the **payment arrangement (PA) sheet** and removes every account that is current on an arrangement.
- Field staff leave a door hanger when service is shut off. They do not take payments or make arrangements in the field.
- After the full balance is paid, including late charges and the **$50 processing fee**, service is restored within one business day (usually the same day).
- Restoring service without our authorization is tampering (fee schedule applies).

## Payment arrangements
- The Customer Service Manager (or the supervisor when the manager is out) may set up a payment arrangement for a customer with extenuating circumstances, to avoid disconnection. The full balance is still owed, on an altered schedule.
- Record every arrangement on the account and on the PA sheet the same day.
- A customer who keeps up with an arrangement is not disconnected and is not charged late charges on the arranged balance.
- Customers who disagree with a decision on an arrangement or a charge may appeal in writing to the Director of Finance.

## Waivers
- Late charges may be waived **once every three years** for a customer in good standing (on-time payments, no late charges and no disconnection for nonpayment in the last 12 months). The customer must ask; it is not automatic. Record the waiver on the account.

## Billing disputes and refunds
- Customers should raise a disputed bill within 10 days of the billing date. We investigate, correct any error and explain.
- Refunds for our record errors go back up to three years and are credited to the account.

## Leak adjustments (customer side of the meter)
- The Authority owns the main, the service line to the meter and everything in the meter box. The customer owns and maintains the line from the meter box to the house and all plumbing.
- Up to three leak adjustments in six years, one billing cycle each, if billed usage is at least double the account's average and the request comes within 60 days. Receipt or proof of repair required. No adjustment for irrigation, fire lines or misuse.
- The customer should keep paying the bill while an adjustment is reviewed.

## Phone script reminders
- Always offer autopay and the portal.
- If a customer cannot pay by the disconnect date, offer to set up an arrangement BEFORE the date.
