Brandi, sorry about today. That's on me for not sending a heads-up to the counter. Here is what happened.

The billing software vendor pushed the update in Saturday night's maintenance window (live about 2 a.m. Sunday the 13th). The release notes said it "requires stronger credentials," which I read as new passwords only. It actually expired every existing password, so every user has to reset once. No account, payment history or AutoPay was changed.

What changed for customers:
- First sign-in after the 13th: click "Forgot password," get the email, set a new one (10 characters, at least one number).
- The reset link is good for 30 minutes and only the newest link works. If they clicked "Forgot password" three times, only the third email works. That is the "expired" message.
- Reset emails come from noreply@ the vendor's domain, not bedfordwater.com, so some are going to junk folders. Gmail and the cable company's email are the worst.
- AutoPay moved. It's now Payments > AutoPay settings. It used to be a tile on the home page. On a phone, tap the three-line menu first.

AutoPay drafts are not affected. Anyone who was enrolled is still enrolled and will draft on their due date. I checked the draft file for the 9/15 due date this afternoon and the count matches last month's (within 6).

I can resend a reset or unlock someone after five failed tries from my side. Send them to ext. 8. I'll take the overflow tomorrow.
