# Start and Stop Service - Counter Checklist
Rev. March 2026 (B. Coles). Holiday note added October 2026. For the front counter and phones. Charges are FY2027 (effective July 1, 2026); see the Rates and Fees summary in Finance for the full schedule.

## Before you start
- Look up the service address first, not the name. Note whether it has water only or water and sewer.
- Check for an open account at the address. If someone is still on it, the new customer's start date must be on or after the current customer's stop date.
- New construction that has never had a meter set is not a move-in. Send them to Engineering (ext. 3, engineering@bedfordwater.com).

## Starting service (move-in)
**What the customer brings**
- Photo ID (driver's license, state ID, passport, military ID).
- A signed lease showing the address and start date, or a deed, closing disclosure or purchase contract.
- A phone number and email address, and payment for the charges below.

**Charges**
- Application charge: $25 per account, added to the first bill (or paid at the counter).
- Deposits, residential: $125 water and $125 sewer (water-only addresses: $125). Nonresidential: $425 each.
- Deposits can be paid in full at sign-up or split over the first three bills.
- A deposit is credited back to the account after 12 straight months of on-time payment, or applied to the final bill.

**Steps**
1. Verify ID and lease or deed; scan both into the account.
2. Create the account (the system assigns the NN-NNNNN-NN number; the last two digits are the occupant number).
3. Enter the start date. We take a start read on that date or the next business day.
4. Offer AutoPay, paperless billing and text alerts. Hand them the portal card.
5. Give them the "Welcome" sheet (billing cycle, due date, who fixes what).
6. If the water is turned off at the meter, create a "turn on" work order for Crew 3. Same-day if the request is in by 2:00 p.m.; otherwise next business day. Someone 18 or older must be home so the crew can check that no faucets are open inside.

## Online start-service form
The start-service form on bedfordwater.com (Customer Service > Start or Stop Service) is available any time, including nights, weekends and holidays. Requests are processed the next business day in the order received. The customer uploads a photo of the ID and lease or deed. If anything is missing, contact them the same day; the request is held five business days, then closed.

## Stopping service (move-out)
1. Confirm the caller is the account holder (name, service address, last four of the phone number on file).
2. Enter the stop date. We take the final read on that date, or the next business day if it falls on a weekend or holiday. Customers do not need to be home for a final read.
3. Get a forwarding address for the final bill. The final bill is mailed or emailed within 5 business days of the read and applies the deposit.
4. If the customer is on AutoPay, tell them the final bill will draft on its due date and AutoPay then ends.
5. Do not turn off the water at the meter on a move-out unless the owner asks (vacant house, winter). Owner requests go on a work order to Crew 3.

## Transfers (moving within our service area)
- Stop the old account and start the new one in the same visit. No new application charge on a transfer.
- The deposit moves to the new account if the old one is paid in full; otherwise it is applied to the final bill and a new deposit is due.
- Remind them to re-register the new account in the portal and re-enroll in AutoPay.

## Landlord accounts
- Landlords may sign a **Landlord Continuous Service Agreement** for each rental address. When a tenant stops service, the account rolls into the landlord's name the next day and the water stays on. The landlord gets the bill for the vacant period.
- Tenants open their own accounts; we do not put a tenant's usage on a landlord's account unless the landlord asks in writing.
- Landlords cannot see a tenant's account or balance; they may ask whether an account is open at the address.

## Final reads and readings disputes
- A customer self-read with a photo of the dials may be entered as a customer read and flagged for the next route read.
- If a new customer disputes the start read, check the handheld photo; if none, send a reader for a free check read within the first 30 days.

## Common questions
- **"Can I start service by phone?"** Yes, but we need the ID and lease or deed (email, upload or counter) before the account is active.
- **"The seller hasn't stopped service."** Start the buyer on the closing date; the seller's account stops the same day once we see the closing documents.
