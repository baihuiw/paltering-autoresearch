# Phone tree and after-hours script
Rev. 9/2026 (B. Coles, D. Sowers). Replaces the 2024 version. Main number (540) 586-7210. Daytime menu runs Mon-Fri 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.; after 5:00, on weekends and on Authority holidays every call goes to the after-hours greeting and option 9 rings the answering service operator. Kayla re-recorded the greetings 9/30.

## Daytime menu
Greeting: "Thank you for calling the Bedford Regional Water Authority. If this is a water or sewer emergency, such as a main break, no water, or sewage backing up into your home, press 9 now. If you know the number of the person you are calling, you may dial it at any time."

| Press | Goes to | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Service queue (ext. 1) | Bills, start/stop service, meter reads, leak adjustments, bulk water accounts |
| 2 | Automated pay-by-phone | Card or bank draft; $3 card convenience charge; account number needed |
| 3 | Engineering (ext. 3) | New service, availability letters, plan review, capital projects |
| 4 | Finance (ext. 4) | Vendors, accounts payable, purchasing |
| 5 | Operations (ext. 5) | Crews, bulk fill station appointments, pump stations |
| 6 | Communications (ext. 6) | Website, Facebook, alerts, newsletter |
| 7 | Water Quality (ext. 7) | Lead service line inventory, testing, cross-connection |
| 8 | Portal and AutoPay help (ext. 8) | Derek Sowers; rolls to ext. 1 if no answer in 4 rings |
| 9 | Emergency | Rings Operations dispatch in the day; answering service after hours |
| 0 | Front counter | Rolls to the ext. 1 queue after 5 rings |

Callers in the Customer Service queue are offered a callback at 8 minutes. Callbacks must be returned the same business day.

## After-hours greeting
"You have reached the Bedford Regional Water Authority. Our offices are closed. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. To pay your bill, press 2 or visit bedfordwater.com. For a water or sewer emergency, press 9 to reach our operator. For all other questions, please call back during office hours or email customerservice@bedfordwater.com."

## Operator script (answering service)
Open with: "Bedford Water emergency line, this is [name]. What is the address where you're having the trouble?"

Always collect: caller name, callback number, street address and nearest cross street, what they see, when it started, and whether there is a traffic hazard. Read the address back.

**Page the on-call crew now (call the standby phone, then text the ticket):**
- Water running in the street or from the ground, or a main break.
- No water at the address, or several neighbors with no water. Ask whether the neighbors on either side have water; note it on the ticket either way.
- Very low pressure on a whole street.
- Sewage backing up into a home or coming out of a manhole or cleanout.
- A hydrant knocked over or damaged **and leaking**, or any hydrant hit in a road where it is a traffic hazard.
- A meter box lid missing in a yard or sidewalk where someone could step in (trip hazard).
If the standby person does not answer in 10 minutes, call the backup on the list (Rusty Tharpe, then Wade Lipscomb). Tell the caller: "I've sent this to our on-call crew. They will call you or come out. If the water is coming into your house, you can turn off the valve where the water line comes into the house."

**Take a message for the next business day (do not page):**
- Billing questions, high bills, payment questions, autopay or portal problems.
- Concerns about a meter reader: gate left open, a reader in the yard, a dog card left, a route question. Message goes to Brandi Coles (ext. 12).
- A hydrant knocked over or damaged but **not leaking** and not in the road. Next-day work order to Crew 1 (hydrants).
- Discolored water during the scheduled flushing (Bedford Central Oct 19-30, Forest Central Nov 2-13). Read: "Crews are flushing hydrants in your area. Run your cold tap 3 to 5 minutes until it clears. If it is still discolored in the morning, call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210 and press 1." Do not page for flushing calls unless the caller also has no water.
- Start or stop service, bulk water, new construction, meter rereads, project or paving questions.
Message tickets go to customerservice@bedfordwater.com.

**Never:** give out staff cell numbers, promise an arrival time, discuss an account balance, or say whether a problem is on their side or ours. Media calls: take name and number and pass to Teresa Halvorsen next morning; if it is about an active emergency, page the standby.

## Holidays and weather
On closed holidays (Nov 11, Nov 26-27, Dec 24-25) the system plays the after-hours greeting all day. Option 2 and option 9 work on every day of the year.
