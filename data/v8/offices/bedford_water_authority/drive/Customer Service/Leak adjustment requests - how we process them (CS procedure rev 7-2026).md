# Leak adjustment requests - how we process them
Customer Service procedure, rev. July 2026 (R. Dowdy, B. Coles). Board billing policy controls if anything here differs.

## Who qualifies
- The leak was on the owner's side of the meter: the line from the meter box to the house, or plumbing and fixtures inside or outside the home. Leaks at the meter or on our side of it are a billing correction, not a leak adjustment; route those to the supervisor.
- Billed usage for the cycle is **at least double the account's average** (200 percent).
- The request comes in **within 60 days** of the billing date of the statement with the high usage.
- The account has had **no more than two** leak adjustments in the past six years. The third one is the last until the oldest one is more than six years old.
- One billing cycle per leak (if it ran across two, adjust the higher one).
- The leak has been repaired, with proof (below).
- Not eligible: watering, filling a pool or garden pond, a hose or outdoor faucet left running, fire lines, or any usage we would call misuse. A broken pipe in an irrigation system that has been repaired is reviewed like any other leak.

## Intake
1. Customer contacts us by phone (ext. 1), email, the contact form ("Billing / My account"), the drop box or the counter.
2. Give them the one-page **Leak Adjustment Request** form: account number, service address, what leaked, date found, date fixed, signature.
3. Attach the proof of repair. Scan everything into the account under Documents > Leak adj.
4. Log the request on the leak adjustment sheet the same day (date, name, area, cause, average and billed gallons). The sheet is the queue.
5. Tell the customer: keep paying the bill as usual while we review, and the credit, if approved, will show on a future statement.

## What counts as proof of repair
- A **repair receipt or invoice** from a licensed contractor showing the service address, date and work done.
- If the owner did the repair, receipts for the parts (dated, with the store name) plus a short signed statement of what was fixed. Photos help.
- A follow-up meter read showing usage back to normal (no repeat trip charge for this read).
No proof, no adjustment. Send the "Need more information" letter and hold the request 30 days; then close it (the customer may resubmit within the 60-day window).

## How we calculate the credit
The leak cycle is rebilled as if the account had used its **average usage**.
- **Average usage** = the average of the 12 billing cycles before the leak, excluding any earlier leak cycle. Under three months of history, use 4,000 gallons.
- **Standard credit:** the gallons above average at the combined water and sewer volume rate ($6.85 + $8.95 = $15.80 per 1,000 gallons for FY2027). This covers most indoor leaks: relief valves, crawlspace pipes, fixtures.
- **Accounts with water only** (no sewer service, or a leak on the line outside the house noted as water only by the supervisor) get the water portion only, at $6.85 per 1,000 gallons.
- **Irrigation and outdoor lines** on an account without a separate irrigation meter are credited at half the combined rate ($7.90 per 1,000 gallons), since part of that water would have been used anyway.
- Base charges are never adjusted. Late charges on the leak portion are removed if the customer kept paying the rest of the bill.

Example: average 4,000 gallons, billed 13,000 gallons, water heater relief valve. Excess 9,000 gallons x $15.80 = $142.20 credit. The same 9,000 gallons on an outdoor irrigation line: 9,000 x $7.90 = $71.10.

## Approval levels
| Credit amount | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to $250 | Customer Service Representative, checked by the Supervisor the same week |
| $250.01 - $750 | Customer Service Supervisor (Brandi Coles) |
| $750.01 - $2,000 | Customer Service Manager (Renee Dowdy) |
| Over $2,000 | Director of Finance (Gordon Ashworth) |

Anything unusual (commercial accounts, requests past 60 days with a good reason) goes to the Manager regardless of amount.

## Turnaround
Requests are reviewed in the order received. Target: **10 business days from the date we have the repair receipt**. The credit posts to the account and appears on the next statement. Do not issue refund checks for leak credits unless the account is closed.

## Letters (templates in the shared CS templates folder)
- **LA-1 Approved:** credit, gallons adjusted, which statement, adjustments remaining.
- **LA-2 Not eligible:** which rule was not met, plus the appeal sentence.
- **LA-3 Need more information:** what is missing, 30-day hold.
- **LA-4 Our side of the meter:** billing correction amount.
Email if we have an address on file, otherwise mail; note the letter on the account.

## Status checks
Customers can check on a request by calling ext. 1 or emailing customerservice@bedfordwater.com with the account number. Give the date received, place in the queue and expected decision date. Do not quote a credit amount until it is approved.

## Appeals
A customer who disagrees may appeal in writing to the Director of Finance within 30 days of our letter. Finance answers in writing within 15 business days.
