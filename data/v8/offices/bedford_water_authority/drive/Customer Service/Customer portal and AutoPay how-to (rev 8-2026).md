# My Water Account portal and AutoPay - how-to for customers and counter staff
Rev. August 2026 (D. Sowers / B. Coles). Screens and menu names updated 9/17/2026 after the September 13 portal update, which moved the AutoPay link and asked every user to reset their password once. Kayla posted a shorter version under Pay My Bill > Help.

## What the portal does
The portal shows statements back to January 2023 and monthly usage, and handles payments, AutoPay, paperless billing and alerts. It does not start or stop service or change the name on an account.

## Step 1 - Get to the portal
1. Go to bedfordwater.com.
2. Click the blue **Pay My Bill** button at the top right of any page. This opens the My Water Account portal in a new tab.
3. First-time users click **Register**. Returning users sign in with their email address and password.

## Step 2 - Register
You need three things from a recent statement:
- **Account number**, top right of the statement, in the format NN-NNNNN-NN (example: 21-04417-01). Type the dashes. The last two digits are the occupant number; they change when a new person moves into the same address, so an old statement from a previous home will not work.
- **Last name on the account**, spelled as it prints on the statement. For a business, use the first word of the business name.
- **Service ZIP code**, the ZIP where the water is used (24523, 24551, 24121, 24556 and so on), not the mailing ZIP if they are different.

Then choose a password (10 or more characters, one number) and confirm the email address; that link is good for 24 hours.

## Step 3 - Set up AutoPay
1. Sign in.
2. Click **Payments** in the menu bar, then **AutoPay settings**. AutoPay is not on the home page anymore; this is the number one question since September 13.
3. Choose **Bank draft** (checking or savings, routing and account number) or **Card**.
4. Read the terms and click **Enroll**. The portal shows a green "AutoPay active" banner and sends a confirmation email.

Good to know:
- Bank draft AutoPay is free. Card AutoPay carries the $3 convenience charge on each payment, the same as any card payment.
- The payment drafts on the due date shown on the statement, not the day the bill is mailed.
- Sign up at least **5 business days before the due date**. If it is closer than that, pay the current bill another way; AutoPay starts with next month's bill. The portal shows "AutoPay begins with your next statement" when this happens.

## Paper AutoPay form
Customers who would rather not use the portal can fill out the paper AutoPay form at the counter or by mail. Attach a voided check (not a deposit slip). Counter staff scan the form into the account the same day; the same 5-business-day rule applies. Forms by mail go to 1723 Falling Creek Road, Bedford, VA 24523, Attn: AutoPay.

## Paperless billing and alerts
- Paperless: **Profile > Bill delivery**, choose Email only. The paper statement stops starting with the next billing cycle.
- Alerts: **Profile > Alerts** for text and email notices about main breaks, flushing and service updates in your area, and a reminder 5 days before the due date.

## Troubleshooting
**"I can't find AutoPay."** Payments > AutoPay settings. On a phone, tap the three-line menu at the top left first; Payments is the second item.

**"It says my account number is not valid."** Check the format NN-NNNNN-NN with dashes, and check the last two digits against the newest statement. Customers often type the old occupant number from a previous address. If it still fails, look up the account at the counter and read the number back to them. Do not read the number to a caller who cannot give the service address and name on the account.

**Password reset.** Click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page; the reset link is good for 30 minutes and only the newest link works. Since the September 13 update every user has to reset once, even if their old password was correct. If the reset email does not arrive in 10 minutes, check the junk folder, then call Derek Sowers (ext. 8), who can resend it or unlock the login after five failed tries.

**AutoPay on a closed account.** When a customer moves out, AutoPay drafts the final bill on its due date and then ends on its own. It does not carry over to a new address. If the final bill includes a deposit credit and comes out to zero or a credit, nothing drafts.

**Moving within our service area.** Close the old account and open the new one (see the start and stop service checklist), then register the new account number in the portal and enroll in AutoPay again. The portal can hold both accounts under one login during the overlap; use **Profile > Linked accounts**.
