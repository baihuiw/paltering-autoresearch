# WS-26-12 Bedford Central 8-inch replacement, Oakwood St / Edmund St
## Paving and restoration punch list - working copy 10/7/26 (SM)

Substantial completion was 9/30/26. New 8-inch DI main is in service on both streets (Oakwood St from Grove St to Center St, about 1,450 ft; Edmund St from Oakwood St to Bridge St, about 1,120 ft). All 64 services were renewed with copper from the main to the meter box; the old 4-inch cast iron main is abandoned in place. What is left is surface restoration. Walked the job 10/6 with the contractor's superintendent and the Town street inspector. Replaces the 10/1 draft.
