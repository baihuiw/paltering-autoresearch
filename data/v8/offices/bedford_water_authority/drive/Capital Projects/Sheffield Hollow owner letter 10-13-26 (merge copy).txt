Thank you for signing on to the Sheffield Hollow Water Line Extension. With 38 participating properties, this project will bring public water to homes along Sheffield Hollow Road, Pine Gap Lane and Corbin Ridge Road for the first time, and many of you have told us how much it is needed after the last two dry summers.

Construction bids for the project were opened on September 3, 2026. Construction bids came in higher than the engineer's estimate, and the participation fee has been updated to reflect the bid results. The Board of Directors will consider award of the construction contract to Craddock Utility Contractors, Inc. at its regular meeting on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. at the Authority Annex. The meeting is open to the public.

Under the participation agreement, the project is funded by a Drinking Water State Revolving Fund loan through the Virginia Department of Health, which covers 75 percent of construction cost, and by the participating owners, who share the remaining 25 percent equally.

Your revised estimated participation fee is $9,658.

You may pay the fee in full before your service is connected, or in 120 monthly installments of $80.48 added to your water bill, with no interest. Capital recovery, tap and meter fees for your connection are covered by the project.

What we need from you: please sign and return the enclosed revised participation agreement by Friday, November 13, 2026, in the envelope provided or at the front counter. If we do not receive it, we will contact you before the contract is signed.

Construction is expected to begin in early 2027 and take about eight months. We will send a schedule and a contact sheet for the contractor before work starts.
