# FY2027 Capital Improvement Program - Wastewater project sheets
Adopted by the Board May 19, 2026 with the FY2027 budget. Water projects are in a separate section.

---

### WW-27-01 Central WWTP influent screen replacement
Replace the 2004 mechanical bar screen and washer compactor. Budget $385,000. Funding: revenue bonds 2025. Schedule: bid winter 2026-27, install spring 2027.

### WW-27-02 Collection system I&I study, Town basins 3 and 4
Flow monitoring and smoke testing to find inflow and infiltration. Budget $120,000. Funding: operating. Schedule: monitoring fall 2026 through spring 2027.

### WW-27-03 Manhole rehabilitation program
Line or replace about 40 manholes a year, worst-first from the inspection database. Budget $210,000. Funding: operating. Ongoing.

### WW-27-04 Pump station generators (4 sites)
Permanent standby generators at four small pump stations that now rely on a portable unit. Budget $260,000. Funding: capital reserve. Schedule: FY2027.

### WW-27-05 Forest area force main air release valves
Replace 18 air release valves on the Forest force mains. Budget $95,000. Funding: operating. Schedule: fall 2026.

### WW-27-06 Mill Branch pump station odor control
Neighbors on Fenwick Drive and nearby streets have reported sewer odor at the Mill Branch pump station for two years, worst on still evenings. A carbon scrubber unit with a fan on the wet well vent will treat the air leaving the station. Budget $94,000 including installation. Funding: capital reserve. Schedule: order summer 2026; **install fall 2026**. Interim: wet well cleaning monthly.

### WW-27-07 SCADA at small pump stations
Add alarm and run-time telemetry at nine small stations. Budget $140,000. Funding: capital reserve. Schedule: FY2027-28.

### WW-27-08 Grinder pump replacement stock
Purchase 60 replacement grinder pumps for the low-pressure sewer areas. Budget $165,000. Funding: operating. Ongoing.

---
Project costs are planning estimates and may change with bids. The Board may amend the CIP during the year.
