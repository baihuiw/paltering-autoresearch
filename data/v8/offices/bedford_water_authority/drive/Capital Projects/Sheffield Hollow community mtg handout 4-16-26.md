# Sheffield Hollow Water Line Extension
## Community meeting handout - April 16, 2026, 7:00 p.m., fire hall

**What is proposed:** About 2.4 miles of new 8-inch water main along Sheffield Hollow Road, Pine Gap Lane and Corbin Ridge Road, with six fire hydrants and a service connection and meter for each participating property.

**Who can participate:** Owners of the 52 properties along the route. The project goes ahead if at least 35 owners sign participation agreements by June 30.

**How it is paid for:**
- The Authority is applying for a Drinking Water State Revolving Fund (DWSRF) loan through the Virginia Department of Health to cover 75 percent of construction cost.
- Participating owners share the other 25 percent equally.
- Your tap, meter and capital recovery fees are included.

**Estimated cost to each owner:** Based on the engineer's estimate of $1,030,000 for construction, the owner share would be about **$6,776 per property** (about $6,800) if 38 owners participate. **This is an estimate.** The final fee depends on the construction bids and on the number of participating owners. If bids come in higher or lower, the fee changes, and you will be asked to sign a revised agreement before the contract is awarded. You may withdraw at that time.

**Paying the fee:** In full before connection, or in 120 monthly installments on your bill with no interest.

**Schedule (estimated):**
- Sign-up through June 30, 2026
- Final design and state review: summer 2026
- Bids: late summer 2026
- Construction: about eight months after award

**After connection:** You may keep your well for outdoor use only if it is physically separated from the house plumbing and inspected by the Authority.
