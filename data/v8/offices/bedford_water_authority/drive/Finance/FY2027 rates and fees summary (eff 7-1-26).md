# FY2027 Water and Sewer Rates and Fees - Summary
Effective for usage beginning July 1, 2026. Adopted by the Board on May 19, 2026 after a public hearing. Same rates in the Town and the County.

## Monthly base charges (by meter size)
| Meter | Water | Sewer |
|---|---|---|
| 5/8 inch | $30.50 | $30.50 |
| 3/4 inch | $43.00 | $43.00 |
| 1 inch | $70.50 | $70.50 |
| 1-1/2 inch | $122.00 | $122.00 |
| 2 inch | $195.00 | $195.00 |

## Volume charges (per 1,000 gallons)
| Customer type | Water | Sewer |
|---|---|---|
| Residential and commercial | $6.85 | $8.95 |
| Industrial | $6.05 | $8.15 |
| Bulk fill station | $12.50 | n/a |

**Typical residential bill** (5/8-inch meter, 4,000 gallons, water and sewer): $30.50 + $30.50 + 4 x ($6.85 + $8.95) = **$124.20 a month** (FY2026: $119.60).

## Other charges
- Late charge: 10 percent of past-due balance
- Processing fee (reconnection after disconnection for nonpayment): $50
- Application fee (new account): $25
- Deposits: residential $125 water, $125 sewer; nonresidential $425 each
- Card payment convenience fee: $3 per transaction
- Meter test at customer request: $50 (no charge if the meter is found defective)
- Repeat service trip (re-read, multiple trips): $20
- Returned payment: $35
- Small grinder pump maintenance fee: $35 a month

## New connection fees (5/8-inch residential)
- Capital recovery fee: water $5,250; sewer $6,200
- Tap fee: water $1,650; sewer $2,600
- Meter fee: $290

Larger meters: see the full Rate Policy or call Engineering.
