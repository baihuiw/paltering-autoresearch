# Monthly Financial Report - August 2026
Prepared by Gordon Ashworth, Director of Finance and Treasurer, for the October 20, 2026 Board packet. Dated October 8, 2026. Unaudited. Two months (16.7 percent) of the fiscal year have passed. FY2027 budget: $27,400,000.

## Summary
- August operating revenue was **$2.02 million**, 3.6 percent above August 2025 ($1.95 million), and 99.1 percent of the monthly budget.
- Year-to-date operating revenue (July-August) is $3.96 million, 97.2 percent of the straight-line budget. Summer usage billed in September and October usually closes most of the gap.
- Expenses are within budget overall. Electricity, chemicals and repairs are running ahead of straight-line, as they do every summer.
- Days of cash on hand at August 31: **211** (214 at July 31).
- Preliminary September operating revenue is $1.97 million; the full September report comes in the November 17 packet.

## Cash and days on hand
Unrestricted cash and investments at August 31 were **$11,041,000**. Days of cash on hand = unrestricted cash divided by one day of budgeted operating expense, excluding debt service and capital ($19,100,000 / 365 = $52,329 a day) = **211 days**. The Board's policy minimum is 180 days.
