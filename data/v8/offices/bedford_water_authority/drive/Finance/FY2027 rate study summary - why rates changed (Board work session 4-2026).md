# FY2027 Rate Study Summary: Why Rates Changed on July 1, 2026
Prepared by Gordon Ashworth, Director of Finance, for the Board work session of Tuesday, April 7, 2026. Updated May 20, 2026 after the Board adopted the rates without changes. Addendum added October 7, 2026. Customer Service uses this sheet to answer rate questions.

## The short version
The typical residential bill (5/8-inch meter, 4,000 gallons a month, water and sewer) went from **$119.60 to $124.20**, an increase of **$4.60 a month, or 3.8 percent**, for usage beginning July 1, 2026. The Authority is funded only by what customers pay in bills and fees. We receive no tax money from the County or the Town, so when our costs go up, rates are the only way to cover them.

## What changed in the rate schedule
| Charge | FY2026 | FY2027 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Water base charge (5/8-inch), monthly | $29.50 | $30.50 | +$1.00 |
| Sewer base charge (5/8-inch), monthly | $29.50 | $30.50 | +$1.00 |
| Water volume, per 1,000 gallons | $6.55 | $6.85 | +$0.30 |
| Sewer volume, per 1,000 gallons | $8.60 | $8.95 | +$0.35 |

Fees for new connections, applications, deposits and meter tests did not change this year. The full schedule is in the FY2027 Rates and Fees summary.

## What is driving costs
The FY2027 budget is $27,400,000, up $1,020,000 (3.9 percent) from the FY2026 budget of $26,380,000.

| Cost driver | Increase |
|---|---|
| Debt service on the 2025 revenue bonds (first full year of principal) | $560,000 |
| 3 percent cost-of-living raise for staff | $222,000 |
| Treatment chemicals (up about 14 percent from FY2025 prices) | $165,000 |
| Electricity (up 6 percent) | $92,000 |
| Purchased water from Lynchburg (up 4 percent) | $53,000 |
| Other operating costs (parts, fuel, software, postage) | $138,000 |
| **Gross increase** | **$1,230,000** |
| Savings: two vacant positions left unfilled | ($145,000) |
| Savings: health insurance re-bid | ($65,000) |
| **Net increase in the budget** | **$1,020,000** |

The 2025 revenue bonds pay for four projects customers are already seeing: the media and underdrain replacement for filters 3 and 4 at the Central Water Treatment Plant, the Oakwood St and Edmund St 8-inch main replacement in Bedford, phase 2 of the 12-inch main along the Route 460 corridor, and the Sunset Ridge recoating.

## How the increase is paid for
| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| Rate increase (3.8 percent on water and sewer charges) | $864,000 |
| Customer growth (about 110 new accounts a year) | $71,000 |
| Higher interest earnings on reserves | $85,000 |
| **Total** | **$1,020,000** |

## What staff did to keep the increase down
- **Deferred the Lakes Central meter replacement** (cycle 2, project WS-26-09, about $900,000) to FY2028. Without the deferral, the increase would have been closer to 7 percent.
- **Left two vacant positions unfilled** (a utility worker and an accounting technician) for FY2027.
- **Re-bid health insurance** (2 percent increase instead of the 9 percent first quoted).

## What it means for your bill
| Monthly usage | FY2026 bill | FY2027 bill | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2,000 gallons (one or two people) | $89.30 | $92.60 | +$3.30 (3.7%) |
| 4,000 gallons (typical household) | $119.60 | $124.20 | +$4.60 (3.8%) |
| 6,000 gallons (larger household) | $149.90 | $155.80 | +$5.90 (3.9%) |
| 4,000 gallons, water only (no sewer) | $55.70 | $57.90 | +$2.20 (3.9%) |

All bills are for a 5/8-inch meter; at 4,000 gallons that is about 3.1 cents a gallon for water and sewer.

## How we compare
Staff compared bills with eleven water and sewer utilities in our region, using each utility's rates in effect July 1, 2026. At 4,000 gallons a month, the **regional average** combined bill is **$131.40**, with a range of about $98 to $172. Our typical bill of $124.20 is about 5 percent below the regional average. Utilities at the high end tend to be rural systems with long distances between customers. Our rates are the same in the Town and the County.
