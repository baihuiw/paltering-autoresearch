## Days the office is OPEN (not Authority holidays)
- **Presidents Day**, Monday, February 16: open (replaced by the floating holiday in 2019).
- **Columbus Day**, Monday, October 12: open. The bank and the post office are closed, so mail and deposits go out Tuesday.
- **Election Day**, Tuesday, November 3: open. Employees who are election officers get civic leave under Policy 4.14; tell your supervisor two weeks ahead.
- **New Year's Eve**, Thursday, December 31: open, regular hours.

## Floating holiday
- One full day per calendar year for full-time employees, taken between January 1 and December 15 with supervisor approval. Many staff use it for Presidents Day, a birthday or a religious holiday.
- It does not carry over and is not paid out at separation.
- Request it in the timekeeping system like annual leave, code FH. Customer Service and Operations supervisors may limit how many people take the same day.
- Employees hired after September 30 receive their first floating day the following January.

## Who works on holidays
- **Office, Customer Service, Engineering, Finance and day crews** are off.
- **Treatment operators** work the posted rotation. Holiday pay and the extra day off are handled under Policy 4.10, section 5.
- **On-call distribution crew** (rotation posted by Rusty Tharpe) answers main breaks and emergencies. D. Pugh has the on-call phone for Veterans Day this year.
- **Emergency line:** (540) 586-7210, press 9. Answered 24 hours a day, every day, by the operator on duty, who pages the on-call crew.
- **Bulk water fill station** at the Operations yard is closed on Authority holidays.
- **Drive-up drop box** is open as usual. Payments left on a holiday are posted the next business day and are not counted as past due if the due date fell on the holiday.

## How closings are announced
1. HR sends the adopted schedule to all staff each fall and posts it here.
2. Kayla Brumfield (Communications, ext. 6) puts closings in "On Tap" (the fall insert lists Nov 11, Nov 26-27 and Dec 24-25), on the website banner the week before, and on Facebook the day before.
3. Customer Service updates the phone hold message and the counter sign three business days ahead. Derek Sowers updates the portal banner.
4. Meter reading routes that fall on a holiday are read the next business day; Brandi Coles adjusts the route calendar.

## Inclement weather
The Executive Director (or Teresa Halvorsen if he is unavailable) decides whether to delay opening or close the office for snow or ice. The decision is made by **6:00 a.m.** whenever possible.
- **Notice to staff:** group text from Joanne Pettus and a recorded message on the staff information line (main number, then 99) by 6:30 a.m.
- **Notice to customers:** Facebook, website banner and the main phone greeting by 7:00 a.m.; text alerts to customers signed up for alerts.
- **Delayed opening:** office opens at 10:00 a.m. unless announced otherwise. Late arrivals within the delay are not charged leave.
- **Closing:** office staff are paid for the scheduled day. Staff who choose not to come in on a delayed-opening day may use annual leave.
- **Essential staff** (treatment operators, on-call crew, the operator on duty) report as scheduled regardless of office status.
- **Early release:** decided by noon when possible. The drop box remains open.

## Payment due dates near holidays
Due dates that fall on a closed day move to the next business day automatically in the billing system. No late charge applies if payment is received that next business day. Derek Sowers confirms the holiday table in the billing system each December.
