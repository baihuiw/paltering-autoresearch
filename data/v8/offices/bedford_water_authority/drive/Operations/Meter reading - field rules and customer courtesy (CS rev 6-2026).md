# Meter Reading: Field Rules and Customer Courtesy
_Customer Service, Meter Reading Section. Rev. 6/2026 (B. Coles). Every reader signs the last page at hire and again each July. Keep a copy in the truck._

---

## Who we are
The Authority has **four meter readers** in Customer Service, supervised by **Brandi Coles, Customer Service Supervisor (ext. 12)**. Together we read about **25,800 water meters every month on 22 routes.** For most customers the reader is the only Authority employee they ever see.

## Route basics
- Routes are read on the dates in the route calendar. Do not read a route early to get ahead; bills are built around a steady 28-32 day period.
- Load your handheld at the start of the day and sync at the end. Unread meters left on the handheld at sync go to Brandi the next morning.
- Example: **Route 14 (Wheat Ridge Rd and the Goode area)** is read around the 15th. October's read is **Thursday, October 15.**

## On the customer's property
1. **Close and latch every gate you open.** Every time, even if it was open when you arrived. Check that the latch caught before you walk away. A loose dog or a lost horse is our responsibility if we left the gate.
2. **Never enter a yard with a loose dog.** It does not matter if the dog looks friendly or the customer says it is. Skip the read, leave a **"We couldn't read your meter" card** on the door or gate, and code the read as "dog" on the handheld. The bill for that month is **estimated** from the account's history.
3. **Never cut through yards.** Use the driveway, walk or the road shoulder. Do not walk through flower beds, gardens or across a neighbor's property to save time.
4. **Wear the uniform and photo ID badge** at all times: Authority shirt or jacket, safety vest along roads, badge visible on the chest. If a customer asks who you are, show the badge and give the main number, (540) 586-7210, so they can call and confirm.
5. **Park legally.** No parking in driveways without permission, on lawns, across mailboxes, or on the wrong side of the road. Use flashers when stopped on a shoulder.
6. **Report damaged meter box lids** and any box that is buried, flooded or has a missing lid. Code it on the handheld so a work order goes to Crew 3 the same day.
7. Replace the lid fully seated. Do not go into a garage, shed, crawl space or house. Inside meters are read by appointment only.

## Estimated reads
- An estimate is a last resort. Before you code a skip, look for a safe way to read: from the road with the reading pole, or by knocking and asking the customer to put the dog up.
- Valid skip codes: dog, locked gate, vehicle on lid, buried/flooded box, unsafe access. Code exactly; "other" is not allowed without a note.
- Two estimates in a row on the same account trigger a call from Customer Service to the customer to arrange access. Three in a row go to Brandi.

## Customer notes on the account
Customers can call Customer Service (ext. 1) and ask to put a **"gate note"** or **"dog note"** on their account. The note shows on the route handheld when you reach that meter. Examples:
- "Gate code 2207, close behind you." "Use side gate, not front."
- "Dog in yard 7-9 a.m. weekdays; reads after 10 a.m. OK."
- "Customer will leave gate unlocked on read date."

If a note is out of date, tell Brandi so it can be updated.

## Talking with customers
- Be polite and brief. Introduce yourself and say you are reading the water meter.
- **If a customer asks you to do something** (check for a leak, explain a bill, move the meter, look at their pipes), do not promise anything. Give them the Customer Service number, ext. 1, and code a "customer request" on the handheld with a one-line description. Customer Service will call them back the same or next business day.
- If a customer says usage looks high, you may show them the dial and leak indicator, then refer them to Customer Service.
- Do not discuss other customers' accounts, bills or usage with anyone.
- If a customer is angry, do not argue. Thank them, leave, and tell Brandi before end of day.

## Gate and conduct concerns
Customer concerns about a reader (a gate left open, a dog let out, driving on a lawn, rudeness) go to **Brandi Coles**. She calls the customer back, talks with the reader and writes up what happened. **Repeat concerns are documented in the reader's file** and handled under the Authority's personnel policy. A gate left open that leads to a lost animal or property damage is reported to Wade Lipscomb and Teresa Halvorsen the same day.

## Safety
- Watch for snakes, wasps and fire ants in meter boxes, especially August through October. Lift lids with the lid hook, never your fingers.
- Do not read during lightning. Get in the truck and wait 30 minutes after the last strike.
- If a person threatens you, leave immediately and call Brandi or 911.
- Report every near miss, bite, sting or vehicle bump to Brandi the same day and to the monthly safety committee.
