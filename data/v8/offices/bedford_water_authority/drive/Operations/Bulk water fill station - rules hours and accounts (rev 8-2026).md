# Bulk Water Fill Station
### Rules, hours and accounts for haulers and bulk customers
_Bedford Regional Water Authority. Rev. 8/2026 (FY2027 rate; card reader replacement note added 10/2026). Hand out at the Customer Service counter and post at the station kiosk._

---

## Where and when
- **Location:** Operations yard gate, 1723 Falling Creek Road, Bedford. Enter by the **side drive past the Authority Annex**; do not use the main office lot. Follow the painted lane to the fill bay.
- **Hours:** Monday-Friday, **7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.** Closed on Authority holidays (this fall: Wednesday, November 11; Thursday-Friday, November 26-27; Thursday-Friday, December 24-25).

## Price
- **$12.50 per 1,000 gallons** (FY2027 rate, effective July 1, 2026).
- **500-gallon minimum per fill.** A fill under 500 gallons is charged as 500 gallons ($6.25).
- Charges come off your prepaid account balance at the end of each fill. The receipt prints at the kiosk and is emailed to the address on the account.

## You need a bulk water account
There are **no walk-up sales.** We do not sell water by cash, card or check at the station, and we do not fill buckets, jugs or loose containers of any kind.

To open an account, come to the **Customer Service counter** in the main office (Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.) with:
1. A completed Bulk Water Account Application (counter or bedfordwater.com, under Forms).
2. The **$25 application fee**.
3. A **$100 minimum prepaid balance**. You can add money at the counter, by phone with Customer Service at ext. 1, or in the My Water Account portal.
4. The make, model and plate number of each truck that will haul, and the capacity of each hauling container.

When the account is approved, you receive a **fill card and a 4-digit PIN.** Each truck gets its own card. Lost or damaged cards are replaced at the counter.

## Equipment check before your first fill
Before an account is activated, Operations staff check each truck at the yard. Call Operations at ext. 5 to set a time (usually the same week). Staff look for:
- **An air gap.** The fill hose must discharge above the top opening of your hauling container, with at least two hose diameters of open air and never less than one inch. No submerged fills and no hoses clamped into a fitting.
- **Potable-only use.** The container and hose must be used only for drinking water. **No container that has ever held chemicals, fuel, pesticides, fertilizer, milk, or wastewater** may be filled here, even if it has been cleaned.
- A clean, capped hose and a container with a tight lid.

Trucks that pass get a decal on the driver's side. Staff may re-check any truck at any time, and a truck that fails a re-check is suspended until it is corrected.

## At the station
1. Pull into the fill bay. One truck at a time; wait at the stop bar if the bay is in use.
2. Insert your card at the kiosk and enter your PIN.
3. Enter the gallons you want (500 minimum). The display shows your balance.
4. Position the hose over the opening with the air gap. Do not let the hose touch the water.
5. Press START. The fill stops automatically at the gallons you entered, or press STOP at any time.
6. Return the hose to its hook and take your receipt.

Do not adjust valves in the bay, leave a running hose unattended, or wash trucks at the station. Report damage to Operations, ext. 5.

## Swimming pools
Most of our water customers fill a pool **from their own tap.** If you do:
- You may ask Customer Service (ext. 1) for a **pool-fill sewer credit**, available **once per calendar year** per account. Because the water goes into the pool, not the sewer, we take the sewer charge off for those gallons.
- **Call before you start filling.** Staff will take a meter read before and after, so we know exactly how many gallons went into the pool. We cannot give the credit for a fill that was not read at the start.

You may also hire a **licensed water hauler**, who buys water at this station on their own bulk account and delivers it to you. The Authority does not deliver water.

## Account rules
- Keep at least $100 on your balance; the kiosk will not start a fill when your balance is below the cost of 500 gallons.
- A closed account's remaining balance is refunded by check within 30 days.
- Cards are not to be shared between companies. Card misuse, an equipment violation of the air gap or potable-only rule, or tampering with the station closes the account.
- During a drought watch or a main break, the Authority may limit fills.
