## What customers should do
Post this section as written:

1. **On your flushing day, avoid doing white laundry** until your cold water runs clear. Flushing can stir up rust-colored sediment that can stain clothes.
2. **If your water looks discolored**, run your **cold** tap (the bathtub is best) for **3 to 5 minutes**. If it is not clear, wait 30 minutes and try again. It usually clears within a few hours.
3. **Do not run hot water** until the cold is clear, so you do not pull sediment into your water heater.
4. **If the water is still discolored the next morning**, call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, ext. 1, and we will send a crew to flush the hydrant closest to you.
5. You may notice lower pressure for a short time while a hydrant on your street is open.

**Discolored water from flushing is not a health risk.** The color comes from iron and mineral deposits that settle in the mains over time.
