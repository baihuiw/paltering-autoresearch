FALL HYDRANT FLUSHING - BEDFORD CENTRAL, OCT 19-30

Our crews will flush fire hydrants in the Bedford Central area Monday, October 19 through Friday, October 30, weekdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Flushing clears sediment out of the water mains and lets us check that every hydrant works.

Mon Oct 19 - Peaks St, Bridge St, Main St (east of Bridge)
Tue Oct 20 - Grove St, Oakwood St, Edmund St
Wed Oct 21 - Macon St, Mountain Ave, Washington St, Center St
Thu Oct 22 - Blue Ridge Ave, Crumpacker Dr, Industrial Park area
Fri Oct 23 - Rt 460 business corridor
Mon Oct 26 - Fri Oct 30 - all other Bedford Central streets, and make-up days

What to expect: your water may look cloudy, yellow or rusty while crews work on your street. This is sediment stirred up in the main. Discolored water from flushing is not a health risk.
- Avoid doing white laundry on your flushing day and until your cold tap runs clear.
- If the water is discolored, run your cold tap for 3 to 5 minutes. It usually clears within a few hours.
- If it is still discolored the next morning, call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, ext. 1.

Forest Central flushing is Nov 2-13 (street list posted by Oct 30). Lakes and Stewartsville are flushed in the spring.
