## Boil water advisory notice (as sent 9/10, 10:20 a.m.)

> **BOIL WATER ADVISORY - Route 221 / Perrowville Rd area, Forest**
> Because of a water main break on Route 221, water pressure dropped in part of the system this morning. As a precaution, customers at the addresses listed (61 homes and businesses on Route 221 between Perrowville Rd and Thomas Jefferson Rd and nearby side streets) should **boil all water used for drinking, cooking, making ice, brushing teeth and preparing food**. Bring water to a rolling boil for one minute and let it cool.
> You do not need to boil water used for bathing, laundry or flushing.
> The Authority will collect water samples once service is restored. We will notify you when the advisory is lifted, which is usually within two days.
> Questions: Customer Service, (540) 586-7210, ext. 1. After hours, press 9.

## Lifting notice (as sent 9/12, 4:00 p.m.)

> **BOIL WATER ADVISORY LIFTED - Route 221 / Perrowville Rd area**
> Two rounds of water samples collected after the repair met all standards. The boil water advisory issued September 10 is lifted. You may use your tap water as usual.
> Before using it, run each cold water tap for a few minutes. Throw out ice made during the advisory, and run your dishwasher and refrigerator water dispenser through a cycle.
> Thank you for your patience. Questions: (540) 586-7210, ext. 1.

## After-action notes (KB, reviewed with W. Lipscomb and R. Tharpe 9/17)

**What worked**
- First alert went out 15 minutes after the first call from the crew. Good.
- Calling all 61 advisory customers and hanging door hangers by 11:30 a.m. caught people who don't use Facebook.
- Posting every two to three hours kept comments calm.

**What to fix**
- **Text alert signups are low in Forest.** Of the 140 customers out of water, only 23 were signed up for text or email alerts (16 percent). Systemwide signup is about 22 percent. Plan: alert signup push in the October bill message line, a Facebook post, and a sign-up card at the Oct 22 Forest community association meeting.
- Updates went to the whole Forest Central list and confused customers far from the break. Next time, target the affected streets with the map tool.
- Phone greeting was updated at 8:30, almost two hours in. Add "update phone greeting" to the main break checklist so CS does it with the first alert.
- Door hanger stock ran low; Crew 2 used the last 70. Reorder from Peaks Sign Shop (bilingual version this time).
- Need a ready-made advisory template with blanks for streets and count.
- Saturday lifting calls fell to one person. Line up two next time.

**Follow-up status (9/18):** door hangers reordered (500); advisory template drafted; checklist change sent to Lipscomb for approval.
