# Fall hydrant flushing begins in Bedford Central October 19

BEDFORD, Va. - Crews from the Bedford Regional Water Authority will flush fire hydrants throughout the Bedford Central service area from Monday, October 19, through Friday, October 30. Flushing will take place on weekdays from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., except for one night of flushing on the Route 460 business corridor.

Flushing sends a high-speed flow of water through the mains to clear out sediment and mineral deposits that settle over time. It also lets crews confirm that each hydrant opens, closes and drains properly, and that valves in the area work as they should. The Authority flushes each part of its system once a year. Bedford Central and Forest Central are flushed in the fall; Lakes Central and Stewartsville are flushed in the spring.

The work will be done by Crew 1, the Authority's hydrant crew, along with a second two-person flushing team.

## What customers may notice

While crews are flushing on or near your street, you may notice:

- Water that looks cloudy, yellow or rusty brown for a short time
- A brief drop in water pressure
- Air in the lines, which can make faucets sputter

Discolored water from flushing is caused by loosened sediment, mostly iron and manganese. It is not a health risk. It usually clears within a few hours.

## What to do

- **Avoid washing white laundry** on your street's flushing day and until your cold tap runs clear. Rust particles can stain light fabrics.
- **Run your cold water tap for 3 to 5 minutes** after crews leave your street. If the water is still discolored, wait about 30 minutes and try again.
- **Do not run hot water** until the cold water is clear, so sediment does not settle in your water heater.
- **Check faucet aerators** if water flow seems weak afterward. Unscrew the aerator, rinse out any grit and screw it back on.
- **If your water is still discolored the next morning**, call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, ext. 1. Staff will make a note on your account and ask a crew to check the nearest hydrant.

Please give crews room to work. Flushed water runs into the street and storm drains, and crew members may be standing near traffic. Drivers are asked to slow down near Authority trucks and to avoid driving through large puddles near open hydrants.

Customers who find a stained load of laundry can ask at the Customer Service counter for a free rust-stain remover packet. Do not use bleach on rust-stained clothing; it can set the stain.
