# Who Fixes What? Your Water Service Line
_Customer information sheet - Bedford Regional Water Authority. Rev. 2025._

**The Authority's side (we fix it, at no charge to you):**
- The water main in the street or easement
- The service line from the main to your meter
- The meter, the meter setter and everything inside the meter box
- Up to two feet of pipe outside the meter box on the house side

**Your side (you fix it):**
- The service line from the meter box to your house
- All plumbing inside the house, outdoor spigots and irrigation

**Think you have a leak?**
1. Turn off every faucet and water-using appliance in the house.
2. Look at the meter. If the dial or the leak indicator is moving, water is going somewhere.
3. Call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, ext. 1. We will come out and check the meter and the meter box at no charge.
4. If the leak is on our side, our crew will repair it. If it is on your side, we will tell you, and you will need a licensed plumber or contractor.

**Leak adjustments:** If a leak on your side caused a high bill, you may qualify for a leak adjustment (up to three in six years). Ask Customer Service within 60 days of the bill and keep your repair receipt.

**Before you dig:** Call 811 (Miss Utility) at least three working days before digging.

**Do not** open or work inside the meter box. Tampering fees apply.
