# Warren Kessinger begins as Executive Director of the Bedford Regional Water Authority

BEDFORD, Va. - Warren Kessinger, P.E., began work Monday as Executive Director of the Bedford Regional Water Authority. The Authority's Board of Directors voted on September 15 to hire Kessinger, who succeeds the previous executive director, who retired September 4.

Kessinger, a licensed professional engineer, comes to Bedford from a regional water authority in the Shenandoah Valley, where he spent 14 years and most recently served as deputy director of operations. He has managed treatment plant upgrades, a system-wide valve and hydrant program and the response to several major main breaks.

"Bedford is a beautiful place to live and work, and the people here deserve water service they never have to think twice about," Kessinger said. "My first priority is to listen: to customers, to our crews and operators, and to the Board. Every customer, wherever they live in our service area, should get the same straight answers and the same treatment from us."

Board Chair Lyle Harrelson said the Board was impressed by Kessinger's operations background and his focus on customers. "Warren has run the kind of system we are becoming, and he knows what our crews do every day. We are glad to have him." Harrelson also thanked Assistant Executive Director Teresa Halvorsen for serving as acting executive director.

Kessinger and his wife moved to Forest this month. He will spend his first weeks visiting each plant and crew and will give his first report to the Board at its October 20 meeting.

The Bedford Regional Water Authority provides water and sewer service to customers in the Town of Bedford and Bedford County. Board meetings are held the third Tuesday of each month at 7:00 p.m. at the Authority Annex, 1723 Falling Creek Road, and are open to the public.
