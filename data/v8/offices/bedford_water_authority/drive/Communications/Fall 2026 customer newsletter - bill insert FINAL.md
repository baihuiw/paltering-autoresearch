## Fall hydrant flushing
Crews will flush hydrants in **Bedford Central Oct 19-30** and **Forest Central Nov 2-13**, weekdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Flushing clears sediment from the mains. Your water may look cloudy or rusty for a short time; run your cold tap for a few minutes until it clears, and avoid doing white laundry while crews are on your street. Lakes and Stewartsville are flushed in the spring.

## Having trouble paying? Call us before your disconnect date
If you cannot pay your full balance by the disconnect date on your statement, call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, ext. 1 **before** that date. We can often set up a payment arrangement so your service stays on. Customers in good standing can also ask for a one-time waiver of a late charge.

## Sign up for autopay and paperless billing
More than 4,100 customers now pay automatically. Sign up on our customer portal at bedfordwater.com or at the front counter. Autopay comes out on your due date, so you never pay late.

## Know your line
We maintain the water main, the service line up to your meter and everything in the meter box. You own the line from the meter box to your house and all your plumbing. If your meter dial moves when everything in the house is off, you may have a leak on your side. Call us and we will check the meter for free. See our "Who fixes what" sheet on our website.

## Lead service line inventory
We are field-checking service lines where our records do not show the material. So far we have verified more than 2,000 and found **no lead service lines**. You can look up your address on the inventory map on our website.

## Fats, oils and grease
Holiday cooking season is coming. Please don't pour grease down the drain. Let it cool, scrape it into a can and put it in the trash. Grease is the number one cause of sewer backups.
