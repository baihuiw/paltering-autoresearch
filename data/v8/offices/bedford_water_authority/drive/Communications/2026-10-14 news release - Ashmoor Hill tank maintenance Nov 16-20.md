# Scheduled maintenance at Ashmoor Hill water tank, November 16-20

FOREST, Va. - The Bedford Regional Water Authority will perform scheduled maintenance on the Ashmoor Hill water storage tank the week of November 16-20, 2026, as part of its regular tank inspection and maintenance program.

The Authority inspects each of its storage tanks on a five-year cycle using an underwater remotely operated vehicle. Following this fall's inspection, the Ashmoor Hill tank will be drained, cleaned and disinfected, and roof fittings will be serviced, by the Authority's tank contractor.

During the work, customers in the Briar Glen and Ashmoor Estates neighborhoods will be served directly from the Ashmoor booster station. Customers may notice brief changes in water pressure. Water service is not expected to be interrupted.

Customers in the area may also notice a slight chlorine taste or smell over the coming weeks. The Authority has adjusted disinfection in the Ashmoor zone as a routine precaution, and staff are taking additional water samples in the neighborhood. Water continues to meet all state and federal drinking water standards.

"Keeping our tanks clean and in good repair is one of the most important things we do," said Executive Director Warren Kessinger. "Our crews and contractor will have the work done in a week."

Customers with questions may call Customer Service at (540) 586-7210, ext. 1.

The Bedford Regional Water Authority provides water and sewer service to customers in the Town of Bedford and Bedford County.
