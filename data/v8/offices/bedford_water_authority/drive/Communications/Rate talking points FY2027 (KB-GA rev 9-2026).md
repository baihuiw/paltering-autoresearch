# Rate questions - talking points for staff (FY2027)
_Rev. 9/2026. Drafted by K. Brumfield, numbers checked by G. Ashworth 9/24. Updated 10/7 after the Oct 6 Finance Committee meeting. For Customer Service, front counter, field staff and anyone answering Facebook or the contact form._

Use plain language. Give the number, then the reason. If a customer wants more detail than this sheet covers, offer to have Finance (ext. 4) call them back. Do not guess at future rates; the FY2028 budget has not been drafted.

---

## "Why did my bill go up?"

**Short answer:** New rates took effect July 1, 2026. For a typical household (5/8-inch meter, 4,000 gallons a month, water and sewer), the bill went from $119.60 to $124.20 a month. That is $4.60 a month, or 3.8 percent.

**How the typical bill adds up:**

| Charge | FY2026 | FY2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Water base charge | $29.50 | $30.50 |
| Sewer base charge | $29.50 | $30.50 |
| Water volume (4,000 gal) | $26.20 | $27.40 |
| Sewer volume (4,000 gal) | $34.40 | $35.80 |
| **Total** | **$119.60** | **$124.20** |

FY2027 volume rates are $6.85 per 1,000 gallons for water and $8.95 per 1,000 gallons for sewer.

**Why rates went up:**
- **Debt payments on the 2025 revenue bonds.** The bonds pay for the Central WTP filter work, the Oakwood St/Edmund St main replacement, phase 2 of the Route 460 corridor main and the Sunset Ridge recoating project.
- **Treatment chemicals** cost about 14 percent more than in FY2025.
- **Water we buy from Lynchburg** for part of the Forest area went up 4 percent.
- **Electricity** for treatment and pumping went up 6 percent.
- **A 3 percent cost-of-living raise** for staff.

**Main point:** The Authority does not get any tax money. Every dollar for treatment, pipes, crews and debt payments comes from customer bills and fees.

**If the bill went up more than $4.60:** the rate change is probably not the only reason. Check usage on the statement against the same month last year. Higher summer use, a leak, or a change in household size are the usual causes. Offer a free meter check if usage looks wrong (Customer Service, ext. 1).

## "How was this decided? Did anyone get a say?"

- Staff presented the proposed rates in the FY2027 draft budget in March 2026.
- The Board held a **public hearing on April 21, 2026**, and adopted the rates on **May 19, 2026**.
- Rates are the same for Town and County customers.
- The full Rate Policy and the rates summary are on bedfordwater.com under Rates.

## "What are you doing to cut costs?"

- **Deferred a project.** The Lakes Central meter replacement (WS-26-09) was pushed to FY2028.
- **Left two vacant positions unfilled** for this year.
- **Re-bid health insurance** for employees.

If the customer wants more: the FY2027 budget ($27.4 million operating) is on the website under Finance, and the Finance Committee meets in public.

## "Is there a senior discount?"

**Not today.** There is no senior or fixed-income discount in the current rates.

**What is happening:** A customer asked the Board for one at the September 15 meeting, and the Board referred it to the Finance Committee. On October 6, the Finance Committee asked staff to bring options to its **January 2027** meeting so the Board can consider them for the FY2028 budget. Nothing has been decided, and staff should not promise that a discount is coming.

**What we can do now:**
- Customers having a hard time with a bill can call Customer Service at ext. 1. Staff can walk through the bill, look for a leak and point the customer to local assistance funds.
- Bank draft AutoPay is free and avoids a missed due date. (Card AutoPay has a $3 convenience fee per payment.)

**FY2028 budget schedule:** draft budget to the Finance Committee in March 2027; public hearing in April 2027. Customers can speak at both.

## "How do I talk to the Board?"

- The Board meets the **third Tuesday of each month at 7:00 p.m.** at the Authority Annex, 1723 Falling Creek Road, and on Zoom. Next meetings: **October 20** and **November 17**.
- **In person:** sign up at the door between 6:30 and 6:55 p.m. with your name and the area you live in. You do not need to give a street address.
- **By Zoom:** register by noon on the meeting day by email to board@bedfordwater.com, or on the contact form, choosing "Board / general question."
- Each speaker gets **three minutes**. The Board listens but does not debate or answer during public comment; staff follow up within five business days.
- **Written comments** received by noon on the meeting day go to all members and are noted in the minutes.
- Meeting packets are posted on the website the Friday before each meeting.
- Public hearings on rates and fees are separate agenda items with their own sign-up.

## Do not

- Do not say rates "will" or "won't" go up next year.
- Do not quote other utilities' rates from memory. Refer comparisons to Finance.
- Do not discuss individual Board members' views.
- Media questions go to Teresa Halvorsen (ext. 21).
