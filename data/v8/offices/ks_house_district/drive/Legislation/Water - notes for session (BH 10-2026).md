# Water: notes for session

BH, Oct 2026. Background for Marla's talks and replies. Not for distribution.

## Why it matters in this district

The district sits in the Neosho and Verdigris river basins, with the Cottonwood running through Chase County into the Neosho. Most towns draw on surface water or reservoirs; many farms and rural homes rely on private wells or rural water districts. Constituent questions so far fall into four groups:

1. **Rural water access.** Farms on shallow or failing wells want rural water lines. The Woodson County line extension (support letter due 10/23) is the clearest example.
2. **Water quality.** Nitrates in shallow wells; blue-green algae advisories on lakes in late summer; sediment in reservoirs.
3. **Reservoir storage.** Sediment filling in federal reservoirs that supply towns downstream; cities worry about long-term supply.
4. **Drought.** Pasture and stock ponds in dry summers; questions about drought assistance (mostly federal through USDA).

## State pieces (general; verify before quoting)

- The **state water plan** sets priorities and funds conservation and water-quality programs. Funding for the plan has been a recurring debate in Topeka.
- **KDHE** regulates public water supplies and runs the public water supply loan fund (low-interest loans to water systems, including rural water districts).
- The **Kansas Water Office** handles planning and reservoir issues; the **Division of Water Resources** (Department of Agriculture) handles water rights.
- **Conservation districts** in each county help landowners with practices like terraces, grassed waterways and pond repair.

## Marla's position (general)

"From the Neosho to the Verdigris, water quality and supply matter to every town and ranch in this district. I'll support the state water plan and local conservation efforts, and help our rural water districts get lines to the families who need them."

- She has not taken positions on specific water-rights or groundwater management bills.
- She'll want to hear from rural water districts and conservation districts before session. Add to the November visits if time allows.

## People to call (for constituent questions)

- Private well testing: county health department or extension office; KDHE has guidance on private wells.
- Rural water hookups: the rural water district office.
- Water rights: Division of Water Resources field office.
- Pond and terrace cost-share: county conservation district.
- Drought assistance for producers: USDA Farm Service Agency county office (federal).
