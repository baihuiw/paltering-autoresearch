# Occupational licensing: how long transfers take

DV and BH, Oct 15, 2026. Working notes for the bill ideas list ("Occupational licensing - faster out-of-state transfers"). Not a position.

## Why we're looking at this

People moving into the district for work keep running into the same wall: they hold a license in another state, they have a job waiting in Eureka or Emporia or Fredonia, and they can't start until a Kansas board approves the transfer. Employers have raised it too: a salon owner at the Chamber coffee on 10/16, a home health agency, and a school district that hired a counselor from Missouri.

## What we've learned

1. **Incomplete files are the biggest delay.** Most boards don't start reviewing until everything is in: application, fee, ID, verification from the other state's board, and sometimes proof of work history. The other state's verification is often the slowest piece and is out of the applicant's hands.
2. **Each board's process is different**, and board websites vary a lot in how easy they are to use. People give up and call us.
3. **Work history requirements** (for example, a minimum period of recent practice in the profession) can mean someone newly licensed elsewhere has to take extra steps.
4. **Background checks** add time for professions that require fingerprints.
5. **Compacts help where they exist.** Nurses with a multistate license from another compact state can usually work here while they switch over.

## Things other states have tried (check with KLRD before citing)

- "Universal recognition" laws that direct boards to license people in good standing in another state after a set period of practice, with exceptions.
- Temporary or provisional permits so someone can work under supervision while the file is processed.
- Processing-time reporting so the Legislature can see which boards are slow.
- Joining more interstate compacts by profession.

Kansas has acted on some of this in recent years; KLRD can tell us what's already law so we don't propose something that exists.
