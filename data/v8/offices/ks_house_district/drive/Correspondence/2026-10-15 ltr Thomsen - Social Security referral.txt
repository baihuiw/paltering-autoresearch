Dear Mrs. Brockhoff, my Social Security check was short $86 this month and

----- REPLY -----
Dear Mrs. Thomsen,

Thank you for your letter. I'm sorry you've had trouble getting an answer;
an unexplained change in a check you count on is worrying.

Social Security is a federal program, so the right office to help is the
office of your member of Congress, which has staff whose job is to look into
Social Security questions for constituents. A state representative's office
can't get answers from Social Security, but your congressional office can.

Diane Volkmann in my office will call you this week to give you the phone
number and, if you'd like, to help you with the short privacy release form the
congressional office will need so they can look at your account. She can also
help you look at your benefit letter; sometimes a change in a Medicare premium
or a tax withholding explains a smaller payment.

If you need anything from a state agency, please call us anytime at
(620) 583-4130. Our office at 118 W. Second St. is open Monday through Thursday,
9 to 4, and Fridays by appointment.

Sincerely,
Marla Brockhoff
State Representative, Kansas House District 13
