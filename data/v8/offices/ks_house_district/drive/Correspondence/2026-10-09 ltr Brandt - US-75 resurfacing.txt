----- REPLY -----
Dear Arlo,

Thank you for writing, and for the warning. Ruts like that are a real hazard,
especially with trucks hauling grain.

US-75 is a state highway, so its maintenance belongs to the Kansas Department
of Transportation. The quickest way to get a specific spot looked at is to
report it to KDOT's local maintenance office for Woodson County, with the
nearest mile marker or crossroad if you can. You can also report road
conditions through KDOT's website. My office has passed your note along to
KDOT's area maintenance staff as well, and we'll let you know what we hear.

As for timing: KDOT schedules resurfacing work by weather and contract
schedules, and in Kansas the warm months are when asphalt work can be done,
which unfortunately overlaps with wheat harvest and fall harvest. If a project
is causing particular trouble for farm traffic, tell us where and we'll pass
that along too.

Sincerely,
Marla Brockhoff
State Representative, Kansas House District 13
