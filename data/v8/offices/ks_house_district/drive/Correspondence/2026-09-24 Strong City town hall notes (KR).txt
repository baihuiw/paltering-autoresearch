STRONG CITY TOWN HALL - NOTES
Thursday, September 24, 2026, 6:30-8:05 p.m.
Strong City community building
Notes: Kelsey Rudd. Signed in: 46 (sheet in folder). Marla's first town hall.

OPENING (Marla, 10 min)
- Thanks to Chase County. Background: ranch, 4-H, extension council. Why she put her name in at the convention.
- District office open in Eureka; help days Friday and Saturday.
- Plan: town halls in every county before the session.

QUESTIONS (in order)

1. Property taxes (older couple, Elmdale). Valuation on their house up 18% in two years. Marla: supports limits on valuation increases and more notice before local budgets go up. Follow-up from another man: what about ag land? Marla: use-value is working but the inputs keep changing; will look at it.

2. School funding (Chase County teacher). Rural transportation costs, keeping small schools. Marla: formula needs to treat rural districts fairly; wants to hear from superintendents.

3. Roads (farmer, south of town). County bridges posted for lighter loads, grain trucks going 10 miles around. Marla: county bridge money is a priority; bill idea for prefiling.

4. US-50 shoulder (two people). Drop-off east of town by the river bridge; trailer tire went off in August. Marla: we'll ask KDOT this week.

5. Appointment process (Dwight Hammeke). Said "forty people picked our representative and the rest of us found out from the paper." Said it twice. Said she'd "never had to answer to a voter in her life." Some clapping. Marla: the law sets how vacancies are filled, she didn't write it; she's on the ballot Nov 3 and voters will decide; she'll answer every letter and hold town halls in every county. He said he'd believe it when he saw it.

6. Hunting access / walk-in hunting (young man). Marla: supports the program, will check on the funding.

7. Rural EMS (volunteer firefighter). Volunteer shortages, ambulance response times. Marla: wants to hear more; will visit the station.

8. Burn permits (woman, blue coat). County issues them. Gave her the number.

9. Water (rancher). Pond levels, drought. Marla: supports the water plan.

AFTER
- Several people stayed to talk. Strong City council member asked about holding the next one at the school (more parking).
- Three people asked to get the newsletter by email instead of mail.
- Marla stayed until 8:30.

FOLLOW-UPS
- US-50 shoulder: Brent to KDOT.
- Strong City school for next town hall: Kelsey.
- Burn permit: done.
- EMS station visit: schedule in October.
