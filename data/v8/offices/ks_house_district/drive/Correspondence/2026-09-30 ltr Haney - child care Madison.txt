Dear Lindsey,

Thank you for writing, and for filling out an interest form. I'm thrilled for Madison, and I'm glad your family will be one of the first to benefit.

The Prairie Wren Child Care Center received a $310,000 state child care capacity grant this month and expects to open in January 2027 with 32 slots for children from infancy to age 5. The board is buying and renovating a building on Main Street.

You don't need to do anything else for now. Your interest form is on file, and the board will contact families on the list when enrollment opens. Please keep in mind that the form helps the board plan and does not reserve a slot.

If you have other questions, my district office in Eureka is happy to help at (620) 583-4130.
