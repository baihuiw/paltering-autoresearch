----- REPLY -----
Dear Corey,

I'd love to meet your scouts. Thank you for thinking of it.

The best time to visit is during the legislative session, which starts January
11. From mid-January through early April the House is usually on the floor most
mornings, and your troop can watch from the House gallery. Tuesday through
Thursday mornings are your best bet to see the House in action and to catch me
between committee meetings.

Here's how to set it up:

1. Pick two or three possible dates and call or email my district office
   (Kelsey Rudd, (620) 583-4130, or reply to this email). Once the session
   starts, my session assistant in Topeka will coordinate the day with you and
   find a time for me to meet the group, usually 15 minutes in my office
   (Room 151-S) or just outside the House chamber for a photo.
2. Book a guided tour with the Capitol tour desk run by the Kansas Historical
   Society: (785) 296-3966 or kshs.capitol@ks.gov. Tours are free. Reservations
   are strongly encouraged for larger groups from January through May, and a
   single tour group can be up to 41 people, so your troop fits in one.
3. Plan for parking: the Statehouse lots fill up during session. A bus or
   carpool dropping off at the Capitol Visitor Center works best.

Since you're working on a badge, the scouts might enjoy the dome tour too (it's
a lot of steps). Ask the tour desk about age limits.

I look forward to seeing you in Topeka.

Sincerely,
Marla Brockhoff
State Representative, Kansas House District 13
