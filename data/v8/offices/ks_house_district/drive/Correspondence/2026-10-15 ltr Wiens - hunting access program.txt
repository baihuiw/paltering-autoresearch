----- REPLY -----
Dear Marvin,

Thank you for writing, and for thinking about opening your ground to hunters.
It's a big help to young hunters and families who don't have land of their own.

The walk-in hunting access program is run by the Kansas Department of Wildlife
and Parks. KDWP leases hunting rights from landowners for a set season, and
the payment depends on the acres, the type of habitat and the length of the
contract. Your district wildlife biologist can walk the property with you and
explain the current rates and rules, including what happens when hunters don't
respect your property. You can reach KDWP through its website or the nearest
regional office, and my office is glad to make the connection: call
(620) 583-4130.

I haven't seen any proposal to cut the program. My office has asked KDWP for
the number of acres enrolled in our counties, and I'll keep an eye on its budget
when the session begins in January.

Sincerely,
Marla Brockhoff
State Representative, Kansas House District 13
