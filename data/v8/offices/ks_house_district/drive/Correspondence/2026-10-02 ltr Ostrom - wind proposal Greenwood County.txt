Representative Brockhoff, I have been out to the meetings about the wind project they want to put west of Hamilton. I'm against it. ----- REPLY -----
Dear Carl,

Thank you for writing, and for going to the meetings. I know how much that view means. I grew up looking at the same hills.

I'm serving on the Legislature's interim committee on rural energy siting, which is hearing from counties, landowners, developers and conservation groups this fall. The committee will make recommendations in October, and any bill would have hearings in the 2027 session.

My view is that people who live with these decisions should have a real voice in them, and that landowners on both sides deserve to be treated with respect. I'll keep listening to landowners on all sides before I take a position on any bill, and I'd welcome your testimony when the time comes.

The Greenwood County Commission has scheduled a public hearing on wind regulations for November 9. I'd encourage you to speak there too.

Sincerely,
Marla Brockhoff
State Representative, Kansas House District 13
