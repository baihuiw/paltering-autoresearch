Congratulations on the appointment. First thing you can do for us: our house

----- REPLY -----
Dear Loren,

Thank you for the kind words, and for writing so soon. You've put your finger
on the thing I hear about most, at the sale barn and now in my mail.

Your valuation is set by the Lyon County Appraiser's office, not by the state.
If you believe it's wrong, the appraiser's office can explain how they arrived
at it. The formal appeal period for this year's notices has passed, but you can
still pay your tax bill under protest in December, which gives you a chance to
have your value reviewed. The county treasurer's office can tell you how.

You and your wife may also qualify for one of the state's property tax refund
programs for seniors through the Kansas Department of Revenue. My office can
help you check. Call Diane Volkmann at (620) 583-4130.

As for what I'll do: I support a limit on how much a home's valuation can
increase in one year, and I'll vote to put one before Kansas voters. I also want
local budget increases to come with clear notice and a recorded vote, so you
know who raised your taxes and why. The Legislature convenes January 11, and
this will be one of my first priorities.

Thank you again for writing. Please keep in touch.

Sincerely,
Marla Brockhoff
State Representative, Kansas House District 13
