# KanCare renewal help: how we do it

District office reference sheet. Rev. Sept 22, 2026 (DV). For staff and help-day volunteers.

## What KanCare renewals are

KanCare is Kansas Medicaid. Members renew eligibility once a year. The KanCare Clearinghouse in Topeka mails a renewal form about 60 days before the due date. If the form isn't returned by the due date, coverage closes at the end of the month.

## What we do for constituents

1. **Check the form is complete.** Signature and date on the signature page, every household member listed, income pages filled in. Missing signatures are the most common reason a renewal is kicked back.
2. **Copy proof of income** if they brought it (pay stubs for the last 30 days, Social Security award letter, pension statement).
3. **Fax it to the Clearinghouse the same day** from the office eFax account. Never hand the form back to the constituent to mail if we've told them we'll send it.
4. **Keep the fax confirmation sheet** with the case and note it on the casework sheet (date, time, "confirmed").
5. **Tell the constituent** what we sent and when, and that the Clearinghouse may call or write for more information.

We do not decide eligibility and we cannot tell anyone whether they qualify. We can help them get the paperwork in and follow up.

## If coverage has already closed

- If coverage closed because the renewal wasn't returned, the member has a **90-day reconsideration period**. If the completed renewal reaches the Clearinghouse within 90 days of the closing, KanCare reconsiders it without a new application.
- If the person is still eligible, **coverage can be backdated** to the closing date, so there is no gap on paper. Pharmacies and providers can rebill once coverage is restored.
- Reconsideration currently takes several weeks. In the meantime the person can call the Clearinghouse to confirm the form was received.
- After 90 days, they need a new application.

## Help days

- Bring: eFax login, scanner, the casework sign-in sheets, blank renewal forms, the agency phone list, pens.
- One volunteer greets and signs people in; staff handle the forms.
- Fax all renewals before the end of the next business day at the latest, and check every confirmation.
