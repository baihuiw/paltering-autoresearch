VITAL RECORDS - NOTES FOR CALLERS
District office front desk. KR, Oct 7, 2026 (after C26-0060). DV checked.

Birth, death, marriage and divorce certificates for events in Kansas come from
the KDHE Office of Vital Statistics in Topeka. Counties do not issue certified
birth certificates in Kansas. (People ask the county clerk first; the clerk
sends them to us or to KDHE.)

FASTEST WAY: IN PERSON IN TOPEKA
  Office of Vital Statistics
  Curtis State Office Building, 1000 SW Jackson, Suite 120, Topeka
  (use the main doors off 10th and Jackson)
  Walk-in hours: Monday-Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.
  Phone: (785) 296-1400
  - Same-day service. If the record is found without trouble, a certified copy
    is usually ready in about 15-20 minutes.
  - Bring a current government-issued photo ID.
  - There is a fee per certified copy (check KDHE's site for the current fee
    before quoting one).
  - Eureka to Topeka is a little over 2 hours each way. For someone who needs
    a copy for a job start date, this is usually the answer.

ONLINE / PHONE / MAIL
  - Orders can be placed online or by mail. These take longer; the website
    gives current processing times. Expedited shipping shortens the mailing
    time, not the processing time.
  - Check whether the employer will accept a receipt of order or start the
    paperwork with another document while the certificate comes (some will).

CAN SOMEONE ELSE PICK IT UP?
  Kansas limits who can get a certified birth certificate (the person, parents
  listed, legal guardian, or someone with a direct interest and documentation).
  A relative or friend going to Topeka for them should call Vital Statistics
  first to confirm what they need to bring.

BORN IN ANOTHER STATE?
  Order from that state's vital records office. We can help find the right
  office.

CORRECTIONS AND AMENDMENTS (wrong spelling, wrong parent info)
  Go through the amendments unit. Usually an affidavit plus supporting
  documents; some need a notary. Takes weeks. Diane handles these as cases
  (example: C26-0008).

DEATH CERTIFICATES
  Usually the funeral home orders the first copies. Families can order more
  online or in person. (C26-0060: ordered online with the family 10/7.)

WHAT TO SAY
  "The quickest way is in person at the Office of Vital Statistics in Topeka,
  Monday through Friday, 9 to 4. Bring your photo ID; they usually have it
  ready in 15 to 20 minutes. If you can't make the trip, we can help you order
  online and look at whether your employer will accept the order receipt for
  now."

  Don't promise a mail-order date. Don't say we can speed up an online order;
  we can't.

MARRIAGE AND DIVORCE
  Certified copies of marriage licenses can come from Vital Statistics (for
  marriages since the state began keeping them) or from the district court
  in the county where the license was issued. Divorce decrees come from the
  district court clerk in the county where the divorce was granted.
  Vital Statistics can issue a divorce certificate (not the decree).

COMMON MIX-UPS
  - "I need my birth certificate for a REAL ID." The driver's license office
    wants a certified copy, not a hospital souvenir certificate.
  - "My name is spelled wrong." That's an amendment, not a new copy. Case for
    Diane.
  - "I was adopted." Rules are different; refer to Vital Statistics directly.
