# Agency liaisons and contacts: quick reference

District office casework. Rev. Oct 7, 2026 (DV, KR). Front-desk copy. Constituent authorization forms are in the gray file box, one folder per agency.

Use the legislative liaison when an agency has one and the case needs a look. For simple questions, give the constituent the public number; many questions are answered faster that way. Log every agency contact on the agency contact log the same day.

## State agencies

**Kansas Department of Labor (KDOL) - unemployment**
- Public claims line (785) 575-1460. Online claims portal through KDOL's website.
- Legislative liaison: cases by email from Diane's or Brent's account. Turnaround usually 2-3 business days for a status answer (more for adjudication). Need the claimant's signed authorization and the last 4 of SSN, claim date, employer.
- See "KDOL cases - how we work them".

**Kansas Department of Revenue (KDOR)**
- Legislative liaison for refund status, homestead and SAFESR refund status, tax notices. Taxpayer authorization form for each case.
- Vehicle titles and tags: county treasurer first. Driver's licenses: county treasurer (by appointment in most of our counties).

**Department for Children and Families (DCF)**
- Public line (888) 369-4777. Legislative liaison for child support, food assistance (SNAP), LIEAP. Constituent inquiry form.

**KDHE Office of Vital Statistics** (birth, death, marriage, divorce certificates)
- Curtis State Office Building, 1000 SW Jackson, Suite 120, Topeka. Walk-in Mon-Fri, 9 a.m.-4 p.m.; certified copies usually ready in about 15-20 minutes if the record is found. Government photo ID required. Phone (785) 296-1400. Fees change; check the KDHE site.
- Online and mail orders take longer. Corrections and amendments go through the amendments unit (affidavit plus documents).
- See "Vital records - notes for callers".

**Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks (KDWP)**
- Deer damage: landowners call the nearest KDWP office or the deer damage line (888) 497-8661. A district wildlife biologist or natural resources officer visits; damage control permits are free.
- Boat registrations, hunting and fishing licenses: online through KDWP.

**Kansas Corporation Commission (KCC)**
- Utility bill disputes and rate questions for regulated utilities: Office of Public Affairs and Consumer Protection, (800) 662-0027. Most rural electric co-ops set their own rates.

**KPERS** - member services; retirement estimates and survivor claims need the member's (or survivor's) signed request.

**Attorney General - Consumer Protection** (800) 432-2310. Complaint form online or by mail.

**Kansas Insurance Department - Consumer Assistance**: insurance claim disputes.

**State Treasurer - Unclaimed Property**: online search; we can sit with people and search.

**State Fire Marshal**: burn permits are a county matter in our counties; give the county number.

## Licensing boards (common ones)

- Board of Nursing; Board of Pharmacy; Board of Cosmetology (2420 NW Button Rd, Topeka; kboc@ks.gov); Behavioral Sciences Regulatory Board; Board of Healing Arts; Board of Technical Professions; Real Estate Commission; Board of Barbering.
- Most status questions: licensing staff by phone or email. See "Licensing boards - out-of-state transfers".

## Federal and local (refer)

- Social Security, Medicare, VA, IRS, passports: the congressional office for the district.
- Property valuation: county appraiser. Tax bills: county treasurer.
- City utilities and streets: city hall.
- Legal disputes: Kansas Legal Services or a private attorney.

## Other state offices people ask about

- **KDOT** (state highways only): road conditions and maintenance requests go to the local KDOT maintenance office or KDOT's website. County roads and bridges belong to the county road department.
- **Kansas Department for Aging and Disability Services (KDADS)**: nursing facility questions, some in-home services. The Area Agency on Aging is usually the better first call for seniors (meals, transportation, in-home help).
- **KSDE** (Department of Education): teacher licensing; questions about school policy go first to the local district.
- **Secretary of State**: business filings, notary commissions. Voter registration and polling places: county clerk / county election office.
- **Kansas Commission on Veterans Affairs Office (KCVAO)**: state veterans services; field offices help with VA claims paperwork (C26-0039 example). The VA itself is federal (congressional office).
- **Kansas Real Estate Commission**, **Board of Technical Professions** (engineers, architects, surveyors), **Board of Accountancy**: licensing status questions, same approach as other boards.
- **Kansas Highway Patrol**: crash reports (request online), commercial vehicle questions.
- **State Treasurer**: unclaimed property; also some college savings and municipal finance programs.

## Phone script for "who do I call?"

"Let me make sure you get to the right place the first time. Is this about [unemployment / a tax refund / a license / a certificate]? Here's the direct number. If you call and get stuck, call us back and we'll reach someone for you." Write down what you told them on the message pad.
