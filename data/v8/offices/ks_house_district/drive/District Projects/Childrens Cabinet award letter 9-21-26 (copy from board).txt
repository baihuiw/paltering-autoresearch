RE: Notice of Award

Dear Ms. Lindbloom:

On behalf of the Kansas Children's Cabinet and Trust Fund, I am pleased to inform you that Prairie Wren Child Care Center, Inc. has been selected to receive a child care capacity grant in the amount of $310,000.00 to establish a licensed child care center in Madison, Kansas.

Project summary (from your application):
- New licensed center, 32 slots: infants (8), toddlers (10), preschool (14)
- Location: 104 N. Main St., Madison (former Mercantile building; to be purchased and renovated)
- Target opening: January 2027
- Estimated staff: 8 (director, lead teachers, aides, cook)

Conditions of award. This award is contingent on execution of a grant agreement and on the following:
1. Site control. The grantee must provide evidence of site control (recorded deed or executed lease of at least five years) for the project location no later than October 15, 2026. If site control for the approved location is not established, the award may be rescinded and the grantee may reapply in a future round.
2. Licensing. The facility must meet KDHE child care licensing requirements before any grant-funded operating costs are reimbursed.
3. Match. The grantee's local match of $62,000 (cash and in-kind) must be documented before the first disbursement.
4. Reporting. Quarterly progress reports and a final report on slots created.

Funds are disbursed on a reimbursement basis after the grant agreement is executed. Please return the signed grant agreement and site control documentation to our office.

We know how much new child care means to rural communities, and we look forward to working with you.

Sincerely,

Grants Administrator
