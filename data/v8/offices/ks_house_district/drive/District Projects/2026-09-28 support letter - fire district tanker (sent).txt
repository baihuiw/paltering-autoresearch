Re: Equipment grant application, Greenwood County Fire District (east) - water tanker

To the review committee:

I write in support of the application from the Greenwood County Fire District
(east) for state grant assistance toward the purchase of a water tanker truck.

The fire district covers a large, sparsely populated area of eastern Greenwood
County, much of it pasture and cropland far from any hydrant. Its volunteers
respond to grass and structure fires, vehicle crashes on state highways, and
mutual-aid calls in neighboring counties. On rural fires the deciding factor is
often how much water can be put on the fire before the tanker has to leave to
refill. The district's current tanker is more than 30 years old, has needed
repeated repairs to its pump and tank, and was out of service for part of the
past summer during a dry stretch when grass fire calls were frequent.

The district has committed local funds toward the purchase and has documented
its call volume and response distances in the application. The volunteers who
staff it are farmers, ranchers, teachers and business owners who leave work
and family to answer calls. Reliable equipment is the least we can give them.

I understand that the State Fire Marshal's office will evaluate all applications
on their merits and under the program's criteria. I ask only that you give this
application full consideration. Please contact my office if I can provide any
further information about the community.

Sincerely,

Marla Brockhoff
State Representative, Kansas House District 13
