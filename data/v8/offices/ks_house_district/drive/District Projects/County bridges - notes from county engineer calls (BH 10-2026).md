# County bridges: notes from calls with county engineers and road supervisors

BH, Oct 2026. Background for the county bridge funding bill draft (rs 27-0112) and for replies.

## Why this came up

At the town halls and in the mail, people keep raising posted bridges: farmers who have to drive miles around a bridge posted for 10 tons with a loaded grain truck, school bus routes that detour, and ambulance and fire crews who can't take the short way. Two county commissions asked Marla directly for state help.

## Calls so far

**Greenwood County (road and bridge department), Oct 6**
- Several bridges on the county system are posted for reduced loads; two are a priority for replacement, both on routes used by grain trucks and a school bus.
- The county intends to apply in the next round of KDOT's local bridge program, which requires a local match. The match is the hard part for a county with a small tax base.
- Rough cost for the two structures: about $1.4 million combined (engineer's estimate, not a bid).
- Suggested we look at: the size of the local match, how often rounds open, and whether low-water crossings or box culverts can be an eligible, cheaper option.

**Chase County (road supervisor), Oct 8**
- One bridge over a creek south of Cottonwood Falls has an engineering study done; estimate about $900,000.
- County is looking for funding; it raised the bridge at the September town hall.
- Concern: inspection findings keep adding bridges to the posted list faster than counties can replace them.

**Woodson County (road and bridge), Oct 15 - left message**
- Bridge north of Yates Center raised at the 10/15 town hall. Brent has the location; waiting for a call back.

## What the bill draft request asks the Revisor (summary of rs 27-0112 request, 10/14)

- Look at increasing state funding available to counties for replacing load-posted bridges on county roads.
- Consider a lower local match for counties below a certain population or valuation.
- Consider allowing funds for lower-cost replacements (box culverts, low-water crossings) where engineering supports it.
- Draft expected in November. Nothing public until Marla decides to introduce it.

## What we tell constituents now

> County bridges are owned and maintained by the county, so the county road department decides which bridges get posted and replaced. But replacing them is expensive for rural counties, and I've asked for a bill to increase state help, especially for small counties. I'll keep you posted when the session starts.
