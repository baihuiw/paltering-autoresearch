# Rep. Marla Brockhoff on the Rural Energy Siting Committee's Recommendations

EUREKA - State Representative Marla Brockhoff (House District 13) issued the following statement on the recommendations adopted Thursday by the Legislature's Special Committee on Rural Energy Siting:

"I spent four days in Topeka listening to county commissioners, ranchers, developers, conservation groups and neighbors on every side of these questions. I heard from people who want turbines and people who don't, and I respect both.

"The committee agreed unanimously that every wind and solar project should post a bond so the land is restored when the project ends, and that developers should sign road agreements with counties before a single truck rolls. Those are protections every rural county needs.

"I also voted for certainty. Landowners on both sides deserve to know what the rules are, and a moratorium that never ends is not a rule. Under the committee's recommendation, counties keep the authority to zone, to set setbacks of up to 1,500 feet from neighbors' homes, and to decide whether a project is approved. Decisions about turbines in our district will still be made in our counties, by our county commissioners.

"Property rights matter to me. A family that wants to lease its land should be able to, and a family next door should have real protection. That is the balance I'll keep working for when the Legislature meets in January.

"These are recommendations. Any bill will go through hearings in the 2027 session, and I want to hear from you before then. My office is at 118 W. Second St. in Eureka, (620) 583-4130."

Brockhoff, a fourth-generation rancher from near Cottonwood Falls, was appointed to the committee in September.
