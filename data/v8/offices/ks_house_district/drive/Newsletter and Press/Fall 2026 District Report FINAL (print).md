### PAGE 1

**Dear Neighbors,**

On September 21 I took the oath of office in the Chase County Courthouse as your state representative. I want to thank the precinct committee members who put their trust in me, my family for their patience, and everyone who called, wrote or stopped me at the sale barn to wish me well.

I'm a rancher, not a politician. Dean and I raise cattle on the same ground my great-grandparents did, and our kids grew up in 4-H in the same barns many of yours did. I know what a dry summer does to a pasture and what a property tax bill does to a family budget. I'll bring that to Topeka.

This newsletter is my first report to you. It tells you how to reach my office, what I expect the Legislature to take up in January, and where to turn for help with state agencies. Please keep it by the phone.

**My district office is open.** 118 W. Second St., Eureka. Monday through Thursday, 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., and Fridays by appointment. Phone (620) 583-4130. Email marla.brockhoff@house.ks.gov or use the contact form at repbrockhoff.com.

### PAGE 2 - WHAT'S AHEAD IN THE 2027 SESSION

The Legislature convenes on Monday, January 11, 2027. Here is what I'm hearing about most, and where I stand.

**Property taxes.** Rising valuations are squeezing homeowners and ag producers alike. I will support limits on how fast valuations can climb and more notice before local budgets go up.

**School funding.** Our schools are the heart of our towns. Rural districts face real costs for transportation and for keeping small schools open. I'll work to make sure the funding formula treats rural districts fairly.

**Roads and bridges.** Many of our county roads and bridges were built for lighter trucks than the ones on them today. I'll push for state help for county bridge replacement.

**Water.** From the Neosho to the Verdigris, water quality and supply matter to every town and ranch in this district. I'll support the state water plan and local conservation efforts.

**Rural health care.** Our hospitals and EMS services are stretched thin. Keeping them open is a priority.
