# Rep. Brockhoff Invites School Groups and Families to Visit the Statehouse During the 2027 Session

EUREKA - State Representative Marla Brockhoff is inviting teachers, scout leaders, 4-H clubs and families in House District 13 to plan a visit to the Kansas Statehouse during the 2027 legislative session, which begins Monday, January 11.

"Some of my best memories as a 4-H mom were trips to Topeka with a bus full of kids," Brockhoff said. "There's nothing like watching the House in session from the gallery to make government real. I'd love to meet every group that makes the trip from our district."

**Planning a visit:**

- **When:** From mid-January through early April, the House meets on the floor most days. Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday mornings are the best times to watch the House in session and to meet Rep. Brockhoff between committee meetings.
- **Tours:** Free guided tours of the Statehouse, including the dome, are offered by the Kansas Historical Society. Groups should book ahead through the Capitol tour desk at (785) 296-3966 or kshs.capitol@ks.gov. Reservations are strongly encouraged for groups from January through May; a single tour group can include up to 41 people.
- **Meeting the Representative:** Call the district office at (620) 583-4130 or email marla.brockhoff@house.ks.gov with two or three possible dates and the size of your group. During the session, Rep. Brockhoff's Statehouse office is Room 151-S.
- **Getting there:** Visitors enter through the Capitol Visitor Center on the north side of the Statehouse, 300 SW 10th Ave., Topeka.

Spring dates fill quickly with fourth-grade Kansas history classes, so teachers are encouraged to call this fall.

The district office at 118 W. Second St. in Eureka is open Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., and Fridays by appointment.
