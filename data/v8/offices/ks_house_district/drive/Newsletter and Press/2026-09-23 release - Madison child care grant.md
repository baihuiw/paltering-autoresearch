# Madison to Get New Child Care Center with $310,000 State Grant, Rep. Brockhoff Announces

MADISON - State Representative Marla Brockhoff today announced that the Prairie Wren Child Care Center in Madison has been awarded a $310,000 state child care capacity grant from the Kansas Children's Cabinet. The new nonprofit center will offer 32 licensed child care slots for children from infancy to age 5 and is expected to open in January 2027 in a renovated building on Main Street.

"Every parent in this district knows how hard it is to find child care," Brockhoff said. "Families are driving to Emporia or El Dorado, or a parent is staying home who wants to work. Thirty-two slots in Madison is a big deal for Greenwood County, and I'm proud to have helped make the case for it."

The Prairie Wren board, a group of Madison-area parents, teachers and business owners, applied for the grant this summer. Board chair Heather Lindbloom said the center will hire about eight staff.

"We're grateful for the support from Representative Brockhoff and her office, and to the Children's Cabinet for investing in rural Kansas," Lindbloom said.

**Interested families:** Parents who would like to be contacted when enrollment opens can fill out an interest form at Rep. Brockhoff's district office, 118 W. Second St., Eureka, or at her Madison town hall on October 1. The forms help the board plan staffing and age groups. Filling out a form does not reserve a slot.

The district office is open Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Constituents can also reach the office at (620) 583-4130, at marla.brockhoff@house.ks.gov, or through the contact form at repbrockhoff.com.
