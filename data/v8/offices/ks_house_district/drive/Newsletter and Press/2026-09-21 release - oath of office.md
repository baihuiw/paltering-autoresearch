# Marla Brockhoff Sworn In as State Representative for House District 13

COTTONWOOD FALLS - Marla Brockhoff took the oath of office as state representative for Kansas House District 13 this morning in a ceremony at the Chase County Courthouse, with her husband Dean, their three children and five grandchildren in attendance.

Brockhoff was chosen on September 12 by a convention of precinct committee members from the district and appointed by the governor on September 18 to fill the vacancy left by the previous representative's resignation. She will serve the remainder of the term, which runs to January 2027.

"I'm a rancher, not a politician, and I don't intend to stop being one," Brockhoff said after the oath. "I'll spend these next months listening: at town halls, at the sale barn, at the café counter. I want to walk into the Statehouse in January knowing what the people of this district need from their state government."

About 70 people attended the ceremony and the reception that followed in the courthouse's second-floor hallway, where 4-H members from Chase County served cookies and coffee.

Brockhoff named three priorities for the coming session:

- **Property taxes.** "Families are being squeezed by valuations that climb every year. I'll work for limits and for more honesty about who's raising the bill."
- **Rural schools.** "Our schools hold our towns together. The funding formula has to work for districts our size."
- **Roads and bridges.** "Too many county bridges are posted for loads lighter than what's crossing them. Counties need a partner."

She also announced that her district office at 118 W. Second St. in Eureka would open at 2 p.m. today. The office will be open Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., and Fridays by appointment. Constituents can call (620) 583-4130, email marla.brockhoff@house.ks.gov, or use the contact form at repbrockhoff.com.

House District 13 includes Chase, Greenwood, Woodson and Wilson counties and part of southwest Lyon County, including the southwest corner of Emporia.

**About Marla Brockhoff:** A fourth-generation rancher from near Cottonwood Falls, Brockhoff was a 4-H leader for 14 years and served on her county extension council. She and Dean have been married 36 years. This is her first public office.
