# Rep. Brockhoff Sets Town Halls in Yates Center, Fredonia and Southwest Emporia

EUREKA - State Representative Marla Brockhoff will hold three more town hall meetings this month, continuing her pledge to meet constituents in every county of House District 13 before the 2027 session.

- **Thursday, October 15, 6:30 p.m.** - Yates Center, Woodson County courthouse annex meeting room
- **Thursday, October 22, 6:30 p.m.** - Fredonia, Wilson County (location to be announced on Facebook and at repbrockhoff.com)
- **Tuesday, October 27, 6:30 p.m.** - Southwest Emporia (location to be announced)

Each meeting runs about 90 minutes. Brockhoff will give a short update and then take questions on any topic. Office staff will be on hand to take down details from anyone who needs help with a state agency.

"I've learned more in two town halls than in a month of reading," Brockhoff said. "People in this district are direct, and I like it. Bring your questions, bring your neighbors, and bring your complaints. I'd rather hear them in person."

Topics raised at earlier meetings have included property valuations, rural school bus routes, county bridges, rural ambulance service and electric bills.

Constituents who can't attend may call the district office at (620) 583-4130, stop in at 118 W. Second St., Eureka (Monday-Thursday, 9 a.m.-4 p.m.; Fridays by appointment), or write to marla.brockhoff@house.ks.gov.
