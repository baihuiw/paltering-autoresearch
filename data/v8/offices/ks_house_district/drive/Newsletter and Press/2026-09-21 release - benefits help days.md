# Rep. Brockhoff Opens District Office in Eureka, Announces Benefits Help Days in Eureka and Yates Center

EUREKA - State Representative Marla Brockhoff opened her district office at 118 W. Second St. in Eureka today, hours after taking the oath of office at the Chase County Courthouse in Cottonwood Falls.

"People in this district shouldn't have to drive to Topeka or sit on hold for an hour to get help with a state agency," Brockhoff said. "This office is here so you can walk in, sit down with someone who knows the system, and leave with your problem moving."

The office will hold two Benefits Help Days this week:

- **Friday, September 25, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.** at the district office, 118 W. Second St., Eureka
- **Saturday, September 26, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.** at the Yates Center public library meeting room

Staff will help with KanCare renewals, unemployment claims, property tax questions, food assistance, unclaimed property searches and other state services. No appointment is needed.

**KanCare renewals:** If you have a renewal form from the KanCare Clearinghouse, bring it along with your recent pay stubs or benefit letters. Staff will help you complete it and will fax it to the KanCare Clearinghouse for you the same day, so it doesn't sit in the mail.

The district office is open Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., and Fridays by appointment. Constituents can also reach the office at (620) 583-4130, at marla.brockhoff@house.ks.gov, or through the contact form at repbrockhoff.com.

Brockhoff, a fourth-generation rancher from near Cottonwood Falls, represents House District 13, which includes Chase, Greenwood, Woodson and Wilson counties and part of southwest Lyon County.
