Quick recap of the two help days before I head out tomorrow.

Friday, Eureka (office): 24 people. Saturday, Yates Center (library meeting room): 19 people. 43 total, every one opened as a case on the casework sheet (C26-0010 through C26-0052).

What people came in for:
- KanCare renewals: 21 (13 Eureka, 8 Yates Center). I scanned and faxed all 21 to the Clearinghouse this morning from the new eFax account. Fourteen were due Sept 30, seven are due Oct 31.

- SNAP / food assistance: 3. LIEAP: 2 (told them it opens in January).
- Unemployment: 2. Sent both to the KDOL liaison.
- Medicare Part D: 2. Referred to the SHICK counselor.
- Unclaimed property: 2. Searched with them on the Treasurer's site; one hit ($46).
- Other: 7 (vehicle title, out-of-state registration, veterans, driver's license, Social Security letter, and two senior services referrals to the Area Agency on Aging).
