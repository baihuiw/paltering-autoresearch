Good question. Safe things to say:

1. "Each profession has its own state board, and each one has an application
   for people licensed in another state. We can help you find it and go
   through the checklist with you."
2. Ask which board, which state they're coming from, how long they've worked
   in the profession, and whether they've already applied.
3. Don't guess at fees or timelines. For cosmetology, the board says to allow
   7-10 business days for documents to be processed and at least three weeks
   overall. Tell them to send everything at once; missing items are what slow
   it down. Name on the ID and Social Security card must match the
   application.
4. If they already applied and haven't heard, open a case and I'll ask the
   board for a status check with their okay.

Plumbers and electricians: in Kansas that's mostly city or county licensing,
not a state board. Send them to the city or county where they'll work.

I put all of this in a notes sheet in Casework ("Licensing boards -
out-of-state transfers"). If someone says they wrote to us and never heard
back, give them to me first.

Diane
