1. Use "a limit on how much a home's valuation can increase in one year." She's
   fine with "cap" in conversation but in writing we pair it with "I'll vote to
   put one before Kansas voters," because a firm cap means a constitutional
   amendment. Don't let anyone think it happens by July.
2. Don't name or promise a bill number. There's no 2027 bill yet. Say she
   supports a limit and will look closely at any proposal that comes before the
   House.
3. Past the deadline: the county appraiser can still explain the value, and
   they can pay under protest in December. Seniors: mention the KDOR refund
   programs and offer Diane's help.

Keep each reply under 200 words. Personalize the first line (their town, their
number). Sign as Marla. I'll spot-check the first three.

Brent
