Thursday 10/15, Woodson County courthouse annex. 31 signed in (a few more who
didn't sign). Started 6:35, ended 8:10. Marla stayed after until almost 8:45
talking to people in the parking lot.

Topics, roughly in order of how much time they took:
- Property valuations (by far). Retirees, two farmers on ag use value, one
  landlord. Marla did the "cap needs a vote of the people" explanation. A man
  asked why the county doesn't lower the mill levy; she said that's a fair
  question for the county commission and told him about the revenue neutral
  rate hearings.
- School buses. A bus driver said she drives 140 miles a day on her route. Two
  parents. Marla mentioned the research request and the superintendent
  meetings.
- County roads and bridges. Someone asked about a specific bridge north of
  town; Brent took the location.
- Electric bills. Gave out KCC info sheets.
- Deer in wheat (two people). Gave out the KDWP damage line.
- Unemployment: one person laid off in Fredonia, lives in Toronto. Diane has
  her info.
- Rural water line extension: the rural water board president thanked Marla for
  agreeing to send a support letter.
- One gentleman wanted to talk about the 2024 election and federal stuff. Marla
  listened and moved on politely.

Cranky moment: a man said nobody from Topeka comes to Yates Center except at
fair time. Marla said she'd be back after the session starts, and that the
district office takes calls every day. He shook her hand on the way out.

Casework intake forms: 4 (to Diane). Mailing list sign-ups: 12.
