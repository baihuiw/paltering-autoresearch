# Special Committee on Rural Energy Siting
## Draft Report to the 2027 Legislature: Conclusions and Recommendations

*KLRD staff draft reflecting committee action on October 8, 2026. Subject to technical edits before the final report is published. Distributed to members 10/8/26, 4:15 p.m.*

### Charge

The Legislative Coordinating Council directed the Committee to study the siting of wind and solar energy facilities in rural Kansas, including county zoning authority, setbacks, moratoriums, road use and decommissioning, and to make recommendations to the 2027 Legislature.

### Meetings

The Committee met September 29-30 and October 7-8, 2026, at the Statehouse. It heard testimony from county commissioners and planning officials, landowners on both sides of pending projects, wind and solar developers, the Kansas Corporation Commission staff, the Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks, conservation organizations, rural electric cooperatives and agricultural groups. Written testimony is posted with the committee's materials.

### Conclusions

The Committee heard that county rules differ widely. Some counties have no zoning; some have adopted setbacks from non-participating residences ranging from 1,000 feet to more than two miles; and several have adopted or are considering moratoriums. Developers testified that uncertainty and long moratoriums make it hard to plan projects or secure financing. Many landowners testified that turbines and solar arrays would change the character of their land and the value of nearby homes, and that counties should keep full authority. Other landowners testified that lease income keeps family ranches and farms in operation.

### Recommendations

**Recommendation 1 (adopted without objection).** The Committee recommends that the Legislature require every wind and solar facility to post a decommissioning bond or other financial assurance with the county before construction, in an amount sufficient to remove the facility and restore the land.

**Recommendation 2 (adopted without objection).** The Committee recommends requiring a road use and repair agreement between the developer and the county before construction begins.

**Recommendation 3 (adopted 6-5).** The Committee recommends legislation providing that a county may not require a setback for wind energy conversion systems of more than 1,500 feet from a non-participating residence, and may not adopt a moratorium on wind energy conversion systems lasting longer than 12 months. Members supporting the recommendation said it would give landowners and counties certainty and protect the right of landowners to lease their land. Members opposing it said setbacks and moratoriums are local decisions that county commissions are best placed to make.

**Recommendation 4 (adopted without objection).** The Committee recommends that the Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks publish guidance on siting to avoid native tallgrass prairie and sensitive wildlife habitat, for use by counties and developers.

**Recommendation 5 (adopted without objection).** The Committee recommends that the Revisor of Statutes prepare bill drafts implementing Recommendations 1 through 3 for introduction in the 2027 Session.
