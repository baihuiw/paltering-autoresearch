# Capitol visits: how to set one up

KR reference for phone calls and replies. Oct 2026. For school groups, scouts, 4-H clubs, families.

## When to come

- **Best:** during the 2027 session, which starts Monday, January 11. Mid-January through early April the House meets on the floor most days, and visitors can watch from the House gallery.
- **Best days:** Tuesday through Thursday mornings. Mondays the House usually doesn't meet until afternoon; Fridays are often short or off as the session goes on.
- **Busiest:** late February (deadline week for bills to pass the House) and the last weeks of March. Lots to watch; less time for Marla to visit.
- **Outside session:** the Statehouse is open for tours year-round, but the chambers are empty. Fine for a history trip; not for watching lawmaking.
- Fourth-grade Kansas history classes usually come in February-April. Teachers should book early; spring dates fill.

## Two things to book

1. **The tour** with the Capitol tour desk (Kansas Historical Society): (785) 296-3966, kshs.capitol@ks.gov. Guided historic tours on weekdays at 9, 10 and 11 a.m. and 1, 2 and 3 p.m.; dome tours on the quarter hour after those. Free. Reservations are strongly encouraged for groups, especially January through May. Maximum tour group size is 41. A class of 22 fits in one tour.
2. **Time with Marla**, through our office. The teacher or leader calls Kelsey at (620) 583-4130 or writes to marla.brockhoff@house.ks.gov with two or three possible dates, group size and grade. Once the session starts, Pat Engstrom (session legislative assistant, Room 151-S) coordinates the day in Topeka. Usually 10-15 minutes: Marla meets the group in her office or outside the House chamber, answers questions and takes a photo. She can't promise a time to the minute; committee meetings run long.

## Getting there

- Statehouse: 300 SW 10th Ave., Topeka. Enter through the Capitol Visitor Center (north side, ground level). Groups go through security.
- Buses can drop off near the visitor center; ask the tour desk about bus parking.
- Eureka to Topeka is a little over two hours by bus; plan a 7 a.m. departure for a 10 a.m. tour.
- Lunch: the Statehouse has a small snack area; most groups bring sack lunches. Ask the tour desk about a place to eat.

## Extras

- Groups can be recognized from the House floor ("introduced in the gallery") on some days. Ask Pat a week ahead; not guaranteed.
- Pages: high school students can serve as a legislative page for a day. Ask our office in December.
- Teachers: KLRD and the Historical Society have student materials online.

## What to tell callers now (October)

"We'd love to have your group. The best time is during the session, January through early April, on a Tuesday, Wednesday or Thursday morning. Book your tour with the Capitol tour desk at (785) 296-3966, and call our office with a couple of dates so we can set up time with Marla."

## Checklist for the teacher or leader (send with the confirmation)

- Tour booked with the Capitol tour desk (date, time, number of people).
- Our office told the date, time of arrival and group size (two weeks ahead at least).
- Chaperones: name and cell of the lead chaperone for Pat.
- Name tags with first names help Marla.
- Questions for Marla: have the kids write two or three ahead of time. Favorites so far: "How does a bill become a law?" "What's the hardest part of your job?" "Do you still feed cows?"
- After the visit: a class photo emailed to Kelsey can go on the office Facebook page with the teacher's okay (no student names).
