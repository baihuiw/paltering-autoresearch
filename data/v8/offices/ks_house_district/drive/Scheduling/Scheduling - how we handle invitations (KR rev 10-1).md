# How we handle invitations and scheduling requests

KR, Rev. Oct 1, 2026. Approved BH.

## Where requests come from

Contact form (topic "Invitation / Scheduling"), phone, email to Marla or to Kelsey, and people catching Marla at events. Marla is asked to tell people "send it to Kelsey" rather than saying yes on the spot. If she does say yes on the spot, she texts Kelsey the same day.

## What to get

1. Organization and contact person (name, phone, email).
2. Event, date, start time and how long they want her.
3. Place (full address).
4. What they want: attend, short remarks, a speech, Q&A, a panel, a tour.
5. Audience size and who they are.
6. Press expected? Is it a political event? (Political events go to Shane Dorrell, not the office; Brent decides.)

## How we decide

- Kelsey checks the calendar (MB calendar, working) and holds the date in pencil.
- Kelsey brings the week's requests to the Monday staff meeting. Routine yeses (civic clubs, schools, 4-H, churches, county events) Brent can approve. Anything with a conflict, travel more than 90 minutes, or a political angle goes to Marla.
- We answer every request within a week, even if the answer is "we'll know by [date]."
- Marla's standing priorities: town halls in each county; schools; ag and civic groups; anything in a town she hasn't visited yet.

## Standing limits

- **Fall 2026:** weeknight town halls through Oct 27. Saturdays are mostly ranch and family. Candidate events and forums are handled by Shane, not this office.
- **Interim committee days** in Topeka are blocked.
- **November:** open after Nov 4 for district visits; superintendent meetings weeks of Nov 9 and 16; Veterans Day program Nov 11; Lions Nov 10; Strong City school Nov 17; 4-H judging Nov 21. Thanksgiving week blocked.
- **December:** new-member orientation in Topeka (early December, dates TBA). Otherwise open.
- **Session (Jan 11 - spring):** Marla in Topeka Monday afternoon through Thursday (sometimes Friday). District events Friday afternoons, Friday evenings and Saturdays. Lunch talks in the district on weekdays are hard in session.

## Speaking

- Civic club lunch talks: 15-20 minutes plus questions. Marla's standard talk: "What I expect in the 2027 session" (property taxes, rural schools, roads and bridges, rural health and EMS, water). Brent keeps the current notes.
- She doesn't use slides. She likes a podium or a table, and a glass of water.
- Kelsey sends a one-paragraph bio and a photo if asked (in the Press folder).

## Confirming

- Confirm in writing (email) with date, time, place, contact's cell number and what she's doing.
- Put it on the calendar with the contact's cell in the notes.
- Day before: Kelsey texts Marla the details and directions.

## Declining

Decline kindly and offer something else (another date, a visit to the office, a call). Use the Lahr letter (OL-26-027) as a model.

## Requests for November and December

Most November weekdays are open after the election except the dates listed above. When someone asks for a November lunch or evening, check the calendar, hold the date in pencil, and tell them we'll confirm within a week. Marla's preference is to say yes to civic clubs in towns she hasn't spoken in yet (Fredonia, Neodesha, Toronto, Madison, Hartford) and to keep Wednesdays and Saturdays light for the ranch when she can.
