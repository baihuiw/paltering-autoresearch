# Office hours and front desk procedures

District Office of State Representative Marla Brockhoff, 118 W. Second St., Eureka, KS 67045. Rev. Oct 7, 2026 (KR; approved BH).

## Hours

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday - Thursday | 9:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. (walk-ins welcome) |
| Friday | By appointment, 9:00 a.m. - noon (afternoons by exception) |
| Saturday - Sunday | Closed |

- **Friday appointments:** call (620) 583-4130 or use the contact form at repbrockhoff.com at least one business day ahead. 30-minute slots. Brent (or Kelsey) covers Fridays. If the visit needs Diane (casework), we either book a Thursday slot with her or take the paperwork Friday and she makes the agency contact Monday.
- **Exceptions:** Fri Oct 16 opens at 10 (Chamber coffee). Closed Nov 11 morning (Veterans Day program) and Thanksgiving Thursday and Friday. Holiday hours for December to be posted.
- **Session (from Jan 11):** same district office hours. Marla is in Topeka most weekdays; her session office is Room 151-S at the Statehouse.

## Phones

- Main line (620) 583-4130 rings at the front desk; rolls to Diane after 4 rings.
- Voicemail checked at 9:00, noon and 3:30, and Friday morning.
- Callback promise: by the end of the next business day (Fridays count).
- Phone messages go on the message pad and, at the end of the day, into Kelsey's weekly "phone messages" email. Casework messages go straight to Diane's sheet.

## Walk-ins

- Greet, offer a chair and water. Sign-in sheet: name, town, what they came for. No case details on the sign-in sheet.
- Agency questions: casework intake sheet; get the agency's authorization form signed if needed; tell them what happens next and when they'll hear from us.
- If Diane is with someone, take the intake and promise a call the same day.
- Opinions: write it down accurately and pass it on. Thank them. No arguing.
- If someone is upset: listen, take notes, offer Brent. If anyone threatens anyone, call 911 and then Brent.

## Contact form

- Kelsey triages every morning. Routing by topic (see the 9/24 routing email). People often pick the wrong topic, so read each one.
- Target: reply within 3 business days. A follow-up from someone we didn't answer goes to the top with an apology.

## Mail

- Incoming mail opened daily; letters logged and routed like the contact form.
- Outgoing letters get a number (OL-26-xxx) and a copy goes in Correspondence.

## Closing

- Lights, space heater off, back door locked, front door locked, alarm set. Last one out texts Brent.
- Sandwich board inside.

## Front desk FAQ

- *"Is the office open Fridays?"* By appointment, 9 to noon. Book by phone or the contact form at least one business day ahead.
- *"Can I see Marla?"* She's in the office most Tuesdays and Thursdays when she isn't in Topeka. Kelsey can set a time; drop-ins are fine but she may not be here.
- *"Do I need an appointment Monday-Thursday?"* No. Walk in.
- *"Do you notarize?"* No. The bank and the courthouse usually do.
- *"Can you make copies or send a document for me?"* We'll copy anything for an agency case.
