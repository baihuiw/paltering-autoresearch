# Files and records: how we keep them

BH, Sept 21, 2026 (day one). Short rules so we can find things in six months.

## Shared drive folders

| Folder | What goes there |
|---|---|
| Legislation | Bill ideas, bill draft requests, position notes, research we've asked for, bill tracking |
| Interim Committee | Interim committee materials and notes |
| Casework | Casework sheet, agency contact log, how-to sheets for each agency. Case details stay here only |
| District Projects | Local projects with a state connection: support letters, project notes, the tracker |
| Newsletter and Press | Releases, columns, statements, Facebook schedule |
| Correspondence | Copies of outgoing letters with the incoming message above the reply |
| Staff Email | Email threads worth keeping, exported one thread per file |
| Scheduling | Marla's calendar (working copy), invitations log, town hall list |
| Office Admin | Procedures, ledger, lease, contacts, weekly reports |

## Naming

- Start dated files with the date: 2026-10-05 release - ..., 2026-10-07 ltr Lastname - topic.
- Working documents: put your initials and "working" or "draft" in the name; "FINAL" only when it's final.
- Don't put a constituent's case details in a file name. Last name and a short topic is fine for letters.

## Numbering

- Cases: C26-0001 and up (casework sheet).
- Agency contacts: AC-26-001 and up (agency contact log). Log the same day.
- Outgoing letters: OL-26-001 and up. Kelsey assigns the number when the letter is approved.

## Email

- Official business on the house.ks.gov account (Marla) and the office accounts (us). No office business on personal email or Facebook Messenger.
- Election or political email: not on office accounts. Forward anything political to the right place and delete it from the office account.
- Export threads we need to keep into Staff Email (one thread per file).

## Paper

- Signed authorization forms: in the case folder (gray box) and scanned to the case.
- Original letters from constituents: scan, then file by month.
- Shred anything with a Social Security number, account number or medical information once it's scanned and the case is closed.

## Keeping and deleting

- Keep case files at least two years after closing, then review.
- Keep correspondence copies for the term.
- Don't delete anything on the shared drive without telling Brent.

## Privacy

- Casework details stay in Casework. Don't put names and problems in Staff Email threads beyond what's needed ("Dahlke case - KDOL answered" is fine).
- Don't discuss a constituent's case with anyone but that constituent (or someone they've authorized in writing).

## Month-end (Brent)

- Check the ledger against the bank statement for the officeholder account.
- Archive the month's contact form messages (export to PDF) and clear the inbox to "answered" or "open".
- Make sure every open case on the casework sheet has a follow-up date.
- Back up the shared drive to the external drive in the file cabinet.
