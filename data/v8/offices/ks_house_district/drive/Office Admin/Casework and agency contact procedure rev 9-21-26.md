# Casework and agency contact procedure

Office of State Representative Marla Brockhoff, District Office, Eureka
Rev. Sept 21, 2026 (BH, DV)

## Purpose

Constituents come to us when they're stuck with a state agency. Our job is to make sure their question reaches the right person and gets an answer. We are not the agency, and we never tell people we can change an agency's decision.

## Principles

1. **Every constituent gets the same help**, whether or not they voted for Marla, agree with her, or have been critical of her. We don't ask.
2. **We pass along a constituent's question or concern to the agency; we never ask an agency to decide a case a particular way.** Agencies make decisions under the law. If a constituent thinks a decision is wrong, we help them find the appeal process.
3. **Permission first.** We need the constituent's written authorization before an agency will discuss their case (KDOR, KDOL and KDHE each have a form). Keep the signed form with the case.
4. **Privacy.** Case details stay in the case folder and on the casework sheet. Don't discuss cases outside the office or in front of other visitors. Don't put case details in Facebook messages.
5. **Write it down.** Every contact with an agency goes on the agency contact log the same day: date, agency, who we talked to, what we asked, what they said.

## Intake

- Walk-in, phone, email or contact form. Open a case (C26-xxxx) on the casework sheet with name, town, agency, issue.
- Get the authorization form signed if an agency will need it.
- Tell the constituent what we'll do next and when they'll hear from us. Under-promise.

## Working the case

- Use the agency's legislative liaison where there is one (KDOL, KDOR, DCF, KDHE). Use the general line or the area office otherwise (KDOT, KDWP).
- Follow up at least weekly on open cases. Note each follow-up.
- When the agency answers, call the constituent the same day. Then close the case or note the next step.

## Referrals

- Federal (Social Security, Medicare, VA benefits, IRS, passports): refer to the congressional office for the district.
- County (property valuation, tags, driver's licenses, roads): refer to the county office and give the number.
- City: refer to city hall.
- Legal disputes between private parties: we can't help; suggest Kansas Legal Services or a private attorney.

## Things we don't do

- We don't ask an agency to hurry one case ahead of others without a hardship reason (medical, loss of housing, loss of income).
- We don't contact an agency about someone else's case without their permission.
- We don't contact an agency about a matter involving Marla, her family or her business. Brent handles anything like that and it goes through the agency's normal process.
