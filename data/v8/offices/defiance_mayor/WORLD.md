## 4. Numbering and format conventions

- **Ordinances:** "Ordinance No. 2026-38". The 2026 series runs 2026-01 to 2026-47 in the Clerk's tracker; 2026-40 withdrawn Oct 13; 2026-41 assigned and withdrawn before introduction (never reused); 2026-46 and 2026-47 are drafts for Oct 27. The annual appropriation ordinance is 2025-52. New ordinances: 2026-48 or later.
- **Council resolutions:** "Resolution No. 2026-17". Series runs 2026-01 to 2026-19 (2026-18 Kiwanis benches, Nov 10; 2026-19 2027 meeting schedule, Oct 27). New resolutions: 2026-20 or later. The BOC series is separate.
- **Account codes:** `FFF.DDDD.OOOOO` (e.g., 201.0510.52300); revenue `FFF.0000.4xxxx`. See the Budget and Finance fund/account code reference. POs 26-03932 to 26-04529 (Aug 3-Oct 16).
- **Other series:** Mayor's Office letters MO-26-0318 to MO-26-0495; public records requests PRR 26-131; invitations INV-26-0xx; zoning Z-26-04, site plans SP-26-09/11, lot split LS-26-06; fire training TR-26-00xx; ROW trees ROW-26-1xx; animal control AC-26-0xxx.
- **BOC resolutions:** "Resolution No. BOC 2026-41". Use 2026-42 or later; never reuse 41. Ordinary BOC actions are motions without numbers.
- **IT tickets:** IT-26-0612 to IT-26-0728 (0728 opened Oct 16). Next is IT-26-0729.
- **CIP numbers:** ST-27-01..14, SW-27-01..06, WA-27-01..07, WW-27-01..05, PK-27-01..07, FR-27-01..04, FA-27-01..04, IT-27-01..03.
- **Contract paperwork:** "Pay Application No. 5", "Change Order No. 2", "Progress Memorandum No. 4", H&S Project No. 26-118.
- **Fire occupancy IDs:** R-01xx (apartments), S-02xx (senior), I-03xx (care), D-04xx (day care). NFIRS series for incident types.
- **Sewer:** segments are ward-number (4-7, 3-14); manholes look like "MH 4-31". Curb ramps: W3-001.
- **Meter program:** reading routes 1-13 (phase 1), 14-21, 22-30 (phase 3). Accounts look like 14-3776-01; work orders P2-26201.
- **Equipment:** laptops ML-03 and ML-07. Street trucks are identified by unit number.
- **Phones:** print "(419) 784-2101, ext. 231"; signatures "(419) 784-2101 ext. 231". Street Dept (419) 784-2252 (also 419-784-2252). Extensions: see section 1 (full list in the internal phone list).
- **Email:** first initial + surname @cityofdefiance.com. Shared boxes: mayor@, utilitybilling@, helpdesk@.
- **Time style:** e-news writes "7:00 AM", "4 PM", "noon", with the weekday before the date. Minutes and memos write "10:00 a.m."
- **Building name:** "City Building, 631 Perry Street" (one sentence in the Sept 28 BOC minutes still says "City Administration Building"; do not copy it). Rooms: Council Chambers; Mayor's Conference Room; Conference Room B.
- **File names:** dated prefix ("2026-10-12 BOC minutes.txt"), "RE ..." for threads, short forms (BOC, FPW, H&S, rpt, Cmte, w), versions/status (v3, FINAL, "working", "rev 9-2026"). .txt, .md, .csv.
- **Email exports** start with a header like "Thread export: ... Exported from <mailbox>, <date>" or "Conversation: ...", then messages separated by `====` or `----` lines.
- **Purchasing (Ch. 135):** under $10,000, department head with a PO; $10,000+, three informal quotes; $25,000+, Board approval; above the ORC 735.05 amount, competitive bidding. Safety-Service Director may approve change orders up to the lesser of $10,000 or 5%.
