## What is it?

The City of Defiance has a property tax levy that pays for part of the cost of fire protection and emergency medical service (EMS). The current levy was approved by voters and expires at the end of 2027. City Council will discuss at a work session on **Tuesday, October 27, at 5:30 PM** in Council Chambers whether to ask voters to renew it.

A renewal continues the levy at the same rate. It is a **renewal, no increase in the rate**.

## What does the levy pay for?

The levy pays for about **31 percent** of the Fire Department's operating budget. The rest comes from the City's general fund and EMS transport billing.

In practical terms, the levy pays mainly for:

- **Staffing the third ambulance (Medic 3).** When both front-line ambulances are already on calls, Medic 3 (or a cross-staffed engine) takes the next call. In September 2026, that happened 17 times.
- **Apparatus replacement.** Fire engines and ambulances are expensive and last 15 to 25 years. The levy sets money aside so the City can replace them on schedule. The next engine replacement (Engine 2) is planned for 2028 at about $890,000.
- **Equipment.** Breathing air packs, heart monitors, rescue tools, and similar equipment.

## What happens if it is not renewed?

The levy would stop being collected after 2027. The City would need to find the money elsewhere in the budget or reduce the services the levy pays for. Council and the Administration would decide how.
