# Fire Prevention Week 2026 - Plan

Defiance Fire Department, Fire Prevention Bureau
Version 3, September 25, 2026 (Lt. E. Mayes)
Distribution: Chief Rummel; shift captains; Hannah Brubaker (Mayor's Office, social media)

## Dates

Sunday, October 4 through Saturday, October 10, 2026.

## Goals

1. Reach every kindergarten through third-grade classroom in the city schools and the two parochial schools.
2. Sign up at least 40 households for the home smoke alarm program.
3. Hold the Station 1 open house (Saturday, October 10).
4. Reach older residents through talks in senior building community rooms and the senior center.
5. Get a smoke alarm and home escape plan message out on the city's social media every day of the week.

## School visits

- 30-minute classroom program: what a smoke alarm sounds like, get low and go, meet a firefighter in gear, and the "two ways out" home escape plan worksheet.
- Each child takes home the escape plan worksheet and a letter to parents with the smoke alarm sign-up number.
- Engine crews stay in service and available. If a crew is dispatched, the Fire Prevention Bureau finishes the visit or reschedules it.
- Teachers have been sent confirmation emails with times (FF/Insp. Heidt, 9/21).

## Senior talks

Topics: cooking safety, space heaters and extension cords, "Close Before You Doze," what to do when the building alarm sounds (stay or go, depending on the building's plan), keeping walkers and wheelchairs near the bed, and File of Life.

The two senior apartment talks this week are at buildings that have not had a talk in the last two years. Ashford Place and Heritage House were done in September. Other buildings will be offered dates in November and December.

## Station 1 open house (Saturday, October 10, 10:00-2:00)

- Apparatus display: Engine 1, Medic 1, Utility 1. (Ladder 1 will not be on display if still out of service; we will put a photo board in the bay instead.)
- Smoke house trailer (borrowed from the county, confirmed for 10/9 pickup).
- Extinguisher training with the digital trainer, every 30 minutes.
- Smoke alarm sign-up table and File of Life packets.
- Kids' activities: hose target, coloring, junior firefighter helmets (500 ordered).
- Hot dogs and drinks from the firefighters' association.
- Child car seat checks by the two certified technicians (by appointment through the bureau).

Staffing: off-duty volunteers from the firefighters' association plus the on-duty crew from Station 1. Engine 1 stays in service; if it goes out on a call, the display continues with Medic 1 and Utility 1.
