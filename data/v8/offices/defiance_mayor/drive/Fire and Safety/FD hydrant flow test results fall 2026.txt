1. PURPOSE

The Utilities Division flushes all 1,412 public hydrants each spring and fall and maintains them. The Fire Department's part is (a) flow testing a rotating sample of hydrants each year to confirm available fire flow, which feeds the department's pre-incident plans and the community's fire protection rating, and (b) a quick visual check of hydrants in each company's first-due area. This fall's flow testing was done alongside the Utilities flushing crews from September 14 through October 1 so that we only disturbed each area once.

3. METHOD

Two-hydrant test: static and residual pressure read at the test hydrant; flow measured with a pitot gauge at the flowing hydrant (2-1/2 inch outlet, coefficient 0.9). Available flow calculated at 20 psi residual. Results are entered into the records system so they show up on the pre-plan for nearby occupancies. Tests were run between 9:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. to avoid peak demand.

Tests were coordinated with the Water Treatment Plant each morning. Operators reported no pressure complaints related to the testing.
