Noise. More than half of September's noise calls came in late on Friday and Saturday nights: house parties, car stereos, and amplified music at a few bars downtown and on Clinton St. An officer responds while the noise is happening; repeat locations get a warning first, then a citation under the noise ordinance. Ongoing equipment noise (fans, compressors) goes to code enforcement in Building and Zoning, not patrol. Residents should call the non-emergency line, (419) 784-5050, while it is going on; an email the next morning doesn't let us do much.

Parking. Most complaints are cars blocking driveways, overtime in the downtown two-hour spaces, and vehicles parked partly on the sidewalk. Lt. Bechtol has asked the weekend shift to check the downtown blocks of Clinton St on Saturday mornings, when sidewalk parking near the churches and the farmers market comes up most.
