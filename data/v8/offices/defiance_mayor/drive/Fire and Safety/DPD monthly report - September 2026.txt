======================================================================
9. NOTES FOR THE MAYOR ON READING THIS REPORT
======================================================================

- "Calls for service" counts every logged event, including things officers start themselves, like traffic stops and business checks. A rise in dispatched calls is the better measure of demand.
- Offenses use the national NIBRS categories required by the state. One incident can have more than one offense.
- Year-to-date comparisons are more meaningful than single months. A single month can swing on two or three incidents.
- Response times are measured from dispatch to first officer on scene, inside city limits.
