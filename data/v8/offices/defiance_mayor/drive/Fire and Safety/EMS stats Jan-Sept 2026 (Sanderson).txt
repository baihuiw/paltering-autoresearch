4. CARDIAC ARREST AND RESPONSE TIMES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Out-of-hospital cardiac arrests, Jan-Sept: 29. Resuscitation attempted 24; bystander CPR before EMS arrival 11 of 24 (46 percent, up from 38 percent in 2025); pulses back at the ED 8 of 24. The hands-only CPR classes at the farmers market Safety Day and the high school helped. We'd like to repeat the class at the Senior Services Initiative kickoff if the program allows.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5. PATIENT AND FAMILY FEEDBACK
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Eight thank-you cards, calls or emails in September, most about crews being patient with an elderly parent. Comments that come to the Mayor's Office or the front desk can go to me (ext. 252) or to the station; I read them at shift change and copy the card into the crew's file. If the family doesn't have names, a date, time and address is enough for us to find the run. No complaints this month.
