6. UNIT UTILIZATION (September)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Unit hour utilization = hours committed to calls divided by hours staffed.

Medic 1 ......... 0.29
Medic 2 ......... 0.26
Medic 3 ......... 0.08 (cross-staffed when at minimum)

National guidance treats anything above about 0.30-0.35 on a 24-hour unit as a busy unit where crews start to feel worn down. Medic 1 has been at or above 0.28 every month since May.

- Drug shortage: dextrose 10% backordered; using the alternate concentration per the medical director's memo of Aug 28.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
8. BILLING (EMS transport, for the levy discussion)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

EMS transport billing is handled by the department's third-party billing vendor and goes to the EMS fund. Through August, gross charges were $2.31 million and net collections $1.02 million (44 percent), typical for our payer mix (Medicare and Medicaid make up about 68 percent of transports). The vendor's quarterly report comes in mid-October and will be in the levy deck backup.

Average cost per run (all runs, fire and EMS, total department operating cost divided by runs, 2025): about $1,040. This is the figure Chief Rummel is using in the levy materials.
