3. PLAN REVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

Four plan reviews were completed in September:

- Wayne Ave. warehouse, Lot 3: fire alarm shop drawings for tenant fit-out (approved; acceptance test completed 09/17).
- Ralston Ave. medical office addition: sprinkler and alarm drawings (approved with two comments on FDC location; resubmittal received 09/29 and approved).

Average turnaround was 6 working days against the bureau's 10-day target. Two reviews were received through the building department's new portal, which forwards to the bureau automatically.

4. FIRE INVESTIGATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Two origin-and-cause investigations were conducted:

- 09/06, single-family dwelling, Second Ward: kitchen fire confined to the range and cabinets. Cause: unattended cooking. Accidental. Working smoke alarm alerted the occupants; no injuries. Estimated loss $14,000.
- 09/26, detached garage, Fourth Ward: fire originated at a workbench area. Cause: battery charger for a lawn tractor, accidental. No injuries. Estimated loss $9,500 (structure and contents).

Neither investigation required referral to the state fire marshal or law enforcement.

6. INSPECTION SOFTWARE TRANSITION
------------------------------------------------------------

The new inspection and records software went live on Monday, September 14, as planned. Status at month end:

- All inspections from 09/14 forward were entered in the field on the tablets.
- September inspections from 09/01-09/11 (15 records) were back-entered from paper forms on 09/16-09/18.
- The occupancy list (about 1,140 occupancies) was imported from the old spreadsheet. Approximately 60 records needed cleanup for duplicate addresses or missing owner contacts; 41 were cleaned up by 09/30, the rest are in progress.
- Reinspection reminders now generate automatically on the due date and appear on the inspector's dashboard. The October dashboard was checked on 10/01 and shows all open orders.
- Letter templates (orders, reinspection notices, occupant notices, plan review comments) were loaded and tested on 09/15.
- Report printouts now carry the occupancy ID; this report's table uses the short occupancy names for readability.

Known issues reported to the vendor: the tablet app occasionally logs out when moving between buildings with poor cell coverage (workaround: save before leaving each floor); and photo attachments over 5 MB fail to upload (workaround: lower camera resolution). Neither has caused data loss.

Training: both inspectors completed vendor training on 09/09-09/10. Engine company officers will receive a shorter module in October so that company-level inspections can be entered directly.
