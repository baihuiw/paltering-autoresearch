# Community Town Hall with Mayor Haverkamp - Planning Doc

**Thursday, November 19, 2026, 6:30-8:00 PM**
**Council Chambers, City Building, 631 Perry Street**

v2, Oct 16, 2026 (M. Ostrander; updates from B. Kahle and H. Brubaker). v1 was Oct 9.

---

## 1. Purpose

An open evening where residents can ask the mayor and department heads about city services. No set agenda beyond a short update from the mayor. This is a city meeting about city business. Keep all materials, remarks and handouts to city services, projects and the budget.

Why now: the two ward meetings (Second Ward 9/22, Fourth Ward 10/15) were well attended and the same questions kept coming up. The budget hearing will have happened on Nov 10, so the mayor can talk about the 2027 budget with real numbers. It's also before Thanksgiving and the first snow.

## 6. Questions: open mic and cards

- Open mic: line up at the two roaming mics. 3 minutes per person, one question plus one follow-up. Megan runs the timer.
- Cards: available at the sign-in table and on every other chair. Hannah collects them during the first 20 minutes and sorts by topic for Megan.
- Any question we don't get to gets a written answer within two weeks. Luis keeps the list. Answers posted on the website as a Q&A page if there are more than ten.
- Questions about a specific property, account or case are handled by Luis afterward, not at the mic. Megan will say so in the housekeeping.
- Personnel and pending legal matters: the mayor will say he can't discuss them and offer to follow up where appropriate.
