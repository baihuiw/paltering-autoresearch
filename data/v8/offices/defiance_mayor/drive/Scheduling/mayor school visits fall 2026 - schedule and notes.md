## Notes from visits so far

**Ottawa Elementary (9/22).** Went very well. He read for about 10 minutes and took questions for 20. Best question: "Do you have a boss?" (Answer: "Everyone in Defiance.") The kids asked about the pool closing, the playground at Pontiac Park, and whether he drives a snowplow. A 4th grade teacher asked if he'd come back in January for their Ohio history unit. Book donated to the classroom library.

**DHS government class (9/29).** Students had prepared questions on how a city budget works, why water rates went up, the fire levy, and whether the city can do anything about vaping near the school (police/school matter; he referred it to the SRO and the principal). Teacher asked for a copy of the budget calendar; Angela's budget schedule was sent 10/8. Two students asked about internships. Kim says the city doesn't have a formal program; Megan to talk with Kim about a spring internship in the Mayor's Office.

**DMS career day (10/6).** Two sessions, about 25 students each. Mostly asked how much the mayor gets paid (public record; he answered) and whether he has a bodyguard (no). He brought the city flag, which was a hit. Lt. Mayes was in the next room with the smoke trailer for Fire Prevention Week, so we walked the kids over at the end.

**Head Start (10/13).** Short and loud. The firefighters loaned six kids' fire hats. Center director asked about the crosswalk outside the building on drop-off; passed to Lt. Bechtol for the city-schools liaison list (it's not a district building, but the traffic issue is the same).

**Defiance College student government (10/16).** About 20 students. Topics: off-campus housing conditions (referred to Building and Zoning and Luis for specific addresses), lighting on the riverfront trail at night (Craig: the paved section to the Pontiac St bridge is lit; the rest is not and isn't planned), and volunteering. Several environmental club members were already signed up for the tree planting on the 17th.
