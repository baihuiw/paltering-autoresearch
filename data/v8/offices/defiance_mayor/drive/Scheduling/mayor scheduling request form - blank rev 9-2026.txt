Notes for staff:
- Log every request on the invitations tracker in Scheduling, even ones we decline.
- Proclamations: the mayor signs proclamations for events with a clear community or public-service purpose. We don't do proclamations for individual businesses' anniversaries; offer a congratulatory letter instead.
- Private weddings: the mayor is not officiating private ceremonies at this time. Refer to the municipal court.
