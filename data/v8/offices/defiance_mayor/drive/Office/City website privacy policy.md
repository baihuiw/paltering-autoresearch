# Website Privacy Policy

City of Defiance, Ohio
cityofdefiance.com

Posted on the city website (footer link "Privacy Policy"). Last revised: March 4, 2024.

---

## Our commitment

The City of Defiance respects the privacy of everyone who visits our website or signs up for city services online. This policy explains what information the city collects through cityofdefiance.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies only to the city's official website and the online services linked from it. It does not apply to other websites that we link to, including those of Defiance County, the State of Ohio, the school district or private organizations.

The City of Defiance does not sell personal information.

## Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, our web server automatically records certain technical information, such as:

- the internet address (IP address) of the device you use;
- the type of browser and operating system you use;
- the date and time of your visit;
- the pages you view and the website that referred you to us.

We use this information in summary form to see how many people use the site, which pages are most popular, and how to make the site work better. We do not use it to identify individual visitors, except when needed to investigate an attack on the website or as required by law.

### Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies, which are small text files stored by your browser. We use cookies to keep the site working (for example, to remember your place in an online form) and to count visits. You can set your browser to refuse cookies. Most of the site will still work, but some online forms may not.

## Information you choose to give us

You do not have to give us any personal information to browse the website. We collect personal information only when you give it to us, for example when you:

- sign up for the city's e-news or emergency notifications;
- submit a question or comment through the online contact form or email a city office;
- apply for a permit, register for a parks program or reserve a shelter house;
- pay a utility bill, ticket or fee online;
- apply for a job with the city.

It may include your name, address, email address, phone number and, for payments, billing information.

## How we use your information

We use the information you give us to provide the service you asked for and to respond to you. For example:

- Contact form messages and emails are routed to the city office that can answer them.
- Permit, program and reservation information is used to process your application or registration.
- Payment information is used only to process your payment.

### E-news and notifications

When you sign up for the city's e-news, we ask for your name and email address and, if you choose, your ward. We use e-news addresses to send the city updates you signed up for, such as trash and recycling schedule changes, road closures, leaf pickup, parks programs and community events. Providing your ward lets us send you notices that apply only to your part of the city.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any e-news message or through the manage-subscription page on our website. Unsubscribe requests are processed right away.

Emergency notifications (for example, severe weather or emergency closures) are sent through a separate system. If you sign up for emergency notifications by text message, standard message and data rates may apply.

## Online payments

Online payments are handled by a third-party payment processor under contract with the city. Card numbers are entered on the processor's secure page and are not stored on city servers. The processor may use your information only to complete the transaction and as its own agreement with the city allows.

## Service providers

The city uses outside companies to host the website, run the e-news service and process payments. These companies may handle your information only to provide services to the city.

## Public records

The City of Defiance is a public office subject to the Ohio Public Records Act. Information you send to the city, including messages sent through the website, may become a public record and may be released in response to a public records request, unless an exception in state or federal law applies. Please do not send confidential or sensitive personal information (such as Social Security numbers or medical information) through the contact form or by email.

## Security

The city takes reasonable steps to protect the information we collect, including secure connections (HTTPS) for online forms and payments and limited staff access to stored information. No website or internet transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information you send us online.

## Links to other sites

Our website links to other government agencies, community organizations and businesses for your convenience. When you follow a link to another site, that site's privacy policy applies. The city is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other sites.

## Changes to this policy

The city may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Changes take effect when they are posted.

## Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or about information the city holds about you, contact:

City of Defiance
