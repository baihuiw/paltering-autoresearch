# City of Defiance Social Media Policy (City Accounts)

**Administrative Policy No. 2019-04, revised June 2025**
Issued by: Office of the Mayor
Applies to: all city departments, employees and volunteers who create or manage content on city social media accounts
Related documents: e-news style guide (rev. Feb 2025); City website privacy policy; Records retention schedule (item MO-11); Employee Handbook, Section 7 (Use of City Technology)

---

## 1. Purpose

The City of Defiance uses social media to share timely information with residents: road closures, trash schedule changes, events, emergency notices and city news. This policy sets out who may run city accounts, how accounts are created and secured, what kind of content belongs on them, and how the city keeps the records the law requires.

This policy covers official city accounts only. Employees' personal social media use is addressed in the Employee Handbook, Section 7.4.

## 2. Official accounts

### 2.1 Current accounts

| Account | Platform | Managed by | Backup administrator |
|---|---|---|---|
| City of Defiance (main) | Facebook | Communications Assistant | Chief of Staff |
| Defiance Parks and Recreation | Facebook | Recreation Coordinator | Communications Assistant |
| Defiance Fire Division | Facebook | Fire Prevention Officer | Fire Chief |
| Defiance Police Department | Facebook | Patrol Division Commander | Police Chief |

The city does not currently operate accounts on other platforms. A second city account opened on another platform in 2019 was closed in 2024 because of low use; its archive is kept by IT.

### 2.2 Creating a new account

No department may create a social media account in the city's name, or using the city seal or a department logo, without written approval from the Chief of Staff. Requests should explain the purpose, the audience, who will maintain it and how often, and why the main city account will not work. Approved accounts must be registered with IT (helpdesk@) and added to the table above.

### 2.3 Account security

- Accounts are created using a city email address, never a personal one.
- Each account must have at least two administrators who are current city employees.
- Two-factor authentication is required. Passwords are stored in the IT password vault, not in personal notes or phones.
- When an administrator leaves city employment or changes jobs, the department head notifies IT the same day, and the person's access is removed.
- Suspected hacking or a suspicious login notice is reported to IT immediately. Do not reply to messages claiming to be from the platform asking for login information.

## 3. Content

### 3.1 What we post

- Service information: trash and recycling changes, leaf collection, street closures and detours, water main breaks, hydrant flushing, snow emergencies.
- Events: festivals, open houses, volunteer days, Council and committee meetings.
- Programs and services: smoke alarm program, shelter reservations, permits, seasonal reminders.
- City news: completed projects, awards, new employees, retirements (with the employee's permission).
- Emergency and public safety information, coordinated with the Fire Division and Police Department.

### 3.2 Writing posts

- Follow the e-news style guide for dates, times and names ("Saturday, October 10, 10 AM to 4 PM"; weekday before the date).
- Put the most important fact first: what, where, when.
- Keep posts short. Link to the city website for details.
- Use plain language. Avoid acronyms that residents won't know (BOC, CIP, WPCF) or spell them out.
- Include a phone number when residents may need to call someone.
- Check dates, times and street names with the department before posting. Construction dates should be confirmed by Engineering or the Street Department the day before.
- When information changes (a closure ends early, an event is moved), post an update promptly and edit the original post to point to the update.

### 3.3 Photos and video

- Wide shots of public events are fine. Do not post close-up photos of children without a parent's permission.
- Signs are posted at entrances to city events stating that photos may be taken.
- Do not post photos that show residents' license plates, house numbers in connection with a call for service, or medical care.
- Credit photos taken by non-employees.

### 3.4 What we do not post

- Personal information about residents or employees (addresses, phone numbers, account information, health information).
- Information about active police or fire investigations, except as released by the Police Chief or Fire Chief.
- Endorsements of private businesses or products, except neutral announcements of ribbon cuttings, Chamber events and city vendors' work on city projects.
- Copyrighted material the city does not have permission to use.
- Jokes or memes at the expense of residents.

### 3.5 Approvals

| Content | Approval needed |
|---|---|
| Routine service information and event reminders | Account manager |
| Department news (projects, programs) | Department head |
| Statements, quotes or positions of the Mayor | Chief of Staff |
| Emergency notices | Incident commander or department head; Chief of Staff informed |
| Paid promotion ("boosted" posts) | Chief of Staff and Finance (purchase order) |

## 4. Comments and messages

### 4.1 Public comments

City Facebook pages allow public comments. The following notice is posted in the "About" section of each page:

> This is an official page of the City of Defiance. Comments are welcome. The city may remove comments that contain obscene or sexually explicit material; threats of violence; personal information about others; spam or advertising; content that promotes illegal activity; or material that infringes copyright. Comments on this page are public records. For service requests, please call the appropriate department or use the "Report a Problem" form on cityofdefiance.com; requests made in comments may not be seen promptly.

Comments are moderated under guidance issued by the Law Director (May 2025). Before removing any comment, the account manager takes a screenshot and saves it to the social media archive folder with the date and the reason for removal. Users are not blocked without the Law Director's approval.

### 4.2 Questions and service requests

When a comment or direct message asks a factual question (trash day, event time, who to call), the account manager may answer it with the factual information or a phone number, or refer the person to the right department. Service requests (potholes, streetlights, code complaints) are forwarded to the Constituent Services Coordinator or the department so they are logged. Account managers do not handle individual account or billing questions in comments or messages; the resident is given the department phone number.

### 4.3 Response times

Account managers check comments and messages at least twice each business day (morning and afternoon). Messages received after hours are answered the next business day. The page's automatic reply states that messages are monitored during business hours and that emergencies should be reported to 911.

## 5. Records

Social media posts, comments and messages on city accounts are public records. They are retained under item MO-11 of the Mayor's Office records retention schedule. The Communications Assistant captures each account's content monthly using the city's archiving tool and saves the export to the shared drive. Account managers must not delete posts to avoid retention; corrections are made by editing or by posting an update.

## 6. Emergencies

During an emergency (severe weather, a major water main break, a hazardous materials incident, a boil advisory), the incident commander or department head approves emergency posts. The Communications Assistant coordinates posting across city accounts so the same information appears everywhere. If the Communications Assistant is unavailable, the Chief of Staff or a backup administrator posts. Emergency posts are pinned to the top of the page until the emergency ends.

## 7. Employee responsibilities

Employees who manage city accounts:
- complete the city's social media training within 30 days of being given access, and every two years after;
- post only from city devices or from the platform's business app logged in with city credentials;
- report mistakes promptly to their supervisor so they can be corrected.

Violations of this policy may result in loss of account access and discipline under the Employee Handbook.
