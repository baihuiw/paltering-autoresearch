CODIFIED ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF DEFIANCE
PART ONE - ADMINISTRATIVE CODE
TITLE THREE - EXECUTIVE BRANCH
CHAPTER 135 - PURCHASING AND CONTRACTS (EXCERPT)

[Office reference copy. Sections 135.01 through 135.12 as amended through Ordinance 2024-37. For the official text consult the Clerk of Council. Sections 135.13 through 135.20 (surplus property, disposal, vendor debarment) omitted.]

135.01 DEFINITIONS.
As used in this chapter:
(a) "Board" means the Board of Control established by the City Charter, consisting of the Mayor, who shall be chair, the Law Director, the Finance Director and the Safety-Service Director.
(b) "Competitive bidding" means the procedure set out in Section 135.03.
(c) "Informal quotation" means a written price quotation obtained without public advertisement.
(d) "Using department" means the department for which supplies, services or construction are purchased.
(e) "Emergency" has the meaning given in Section 135.09.

135.02 AUTHORITY TO CONTRACT; THRESHOLDS.
(a) Purchases and contracts of less than $10,000.00 may be made by the head of the using department with a purchase order certified by the Finance Director.
(b) Purchases and contracts of $10,000.00 or more but not more than the amount set out in Section 135.03(a) shall be made by the Safety-Service Director after obtaining at least three informal quotations, where practicable, and shall be approved by the Board when they are $25,000.00 or more.
(c) Purchases and contracts exceeding the amount set out in Section 135.03(a) shall be awarded by the Board after competitive bidding, except as otherwise provided in this chapter.

135.03 COMPETITIVE BIDDING.
(a) Except as provided in Sections 135.07, 135.08 and 135.09, every contract for supplies, services or construction in an amount exceeding the amount established for municipal corporations in Ohio R.C. 735.05, as that amount may be adjusted, shall be awarded after competitive bidding.
(b) The Safety-Service Director shall cause notice of the bidding to be published once a week for two consecutive weeks in a newspaper of general circulation in the City and may also post the notice on the City website and in electronic bid services. The first publication shall be at least fourteen days before the date set for opening bids.
(c) Plans and specifications shall be on file and available for inspection from the date of first publication.
(d) Bids shall be sealed and opened publicly at the time and place stated in the notice. A bid tabulation shall be made available to the public.
(e) The Board shall award the contract to the lowest and best bidder, or may reject all bids.
(f) The Safety-Service Director may require bid guaranties and performance and payment bonds as provided in Ohio R.C. Chapter 153.

135.04 SPLITTING OF CONTRACTS PROHIBITED.
No purchase or contract shall be divided into two or more purchases or contracts for the purpose of avoiding the requirements of Section 135.02 or 135.03.

135.05 CERTIFICATION OF FUNDS.
No contract or purchase order shall be valid unless the Finance Director has certified that the amount required has been lawfully appropriated and is in the treasury or in the process of collection, as required by Ohio R.C. 5705.41.

135.06 LOWEST AND BEST BIDDER.
In determining the lowest and best bidder the Board may consider, in addition to price, the bidder's experience, past performance on City contracts, financial responsibility, equipment, and the ability to complete the work within the time required. A bidder's location within the City or County shall not be a basis for award except as permitted by state law.

135.07 COOPERATIVE PURCHASING.
The Board may authorize purchases through the cooperative purchasing program of the Ohio Department of Administrative Services, the Ohio Department of Transportation, or any other cooperative program authorized by state law, without competitive bidding under Section 135.03.

135.08 SOLE SOURCE; PROFESSIONAL SERVICES.
(a) The Board may authorize a purchase without competitive bidding where the Safety-Service Director certifies in writing that the supplies or services are available from only one source.
(b) Contracts for professional services, including engineering, architectural, legal and accounting services, are not subject to Section 135.03 and shall be awarded under the procedures of Ohio R.C. 153.65 to 153.71 where applicable.

135.09 EMERGENCIES.
(a) When the Board determines that a real and present emergency exists that threatens the public health, safety or welfare, or that threatens damage to public property or the interruption of an essential public service, the Board may authorize the purchase of supplies or services or the making of a contract for construction without competitive bidding under Section 135.03.
(b) The emergency determination shall be made by resolution of the Board adopted at a public meeting and recorded in its minutes, and the resolution shall state the facts that constitute the emergency.
(c) A contract made under this section shall be limited to the work reasonably necessary to address the emergency.
(d) Where time permits, the Safety-Service Director shall obtain informal quotations before the contract is made.
(e) Nothing in this section relieves the City of the requirement to certify funds under Section 135.05.

135.10 CHANGE ORDERS.
(a) Change orders on contracts awarded by the Board may be approved by the Safety-Service Director up to a cumulative total of $10,000.00 or five percent of the original contract amount, whichever is less. Change orders exceeding that amount shall be approved by the Board.
(b) No change order shall increase a contract awarded after competitive bidding by more than ten percent of the original contract amount, cumulatively, without approval of Council.

135.11 RECORDS.
The Safety-Service Director shall maintain a record of all contracts awarded under this chapter, including the method of award, and shall make the record available for public inspection.

135.12 INTEREST IN CONTRACTS.
No officer or employee of the City shall have an interest in any contract with the City, except as permitted by Ohio R.C. 2921.42 and Chapter 102. Any officer or employee who has or acquires an interest in a matter before the Board shall disclose that interest in writing and shall not vote on the matter.
