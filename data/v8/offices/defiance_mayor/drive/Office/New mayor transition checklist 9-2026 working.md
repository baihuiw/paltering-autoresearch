## D. Things to tell the mayor (running list)

- Board of Control agenda items are due to Janelle by Thursday noon for the Monday meeting.
- Council agendas are posted the Friday before each meeting; the mayor's report items should be to Sandra by Wednesday.
- Department heads meet Mondays at 8:30 a.m. before BOC.
- Rotary is Wednesday lunch; Kiwanis is Thursday lunch. He does not need to attend every week.
- Proclamation requests: at least two weeks' notice, through Brenda; MO approves wording.
- Change orders: the Safety-Service Director can approve up to the lesser of $10,000 or 5 percent; anything above goes to BOC.
- All media calls go through MO first.
- Do not sign anything from Law or Finance that hasn't been initialed by the director.
- He has asked for a one-page weekly calendar on paper Fridays. Brenda prints it.
