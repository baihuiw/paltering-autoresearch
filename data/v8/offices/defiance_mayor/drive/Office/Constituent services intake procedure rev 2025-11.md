# Constituent Services: Intake, Logging and Routing Procedure

**Office of the Mayor, City of Defiance**
Revision: November 2025 (replaces the 2021 version). Owner: Constituent Services Coordinator. Approved by the Chief of Staff.
Signature block updated September 2026 for the new mayor; no other changes.

How the Mayor's Office receives, logs, routes and closes contacts from residents and others.

## 1. Where contacts come in

- **Website contact form** ("Contact the Mayor") and **email to mayor@cityofdefiance.com**: mayor@ inbox; handled by the Coordinator. Mail to the mayor's own city address is forwarded to mayor@.
- **Phone, walk-ins and paper letters**: the front desk takes a contact slip or transfers; letters are scanned for the Coordinator.
- **Facebook** messages asking for service: the Communications Assistant forwards them to mayor@ with a screenshot.
- **Council referrals**: forwarded to mayor@ with "Council referral" in the subject.

The web form asks for name, address, email, phone (optional), a dropdown topic and the message.

## 2. Logging

Every contact that asks a question, requests service or expresses an opinion is logged the day it is received (next business day if after hours) with a log number, MO-YY-NNNN, assigned in order and never reused (duplicates keep their number and are marked "Duplicate of MO-26-____"). Log the channel, name, address and ward, topic, a one-line summary, category, routing, and the dates sent and closed. The front desk puts phone slips in the Coordinator's tray by 11:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m.

## 3. Topics (dropdown list)

General question; Streets & Sidewalks; Trash & Recycling; Water / Sewer; Water / Sewer Billing; Parks; Events; Fire and EMS; Police; Planning / Zoning; Opinion on city business; Other.

Residents often pick "General question" or "Other"; log the topic that fits the message, not the dropdown.

## 4. Categories and routing

- **Opinion** (a Council vote, a project, a policy): logged, summarized weekly, answered by Mayor's Office letter.
- **Information request** (when does leaf pickup start; who do I call about a permit): the Coordinator answers from published information or checks with the department.
- **Casework** (a resident's own account, permit, code complaint, damage claim, service request): routed to the department, which contacts the resident; the Mayor's Office confirms the referral in a short letter.
- **Invitations and meeting requests**: Scheduler (Brenda Kahle). **Press**: Chief of Staff (Megan Ostrander).
- **Legal or threatening** (notice of a claim, a lawsuit, a threat to staff or the mayor): Chief of Staff the same day, with the Law Director.

**How to route:** forward the original message to the department contact with the log number in the subject ("MO-26-0412 - pothole Fulton St"). For Street Department requests, also enter a work order and record its number in the log.

## 5. Response times

Log the same business day (the web form and mayor@ acknowledge automatically). Route casework within 1 business day; the department contacts the resident within 3 business days. Reply to opinions and information requests within 5 business days. Check open casework weekly.

If a reply will take longer (for example, waiting on a department), send a short holding note giving an expected date.

## 6. Letters

- Replies go out over the mayor's signature unless the Chief of Staff directs otherwise. When the office of mayor is vacant, replies are signed by the Chief of Staff for the office.
- Format: opens "Dear [first name]," and closes:

  > Sincerely,
  > Paul Haverkamp
  > Mayor, City of Defiance

- Keep replies short: a few paragraphs, about 150 words or less. Include a department name and phone number whenever the resident may need to follow up.
- Email replies are sent from the mayor@ account. Paper replies go on letterhead and are mailed the day they are signed.
- The Coordinator drafts; the Chief of Staff approves before sending, except standard thank-you notes for congratulations, which the Coordinator may send directly.
- File a copy of each reply in the Correspondence folder, with the resident's original message above it ("YYYY-MM-DD ltr [Surname] - [short topic].txt"), and update the log.

## 7. Closing a contact

A contact is closed when the reply has been sent, when the department reports the case resolved and the resident has been told, or when it has been handed to the Scheduler or Chief of Staff. Do not delete log entries.

## 8. Weekly summary

Each Tuesday by 3:00 p.m., the Coordinator emails the Chief of Staff a summary of the previous week's contacts (Wednesday through Tuesday): totals, counts by topic, items still open, casework open more than 30 days, and anything needing a decision.

## 9. Coverage and records

When the Coordinator is out, the Administrative Assistant checks the mayor@ inbox at 9:00 a.m., noon and 3:30 p.m. The log is a public record.
