# Constituent Services: Intake, Logging and Routing Procedure

**Office of the Mayor, City of Defiance**
Revision: November 2025 (replaces the 2021 version). Owner: Constituent Services Coordinator. Approved by the Chief of Staff.
Signature block updated September 2026 for the new mayor; no other changes.

---

## 1. Scope

This procedure covers how the Mayor's Office receives, logs, routes and closes contacts from residents and others. It applies to the Constituent Services Coordinator, the front desk, the Scheduler, the Communications Assistant, the Administrative Assistant and anyone covering for them.

It is a workflow document. It does not cover the content of replies.

## 2. Where contacts come in

| Channel | Arrives at | First handled by |
|---|---|---|
| Website contact form ("Contact the Mayor") | mayor@ inbox, tagged "Web form" | Constituent Services Coordinator |
| Email to mayor@cityofdefiance.com | mayor@ inbox | Constituent Services Coordinator |
| Email to the mayor's personal city address | Mayor; forwarded to mayor@ | Constituent Services Coordinator |
| Phone (ext. 201 / main line) | Front desk or Mayor's Office | Front desk takes a message on the contact slip, or transfers |
| Walk-in | Front desk | Front desk; Coordinator comes down if available |
| Paper letter | Mail, front desk | Scanned by front desk, original to Coordinator |
| Facebook message or comment asking for service | Communications Assistant | Forwarded to mayor@ with a screenshot |
| Referred by a Council member | Clerk of Council or Chief of Staff | Forwarded to mayor@ with "Council referral" in the subject |

The website contact form asks for name, address, email, phone (optional), a topic from the dropdown list, the message and whether the person wants a response.

## 3. Logging

Every contact that asks a question, requests service or expresses an opinion is logged on the day it is received (or the next business day if it arrives after hours).

**Log number:** MO-YY-NNNN, assigned in order (for example, MO-26-0412). Numbers are not reused. A contact that turns out to be a duplicate keeps its number and is marked "Duplicate of MO-26-____."

**Log fields:**
- Log number
- Date and time received
- Channel
- Name, address and ward (look up the ward on the GIS ward map if not given)
- Contact information
- Topic (see the topic list in Section 4)
- One-line summary
- Category: Opinion / Information request / Casework / Invitation / Press / Other
- Routed to (department and person)
- Response due date
- Date response sent and who signed it
- Date closed
- Staff initials

Spam, sales solicitations and newsletters from other organizations are deleted and not logged.

Phone messages are logged from the contact slip. The front desk puts slips in the Coordinator's tray by 11:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m.

| Casework | A resident's own utility account, permit, code complaint, damage claim, service request | Department (see table below) | Department contacts the resident; Mayor's Office sends a short letter confirming the referral when the resident wrote to the mayor |

## 6. Response times

| Step | Target |
|---|---|
| Log the contact | Same business day |
| Automatic acknowledgment (web form and mayor@) | Immediate (system) |
| Route casework to department | Within 1 business day |
| Department first contact with resident on casework | Within 3 business days |
| Mayor's Office reply to opinions and information requests | Within 5 business days |
| Invitations acknowledged by the Scheduler | Within 3 business days |
| Status check on open casework | Weekly, until closed |
| Casework open more than 30 days | Listed separately on the weekly summary |

If a reply will take longer than five business days (for example, waiting on a department), the Coordinator sends a short holding note giving an expected date.

## 7. Letters

- Replies from the Mayor's Office go out over the mayor's signature unless the Chief of Staff directs otherwise. When the office of mayor is vacant, replies are signed by the Chief of Staff for the office.
- Format: opens "Dear [first name]," and closes:

  > Sincerely,
  > Paul Haverkamp
  > Mayor, City of Defiance

- Keep replies short: a few paragraphs, about 150 words or less.
- Include a department name and phone number whenever the resident may need to follow up.
- Email replies are sent from the mayor@ account. Paper replies go on letterhead and are mailed the day they are signed.
- Drafts are prepared by the Coordinator (or the staff member assigned) and approved by the Chief of Staff before sending, except for the standard thank-you letter for congratulations and welcome notes, which the Coordinator may send directly.
- A copy of each reply is filed in the Correspondence folder on the shared drive, with the resident's original message above it, and the log is updated with the date sent.

**File naming:** "YYYY-MM-DD ltr [Surname] - [short topic].txt". Short replies sent in the same week may be filed together ("letters out wk of MM-DD-YY.txt").

## 8. Closing a contact

A contact is closed when:
- the reply has been sent (opinions and information requests); or
- the department reports that the service request or case is resolved, and the resident has been told (casework); or
- the invitation has been answered (Scheduler); or
- it has been handed to the Chief of Staff (press, legal).

Record the close date and initials in the log. Do not delete log entries.

## 9. Weekly summary

Each Tuesday by 3:00 p.m., the Coordinator emails the Chief of Staff a summary of the previous week's contacts (Wednesday through Tuesday), copying the Scheduler and the Communications Assistant. The summary gives:
- the total number of contacts and the previous week's total;
- counts by topic, with a line or two on each;
- items routed to each department and anything still open;
- casework open more than 30 days;
- anything the Coordinator needs a decision on.

## 10. Coverage

When the Coordinator is out, the Administrative Assistant (or the Communications Assistant) checks the mayor@ inbox at 9:00 a.m., noon and 3:30 p.m., logs new contacts and routes casework. Opinions and information requests wait for the Coordinator's return unless the reply would otherwise be late.

## 11. Records

Contact logs and correspondence are kept under the Mayor's Office records retention schedule (items MO-04, MO-05 and MO-06). The log is a public record.
