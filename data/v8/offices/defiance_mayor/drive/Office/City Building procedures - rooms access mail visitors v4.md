# City Building Procedures

**City Building, 631 Perry Street**
Version 4, updated September 2026 (keycard and room booking changes). Maintained by the Mayor's Office front desk with Building Maintenance.
Questions: Jeanne Hench, front desk (ext. 200) | Rick Zeller, Building Maintenance (ext. 228) | IT: helpdesk@

---

## 1. Hours and access

**Public hours:** Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., except city holidays. The Perry Street entrance is unlocked from 7:45 a.m. to 4:45 p.m. on business days.

**Evening meetings:** For City Council (2nd and 4th Tuesdays, 7:00 p.m.), Committee of the Whole and committee meetings, Planning Commission and other public meetings, the Perry Street entrance stays unlocked until 15 minutes after the meeting adjourns. The Clerk of Council (or the staff person running the meeting) confirms the lobby is empty and the door has locked before leaving.

**Employee access:** Employees enter outside public hours with their keycard at the Perry Street door or the after-hours entrance by the employee lot. Keycards are issued by the Safety-Service Director's office through the alarm company's portal. Report a lost keycard to Tom Wiechers's office immediately so it can be turned off.

- Do not prop doors open.
- Do not let anyone follow you in after hours, including people you know, unless they are city employees with their own card.
- The alarm system arms automatically at 10:00 p.m. on weekdays and all day on weekends and holidays. If you are in the building when it arms, enter your code at the panel by the after-hours entrance. If you set off the alarm, call the alarm company at the number on the panel and give your passcode, then tell Rick the next morning.

**Keys:** Office and desk keys are issued by Building Maintenance and recorded on the key log. Keys may not be copied. Lost keys are reported to Rick the same day.

## 2. Front desk and visitors

- All visitors to offices on the second floor and basement sign in at the front desk and receive a visitor badge. The visitor log is kept for one year.
- Residents doing business at the first-floor counters (Utility Billing, Income Tax, Building and Zoning) do not need to sign in.
- The front desk calls the employee to come down and meet the visitor. Visitors are not sent upstairs on their own.
- Contractors and vendors working in the building sign in each day and wear a badge.
- Deliveries for the Mayor's Office, Council, Law, Engineering and Safety-Service are left at the front desk unless they are too large; large deliveries go to the loading door off the employee lot (call Rick).

**Booking rules**
- Book rooms on the shared calendar at least two business days ahead. Room requests from outside groups go to the Mayor's Office and require approval and a certificate of insurance for events with food or more than 25 people.
- Leave the room as you found it: tables back in the standard layout, whiteboards erased, trash in the bins, coffee pots rinsed.
- Council Chambers may not be used for private events or for any event charging admission.
- Food and drinks are allowed in the conference rooms and the lobby. Food in Council Chambers only for city-sponsored events (for example, a kickoff lunch or a retirement), with Building Maintenance told in advance so extra trash cans are set out.
- Report A/V problems to IT (helpdesk@). Report heating and cooling problems to Rick.

## 4. Mail and shipping

- Incoming U.S. mail arrives at the front desk at about 10:30 a.m. and is sorted into department mailboxes by 11:30 a.m.
- Mail addressed only to "City of Defiance" or "City Hall" is opened by the front desk and routed to the department; mail marked "Personal" or "Confidential" is delivered unopened.
- Outgoing mail goes in the front desk tray by 3:00 p.m. for same-day pickup. Postage is charged to each department through the postage meter account code.
- Certified mail requires a green card or electronic receipt; the sending department keeps the receipt with its file.
- Checks received by mail are logged by the front desk and delivered the same day to Utility Billing, Income Tax or Finance as addressed. Never leave checks in a department mailbox overnight.

## 5. Opening and closing (front desk)

**Opening (by 7:45 a.m.)**
1. Disarm the alarm (if not already off) and unlock the Perry Street door.
2. Turn on lobby lights and the lobby directory board.
3. Switch the phone system from night greeting to day mode.
4. Check the drop box at the Perry Street door; log and route anything inside (utility payments go to Utility Billing).
5. Check the visitor log sheet; start a new sheet if needed.

**Closing (4:30-4:45 p.m.)**
1. Ask anyone remaining at the counters to finish up; lock the Perry Street door at 4:45 p.m. unless an evening meeting is scheduled.
2. Switch the phone system to night mode.
3. Confirm the Council Chambers doors are locked if no meeting.
4. Turn off lobby lights except the night lights.
5. Note any maintenance problems on the maintenance request sheet for Rick.

## 6. Building maintenance requests

Submit non-urgent requests (light bulbs, furniture, paint, temperature complaints) on the maintenance request sheet at the front desk or by email to Rick. Urgent problems (water leaks, no heat or cooling in winter or summer, broken door locks, elevator problems) are called in to Rick directly, or to the Safety-Service Director's office if Rick is at the street garage.

The second-floor west wing is served by rooftop unit RTU-2. Temperature complaints on that side are logged so the HVAC contractor can see the pattern. Do not adjust thermostat settings; most are locked.

## 7. Emergencies

- **Fire alarm:** Leave by the nearest exit. Do not use the elevator. Meet in the employee lot, by the flagpole. Department heads or their designees account for their staff and report to the front desk or the first firefighter to arrive. Front desk staff take the visitor log.
- **Severe weather (tornado warning):** Move to the basement hallway outside the IT office, away from windows. The lobby restroom corridor on the first floor is the alternate shelter area.
- **Medical emergency:** Call 911. The AED is mounted in the lobby beside the elevator, with a second unit outside Council Chambers. Send someone to the Perry Street door to meet responders.
- **Threats or disruptive visitors:** Do not argue. Call 911 if anyone is in danger. The front desk and the Utility Billing and Income Tax counters have panic buttons connected to police dispatch.
- **Power outage:** Emergency lights come on automatically. The building generator powers the server room, phones and emergency lighting, not general office power. IT will advise on computer shutdown.

A fire drill is held twice a year (spring and fall). The fall drill is scheduled during Fire Prevention Week when possible.

## 8. Parking

- Employees park in the employee lot or the Perry Street municipal lot. The two-hour on-street spaces on Clinton Street are for customers of downtown businesses and visitors.
- Visitor parking and two accessible spaces are in front of the Perry Street entrance.
- Pool vehicles are parked in the marked spaces in the employee lot.
- During declared snow emergencies, follow the posted snow emergency route signs.

## 9. Lost and found

Items found in the building are turned in to the front desk, logged and kept for 30 days. Items found in parks or at events go to the Parks office. Wallets, phones and keys are taken to the Police Department at the end of the day.

## 10. Recycling and shredding

- Blue bins at each workstation for paper; central bins on each floor for cans and bottles.
- Locked shredding bins on each floor for paper with personal information. The shredding contractor empties them every other Thursday. Do not put paper with personal information in the blue bins.
