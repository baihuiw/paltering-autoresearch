Approved by the Safety-Service Director and Human Resources. Posted September 2026; payroll note added October 1.
Applies to full-time non-uniformed employees. Police, Fire and Water/Wastewater plant operators follow their collective bargaining agreements for holiday pay; staffing is maintained 24/7.

## Floating holiday

Each full-time employee receives one floating holiday per calendar year, to be scheduled with the supervisor's approval like vacation. The floating holiday does not carry over to the next year. (See Employee Handbook, Section 5.)

## Rules that apply to all holidays

- A holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed the Friday before; a holiday that falls on a Sunday is observed the Monday after.
- To be paid for a holiday, an employee must work, or be on approved paid leave, the scheduled workday before and the scheduled workday after the holiday.
- Holidays that fall during an employee's vacation are charged as holiday, not vacation.
- Part-time and seasonal employees are not paid for holidays unless they work them.

## Public notices

The Communications Assistant posts holiday closures and trash schedule changes to the city website and Facebook at least one week ahead, and includes them in the City Update. The front desk updates the lobby sign and the phone system holiday greeting the afternoon before each closure (IT can schedule the greeting in advance; submit a helpdesk ticket by the Wednesday before).

**Holiday phone greeting (standard):** "You've reached the City of Defiance. City offices are closed today for [holiday] and will reopen [day] at 8 a.m. For a water or sewer emergency, press 1 for the Water Division operator. For police, fire or medical emergencies, hang up and dial 911."

## Payroll

Payroll dates that fall on a holiday move to the business day before. The 2027 payroll calendar is issued separately by Finance.
