----------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE THIS SCHEDULE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Records are kept by CONTENT, not by format. An email, a paper letter and a scanned document with the same content have the same retention period.
2. The retention period starts at the event listed (for example, "after the end of the calendar year" or "after the contract expires").
3. Nothing may be destroyed without a Certificate of Records Disposal (RC-3) approved under the city's schedule. Send completed RC-3 forms to the Clerk of Council. The Clerk forwards them to the Ohio History Connection when required, and keeps the signed copy permanently.
4. Records that are the subject of a public records request, a subpoena, a litigation hold or an audit must NOT be destroyed, even if the retention period has run, until the Law Director releases the hold.
5. Transitory records (drafts that were never sent or relied upon, routine "thanks, got it" emails, meeting reminders, duplicate copies) may be deleted when no longer of administrative value. When in doubt, keep it and ask the Clerk.
6. Retention is the minimum. Departments may not keep records longer than the schedule allows without the Records Commission's approval, except under a hold.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
EMAIL
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Email has no single retention period. Each message is kept according to its content under the series above. Staff should:
- File correspondence that is a record (MO-03, MO-04, MO-05) in the appropriate shared drive folder or case log, not only in a personal mailbox.
- Not use personal email accounts or text messages for city business. If it happens, forward the message to a city account the same day so it is captured.
- Leave mailbox auto-delete rules OFF. IT keeps mailbox backups for 90 days for system recovery; backups are not a retention method.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
LEAVING OR CHANGING ADMINISTRATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------

When a mayor or staff member leaves office:
- City records stay with the city. Records may not be taken, deleted or wiped from devices.
- The outgoing office's files are boxed and inventoried and turned over to the Clerk of Council for retention review (see MO-20).
- IT preserves the outgoing mailbox and calendar for the full retention period of their contents before the account is disabled.
- Personal items and purely personal correspondence (not related to city business) may be removed.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
DISPOSAL
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Paper records with personal information (names with addresses, phone numbers, account numbers) are shredded through the city's contracted shredding service (locked bins on each floor; pickup every other Thursday).
- Electronic records are deleted from the shared drive and email by the custodian after the RC-3 is approved. IT will purge backups on the normal cycle.
- The Mayor's Office submits an RC-3 once a year, in January, for records that reached the end of their retention period in the prior calendar year.
