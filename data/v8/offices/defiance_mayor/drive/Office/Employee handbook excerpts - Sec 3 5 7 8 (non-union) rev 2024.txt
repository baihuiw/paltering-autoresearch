SECTION 3. WORK HOURS AND ATTENDANCE
======================================================================

3.1 Office hours
City Building offices are open to the public Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., except holidays. Departments must have the public counter and main phone covered during all open hours, including the lunch period. Department heads set staff schedules to ensure coverage.

3.2 Standard work week
The standard work week for full-time office employees is 40 hours, Monday through Friday, normally 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. with a 30-minute unpaid lunch. Field, plant and shift employees work the schedules set by their departments.

3.3 Flexible schedules
A department head may approve a regular alternate schedule (for example, 7:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.) if office coverage is maintained. Requests are made in writing and kept in the department file. Flexible schedules may be suspended during budget season, elections, emergencies or staff shortages.

3.4 Breaks
Employees may take one 15-minute paid break in the morning and one in the afternoon, scheduled so coverage is maintained. Breaks may not be combined with lunch or used to leave early.

3.5 Timekeeping
- All non-exempt employees record time in the city's electronic timekeeping system at the start and end of each shift and lunch period.
- Exempt employees record leave taken but not daily hours.
- Supervisors approve timesheets by noon Monday for the prior week. Late approvals delay payroll processing for the whole department.
- Employees may not record time for another employee or ask another employee to do so.
- Corrections are made by the supervisor with a note explaining the correction.

3.6 Overtime
Non-exempt employees are paid overtime at one and one-half times the regular rate for hours worked over 40 in a work week. Overtime must be approved in advance by the supervisor except in emergencies (water main breaks, snow and ice, storm cleanup). Compensatory time in place of overtime may be granted under the limits in Section 4.

3.7 Attendance and tardiness
Employees are expected to be at work on time as scheduled. An employee who will be late or absent must notify the supervisor (by phone or text to the supervisor, not by email to a general mailbox) no later than 30 minutes after the start of the shift, or as early as possible. Field crews call the department's call-off line.

3.8 Inclement weather
City offices normally remain open during snow emergencies. If the Mayor closes or delays opening of city offices, notice is given through the front desk phone greeting, the city website and Facebook, and by text from department heads. Essential personnel (Street, Utilities, Police, Fire, and others designated by their department heads) report as directed regardless of office closures.

3.9 Working from home
Working from home is not a standard arrangement. A department head may approve occasional remote work for specific tasks when the employee's position allows it and city equipment is used. Remote work may not be used in place of sick leave or to care for children during work hours.

SECTION 5. LEAVE
======================================================================

5.1 Holidays
Full-time employees receive the paid holidays listed on the annual holiday schedule issued by Human Resources, plus one floating holiday each calendar year. A holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed the preceding Friday, and a holiday that falls on a Sunday is observed the following Monday. To receive holiday pay, an employee must work, or be on approved paid leave, the scheduled workday before and after the holiday.

5.2 Vacation
Full-time employees earn vacation based on years of continuous service with the city (prior public service in Ohio counts as provided by law):

   Years of service             Vacation per year
   Less than 1 year             None (accrual begins; usable after 1 year)
   1 year but less than 8       80 hours (2 weeks)
   8 years but less than 15     120 hours (3 weeks)
   15 years but less than 25    160 hours (4 weeks)
   25 years or more             200 hours (5 weeks)

Vacation accrues each pay period. Requests are submitted through the timekeeping system at least one week in advance, except in an emergency. Supervisors approve vacation based on coverage needs; when two requests conflict, seniority decides unless the earlier request was already approved. Employees may carry over up to two years' accrual; vacation over that amount is forfeited on December 31 unless the department head and Human Resources approve an extension because the employee was denied leave for city business.

5.3 Sick leave
Full-time employees earn 4.6 hours of sick leave for each 80 hours in active pay status (about 15 days per year). Unused sick leave accumulates without limit. Sick leave may be used for:
- the employee's own illness, injury or medical appointments;
- illness, injury or medical appointments of an immediate family member;
- pregnancy and childbirth;
- death of an immediate family member (see also 5.5).

An employee who uses sick leave for more than three consecutive workdays may be asked for a statement from a health care provider. Patterns of sick leave use (for example, regularly on Mondays or the day after a holiday) will be discussed with the employee.

5.4 Personal days
Full-time employees receive two personal days (16 hours) each January 1. Personal days must be used by December 31 and do not carry over. New employees receive personal days on a prorated basis.

5.5 Bereavement leave
Full-time employees may take up to three days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, and one day for the death of a grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, niece or nephew. Bereavement leave is not charged to sick leave. Additional time may be taken from sick leave or vacation with approval.

5.6 Jury duty and court leave
An employee called for jury duty receives regular pay for scheduled work hours missed and turns over jury fees (other than mileage) to the city. An employee released from jury duty before noon is expected to report to work for the rest of the day. Employees subpoenaed as witnesses in city matters are on paid work time.

5.7 Military leave
Military leave is provided as required by state and federal law. Contact Human Resources for the current rules on paid military leave and supplemental pay.

5.8 Family and medical leave
Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of job-protected family and medical leave in a rolling 12-month period under the federal Family and Medical Leave Act. Paid leave runs at the same time as FMLA leave. Contact Human Resources as soon as the need for leave is known.

5.9 Leave without pay
Leave without pay may be granted for up to six months by the Mayor upon the department head's recommendation, after all appropriate paid leave is used.

5.10 Leave requests during busy periods
Departments may restrict vacation during their busiest periods with advance notice to employees (for example, Street Department during leaf collection and snow season, Finance during budget and year-end close, Parks during festival weeks, Income Tax in March and April).

SECTION 7. USE OF CITY TECHNOLOGY
======================================================================

7.1 City systems belong to the city
City computers, phones, email, network, software and internet access are provided for city business. Employees have no expectation of privacy in anything created, stored, sent or received on city systems. The IT Coordinator may monitor and access city systems as needed for security, maintenance, public records requests and investigations authorized by the Mayor, Law Director or Human Resources.

7.2 Acceptable use
Limited, occasional personal use of city equipment (a short personal email, checking the weather) is allowed if it does not interfere with work, cost the city money or violate any other part of this handbook. Employees may not use city systems to:
- run a private business or sell items;
- view, download or send obscene, harassing or discriminatory material;
- install software, browser extensions or apps without IT approval;
- connect personal devices to the internal city network (the guest wireless network may be used);
- store city records on personal devices or personal cloud accounts;
- share passwords or let anyone else use their account.

7.3 Email
- Use your city email for all city business. Do not forward city business to a personal email account.
- City email is subject to the public records law and the records retention schedule.
- Use caution with attachments and links. Report suspicious messages to helpdesk@ using the "Report Phishing" button. Do not reply to the sender.
- Do not use "reply all" for large distribution lists unless everyone needs the reply.
- Use the shared mailboxes (mayor@, utilitybilling@, helpdesk@) as directed by the department.

7.4 Personal social media
Employees may use personal social media on their own time and devices. When using personal accounts, employees:
- may not post confidential information learned through city employment (for example, personnel information, information about residents' accounts or calls for service, or information about active investigations);
- may not speak on behalf of the city or appear to do so. If an employee identifies themselves as a city employee and comments on city matters, the employee should make clear that the views are their own;
- may not use the city seal, logos or uniform photos in a way that suggests an official city post;
- should remember that harassment of co-workers is prohibited online as well as at work.

Official city accounts are governed by the City Social Media Policy.

7.5 Phones
City-issued mobile phones are for city business. Employees who receive a monthly phone stipend instead of a city phone must keep city business on their phone available for public records purposes and turn over city records on request. Texting is not to be used for decisions or approvals that need to be recorded; follow up by email.

7.6 Equipment issue and return
IT records each device issued (laptops such as ML-03 and ML-07, phones, tablets, keycards) and has the employee sign the equipment form. Lost or stolen equipment is reported to IT and the supervisor immediately. All equipment is returned on the employee's last day; the final paycheck is not released until IT confirms return.

7.7 Passwords and security
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and must not be reused from personal accounts. Multifactor authentication is required for email and remote access. Lock your computer when you step away (Windows key + L). Annual cybersecurity training is required for every employee with a city account.

SECTION 8. CITY VEHICLES AND EQUIPMENT
======================================================================

8.1 Who may drive
Only employees with a valid driver's license, on file with Human Resources, may drive city vehicles. Employees must report any suspension, revocation or restriction of their license to their supervisor before their next shift. Positions requiring a commercial driver's license (CDL) are subject to drug and alcohol testing as required by federal rules.

8.2 Use
City vehicles are for city business only. Passengers who are not city employees may ride only when needed for city business (for example, a contractor on a site visit). Vehicles may not be taken home unless assigned for on-call or take-home use by the department head and the Safety-Service Director.

8.3 Pool vehicles
City Building pool vehicles are reserved through the front desk calendar. Return the keys to the front desk key box, note mileage on the trip log, and report any problems. Leave the vehicle with at least a half tank of fuel; fuel at the street garage pumps using your fuel card.

8.4 Safety
Drivers and passengers wear seat belts at all times. Drivers may not use a hand-held phone while driving. Accidents involving a city vehicle, however minor, are reported immediately to the police and the supervisor, and an accident report is completed before the end of the shift.

8.5 Personal vehicles
Employees who use a personal vehicle for approved city business are reimbursed at the IRS mileage rate, using the mileage form submitted monthly to Accounts Payable. Commuting miles are not reimbursed.

8.6 Equipment
Employees may use only equipment they have been trained on. Defects are reported on the equipment's daily inspection sheet and the equipment is tagged out of service if unsafe. City tools and equipment may not be borrowed for personal use.
