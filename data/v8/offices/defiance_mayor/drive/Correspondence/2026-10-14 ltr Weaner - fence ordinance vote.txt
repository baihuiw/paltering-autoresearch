I'm disappointed Council passed the new fence rules. 42 inches in the front yard is too low to keep a dog in. I was planning to replace my old 48 inch picket fence next spring and now I'm told I can only go 42. Mr. Pfahler was the only one who listened. Is there any chance this gets changed?

----- REPLY -----
Dear Phil,

Thank you for writing. Council passed Ordinance No. 2026-37 on September 23 by a 6-1 vote. The Planning Commission recommended a 42-inch front-yard limit so drivers backing out of driveways can see children and other drivers.

There are two things that may help. First, an existing fence that was legally built can stay and be repaired; the new limit applies to new fences and to replacing more than half of a fence. Second, a property owner may apply to the Board of Zoning Appeals for a variance.

Building and Zoning can look at your lot and explain your options before you plan the project: (419) 784-2101, ext. 230. Changing the ordinance itself would take a new action by Council.

Sincerely,
Paul Haverkamp
Mayor, City of Defiance
