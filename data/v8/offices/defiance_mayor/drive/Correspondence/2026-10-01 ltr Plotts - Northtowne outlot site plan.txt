----- REPLY -----
Dear Richard,

Thank you for coming to the Planning Commission and for writing. I attended only to observe and did not take part.

Two different items were on the agenda. The Northtowne building was not a rezoning. That outlot is already zoned for general business, so the Commission reviewed the site plan and approved it with conditions. The rezoning was for two lots at 1536 and 1542 North Clinton Street, from residential to professional office, for a dental office. The Commission recommended it, and City Council will make the final decision after a public hearing.

Your point about traffic at the plaza entrance is fair. I've asked our City Engineer, Rachel Dunlap, to look at turning traffic there once the building opens. You can reach Engineering at (419) 784-2101, ext. 231.

Sincerely,
Paul Haverkamp
Mayor, City of Defiance
