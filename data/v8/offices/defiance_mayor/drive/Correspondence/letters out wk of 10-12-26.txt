Dear Dave,

Thank you for writing. The railroad's contractor closed the Hopkins Street crossing at 7 a.m. today to replace the crossing surface. It is expected to reopen the evening of Wednesday, October 14. Rain could push that back a day, and we will post on the city's Facebook page as soon as it reopens.

Dear Lorraine,

Thank you for writing. Leaf collection begins Monday, October 26 and runs through Friday, December 4, with three passes through the city. Each pass goes ward by ward: First, Second, Third, then Fourth.

Dear Brittany,

Welcome to Defiance! Trick-or-Treat this year is on Halloween itself, Saturday, October 31, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. If you'd like visitors, just turn on your porch light.

You can also sign up for city news on our website, cityofdefiance.com.
