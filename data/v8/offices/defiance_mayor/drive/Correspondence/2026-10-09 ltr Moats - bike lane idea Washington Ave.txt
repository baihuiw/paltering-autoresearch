----- REPLY -----
Dear Ethan,

Thank you for the idea. You're right that repaving is the cheapest time to change pavement markings.

Washington Avenue between Perry and Clinton Streets is being repaved on Friday, October 16. For this year's project, the striping plan was set when the contract was bid, and it keeps the existing layout, so bike lanes can't be added on short notice. A change also needs an engineer's review of street width, parking and intersections.

I've passed your suggestion to our City Engineer, Rachel Dunlap, so it can be considered as the 2027 resurfacing list is put together. That list goes to Council's Streets and Sidewalks Committee in February. You can reach Engineering at (419) 784-2101, ext. 231.

Sincerely,
Paul Haverkamp
