----- REPLY -----
Dear Nick,

Thank you for writing, and I'm sorry you're losing sleep over this.

Yes, the city has a noise ordinance, and it applies downtown. Ongoing equipment noise, like a rooftop fan, is handled by code enforcement in our Building and Zoning Department. I've entered your report, and an officer will check the fan during evening hours and talk with the property owner. You can follow up with Building and Zoning at (419) 784-2101, ext. 230.

For noise happening right now, such as trucks idling in the alley early in the morning, please call the police non-emergency line, (419) 784-5050, so an officer can respond while it's happening.

Sincerely,
Paul Haverkamp
Mayor, City of Defiance
