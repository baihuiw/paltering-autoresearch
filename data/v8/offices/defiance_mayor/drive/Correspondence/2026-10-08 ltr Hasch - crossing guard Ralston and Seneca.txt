Dear Jenna,

Thank you for writing, and for walking your kids through it. A safe crossing on the way to school matters to every parent.

School crossing guards are assigned by the city in cooperation with Defiance City Schools. I met with the superintendent in September, and crossing guard coverage was one of the topics we discussed. I've asked our Safety-Service Director, Tom Wiechers, to have the corner of Ralston Avenue and Seneca Street observed during morning arrival times over the next two weeks to count students and check vehicle speeds. That will tell us whether a guard or other measures are needed there.

You can reach Director Wiechers at (419) 784-2101, ext. 214.
