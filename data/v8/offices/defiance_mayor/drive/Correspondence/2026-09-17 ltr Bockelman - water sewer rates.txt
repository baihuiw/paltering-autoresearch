Mr. Mayor,

Welcome aboard, but I have to tell you I think Council got it wrong on the water and sewer increase. Six percent when everything else is already going up. I'm retired and on a fixed income and so are most of my neighbors. Mr. Bell had the right idea to phase it in. Why does the city always go to the residents first instead of cutting somewhere else? I'd like an answer.

Walt Bockelman

----- REPLY -----
Dear Walt,

Thank you for writing, and I understand. A 6 percent increase matters on a fixed income.

Council passed the increase on September 8, before I took office. It is the first since October 2023. For a typical household using 9,000 gallons a quarter, the quarterly water and sewer bill goes from $100 to $106. The money goes to the Water Treatment Plant filter and clearwell project, treatment plant equipment, and chemical and power costs, which have risen sharply. Water and sewer are run as separate funds paid for by customer charges, so savings elsewhere in the city budget can't be moved over to them.

Councilman Bell's phase-in idea was a fair one. I'll keep a close eye on these costs, and I appreciate you telling me where you stand.

Sincerely,
Paul Haverkamp
Mayor, City of Defiance
