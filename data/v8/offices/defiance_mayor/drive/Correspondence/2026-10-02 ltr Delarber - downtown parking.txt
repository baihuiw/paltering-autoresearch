Message:
I work part time at a shop on Clinton and I got a ticket for parking over 2 hours in front of the store. Where are employees supposed to park? I'm 67 and I can't walk 6 blocks in the snow. Is there anywhere downtown you can park all day for free? The signs aren't clear.

----- REPLY -----
Dear Sharon,

Thank you for writing. Yes, there is free all-day parking downtown. The city's municipal lot on Perry Street has 86 spaces, including four accessible spaces, with no time limit, and it usually has plenty of room on weekdays.

The two-hour spaces on Clinton Street are meant for shoppers, and I agree the signs could be clearer. This month the city is putting up "Free All-Day Parking" signs on Clinton Street pointing to the Perry Street lot.

If you have a question about the ticket itself, the Police Department can help at its non-emergency line, (419) 784-5050.

Sincerely,
Paul Haverkamp
Mayor, City of Defiance
