----- REPLY -----
Dear Miguel,

Thank you for writing about the intersection of Ottawa and Arabella. Safety concerns like this are worth taking seriously.

Stop signs can only be added after an engineering study under the state traffic manual, which looks at traffic counts, crash history and how far drivers can see. I've asked our City Engineer, Rachel Dunlap, to have that study done this fall, including the sight lines at the corner you describe. Engineering expects to have results in about six weeks and will let you know what they find.

If hedges on private property are blocking the view, code enforcement can also ask the owner to trim them. You can reach Engineering at (419) 784-2101, ext. 231.

Sincerely,
Paul Haverkamp
Mayor, City of Defiance
