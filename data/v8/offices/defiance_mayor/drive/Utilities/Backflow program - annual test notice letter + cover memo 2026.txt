Dear Customer:

Our records show that the backflow prevention assembly or assemblies listed above at your property are due for their annual test. Ohio Administrative Code Chapter 3745-95 and the City's cross-connection control ordinance require that every backflow prevention assembly be tested at least once a year by a tester certified by the Ohio Department of Commerce, and that the results be sent to the City.

WHY THIS MATTERS

A backflow prevention assembly keeps water that has been used inside your building, such as water in a boiler, a fire protection line, a lawn irrigation system, a beverage machine or a process line, from flowing back into the City's water mains if pressure drops. Annual testing makes sure it still works. A failed assembly can put your own building's water, and your neighbors', at risk.

WHAT YOU NEED TO DO

1. Hire a certified backflow tester of your choice. A list of testers who have registered with the City is enclosed. You may use any tester certified by the State of Ohio; the City does not recommend any particular company.
2. Have the tester send the test report to the City by **October 31, 2026**. Testers may email reports to the Water Treatment Plant or mail them to the address below. Please make sure the report includes the assembly serial number.
3. If the assembly fails, it must be repaired and retested within 30 days.

Lawn irrigation pressure vacuum breakers that are already shut down and drained for the season may be tested at startup next spring instead. If that applies to you, please call or email by October 31 so we can note it in your file.

IF WE DO NOT RECEIVE A REPORT

A second notice will be sent in early November. If a test report is still not received, the City is required to send a final notice and may discontinue water service to the property until the assembly is tested, as provided in the City's ordinance and state rules. We would much rather not. Most customers take care of this with one phone call to a tester.
