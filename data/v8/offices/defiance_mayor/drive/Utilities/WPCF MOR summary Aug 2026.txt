4. PROCESS NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

- Aeration: Blowers 1 and 3 in service, Blower 2 standby. Blower 1 (1997) needed a new inlet filter and belt set Aug 13. Average DO in aeration basins 1.8-2.2 mg/L. Energy use for aeration 61 percent of plant total. (Blower replacement is CIP WW-27-01.)
- MLSS averaged 2,850 mg/L; SRT about 14 days.
- Phosphorus removal: ferric chloride feed at the primary clarifier effluent; dose averaged 9.8 mg/L as Fe. Ferric is purchased under the City's annual water and wastewater chemical contract.
- Secondary Clarifier No. 2: drive vibration readings continue to rise (from 0.18 in/sec in July to 0.26 in/sec at month end). Gearbox oil sample taken Aug 28, results pending. Running both clarifiers.
- UV disinfection: Bank B lamp replacement (64 lamps) completed Aug 18. Lamp hours on Bank A at 11,200 of 12,000 rated; replacement planned for spring before the disinfection season.
