RE:      Liquid biosolids hauling and land application - 2027-2028 agreement (for October 19 Board of Control)

PURPOSE

The City's agreement for hauling and land-applying liquid Class B biosolids from the Water Pollution Control Facility expires December 31, 2026. I'm requesting Board of Control approval of a two-year agreement for 2027-2028 based on bids opened September 30.

BACKGROUND

The WPCF produces about 520 dry tons of biosolids a year, stored as liquid (about 4 percent solids) in the storage tank until it can be land-applied. The City applies it on its own 41-acre field on Domersville Road (leased to D. Wittenmyer for crops, renewed by the Board on September 28) and, when needed, on two private farm fields approved under the WPCF's NPDES permit and nutrient management plan.

The hauler supplies the tanker trucks and the drag-hose or injection equipment, loads at the WPCF, and applies at the rates set by the City's nutrient management plan. The City keeps the records, does the soil and biosolids sampling, and files the annual report with Ohio EPA. Applications happen twice a year - spring before planting and fall after harvest - and only when soils are not frozen, snow-covered or saturated.

Current agreement (2025-2026): Hensler Ag Services, LLC, $0.041 per gallon hauled and applied, about 3.0 million gallons per year. 2025 cost: $118,940. 2026 estimated: $124,000.

BIDS (opened September 30, 2026)

Because the expected annual cost is above the competitive bidding threshold, the service was advertised in the newspaper on September 10 and 17, the same way as the 2025-2026 agreement. Three bids were received and read aloud at 2:00 p.m. on September 30 in Conference Room B. Steve has reviewed the low bid and the bid bond; both are in order.

  Hauler                                  Per gallon   Est. annual (3.0 MG)   Notes
  --------------------------------------  ----------   --------------------   ---------------------------------
  Hensler Ag Services, LLC                $0.043       $129,000               Current hauler; injection equip.
  Kaltenbach Farm Hauling                 $0.046       $138,000               Drag-hose only; 2 trucks
  Paulding County Nutrient Services       $0.049       $147,000               Would subcontract fall work

Hensler's price is up 4.9 percent from the current agreement. They cite fuel and a new tanker. Their price is fixed for both years.

PERFORMANCE OF CURRENT HAULER

Good. In two seasons Hensler has met every application window we gave them, including the wet fall last year when we had a four-day window and they ran three trucks. No spills, no complaints from neighbors of the fields, and their application logs have been complete each time. Ohio EPA's 2025 inspection of the land application program had no findings.

RECOMMENDATION

Approve a two-year agreement with Hensler Ag Services, LLC for 2027 and 2028 at $0.043 per gallon as the lowest and best bidder, not to exceed $135,000 per year, from the Sanitary Sewer Operating Fund. The not-to-exceed amount allows for a wetter year with more volume.

The 2027 budget request for the WPCF already includes $132,000 for this line.

FALL 2026 APPLICATION (under the current agreement)

For your information:
- Storage tank is at about 88 percent today.
- Soybeans are off the north 24 acres of the Domersville Road field as of October 6. Hensler will start applying there October 13, about 1.1 million gallons over 4 days, weather permitting.
- The south 17 acres should be harvested about October 20. Remaining fall application there and on one private field, about 0.9 million gallons, by mid-November.

Neighbors: the two homes on Domersville Road closest to the field get a courtesy call the day before we start. D. Hagans makes those calls.
