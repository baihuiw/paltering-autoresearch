1. PROJECT SUMMARY

The project replaces the underdrains, support gravel and filter media in all four of the plant's dual-media gravity filters, adds an air scour system for more effective backwashing, replaces filter valves and actuators, and repairs and recoats both cells of the clearwell beneath the filter building. Filters 1 and 2 were originally constructed in 1974 and Filters 3 and 4 in 1989. The clearwell was last rehabilitated in 1996.

The work is being performed in stages so that the plant can continue to meet demand throughout construction. Two filters remain in service at all times, and one clearwell cell remains in service while the other is drained for repairs.

2. WORK COMPLETED THIS PERIOD

Filters 3 and 4:
- Completed installation of new plastic block underdrains with integral air scour laterals in both filters.
- Placed support gravel and new media (anthracite over sand) in both filters to design depths. Media gradation test reports received and accepted.
- Installed and tested new influent, effluent, backwash and drain valves and electric actuators.
- Air scour blower No. 1 set, wired and started. Manufacturer's startup representative on site September 22-23.
- Disinfection and bacteriological sampling completed September 25-28. Both sets of samples satisfactory.
- Filters 3 and 4 returned to service September 29 following City operator acceptance. Filter effluent turbidity during the first 48 hours averaged 0.04 NTU, well below the regulatory limit.

North clearwell cell:
- Cell drained and isolated September 14 after Filters 3 and 4 were nearly complete.
- Interior inspection with the project engineer September 15. Concrete deterioration at the ceiling slab and two interior columns was more extensive in two areas than shown on the drawings. The quantities remain within the unit-price allowance in the contract; no change order needed at this time.
- Surface preparation by hydro-demolition began September 21. Approximately 40 percent of the ceiling surface prepared by month end.

Electrical and controls:
- New filter control panels for Filters 3 and 4 installed and integrated with the plant SCADA system.
- Programming for automated air scour and backwash sequences completed for Filters 3 and 4.

- Two nonconformance reports opened and closed this period: (1) one valve actuator delivered with incorrect voltage, replaced by supplier; (2) media depth in Filter 4 initially 2 inches short of design, corrected before final placement.

7. PLANT OPERATIONS DURING CONSTRUCTION

City operators report no impact on finished water quality this period. Average daily production in September was 2.31 million gallons, with a peak day of 2.94 million gallons on September 4. With Filters 3 and 4 back in service and the new air scour, operators expect backwash water use to drop by roughly 25 percent compared with the old filters, which will reduce chemical and pumping costs.

Customer notices: none required this period.

8. BENEFITS WHEN COMPLETE

When the project is finished, all four filters will have new underdrains and media with an expected service life of 30 years or more. The air scour system will allow more thorough cleaning of the filters with less backwash water. New valves and controls will allow automated filter operation and better monitoring of individual filter performance. Clearwell repairs will address concrete deterioration and coating failure that, if left untreated, could eventually allow contamination of stored finished water.
