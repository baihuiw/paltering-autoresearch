## Public notice (as posted)

The City of Defiance will flush fire hydrants throughout the city from **Tuesday, September 8 through Friday, October 2**, Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM.

Flushing clears sediment from the water mains and lets the City check that every hydrant works properly for the Fire Department. While crews are working in your area you may notice lower water pressure or discolored (rusty) water. The water is safe, but it may stain laundry.

**What to do if your water is discolored:**
- Run the cold water tap nearest the water meter (usually a basement or utility sink) for 5 to 10 minutes until it runs clear.
- Avoid doing laundry while crews are flushing in your area. Check that the water is clear before starting a load.
- If laundry is stained, do not dry it. Rewash it with a rust remover product.
- If the water does not clear after 20 minutes, call the Water Treatment Plant at (419) 784-2101, ext. 260.

Please do not park in front of hydrants, and slow down around crews and flowing water.

## Crew notes (internal - not for posting)

**Crews:** two 2-person flushing crews, plus a valve crew exercising valves in the same blocks. Crew A starts at the plant each morning; Crew B starts at the far end of the day's area.

**Goal:** all 1,412 hydrants. Flush each until clear or 15 minutes. Record static pressure, residual on a nearby hydrant at about 1 in 10, time to clear, operation (opens/closes, drains), cap and chain condition, paint, clearance.

**Coordination:**
- Fire Department: send the daily area to Capt. Schaefer by 7:30 a.m. so Station 1 and 2 know. FD is doing its flow tests on the north side week 2 at the same time.
- Resurfacing contractor: Holgate, Jefferson, Hopkins, Washington milling starts Oct 5. Flush those streets in week 4 at the latest so we're not opening hydrants on fresh pavement.
- Washington Ave waterline project: skip the new main between Perry and Clinton; contractor flushed it at acceptance.
- Ayersville Ave: three hydrants buried by the 2025 regrading. Mark and flush if possible, record for raising.

**Customer calls:** WTP takes them. Any call about laundry claims goes to the Superintendent. We do not reimburse laundry but we can provide rust remover packets (box at the plant office and at the front desk in the City Building).
