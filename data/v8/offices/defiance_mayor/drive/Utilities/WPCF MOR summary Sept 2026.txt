4. PROCESS NOTES
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Secondary Clarifier No. 2 drive:
- The Aug 28 oil sample was inconclusive. At the Sept 9 oil change the drained oil had visible bronze particles (gear wear), and we found a crack at one mounting ear on the gearbox housing. Vibration at 0.29 in/sec by Sept 10.
- Torque alarm setpoint lowered and the drive is running at reduced torque. Sludge blanket checked twice per shift; target under 2 ft.
- Replacement gearbox ($41,780 from the manufacturer's representative, 9-week lead time) approved by the Board of Control September 28. PO issued September 29. Delivery expected the first week of December; installation by plant staff with the manufacturer's rep, about 3 days with the clarifier drained.
- Until then: Clarifier No. 1 carries average flow without trouble. Wet-weather peaks are handled with step-feed and by holding the return rate steady. Kevin has asked operators to call him for any forecast over 1.5 inches.

Aeration: Blowers 1 and 3 in service. Blower 3 tripped on high temperature twice (Sept 4 and Sept 5, both afternoons over 88F). Reset; cooling fan cleaned. Average DO 1.9 mg/L.

Phosphorus: ferric dose 10.1 mg/L as Fe. The new annual chemical contract starts November 1 (ferric at $612.00/dry ton from Lakeshore Water Products, awarded Oct 12).

UV: Bank A lamp hours at 11,900. Will run through end of disinfection season Oct 31 and replace over the winter.
