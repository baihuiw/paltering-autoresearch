2026 STREET RESURFACING PROGRAM STARTS MONDAY, OCTOBER 5

DEFIANCE, Ohio - The City of Defiance's 2026 street resurfacing program begins Monday, October 5, at 7:00 AM with milling on Holgate Avenue. Work on six streets, about 3.1 miles in all, is scheduled to finish by the end of October, weather permitting.

The resurfacing contract was awarded at $1,184,600, under the engineer's budget of $1,210,000. The work is paid for by the city's voter-approved street levy.

SCHEDULE

Each street is milled first (the top layer of worn asphalt is ground off) and repaved about a week later. Dates below are the contractor's current schedule and can move a day or two because of rain.

Holgate Avenue (Fifth Street to Pontiac Street)
  Milling: Monday, October 5 - Tuesday, October 6
  Paving: Monday, October 12 - Tuesday, October 13

Jefferson Avenue (Third Street to Sixth Street)
  Milling: Wednesday, October 7
  Paving: Wednesday, October 14

Hopkins Street (Clinton Street to Wayne Avenue)
  Milling: Thursday, October 8
  Paving: Thursday, October 15

Washington Avenue (Perry Street to Clinton Street)
  Milling: Friday, October 9
  Paving: Friday, October 16

Elliott Road (Domersville Road to the corporation limit)
  Full-depth patching of failed areas, then milling and paving the week of October 19

Summit Street (Jefferson Avenue to Holgate Avenue)
  Paving only (no milling), week of October 19

Note for Hopkins Street: the railroad crossing on Hopkins Street will be closed separately by the railroad's contractor from Monday, October 12, through the evening of Wednesday, October 14. That closure is not part of the city's paving work. Detour: Second Street and Holgate Avenue.

WHAT TO EXPECT

- "No Parking" signs will be posted at least 48 hours before work starts on each block. Vehicles left in the work zone during posted hours may be towed at the owner's expense.
- Between milling and paving, the street stays open but will be rough. Manhole covers and water valve boxes may stick up an inch or two above the milled surface. Please slow down. Motorcyclists and cyclists should take extra care.
- Driveways may be blocked for a few hours on paving day. Residents will receive a door hanger two to three days ahead with the date and the Street Department phone number.
- Garbage and recycling pickup will continue on the normal day. Please set carts out the night before as usual; crews will move them if needed.
- Work hours are generally 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Saturday work is possible if rain causes delays.

"Every one of these streets was on residents' lists, and every one of them is overdue," said Mayor Paul Haverkamp. "I appreciate everyone's patience with the no-parking signs and the rough ride for a week. The finished streets will be worth it."

Line striping will follow paving by about one to two weeks, after the new asphalt cures. Temporary tabs will mark the lanes until then.

Street Department crews will also continue routine pothole patching on other city streets throughout October. Residents can report potholes by calling the Street Department at (419) 784-2252 or using the "Report a Problem" form on the city website.

Engineering Department staff are on site daily during the work. Questions about the project can go to Engineering at (419) 784-2101, ext. 231.
