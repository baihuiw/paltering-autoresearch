# City Council Highlights: September 23, 2026 Regular Meeting

DEFIANCE, Ohio - Defiance City Council held its regular meeting Wednesday, September 23, at 7:00 PM in Council Chambers. The meeting was moved from its usual Tuesday this month. It was Mayor Paul Haverkamp's first regular Council meeting since taking office on September 15. All seven voting members were present, with Council President Gary Lehman presiding.

These highlights are provided as a public service. The official record of the meeting is the minutes, which will be available from the Clerk of Council once Council approves them.

## Mayor's report

Mayor Haverkamp thanked Council President Lehman for serving as acting mayor and thanked city employees for "keeping the lights on and the water running" during the transition. Since taking office he has met with each department head, toured the Water Treatment Plant and the Water Pollution Control Plant, ridden along with the Street Department and chaired his first Board of Control meeting.

The mayor said he will meet individually with each member of Council in the coming weeks, and that he will give Council a written monthly report beginning with the October 27 meeting. Residents with questions or casework should continue to contact Luis Ortega, Constituent Services Coordinator, at (419) 784-2101, ext. 207.

He reminded residents of upcoming events:

- Fall cleanup volunteer day at Kingsbury Park, Saturday, September 26, 9 AM at the main shelter.
- Homecoming parade, Saturday, October 3, 10 AM.
- Harvest on the Riverfront, Saturday, October 10, 10 AM to 4 PM, Kingsbury Park (rain date October 11).

## Fire Prevention Week proclamation

The mayor read a proclamation declaring October 4 - 10, 2026, as Fire Prevention Week in the City of Defiance. Fire Chief Dale Rummel and Lt. Erin Mayes, Fire Prevention Officer, accepted the proclamation. Lt. Mayes said firefighters will visit kindergarten through third-grade classrooms during the week and will hold the Station 1 open house on Saturday, October 10, from 10 AM to 2 PM. The free home smoke alarm program is open to homeowners and renters in single-family homes; call the Fire Prevention Bureau to sign up.

## Legislation

- **Ordinance No. 2026-35** (Nuisance Abatement) was adopted 7-0. It replaces Chapter 1355 of the Codified Ordinances and updates notice, abatement and cost-recovery procedures for weeds, rubbish and inoperable vehicles. The new administrative fee applies only to abatements ordered after the ordinance takes effect.
- **Ordinance No. 2026-37** (Fences and Walls) was adopted 6-1. Existing fences that were lawfully built may stay. New front-yard fences are limited to 42 inches.
- **Ordinance No. 2026-42**, an amendment to the 2026 appropriations, was adopted 7-0 as an emergency. It includes $68,412 for two powered stretchers for Medic 2 and Medic 3, half of which is paid by a Bureau of Workers' Compensation safety grant, and $12,500 moved within the street fund for hot mix and cold patch for pothole repair.
- **Resolution No. 2026-15**, certifying unpaid weed-cutting and rubbish removal charges on 38 parcels to the County Auditor, was adopted 7-0.
- **Ordinance No. 2026-39** (alley vacation between Jackson Avenue and Agnes Street, Seventh to Eighth Street) had its second reading.
- **Ordinance No. 2026-40** (lease of the city's 41-acre Domersville Road field for 2027-2029) and **Ordinance No. 2026-43** (three-year EMS billing services contract) had their first readings.

## 2027 budget schedule

Finance Director Angela Marquardt confirmed that Council will meet as a **Committee of the Whole on the 2027 budget on Tuesday, October 6, and Tuesday, October 20, at 6:00 PM** in Council Chambers. Department capital requests are due to Finance on Friday, October 9. The meetings are open to the public.

## Department reports in brief

- **Paving:** The 2026 resurfacing program starts Monday, October 5, with milling on Holgate Avenue at 7 AM. Ralston Avenue was paved September 11 and striped September 16.
- **Hydrant flushing** began September 14 and is about half done. It should be finished by early October.
- **Leaf collection:** The schedule will be published by mid-October, with collection beginning late in the month.
- **Third Street storm outfall:** 60 percent plans were received September 17; bidding is planned for January 2027.
- **Harding Street culvert:** The steel plate over the Hubbard ditch culvert remains in place and is checked weekly. Replacement is expected to be advertised for bids in early November.

## Public comment

Five residents spoke. Topics included lawn and driveway restoration after the Washington Avenue waterline work, thanks for the Ralston Avenue paving, traffic speeds and crossings on Holgate Avenue, lighting on the riverfront trail, and the cost of the Water Treatment Plant project.
