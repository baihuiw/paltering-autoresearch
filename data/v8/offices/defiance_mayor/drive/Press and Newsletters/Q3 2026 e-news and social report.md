# E-News and Social Media: Q3 2026 Report

July 1 - September 30, 2026
Prepared by: Hannah Brubaker, Communications Assistant
For: Megan Ostrander, Chief of Staff
Date: October 1, 2026

## Summary

- E-news subscribers: 4,207 on September 30, up from 4,061 on June 30 (+146, or 3.6%).
- 19 sends this quarter (7 City Updates, 10 special notices, 2 ward notices).
- Average open rate 57%, average click rate 10%. Both slightly above Q2.
- Facebook followers: 9,840, up from 9,415. Biggest post of the quarter: the September 3 Labor Day trash delay notice (reach about 14,200).
- Two things to watch: the leaf collection season (our biggest sign-up driver every year) and the new administration's welcome note in October.

### Unsubscribes

25 unsubscribes over 19 sends is low (about 0.03% per send). Most came after the two ward notices in July, which I think went to some people who had moved. No complaints this quarter.

Holiday trash schedule posts and road closures always do best. Event photo albums get the most comments. We had three posts this quarter that drew mostly negative comments (the August storm debris pickup post, the pool closing date, and one about parking at the fair), which I answered with the department contact and left up.

## Other notes

- The e-news is used only for city notices. This quarter that held without exceptions: every send was a city schedule change, closure, program or event.
- The new administration transition went smoothly on the communications side. The September 15 notice introducing Mayor Haverkamp was the second most-opened send of the quarter. The website "Mayor" page was updated September 16.
- I have editor access to the e-news platform; export and admin settings stay with Ben Rohrs (IT). That setup worked fine this quarter.
