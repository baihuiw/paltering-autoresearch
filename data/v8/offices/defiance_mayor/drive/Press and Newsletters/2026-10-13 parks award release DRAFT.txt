DEFIANCE PARKS AND RECREATION RECEIVES REGIONAL VOLUNTEER PARTNERSHIP AWARD

DEFIANCE, Ohio - The City of Defiance Parks and Recreation Department has received the 2026 Community Partnership Award from the Northwest Ohio Parks and Recreation Council for its park volunteer program. The award was presented October 8 at the council's fall meeting in Findlay.

The award recognizes the department's volunteer days at city parks, including the spring and fall cleanup days at Kingsbury Park, the riverfront trail litter pickups and the volunteer crews who help run Harvest on the Riverfront each October.

In 2026, 412 volunteers gave an estimated 1,650 hours of work in Defiance parks, up from 290 volunteers and about 1,100 hours in 2025. Volunteers this year:

- removed about 4.5 tons of litter and brush from Kingsbury Park, Riverside Park and the riverfront trail;
- spread 140 cubic yards of mulch at playgrounds and planting beds;
- painted 22 picnic tables and repainted the shelter house at Pontiac Park; and
- planted 65 trees and shrubs, with 40 more trees to be planted along the riverfront trail on Saturday, October 17.

The fall cleanup volunteer day at Kingsbury Park on September 26 alone drew 96 volunteers, including students from Defiance High School and Defiance College, two Scout troops, the Kiwanis and Rotary clubs and several church youth groups.

"This award belongs to the people who show up on a Saturday morning with work gloves," said Parks and Recreation Director Craig Fenstermaker. "Our full-time crew is six people. There's no way we keep up with eleven parks and the trail without the volunteers."

"Craig and his team built this program one cleanup day at a time," said Mayor Paul Haverkamp, who gave the kickoff remarks at the September 26 cleanup. "When I talk about the basics done well, this is what I mean, and it's residents doing a lot of the work."

The department will use the $500 award that comes with the recognition to buy tools and gloves for future volunteer days.

The next volunteer day is the riverfront tree planting on Saturday, October 17, from 9 AM to noon. Volunteers should meet at the Riverside Park boat ramp lot. Gloves and tools are provided. To sign up, visit the Parks page at cityofdefiance.com or call the Parks office at (419) 784-2101, ext. 240.

ABOUT THE AWARD
The Northwest Ohio Parks and Recreation Council is an association of park and recreation agencies in the northwest Ohio region. Its Community Partnership Award recognizes an agency that has worked with volunteers, schools or community groups to improve public parks.
