# City of Defiance E-News: Style and Sending Guide

Owner: Communications (Office of the Mayor)
Revised: February 2025. Replaces the 2022 version.

This guide covers the city's email newsletter ("City Update") and the one-off notices sent to the e-news list. It is for anyone in city government who drafts copy for the e-news or asks Communications to send something.

## 1. What the e-news is for

The e-news is how the city tells subscribers about things that affect their week: trash and recycling schedule changes, road and lane closures, leaf and brush collection, parks programs and city events, council and board meeting notices, and seasonal reminders.

The e-news is for city notices only: no advertising, no third-party promotions and no political content.

Community organizations sometimes ask us to include their events. We can link to the community calendar on the website, but we do not send other organizations' announcements to the list. Refer requests to Communications.

## 2. Types of sends

**City Update (regular).** Every other Friday at 7:00 AM. Four to seven items. Lead with whatever changes what people do this week (a closure, a schedule change). Events and reminders go lower.

**Special notice.** A single-topic send when something can't wait for the next City Update: a holiday trash schedule change, a road closure on short notice, a weather-related cancellation. Keep it short. Subject line says what it is.

**Ward notice.** Sent only to subscribers who chose a ward, for something that affects one ward (for example, a lane restriction or a ward's leaf collection days). Remember that not every subscriber gives a ward. If the notice matters a lot, put it in the City Update as well.

**Emergency notifications** do not go through the e-news. They go through the emergency notification system run by Fire/EMS and the county.

## 3. Voice and style

- Write the way you'd explain it to a neighbor. Short sentences. Plain words.
- Lead with the what and the when. "Leaf collection begins Monday, October 27" is better than "The Street Department is pleased to announce."
- Use the day of the week with the date: "Monday, October 13."
- Times: 7:00 AM, 4 PM, noon. Not "0700" or "4:00 p.m."
- Street names in full the first time: "Hopkins Street." After that "Hopkins" is fine.
- Say where to go for more: a link to the page, a phone number, or the office email.
- No jargon or department shorthand ("ROW," "WO," "BOC"). Spell it out or leave it out.
- Don't promise what we can't control. "Expected to reopen" for anything that depends on a contractor or the weather.
- One exclamation point per issue, at most.

## 4. Subject lines

- City Update: "City Update: [first item], [second item], [third item]"
- Special notice: say the change. "Trash pickup delayed one day for Labor Day."
- Under 70 characters if you can.

## 5. Photos and graphics

- Use city photos or photos we have permission to use. No stock photos of other cities.
- Alt text on every image.
- Kids: wide shots only, unless a parent has signed a release.
- Graphics for schedules (leaf pickup, holiday trash) should also be written out in the text for people who don't load images.

## 6. The subscriber list

- Residents and others sign up on the website. We ask for name, email and an optional ward.
- Do not add anyone to the list who hasn't signed up. That includes people who emailed a city office, people on parks registration lists and people who filled out a survey. Ask them to sign up instead.
- Unsubscribes are automatic. Never re-add someone who unsubscribed.
- Bounced addresses are cleaned out by the platform after three hard bounces.
- Only Communications staff have editor access to the e-news platform. The platform administrator account is held by the city's IT coordinator.

## 7. Sending checklist

Before scheduling any send:

1. Read it on your phone (preview mode). Most subscribers open on a phone.
2. Check every link.
3. Check every date against the day of the week.
4. Check the audience (all subscribers or a ward list).
5. Send a test to the communications inbox and one other staff member.
6. Log the send in the e-news send log (Press and Newsletters folder) with the date, subject and audience. Add open and click numbers after 7 days.

## 8. Corrections

If we send something wrong, send a short correction as a special notice within the same day if it affects what people do (a wrong date or time). Small typos don't need a correction. Note the correction in the send log.

## 9. Facebook and the website

Every e-news item should also exist on the website (news page or the department page). Facebook posts link back to the website, not to the e-news archive. Post the City Update link on Facebook at 7:15 AM on send day.

## 10. Who to ask

Communications Assistant: drafting, scheduling and the list.
Chief of Staff: approval for anything sensitive, anything quoting the mayor, and anything that isn't routine.
