CITY COMPLETES FOURTH WARD SEWER LINING AND RALSTON AVENUE RESURFACING

DEFIANCE, Ohio - The City of Defiance has completed two public works projects this fall: the lining of a section of the Fourth Ward sanitary sewer interceptor and the resurfacing of Ralston Avenue between Fifth Street and Elliott Road.

Crews from Vandersall Pipe Renewal LLC, a Defiance company, finished lining about 1,180 feet of 21-inch sanitary sewer in the easement between Pierce Street and Wayne Avenue on October 14, two days ahead of schedule. The final cost of $462,300 came in $17,700 under the amount approved by the Board of Control.

The 1958 clay sewer line, which carries most of the Fourth Ward's wastewater, had cracking, roots and groundwater leaking in at joints. Cured-in-place lining installs a new pipe inside the old one without digging up back yards. The project was approved by the Board of Control in a public meeting on September 28 under the emergency provisions of the City's purchasing ordinance so that the work could be finished before winter.

"I want to thank the neighbors along Pierce and Wayne for their patience with the pumps and trucks over the past two weeks," said Mayor Paul Haverkamp. "This line serves thousands of residents, and getting it done before winter, on time and under budget, by a local company, is the kind of result people should expect from their city."

No sewer backups or service interruptions were reported during the work. Easement areas have been seeded and will be checked again in the spring.

On Ralston Avenue, paving was completed September 11 and final punch-list items on September 29. The project resurfaced 0.62 miles of roadway, repaired the road base at nine locations and replaced six catch basin castings. The final cost of $357,512 was about 2 percent under the contract amount.

"Both of these projects were finished under budget," said Safety-Service Director Tom Wiechers. "Our engineering and utilities staff did a great job keeping residents informed."

Residents with questions about either project can contact the Engineering Department at (419) 784-2101, ext. 231.
