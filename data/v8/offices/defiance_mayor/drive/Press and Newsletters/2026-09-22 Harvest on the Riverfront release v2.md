# Harvest on the Riverfront Returns to Kingsbury Park Saturday, October 10

*Pumpkins, hayrides, local vendors and a pet costume parade; free admission*

**DEFIANCE, Ohio** - The City of Defiance Parks and Recreation Department will host its annual **Harvest on the Riverfront** festival on **Saturday, October 10, from 10 AM to 4 PM at Kingsbury Park**. Admission is free. The rain date is Sunday, October 11.

The festival is the city's only fall festival and one of the Parks Department's largest events of the year.

## What's happening

- **Pumpkin decorating for kids**, free while supplies last. The Parks Department has 400 pumpkins this year, up from 300 in 2025, after running out by early afternoon last fall.
- **Hayrides** around the park every 30 minutes, starting at 11 AM. The last hayride leaves at 3:30 PM.
- **More than 30 craft and food vendors**, with food trucks lined up along the park drive.
- **Pet costume parade at 2 PM.** Registration is at the Parks table the day of the event. There is no fee. Pets must be leashed and current on vaccinations.
- **Live music** on the shelter house stage from noon to 3 PM.
- **Fire safety table** staffed by the Defiance Fire Division's Fire Prevention Bureau, with smoke alarm sign-ups for residents.

The Station 1 open house and the Pumpkin Run 5K at Pontiac Park are also that morning, so families can make a day of it. The 5K starts at 9 AM.

## Mayor's comment

"Harvest on the Riverfront is one of the things people tell me they love about living here," said Mayor Paul Haverkamp, who walked the festival grounds with Parks staff on September 19. "Craig and his crew put a lot of work into it every year. I hope everybody comes out, brings the kids and the dog, and supports our local vendors."

## Parking and getting there

Parking is available in the Kingsbury Park lots and on nearby streets. **Overflow parking** is available at the old school lot, with a golf cart shuttle running to the festival entrance throughout the day. Accessible parking is reserved in the main park lot closest to the shelter house.

Please do not park on lawns or block driveways on neighboring streets. Parking enforcement will be in the area.

## Volunteers needed

The Parks Department is looking for volunteers to help with pumpkin decorating, the Parks table, the shuttle line and cleanup. Shifts are two hours long. Sign up on the Parks page at cityofdefiance.com or call the Parks office at (419) 784-2101, ext. 240. Volunteers under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

## Vendors

Vendor registration closes **Friday, October 2**. A few food truck spaces remain. Vendor applications are available on the Parks page. All food vendors must have a current license from the Defiance County Health Department, and vendors using cooking equipment or generators are subject to a fire safety check by the Fire Prevention Bureau the morning of the event.

## Photography

City staff will take photos during the event for the city website and Facebook page. Signs will be posted at the park entrances. Parents who prefer that their children not be photographed may let any staff member at the Parks table know.
