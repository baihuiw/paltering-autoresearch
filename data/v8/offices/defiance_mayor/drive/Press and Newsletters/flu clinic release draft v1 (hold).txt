CITY AND COUNTY HEALTH DEPARTMENT TO HOLD COMMUNITY FLU SHOT CLINIC AT THE CITY BUILDING

DEFIANCE, Ohio - The City of Defiance and the Defiance County Health Department will hold a walk-in community flu shot clinic on [Thursday, November 5, from 2 to 6 PM] in Council Chambers at the City Building, 631 Perry Street.

Flu shots will be given by Health Department nurses to anyone age [6 months] and older. [High-dose vaccine will be available for adults 65 and older.] No appointment is needed.

WHAT TO BRING

- Your insurance card, including Medicare or Medicaid. The Health Department will bill insurance when possible.
- No one will be turned away because they are uninsured or cannot pay.
- Wear a short-sleeved shirt or one with sleeves that roll up easily.

Children under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

The clinic is part of the city's Senior Services Initiative, which Mayor Paul Haverkamp announced on September 30. The City Building is accessible from the Perry Street entrance, and parking is available in the city lot next to the building. Residents who need a ride can call Luis Ortega in the Mayor's Office at ext. 207 by [Monday, November 2], and he will help arrange transportation through the Defiance County Senior Center.

"Getting a flu shot is one of the simplest things any of us can do to stay healthy through the winter," said Mayor Haverkamp. "We want to make it as easy as possible, especially for older residents, by bringing the clinic right downtown."

WHY GET A FLU SHOT

Flu season usually peaks between December and February. The flu shot takes about two weeks to become fully effective, so getting vaccinated in October or November gives the best protection through the season. Health officials recommend a yearly flu shot for everyone 6 months and older, with rare exceptions.

Residents who cannot attend the clinic can get a flu shot from their doctor, at local pharmacies or at the Health Department's regular immunization clinics. Call the Health Department for its clinic hours.

City employees: a separate employee clinic will be scheduled through Human Resources. Watch for the notice in the payroll envelope.
