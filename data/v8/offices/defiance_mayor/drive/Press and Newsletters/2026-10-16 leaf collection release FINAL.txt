CURBSIDE LEAF COLLECTION BEGINS MONDAY, OCTOBER 26

DEFIANCE, Ohio - The City of Defiance Street Department will begin curbside leaf collection on Monday, October 26. Crews will make three passes through the city, ending Friday, December 4, weather permitting.

Each pass works ward by ward in the same order: First Ward, then Second, Third and Fourth. Crews spend about two to three working days in each ward per pass.

  Pass 1: Monday, October 26 - Friday, November 6
  Pass 2: Monday, November 9 - Friday, November 20
  Pass 3: Monday, November 23 - Friday, December 4 (no collection Thanksgiving Day or the day after)

There is no collection on Wednesday, November 11, Veterans Day, when city offices are closed.

Crews go street by street within each ward, so the city cannot give a specific day for each street. A ward map is posted on the city website and at the City Building. Residents who aren't sure of their ward can call the Mayor's Office at (419) 784-2101, ext. 201.

HOW TO PREPARE YOUR LEAVES

- Rake leaves to the tree lawn (the strip between the sidewalk and the curb), within about 3 feet of the curb.
- Do not rake leaves into the street. Leaves in the street wash into storm drains and can cause flooding and street ponding.
- Leaves only. Sticks, brush, rocks, grass clippings, pumpkins and trash damage the vacuum equipment. Crews will skip piles with other material in them.
- No bags. Bagged leaves will not be picked up at the curb.
- Do not park on or next to leaf piles. Hot exhaust can start a fire, and parked cars block the vacuum trucks.
- Keep piles away from fire hydrants, mailboxes and utility poles.

BAGGED LEAVES AND BRUSH

Bagged leaves and brush can be taken to the city's compost site on Island Park Avenue on Saturdays from 8 AM to noon through November 21. The site is for City of Defiance residents only. Bring a utility bill or driver's license. Branches must be under 4 inches across. No stumps, lumber or trash.

"Leaf season is the Street Department's busiest time of year," said Safety-Service Director Tom Wiechers. "This year we've repaired our second leaf vacuum and brought on seasonal help so we can keep the passes on schedule. If everyone keeps the leaves out of the street and the piles clean, the crews can move a lot faster."

The Board of Control approved the repair of the city's No. 2 leaf vacuum ($9,860) and the hiring of up to four seasonal laborers at its October 12 meeting.

Weather delays and progress updates will be posted on the city website and the City of Defiance Facebook page.

Questions about leaf collection can go to the Street Department at (419) 784-2252, Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 3:30 PM.
