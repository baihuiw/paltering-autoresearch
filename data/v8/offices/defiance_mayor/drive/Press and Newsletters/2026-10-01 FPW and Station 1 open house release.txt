DEFIANCE FIRE DIVISION MARKS FIRE PREVENTION WEEK, INVITES PUBLIC TO STATION 1 OPEN HOUSE OCTOBER 10

DEFIANCE, Ohio - The Defiance Fire Division will observe Fire Prevention Week, October 4 - 10, with school visits, talks for older residents and a free open house at Station 1 on Saturday, October 10, from 10 AM to 2 PM.

Mayor Paul Haverkamp proclaimed Fire Prevention Week in the City of Defiance at the September 23 City Council meeting.

"The single most important thing in a house fire is getting out fast, and that starts with a working smoke alarm and a plan," said Fire Chief Dale Rummel. "Our crews would much rather meet you at the open house than on the worst day of your life."

STATION 1 OPEN HOUSE - SATURDAY, OCTOBER 10, 10 AM TO 2 PM

The open house is free and open to everyone. Activities include:

- Fire engine, ambulance and utility truck display
- The county's fire safety smoke house trailer, where children practice getting out of a smoky room
- Fire extinguisher training on a digital trainer, every 30 minutes
- Kids' activities, including a hose target, coloring and junior firefighter helmets
- Hot dogs and drinks provided by the firefighters' association
- Smoke alarm program sign-up table and File of Life packets for older residents
- Child car seat checks by certified technicians (by appointment; call the Fire Prevention Bureau to schedule)

The on-duty crew from Station 1 will remain in service during the open house. If they are called out, the display will continue.

The open house is the same morning as Harvest on the Riverfront at Kingsbury Park (10 AM - 4 PM) and the Pumpkin Run 5K at Pontiac Park (9 AM).

FREE SMOKE ALARMS

The Fire Division's home smoke alarm program provides and installs free smoke alarms for Defiance homeowners and renters in single-family homes. Firefighters install the alarms, test them and help the family make a home escape plan. Since January the program has installed alarms in 312 homes. To sign up, stop by the open house, call the Fire Prevention Bureau, or contact the Mayor's Office.

Residents 60 and older can also request a free alarm and a File of Life packet through Luis Ortega in the Mayor's Office, (419) 784-2101, ext. 207, as part of the city's Senior Services Initiative.

FIRE PREVENTION WEEK TIPS

- Test smoke alarms once a month. Replace any alarm more than 10 years old.
- Have a home escape plan with two ways out of every room, and a meeting place outside.
- Stay in the kitchen when you are frying, grilling or broiling food. Cooking is the leading cause of home fires.
- Give space heaters at least three feet of space from anything that can burn, and plug them directly into the wall.
- Close bedroom doors at night. A closed door can slow the spread of smoke and heat.

The Fire Division will post a safety tip each day of Fire Prevention Week on the City of Defiance Facebook page.
