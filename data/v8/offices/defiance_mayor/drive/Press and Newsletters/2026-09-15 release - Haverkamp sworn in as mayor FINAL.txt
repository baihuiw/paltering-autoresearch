PAUL HAVERKAMP SWORN IN AS MAYOR OF DEFIANCE

DEFIANCE, Ohio - Paul Haverkamp took the oath of office as Mayor of the City of Defiance this morning at 9:00 AM in Council Chambers at the City Building, 631 Perry Street. City Council appointed Haverkamp at a special meeting on Friday, September 11, to fill the vacancy created by the resignation of the previous mayor, which took effect September 2. He will serve until a successor is elected. The seat is on the ballot in November 2027.

The oath was administered before a crowd of about 80 residents, city employees, members of City Council and the Haverkamp family. A short reception followed in the City Building lobby.

Haverkamp owned and operated an insurance agency on Clinton Street for 20 years and served on the City of Defiance Planning Commission. He is a longtime member of the Defiance Rotary Club.

"I'm grateful for the trust that's been placed in me, and I know the job is about the basics," Haverkamp said after taking the oath. "Clean water, safe streets, good parks and a City Hall that answers the phone. Defiance has excellent people working for it. My job is to make sure they have what they need, and to make sure residents get straight answers."

Council President Gary Lehman, who served as acting mayor from September 2 until this morning under the City Charter, welcomed the new mayor and thanked city staff for keeping day-to-day operations running during the vacancy.

"These last two weeks went smoothly because our department heads and employees kept doing their jobs," Lehman said. "I'm happy to hand the keys to Paul. He knows this town and he knows how to listen."

FIRST WEEKS

Haverkamp will spend his first days in briefings with each city department, including Finance, Law, Safety-Service, Engineering, Utilities, Parks and Recreation and the Fire Division. He will chair his first meeting of the Board of Control on Monday, September 21, and attend his first City Council meeting on Wednesday, September 23, at 7:00 PM. (The September 22 meeting was moved to Wednesday this month.)

Mayor's Office staff remain in place:

- Megan Ostrander, Chief of Staff
- Luis Ortega, Constituent Services Coordinator
- Brenda Kahle, Scheduler
- Hannah Brubaker, Communications Assistant

Residents may contact the Mayor's Office at (419) 784-2101, ext. 201, or mayor@cityofdefiance.com. Requests for the mayor to attend an event or meeting should go to Brenda Kahle at bkahle@cityofdefiance.com.

City services are not affected by the transition. City offices are open Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM.

ABOUT THE OFFICE OF MAYOR

Under the City Charter, the mayor is the chief executive officer of the City and chairs the Board of Control, which awards contracts and approves purchases. The mayor appoints department directors with the consent of Council. City Council, made up of four ward members, three at-large members and a Council President, passes ordinances and adopts the budget.
