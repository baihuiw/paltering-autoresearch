FALL HYDRANT FLUSHING BEGINS SEPTEMBER 14

DEFIANCE, Ohio - The City of Defiance Water Division will begin its fall fire hydrant flushing program on Monday, September 14. Crews expect to flush all of the city's approximately 1,410 public hydrants in about three weeks, finishing by Friday, October 2, weather permitting.

Flushing runs Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 3:30 PM. Crews work through the city by area rather than by ward, starting on the west side near the Water Treatment Plant and moving east and north so that clean water is pushed out ahead of the crews. The approximate schedule is:

- September 14 - 18: West side, downtown and the First Ward, including Ayersville Avenue, Island Park Avenue, Riverview Drive, Clinton Street, Perry Street and Summit Street
- September 21 - 25: Second and Third Wards, including Washington Avenue, Jefferson Avenue and Ralston Avenue
- September 28 - October 2: North side and the Fourth Ward, including Holgate Avenue, Hopkins Street, Elliott Road and the area around the north water tower

A map showing the week each area is scheduled is posted on the city website and at the Utility Billing counter on the first floor of the City Building.

WHY THE CITY FLUSHES HYDRANTS

Flushing pushes a high volume of water through the mains to clear out sediment and mineral deposits that settle in the pipes over time. It also lets crews confirm that every hydrant opens, closes and drains properly before winter, and gives the Fire Division up-to-date flow information.

"Flushing is one of those jobs nobody notices when it goes right," said Utilities Superintendent Kevin Shaffer. "It keeps the water clearer in the long run and makes sure the hydrants work when the fire department needs them. We ask folks to be patient with us for a few weeks."

WHAT RESIDENTS MAY NOTICE

- Water may look rusty or cloudy for a short time while crews are working nearby. The water remains safe to drink.
- If your water is discolored, run the cold water tap in a bathtub for 5 to 10 minutes until it clears. Avoid using hot water until the cold runs clear so sediment isn't drawn into your water heater.
- Check the water before doing laundry, especially whites. If clothes become stained, do not dry them. Rust remover for laundry is available at no charge at the Utility Billing counter while supplies last.
- Water pressure may be lower than usual for a short time.

Drivers should slow down and use caution around flushing crews and the water running in the gutters. Please do not drive through the stream from an open hydrant.

Residents who still have discolored water after running the tap for 15 minutes, or who see a hydrant leaking after crews have left, should call the Water Division at (419) 784-2101, ext. 260, during business hours. After hours, call the Water Treatment Plant operator through the same number and follow the prompts.
