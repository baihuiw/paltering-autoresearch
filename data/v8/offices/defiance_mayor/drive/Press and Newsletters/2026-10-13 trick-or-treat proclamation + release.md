# Mayor Haverkamp Sets Trick-or-Treat for Saturday, October 31, 5:30 to 7:30 PM

DEFIANCE, Ohio - Mayor Paul Haverkamp has signed a proclamation setting Trick-or-Treat in the City of Defiance for **Saturday, October 31, from 5:30 to 7:30 PM**.

Residents who want trick-or-treaters should turn on their porch light. Homes with the porch light off should be skipped.

"Trick-or-Treat is one of my favorite nights of the year in this town," Mayor Haverkamp said. "I'll be handing out candy at home. All I ask is that drivers take it slow that evening and that families help the little ones be seen."

## Safety tips from the Defiance Fire Division

**For families**
- Carry a flashlight or glow stick and add reflective tape to costumes and bags.
- Choose face paint instead of masks that block vision.
- Stay on sidewalks, cross at corners and don't cut between parked cars.
- Younger children should go with an adult. Older children should go in groups and have a set route and time to be home.
- Check treats before eating. Throw away anything unwrapped or opened.

**For homeowners**
- Clear porches and walks of leaves, hoses, pumpkins and other tripping hazards.
- Use battery candles or LED lights in jack-o'-lanterns instead of open flames.
- Keep pets inside.

**For drivers**
- Slow down in neighborhoods between 5 and 8 PM.
- Watch for children crossing mid-block and between parked cars.
- Pull in and out of driveways slowly.

## Leaf piles

Curbside leaf collection begins October 26, so many streets will have leaf piles on the tree lawn on Trick-or-Treat night. Children should stay out of leaf piles near the street, and drivers should not park next to them.

## Proclamation

**OFFICE OF THE MAYOR**
**CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO**

**PROCLAMATION**

WHEREAS, Halloween is a long-standing tradition enjoyed by children and families in the City of Defiance; and

WHEREAS, setting a single, citywide time for trick-or-treating helps families plan, allows residents to prepare to welcome visitors, and alerts drivers to take extra care; and

WHEREAS, the Defiance Fire Division and the Office of the Mayor encourage all residents to observe common-sense safety practices on that evening;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Paul Haverkamp, Mayor of the City of Defiance, do hereby proclaim

**Saturday, October 31, 2026, from 5:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m.**

as

**TRICK-OR-TREAT IN THE CITY OF DEFIANCE**

and encourage all residents who wish to participate to turn on their porch lights, and all drivers to use extra caution in residential neighborhoods during those hours.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand and caused the seal of the City of Defiance to be affixed this 13th day of October, 2026.

_______________________________
Paul Haverkamp, Mayor
