CITY UPDATE | October 9, 2026

A note from Mayor Haverkamp
[welcome note, pending Megan]

Leaf collection begins October 26
The city's leaf vacuum trucks begin their fall rounds on Monday, October 26 and run through Friday, December 4, weather permitting. Crews make three passes, each going ward by ward (First, Second, Third, then Fourth Ward):
Pass 1: October 26 - November 6
Pass 2: November 9 - November 20
Pass 3: November 23 - December 4
Rake leaves to the tree lawn. Keep leaves out of the street and away from storm drains. No bags, brush or grass clippings, please, and don't park on top of your leaf pile. Crews go street by street, so we can't give a specific day for your street. Not sure which ward you live in? Use the ward map on our website.

Hopkins Street crossing closed October 12-14
The railroad crossing on Hopkins Street will be closed to all traffic, including pedestrians, from 7:00 AM Monday, October 12. It is expected to reopen the evening of Wednesday, October 14. Detour: Second Street and Holgate Avenue. Trucks should use the state routes. We'll post on Facebook when the crossing reopens.

Harvest on the Riverfront: Saturday, October 10
Join us at Kingsbury Park from 10 AM to 4 PM for pumpkin decorating (free for kids while supplies last), hayrides every half hour starting at 11, more than 30 craft and food vendors, and the pet costume parade at 2 PM. Overflow parking with a shuttle at the old school lot. Rain date: Sunday, October 11. Volunteers needed for two-hour shifts; sign up on the Parks page.

Brush and bagged leaf drop-off
The compost site on Island Park Avenue accepts brush and bagged leaves from city residents on Saturdays, 8 AM to noon, through November 21. Bring a utility bill or driver's license. Branches under 4 inches across; no stumps or lumber.

Shelter house reservations
2026 shelter house reservations close October 31. Reservations for 2027 open online January 4.
