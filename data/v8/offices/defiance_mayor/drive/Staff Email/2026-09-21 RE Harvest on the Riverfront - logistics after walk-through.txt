Jake and I finished the pre-event vendor and booth plan review this afternoon. Approved, with the usual conditions:

- Food trucks: all 4 checked (Engine 1's crew helped with two of them while they were in service at the park). Each needs a K-class extinguisher in addition to the ABC, and they all had them. All,

Quick wrap-up. Weather held, no rain date needed. Rough count from the gate clickers was about 5,200 through the main entrance, plus the shuttle riders and the people who walked in from the trail, so call it 6,000 over the day (the shuttle did 212 trips, which I think is a record for the golf carts). We ran out of pumpkins at 1:40. 41 pets in the costume parade. The mayor did judge, and he gave "Best in Show" to a basset hound dressed as a hot dog, so I think his political career is secure.

Minor stuff:
- One vendor's canopy blew into the aisle around 12:30 when the wind picked up. No one hurt. We'll require weights on every leg next year, not just "recommended."
- The ADA unit at the north end had a door latch problem. Kreischer is crediting the service visit.
- Lost and found: two kids' jackets, a phone (returned), and a pair of reading glasses. At the parks office.

Ron, barricades are stacked by the north gate. Thanks for getting to them after the Hopkins detour.

I'll do a full debrief at dept heads next Monday. Thanks everyone.
