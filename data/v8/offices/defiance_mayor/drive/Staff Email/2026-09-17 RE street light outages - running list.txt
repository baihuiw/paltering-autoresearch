Luis,

Good idea. Rule of thumb:
- Cobra head lights on wood poles (most residential streets) belong to the power company. The city pays a monthly rate per light. We report them, they fix them. Their target is 5 business days, which they hit maybe half the time. Each pole has a tag with a number on it. If the resident can give you the tag number it goes much faster.
- Decorative black posts downtown (Clinton from First to Fifth, Perry in front of the City Building and the courthouse block) and the riverfront trail lights are the city's. We fix those through our electrical contractor, Schaublin Electric, unless it's just a bulb, which Terry's crew can do.
- Park lights are Parks.

So from your list: 1, 2, 6, 7, 8 are the power company's. I'll report them today through their portal and Terry will drive Jackson this afternoon for the tag numbers. 3, 4 and 5 are ours. The leaning post at Clinton and Third worries me more. If somebody hit it with a truck the base may be cracked. Terry, please look today and cone it if it's bad.

Ron Diehl, Street Superintendent, (419) 784-2252
