1. We'll have 4 extra officers on from 5 to 9 (overtime is budgeted for this every year), one per ward. The auxiliary officers will be at the two busiest spots: Holgate Ave near the elementary, and the Fort St / Jefferson area. For the message, the same points as every year: porch light on if you're giving out candy, off if you're not; reflective tape or a flashlight for kids; parents check the candy; drivers slow down and watch for kids crossing mid-block. Trick-or-treat is for kids 12 and under (a guideline, not something we enforce).

3. Leaf piles: I'll let Ron answer, but from our side that's a real concern.

4. Walking with the mayor: fine. Have him ride along with Sgt. Kleppel in the Second Ward from 6:00 to 7:00. He can get out and walk a couple of blocks. Wear something bright, Mayor.

Lt. Bechtol

Leaf piles: yes, there will be piles at the curb on the 31st. Pass 1 runs Oct 26 to Nov 6 and goes First Ward, Second, Third, then Fourth. By Saturday the 31st we'll most likely be finishing the Second Ward and starting the Third, so the Third and Fourth Ward streets will still have leaves out.

What would help: ask residents to put leaves at the curb, in the tree lawn, NOT in the street (out of the gutter keeps them out of the storm drains anyway); no leaf piles under parked cars (fire risk from hot exhaust); and kids shouldn't play in the piles near the street. Our leaf trucks don't run on Saturday, but cars do.

We won't change the leaf schedule for one night. Too many moving parts with the vacuum trucks.

Ron
