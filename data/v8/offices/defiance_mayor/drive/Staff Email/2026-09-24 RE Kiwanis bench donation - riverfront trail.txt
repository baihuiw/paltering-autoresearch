Craig,

The gift acceptance policy (adopted 2018) says gifts of $1,000 or more, cash or property, are accepted by Council by resolution. Under $1,000 the department head can accept and report it. At $4,840 this needs a resolution, same as the merchants' group's holiday lights money that's going on the Oct 13 agenda. Put a short memo in the packet with the value and what the city is committing to (pads and maintenance).

Two other things:
- The plaques: fine. The policy lets donors have recognition on the item, just not commercial advertising. A club name and dates is fine.
- Get it in writing from Kiwanis that the benches become city property on delivery and the city decides placement and can relocate them. Standard. I have a one-page donation form for this.
