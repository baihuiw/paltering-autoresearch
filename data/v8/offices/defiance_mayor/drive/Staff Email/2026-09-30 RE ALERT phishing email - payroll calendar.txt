All,

Starting around 1:30 this afternoon a number of city mailboxes received an email with the subject "Updated 2027 Payroll Calendar - Action Required." It looks like a shared-document notice and has a blue "Review Document" button. It is NOT from Finance or Payroll. The link goes to a fake sign-in page that asks for your city email password.

If you got it:
- Do not click the button.
- If you already clicked it but did NOT type your password, close the page and let me know. You're probably fine.
- If you typed your password on that page, call me right away at the helpdesk line or come to my office. I'll reset it and check the sign-in log.
- Delete the email. You can also use "Report Phishing" in Outlook, which sends me a copy.

So far I've blocked the sender and the link at the filter. About 60 copies got through before the block.

Real payroll notices come from payroll@ and never ask you to sign in to see a calendar. When in doubt, call Finance.

Ben
