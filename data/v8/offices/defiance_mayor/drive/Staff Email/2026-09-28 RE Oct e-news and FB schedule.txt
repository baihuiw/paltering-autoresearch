Hannah,

Leaf collection confirmed with the street crew this morning. Vacuum trucks go out Monday, Oct 26 and we run through Friday, Dec 4, weather permitting. Three passes, and each pass goes ward by ward in the same order (First, Second, Third, Fourth), about two to three working days per ward:

- Pass 1: Oct 26 - Nov 6
- Pass 2: Nov 9 - Nov 20
- Pass 3: Nov 23 - Dec 4 (short week for Thanksgiving)

Ask people to rake leaves to the tree lawn, not into the street. No bags, no brush, no grass clippings mixed in, the vacuum can't take them and the crew has to skip the pile. Also please say we can't promise a specific day. Crews go street by street and a bad day of rain pushes everything back.

Bagged leaves and brush go to the compost site on Island Park Avenue, Saturdays 8 AM to noon through Nov 21. Residents only, bring a utility bill or license. Branches under 4 inches across, no stumps, no lumber.

The railroad's contractor is replacing the crossing surface on Hopkins Street. Their notice has the crossing closed to all traffic from 7:00 AM Monday, Oct 12 until 6:00 PM Wednesday, Oct 14. Pedestrians will not be able to cross either.

Detour for cars: use Second Street and Holgate Avenue. Trucks should stay on the state routes. Please don't put a specific reopening hour in big letters; railroads slip, and if it rains Monday they will likely go into Thursday. "Expected to reopen the evening of Wednesday, Oct 14" is fine.

I'll send a detour map PDF tomorrow. If you post it on Facebook, please also post the update when it actually reopens. Last time we had people driving up to the barricades for two days after it was open again because the only post said it was closed.
