Luis,

Current state: we have one part-time parking enforcement officer, 20 hours a week, Tuesday through Friday mornings. She chalks (well, it's the tire photo app now) Clinton and Perry downtown and the courthouse block. Two-hour violation is a $15 ticket, $30 if not paid in 10 days. Last year we wrote about 1,100 parking tickets downtown, most of them two-hour.

The "move the car one space" thing is the real problem. Our ordinance says two hours "in any one space," so moving to the next space technically resets the clock. Some cities have changed theirs to "on the same block face." That's a council question and Steve would need to draft it.

What I can do now without any changes:
- Shift some of her hours to Monday and to lunchtime, since she's mostly out before 11.
- Do a week of warning notices first (yellow cards on the windshield that say "two-hour parking is for customers; all-day parking is free in the Perry St lot") so the employees know it's coming.
- Then a week of actual enforcement with an extra patrol officer on foot for an hour at lunch.

I would NOT do Saturdays. She doesn't work Saturdays, and paying overtime to ticket Saturday shoppers isn't a good look.

Lt. Scott Bechtol
Patrol Division Commander
Defiance Police Department
