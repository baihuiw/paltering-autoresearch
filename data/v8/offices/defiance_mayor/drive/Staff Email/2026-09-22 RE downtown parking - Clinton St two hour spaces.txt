From: Lt. Scott Bechtol <sbechtol@cityofdefiance.com>
Date: Tue, Sep 22, 2026 2:30 PM
To: Luis Ortega

Current state: we have one part-time parking enforcement officer, 20 hours a week, Tuesday through Friday mornings. She covers Clinton and Perry downtown and the courthouse block with the tire photo app. Two-hour violation is a $15 ticket, $30 if not paid in 10 days. Last year we wrote about 1,100 parking tickets downtown, most of them two-hour. Anything outside her hours (blocked driveways, cars on the sidewalk, Saturdays) is a call to the non-emergency line, (419) 784-5050, and the shift sends a patrol car when one is free.

The "move the car one space" thing is the real problem. Our ordinance says two hours "in any one space," so moving to the next space technically resets the clock. Some cities have changed theirs to "on the same block face." That's a council question and Steve would need to draft it.

What I can do now without any changes:
- Shift some of her hours to lunchtime, since she's mostly out before 11.
- Do a week of warning notices first (yellow cards on the windshield: "two-hour parking is for customers; all-day parking is free in the Perry St lot").
- Then regular enforcement, with an extra patrol officer on foot for an hour at lunch.

I would NOT do Saturday ticketing. She doesn't work Saturdays, and paying overtime to ticket Saturday shoppers isn't a good look.

Lt. Scott Bechtol, Patrol Division Commander
