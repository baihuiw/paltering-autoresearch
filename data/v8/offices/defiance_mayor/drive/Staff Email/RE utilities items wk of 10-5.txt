#3 Chemical bids - Recommend low bid both categories. Northwest Ohio Chemical at $1.84/gal for hypo and Lakeshore at $612/dry ton for ferric. Both have supplied us before and we've had no delivery problems. Hypo is up about 6 percent from last year, ferric is basically flat. Tab is attached for Janelle.

#6 Phase 3 - Talked to Josh at Brandt yesterday. They can start the first week of April if we give them the address list by mid-January. Phase 3 is the rest of the Fourth Ward and the north side, roughly 1,700 meters. That finishes residential. Rachel, yes, we can add the service-line notes to their scope. They'll want a small bump per stop for the photo and note. I'll get a number.

#7 Billing - Donna's office caught this yesterday after a run of high-bill calls Friday and Monday. The meter classes we set up in the billing system for the phase 2 meters (HRE58 and HRE34) were built off the phase 1 class and carried its read multiplier of 2. The phase 1 registers read in 2-gallon units, so they need it; the phase 2 registers read in straight gallons and should be 1. And because the old meter final reads were moved into the new meter record at changeout, the 2 got applied to the whole quarter's consumption, not just the days since the swap. So the 10/1 bills for the phase 2 group, about 1,140 accounts, show roughly twice the gallons actually used. The rate increase is in there too but that's only the 6 percent. Donna's people have the classes fixed in the system effective with tomorrow's reads, so nothing going forward is affected. I spot-checked a dozen accounts against the register photos Brandt took at install and they all line up with that explanation. Angela ran a rough number on re-billing or crediting the whole group and it's about $118,000 between water and sewer. I think the Board has to decide how we handle the 10/1 bills, so can you put it on Monday's agenda? Donna and I will both be there.

#8 Software renewal - Donna has the quote, $41,280. She'll send it to Janelle.

Kevin
