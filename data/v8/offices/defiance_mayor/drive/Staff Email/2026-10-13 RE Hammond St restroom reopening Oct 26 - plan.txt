Brenda,

1. Kurtzman's completion date is Friday Oct 23 (they got an extension when the second change order went through). Building and Zoning will do the final inspection that afternoon if Kurtzman is ready, or Monday morning the 26th at the latest. Until the final is signed, we can't open it, which is why it's tentative. Kurtzman says they're on track. The tile is done and the fixtures are going in this week. The last thing is the exterior lighting and the door hardware.
2. Hammond is in the Second Ward, yes. Rita Okuley.
3. The petition in 2024 was from the youth baseball parents. Their league president is Jim Gerschutz. I'd invite him and the neighborhood association president on Hammond (I'll get you her name). And the families from the playground who complained all summer about the portable toilets, but I don't have a list.
4. The Chamber has the big scissors. We have a ribbon roll from the pool opening.

Event: 15 minutes. Mayor says a few words, Rita says a few words, cut the ribbon, people can tour the building. Kurtzman's project manager will be there. It's a restroom, so I don't think we need a tent.

Budget for the record: original contract $146,300, two change orders ($2,215 and $8,940), final $157,455 against a $162,000 budget. The portable toilets stay until the day it opens.

Craig
