Megan,

Here's the weekly rundown of what came in through the mayor@ inbox, the website contact form and the front desk phone, Wednesday Sep 30 through this morning. 61 contacts total (last week 48). Counts are approximate where people wrote more than once.

1. Congratulations / welcome notes to Paul: 9
All answered with the standard thank-you letter. Two asked for a meeting; those went to Brenda.

2. Invitations and meeting requests: 7 (routed to Brenda)
Rotary lunch speaker request (November), a 4-H awards banquet, two neighborhood association meetings (Second Ward, Fourth Ward), Chamber ribbon cutting for a new dental office on Second Street, a church fall dinner, and a Scout troop that wants to tour the City Building.

3. Potholes and street condition: 8
Forwarded to the street department through the work order system. Worst ones are on Jefferson Avenue near the bridge approach (three separate people) and the alley behind the 900 block of Washington. Tom's crew has the Jefferson ones on the list for this week.

4. Trash and recycling: 6
Four missed pickups (all on the same Wednesday route in the Third Ward, the truck broke down; route was finished Thursday). One request for a second recycling cart. One complaint about a neighbor's cart left at the curb all week; logged for code enforcement.

5. Leaf collection questions: 5
People asking when we start. The Facebook graphic went up Monday, so I'd expect this to drop off. I've been pointing them to the pass schedule (Oct 26 start).

6. Hopkins Street crossing: 3
Two business owners on Hopkins worried about deliveries during the closure, one resident asking if the school bus route changes. I gave them Rachel's detour and passed the bus question to the school district transportation office.

7. Code complaints: 4
Junk car in a driveway on Pierce Street, tall grass at a vacant house on Fifth, a fence that may be over a property line (told them it's a civil matter, gave them the surveyor info), and an unpermitted shed. All entered for the building department.

8. Streetlights: 3
Two out on North Clinton, one flickering on Holgate. Reported to the utility.

9. Parks: 3
Two shelter house questions (answered: reservations close Oct 31) and one thank-you for the fall cleanup volunteer day at Kingsbury.

10. Utility billing: 4
Routed to the billing office as casework. Nothing unusual, mostly autopay sign-up questions.

11. E-news: 6
Five residents wrote in that they got the Friends of Paul Haverkamp kickoff invitation on Monday at the email address they only use for the city e-news, and asked whether the city gave out their address. Two of them asked to be taken off the e-news altogether. A sixth person called the front desk with the same question; Jeanne took a message. None answered yet.

12. Miscellaneous: 3
A request for a copy of the 2027 budget calendar (sent), a question about whether the city sells surplus vehicles (pointed to the online auction page), and a complaint about the noise from the Saturday night car meet at the shopping plaza (passed to police non-emergency).

Everything else is handled or routed.
