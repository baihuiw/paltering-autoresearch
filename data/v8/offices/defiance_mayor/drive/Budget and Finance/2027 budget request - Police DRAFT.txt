3. EXPLANATIONS

Overtime (+$22,000). We are running about 6 percent over last year's overtime, mostly from the two vacancies. Even if the vacancies are filled, the new officers won't be solo until late 2027. I used the 2026 projection less the grant-funded enforcement hours, which come back as reimbursement (see section 5).

Contract services (+$5,800). The body-worn camera and in-car video storage subscription with Sentinel Evidence Systems renews in January. Finance has the vendor's quote: $28,600 for 2027, up 4%. Records management system support is up 3%. Our share of the county radio system user fee is flat.

Training (+$3,000). The two new officers' academy costs are in personal services, but they need equipment and field training. The rest is state-mandated continuing professional training hours, crisis intervention training for four more officers, and one supervisor school.

Fuel (-$3,000). The two new patrol vehicles (approved September 14, $112,860 from the Capital Improvement Fund, delivery 14-18 weeks) should be more efficient than Cruisers 11 and 12.

Uniforms and equipment (+$2,200). Body armor replacement is on a five-year cycle. The 2026 vests were bought with a 50% federal Bulletproof Vest Partnership reimbursement. I have assumed 50% again for 2027.

K-9. Vet care at Town & Country Veterinary plus food. Our dog is 6 and doing well.


4. ITEMS NOT INCLUDED / FOR DISCUSSION WITH THE MAYOR

- Records Clerk. This position has been held vacant since 2024 to save money. Records requests are up (body camera video requests especially, which take a lot of staff time to redact). I'd like to talk about filling it as part-time. I have not included it.
- Patrol vehicles. Cruisers are bought from the Capital Improvement Fund, two per year on a rotation. That stays in the capital request, not here. If the rotation slips, our repair line goes up. It always does.
- Dispatch console furniture is 16 years old. It needs replacing in the next two to three years (about $85,000). I will put it in the capital request as a new item without a CIP number.
- Hybrid patrol vehicles were not on the state contract at a price inside our budget this year. Director Cline asked about it at the Board on September 14. I'll look again for 2027.
