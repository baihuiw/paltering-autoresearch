# City of Defiance - Fund Structure and Account Codes (Quick Reference)

*Finance Department. Rev. January 2025. For department heads, clerks and anyone entering requisitions. Not a substitute for the chart of accounts in the accounting system.*

---

## How an account number reads

`FFF.DDDD.OOOOO`

- **FFF** = fund (who pays)
- **DDDD** = department or division (who spends)
- **OOOOO** = object (what it's for)

Example: `201.0510.52300` = Street Construction, Maintenance & Repair Fund, Street Department, fuel.

If you aren't sure which fund pays for something, ask Finance **before** you order it. Fixing a coding error after the PO is issued means a PO change, and sometimes a then-and-now certificate.

## Major funds

| Fund | Name | Main revenue | Typical uses |
|---|---|---|---|
| 101 | General Fund | Income tax (80% share), property tax (inside millage), Local Government Fund, fines, permits, investment income | Police, Fire/EMS (GF share), Parks, administration, Law, Engineering, Building & Zoning |
| 201 | Street Construction, Maintenance & Repair ("Street Fund") | Gasoline tax and motor vehicle license fees | Street Department operations, snow and ice, street equipment, signals |
| 202 | State Highway | 7.5% of gas tax and license fees | State routes inside the city (salt, patching) |
| 203 | Street Levy | Voter-approved property tax levy | Annual resurfacing program, sign retroreflectivity program, some street equipment per CIP |
| 210 | Fire/EMS Levy | Voter-approved property tax levy; EMS billing | Third medic unit staffing, apparatus maintenance and replacement, Engine 1 lease-purchase |
| 215 | EMS Capital | Transfers; grants | EMS equipment (stretchers, monitors) |
| 301 | Bond Retirement | Transfers from 101 and 401 | General obligation bond principal and interest |
| 401 | Capital Improvement | Income tax (20% share); grants; donations | Park projects, police vehicles, buildings, technology per CIP |
| 501 | Water Operating | Water charges | Water treatment and distribution, utility billing (shared) |
| 502 | Water Capital Improvement | Transfers from 501; OWDA loan proceeds | Water plant and main projects |
| 511 | Sanitary Sewer Operating | Sewer charges | WPCF, sewer maintenance, utility billing (shared), OWDA Loan No. 5841 |
| 512 | Sanitary Sewer Capital Improvement | Transfers from 511 | Sewer capital projects |
| 521 | Stormwater | Stormwater utility fee | Storm sewer maintenance, outfalls, street sweeping (shared) |

Smaller special revenue and agency funds (law enforcement trust, court computer, FEMA/state reimbursements, unclaimed monies, payroll clearing and others) are listed in report FIN-110.

## Purchasing reminders (Codified Ord. Ch. 135)

- Under $10,000: department head approval with a purchase order.
- $10,000 and up: three informal quotes, attached to the requisition.
- $25,000 and up: Board of Control approval. Agenda items to the Deputy Clerk by Thursday noon.
- Over the state competitive bidding threshold (ORC 735.05): formal bid.
- **Get the PO before you order.** If you can't, call Finance. A then-and-now certificate is for real exceptions, not a routine fix.
- Blanket POs are for recurring items (fuel, uniforms, office supplies) and expire December 31.

## Income tax distribution

Net collections are distributed monthly: **80% to Fund 101** and **20% to Fund 401**, per the ordinance that set the current rate.
