The health benefits consortium sent its preliminary 2027 renewal yesterday. Please use these numbers in your 2027 budget workbooks. Finance will calculate the benefits lines for you anyway; this is so you know what's coming.

Preliminary renewal: +7.8% over 2026 premium-equivalent rates, all tiers.

                              2026 monthly     2027 prelim monthly    Employee share (12%)
  Single                        $812.40             $875.77               $105.09
  Employee + spouse           $1,706.05           $1,839.12               $220.69
  Employee + child(ren)       $1,543.55           $1,663.95               $199.67
  Family                      $2,355.95           $2,539.71               $304.77

City cost for 2027 at current enrollment (174 covered employees) is estimated at $3.26 million across all funds, up about $236,000. The General Fund share of the increase is about $150,000. Dental and vision renewals are flat.

The final renewal comes the last week of October. The consortium's board meets October 22. Last year the preliminary was 8.5% and the final was 6.9%. I'm not counting on that happening again, but it's possible. The consortium said its claims were higher than expected in the first half of 2026 and that stop-loss reinsurance went up 11%.

Nothing changes for employees until January. The employee share stays at 12% under the current AFSCME, FOP and firefighters' agreements. Open enrollment is November 2-13. HR will send the packets and schedule the two on-site sessions (City Building and the WTP).

Questions to me or to Marcy in Finance.
