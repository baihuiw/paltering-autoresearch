## 1. Where 2026 is heading (General Fund)

| | 2026 Budget | 2026 Projected |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 14,680,000 | 14,972,000 |
| Expenditures | 14,980,000 | 14,610,000 |
| Change in cash balance | (300,000) | +362,000 |
| Jan 1 balance | 5,284,117 | 5,284,117 |
| Projected Dec 31 balance | 4,984,117 | 5,646,117 |

Through August, revenue was 66.7% of the estimate and spending 63.8% of appropriations. Income tax was 2.9% ahead of 2025 and 1.4% over the year-to-date estimate. The improvement over budget comes mostly from income tax, investment income, and building permits. About $150,000 of it is one-time (insurance reimbursement, workers' comp rebate, higher-than-normal investment yields). September figures will be in the September financial report on October 9, and the projection will be updated for October 20.

Licenses and permits reflect the 2027 fee schedule effective January 1. The 2026 miscellaneous projection includes one-time items that won't repeat.
