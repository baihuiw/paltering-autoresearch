2. COMMENTS

Withholding. September withholding was up 3.0 percent from last year. The three largest employers' remittances were each up between 2 and 4 percent. One mid-sized manufacturer moved from quarterly to monthly filing in July, which shifts some of its payments earlier in the year compared with 2025. Year to date, withholding is up 3.4 percent.

Individual. Third-quarter estimated payments were due September 15. We mailed reminder coupons on September 8 to 1,862 taxpayers with estimate history. The main mailing went out on time. A second batch of coupons for taxpayers who called in for them couldn't be printed until a form template problem was fixed on 9/22 (IT-26-0684). Individual estimates were up 5.2 percent for the month.

Net profit. Net profit collections are flat for the year. Two large business filers had lower 2025 profits and reduced their 2026 estimates. This was expected when the budget was built.

Refunds. Refunds are slightly lower than last year. Most 2025 individual refunds were issued by July. September refunds were mostly amended returns and two employer overpayment corrections.

4. DELINQUENCY AND COMPLIANCE

Non-filer letters for tax year 2025 went out August 14 to 1,214 residents who filed in 2024 but not in 2025 and had no exemption on file. As of September 30:
  - 512 have filed a return (42%)
  - 288 responded with an exemption (retired, moved, under 18, etc.)
  - 414 no response. A second notice goes out October 15.

Balance-due accounts referred to collections in September: 31, totaling $18,406.22. Collections from referred accounts in September: $6,882.15. We filed four small claims cases in Municipal Court in September for employer accounts that withheld tax but did not remit. Two of them paid in full before the hearing date.

Employer withholding reconciliations (Form W-3 equivalent) for 2025: 1,038 of 1,062 accounts filed. The remaining 24 are mostly closed businesses. Letters were sent in July.
