This letter confirms our understanding of the services the Auditor of State (AOS) will provide to the City of Defiance (the City) for the year ending December 31, 2026. Please review it carefully. If it agrees with your understanding, have it signed by the officials named below and return one copy to our regional office. We ask that you return it by October 30, 2026.

Congratulations on your appointment, Mayor Haverkamp. We look forward to working with you and your staff.


1. SCOPE OF THE ENGAGEMENT

We will audit the financial statements of the governmental activities, the business-type activities, each major fund, and the aggregate remaining fund information of the City as of and for the year ending December 31, 2026, and the related notes, which together make up the City's basic financial statements.

We will also report on internal control over financial reporting and on compliance with certain provisions of laws, regulations, contracts and grant agreements, as required by Government Auditing Standards.

If the City spends $1,000,000 or more in federal awards during 2026, we will also perform a single audit under the Uniform Guidance (2 CFR Part 200, Subpart F). Based on the grant information the Finance Department provided, federal expenditures in 2026 are expected to be below that threshold. Please let us know right away if this changes, for example if a federal grant application now pending is awarded and spent before year end.

Our audit will be conducted in accordance with auditing standards generally accepted in the United States and Government Auditing Standards issued by the Comptroller General of the United States. The objective is to express opinions on whether the financial statements are fairly presented, in all material respects. An audit is not designed to detect every error or instance of fraud, and it is not a guarantee.


2. MANAGEMENT'S RESPONSIBILITIES

City management is responsible for:

a. Preparing and fairly presenting the financial statements, including the notes and required supplementary information, and filing the annual financial report with the Auditor of State within the time required by Ohio Revised Code Section 117.38 (generally 60 days after year end for cash-basis filers, or 150 days for GAAP-basis filers);
b. Designing, implementing and maintaining internal control relevant to the preparation of the financial statements;
c. Identifying and complying with the laws, regulations, contracts and grant agreements that apply to the City's activities, including the budgetary requirements of Ohio Revised Code Chapter 5705;
d. Giving us access to all records, documents and staff that we consider relevant, and making any electronic records available in a usable format;
e. Correcting misstatements we identify, or determining that uncorrected misstatements are immaterial, and confirming this in writing;
f. Telling us about any known or suspected fraud, noncompliance or significant deficiencies, and about any communications from regulators or grantors;
g. Providing a signed management representation letter at the end of fieldwork.

The City has elected to file its annual financial report on the regulatory cash basis, as in prior years. Notes to the financial statements will be prepared by the Finance Department using the AOS cash-basis note templates.


3. TIMING AND STAFFING

We plan the following schedule, subject to change after we confirm it with the Finance Director:

- Planning and risk assessment: January 2027.
- Interim fieldwork (internal control, payroll, purchasing, income tax): February and March 2027.
- Final fieldwork: after the annual financial report is filed, expected May 2027.
- Exit conference and release: June-July 2027.

The engagement will be supervised by Janet Kemerer, CPA, Audit Manager, with a senior auditor and one or two staff auditors on site. We would like a workspace with network access during fieldwork. For the fiscal 2025 audit the City provided Conference Room B, which worked well.

During interim fieldwork we will ask for the items on the provided-by-client list, which we will send in December. The list includes the year-end bank reconciliations, the investment ledger and safekeeping statements, payroll registers, Board of Control and Council minutes, the appropriation ordinance and amendments, certificates of estimated resources, the income tax system reconciliation, utility billing system reports, the debt schedule, and capital asset additions and disposals.

Electronic data requests should go through the AOS secure file transfer portal. Please do not email data files containing personal information.


4. FEES

Our fees are based on the hours worked by our staff at the rates set by the Auditor of State, plus travel and other direct costs. We estimate the cost of this audit at $46,750, compared with $44,900 for fiscal 2025. The increase reflects the hourly rate adjustment effective July 1, 2026, and some additional time for the change in the mayor's office and any new financial system procedures.

The estimate assumes:
- records are ready on the dates agreed;
- no single audit is required;
- no significant audit findings require additional work; and
- the annual financial report is filed on time.

If any of these assumptions change, we will tell you before additional hours are spent. We bill monthly while the engagement is in progress. Invoices are due within 30 days.


5. REPORTING

We will issue a written report on the financial statements and the report on internal control and compliance required by Government Auditing Standards. If we identify findings for recovery, material noncompliance or material weaknesses, they will be included in the report with management's response. We may also give the City a separate management letter with recommendations that do not rise to the level of findings.

The report is a public record when released and will be posted on the Auditor of State's website.


6. OTHER MATTERS

- The audit documentation is the property of the Auditor of State and will be kept under the AOS records retention schedule.
- Under Ohio law the Auditor of State may perform other procedures at any time, including special audits, if circumstances warrant.
- This letter remains in effect until the audit is released or the engagement is ended in writing by either party.

If you have questions about this letter, please call me or Janet Kemerer at the Northwest Region office.
