1. SUMMARY

Through August, eight months or 66.7 percent of the year, the City's major operating funds are in line with the 2026 budget.

- General Fund revenue is $9,792,366.41, or 66.7 percent of the annual estimate. Expenditures are $9,560,215.30, or 63.8 percent of appropriations. The General Fund cash balance is $5,516,268.53, up $232,151.11 from January 1.
- Municipal income tax collections (all funds) through August are $9,612,480.00. That is 2.9 percent more than the same period in 2025 and about 1.4 percent above the year-to-date budget estimate.
- The second-half real estate tax settlement from the County Auditor had not been received at the end of August. It is expected in mid-September. That is why property tax revenue in the General Fund, the Street Levy Fund and the Fire/EMS Levy Fund shows only about half of the annual estimate.
- The Water Operating Fund has a cash balance of $1,169,504.01. July quarterly billing collections came in as expected. Water and sewer rates under Ordinance No. 2026-38 apply to bills issued on or after October 1, so this year's revenue estimate includes only one quarter at the new rates.
- One appropriation amendment is needed this month: Ordinance No. 2026-42 (the BWC grant for the Medic 2 and Medic 3 powered stretchers, plus three small transfers). It goes to the Finance Committee September 17 for Council's next regular meeting.

7. ITEMS TO WATCH

- Overtime. Fire/EMS overtime is about 11 percent above last year, mostly because of injury leave and a paramedic class. Police overtime is up about 6 percent because of two vacancies. The Police vacancies are expected to be filled this fall from the current eligibility list.
- Health insurance. The consortium's preliminary 2027 renewal is 7.8 percent. The final rate is expected in late October and will be built into the 2027 budget.
- Road salt. The ODOT winter contract price has not been announced yet. The Street Fund 2026 budget assumed last season's $78.20 per ton.
- Water Fund. Cash is lower than at this point last year, partly because of the timing of reimbursements on the Ohio Water Development Authority loan for the treatment plant filter project. Draws are paid to the contractor from the Water Capital Improvement Fund and reimbursed by OWDA, usually within 30 to 45 days. Debt service coverage for 2026 is projected to meet the 1.15 times requirement once fourth-quarter billing at the new rates is collected. I will update the Finance Committee in November.
- 2027 budget. The draft budget calendar is attached for the Finance Committee's review on September 17. Operating requests will be due Friday, October 2, and capital requests Friday, October 9.
