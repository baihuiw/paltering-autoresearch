ORDINANCE NO. 2026-44

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE ZONING DISTRICT MAP OF THE CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO, ESTABLISHED BY SECTION 1121.02 OF THE PLANNING AND ZONING CODE, TO REZONE PARCELS AT 1536 AND 1542 NORTH CLINTON STREET FROM R-2 SINGLE-FAMILY RESIDENTIAL DISTRICT TO O-1 OFFICE DISTRICT

Introduced: October 13, 2026 (first reading)
Public hearing: Tuesday, November 10, 2026, 6:45 p.m., Council Chambers (set by motion October 13, 2026)
Second reading: October 27, 2026 (scheduled)
Third reading: November 10, 2026, following the public hearing (scheduled)
Planning Commission recommendation: September 29, 2026 (approve, 4-0)
Application No. Z-26-04

WHEREAS, Hartwell Dental Group, Inc., owner of the parcels at 1536 and 1542 North Clinton Street, has applied for an amendment to the Zoning District Map to rezone those parcels from R-2 Single-Family Residential District to O-1 Office District, in order to construct an addition to its existing dental office at 1530 North Clinton Street and additional off-street parking; and

WHEREAS, the parcel at 1530 North Clinton Street is zoned O-1 Office District, and the parcels to the north and east of the subject parcels are zoned R-2 and are developed with single-family dwellings; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission, after a public meeting held September 29, 2026, at which the applicant and interested persons were heard, recommended approval of the amendment by a vote of 4-0, and noted that its approval of the related site plan was conditioned on a six-foot solid fence along the north and east lot lines, landscaping in accordance with Chapter 1171, and full-cutoff parking lot lighting not exceeding 0.5 foot-candle at the residential property lines; and

WHEREAS, Council has set a public hearing on this ordinance for November 10, 2026, and has directed the Clerk to publish notice at least thirty days before the hearing and to mail notice to the owners of property within 200 feet of the parcels, as required by Section 713.12 of the Ohio Revised Code and Section 1113.06 of the Planning and Zoning Code;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio:

SECTION 1. That the Zoning District Map of the City of Defiance, as established by Section 1121.02 of the Planning and Zoning Code, is hereby amended to change the zoning classification of the following described real property from R-2 Single-Family Residential District to O-1 Office District:

Parcel 1: Situated in the City of Defiance, County of Defiance, State of Ohio, and being Lot 18 of Clinton Heights Addition, as recorded in Plat Volume 4, Page 61 of the Defiance County Plat Records, commonly known as 1536 North Clinton Street (Parcel No. D03-0207-0018), containing 0.21 acre, more or less.

Parcel 2: Situated in the City of Defiance, County of Defiance, State of Ohio, and being Lot 19 of Clinton Heights Addition, as recorded in Plat Volume 4, Page 61 of the Defiance County Plat Records, commonly known as 1542 North Clinton Street (Parcel No. D03-0207-0019), containing 0.21 acre, more or less.

Total area: 0.42 acre, more or less.

SECTION 2. The Zoning Administrator is directed to amend the official Zoning District Map to reflect the change made by this ordinance upon its effective date.

SECTION 3. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the passage of this ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 4. This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after the earliest period allowed by law.
