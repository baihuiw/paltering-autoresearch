# CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO
## Defiance City Council: Regular Meeting

**Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 7:00 p.m.**
**Council Chambers, City Building, 631 Perry Street**
**Posted:** Friday, October 9, 2026

Presiding: Gary Lehman, President of Council
Clerk: Sandra Moll, Clerk of Council

---

### ORDER OF BUSINESS

1. Call to order
2. Pledge of Allegiance and moment of silence
3. Roll call
4. Approval of minutes: regular meeting of September 23, 2026 *(includes one clerk's correction to the Fire Chief's report; see red-line in packet)*
5. Recognition: Gene Wolfrum, Street Department, retiring after 31 years of service (Mayor Haverkamp)
6. Communications
   a. Ohio Department of Transportation, District 1: notice of railroad crossing work, Hopkins Street, October 12-14 (information)
   b. Downtown merchants' group: letter offering a $2,500 donation toward downtown holiday lighting (see Res. 2026-16)
7. Public comment (3 minutes per speaker; please sign in with the Clerk)
8. Report of the Mayor
9. Reports of the administration
   a. Finance Director
   b. Safety-Service Director
   c. City Engineer
   d. Fire Chief
   e. Law Director
10. Reports of committees
    a. Committee of the Whole: budget session of October 6 (Lehman)
    b. Safety Committee: minutes of October 7 (Gaskins)
    c. Parks and Recreation Committee: minutes of September 30 (Okuley)
    d. Utilities Committee (Pfahler)
    e. Finance Committee (Ruhl)
    f. Streets and Sidewalks Committee (Sprow): meeting October 15, 5:30 p.m., Conference Room B
    g. Planning Commission: meeting of September 29 (Brinkman)
11. Legislation

**Third reading**

- **Ordinance No. 2026-39.** Vacating a portion of the alley between Jackson Avenue and Agnes Street, Seventh Street to Eighth Street, and reserving a utility easement.

**Second reading**

- **Ordinance No. 2026-40.** Authorizing the Mayor to enter into a lease of approximately 41 acres on Domersville Road for agricultural use, crop years 2027-2029. *(Law Director recommends withdrawal; the Board of Control approved the lease September 28)*
- **Ordinance No. 2026-43.** Authorizing the Board of Control to enter into a three-year contract for EMS billing and collection services. *(Safety Committee recommendation of Oct 7)*

**First reading**

- **Ordinance No. 2026-44.** Amending the Zoning District Map to rezone 1536 and 1542 North Clinton Street (0.42 acre, two parcels) from R-2 Single-Family Residential to O-1 Office District. *(Planning Commission recommendation of September 29; public hearing to be set)*
- **Ordinance No. 2026-45.** Amending the 2026 annual appropriation ordinance to provide for additional appropriations and transfers within the Fire/EMS Levy Fund, the Street Construction, Maintenance and Repair Fund and the Capital Improvement Fund, and declaring an emergency. *(Finance Director; suspension of the rules requested)*

**Resolutions**

- **Resolution No. 2026-16.** Accepting a donation of $2,500 from the Downtown merchants' group for downtown holiday lighting.
- **Resolution No. 2026-17.** Confirming the Mayor's appointment of Linda Hohenberger to the Planning Commission for the unexpired term ending December 31, 2028.

12. Old business
13. New business
    a. Motion to set a public hearing on Ordinance No. 2026-44 for Tuesday, November 10, 2026, at 6:45 p.m.
14. Council member comments
15. Adjournment
