ORDINANCE NO. 2026-38

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 1042.03 AND 1048.04 OF THE CODIFIED ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO, TO ADJUST WATER AND SEWER RATES AND CHARGES EFFECTIVE WITH BILLS ISSUED ON OR AFTER OCTOBER 1, 2026, AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

Introduced: August 25, 2026 (first reading)
Second reading waived: September 8, 2026
Passed: September 8, 2026
Sponsor: Utilities Committee (Councilman Dan Pfahler, chair)

WHEREAS, the City's Water Treatment Plant filters, underdrains and clearwell, portions of which date to 1974, have reached the end of their useful life, and Council by Ordinance No. 2025-18 authorized the Filter Rehabilitation and Clearwell Improvements project and a loan from the Ohio Water Development Authority to finance it; and

WHEREAS, the City's Water Pollution Control Plant requires replacement of its aeration blowers and improvements to its sludge handling equipment to maintain compliance with its discharge permit; and

WHEREAS, the cost of treatment chemicals, electricity, and materials for the water and sewer systems has increased substantially since water and sewer rates were last adjusted in October 2023; and

WHEREAS, the City's rate consultant, in its Water and Sewer Rate Study dated June 2026, recommended an increase of 6 percent effective October 2026 to pay operating costs, fund the capital improvements described above, and maintain debt service coverage of at least 1.15 times as required by the City's loan agreements; and

WHEREAS, the Utilities Committee reviewed the rate study in public meetings on July 13 and August 10, 2026, and recommended the increase;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio, a majority of all members elected thereto concurring:

SECTION 1. Section 1042.03, "Water Rates," is hereby amended to read as follows:

1042.03 WATER RATES.

(a) Quarterly service charge, by meter size:

(b) Commodity charge for all water used, as measured by the meter:

(c) Charges shall be billed quarterly in arrears, based on meter readings taken by the Division of Utilities.

(c) Water measured by an approved separate irrigation meter is not subject to the sewer commodity charge.

SECTION 3. Effect on a typical residential customer. For the information of Council and the public, the rate consultant has estimated that a typical inside-city residential customer with a 5/8 x 3/4 inch meter using 9,000 gallons per quarter will pay $106.00 per quarter for water and sewer service under the rates in Sections 1 and 2, compared with $100.00 per quarter under the current rates, an increase of $6.00 per quarter or about $2.00 per month.

SECTION 4. The rates and charges set forth in this ordinance shall apply to all bills issued on or after October 1, 2026, including bills for service periods that began before that date.

SECTION 5. Water revenue shall be deposited in the Water Fund and sewer revenue in the Sewer Fund.

SECTION 6. Sections 1042.03 and 1048.04 as amended by Ordinance No. 2023-37 continue to apply to bills issued before October 1, 2026.

SECTION 7. All formal actions and deliberations concerning this ordinance took place in open meetings in compliance with Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 8. This ordinance is declared an emergency measure necessary for the preservation of the public peace, health, safety and welfare, and so that the adjusted rates are in effect for the quarterly billing issued October 1, 2026; it takes effect immediately upon passage and approval.

VOTE ON SUSPENSION OF THE RULES (SECOND READING): Yeas: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins. Nays: Bell. Motion carried, 6-1.

VOTE ON PASSAGE AND EMERGENCY CLAUSE: Yeas: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins. Nays: Bell. Passed as an emergency measure, 6-1.
