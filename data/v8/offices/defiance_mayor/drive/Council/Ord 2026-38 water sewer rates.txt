ORDINANCE NO. 2026-38

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTIONS 1042.03 AND 1048.04 OF THE CODIFIED ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO, TO ADJUST WATER AND SEWER RATES AND CHARGES EFFECTIVE WITH BILLS ISSUED ON OR AFTER OCTOBER 1, 2026, AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

Introduced: August 25, 2026 (first reading)
Second reading waived: September 8, 2026
Passed: September 8, 2026
Sponsor: Utilities Committee (Councilman Dan Pfahler, chair)

WHEREAS, the City of Defiance owns and operates a public water system and a wastewater collection and treatment system serving the residents and businesses of the City, and is responsible for operating those systems in compliance with the requirements of the Ohio Environmental Protection Agency and the federal Safe Drinking Water Act and Clean Water Act; and

WHEREAS, the City's Water Treatment Plant filters, underdrains and clearwell, portions of which date to 1974, have reached the end of their useful life, and Council by Ordinance No. 2025-18 authorized the Filter Rehabilitation and Clearwell Improvements project and a loan from the Ohio Water Development Authority to finance it; and

WHEREAS, the City's Water Pollution Control Plant requires replacement of its aeration blowers and improvements to its sludge handling equipment, as identified in the Wastewater Facilities Plan accepted by Council in 2025, in order to maintain compliance with the plant's discharge permit; and

WHEREAS, the City is replacing aging residential water meters citywide with radio-read meters, which will improve the accuracy of meter reading, reduce estimated bills, and allow earlier detection of leaks on customers' property; and

WHEREAS, the cost of treatment chemicals, electricity, and materials for the water and sewer systems has increased substantially since water and sewer rates were last adjusted in October 2023; and

WHEREAS, the City's rate consultant, in its Water and Sewer Rate Study dated June 2026, recommended an increase of 6 percent in water and sewer rates effective October 2026 to provide revenue sufficient to pay operating costs, fund the capital improvements described above, and maintain debt service coverage of at least 1.15 times as required by the City's loan agreements; and

WHEREAS, the Utilities Committee of Council held public meetings on the rate study on July 13 and August 10, 2026, and recommended the increase to Council;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio, a majority of all members elected thereto concurring:

SECTION 1. That Section 1042.03 of the Codified Ordinances of the City of Defiance, "Water Rates," is hereby amended to read as follows:

1042.03 WATER RATES.

(a) Quarterly service charge. Each water account shall be charged a quarterly service charge based on meter size, as follows:

Meter size        Current (through 9/30/2026)    Effective with bills issued on or after 10/1/2026
5/8 x 3/4 inch    $5.40                          $5.72
3/4 inch          $7.80                          $8.27
1 inch            $13.20                         $13.99
1-1/2 inch        $26.10                         $27.67
2 inch            $41.60                         $44.10
3 inch            $83.00                         $87.98
4 inch            $129.50                        $137.27
6 inch            $259.00                        $274.54

(b) Commodity charge. In addition to the quarterly service charge, each account shall be charged for all water used, as measured by the meter, at the following rate:

Inside City:      $5.10 per 1,000 gallons (current); $5.41 per 1,000 gallons (effective with bills issued on or after 10/1/2026)
Outside City:     $7.65 per 1,000 gallons (current); $8.11 per 1,000 gallons (effective with bills issued on or after 10/1/2026)

(c) Bulk water sold at the City bulk station shall be charged at $9.15 per 1,000 gallons, with a minimum charge of $5.00 per transaction.

(d) Charges for water used and service charges shall be billed quarterly in arrears, based on meter readings taken by the Division of Utilities. Where a reading cannot be obtained, consumption may be estimated in accordance with Section 1042.07.

SECTION 2. That Section 1048.04 of the Codified Ordinances of the City of Defiance, "Sewer Rates," is hereby amended to read as follows:

1048.04 SEWER RATES.

(a) Quarterly service charge. Each sewer account shall be charged a quarterly service charge based on the size of the water meter serving the premises, as follows:

Meter size        Current (through 9/30/2026)    Effective with bills issued on or after 10/1/2026
5/8 x 3/4 inch    $4.60                          $4.88
3/4 inch          $6.65                          $7.05

6 inch            $220.50                        $233.73

(b) Commodity charge. In addition to the quarterly service charge, each sewer account shall be charged on the basis of water used, as measured by the water meter serving the premises, at the following rate:

Inside City:      $4.90 per 1,000 gallons (current); $5.19 per 1,000 gallons (effective with bills issued on or after 10/1/2026)
Outside City:     $7.35 per 1,000 gallons (current); $7.79 per 1,000 gallons (effective with bills issued on or after 10/1/2026)

(c) Where a customer has installed a separately metered irrigation service approved by the Division of Utilities, water measured by the irrigation meter shall not be subject to the sewer commodity charge.

(d) Industrial users subject to pretreatment surcharges shall continue to be billed in accordance with Section 1048.09.

SECTION 3. Effect on a typical residential customer. For the information of Council and the public, the rate consultant has estimated that a typical inside-city residential customer with a 5/8 x 3/4 inch meter using 9,000 gallons per quarter will pay $106.00 per quarter for water and sewer service under the rates in Sections 1 and 2, compared with $100.00 per quarter under the current rates, an increase of $6.00 per quarter or about $2.00 per month.

SECTION 4. The rates and charges set forth in this ordinance shall apply to all bills issued on or after October 1, 2026, including bills for service periods that began before that date.

SECTION 5. Revenue from water rates and charges shall be deposited in the Water Fund and revenue from sewer rates and charges shall be deposited in the Sewer Fund, to be used for the operation, maintenance, improvement and debt service of the respective systems as provided by law.

SECTION 6. All ordinances and parts of ordinances inconsistent with this ordinance are hereby repealed to the extent of the inconsistency. The provisions of Sections 1042.03 and 1048.04 as amended by Ordinance No. 2023-37 shall continue to apply to bills issued before October 1, 2026.

SECTION 7. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the passage of this ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 8. This ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, safety and welfare of the City, and for the further reason that the adjusted rates must be in effect for the quarterly billing cycle issued October 1, 2026 in order to provide revenue for the operation of the water and sewer systems and to meet the City's debt service coverage obligations. Wherefore, this ordinance shall take effect and be in force immediately upon its passage and approval.
