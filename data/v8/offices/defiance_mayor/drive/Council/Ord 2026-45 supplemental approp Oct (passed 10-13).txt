ORDINANCE NO. 2026-45

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 2025-52, THE ANNUAL APPROPRIATION ORDINANCE FOR THE YEAR ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2026, TO PROVIDE FOR ADDITIONAL APPROPRIATIONS AND TRANSFERS WITHIN THE FIRE AND EMS LEVY FUND, THE STREET CONSTRUCTION, MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR FUND AND THE CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT FUND, AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

Introduced, rules suspended and passed: October 13, 2026
Sponsor: Finance Committee, at the request of the Finance Director

WHEREAS, the City's aerial ladder truck (Ladder 1) was removed from service on September 24, 2026 because of a failure of its main lift cylinder, and the Board of Control on October 5, 2026 approved its repair at a cost of $18,400; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Control on October 12, 2026 approved repairs to leaf vacuum unit No. 2 at a cost not to exceed $9,860 and authorized the employment of up to four seasonal laborers for the 2026 leaf collection season; and

WHEREAS, the Riverside Park shelter house roof was damaged in the storm of August 24, 2026, the City's insurer has approved the claim subject to the City's $5,000 deductible, and the Board of Control on October 12, 2026 awarded the roof replacement in the amount of $23,800; and

WHEREAS, the Finance Director has certified that the amounts appropriated by this ordinance are available or in the process of collection;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio, at least two-thirds of all members elected thereto concurring:

SECTION 1. FIRE/EMS LEVY FUND (210).
That there is hereby appropriated from the unappropriated balance of the Fire and EMS Levy Fund the following additional amount:

  210.0220.53300  Fire/EMS - Repairs and Maintenance (apparatus)      $18,400.00

SECTION 2. STREET CONSTRUCTION, MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR FUND (201).
That there are hereby appropriated from the unappropriated balance of the Street Construction, Maintenance and Repair Fund the following additional amounts:

  201.0510.53300  Street - Repairs and Maintenance (equipment)        $9,860.00
  201.0510.51200  Street - Salaries, Part Time / Seasonal             $14,600.00
  201.0510.51400  Street - Employer Pension and Medicare (seasonal)   $2,250.00

SECTION 3. CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT FUND (401).
(a) That the estimated resources of the Capital Improvement Fund are hereby increased by $18,800.00 to reflect insurance proceeds for storm damage to the Riverside Park shelter house (revenue account 401.0000.48700, Insurance Recoveries).
(b) That there is hereby appropriated from the Capital Improvement Fund the following additional amount:

  401.0310.55200  Parks Capital - Capital Outlay, Improvements        $18,800.00

(c) That the following transfer of appropriation within the Capital Improvement Fund is hereby made to cover the City's deductible:

  From: 401.0310.55100  Parks Capital - Capital Outlay, Equipment     ($5,000.00)
  To:   401.0310.55200  Parks Capital - Capital Outlay, Improvements  $5,000.00

SECTION 4. SUMMARY.

  Fund                                     Additional appropriation
  Fire/EMS Levy Fund (210)                 $18,400.00
  Street Constr., Maint. and Repair (201)  $26,710.00
  Capital Improvement Fund (401)           $18,800.00
  TOTAL                                    $63,910.00

SECTION 5. The Finance Director is hereby authorized and directed to make the necessary entries in the City's accounting records and to certify a copy of this ordinance to the Defiance County Auditor.

SECTION 6. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the passage of this ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 7. This ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, safety and welfare of the City, and for the further reason that the repair of Ladder 1 is necessary to restore the City's aerial fire protection capability and that the leaf collection season begins October 26, 2026. Wherefore, this ordinance shall take effect and be in force immediately upon its passage and approval.

VOTE ON SUSPENSION OF THE RULES:
Yeas: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Bell, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins
Nays: none
Motion carried, 7-0.

VOTE ON PASSAGE AND EMERGENCY CLAUSE:
Yeas: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Bell, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins
Nays: none
Ordinance passed as an emergency measure, 7-0.
