# CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO
## Defiance City Council: Regular Meeting

**Tuesday, September 8, 2026, 7:00 p.m.**
**Council Chambers, City Building, 631 Perry Street**
**Posted:** Friday, September 4, 2026

Presiding: Gary Lehman, President of Council (serving as Acting Mayor)
Clerk: Sandra Moll, Clerk of Council

### ORDER OF BUSINESS

1. Call to order
2. Pledge of Allegiance and moment of silence
3. Roll call
4. Approval of minutes: regular meeting of August 25, 2026
5. Communications
   a. Letter of resignation from the previous mayor, effective September 2, 2026 (received and filed)
   b. Memorandum from the Law Director: Charter provisions for filling a vacancy in the office of mayor
   c. Defiance County Budget Commission: certificate of amounts and rates for tax year 2026 / collection year 2027
   d. Ohio Division of Liquor Control: notice of transfer of D-5 permit, 412 Clinton Street (no objection recommended by Police)
6. Public comment (agenda and non-agenda items; 3 minutes per speaker; please sign in with the Clerk)
7. Report of the Acting Mayor
8. Reports of the administration
   a. Finance Director
   b. Safety-Service Director
   c. City Engineer
   d. Law Director
9. Reports of committees
   a. Utilities Committee (Pfahler)
   b. Finance Committee (Ruhl)
   c. Streets and Sidewalks Committee (Sprow)
   d. Parks and Recreation Committee (Okuley)
   e. Safety Committee (Gaskins)
10. Legislation

**Third reading**

- **Ordinance No. 2026-34.** An ordinance amending Section 351.14 of the Codified Ordinances to prohibit parking on the east side of Agnes Street between Second Street and Third Street. *(Streets and Sidewalks Committee)*

**Second reading**

- **Ordinance No. 2026-35.** An ordinance repealing and replacing Chapter 1355 of the Codified Ordinances, "Nuisance Abatement," to update notice, abatement and cost-recovery procedures for weeds, rubbish and inoperable vehicles. *(Safety Committee; Law Director)*
- **Ordinance No. 2026-37.** An ordinance amending Section 1155.08 of the Planning and Zoning Code, "Fences and Walls," regarding fence height in front yards and on corner lots. *(Planning Commission recommendation of July 28, 2026)*
- **Ordinance No. 2026-38.** An ordinance amending Sections 1042.03 and 1048.04 of the Codified Ordinances to adjust water and sewer rates and charges effective with bills issued on or after October 1, 2026, and declaring an emergency. *(Utilities Committee)* Motion to suspend the rule requiring three readings is anticipated.

**First reading**

- **Ordinance No. 2026-39.** An ordinance vacating a portion of the 16-foot north-south alley lying between Jackson Avenue and Agnes Street, from Seventh Street to Eighth Street, upon petition of all abutting owners, and reserving a utility easement. *(Planning Commission recommendation of July 28, 2026)*

**Resolutions**

- **Resolution No. 2026-14.** A resolution accepting the amounts and rates as determined by the Defiance County Budget Commission and authorizing the necessary tax levies and certifying them to the County Auditor, and declaring an emergency. *(Finance Committee)*

11. Old business
12. New business
    a. Scheduling of a special meeting to act on the appointment to fill the vacancy in the office of mayor
13. Council member comments
14. Adjournment
