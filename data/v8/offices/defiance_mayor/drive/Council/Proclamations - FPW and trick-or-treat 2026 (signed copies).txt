OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO

PROCLAMATION
FIRE PREVENTION WEEK, OCTOBER 4-10, 2026

WHEREAS, the City of Defiance is committed to ensuring the safety and security of all those living in and visiting our city; and

WHEREAS, home fires remain the source of most fire deaths in the United States, and working smoke alarms cut the risk of dying in a reported home fire roughly in half; and

WHEREAS, residents who have planned and practiced a home fire escape plan, with two ways out of every room, are more prepared and more likely to get out safely in the few minutes they may have after the smoke alarm sounds; and

WHEREAS, older adults and young children face a higher risk of injury and death from fire, and the Defiance Fire Department installs smoke alarms free of charge for residents who need them through its home smoke alarm program; and

WHEREAS, the firefighters and Fire Prevention Bureau of the Defiance Fire Department are dedicated to reducing the occurrence of home fires and home fire injuries through prevention and education, including classroom visits, safety talks for older residents, and the Station 1 open house; and

WHEREAS, Fire Prevention Week has been observed nationally each October since 1922 in remembrance of the Great Chicago Fire;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Paul Haverkamp, Mayor of the City of Defiance, Ohio, do hereby proclaim October 4 through 10, 2026 as

FIRE PREVENTION WEEK

in the City of Defiance, and I urge all residents to test their smoke alarms, to replace any alarm that is ten years old or older, to make and practice a home escape plan with their families, and to take part in the many public safety activities and efforts of the Defiance Fire Department during Fire Prevention Week and throughout the year.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand and caused the Seal of the City of Defiance to be affixed this 23rd day of September, 2026.

______________________________
Paul Haverkamp, Mayor

(Read at the regular meeting of Council, September 23, 2026, and presented to Chief Dale Rummel and Lt. Erin Mayes.)

OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO

PROCLAMATION
TRICK-OR-TREAT, 2026

WHEREAS, Halloween trick-or-treating is a long-standing tradition enjoyed by the children and families of Defiance; and

WHEREAS, setting a single evening and set hours for trick-or-treat helps parents plan, helps drivers know when to expect children on the streets, and allows the Fire Department and city crews to be prepared; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor has consulted with the Safety-Service Director, the Fire Chief, and the Police Department, and with neighboring communities in Defiance County, in setting the date and hours;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, Paul Haverkamp, Mayor of the City of Defiance, Ohio, do hereby proclaim that trick-or-treat in the City of Defiance shall be held on

SATURDAY, OCTOBER 31, 2026, FROM 5:30 P.M. TO 7:30 P.M.

and I encourage residents who wish to welcome trick-or-treaters to turn on their porch lights during those hours; I ask that children be accompanied by an adult or older sibling, carry a flashlight or wear reflective clothing, and stay on sidewalks where they are available; and I ask all drivers to slow down and use extra care in residential neighborhoods that evening.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have hereunto set my hand and caused the Seal of the City of Defiance to be affixed this 13th day of October, 2026.

______________________________
Paul Haverkamp, Mayor

(Announced at the regular meeting of Council, October 13, 2026. Copy to H. Brubaker for website, Facebook and the Oct 23 City Update.)
