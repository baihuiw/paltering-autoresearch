RESOLUTION NO. 2026-16

A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING A DONATION OF $2,500 FROM THE DOWNTOWN MERCHANTS' GROUP FOR DOWNTOWN HOLIDAY LIGHTING

Adopted: October 13, 2026
Sponsor: Parks and Recreation Committee (Councilwoman Rita Okuley, chair)

WHEREAS, the Downtown merchants' group, by letter dated September 21, 2026, has offered to donate $2,500 to the City of Defiance for the purchase of holiday lighting for downtown Clinton Street; and

WHEREAS, the Parks and Recreation Department installs and maintains the downtown holiday lighting each year, and many of the existing incandescent light strands are more than fifteen years old; and

WHEREAS, the Parks and Recreation Committee at its meeting of September 30, 2026 recommended that Council accept the donation;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio:

SECTION 1. That the donation of $2,500 from the Downtown merchants' group is hereby accepted with gratitude, to be deposited in the Parks and Recreation Donations account (101.0000.48100) and used for the purchase of LED holiday lighting for downtown Clinton Street.

SECTION 2. That the Clerk of Council is directed to send a copy of this resolution, with the thanks of Council, to the Downtown merchants' group.

SECTION 3. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the adoption of this resolution were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, in compliance with Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 4. This resolution shall take effect at the earliest period allowed by law.

VOTE: Yeas: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Bell, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins. Nays: none. Adopted, 7-0.

RESOLUTION NO. 2026-17

A RESOLUTION CONFIRMING THE MAYOR'S APPOINTMENT OF LINDA HOHENBERGER TO THE PLANNING COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF DEFIANCE FOR THE UNEXPIRED TERM ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2028

Adopted: October 13, 2026

WHEREAS, a vacancy exists on the Planning Commission by reason of the resignation of Paul Haverkamp from the Commission, effective September 15, 2026, upon his taking office as Mayor; and

WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Code provides that citizen members of the Planning Commission are appointed by the Mayor with the confirmation of Council, and that a person appointed to fill a vacancy serves for the remainder of the unexpired term; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor has appointed Linda Hohenberger, a resident and elector of the City, to fill the vacancy;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio:

SECTION 1. That the appointment by the Mayor of Linda Hohenberger to the Planning Commission of the City of Defiance, for the unexpired term ending December 31, 2028, is hereby confirmed.

SECTION 2. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the adoption of this resolution were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, in compliance with Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately upon its adoption.

VOTE: Yeas: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Bell, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins. Nays: none. Adopted, 7-0.
