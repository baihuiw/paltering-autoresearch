ORDINANCE NO. 2026-42

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING ORDINANCE NO. 2025-52, THE ANNUAL APPROPRIATION ORDINANCE FOR THE YEAR ENDING DECEMBER 31, 2026, TO PROVIDE FOR ADDITIONAL APPROPRIATIONS AND TRANSFERS WITHIN THE GENERAL FUND, THE EMS CAPITAL FUND, THE STREET CONSTRUCTION, MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR FUND AND THE WATER FUND, AND DECLARING AN EMERGENCY

Introduced, rules suspended and passed: September 23, 2026
Sponsor: Finance Committee (Councilwoman Nancy Ruhl, chair), at the request of the Finance Director

WHEREAS, the Finance Director has advised Council that certain appropriations within the 2026 annual appropriation ordinance are insufficient for the remainder of the year, and that the City has been awarded a safety intervention grant by the Ohio Bureau of Workers' Compensation that requires appropriation; and

WHEREAS, the Finance Director has certified that the amounts appropriated by this ordinance are available or in the process of collection, as required by Section 5705.40 of the Ohio Revised Code;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio, at least two-thirds of all members elected thereto concurring:

SECTION 1. GENERAL FUND (101).
That the following transfer of appropriation within the General Fund is hereby made:

  From: 101.0130.59900  General Fund - Contingency (Finance)          ($4,000.00)
  To:   101.0120.53400  Council - Printing and Advertising            $4,000.00

SECTION 2. EMS CAPITAL FUND (215).
(a) That the estimated resources of the EMS Capital Fund are hereby increased by $34,206.00 to reflect the receipt of a safety intervention grant from the Ohio Bureau of Workers' Compensation (revenue account 215.0000.44150, State Grants - BWC).
(b) That there is hereby appropriated from the EMS Capital Fund the following additional amount:

  215.0220.55100  EMS Capital - Capital Outlay, Equipment             $68,412.00

for the purchase of two powered ambulance stretchers and associated mounting systems for Medic 2 and Medic 3. Of this amount, $34,206.00 is funded by the grant described in subsection (a) and $34,206.00 from the unappropriated balance of the fund.

SECTION 3. STREET CONSTRUCTION, MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR FUND (201).
That the following transfer of appropriation within the Street Construction, Maintenance and Repair Fund is hereby made:

  From: 201.0510.53200  Street - Contract Services                    ($12,500.00)
  To:   201.0510.52200  Street - Operating Supplies                   $12,500.00

SECTION 4. WATER FUND (501).
That the following transfer of appropriation within the Water Fund is hereby made:

  From: 501.0610.53200  Water Treatment - Contract Services           ($9,500.00)
  To:   501.0610.52200  Water Treatment - Supplies (chemicals)             $9,500.00

SECTION 5. SUMMARY.

  Fund                                     Additional appropriation   Net transfers
  General Fund (101)                       $0.00                      $0.00
  EMS Capital Fund (215)                   $68,412.00                 n/a
  Street Constr., Maint. and Repair (201)  $0.00                      $0.00
  Water Fund (501)                         $0.00                      $0.00
  TOTAL                                    $68,412.00

SECTION 6. The Finance Director is hereby authorized and directed to make the necessary entries in the City's accounting records and to certify a copy of this ordinance to the Defiance County Auditor.

SECTION 7. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the passage of this ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 8. This ordinance is hereby declared to be an emergency measure necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, safety and welfare of the City, and for the further reason that the grant agreement requires the purchase of the stretchers to be committed by October 15, 2026, and that the transferred appropriations are needed for current obligations. Wherefore, this ordinance shall take effect and be in force immediately upon its passage and approval.

VOTE ON SUSPENSION OF THE RULES:
Yeas: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Bell, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins
Nays: none
Motion carried, 7-0.

VOTE ON PASSAGE AND EMERGENCY CLAUSE:
Yeas: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Bell, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins
Nays: none
Ordinance passed as an emergency measure, 7-0.
