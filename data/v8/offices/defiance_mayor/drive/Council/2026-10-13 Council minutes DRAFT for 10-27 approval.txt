CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO
MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING OF CITY COUNCIL
Tuesday, October 13, 2026
Council Chambers, City Building, 631 Perry Street

CALL TO ORDER
President of Council Gary Lehman called the meeting to order at 7:01 p.m.

ROLL CALL
Present: Pfahler, Okuley, Sprow, Bell, Ruhl, Brinkman, Gaskins. Also present: Mayor Haverkamp, Chief of Staff Ostrander, Law Director Cline, Finance Director Marquardt, Safety-Service Director Wiechers, City Engineer Dunlap, Fire Chief Rummel, Parks Director Fenstermaker, Street Superintendent Diehl, Clerk Moll. About 26 members of the public.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES
Motion by Ruhl, second by Gaskins, to approve the minutes of September 23, 2026, as corrected (the Fire Chief's figure of 301 calls was a partial September count through September 22). Carried, 7-0.

RECOGNITION
The mayor recognized Gene Wolfrum of the Street Department, who retires October 30 after 31 years.

COMMUNICATIONS
a. ODOT District 1: Hopkins Street railroad crossing work, October 12-14; detour by way of Second Street and Holgate Avenue.
b. Downtown merchants' group: $2,500 offered toward holiday lighting (Res. 2026-16).

PUBLIC COMMENT
Brian Kellermeyer, 1611 Hopkins Street, asked for more notice of crossing closures. Mr. Wiechers said the city posted notice the day the railroad confirmed its dates and will ask for more lead time.

Marilyn Voigt, 1548 North Clinton Street, asked for a solid fence and shielded lights at the proposed dental office addition (Ord. 2026-44). Councilman Brinkman said both are Planning Commission conditions.

Carl Hoenie, 902 Riverview Drive, asked about leaf collection. Mr. Wiechers said curbside collection begins Monday, October 26, with three passes through the city, each going First, Second, Third and then Fourth Ward. The schedule will be posted on the city website and Facebook by October 19. Rake leaves to the curb, not into the street, and do not mix in brush.

Teresa Vance, 520 Arabella Street, a Harvest on the Riverfront vendor, thanked Parks and asked about electric hookups for vendors next year.

REPORT OF THE MAYOR
- Harvest on the Riverfront, October 10, Kingsbury Park: about 6,000 attended. The Pumpkin Run 5K had 214 runners.
- Senior Services Initiative kickoff: October 14, 11:30 a.m., Council Chambers.
- Ladder 1, out of service since September 24, left for repair in Toledo this morning ($18,400, approved by the Board of Control October 5) and should return in early November. Mutual aid covers aerial calls.
- The Hammond Street Park restroom is expected to be complete by October 23. A reopening is tentatively planned for Monday, October 26 at 1:30 p.m. The portable toilets will stay until then.
- He has signed a proclamation setting trick-or-treat for Saturday, October 31, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m.
- Riverfront tree planting: Saturday, October 17, 9 a.m. to noon, Riverside Park boat ramp lot.
- Fire and EMS levy work session: October 27 at 5:30 p.m. The administration will present a renewal, with no increase in the rate.
- City offices will be closed Wednesday, November 11 for Veterans Day.

REPORTS OF THE ADMINISTRATION
a. Finance. Income tax through September: $10,760,696, up 2.8 percent. 33 capital requests received; recommended list to the Committee of the Whole October 20.
b. Safety-Service. Hydrant flushing finished October 2 (1,412 hydrants). The Board of Control approved calcium chloride, the leaf vacuum No. 2 repair and seasonal leaf laborers October 12. Paving: Jefferson October 14, Hopkins October 15, Washington October 16; Elliott Road and Summit Street the week of October 19.
c. Engineering. WTP Filters 3 and 4 back in service September 29. Harding Street culvert bids open November 24.
d. Fire. September: 392 runs (284 EMS); 3,447 year to date; 90th-percentile response 8:40.
e. Law. Mr. Cline reported that Ordinance No. 2026-35 (nuisance abatement) took effect October 24 [sic; see note]. [Clerk's note for draft: should read "will take effect October 24," 30 days after the mayor's approval on September 24. To confirm with the Law Director.]

REPORTS OF COMMITTEES
a. Committee of the Whole: second budget session October 20, 6:00 p.m.
b. Safety (Gaskins), October 7: recommends Ord. 2026-43.
c. Parks and Recreation (Okuley), September 30: recommends accepting the lighting donation.
d. Streets and Sidewalks (Sprow) meets Thursday, October 15 at 5:30 p.m. in Conference Room B. The agenda includes sidewalk snow clearing on school walking routes, as Councilman Bell requested, the 2027 sidewalk cost-share program, and the 2027 resurfacing candidate list.
e. Planning Commission (Brinkman), September 29: recommended Ord. 2026-44, 4-0.

LEGISLATION
Ord. 2026-39, alley vacation, Jackson Avenue and Agnes Street, Seventh to Eighth (third reading). Motion by Sprow, second by Bell. Passed, 7-0.
Ord. 2026-40, Domersville Road field lease (second reading). Withdrawn by Councilman Pfahler on the Law Director's advice; the Board of Control approved the lease September 28.
Ord. 2026-43, EMS billing contract (second reading). Third reading October 27.
Ord. 2026-44, rezoning 1536 and 1542 North Clinton Street, R-2 to O-1 (first reading). Held for public hearing.
Ord. 2026-45, October supplemental appropriations (Ladder 1 repair; leaf vacuum and seasonal laborers; Riverside Park shelter roof). Rules suspended, 7-0. Passed as an emergency, 7-0.

RESOLUTIONS
Res. 2026-16, accepting the holiday lighting donation. Adopted, 7-0.
Res. 2026-17, confirming Linda Hohenberger to the Planning Commission. Adopted, 7-0.

NEW BUSINESS
Public hearing on Ord. 2026-44 set for Tuesday, November 10, 2026, at 6:45 p.m. Carried, 7-0.

COUNCIL MEMBER COMMENTS
Members congratulated Mr. Wolfrum and thanked Parks and volunteers for Harvest on the Riverfront. Councilman Bell announced the Fourth Ward neighborhood meeting October 15, 6:30 p.m. Councilwoman Gaskins reminded residents of trick-or-treat hours.

ADJOURNMENT
The meeting adjourned at 8:37 p.m.

Gary Lehman, President of Council
Attest: Sandra Moll, Clerk of Council
