ORDINANCE NO. 2026-39

AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PORTION OF THE SIXTEEN-FOOT NORTH-SOUTH ALLEY LYING BETWEEN JACKSON AVENUE AND AGNES STREET, FROM SEVENTH STREET TO EIGHTH STREET, IN THE CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO, UPON PETITION OF ALL ABUTTING OWNERS, AND RESERVING A PERMANENT UTILITY EASEMENT

Introduced: September 8, 2026 (first reading)
Second reading: September 23, 2026
Third reading and passage: October 13, 2026
Planning Commission recommendation: July 28, 2026 (approve, 5-0)

WHEREAS, a petition dated June 30, 2026 has been filed with the Clerk of Council requesting the vacation of that portion of the sixteen-foot alley described in Section 1 of this ordinance, and the petition is signed by the owners of all four parcels abutting that portion of the alley, namely:

  Gerald A. and Ruth M. Wilhelm, 704 Jackson Avenue (Parcel No. D01-0412-0073)
  Karen L. Bostelman, 718 Jackson Avenue (Parcel No. D01-0412-0074)
  Matthew and Erin Schoenauer, 705 Agnes Street (Parcel No. D01-0412-0081)
  Second Chance Properties LLC, by Phillip Rau, member, 719 Agnes Street (Parcel No. D01-0412-0082); and

WHEREAS, because all owners of abutting property have signed the petition, the notice provisions of Section 723.07 of the Ohio Revised Code do not apply; and

WHEREAS, the alley segment is unimproved, has not been maintained by the City for at least twenty years, does not provide the only access to any parcel, and is not needed for public travel; and

WHEREAS, the City Engineer has reviewed the petition and reports that no City water main, sanitary sewer or storm sewer is located within the alley segment, and that overhead electric distribution and telecommunications lines are located within it; and

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission at its meeting of July 28, 2026 recommended that the vacation be granted subject to the reservation of a utility easement; and

WHEREAS, Council finds that there is good cause for the vacation, that it will not be detrimental to the general interest, and that it should be made;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio:

SECTION 1. That the following described portion of public alley in the City of Defiance, Defiance County, Ohio, is hereby vacated:

Being all of the sixteen (16) foot wide north-south alley lying between Lots 412 through 415 of the Original Plat of the Town of Defiance, Second Addition (being the parcels fronting on Jackson Avenue) and Lots 420 through 423 of said addition (being the parcels fronting on Agnes Street), bounded on the south by the north right-of-way line of Seventh Street and on the north by the south right-of-way line of Eighth Street, containing approximately 292 linear feet and 4,672 square feet (0.107 acre), more or less, as shown on the plat prepared by the Office of the City Engineer dated August 19, 2026, attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated herein.

SECTION 2. Title to the vacated alley shall vest in the owners of the abutting lots as provided in Section 723.08 of the Ohio Revised Code, each to the centerline of the vacated alley.

SECTION 3. The City hereby reserves to itself and to all public utilities now or hereafter authorized to operate in the City a permanent easement over, under and across the entire sixteen-foot width of the vacated alley for the construction, operation, maintenance, repair, replacement and removal of utility lines and facilities, including the right of access for those purposes. No building, structure, fence (other than a removable fence with a gate of at least twelve feet in width at each end), or permanent planting shall be placed within the easement without the written consent of the City Engineer. Owners place any improvements within the easement at their own risk and the City and utilities shall not be responsible for restoring them.

SECTION 4. Upon the effective date of this ordinance, the abutting owners shall be responsible for maintenance of the vacated area, including mowing and the control of weeds under Chapter 1355 of the Codified Ordinances.

SECTION 5. The petitioners shall pay the costs of preparing the plat and legal description ($275.00) and the County Recorder's recording fees. The Clerk of Council shall, upon receipt of those costs, cause a certified copy of this ordinance and Exhibit A to be filed with the Defiance County Recorder and the Defiance County Auditor, and the City Engineer shall revise the City's official maps accordingly.

SECTION 6. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the passage of this ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 7. This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after the earliest period allowed by law.
