ORDINANCE NO. 2026-35

AN ORDINANCE REPEALING EXISTING CHAPTER 1355 AND ENACTING NEW CHAPTER 1355 OF THE CODIFIED ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO, "NUISANCE ABATEMENT," TO UPDATE NOTICE, ABATEMENT AND COST-RECOVERY PROCEDURES FOR WEEDS, RUBBISH AND INOPERABLE VEHICLES

Introduced: August 25, 2026 (first reading)
Second reading: September 8, 2026
Third reading and passage: September 23, 2026
Sponsors: Safety Committee; Law Director

WHEREAS, Chapter 1355 of the Codified Ordinances was last amended in 2009 and requires a separate written notice each time a property owner allows weeds or grass to exceed the permitted height, which results in repeated notices to the same owners within a single growing season and delays abatement; and

WHEREAS, the administrative charge of $50 per abatement established in 2009 no longer covers the City's cost of inspection, notice, photographs, contractor coordination and billing; and

WHEREAS, Section 731.51 et seq. of the Ohio Revised Code authorizes the City to abate noxious weeds and litter and to certify unpaid costs to the County Auditor for collection as a lien on the property; and

WHEREAS, the Safety Committee reviewed the proposed revisions at its meeting of June 17, 2026 and recommended them to Council;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio, a majority of all members elected thereto concurring:

SECTION 1. That existing Chapter 1355, "Nuisance Abatement," of the Codified Ordinances of the City of Defiance is hereby repealed.

SECTION 2. That new Chapter 1355, "Nuisance Abatement," is hereby enacted to read as follows:

CHAPTER 1355
NUISANCE ABATEMENT

1355.01 DEFINITIONS.
As used in this chapter:
(a) "Code Official" means the Safety-Service Director or the Director's designee, including any code enforcement officer of the City.
(b) "Owner" means the person or persons shown as owner of a parcel on the records of the Defiance County Auditor, and includes any agent, lessee or person in control of the premises.
(c) "Weeds" means grass, weeds and other vegetation, excluding cultivated flowers, gardens, shrubs and trees, and excluding areas designated as native plantings under Section 1355.03(c).
(d) "Rubbish" means discarded materials of any kind, including but not limited to garbage not properly containerized, paper, cartons, furniture, appliances, tires, building materials, yard waste not properly stored or set out for collection, and scrap.
(e) "Inoperable vehicle" means any motor vehicle that is not currently licensed, or that is wrecked, dismantled, partially dismantled or incapable of being driven under its own power, and that is parked or stored outside of a completely enclosed building.

1355.02 NUISANCES PROHIBITED.
No owner of any lot or land within the City shall permit or maintain on such lot or land:
(a) Weeds exceeding eight inches in height;
(b) Rubbish accumulated in such a manner as to be visible from a public street or adjoining property, or to harbor rodents or insects;
(c) An inoperable vehicle for more than fourteen days, except as permitted under Section 1355.08.

1355.03 WEEDS; EXCEPTIONS.
(a) Section 1355.02(a) does not apply to land used for agricultural purposes in an area zoned for agricultural use, or to lots of more than three acres in area beyond fifty feet of any public street or occupied dwelling.
(b) Areas within twenty feet of a stream bank or drainage ditch may be maintained at a height of up to eighteen inches.
(c) An owner may establish a native planting or pollinator garden of not more than 25 percent of the front yard, or any portion of a side or rear yard, provided the area is clearly defined by edging, a border or a sign, is set back at least five feet from any sidewalk and three feet from any lot line, and is free of noxious weeds as defined by the Ohio Department of Agriculture.

1355.04 NOTICE.
(a) Upon finding a violation of Section 1355.02, the Code Official shall serve written notice on the owner. The notice shall describe the violation, identify the parcel, state the action required to correct it, and state that if the violation is not corrected within the time stated the City will abate it and assess the costs against the owner and the property.
(b) The notice shall be served by first class mail to the owner's address on the County Auditor's records. In addition, the Code Official may post the notice on the premises. Service is complete on the date of mailing.
(c) Time to correct: five days from service for weeds; seven days from service for rubbish; fourteen days from service for inoperable vehicles.
(d) Seasonal notice. A notice of a violation of Section 1355.02(a) shall remain in effect for the remainder of the calendar year. If the weeds on the parcel again exceed the permitted height during the same calendar year, the City may abate the violation without further notice.
(e) Where the owner's address is unknown or mail is returned undeliverable, posting on the premises and publication once on the City's website shall constitute notice.

1355.05 ABATEMENT BY CITY.
If the owner fails to correct a violation within the time stated in the notice, the Code Official may cause the violation to be abated by City forces or by a contractor engaged by the City. Abatement of an inoperable vehicle shall be by towing and disposition in accordance with Chapter 303 of these Codified Ordinances and Sections 4513.60 to 4513.65 of the Ohio Revised Code.

1355.06 COSTS; ADMINISTRATIVE FEE; COLLECTION.
(a) The owner shall be liable for the actual cost of abatement plus an administrative fee of $150 for each abatement.
(b) The Finance Director shall invoice the owner within thirty days after abatement. If the invoice is not paid within thirty days, the Finance Director shall send a second notice.
(c) Charges remaining unpaid after the second notice shall be certified by resolution of Council to the County Auditor for placement on the tax duplicate as a lien on the property, to be collected as other taxes.
(d) The remedies in this section are in addition to any other remedy available by law, including civil action.

1355.07 APPEALS.
An owner may appeal a notice issued under Section 1355.04 by filing a written appeal with the Safety-Service Director within the time allowed to correct the violation. The Director shall decide the appeal within ten days. Filing an appeal stays abatement except where the Director finds that the condition presents an immediate hazard to health or safety.

1355.08 INOPERABLE VEHICLES; EXCEPTIONS.
Section 1355.02(c) does not apply to:
(a) One inoperable vehicle stored in a rear yard, fully covered by a fitted vehicle cover, and not visible from the street;
(b) Vehicles kept at a lawfully operating motor vehicle repair facility, salvage yard or dealership in a zoning district that permits that use;
(c) Collector vehicles registered as historical vehicles under the Ohio Revised Code and stored in compliance with Section 4513.65.

1355.99 PENALTY.
Whoever violates any provision of this chapter is guilty of a minor misdemeanor for a first offense. A second offense within twelve months is a misdemeanor of the fourth degree. Each day a violation continues after the time allowed to correct it is a separate offense. Prosecution under this section does not limit the City's right to abate and collect costs.

SECTION 3. The administrative fee established in Section 1355.06(a) shall apply only to abatements ordered after the effective date of this ordinance. Charges for abatements ordered before the effective date shall be governed by former Chapter 1355.

SECTION 4. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the passage of this ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 5. This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after the earliest period allowed by law.
