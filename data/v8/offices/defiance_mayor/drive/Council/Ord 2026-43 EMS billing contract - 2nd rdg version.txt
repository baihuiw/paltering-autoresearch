ORDINANCE NO. 2026-43

AN ORDINANCE AUTHORIZING THE BOARD OF CONTROL TO ENTER INTO A CONTRACT WITH AMERIDALE REVENUE SERVICES, LLC FOR EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES BILLING AND COLLECTION SERVICES FOR THE PERIOD JANUARY 1, 2027 THROUGH DECEMBER 31, 2029

Introduced: September 23, 2026 (first reading)
Second reading: October 13, 2026
Third reading: scheduled October 27, 2026
Sponsor: Safety Committee (Councilwoman Theresa Gaskins, chair)

[Version for second reading. No changes from the first-reading text except that the vendor name has been inserted in the title and Section 1 following the Safety Committee's recommendation of October 7, 2026. SM]

WHEREAS, the City of Defiance, through its Division of Fire, provides emergency medical services and ambulance transportation, and by Section 155.04 of the Codified Ordinances has established fees for those services; and

WHEREAS, the City's current agreement for the billing and collection of emergency medical services fees expires December 31, 2026; and

WHEREAS, the Fire Chief issued a request for proposals on July 13, 2026, four proposals were received on August 14, 2026, and a review team consisting of the Fire Chief, the Finance Director and the EMS Coordinator has recommended Ameridale Revenue Services, LLC, of Findlay, Ohio, as the proposer offering the best value to the City; and

WHEREAS, the recommended proposal provides for compensation of 6.75 percent of net collections, compared with 8.50 percent under the City's current agreement; and

WHEREAS, because the contract term exceeds one year and the estimated total compensation over the term exceeds $50,000, the authorization of Council is required;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED by the Council of the City of Defiance, Ohio, a majority of all members elected thereto concurring:

SECTION 1. That the Board of Control is hereby authorized to enter into a contract with Ameridale Revenue Services, LLC, Findlay, Ohio, for the billing and collection of emergency medical services fees for runs occurring on and after January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2029, in substantially the form on file with the Clerk of Council, at a fee of 6.75 percent of net collections. "Net collections" means amounts actually received by the City for emergency medical services, less refunds.

SECTION 2. The contract shall include, at a minimum, the following terms:

(a) The contractor shall bill Medicare, Medicaid, and private and commercial insurers in accordance with applicable federal and state law and the rules of each payer.

(b) For patients who reside in the City of Defiance, the contractor shall accept payment from the patient's insurer as payment in full and shall not bill the resident for any remaining balance, consistent with the policy established by Council in Section 155.04(d) of the Codified Ordinances. The contractor shall not report any city resident to a credit reporting agency.

(c) For non-resident patients, the contractor may bill the patient for any balance after insurance, shall offer payment plans and hardship review, and shall not refer any account to an outside collection agency without the written approval of the Finance Director.

(d) The contractor shall comply with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act and shall execute the City's business associate agreement.

(e) All funds shall be deposited directly into a City account. The contractor shall not handle or hold City funds.

(f) The contractor shall provide monthly reports to the Fire Chief and the Finance Director, including runs billed, amounts collected by payer type, denials and their reasons, and aging of receivables.

(g) The contractor shall maintain professional liability and cyber liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 each.

(h) Either party may terminate the contract without cause on 120 days' written notice.

SECTION 3. The Fire Chief is designated as the City's contract administrator. Compensation under the contract shall be paid from the Fire/EMS Levy Fund (210), EMS billing service line, to which EMS billing collections are deposited.

SECTION 4. It is found and determined that all formal actions of this Council concerning and relating to the passage of this ordinance were adopted in an open meeting of this Council, and that all deliberations of this Council and of any of its committees that resulted in such formal action were in meetings open to the public, in compliance with all legal requirements including Section 121.22 of the Ohio Revised Code.

SECTION 5. This ordinance shall take effect and be in force from and after the earliest period allowed by law.
