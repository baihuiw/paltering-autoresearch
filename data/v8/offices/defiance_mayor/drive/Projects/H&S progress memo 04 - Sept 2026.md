### 1. Summary

Field survey is complete and the base mapping has been delivered to the City. Preliminary (60%) plans are in progress and remain on schedule for submittal on October 16, 2026. Supporting exhibits for the City's funding application were delivered to the City Engineer on August 28, 2026.

### 2. Scope and limits

Per the professional services agreement approved by the Board of Control on March 16, 2026 (lump sum $46,850), the scope covers survey, curb ramp and sidewalk assessment, preliminary through final plans, specifications and estimate, and bidding assistance. Construction-phase services are by separate amendment if requested.

Project limits: East Second Street, Clinton Street to Wayne Avenue; Wayne Avenue, East Second Street to East Fifth Street; connecting segments of East Third and East Fourth Streets between Wayne Avenue and Jefferson Avenue; and all curb ramps at intersections within the limits.

### 3. Work completed this period

**3.1 Survey.** Field crews completed the topographic survey on August 19, back-of-walk to back-of-walk across the full limits, including ramps, driveway aprons, tree lawn trees over 6 inches, utility appurtenances and visible property pins. Base mapping (CAD and PDF) was transmitted on August 24.

**3.2 Condition assessment.** We walked every block within the limits with the City's curb ramp inventory form:

- Approximately 5,400 linear feet of sidewalk is recommended for replacement, mostly for heaving at tree roots, vertical displacement over 1/2 inch at joints, spalling and settlement at driveway aprons.
- 32 curb ramps are recommended for reconstruction. None meet current PROWAG/ADA criteria: running slopes over 8.3%, missing or non-compliant detectable warnings, insufficient landings, or no ramp at all (five corners).
- 11 additional ramps were reconstructed in 2019-2021 and meet criteria. No work is proposed there beyond minor joint sealing.

Results have been entered into the City's curb ramp inventory spreadsheet (Ward 3 rows).

**3.3 Utility coordination.** Plan notification letters went to the utilities on August 21. Two poles at the NE and SW corners of East Second Street and Wayne Avenue may conflict with compliant ramp landings. We are evaluating shifted ramp alignments before asking for relocation, which typically takes 6-9 months.

**3.4 Trees.** Fourteen tree lawn trees are within the work footprint. Nine are ash already on the Parks Department removal list; we recommend the City Arborist review the other five (two maple, three linden).

**3.5 Funding application support.** At the City's request we delivered a location map, typical sections, the cost estimate (Table 2), a useful life statement (25 years, concrete) and photographs on August 28. We understand the application itself, including the required local legislation and certifications, is prepared and submitted by the City.
