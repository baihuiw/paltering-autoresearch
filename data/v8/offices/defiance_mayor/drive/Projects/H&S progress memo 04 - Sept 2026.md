### 3. Work completed this period

**3.1 Survey.** Field crews completed the topographic survey on August 19. Coverage includes back-of-walk to back-of-walk across the full limits, all curb ramps, driveway aprons, tree lawn trees over 6 inches, utility appurtenances (water valves, hydrants, manholes, telephone and cable pedestals, light poles) and visible property pins. Right-of-way lines were compiled from county tax maps and recorded plats and checked against found pins. Base mapping (CAD and PDF) was transmitted on August 24.

**3.2 Condition assessment.** We walked every block within the limits with the City's curb ramp inventory form. Findings:

- Approximately 5,400 linear feet of sidewalk is recommended for replacement. Defects are mostly heaving at tree roots, vertical displacement over 1/2 inch at joints, spalling and settlement at driveway aprons.
- 32 curb ramps are recommended for reconstruction. None of the 32 meet current PROWAG/ADA criteria: running slopes over 8.3%, missing or non-compliant detectable warning surfaces, insufficient landing area, or no ramp at all (five corners).
- 11 additional ramps within the limits were reconstructed in 2019-2021 and meet criteria. No work is proposed at those locations beyond minor joint sealing.

Assessment results have been entered into the City's curb ramp inventory spreadsheet (Ward 3 rows) for the City's records.

**3.3 Utility coordination.** First-round plan notification letters went to the telephone, cable, gas and electric utilities on August 21. We have received responses from the gas and electric utilities. Two utility poles at the NE and SW corners of East Second Street and Wayne Avenue may conflict with compliant ramp landings. We are evaluating shifted ramp alignments before asking for a pole relocation, since relocations typically take 6-9 months.

**3.4 Trees.** Fourteen tree lawn trees are within the proposed work footprint. Nine are ash and were already on the Parks Department removal list. We recommend the remaining five (two maple, three linden) be evaluated by the City Arborist. Where possible we will narrow the walk locally or use a meander to save healthy trees.

**3.5 Funding application support.** At the City's request we prepared the following for the City's state funding application:

- Project location map
- Typical sections (sidewalk replacement, standard perpendicular ramp, blended transition)
- Engineer's opinion of probable cost (Table 2)
- Useful life statement (sidewalk and ramps: 25 years, concrete)
- Photographs of representative existing conditions

These were delivered on August 28. We understand the application itself, including the required local legislation and certifications, is prepared and submitted by the City.

### 6. Issues and decisions needed from the City

1. **Pole conflicts at E. Second / Wayne.** Please confirm the City prefers shifted ramp alignments over relocation requests. We will show both options in the 60% plans.
2. **Trees.** Arborist review of five non-ash trees (list attached to transmittal email).

Common questions were construction timing, whether property owners will be assessed (our understanding is no, since this project is City-funded), and driveway access during construction. We suggest the City provide talking points before the 60% public meeting, if one is planned.
