The City is responsible for routine inspection and maintenance of the bridges on City streets. Annual inspections are performed under the state's bridge inspection program and entered into the state bridge management system. This summary covers the City-owned structures inspected this year. Ratings use the 0-9 condition scale (9 excellent, 4 poor, 0 failed). The general appraisal (GA) is the lowest of the deck, superstructure and substructure ratings (or the culvert rating for culverts).

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PONTIAC STREET BRIDGE OVER PONTIAC RUN
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Structure type: three-span continuous reinforced concrete slab on concrete piers and stub abutments
Built: 1972; deck overlay 1996
Length: 118 ft; width curb to curb 36 ft; sidewalks both sides
Average daily traffic: approx. 3,800
Inspected: August 19, 2026 (routine annual)

Ratings:
                               2024      2025      2026
  Deck                          7         6         6
  Superstructure                7         7         7
  Substructure                  6         6         6
  Channel                       7         7         7
  Approaches                    7         7         6
  General appraisal             6         6         6

Findings:
- Deck: Map cracking over most of the surface, with longitudinal cracks over the piers. Two small spalls (each less than 1 sq ft) near the east joint. No exposed reinforcing steel. Chloride testing is not required at this rating. Recommendation: clean and seal the deck with a penetrating sealer; patch the two spalls.
- Joints: Compression seal at the east abutment torn for about 6 feet; debris in the joint. Recommendation: replace the seal.
- Railings: Concrete parapets in good condition. Chain link fence on top of parapet on the downstream side has a loose post. Street Department to tighten.
- Substructure: Light scaling at the waterline on Pier 1. Minor efflorescence under the deck at the west abutment. No undermining.
- Channel: Small gravel bar forming on the inside of the bend upstream of Pier 2. Not affecting flow. Monitor.
- Approaches: West approach slab has settled about 3/4 inch at the joint, producing a bump. Recommendation: mill and patch the transition. (Previously rated 7; lowered to 6 for the bump.)
- Load rating: adequate for legal loads. No posting required.

Recommended work and cost:
  Deck cleaning and penetrating sealer ................... $14,500
  Spall patching (2 locations) ........................... $ 1,800
  East joint seal replacement ............................ $ 4,200
  Approach transition mill and patch ..................... $ 1,500
                                                           -------
                                                           $22,000

This is the $22,000 carried in the CIP as ST-27-06 for 2027. Deck sealing should be done in warm, dry weather (June-August). Ron would like to add the approach patch to the 2026 resurfacing contract as an extra if the contractor will hold unit prices; I've asked them. If not, the Street Department will do it in the spring.
