General: Plans are in good shape for 60 percent. The alignment and the decision to replace rather than slip-line are fine. My comments are mostly about the outlet end, constructability at low water, and making sure the estimate holds. Please respond to each item in writing with the 90 percent submittal.

The estimate at 60 percent is **$286,000**, up from **$262,000** at 30 percent. The increase is almost all in the riprap apron (item 14) and the cofferdam allowance (item 9). See comments 6 and 9.

## Permitting

The work below ordinary high water needs a Nationwide Permit from the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. Please prepare the pre-construction notification for my signature by **October 23** so it can go in before the holidays. We will also need the Ohio EPA water quality certification that goes with it. Please confirm in writing which nationwide permit you intend to use and whether any time-of-year restriction for fish spawning applies beyond the low-water window we are already planning around.
