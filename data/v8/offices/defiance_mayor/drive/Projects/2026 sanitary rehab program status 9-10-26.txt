1. BACKGROUND

The City's sanitary sewer system has about 96 miles of gravity sewer, of which roughly 31 miles is vitrified clay pipe installed before 1970. Since 2019 the City has lined a share of the older clay pipe each year through an annual rehabilitation program, funded from the Sanitary Sewer Capital Improvement Fund. Segments are selected from the CCTV inspection program, which televises about one quarter of the system each year on a ward rotation, and are prioritized by structural condition, infiltration, and criticality.

The program is bid each year as a single unit-price contract so that several segments can share mobilization and bypass costs.

Door hangers went out 48 hours ahead for every segment. One resident on Summit Street reported a backup on July 22; the contractor's crew found the lateral had been reinstated and the blockage was in the private lateral. No claim was made.

The Fourth Ward interceptor segments (4-5 and 4-7) are the largest-diameter pipe the program has lined. They will need a contractor with 21-inch capability and a larger bypass setup. Last year's bidders all have that capability. If both segments are included, one bypass setup at MH 4-31 can serve both, which should reduce cost.

Preliminary budget for the 2027 program: $850,000 to $1,000,000, depending on whether both interceptor segments are included. This will be refined after the fall CCTV and goes into the capital plan request due to Finance October 9.
