1. PURPOSE

Under the City's small MS4 general permit, each mapped outfall is to be screened in dry weather at least once during the permit term, and priority outfalls annually. This report covers the fall 2026 round. Dry weather means at least 72 hours with less than 0.1 inch of rain. All screening this round was done between September 8 and September 30. Rain at the WPCF gauge during that window was 0.06 inch on September 10 and 1.3 inches on September 18; no screening was done September 18 through September 21.

3. FIELD METHOD

At each outfall the crew records the date, time since last rain, flow (none, trickle, moderate, substantial), color, odor, turbidity, floatables, and any deposits or staining. Where there is flow, field tests are run for ammonia, pH, temperature, detergents (surfactants) and total chlorine using the test kits in the stormwater truck. Results above the screening thresholds in the City's IDDE plan go to the Utilities lab for E. coli and a follow-up upstream walk.

Screening thresholds (IDDE plan, rev. 2024):
  Ammonia ............... above 0.5 mg/L
  Surfactants ........... above 0.25 mg/L
  Total chlorine ........ above 0.1 mg/L
  pH .................... below 6.0 or above 9.0
  E. coli ............... above 1,000 cfu/100 mL (lab)

4. OUTFALLS NEEDING FOLLOW-UP

4.1 Outfall OF-37 (river bank below Front Street, 18-inch RCP)
  Sept 9: moderate flow, gray tint, slight sewage odor. Ammonia 1.2 mg/L, surfactants 0.4 mg/L.
  Sept 10: lab E. coli 4,100 cfu/100 mL.
  Follow-up: crew walked the storm line upstream on Sept 14 and dye-tested four buildings on the 300 block. No dye observed at the outfall from three of them. The fourth (a vacant commercial building) could not be entered. Building and Zoning has contacted the owner. Re-sample of OF-37 on Sept 29 showed flow down to a trickle, ammonia 0.3 mg/L, surfactants below detection. Flow may have been from a floor drain that was used during cleanout of the vacant building in early September. We will dye-test the building once access is arranged and re-screen OF-37 in November.

4.2 Outfall OF-61 (Hubbard ditch, 24-inch CMP north of Ralston Ave)
  Sept 15: trickle, clear, total chlorine 0.4 mg/L. pH 7.6.
  Likely source: chlorinated water. Utilities confirmed a water service leak at a curb stop on Spruce St that was repaired Sept 17. Re-screen Sept 28: no flow. Closed.

4.3 Outfall OF-88 (river bank, Pontiac Park, 12-inch PVC)
  Sept 23: trickle, clear, surfactants 0.3 mg/L, no odor.
  Source: likely car washing at the Pontiac Park shelter lot (charity car wash Sept 19, permitted by Parks). Re-screen Sept 30: no flow. Parks has been asked to add the "wash water to sanitary" condition to the shelter rental form for car washes. Closed.
