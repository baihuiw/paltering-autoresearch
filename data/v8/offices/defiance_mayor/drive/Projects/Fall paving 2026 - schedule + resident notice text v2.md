## 1. Summary

The 2026 resurfacing program covers about 3.1 miles on six streets, paid from the street levy. The contract with Weidenhamer Paving Co. is $1,184,600 against a budget of $1,210,000. The preconstruction meeting was held September 22 in Conference Room B. Milling starts on Holgate Avenue at 7:00 a.m. Monday, October 5.

All dates below are weather permitting. The contractor does not pave in rain or when the air temperature is below 40 degrees and falling. If we lose a day, everything after it slides one working day. Please do not promise residents a specific day beyond "this week" or "next week."

## 3. What residents should expect

- **Milled streets stay open** to local traffic. The milled surface is rough and the castings (manholes, water valves) will stick up an inch or two. They are marked with cones or ramped with cold patch. Drive slowly.
- **The week between milling and paving** is the part people call about. It is normal. The contractor mills a whole group of streets, then the paving crew follows.
- **Paving day:** no parking on the street from 7:00 a.m. until the crew leaves. Driveways will be blocked for a short time while the paver passes, usually 15 to 30 minutes. Fresh asphalt can be driven on once the roller crew has finished and the flaggers have opened the lane, but avoid sharp turns on it for a day or two.
- **Trash day:** Waste hauler has the schedule. Put carts out as usual; if the street is being paved that day, put carts at the end of the driveway, not in the street.
- **Mail and deliveries** continue. The post office has the schedule.
- **Lawn damage** at driveway aprons or the curb line gets topsoil and seed when the contractor does restoration, typically within two weeks of paving.
- **Pavement markings** go down within about two weeks of paving. Temporary tabs are placed the day of paving.
