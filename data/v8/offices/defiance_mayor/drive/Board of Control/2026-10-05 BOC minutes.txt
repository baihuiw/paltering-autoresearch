4. FIRE DEPARTMENT - LADDER 1 REPAIR AND ANNUAL AERIAL CERTIFICATION

Chief Rummel reported that Ladder 1 has been out of service since September 24. The manufacturer's road technician found on September 29 that the hydraulic leak is at the rod seal of the main lift cylinder, with scoring on the rod. The cylinder must be rebuilt and load tested at the manufacturer's authorized service center in Toledo before the aerial can be recertified.

The quote is $18,400.00: $11,950.00 for the cylinder rebuild, $4,850.00 for the annual aerial certification (due in November in any case) and $1,600.00 for transport. The unit would leave Tuesday, October 13, and should be back in service in early November. Meanwhile the department relies on mutual aid for an aerial at working structure fires, and Engine 1 carries Ladder 1's ground ladders.

Wiechers said the amount is below the $25,000 Board threshold but above the Chief's authority, and that only the authorized service center can do the work. Cline agreed it is a proper sole-source purchase. Marquardt said the Fire/EMS Levy Fund apparatus line is short, but the fund has an unappropriated balance; she will include $18,400.00 in the appropriation ordinance for Council's October 13 meeting. Asked the unit's age, Chief Rummel said Ladder 1 is 19 years old.

Motion by Wiechers, second by Marquardt, to authorize the repair and certification in an amount not to exceed $18,400.00 from the Fire/EMS Levy Fund, subject to Council's appropriation. Ayes: Haverkamp, Cline, Marquardt, Wiechers. Nays: none. Motion carried.

5. STREET DEPARTMENT - PLOW CUTTING EDGES; FALL PAVING UPDATE

Superintendent Diehl requested approval to purchase plow cutting edges, curb guards and carbide inserts for the 2026-2027 season. Quotes: Fruchey Truck Equipment $14,280.00; second supplier $15,066.50; third supplier $15,930.00. Street Construction, Maintenance and Repair Fund.

Motion by Cline, second by Wiechers, to authorize the purchase from Fruchey Truck Equipment for $14,280.00. Ayes: Haverkamp, Cline, Marquardt, Wiechers. Nays: none. Motion carried.

6. ENGINEERING - THIRD STREET STORM OUTFALL, GEOTECHNICAL BORINGS

City Engineer Dunlap presented a proposal from Ross & Feltz Testing Lab for four soil borings, lab testing and a geotechnical report for the Third Street storm outfall, needed to finish the headwall design before bidding in January 2027. One other firm quoted $15,240.00; a third declined. Ross & Feltz: $12,600.00. The outfall, estimated at $286,000, also needs a Corps of Engineers Nationwide Permit. Stormwater Fund.

Motion by Marquardt, second by Wiechers, to approve the proposal for $12,600.00. Ayes: Haverkamp, Cline, Marquardt, Wiechers. Nays: none. Motion carried.
