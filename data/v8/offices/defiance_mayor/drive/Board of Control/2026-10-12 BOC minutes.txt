CITY OF DEFIANCE
BOARD OF CONTROL
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
Monday, October 12, 2026, 10:00 a.m.
Mayor's Conference Room, City Building, 631 Perry Street

MEMBERS PRESENT: Paul Haverkamp, Mayor (Chair); Steven Cline, Law Director; Angela Marquardt, Finance Director; Tom Wiechers, Safety-Service Director

ALSO PRESENT: Rachel Dunlap, City Engineer; Kevin Shaffer, Utilities Superintendent; Donna Pruett, Utility Billing Supervisor; Craig Fenstermaker, Parks and Recreation Director; Ron Diehl, Street Superintendent; Janelle Rohrs, Deputy Clerk (recording)

1. CALL TO ORDER

Mayor Haverkamp called the meeting to order at 10:02 a.m. A quorum was declared.

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES

Motion by Cline, second by Wiechers, to approve the minutes of the October 5, 2026 regular meeting as distributed. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

3. STREET DEPARTMENT - LIQUID CALCIUM CHLORIDE, 2026-2027 WINTER SEASON

Street Superintendent Diehl presented ODOT cooperative pricing of $1.06 per gallon delivered (last season $1.11). About 4,500 gallons are carried over in the brine tanks.

Motion by Wiechers, second by Marquardt, to purchase up to 26,000 gallons at $1.06 per gallon, not to exceed $27,560, from the Street Construction, Maintenance and Repair Fund. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

4. STREET DEPARTMENT - FALL LEAF COLLECTION

Diehl reported that leaf vacuum No. 2 needs a new impeller housing and fan bearings before collection starts October 26 (quote $9,860), and requested up to four seasonal laborers.

Motion by Wiechers, second by Cline, to approve repairs to leaf vacuum No. 2 not to exceed $9,860 and to authorize up to four seasonal laborers. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

5. WATER TREATMENT PLANT - FILTER REHABILITATION AND CLEARWELL, PAY APPLICATION NO. 5

City Engineer Dunlap presented Pay Application No. 5 from Hollister & Graf Mechanical, Inc. for work through September 30, 2026: gross $214,600.00, less 5 percent retainage of $10,730.00, net $203,870.00. The project is about 58 percent complete and on schedule for substantial completion in May 2027.

Motion by Marquardt, second by Wiechers, to approve Pay Application No. 5 as presented. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

6. WATER TREATMENT PLANT - TREATMENT CHEMICALS, ANNUAL SUPPLY

Utilities Superintendent Shaffer reported on bids opened September 30 for the November 2026 through October 2027 supply and recommended the low bidder in each category.

Motion by Wiechers, second by Marquardt, to award sodium hypochlorite to Northwest Ohio Chemical Supply at $1.84 per gallon (estimated $84,640) and ferric chloride to Lakeshore Water Products at $612.00 per dry ton (estimated $128,520), from the Water Fund. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

7. WASHINGTON AVENUE WATERLINE REPLACEMENT - CHANGE ORDER NO. 2

Dunlap presented Change Order No. 2 for capping an abandoned 4-inch cast iron line found near Third Street. Add $8,420.00; revised contract $612,935.00; no change in contract time.

Motion by Cline, second by Wiechers, to approve Change Order No. 2. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

8. UTILITY BILLING - READ MULTIPLIER, PHASE 2 ACCOUNTS

Shaffer and Utility Billing Supervisor Pruett reported that the October 1, 2026 quarterly billing for the approximately 1,140 phase 2 meter accounts (routes 14 through 21, Walnut Street through Woodlawn Avenue) was calculated with a read multiplier of 2 instead of 1, so billed consumption was approximately twice actual use. The billing system classes for the phase 2 meters (HRE58 and HRE34) were set up from the phase 1 class; the multiplier was changed to 1 effective with reads taken on and after October 9, 2026.

Marquardt reported that the estimated total of adjustments for the phase 2 accounts, if processed for all affected accounts, is approximately $118,000 across the Water and Sewer Funds. She reviewed the Water Fund cash position and the debt service coverage requirement on the Ohio Water Development Authority loan for the treatment plant project.

Motion by Marquardt, second by Wiechers, that the corrected multiplier apply to all billings going forward and that adjustments for affected accounts be processed upon customer request through the Utility Billing Office. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

9. UTILITY BILLING SYSTEM - 2027 SOFTWARE SUPPORT RENEWAL

Pruett presented the 2027 support and maintenance renewal for the utility billing and customer information system: $41,280, up 3.5 percent from 2026.

Motion by Marquardt, second by Cline, to approve the renewal, split equally between the Water and Sewer Funds. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

10. LEAD SERVICE LINE INVENTORY - AMENDMENT NO. 1

Dunlap presented Amendment No. 1, $26,400, adding field verification of up to 220 service lines classified as "unknown."

Motion by Wiechers, second by Marquardt, to approve Amendment No. 1 from the Water Capital Improvement Fund. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

11. PARKS - RIVERSIDE PARK SHELTER HOUSE ROOF

Parks and Recreation Director Fenstermaker presented quotes to replace the storm-damaged shelter house roof: Kuhlman Roofing $23,800; Bauer & Sons Exteriors $25,150; Northwest Roofing Co. $27,400. Insurance deductible $5,000; completion before November 15.

Motion by Cline, second by Wiechers, to award the roof replacement to Kuhlman Roofing, $23,800. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

12. PURCHASE ORDERS AND THEN-AND-NOW CERTIFICATES

Marquardt presented 14 purchase orders between $5,000 and $50,000 ($187,312.66) and two then-and-now certificates (tires, $6,184.20; hydrant parts, $5,467.80).

Motion by Wiechers, second by Cline, to approve the list and certificates as presented. Ayes: all four members. Motion carried.

13. OTHER BUSINESS

City offices will be closed Wednesday, November 11, for Veterans Day. Wiechers reported that fall hydrant flushing was completed October 2. Next regular meeting Monday, October 19, 10:00 a.m.

14. ADJOURNMENT

Motion by Wiechers, second by Cline, to adjourn. Ayes all. The meeting adjourned at 11:14 a.m.

Respectfully submitted,
