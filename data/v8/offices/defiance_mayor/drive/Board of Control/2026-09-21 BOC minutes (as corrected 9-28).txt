CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO
BOARD OF CONTROL
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
Monday, September 21, 2026, 10:00 a.m.
Mayor's Conference Room, City Building, 631 Perry Street

[Corrected copy. Item 6 not-to-exceed amount corrected from $43,500.00 to $34,500.00 at the September 28, 2026 meeting. JR]

MEMBERS PRESENT: Paul Haverkamp, Mayor (Chair); Steven Cline, Law Director; Angela Marquardt, Finance Director; Tom Wiechers, Safety-Service Director

ALSO PRESENT: Kevin Shaffer, Utilities Superintendent; Dale Rummel, Fire Chief; Scott Wenninger, Police Chief; Craig Fenstermaker, Parks and Recreation Director; Ron Diehl, Street Superintendent; Ben Rohrs, IT Coordinator; Megan Ostrander, Chief of Staff; Janelle Rohrs, Deputy Clerk (recording)

1. CALL TO ORDER

Mayor Haverkamp called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m. All four members were present. The Mayor said this was his first meeting as Chair and that he would keep the Board's practices as they are: agenda items to the Deputy Clerk by Thursday noon and no walk-on items unless they cannot wait a week.

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES

Motion by Cline, second by Wiechers, to approve the minutes of the September 14, 2026 regular meeting as distributed. Motion carried 4-0.

3. BILLS LIST

Director Marquardt presented the bills list for September 12 through September 18, 2026, totaling $388,940.27 across all funds. No then-and-now certificates this week.

Motion by Wiechers, second by Marquardt, to approve the bills list. Motion carried 4-0.

4. HARVEST ON THE RIVERFRONT - SPECIAL EVENT USE OF KINGSBURY PARK

Director Fenstermaker presented the special event plan for Harvest on the Riverfront, Saturday, October 10, 2026, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., at Kingsbury Park, rain date Sunday, October 11. The plan follows Saturday's site walk-through, with vendors on the lower lawn, food trucks along the park drive and the stage by the river overlook. He asked for approval of:

- Closure of the park drive to through traffic at the north gate from 6:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., with Street Department barricades;
- Temporary food vendor permits, subject to the Fire Prevention Bureau's vendor and booth plan review on September 24;
- Waiver of the shelter house rental fee for the event;
- Portable toilets and hand wash stations to supplement the two units already at the park.

Chief Rummel said the Fire Prevention Bureau will review propane use and generator placement at each truck. Chief Wenninger will assign an officer to the north gate in the morning.

Motion by Wiechers, second by Cline, to approve the special event plan, park drive closure and fee waiver as presented. Motion carried 4-0.

5. FIRE DEPARTMENT - HOSE AND GROUND LADDER TESTING

Chief Rummel presented a two-year agreement with Precision Fire Equipment Testing for annual service testing of fire hose and ground ladders, $7,980.00 per year (two-year total $15,960.00). Testing will be done at Station 1 in late September with crews rotating apparatus so both stations stay in service.

Motion by Marquardt, second by Wiechers, to approve the two-year testing agreement. Motion carried 4-0.

6. RIGHT-OF-WAY TREES - ANNUAL TRIMMING AND REMOVAL CONTRACT

Director Wiechers presented the annual contract for trimming and removal of trees in the public right-of-way, October 2026 through September 2027. Work is scheduled by the City Arborist from the street tree inventory and resident requests, and includes the 41 removals identified this year, most of them dead or declining ash. Pricing is per tree by diameter class, plus hourly crew rates for storm work. Quotes on the City's unit-price form:

- Schwiebert Tree Service: $34,500.00
- Second contractor: $38,900.00
- Third contractor: $41,260.00

Superintendent Diehl said the Street Department will continue to handle stump grinding and brush pickup.

Motion by Cline, second by Marquardt, to award the contract to Schwiebert Tree Service as a blanket purchase order not to exceed $34,500.00. Motion carried 4-0.

7. POLICE AND IT PURCHASES

a. Chief Wenninger requested replacement of six in-car mobile computers through Northpoint Computer Services at state cooperative pricing, $28,600.00 including docks and setup. The current units are seven years old and two failed this summer.
b. Ben Rohrs presented the annual renewal of the City's productivity and email licenses (186 users) through Maumee Data Systems, $31,240.00, four fewer licenses than last year.

Motion by Wiechers, second by Cline, to approve items 7a and 7b. Motion carried 4-0.

8. WATER DISTRIBUTION - REPLACEMENT FIRE HYDRANTS

Superintendent Shaffer requested twelve replacement hydrants with valves and fittings, for units found broken or obsolete during fall flushing (begun September 14, about a third complete). Low quote: Maumee Valley Waterworks Supply, $38,412.00 (others $40,188.00 and $41,950.00). City crews will install them over the winter. Water Fund.

Motion by Wiechers, second by Marquardt, to authorize the purchase. Motion carried 4-0.

9. PERSONNEL ACTIONS

a. Appointment of Kyle Strausbaugh as Water Plant Operator Trainee, effective October 5, 2026, Step 1, subject to probation.
b. Appointment of a part-time Clerk, Income Tax (20 hours per week), effective October 12, 2026.
c. Up to two seasonal laborers for Parks through November 20 for leaf and fall cleanup in the parks.

Motion by Cline, second by Marquardt, to approve. Motion carried 4-0.

10. OTHER BUSINESS

Mr. Fenstermaker reported, for information, that the municipal pool closed for the season on Labor Day with 9,400 swimmers. The Mayor will attend the Northwest Ohio Mayors Association meeting in Bowling Green on September 28 and asked the Deputy Clerk to post notice of a special meeting that afternoon. Director Marquardt said the 2027 budget calendar and instructions memo will go to department heads this week.

11. ADJOURNMENT

Motion by Wiechers, second by Cline, to adjourn. Motion carried 4-0. Adjourned at 10:58 a.m.
