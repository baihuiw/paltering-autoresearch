1. CALL TO ORDER

Mayor Haverkamp called the meeting to order at 10:00 a.m. All four members were present and a quorum was declared.

The Mayor said this was his first meeting as Chair and that he intended to keep the Board's practices as they are: agenda items to the Deputy Clerk by Thursday noon, backup material in the packet, and no walk-on items unless they truly cannot wait a week. He asked department heads to tell him in advance if an item is likely to draw questions so that he can read up on it.

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES

The minutes of the regular meeting of September 14, 2026 were distributed in advance.

Motion by Director Cline, second by Director Wiechers, to approve the minutes as distributed. Motion carried 4-0.

3. BILLS LIST

Director Marquardt presented the bills list for the period September 12 through September 18, 2026, totaling $388,940.27 across all funds. There were no then-and-now certificates this week.

Motion by Director Wiechers, second by Director Marquardt, to approve the bills list. Motion carried 4-0.

4. HARVEST ON THE RIVERFRONT - SPECIAL EVENT USE OF KINGSBURY PARK

Parks Director Fenstermaker presented the special event plan for Harvest on the Riverfront, Saturday, October 10, 2026, 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., at Kingsbury Park, with a rain date of Sunday, October 11. The plan follows Saturday's site walk-through. It includes two rows of craft and market vendors on the lower lawn, food trucks along the park drive, the kids' area and pumpkin tent near the playground, and the stage at the east end by the river overlook. He asked for approval of the following:

- Closure of the park drive to through traffic at the north gate from 6:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., with Street Department barricades;
- Temporary food vendor permits for food trucks and concession tents, subject to the Fire Prevention Bureau's pre-event vendor and booth plan review on September 24;
- Waiver of the shelter house rental fee for the event;
- Portable toilets and hand wash stations to supplement the two units already at Kingsbury Park.

Mr. Fenstermaker said the event costs are within the Parks special events line and the donations received so far. Chief Rummel said the Fire Prevention Bureau will review propane use and generator placement at each truck. Chief Wenninger said he would assign an officer to the north gate in the morning.

Motion by Director Wiechers, second by Director Cline, to approve the special event plan, park drive closure and fee waiver as presented. Motion carried 4-0.

5. FIRE DEPARTMENT - HOSE AND GROUND LADDER TESTING

Chief Rummel presented a two-year agreement with Precision Fire Equipment Testing for annual service testing of all fire hose and ground ladders under the applicable NFPA standards. The 2026 price is $7,980.00, and the 2027 price is fixed at the same amount, for a two-year total of $15,960.00. Testing will be done at Station 1 over three days in late September with crews rotating apparatus so that both stations stay in service. Any hose that fails will be removed from service and the department will report the replacement need in its 2027 budget request.

Motion by Director Marquardt, second by Director Wiechers, to approve the two-year testing agreement. Motion carried 4-0.

6. RIGHT-OF-WAY TREES - ANNUAL TRIMMING AND REMOVAL CONTRACT

Director Wiechers presented the annual contract for trimming and removal of trees in the public right-of-way for October 2026 through September 2027. The work is scheduled by the City Arborist from the street tree inventory and resident requests, and includes the 41 right-of-way removals identified this year, most of them dead or declining ash. Pricing is by unit (per tree by diameter class, plus hourly crew rates for storm work).

Three quotes were received on the City's unit-price form, evaluated against the same estimated quantities:

- Schwiebert Tree Service: $34,500.00
- Second contractor: $38,900.00
- Third contractor: $41,260.00

Director Wiechers recommended award to Schwiebert Tree Service, which held the contract for the last two years without complaint, as a blanket purchase order not to exceed $34,500.00. Superintendent Diehl said the Street Department will continue to handle stump grinding and brush pickup.

Motion by Director Cline, second by Director Marquardt, to award the annual right-of-way tree trimming and removal contract to Schwiebert Tree Service in an amount not to exceed $34,500.00. Motion carried 4-0.

7. POLICE DEPARTMENT - IN-CAR MOBILE COMPUTERS

Chief Wenninger requested authorization to replace six in-car mobile computers (rugged laptops with vehicle docks and mounts) in the patrol fleet. The current units are seven years old, no longer receive security updates, and two have failed this summer. The purchase is through Northpoint Computer Services at state cooperative contract pricing, $28,600.00 including docks, setup and transfer of the records and dispatch software. IT Coordinator Rohrs said he will configure the units and that the two newest existing laptops will be kept as spares. Director Marquardt confirmed that the cost is in the Police budget for equipment replacement.

Motion by Director Wiechers, second by Director Cline, to authorize the purchase in the amount of $28,600.00. Motion carried 4-0.

8. INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY - PRODUCTIVITY AND EMAIL LICENSING

IT Coordinator Ben Rohrs presented the annual renewal of the City's office productivity and email licenses (186 user licenses plus shared mailboxes) through Maumee Data Systems under a state cooperative contract, for $31,240.00. The count is down four licenses from last year after an audit of inactive accounts. Mr. Rohrs said two-factor sign-in is now required for all users and that he will offer a short training session for the new Mayor's Office staff.

Motion by Director Marquardt, second by Director Wiechers, to approve the renewal in the amount of $31,240.00. Motion carried 4-0.

9. WATER DISTRIBUTION - REPLACEMENT FIRE HYDRANTS

Superintendent Shaffer requested authorization to purchase twelve replacement fire hydrants with valves and fittings to replace hydrants identified as broken or obsolete during fall flushing, which began September 14 and is about a third complete. Three quotes were received:

- Maumee Valley Waterworks Supply: $38,412.00
- Second supplier: $40,188.00
- Third supplier: $41,950.00

Mr. Shaffer said installation will be done by City crews over the winter as weather allows, and that the hydrants are the same make as the City's standard so that parts stay interchangeable. Funding is from the Water Fund distribution line.

Motion by Director Wiechers, second by Director Marquardt, to authorize the purchase from Maumee Valley Waterworks Supply in the amount of $38,412.00. Motion carried 4-0.

10. PARKS - MUNICIPAL POOL 2026 SEASON REPORT (INFORMATION ONLY)

Mr. Fenstermaker reported that the municipal pool opened June 6 and closed for the season on Labor Day, Monday, September 7, with 9,400 swimmers over the season. Season pass sales were up slightly. Two days were lost to weather and one afternoon to a pump failure in July. The pool liner and pump room remain in the five-year capital plan. No action was required.

11. PERSONNEL ACTIONS

Director Wiechers presented the following:

a. Appointment of Kyle Strausbaugh as Water Plant Operator Trainee, Utilities Department, effective October 5, 2026, at Step 1 of the established pay range, subject to the probationary period.
b. Appointment of a part-time Clerk, Income Tax Department (20 hours per week), effective October 12, 2026, to cover the winter filing season.
c. Authorization to hire up to two seasonal laborers for the Parks Department through November 20 for leaf and fall cleanup work in the parks.

Motion by Director Cline, second by Director Marquardt, to approve the personnel actions. Motion carried 4-0.

12. OTHER BUSINESS

The Mayor said he will be at the Northwest Ohio Mayors Association quarterly meeting in Bowling Green next Monday, September 28, and asked the Deputy Clerk to post notice for a special meeting that afternoon so that next week's items are not delayed.

Director Marquardt said the 2027 budget calendar and instructions memo will go out to department heads this week following the Finance Committee's review on September 17 and her meeting with the Mayor tomorrow.

13. ADJOURNMENT

Motion by Director Wiechers, second by Director Cline, to adjourn. Motion carried 4-0. The meeting adjourned at 10:58 a.m.
