CITY OF DEFIANCE, OHIO
BOARD OF CONTROL
MINUTES OF SPECIAL MEETING
Monday, September 28, 2026, 3:30 p.m.
Mayor's Conference Room, City Building, 631 Perry Street

This special meeting was called by the Chair to take up the business of the regular Monday meeting, which could not be held at 10:00 a.m. because the Mayor was attending the Northwest Ohio Mayors Association quarterly meeting. Notice of the special meeting was posted at the City Building and on the City website and sent to the news media on Friday, September 25, 2026, in accordance with the City's open meetings rules.

1. CALL TO ORDER AND ROLL CALL

Mayor Paul Haverkamp, Chair, called the meeting to order at 3:31 p.m.

Members present: Mayor Paul Haverkamp; Law Director Steven Cline; Finance Director Angela Marquardt; Safety-Service Director Tom Wiechers.

Also present: Superintendents Kevin Shaffer and Ron Diehl; Parks Director Craig Fenstermaker; Lt. Mark Sanderson; Chief of Staff Megan Ostrander; Janelle Rohrs, Deputy Clerk (recording). Public: two (sign-in sheet on file).

2. APPROVAL OF MINUTES

Minutes of September 21, 2026, with one correction (Item 6, tree trimming not-to-exceed $34,500.00, not $43,500.00). Motion by Director Cline, second by Director Marquardt, to approve as corrected. Motion carried 4-0.

3. BILLS LIST AND THEN-AND-NOW CERTIFICATES

Bills list for September 19-25, 2026, $612,884.17 all funds, with three then-and-now certificates under ORC 5705.41(D) totaling $4,446.02. Motion by Director Wiechers, second by Director Cline, to approve. Motion carried 4-0.

4. STREET DEPARTMENT - SINGLE-AXLE DUMP TRUCK

Superintendent Diehl requested a 2027 single-axle dump truck with plow and spreader through the ODOT Cooperative Purchasing Program, replacing Unit 22 (2009), which failed inspection with frame rust. Price $187,465.00; delivery 10 to 14 months. Funds are in the 2026 Street Fund capital budget.

Motion by Director Wiechers, second by Director Marquardt, to authorize the purchase in an amount not to exceed $187,465.00. Motion carried 4-0.

5. PARKS - HAMMOND STREET PARK RESTROOM, CHANGE ORDER NO. 2

Director Fenstermaker presented Change Order No. 2 to the Kurtzman Site Development contract, $8,940.00, for a broken floor drain and 22 feet of building sewer found during demolition. Revised contract $157,455.00, within the $162,000.00 budget; completion now October 23, 2026.

Motion by Director Cline, second by Director Wiechers, to approve Change Order No. 2. Motion carried 4-0.

6. WPCF - CLARIFIER NO. 2 DRIVE GEARBOX

Superintendent Shaffer requested replacement of the Clarifier No. 2 drive gearbox (rising vibration, bronze in the oil). Three quotes: $41,780.00 new; $33,950.00 rebuild (six weeks out of service); $44,210.00. He recommended the new unit; the housing is cracked.

Motion by Director Wiechers, second by Director Marquardt, to authorize the purchase in the amount of $41,780.00 from the Sanitary Sewer Operating Fund. Motion carried 4-0.

7. SANITARY SEWER - FOURTH WARD INTERCEPTOR, SEGMENT 4-7

Director Wiechers presented Resolution No. BOC 2026-41, declaring an emergency pursuant to Section 135.09 of the Codified Ordinances and awarding a contract for cured-in-place lining of Fourth Ward Interceptor Segment 4-7 to Vandersall Pipe Renewal LLC of Defiance, Ohio, in an amount not to exceed $480,000.00, without competitive bidding.

Director Wiechers reported that the fall closed-circuit television inspection of the Fourth Ward interceptor, completed in mid-September, found longitudinal and circumferential cracking, root intrusion and infiltration at joints in Segment 4-7, approximately 1,180 linear feet of 21-inch vitrified clay pipe installed in 1958 in the rear-lot easement between Pierce Street and Wayne Avenue. He stated that the segment carries most of the Fourth Ward's sanitary flow and that advertising and awarding under Section 135.03 would not allow the work to be done before cold weather.

Greg Vandersall, owner of Vandersall Pipe Renewal LLC, was present and stated that his crews could mobilize on September 30 and complete the work by October 16, 2026.

Director Cline asked that the resolution set out the facts supporting the emergency determination, as Section 135.09 requires; Director Wiechers said the findings were taken from the inspection results. Director Marquardt certified that funds are available in the Sanitary Sewer Capital Improvement Fund.

Motion by Director Cline, second by Director Marquardt, to adopt Resolution No. BOC 2026-41.

Roll call: Haverkamp, yes; Cline, yes; Marquardt, yes; Wiechers, yes. Motion carried 4-0.

8. BIOSOLIDS FIELD - FARM LEASE RENEWAL
