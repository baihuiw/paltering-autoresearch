Requested action: Authorize repair of Ladder 1's main lift cylinder, together with the annual aerial ladder certification, at the apparatus manufacturer's authorized service center in Toledo, in an amount not to exceed $18,400.00.

Fund / account: Fire/EMS Levy Fund, apparatus repair and maintenance (210.0220.53300). Angela says she can put it in the October appropriation ordinance for Council on October 13.

Background

Ladder 1 has been out of service since Thursday, September 24, when the operator found hydraulic fluid on the turntable during the weekly check. We first hoped it was a hose or fitting. The shop's road technician inspected it on September 29 and found that the leak is at the rod seal of the main lift cylinder, with scoring on the rod. That is not a repair we can do in house or that a general hydraulic shop is set up to do, because the cylinder has to be removed, rebuilt and load tested to the manufacturer's procedure before the aerial can be recertified.

The quote is:

  Remove, rebuild and reinstall main lift cylinder (parts and labor) ...... $11,950.00
  Annual aerial ladder service test and certification (NFPA 1911) ......... $4,850.00
  Transport (shop's driver both ways) ..................................... $1,600.00
  Total ................................................................... $18,400.00

The annual certification is due in November anyway. Doing it while the ladder is in the shop saves a second trip to Toledo and the transport charge, and it means the unit comes back certified for the next twelve months.

Why this is coming to the Board

The amount is under the $25,000 Board threshold, but it is over my department purchasing authority and the work can only be done at the manufacturer's authorized service center, so I don't have three quotes to attach. Director Wiechers asked that it go on the agenda rather than be handled as a department purchase order so the sole-source reason is on the record.

Schedule

The shop has a bay open the week of October 12. Their driver would pick up the unit on Tuesday, October 13. Estimated shop time is three weeks, so we expect Ladder 1 back in service in early November, assuming the cylinder rebuild kit ships on time.

Coverage in the meantime

- Mutual aid agreement for an aerial on working structure fires remains in place. Dispatch has the run cards updated.
- Engine 1 is carrying the extra ground ladders from Ladder 1.
- Crews are doing the aerial portion of their monthly training on the mutual aid unit when it is available.

I'm not comfortable going into the winter without our own aerial, and the unit is 19 years old, so I'd also ask that this be noted for the apparatus replacement discussion with the levy and the 2027 budget.
