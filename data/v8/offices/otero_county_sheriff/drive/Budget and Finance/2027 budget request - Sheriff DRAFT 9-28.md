Without the K-9 program the request is $6,899,200, up 3.6%.

## What drives the increase

1. **Salaries and benefits.** The county's 3% cost-of-living adjustment for all staff, and the step increases already in the pay plan. Health insurance renewal estimate +7% (Finance to confirm 10/2).
2. **Detention contracts.** The Plains Correctional Health agreement renews at a 3% escalator. Food service is up 4.5% under its CPI clause. The new inmate banking and commissary contract (Corvane) costs the county nothing; it is paid from commissary commissions.
3. **Fuel and fleet.** Two patrol vehicles in the replacement cycle (units 14 and 19, both over 150,000 miles). Fuel budgeted at $3.60/gal.
4. **K-9 program.** See below.

## K-9 program (new request)

The Sheriff's Office is requesting the county share of the K-9 Partner Project for 2027:

| Item | 2027 |
|---|---|
| Handler deputy (Deputy I, new position, salary and benefits) | $76,400 |
| K-9 vehicle insert, heat alarm and upfit (one-time) | $9,800 |
| Food, vet care, equipment, recertification | $5,400 |
| **Total** | **$91,600** |

The dog and the handler course ($16,300) are paid by community donations, not the general fund. Residents have given $12,840 so far, and the agreement with Sandhill K-9 was signed September 18 with delivery expected in December. The handler position is requested as a new position rather than taken from patrol, because patrol is already below the minimum of two deputies on the road per shift on many nights.
