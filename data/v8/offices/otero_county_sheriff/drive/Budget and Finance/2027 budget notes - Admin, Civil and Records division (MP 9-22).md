The records clerk was a full-time position that went vacant in August. We are converting it to part-time (20 hours) for 2027, Tue/Thu afternoons plus some mornings, because that's when the counter is busiest. Net, the change saves money against the old full-time position with benefits, but shows as an increase in the part-time line.

Notes:
- Civil fees: Lorraine expects 2026 to finish around $36,500. Fewer district court papers. 2027 at $38,500 assumes the statute's fee levels stay the same and evictions hold steady.

- VIN: steady; most are trailers and vehicles from out of state.
