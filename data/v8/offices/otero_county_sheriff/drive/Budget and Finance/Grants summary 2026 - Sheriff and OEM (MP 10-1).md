## Notes by grant

**EMPG.** Pays half of the Emergency Manager's salary and benefits up to the award. The 2026-27 application was submitted August 14; award letter expected late October. The award has been flat at $38,600 for three years. Quarterly reports and the annual exercise requirement (we used the June 27 search training with CERT and the posse, plus a tabletop with the fire districts in March) are complete.

**HVE overtime.** CDOT reimburses overtime for deputies working enforcement during the statewide enforcement periods (Labor Day, holidays, "Click It or Ticket" in May). We claimed $9,840 of $12,000. The rest lapsed because we didn't have deputies to put on overtime shifts in July. The 2026-27 award is $11,000, starting October 1. Deputies must log every stop on the grant activity sheet or the hours are not reimbursable.

**VALE.** Pays part-time victim advocate hours (contracted through the regional advocate office) and crime victim notification mailings. VALE applications for 2027 are due to the district VALE board November 13. Dana is the lead.

**Body armor.** We will apply in the spring round for the three vests that expire December 2026 through February 2027. Vests must be bought after approval to be reimbursed. The two expiring in October are being bought now from the general fund (see purchasing memo 10/2).

**POST training.** Per-officer allocation from the state for in-service training. We use it for the regional arrest control instructor and the radar recertification. Must be spent by December 31.

**Fire reimbursement.** Ken is checking whether the October 2 fire meets the cost threshold for state reimbursement of county costs. Patrol and posse hours that day are being coded to the fire's incident number (Finance note 4 on the September expenditure report).

## Tips for whoever has these next year

- Every grant has its own timesheet or activity log. If the deputy doesn't fill it in that day, we can't claim it later.
- Keep the award letters, the reports and the claims together in one folder per grant per year (shared drive: Budget and Finance > Grants).
- The state agencies change their portals every couple of years. Keep the login list current with Marcy.
