# Otero County Sheriff's Office Policy Manual

## Policy 3.40 - Extra-Duty Employment

Effective: March 1, 2024 (replaces Policy 3.40 of June 2019)
Approved: the Sheriff
Review: every two years (next review due March 2026; carried over)

### 3.40.1 Purpose

To set out how private and nonprofit hosts may obtain law enforcement services from the Otero County Sheriff's Office for events and activities, and how deputies who work those assignments are scheduled and paid.

### 3.40.2 Definitions

**Extra duty** means law enforcement services, such as traffic control, crowd management or security, provided to a private or nonprofit host at the host's request, outside a deputy's regular scheduled shift, and paid for by the host through the County.

**Host** means the person or organization that requests and pays for extra-duty services.

### 3.40.3 Requests and agreements

1. A host must request extra-duty services in writing to the Patrol Lieutenant at least fourteen (14) days before the event, stating the date, hours, location, expected attendance and whether alcohol will be served.
2. The Patrol Lieutenant decides the number of deputies needed. Events with alcohol service or expected attendance over 500 require at least two deputies.
3. The host signs an Extra-Duty Agreement (form ED-1) before the event. The agreement states the rates, the minimum hours and the host's duty to pay within thirty (30) days.
4. Extra-duty assignments are offered to deputies by seniority rotation. Deputies are not required to accept them.

### 3.40.4 Rates (set by the Board of County Commissioners, Resolution 2024-09)

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Deputy | $58.00 per hour, four-hour minimum |
| Sergeant (required when four or more deputies are assigned) | $68.00 per hour, four-hour minimum |
| Patrol vehicle | $0.70 per mile, from the Sheriff's Office and back |
| Cancellation less than 24 hours before the event | four hours per deputy assigned |

Rates cover the deputy's overtime pay, the County's payroll costs and an administrative charge.

### 3.40.5 Billing and payment

1. Administrative Services invoices the host within ten (10) days after the event and records the invoice in the extra-duty log.
2. Payments are deposited to general fund revenue account 1100-4420 (Sheriff - extra-duty services).
3. A host with an unpaid invoice more than 60 days old will not be scheduled for further extra-duty services until paid.
4. Rates may be waived only for events run by a government agency or a school district, with the Sheriff's written approval noted in the log.

### 3.40.6 Conduct on extra duty

1. Deputies on extra duty are in uniform and act under the authority and policies of the Sheriff's Office.
2. Deputies may not accept payment, food, tickets or gifts from the host beyond what is customary at the event for all workers.
3. Deputies on extra duty remain available to respond to an emergency nearby and notify dispatch when they do.

### 3.40.7 On-duty personnel

1. On-duty deputies are not assigned to provide security or traffic control for a private event in place of extra duty. When a host wants law enforcement at its event, it requests extra duty under this policy.
2. Nothing in this policy limits the Sheriff's authority to assign on-duty personnel to any location when public safety requires.
3. Patrol coverage of the County is not reduced below the minimum staffing in Policy 3.10 to fill an extra-duty assignment.

### 3.40.8 Records

The extra-duty log (event, host, agreement, deputies, hours, amount billed, invoice, status) is kept by Administrative Services and is a public record.
