# Traffic Enforcement Plan - South and East County, October-December 2026

Lt. Cody Brandvold, Patrol and Operations. Approved by Sheriff Strohm October 9, 2026.
Distribution: patrol sergeants, day shift, Road and Bridge supervisor, Bonnie Sauer (for replies to residents).

## Why

Traffic is the most common thing residents raise at town meetings and in email: CR 31 near Cheraw (school bus stop), CO-10 and CO-71 during hunting season, and US-50 east of La Junta. With two deputy positions vacant we can't sit on every road, so this plan puts the time we have where children and crashes are.

## 1. CR 31 - Cheraw school bus stop (priority)

- **Location:** CR 31 at the bus stop just north of the Cheraw town limit (the shelter on the east side). Posted 55 mph; school bus stop ahead sign southbound only.
- **What residents report:** pickups and trucks well over 55 between 6:45 and 7:30 a.m. while children wait and when the bus stops with red lights flashing. One near-miss reported September 24 at the Cheraw town meeting.
- **Plan:**
  - A day-shift deputy is assigned to CR 31 at the bus stop **Tuesdays and Thursdays, 6:50-7:30 a.m.**, starting **October 13** and running through the last school day before winter break (December 18). On Mondays and Wednesdays we add a car when day shift has four or more deputies on.
  - The deputy parks visibly. Stops for speed and for passing a stopped school bus (C.R.S. 42-4-1903). Warnings the first week; citations after.
  - **Speed trailer** from Road and Bridge placed on CR 31 southbound the week of October 19 for two weeks, collecting speeds 24 hours a day. Data to Lt. Brandvold.
  - Road and Bridge reviewing signs (a northbound school bus stop sign and a reduced speed zone are possible; that's the Board's decision on Road and Bridge's recommendation).
  - School district transportation asked to report any bus passing by plate number; bus drivers can call dispatch directly.
- **Measure:** speeds from the trailer before and after; stops and citations by week; reports from the bus driver.

## 2. CO-10 and CO-71 - hunting season (Oct 10 - Nov 30)

- Pronghorn, deer and late waterfowl seasons bring heavy pickup traffic at dawn and dusk.
- Swing shift runs one car through CO-10 east of La Junta and CO-71 south of Rocky Ford at dusk on weekends.
- Watch for vehicles parked on shoulders and anyone shooting from or across a roadway (that's illegal; contact Colorado Parks and Wildlife through dispatch for the wildlife violation, and we handle the traffic and any trespass).
- Livestock on the road on CO-71: call the owner, then CSP if it's on the state route.

## 3. US-50 east of La Junta

- CSP has primary on US-50. We back up crashes and handle county roads at each end.
- No dedicated assignment; coordinate through the monthly CSP troop coffee.

## 4. Fowler and Manzanola county roads

- Gravel road speed complaints (Road 18, Road GG, CR 21): swing shift passes when call load allows. Speed trailer rotates there in November.

## Staffing

| Item | Hours per week |
|---|---|
| CR 31 bus stop (Tue/Thu, + Mon/Wed when staffed) | 1.3 - 2.7 |
| CO-10 / CO-71 dusk patrols (weekends) | 4 |
| Speed trailer moves (Road and Bridge does the move) | 0 |
| **Total patrol time** | **about 5-7 hours a week** |

No overtime is authorized for this plan. If day shift falls below three, the CR 31 assignment yields to calls for service; the sergeant notes it on the shift log.

## Reporting

Lt. Brandvold reports results at the November 10 command staff meeting and to the Sheriff for the November Board report. Bonnie can use this plan when residents write in about CR 31 or hunting traffic.

## How to answer residents (for Bonnie and the front counter)

- CR 31: "A deputy is at the CR 31 bus stop Tuesday and Thursday mornings, 6:50 to 7:30, starting October 13, and on more mornings when we have enough deputies on day shift. Road and Bridge is putting a speed trailer out the week of October 19. Please report any vehicle that passes a stopped school bus to 911 or dispatch with the plate."
- Other roads: "We've added it to our list for area checks. If you see a pattern (same truck, same time of day), call dispatch while it's happening, and send us the details so we can target that time."
- Don't promise a deputy every day or a specific number of tickets.
