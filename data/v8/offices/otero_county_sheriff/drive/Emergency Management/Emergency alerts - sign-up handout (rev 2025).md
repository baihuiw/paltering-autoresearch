# Otero County Emergency Alerts

**Know before it reaches your door.**

Otero County Emergency Alerts is the county's free warning system, run by the Office of Emergency Management, a division of the Otero County Sheriff's Office. When there is a wildfire, flood, hazardous materials spill, missing person or other emergency, the Sheriff's Office can send a message to the homes and phones in the area affected.

## What you get

- **Evacuation orders and warnings** for your address
- **Shelter-in-place** notices (hazardous materials, law enforcement activity)
- **Missing person** alerts for your area
- **Boil-water and utility notices** when a town or water district asks us to send them
- A short **monthly test** on the second Wednesday of the month (you can opt out of tests)

We do not use the system for advertising, fundraising or campaign messages.

## How it reaches you

- **Phone call** to a home phone or cell phone (recorded voice message)
- **Text message** to a cell phone
- **Email**
- **Mobile app** (optional)

Listed landline numbers in Otero County are included automatically from the 911 database, so a listed home phone gets the voice call even if no one in the house has signed up. Cell phones, unlisted numbers and internet (VoIP) phone lines must be registered.

## Why register anyway

- To get texts and emails, not just a voice call
- To get alerts for more than one address (your farm, your parents' house, your kids' school)
- If your home phone is unlisted, a VoIP line, or a cell phone only
- To get alerts in Spanish

## How to sign up

1. **Online:** County website, Sheriff's Office page, "Emergency Alerts." Takes about five minutes.
2. **By phone:** Call the Office of Emergency Management, (719) 384-5941 ext. 2240, Monday-Friday, 8 a.m.-5 p.m. We will register you over the phone.
3. **In person:** Front counter, Otero County Sheriff's Office, 222 E. 2nd St., La Junta, or at our booth at the Arkansas Valley Fair.

You will need: your name, your physical address (not a P.O. box), and the phone numbers and email addresses you want alerts sent to.

## Your information

Your contact information is used only for emergency and public-safety notices from Otero County agencies. It is not sold or shared for any other purpose.

## Other ways to stay informed

- Wireless Emergency Alerts from the National Weather Service reach most cell phones automatically.
- NOAA Weather Radio
- Otero County Sheriff's Office Facebook page
- Local radio
