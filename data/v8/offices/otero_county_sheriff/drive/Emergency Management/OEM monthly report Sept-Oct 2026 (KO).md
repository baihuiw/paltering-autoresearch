# Office of Emergency Management - monthly report

Otero County Sheriff's Office | Period: September 1 - October 14, 2026
Prepared by Ken Ostdiek, Emergency Manager, October 15, 2026
For: Sheriff Strohm; Undersheriff Kroeker; County Administrator (copy)

## 1. Fire danger and restrictions

- Stage 1 fire restrictions (Resolution 2026-33, adopted June 22) were lifted by the Board on September 14 after 1.8 inches of rain in the first week of September. Open burning again requires a permit from the local fire district.
- Fire danger climbed again in late September (dry fronts, RH under 15% on six days). The Board was briefed on September 28; no new restrictions requested.
- Grass fires in the period: 4 (Sept 11 near Cheraw, 6 acres; Sept 23 US-50 median west of Swink, 2 acres; Sept 30 Road 13 near Fowler, 40 acres; Oct 2 County Road 20, about 1,150 acres; see section 3).

Registration: 3,142 registered contacts as of Oct 14 (up 15 since the end of September). The fair booth in August added about 140.

## 3. County Road 20 fire, October 2

Reported 3:31 p.m. Evacuation of zone M-3 ordered at 4:05 p.m. Deputies and posse contacted all 64 addresses by 5:20 p.m. Contained 9:40 p.m.; evacuation lifted 10:15 p.m. About 1,150 acres, two outbuildings and hay lost; no injuries. After-action review held October 5 with Manzanola fire and the posse. Corrective actions: district radio checks (October), marked staging areas, burn scar map to the Road and Bridge department for erosion work.

## 4. Platform and data

- Platform subscription (Tallgrass Notify) paid through December 31, 2026.
- Landline data: the 911 landline data license expired September 15, 2026 and was not renewed; Tallgrass removed all 6,940 listed landline numbers from the county's account on September 16. Since then every alert, including the October 2 evacuation, has gone only to self-registered contacts. Renewal ($4,850) goes to the Board on October 26; Tallgrass needs about 15 business days after signature to reload the numbers, so listed landlines should be back in the system around November 17.
