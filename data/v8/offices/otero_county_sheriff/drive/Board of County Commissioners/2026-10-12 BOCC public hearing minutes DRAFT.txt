OTERO COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
PUBLIC HEARING - MINUTES (DRAFT, for approval October 26)
Monday, October 12, 2026, 10:00 a.m.
Commissioners' Meeting Room, Otero County Courthouse, La Junta, Colorado

PRESENT: Chair (District 1); Chair Pro-Tem (District 2); Commissioner (District 3). Also present: Russ Delgado, County Administrator; the County Attorney; Pam Witcher, Finance Director; Deputy Clerk to the Board. Sheriff Lonnie Strohm attended for item 7.

1. CALL TO ORDER at 10:00 a.m. Pledge of Allegiance. Moment of silence for a former county road supervisor who died October 8.

2. MINUTES. Minutes of the September 28, 2026 public hearing approved as presented, 3-0.

3. CONSENT AGENDA. Approved 3-0: accounts payable warrants in the amount of $1,042,816.55; payroll of September 30; special event liquor permit for the Swink Booster Club (October 17, 24); Road and Bridge surplus sale of two 2009 dump trucks by online auction; renewal of the copier lease for the Clerk and Recorder.

4. PROCLAMATION. The Board proclaimed October 2026 Fire Prevention Month in Otero County and thanked the volunteer fire departments, the Sheriff's Posse and county road crews for their work on the October 2 County Road 20 fire.

5. HUMAN SERVICES. Approved the LEAP (Low-income Energy Assistance) outreach agreement with the regional council of governments for the 2026-27 heating season, 3-0.

6. PUBLIC HEALTH. Report on flu vaccine clinics: 612 doses given at county clinics since September 21. Clinics continue Wednesdays in La Junta and on October 21 in Fowler and October 28 in Manzanola.

7. SHERIFF'S MONTHLY REPORT (verbal). Sheriff Strohm reported:
- The Detention Center averaged 86 inmates in September, with 141 bookings.
- The new deposit, commissary and video visitation system started September 14; deposits and video visits are up.
- Patrol answered 1,214 calls for service in September. Two deputy positions remain vacant; interviews are scheduled for October 20.
- He thanked the fire departments, posse and road crews for the County Road 20 fire response and asked residents to register for emergency alerts.
- Hunting season traffic is up on county roads; deputies are focusing on the CO-10 and CO-71 corridors.
Commissioner (District 3) asked about patrol coverage at night in the south end. The Sheriff said the goal remains two cars south of the river at night and that filling the vacancies is the key.

8. ROAD AND BRIDGE. Approved a change order for the CR 24.5 culvert (additional riprap), $6,320, 3-0. Report: chip seal season completed October 2; 41 miles sealed in 2026.

9. LAND USE. Public hearing on a special use permit for a 1 MW solar array on private land east of Swink. No one spoke against. Approved 3-0 with the planning commission's conditions.

10. COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR. The proposed 2027 budget will be presented October 15. Budget hearing December 7; adoption December 14. Departments were directed at the October 5-6 work sessions to submit requests without new positions.

11. PUBLIC COMMENT (non-agenda). A Cheraw resident asked about a speed study on CR 31. Referred to Road and Bridge.

12. ADJOURNMENT at 11:41 a.m.

Attest: Deputy Clerk to the Board
