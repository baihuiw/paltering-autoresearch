BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
OTERO COUNTY, COLORADO

RESOLUTION NO. 2026-57

A RESOLUTION RESCINDING RESOLUTION NO. 2026-33 AND ENDING STAGE 1 FIRE RESTRICTIONS IN THE UNINCORPORATED AREAS OF OTERO COUNTY

WHEREAS, pursuant to C.R.S. 30-15-401(1)(n.5), the Board of County Commissioners may adopt an ordinance or resolution to ban or restrict open fires and fireworks in the unincorporated areas of the county when the danger of forest or prairie fire is found to be high, based on competent evidence; and

WHEREAS, on June 22, 2026, the Board adopted Resolution No. 2026-33, imposing Stage 1 fire restrictions in the unincorporated areas of Otero County on the recommendation of the Sheriff, as county fire warden under C.R.S. 30-10-513, the Office of Emergency Management and the fire protection districts; and

WHEREAS, Resolution No. 2026-33 provides that the restrictions remain in effect until rescinded by the Board; and

WHEREAS, the Emergency Manager has reported that the county has received approximately 1.8 inches of rain across most areas since September 1, 2026, that the energy release component for the county's fire danger rating area has fallen below the 80th percentile for seven consecutive days, and that the National Weather Service forecasts no red flag conditions for the coming week; and

WHEREAS, the chiefs of the fire protection districts serving Otero County and the Acting Sheriff concur that the conditions that warranted Stage 1 restrictions no longer exist; and

WHEREAS, the Board finds that rescinding the restrictions is in the interest of the public health, safety and welfare, while continuing to require permits for open burning;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the Board of County Commissioners of Otero County, Colorado:

1. Resolution No. 2026-33 is rescinded, and Stage 1 fire restrictions in the unincorporated areas of Otero County end effective at 12:01 a.m. on Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

2. Open burning, including agricultural burning of ditches and fields and the burning of trash or slash, remains subject to the rules of the fire protection district in which the burning takes place. Anyone planning to burn must obtain a burn permit from, or notify, the local fire protection district as that district requires, and must not burn on days of high wind or when a red flag warning is in effect.

3. Nothing in this resolution limits the authority of a fire protection district or of the Sheriff, as county fire warden, to stop a burn that endangers life or property.

4. The Office of Emergency Management will continue to monitor fire danger. If conditions again meet the criteria for restrictions, the Sheriff and the Emergency Manager may recommend that the Board reinstate restrictions, and the Board may consider such a resolution at any regular or special meeting.

5. Staff shall publish notice of this resolution and post it on the county website.

ADOPTED this 14th day of September, 2026, by a vote of 3-0.
