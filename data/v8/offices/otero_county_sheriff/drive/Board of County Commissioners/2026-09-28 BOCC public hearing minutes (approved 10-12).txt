OTERO COUNTY BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS
PUBLIC HEARING - MINUTES
Monday, September 28, 2026, 10:00 a.m.
Commissioners' Meeting Room, Otero County Courthouse, La Junta, Colorado
Approved October 12, 2026

PRESENT: Chair (District 1); Chair Pro-Tem (District 2); Commissioner (District 3). Also present: Russ Delgado, County Administrator; the County Attorney; Pam Witcher, Finance Director; Deputy Clerk to the Board. Sheriff Lonnie Strohm present for items 5 and 9.

1. CALL TO ORDER at 10:00 a.m. Pledge of Allegiance.

2. MINUTES. Minutes of the September 14, 2026 public hearing approved as presented, 3-0.

3. CONSENT AGENDA. Approved 3-0: accounts payable warrants in the amount of $968,211.40; payroll of September 15; retail liquor license renewal (Fowler); abatement petitions recommended by the Assessor (two, totaling $1,184.62); Road and Bridge purchase of a used water truck from the state surplus sale, $38,500.

4. RECOGNITION. The Board recognized the Arkansas Valley Fair board and volunteers for the 2026 fair, which drew record attendance in August.

5. SHERIFF - INTRODUCTION AND COURTHOUSE SECURITY. Sheriff Strohm thanked the Board for the appointment and introduced his command staff. He reported on his September 23 meeting with the court on courthouse security: the magnetometer at the north entrance will be staffed on all jury days, and one additional detention officer will be assigned to Courtroom 2 when in-custody defendants are in court. No Board action needed.
Commissioner (District 3) asked whether residents in the south end could expect more patrol presence. The Sheriff said the two open deputy positions would be filled as soon as possible and that he had asked for the night schedule to keep one car south of the river.

6. ROAD AND BRIDGE. Report: CR 24.5 culvert work began September 21 and is on schedule. Chip seal season to finish the first week of October. A resident asked for a speed study and better signs on CR 31 near the Cheraw school bus stop; the Road and Bridge supervisor will review signage. Referred also to the Sheriff's Office for enforcement.

7. LAND USE. Planning commission recommendation on the Swink solar special use permit set for public hearing October 12. A Swink resident asked that the hearing notice be mailed to all neighbors within one mile; the County Administrator said notice will go to owners within 1,500 feet as the code requires, and the Board asked staff to also post it at the Swink post office.

7a. HUMAN SERVICES. Monthly report: SNAP and Medicaid caseloads steady; the LEAP heating assistance season opens November 1. The Board approved the purchase of a used minivan for client transportation, $18,900, from the Human Services fund, 3-0.

8. BUDGET. The County Administrator reported that department budget requests were received today from all departments and elected officials, including the Sheriff's Office, as directed in the budget calendar. Requests total $3.1 million over the 2026 general fund budget before any reductions. Work sessions October 5 and 6.

9. INTERGOVERNMENTAL. Informational item: the Sheriff's Office proposes to raise the 2027 rate Bent County pays for housing its inmates at the Otero County Detention Center from $65 to $68 per bed-day. Bent County has been notified. The Sheriff will bring the agreement amendment to a later meeting.

9a. EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT - WILDLAND DANGER BRIEFING. Ken Ostdiek, Emergency Manager, reported that wildland danger has climbed again since mid-September, with dry fronts and relative humidity under 15 percent on six days. The energy release component is near but below the level used to recommend restrictions, and the district chiefs are not asking for restrictions at this time. He will keep watching daily readings and bring a recommendation to the Board if conditions meet the criteria. Open burning still requires a district burn permit. No action.

10. PUBLIC COMMENT (non-agenda). A Rocky Ford resident thanked the Sheriff's Office for help with a stray cattle call on CO-71. A La Junta resident asked about the timing of the county's 2027 property tax certification; the Finance Director explained that values are certified by the Assessor in August and mill levies in December.

10a. COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR. The County Administrator reported that the county's property and liability insurance renewal came in 4.2 percent higher for 2027; the increase is included in the department budgets. The Veterans Day ceremony will be held November 11 at 11 a.m. at the courthouse lawn; county offices will be closed that day.

11. ADJOURNMENT at 11:12 a.m.

Attest: Deputy Clerk to the Board
