3. CONSENT AGENDA. 4. RESOLUTION 2026-57, RESCINDING STAGE 1 FIRE RESTRICTIONS. Ken Ostdiek, Emergency Manager, reported 1.8 inches of rain across most of the county since September 1 and recommended lifting the Stage 1 restrictions adopted June 22. Fire districts concur. Open burning again requires a permit from the local fire district. Adopted 3-0.

5. RESOLUTION 2026-58, APPOINTMENT TO FILL VACANCY IN THE OFFICE OF SHERIFF.
The County Attorney explained that the previous sheriff's retirement took effect September 4, 2026; that under C.R.S. 30-10-505 the undersheriff executes the office until a sheriff is appointed and qualified; and that under Article XIV, Section 9 of the Colorado Constitution the Board fills a vacancy in a county office by appointment, the appointee to hold office until the next general election or until the vacancy is filled by election. Because the office of sheriff is on the November 3, 2026 general election ballot, the appointee will serve until the person elected takes office on January 12, 2027.

The Board interviewed three applicants in a work session on September 10. The Chair thanked Undersheriff Dana Kroeker for serving as acting sheriff since September 4.

Letters of support for Lonnie Strohm were received from 14 people, including the Manzanola fire chief, two retired Colorado State Patrol supervisors, the Fowler town board, Boyd Tamlin of Tamlin Ranch, and the Otero County Sheriff's Posse captain. Letters for the other applicants: 6 and 3.

Public comment: three people spoke. A Rocky Ford resident asked that the appointee commit to keeping two patrol cars in the south end at night. A La Junta resident asked about jail visitation since the move to the new building. A Manzanola resident spoke in support of Mr. Strohm.

Mr. Strohm told the Board he would not be a candidate in November and would "run the office steady and hand it to whoever the voters choose in good shape." He said his priorities would be patrol coverage, a safe jail and keeping the office's budget in line.

Motion by the Chair Pro-Tem, second by Commissioner (District 3), to adopt Resolution 2026-58 appointing Lonnie Strohm Sheriff of Otero County, effective upon his taking the oath of office and filing his bond. Adopted 3-0.

The oath will be administered by the County Clerk on Tuesday, September 15, at 9:00 a.m. in the courthouse.
