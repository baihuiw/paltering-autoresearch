INTERGOVERNMENTAL AGREEMENT FOR THE HOUSING OF INMATES
between the County of Otero, by and through its Board of County Commissioners and the Otero County Sheriff ("Otero"), and the County of Bent, by and through its Board of County Commissioners and the Bent County Sheriff ("Bent")

Approved by the Otero County Board December 9, 2024 (Resolution 2024-71). Term January 1, 2025 - December 31, 2027, with rates set by amendment each year.

EXCERPT - sections 1-9 and Exhibit A. Full signed copy with the Clerk to the Board.

1. PURPOSE. Bent County does not operate a full-service jail. Otero agrees to house persons in the custody of the Bent County Sheriff at the Otero County Detention Center, as space allows, under the terms below. This agreement is made under C.R.S. 29-1-203 and C.R.S. 17-26-120.

2. ACCEPTANCE. Otero will accept Bent County inmates who are 18 or older and have been booked by Bent, with a Bent County commitment, warrant or court order. Otero is not required to accept more than 15 Bent County inmates at one time.

3. TRANSPORT. Bent is responsible for transport to and from the Otero County Detention Center, to Bent County court appearances, and to outside appointments unless the parties agree otherwise in writing for a particular trip. Otero will provide video court appearances from the Detention Center when the Bent County court allows.

4. RATE. Bent pays a daily rate per inmate ("bed-day") as set in Exhibit A. The day of booking counts as a day; the day of release does not. Rates are amended each year by written amendment approved by both Boards before January 1.

5. OUTSIDE COSTS. Bent pays the actual cost of hospital, specialist and dental visits and ambulance service outside the Detention Center for its inmates, invoiced at cost.

6. BILLING. Otero invoices Bent monthly in arrears, by the 10th of the month, listing each inmate by booking number, dates and days. Bent pays within 30 days.

7. CUSTODY AND RULES. Bent County inmates are subject to the same rules, classification and discipline as all other inmates. Otero may move an inmate within the facility for safety and security. Otero will notify Bent within 24 hours of any serious incident involving a Bent County inmate.

8. RELEASE. Otero releases a Bent County inmate only on written order of a Bent County court or the Bent County Sheriff, or on posting of bond set by the court. Bonds for Bent County cases may be posted at the Otero County Detention Center lobby window and are forwarded to the Bent County court.

9. TERMINATION. Either party may end this agreement on 90 days' written notice.
