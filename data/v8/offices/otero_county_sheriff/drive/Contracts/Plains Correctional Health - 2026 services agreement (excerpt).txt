INMATE HEALTH SERVICES AGREEMENT (EXCERPT)
Board of County Commissioners of Otero County, Colorado, and Plains Correctional Health LLC
Approved December 8, 2025 (Resolution 2025-88). Term: January 1 - December 31, 2026, renewable by the Board for one-year terms.
Excerpt prepared for the Detention Center binder: Sections 2, 3, 5, 7 and Exhibit A. Full agreement on file with the Clerk to the Board.

SECTION 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES
2.1 Contractor shall provide all medical, nursing, mental health screening and pharmacy management services for persons held at the Otero County Detention Center, consistent with the standards of the National Commission on Correctional Health Care for jails.
2.2 Services include: receiving screening at booking; health assessments within 14 days; sick call; chronic care (including diabetes, hypertension, seizure disorders and withdrawal); medication administration; emergency response; referral and coordination of off-site care; and medical records.
2.3 Contractor shall provide a physician or advanced practice provider on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, who shall respond by phone within 30 minutes.

SECTION 3. STAFFING
3.1 Contractor shall staff the Detention Center according to the Staffing Plan in Exhibit A, including 24-hour on-site nursing coverage, 7 days a week.
3.2 Contractor shall notify the Detention Commander in writing within 24 hours of any shift in Exhibit A that is not filled.
3.3 For each hour of a nursing shift in Exhibit A that is not filled, Contractor shall credit the County $65.00 against the next monthly invoice.
3.4 If any Exhibit A position is unfilled for more than 14 consecutive days, Contractor shall provide temporary or agency staff at its own cost.

SECTION 5. COMPENSATION
5.1 The County shall pay Contractor $41,200.00 per month, invoiced monthly in arrears, based on an average daily population of up to 110.
5.2 Off-site hospital, specialist and pharmacy costs above $15,000 per person per year are the County's responsibility.

SECTION 7. REPORTS
7.1 Contractor's Health Services Administrator shall submit a monthly report to the Detention Commander by the 10th of each month, including staffing coverage, sick call volume, off-site transports, medication errors and grievances.
7.2 The Detention Commander shall include the contractor's performance in the Sheriff's monthly report to the Board.
