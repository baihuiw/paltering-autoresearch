CORVANE INMATE SERVICES - AGREEMENT SUMMARY FOR IMPLEMENTATION
Prepared by Marcy Peralta, Administrative Services Manager, September 3, 2026
For: Capt. Arguello, detention sergeants, Finance (P. Witcher), Treasurer's office

Agreement: Inmate Services Agreement between the Board of County Commissioners of Otero County and Corvane Inmate Services, approved by the Board on August 24, 2026 (Resolution 2026-51). Term: September 14, 2026 through September 13, 2029, with two one-year renewals.

Go-live: Monday, September 14, 2026. Old vendor's kiosk and phones shut off 6 p.m. Sunday, September 13. Balances transfer automatically from the old vendor's export.

WHAT CORVANE PROVIDES
- Lobby kiosk (cash, debit, credit) and 2 housing-unit kiosks
- 48 inmate tablets (calls, messages, commissary orders, handbook, grievances, law library)
- Online and phone deposits for families
- Commissary ordering and delivery Tues/Fri (warehouse in Pueblo)
- Video visitation (remote and lobby)
- Inmate trust accounting software (ledger, statements, release accounting)

COUNTY COSTS
None. Corvane keeps deposit and phone fees and pays the county a commission:
- Commissary: 22% of net sales
- Phones and messages: 15% of net revenue
Commissions go to 1100-4460 (commissary commissions), monthly, by the 20th.

FEES PAID BY FAMILIES (from Exhibit B)
- Kiosk cash deposit: $0
- Kiosk card deposit: $2.95 up to $20; $4.95 up to $100; 5% over $100
- Online/phone deposit: $3.45 up to $20; $5.45 up to $100; 5.5% over $100
- Calls: $0.12 per minute (local and long distance)
- Video visit (remote): $4.00 per 20 minutes; lobby video visits free

TRUST ACCOUNT AND BANKING
- Inmate trust account stays at the county's bank. The Treasurer is the custodian.
- Corvane runs the ledger and daily reconciliation report (RB-01) and the released-with-balance report (RB-02).

ADDENDA (separate signatures needed)
- Addendum A - Commissary product list and pricing. Signed with the agreement.
- Addendum B - Video visitation rules. Signed with the agreement.
- Addendum C - Release card program. Corvane issues prepaid debit release cards for balances at release, funded daily from a settlement sub-account at the county's bank. Needs: county signature on Addendum C; Treasurer to open the settlement sub-account and give Corvane the account details; Finance to set up the daily transfer. Free to the cardholder for 30 days, then $2.50/month inactivity fee. STATUS 9/3: Corvane sent Addendum C to the Sheriff on 8/28; not yet routed. Needs to go to the Board.
- Addendum D - Tablet content (education, religious, law library). Signed with the agreement.

RELEASE PROCEDURE (from 9/14)
1. Release officer closes the booking in the jail management system.
2. Kiosk prints release receipt with balance.
3. Balance goes to a release card (Addendum C) mailed to the release address, or held for lobby pickup if no address.
4. Old county check process is discontinued.
