CONTRACT SUMMARY - FOR FILE
Detention food service

Contractor: Arkvale Food Service, Inc. (Pueblo, Colorado)
County agreement: approved by the Board January 12, 2026 (Resolution 2026-04); term January 1 - December 31, 2026, with up to two one-year renewals
Account: 1120 Detention - Food service (contract)
Summary prepared by: Marcy Peralta, 1/20/2026; revised 9/30/2026 for the 2027 budget
County contact: Capt. Gil Arguello (operations), Marcy Peralta (invoices)
Contractor contact: site food service manager on site daily; district manager visits monthly

1. SCOPE
- Arkvale operates the Detention Center kitchen with its own staff (one site manager, two cooks) and up to six inmate kitchen workers supervised by Arkvale staff and a detention officer.
- Three meals a day, 7 days a week, for inmates; two of the three meals hot. Sack meals for court and transport as ordered.
- Staff meals for the on-duty detention shift (one meal per shift) at the same per-meal price.
- Menus on a 5-week cycle, reviewed and signed by a registered dietitian, meeting at least 2,800 calories a day average for adult males.
- Religious diets (e.g., no pork, vegetarian) and other approved special diets per county policy.
- Holiday meals on Thanksgiving, Christmas and Easter.

2. PRICE (2026)
- $2.48 per meal for counts of 81-100 inmates (current range).
- $2.61 per meal for counts of 60-80; $2.39 above 100.
- Staff meals: $2.48.
- Billing is by actual meals served, from the daily meal count sheets signed by the shift sergeant.
- Annual adjustment on renewal: change in the Consumer Price Index (Denver-Aurora-Lakewood, food away from home), capped at 5%. Arkvale's renewal letter of September 15 proposes 4.5% for 2027.

3. COUNTY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Kitchen space, major equipment, utilities, and repair of county-owned equipment (the tilt skillet and the two combi ovens are county-owned; the tilt skillet was replaced under the building warranty in September).
- Security, inmate worker screening and supervision.

4. CONTRACTOR RESPONSIBILITIES
- Food, supplies, paper goods and chemicals.
- Food handler training for all kitchen workers, including inmate workers.
- Compliance with the county health department's retail food rules; the kitchen is inspected twice a year.
- Insurance: general liability $1,000,000 per occurrence; workers' compensation as required.

5. PERFORMANCE
- Meals served within 30 minutes of the posted times (breakfast 5:30 a.m., lunch 11:00 a.m., dinner 4:30 p.m.).
- Grievances about food go through the jail grievance process; Arkvale responds within 3 business days.
- Either party may terminate on 60 days' written notice.
