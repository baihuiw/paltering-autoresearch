PURCHASE AND TRAINING AGREEMENT
Sandhill K-9 / Otero County Sheriff's Office

Agreement No. SK9-2026-031
Date: September 18, 2026

Seller: Sandhill K-9, 418 Sandhill Trail, Pueblo West, CO 81007. Owner and lead trainer: Dale Whitacre.
Buyer: Board of County Commissioners of Otero County, Colorado, for the Otero County Sheriff's Office, 222 E. 2nd St., La Junta, CO 81050.

Note (MP): Signed by the Sheriff 9/18 under the Board's standing authority for purchases paid from restricted donations under $25,000 (Resolution 2024-12). Copy to Finance 9/21 with the deposit request.

1. DOG. Seller will furnish one (1) dual-purpose police service dog, Belgian Malinois or German shepherd, male or female, 18 to 36 months old, imprinted for narcotics detection (cocaine, heroin, methamphetamine and fentanyl; not marijuana) and trained in tracking, area and building search, handler protection and obedience. Seller will provide hip and elbow x-rays, a veterinary health certificate and vaccination records at delivery.

2. HANDLER COURSE. Seller will provide a five (5) week handler course for one deputy and the dog, at Seller's facility, Monday through Friday, ending in a team certification under Seller's standards. The course will begin within ten (10) business days of delivery.

3. PRICE.
   Dog ........................................ $11,500.00
   Handler course (5 weeks) ................... $ 4,800.00
   Total ...................................... $16,300.00

4. PAYMENT. Deposit of $2,000.00 on signing. Balance due at delivery. Buyer's payments will be made by Otero County warrant from account 1100-2190.

5. DELIVERY. Seller expects to deliver the dog on or about December 7, 2026. Seller will tell Buyer of any change in the expected date at least fourteen (14) days ahead.

6. CANCELLATION. Either party may cancel by written notice. If Buyer cancels before a dog has been assigned and imprinted for Buyer, Seller will refund the deposit in full within thirty (30) days. After assignment, the deposit is non-refundable.

7. HEALTH GUARANTEE. Seller guarantees the dog against disqualifying hip or elbow dysplasia for twenty-four (24) months from delivery. If the dog is disqualified, Seller will replace it at no charge.

8. RECERTIFICATION. Annual recertification at Seller's facility, $350 per year, billed separately.

9. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY. Seller carries general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 and will name Otero County as additional insured during the handler course. Nothing in this agreement waives the County's protections under the Colorado Governmental Immunity Act.

10. GOVERNING LAW. Colorado. Venue: Otero County District Court.

11. APPROPRIATION. Payment obligations beyond the current fiscal year depend on funds being appropriated, budgeted and otherwise made available.
