MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING - COURTHOUSE SECURITY
Otero County Courthouse and Combined Courts, La Junta

Parties: the Chief Judge of the 16th Judicial District; the Otero County Board of County Commissioners; the Otero County Sheriff
Effective: January 1, 2024, continuing until amended. Reviewed by the parties September 23, 2026 (no changes; notes below).

1. PURPOSE
The parties share responsibility for security in the Otero County Courthouse. Under C.R.S. 13-3-108 and the Chief Justice's directive on court security, each district has a court security committee. This MOU sets out who does what in Otero County.

2. SHERIFF'S OFFICE RESPONSIBILITIES
a. Staff the public entrance screening station (magnetometer and X-ray) at the north entrance on all days the courts are in session, 7:45 a.m. to 5 p.m. The Sheriff assigns one court security deputy and, on jury days and other days with heavy dockets, a second officer.
b. Escort and supervise all in-custody defendants from the Detention Center to court and back. In-custody appearances are by video from the Detention Center unless the judge orders the person brought in person.
c. Provide a deputy or detention officer in each courtroom in which an in-custody defendant appears in person.
d. Respond to duress alarms and calls for assistance anywhere in the courthouse.
e. Keep a log of items surrendered at screening; prohibited items are held for pickup or, if contraband, handled as evidence.

3. COURTS' RESPONSIBILITIES
a. Give the Sheriff's Office the weekly docket by Thursday for the following week, including jury trials and high-conflict domestic matters.
b. Notify the court security deputy as early as possible of any threat to a judge, staff or party.
c. Provide panic alarm testing with the county facilities department quarterly.

4. COUNTY RESPONSIBILITIES
a. Maintain the screening equipment, duress alarms and cameras.
b. Fund the court security deputy positions through the Sheriff's budget.

5. COMMITTEE
The court security committee (a judge designated by the Chief Judge, the district administrator, the Sheriff or designee, the county administrator, and the District Attorney's office) meets at least twice a year.

6. WEAPONS
Only on-duty law enforcement officers may carry weapons past screening. Off-duty officers check weapons at the screening station. Concealed handgun permit holders may not carry in the courthouse; the screening station has lockers for that purpose.

7. AMENDMENT
Any party may request changes in writing. Changes take effect when signed by all parties.
