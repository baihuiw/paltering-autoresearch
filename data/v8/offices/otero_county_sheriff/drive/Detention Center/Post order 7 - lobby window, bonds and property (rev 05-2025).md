# Otero County Detention Center - Post Order 7: Lobby Window (Bonds and Property)

Revised May 2025 (GA). Reviewed October 2026, no changes. Applies to the detention officer or clerk assigned to the lobby window on any shift.

## Hours

The lobby window is staffed **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, including holidays. Between 11 p.m. and 7 a.m. the booking officer covers the window; the person posting bond may wait up to 15 minutes if booking is busy. Do not leave the public waiting without telling them how long it will be.

## Who can be bonded out

A person may be released on bond only after a bond amount has been set:
- **Bond schedule:** for many misdemeanors and some low-level felonies, the 16th Judicial District's standing bond schedule sets the amount at booking. These bonds can be posted any time, day or night, once booking is complete (fingerprints, photo, property, screening).
- **Set by a judge:** for other charges, warrants with "no bond" or "bond to be set," and domestic violence arrests, the person waits for advisement before a judge. Advisements are held by video from the Detention Center Monday-Friday at 1:30 p.m. (except court holidays). After the judge sets bond, it can be posted right away at the window.
- **Holds** for other agencies or with a "no bond" order cannot be bonded here.

Look up the bond amount and type on the booking screen. Tell the person posting the amount, the type (cash only, cash/surety, or personal recognizance), and any conditions the judge set. Do not guess.

## Accepted payment for cash bonds

- **Cash** (exact amount if possible; the window keeps $100 in change)
- **Cashier's check** or **money order** payable to "Otero County Sheriff"
- **No personal checks.**
- **No credit or debit cards for bonds.** The lobby kiosk is for deposits to inmate accounts only and cannot be used to post a bond. If someone has only a card, they may use an ATM; the window does not give cash back.

A **$10 bonding fee** (C.R.S. 30-1-104) is collected for each bond, in cash, separately from the bond amount. One fee per bond, not per person.

## Surety bonds (bail bond agents)

Licensed bail bond agents post surety bonds at the window with a valid power of attorney and their insurance license. Check the agent's license against the current list from the Division of Insurance (binder at the window). We do not recommend any agent. The posted list in the lobby is in alphabetical order and is the only list we give out.

## Posting the bond

1. Photo ID from the person posting the bond.
2. Complete the bond form (cash or surety). The defendant signs the bond and the conditions before release.
3. Count cash twice in view of the camera; second officer verifies anything over $1,000.
4. Give the person posting a copy of the bond paperwork with the bond number. They need it to ask the court about the money after the case.
5. Notify the housing unit for release processing. Release usually takes 45 minutes to 2 hours after the bond is posted, depending on booking activity and shift change.

## Where the money goes

Cash bonds are turned over to the court of the case on the next business day. The court, not the Sheriff's Office, decides when a cash bond is returned or applied to fines and costs at the end of the case. Refer questions about getting bond money back to the clerk of that court.

## Property pickup

- Weekdays 8 a.m.-4 p.m. only.
- Written property release signed by the inmate, and the picker-up's photo ID.
- No property is released at night except a released person's own property at release.

## Phones and questions at the window

Callers asking if someone can bond out: give the bond amount and type only if the person is booked and the amount is on the screen. Otherwise: "The bond hasn't been set yet. If the judge sets it at advisement, it is usually set by mid-afternoon on a weekday."

## Added October 2026 (GA)

- Signs at the window and on the deposit kiosk: "Bonds: cash, cashier's check or money order only. The kiosk cannot be used for bonds." Brief every new window officer on this.
- Night callers asking whether they must wait for the court to open: if the bond is on the schedule or has been set, no; it can be posted at the window any hour. If it has not been set, the person must wait for the weekday advisement.
- Bent County cases: bonds may be posted here and are forwarded to the Bent County court the next business day.
