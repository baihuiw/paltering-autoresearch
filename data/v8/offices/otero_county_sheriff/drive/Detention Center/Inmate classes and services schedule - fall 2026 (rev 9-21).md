## Rules for classes and groups

- Inmates sign up on the tablet request form by Sunday night for the following week. Classification decides who may attend together.
- Two detention officers in the MPR for any group over 12.
- Volunteers must pass the background check and the half-day volunteer orientation (next orientation: Saturday, November 7, 9 a.m.-1 p.m.). Volunteers sign in at the lobby window and wear the orange volunteer badge.
- Materials brought in by volunteers are approved by the activities sergeant in advance. No staples, spiral bindings or hardcovers.
- A class or group may be cancelled for a lockdown or staffing; the sergeant on duty decides and tells the volunteer as early as possible.
