# Inmate Workers - Eligibility and Assignments

Otero County Detention Center. Policy reference D-7.12. Revised July 2025 (GA). Reviewed September 2026; no changes.

## Purpose

Inmate workers ("trusties") help keep the facility clean and running: kitchen, laundry, facility cleaning, and some outside grounds work. Work assignments give sentenced inmates productive work, earned time where the court or statute allows it, and work habits to take with them.

## Who is eligible

All of the following:
- Sentenced to the county jail, or pretrial with the written approval of the Detention Commander (kitchen and laundry only).
- Classified minimum custody for at least 14 days with no major disciplinary reports.
- No current charge or conviction for escape, sexual assault, or assault on staff.
- No active holds for other agencies (including Bent and Crowley County inmates, who are not eligible).
- Passes the food handler briefing (kitchen workers).
- Agrees in writing to the worker rules.

Outside grounds workers never leave the county property and are within sight of an officer at all times. The outside crew does not work on public roads.

## Earned time

Sentenced workers may earn good time and work credit as allowed by C.R.S. 17-26-109 and the sentencing court's order. The records sergeant calculates release dates; workers can ask through the tablet request form.

## Privileges

- Housing in the worker pod (Unit A, east side).
- Extra commissary order day.
- One additional 20-minute phone call a week.
- Clean uniform daily.

## Rules (summary)

- Report to the assignment on time and in the correct uniform.
- No trading, passing items or messages between units or to the public.
- Kitchen workers may not take food from the kitchen.
- No contact with the public in the lobby; lobby cleaning is done before 7:30 a.m. or after the lobby is cleared.
- Removal from work assignments for any major disciplinary report, refusal to work, or a security concern.

## Removal and review

The shift sergeant may remove a worker from an assignment at once for a rule violation. The activities sergeant reviews removals weekly. A removed worker may apply again after 30 days.

## Records

The worker roster, hours and assignments are kept by the activities sergeant (Sgt. Tafoya). The roster is internal and lists booking numbers only.
