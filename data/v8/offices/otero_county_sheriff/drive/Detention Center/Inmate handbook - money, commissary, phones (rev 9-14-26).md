# Otero County Detention Center - Inmate Handbook

## Section 6: Money, commissary and phones

Revised September 14, 2026 (Corvane changeover). Replaces Section 6 of the January 2025 handbook. Copies are posted in each housing unit and on the tablets.

### 6.1 Your account

When you are booked, any cash you have is counted in front of you, deposited to your inmate account, and you sign a receipt. Your account is held in the Detention Center's inmate trust account at a local bank and is managed through Corvane Inmate Services.

You can check your balance on the housing unit kiosk or tablet at any time.

### 6.2 Deposits from family and friends

Family and friends can put money on your account:
- at the lobby kiosk (cash, debit or credit card), 24 hours a day;
- online or by phone through Corvane (debit or credit card);
- by money order mailed to the Detention Center with your full name and booking number.

Corvane charges the sender a fee for card deposits. Lobby cash deposits have no fee. Deposits usually post within 15 minutes (kiosk and online) or one business day (money orders).

### 6.3 Commissary

Commissary orders are placed on the tablet or kiosk by Sunday 10 p.m. for Tuesday delivery and by Wednesday 10 p.m. for Friday delivery. Weekly limit: $90. Indigent kits (hygiene, paper, envelopes, stamps) are provided free once a week if your balance has been under $3 for 7 days.

### 6.4 Phones and tablets

Phone calls and messages are through the tablet system. Call rates are posted on the tablet. Calls to your attorney are free and are not recorded. Video visits are covered in the Visitor and Family Guide.

### 6.5 Fees the Detention Center charges

- Booking fee: none.
- Medical co-pay: $5 per sick call request you initiate (never for emergencies, follow-up visits ordered by the provider, or mental health). No one is refused care for lack of money.
- Damage to county property: actual cost, after a hearing.

### 6.6 When you are released

Any money left in your account when you are released is returned to you on a Corvane release card. You do not have to ask for it.

- At release, the lobby kiosk prints a release receipt showing your balance.
- Your release card is mailed to the address you give at release. Make sure it is an address where you can get mail.
- Release cards can be used anywhere debit cards are accepted and at ATMs, and are free to use for the first 30 days.
- If you do not have an address, tell the release officer; you can pick up your card at the Detention Center lobby with photo ID.

If you were released and have a question about your balance or card, call the Detention Center at (719) 384-5941 and ask for the inmate accounts clerk.

### 6.7 Property

Personal property not allowed in housing is inventoried and stored. Property must be picked up within 30 days of release or it will be disposed of under county policy.
