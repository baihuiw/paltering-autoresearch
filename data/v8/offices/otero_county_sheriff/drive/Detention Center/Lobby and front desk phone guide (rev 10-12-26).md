# Detention Center lobby and front desk - phone guide

Revised October 12, 2026 (DK). Keep by the front desk phone. Be polite, keep calls short, and never give out information about a person's charges, medical care or housing location beyond what is below.

## Who is in custody?

We can confirm whether a person is in custody and give the booking number, charges as booked, bond amount and next court date. That information is also on the public inmate lookup on the county website. We do not discuss case details. Refer callers about a case to the person's attorney or the 16th Judicial District courts.

## Bond

- Cash and surety bonds are posted at the lobby window, 24 hours.
- Cash bonds: cash, cashier's check or money order. No personal checks.
- Bail bondsmen: we don't recommend one. The list is posted in the lobby.

## Money on an account

- Lobby kiosk: cash, debit or credit, 24/7. No fee for cash.
- Online or by phone: Corvane customer care, (800) 555-0173. Corvane charges a fee for card deposits.
- Money orders by mail: full name and booking number on the money order.

## Release cards (people already released)

- "Release cards are issued by Corvane. Your release card is being processed and will be mailed to the address you gave at release."
- Confirm the caller's mailing address and write it on the callback sheet for Captain Arguello's office.
- Do not give a date. Do not discuss county paperwork.

## Visits

- Video visits: 7 days a week, 8 a.m.-9 p.m., scheduled at the lobby kiosk or online up to 7 days ahead. Lobby video visits are free.
- In-person visits: Saturdays and Sundays, 1-4 p.m., by appointment, 30 minutes, two adult visitors and children with a parent. Photo ID required.
- Attorneys: any day, 8 a.m.-8 p.m.; call ahead.

## Mail

Postcards and letters only, to: [Full name, booking number], Otero County Detention Center, 222 E. 2nd St., La Junta, CO 81050. No packages, stickers, perfume, or anything glued. Books only from a publisher or retailer.

## Medications and medical

- Families may not drop off medications. Medical staff verify prescriptions with the pharmacy or doctor and order through the jail pharmacy.
- Callers with a concern about someone's health: take the caller's name and number and give them to the shift sergeant, who will pass it to medical. Do not discuss anyone's medical information on the phone.

## Property pickup

Weekdays 8 a.m.-4 p.m., with written release signed by the inmate and photo ID.

## Calls to route

- Reporters: Undersheriff Kroeker.
- Attorneys asking about records: Civil and Records, Lorraine Pacheco.
- Anyone threatening staff or an inmate: shift sergeant immediately.
