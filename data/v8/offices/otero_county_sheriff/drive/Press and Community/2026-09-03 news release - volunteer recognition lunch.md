# Sheriff's Office thanks volunteers at annual lunch

LA JUNTA - Acting Sheriff Dana Kroeker thanked about 40 volunteers at the Sheriff's Office annual volunteer recognition lunch on Thursday at the Otero County Fairgrounds. The lunch honored the Sheriff's Posse, the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT), the jail's chaplains and program volunteers, and the callers in the office's Daily Check-In program.

"These folks give us hundreds of hours every month and ask for nothing," Kroeker said. "Every deputy in this office knows we could not do this job without them."

Posse members gave hundreds of hours this year, riding in parades, searching for missing people and helping move livestock. CERT volunteers train for floods, fires and shelter duty. Jail volunteers lead GED tutoring, recovery meetings and chapel services every week.

The lunch also marked the end of Joyce Lindquist's service as coordinator of the Daily Check-In. Lindquist started the program in 2019 and is moving to Colorado Springs to be near her family. Over the past twelve months, the program's callers made about 13,900 morning calls, and deputies were sent to 17 homes when no one answered. Lindquist led the callers' team through all of it.

"Joyce built something that has been a lifeline for a lot of families," Kroeker said. "We are grateful, and we wish her well."

The Daily Check-In program will continue. The Sheriff's Office is looking for a new volunteer coordinator and new callers. Anyone interested can call Bonnie Sauer at (719) 384-5941.

###

**About the Sheriff's Office:** The Otero County Sheriff's Office runs the county detention center in La Junta, patrols the unincorporated county, serves civil process for the courts, issues concealed handgun permits, provides courthouse security, and through its Office of Emergency Management coordinates the county's response to fires, floods and other disasters.
