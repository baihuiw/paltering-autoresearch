# Sheriff Strohm's first week: thank-you to residents, K-9 project on track

LA JUNTA - Sheriff Lonnie Strohm, appointed by the Otero County Board of County Commissioners on September 14 and sworn in September 15, thanked residents this week for a warm welcome and laid out his priorities for the months he will serve.

"I told the commissioners I'd be a working sheriff, not a campaigning one," Strohm said. "My job between now and January is to keep this office steady, keep deputies on the road, run a safe jail, and hand the next sheriff an office in good order."

Strohm said his first-week priorities are:

- **Patrol coverage.** Meeting with every shift and reviewing coverage in the east end of the county and along US-50.
- **The detention center.** Touring the facility with Captain Gil Arguello and the medical and commissary contractors.
- **Budget.** Preparing the Sheriff's Office's 2027 budget request for the Board's October work sessions.
- **Community programs.** Keeping the K-9 Partner Project, the volunteer posse and the school safety talks going.

## K-9 Partner Project

Residents, businesses and clubs have given **$9,615** to the K-9 Partner Project since it began this summer, from 74 donors. On September 18 Sheriff Strohm signed an agreement with Sandhill K-9 of Pueblo West for a dual-purpose (patrol and narcotics detection) dog and a five-week handler course. Sandhill expects the dog to be ready for delivery in December, with the team on the road in early 2027.

"People in this county put their own money into this, and I want them to know I'm grateful," Strohm said. "A dog is a tool that finds lost kids and keeps deputies safer. Every dollar given goes to the K-9 program."

A community pancake breakfast for the project is planned for **Saturday, September 26, 7-11 a.m.**, at the Rocky Ford community building. Deputies will be flipping pancakes. Free-will donations; kids under 6 eat free.

Donations can be made at the Sheriff's Office front counter or mailed to the Otero County Sheriff's Office, attn: K-9 Partner Project. Checks payable to Otero County, memo "K-9".

## About the Sheriff

Sheriff Strohm grew up in Manzanola and served 26 years with the Colorado State Patrol, retiring in 2025 as a sergeant at the La Junta troop office. He was a Manzanola volunteer firefighter for 11 years. He has said he will not be a candidate in November and will serve until the sheriff-elect is sworn in on January 12, 2027.
