# Community Outreach: Speaker Requests, School Visits and Events

Otero County Sheriff's Office. Coordinated by Bonnie Sauer, Executive Assistant (bonnie.sauer@oterogov.org, (719) 384-5941).
Revised September 25, 2026.

## How to ask

Use the contact form on the Sheriff's Office page of the county website (choose "Invitation / event"), email sheriff@oterogov.org, or call Bonnie. Tell us:
- The group and the age or grade of the audience
- The date(s) and time you'd like, and how long
- The topic
- Whether you're asking for the Sheriff himself or any deputy

Please ask **at least three weeks ahead**. Bonnie confirms within five business days and sends the name of who is coming.

## Schools

- School visits are usually scheduled **Tuesday through Thursday mornings, 8:30-11:30 a.m.** The day-shift deputy closest to the school takes it when calls allow. If a call comes in, the deputy may need to leave; we will reschedule.
- The Sheriff does classroom visits himself when his calendar allows. He has blocked Wednesday mornings in November for school visits.
- For multiple classes in a grade, we can do one combined session (gym or library) or back-to-back 30-minute sessions.
- No cost to schools or groups.
- Deputies bring coloring sheets and junior deputy stickers for the youngest grades. The patrol car's lights may be demonstrated; the siren is not used inside town limits.

## Events

- Parades: request one patrol car and/or the posse at least four weeks ahead.
- Booths at fairs and festivals: we bring the child ID kit table when staff are available.
- Trunk-or-treat, La Junta, Saturday, October 31, 5-8 p.m. (deputies and posse).

## What we don't do

- We don't attend political events or candidate forums in uniform.
- We can't provide security for private events as a community visit.

## Tips for teachers

- A 30-minute visit works best for K-3, and up to 45 minutes for 4th grade and up.
- If your class has written questions or letters ahead of time, send them to Bonnie; the deputy can answer them in person.
- Let us know if any student may be upset by a uniform or a patrol car (for example, a family member was recently arrested). We'll keep it low-key.
- Outdoor patrol car visits depend on weather and on the deputy not being called away.
- We can leave behind "know your address and phone number" cards and 911 coloring sheets for the class.
