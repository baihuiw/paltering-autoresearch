# Otero County Daily Check-In

A free morning phone call from a Sheriff's Office volunteer, for county residents who live alone.

Otero County Sheriff's Office | (719) 384-5941 | sheriff@oterogov.org
Revised 2025

## How it works

- Volunteer callers phone each enrolled resident every morning, seven days a week, between 7:30 and 9:00 a.m.
- If there is no answer, the caller tries again about 30 minutes later.
- If there is still no answer, the caller notifies dispatch and a deputy is sent to the home for a welfare check.
- Calls take about two minutes. You can chat or just say "I'm fine."
- The program is free. Callers are screened volunteers, and all calls come from the Sheriff's Office.

## Who can enroll

Any Otero County resident who lives alone and is 65 or older, or who has a disability or medical condition that makes a daily contact worthwhile. Family members may enroll a parent or relative with that person's agreement.

## How to enroll

Call (719) 384-5941 and ask for Bonnie Sauer, or choose "Community programs" on the contact form on the Sheriff's Office page of the county website. We will ask for:

1. The resident's name, address and the phone number we should call
2. A second contact (a relative, neighbor or friend) we can reach if we cannot reach the resident
3. The best time to call between 7:30 and 9:00 a.m.
4. Anything a deputy should know on arrival (a locked gate, a dog, hearing trouble)

Enrollment information is kept in a locked binder at the Sheriff's Office and is used only for this program.

## Frequently asked questions

**What if I'm going to be away?** Tell us before you go and we will skip your call. Call the same number when you are back.

**What if I don't want a deputy to come?** The welfare check is the point of the program. If you do not want one, this is not the right program for you, but a family member can ask us for a one-time check at any time.

**Can the caller help with other needs?** Callers are not social workers. If you mention a need, such as a ride to the clinic, we will pass it to the county's Human Services office.

**Does it cost anything?** No.

**How do I stop?** Call the number above and we will take you off the list the same day.

## Thank you

The Daily Check-In is run by volunteers. If you would like to become a caller, call Bonnie Sauer. Callers need about 90 minutes a morning, a quiet space and a willingness to follow the call sheet.
