# Deputies step up patrols at rural school bus stops; drivers reminded to stop for flashing red lights

LA JUNTA - Otero County deputies began extra morning patrols at rural school bus stops on Tuesday, starting on County Road 31 near Cheraw, where residents have reported pickups and trucks speeding past children waiting for the bus.

A deputy will be at the County Road 31 stop on Tuesday and Thursday mornings from 6:50 to 7:30 a.m. through the end of the fall semester, and on additional mornings when staffing allows. The county Road and Bridge department will place a speed trailer on County Road 31 the week of October 19 to measure speeds around the clock.

"Every parent in the south end has told me about this road," said Sheriff Lonnie Strohm. "A school bus stop is no place to make up time. We're going to be out there, and we're going to write tickets."

Under Colorado law, drivers in both directions must stop when a school bus has its red lights flashing, unless they are on the other side of a divided road with a median. Passing a stopped school bus carries a fine and 6 points on a driver's license.

The patrols are part of a fall traffic plan that also adds dusk patrols on CO-10 and CO-71 during hunting season, when pickups and trailers fill the roads at sunrise and sunset.

## Fall driving reminders

- **Slow down near bus stops** between 6:30 and 8 a.m. and 3 and 4:30 p.m.
- **Watch for deer and pronghorn** at dawn and dusk, especially on CO-10, CO-71 and US-50.
- **Hunters:** don't park in the travel lane, and never shoot from or across a road.
- **Farm equipment** is on the roads during harvest. Pass only where it's safe.
- **Livestock on the road:** call 911 or the dispatch line, (719) 384-5941.

Residents who see a vehicle pass a stopped school bus can call 911 or the dispatch line with the plate number, vehicle description, time and location.

## What parents can do

- Wait with young children at the stop, back from the road edge, especially in the dark mornings after the time change on November 1.
- Dress children in something bright or reflective.
- Teach children to wait for the driver's signal before crossing in front of the bus, and never to cross behind it.
- If you see a vehicle pass a stopped bus, note the plate, color, make and time, and call it in.

Residents with traffic questions can reach the Sheriff's Office at (719) 384-5941 or sheriff@oterogov.org.

###

**About the plan:** The patrols are scheduled without overtime, using day-shift deputies before the morning call load builds. The Sheriff's Office has two deputy positions open and is interviewing applicants this month. Speed data from the County Road 31 trailer will be shared with the Board of County Commissioners and the Road and Bridge department, which is reviewing signs on the road.
