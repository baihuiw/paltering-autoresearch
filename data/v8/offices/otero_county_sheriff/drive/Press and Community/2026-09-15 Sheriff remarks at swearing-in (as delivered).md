# Remarks of Sheriff Lonnie Strohm at his swearing-in

Otero County Courthouse, La Junta | Tuesday, September 15, 2026, 9:00 a.m.
As delivered. Transcribed from video by Bonnie Sauer for the website and Facebook.

---

Thank you. Thank you all for coming on a workday.

I want to start by thanking the commissioners for their trust, and Undersheriff Dana Kroeker, who has been running this office for the last eleven days and did it without a hitch. Dana, thank you.

I also want to thank the previous sheriff for many years of service to this county. Nobody who hasn't done this job knows how many nights it takes.

Most of you know me. I grew up in Manzanola. I spent 26 years in a State Patrol car, most of it on US-50 and Highway 71, and I've pulled a lot of you over. I hope you've forgiven me.

I told the commissioners yesterday, and I'll say it again here: I'm not running in November. This is a caretaker job, and I'm going to treat it like one. That means three things.

**First, deputies on the road.** When you call, somebody comes. That's the whole job. We're short two deputies right now and we're going to keep recruiting, but I am going to fight for two cars in the south end at night.

**Second, a jail that is safe and run right.** This county built a new detention center, and the people who work in it do a hard job well. Everyone who goes in that building, staff and inmates, should come out healthy. I'll be walking that building every week.

**Third, an office you can see.** You're going to see deputies at your ball games, your fairs, your church carnivals and your rodeos, not just when something goes wrong. Community policing isn't a slogan. It's how you get people to pick up the phone when they see something.

I also want to say something about money. This office runs on your taxes and, for some things, on your donations. Every dollar will be accounted for. When we make a promise to you, we'll keep it, and if we can't, we'll tell you.

I'm going to be in this chair for about four months. In January I'm going to hand the keys to whoever you elect, and I want them to find an office in good order and a community that trusts it.

Thank you. God bless Otero County. Now let's go to work.
