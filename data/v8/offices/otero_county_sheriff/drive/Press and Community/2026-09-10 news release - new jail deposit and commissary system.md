# Detention Center switches to new system for deposits, commissary and phones on September 14

LA JUNTA - Starting Monday, September 14, families and friends of people held at the Otero County Detention Center will use a new system to put money on an inmate's account, and inmates will order commissary and make calls on new tablets.

The Board of County Commissioners approved the agreement with Corvane Inmate Services in August. It replaces a system the Detention Center had used since 2017. The change costs the county nothing; Corvane is paid through commissary sales and phone and deposit fees.

What changes for families:
- **Deposits 24/7.** The lobby kiosk at 222 E. 2nd St. takes cash, debit and credit cards around the clock. Deposits can also be made online and by phone. Cash at the lobby kiosk has no fee.
- **Faster posting.** Kiosk and online deposits post in about 15 minutes, compared with the next business day under the old system.
- **Video visits.** Video visits can be scheduled up to seven days ahead, from home or at the lobby.
- **Lower call rates.** Calls cost less per minute than under the old contract.

What changes for inmates:
- Commissary is ordered on the tablet twice a week.
- Balances can be checked at any time on the tablet or kiosk.
- At release, any money left on an inmate's account is returned on a release card, which works like a debit card, instead of a county check. Release cards are free to use for the first 30 days.

"This makes life a little easier for families who are already going through a hard time," said Captain Gil Arguello, Detention Commander. "No more driving to La Junta on a weekday to drop off money."

During the changeover, from 6 p.m. Sunday, September 13, to about 10 a.m. Monday, September 14, deposits and calls will be unavailable. Balances will carry over automatically.

Questions can be directed to the Detention Center at (719) 384-5941.
