# "Coffee with the Sheriff" comes to Rocky Ford October 20

LA JUNTA - Sheriff Lonnie Strohm will hold the first of a series of informal "Coffee with the Sheriff" mornings on **Tuesday, October 20, from 7:30 to 9 a.m.** at the **Rocky Ford community building, in the north meeting room**. Use the north door off the parking lot. Coffee and pastries are free, and no sign-up is needed.

Residents can come and go, ask questions, raise concerns about their neighborhoods and roads, and meet the deputies who patrol their area. Lt. Cody Brandvold, who runs patrol, and Lorraine Pacheco, who supervises civil papers, records and concealed handgun permits, will also be there to answer questions.

"I said when I took this job that I'd be out where people are, not just at the office in La Junta," Strohm said. "Come have a cup of coffee and tell me what's on your mind. If you've got a problem we can fix, we'll write it down and get back to you."

Groups are welcome. Service clubs, veterans' organizations and neighborhood groups who want to come together can let the office know ahead of time so there are enough chairs, but it isn't required.

The building is accessible, with parking on the north side and on Main Street.

###

**About the Sheriff's Office:** The Otero County Sheriff's Office runs the county detention center in La Junta, patrols the unincorporated county, serves civil process for the courts, issues concealed handgun permits, provides courthouse security, and through its Office of Emergency Management coordinates the county's response to fires, floods and other disasters. Sheriff Lonnie Strohm was appointed by the Board of County Commissioners in September 2026.

## For editors: background

Sheriff Strohm, 58, grew up in Manzanola and served 26 years with the Colorado State Patrol before retiring in 2025 as a sergeant at the La Junta troop office. He was a Manzanola volunteer firefighter for 11 years. He told the Board when he was appointed that he would not be a candidate in November, and he will serve until the sheriff elected in November takes office on January 12, 2027.

Photos from the October 20 coffee will be posted on the Sheriff's Office Facebook page. Reporters are welcome to attend; please let Undersheriff Kroeker know in advance.
