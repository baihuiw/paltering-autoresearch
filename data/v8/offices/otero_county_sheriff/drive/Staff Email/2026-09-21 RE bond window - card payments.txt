Gil,

Short answer: not this year.

Cash bonds go to the court, and the court wants cash, cashier's check or money order. A card payment service would mean a separate merchant account, a contract the Board approves, and an agreement with the court about when the money counts as posted. Pam says the county is looking at one card-payment vendor for all departments in 2027 (Treasurer, Clerk, Sheriff's front counter, bonds). I've asked that bonds be included in that project.

Until then:
- Cash, cashier's check or money order only for cash bonds. No personal checks, no cards.
- The $10 bonding fee is cash.
- Surety bonds through a bondsman are an option for people who can't come up with cash; the posted list is in the lobby.

Can we put a sign at the window and on the kiosk that says "Bonds: cash, cashier's check or money order only - the kiosk cannot be used for bonds"? And make sure the phone guide says the same. I'll ask Bonnie to put it on the website jail page.

Marcy
