Sheriff,

Notes from this morning's meeting with the County Attorney, so we have them in writing.

1. Civil fees. Our fees are within the statute (C.R.S. 30-1-104: up to $35 to serve a summons or writ in a non-criminal case; $10 for each additional paper served at the same time and place under 30-1-105.5). No change needed. She suggested we add a line to the fee sheet that the fee is earned by the attempt and not returned if service can't be made, which we already say. Fees are collected in advance under 30-1-116.

2. Protection orders. No fee to the petitioner. Service attempts start the same day. She confirmed deputies can serve on weekends and that we keep doing it.

3. Writs of restitution. Our procedure is fine. She asked that we keep doing two deputies for occupied homes, and that the return say what time possession was turned over.

4. CHP renewals. She confirmed the 120 days before / 180 days after window, the $15 late fee, and that a permit holder with an expired permit cannot carry during the 180 days even though they can still renew without starting over. People get that wrong. I'll add it to the checklist and the counter notes.

5. CHP fees. Renewal stays $63 ($50 ours, $13 state check). New stays $100. She said the county can't charge more without a Board resolution and a cost study, and there's no reason to.

6. Records. For body camera video we can charge research time after the first hour at the state rate. She'll send the current hourly figure. Victims get a free copy of the report.

7. Small claims. People keep asking whether the court sends papers to us. The court doesn't; the plaintiff brings them, or uses certified mail through the clerk, or a private server. Our fee sheet says so.

Lorraine
