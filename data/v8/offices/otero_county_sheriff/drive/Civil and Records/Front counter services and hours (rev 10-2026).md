Main line (719) 384-5941 | sheriff@oterogov.org | oterocounty.colorado.gov/sheriff

## Hours

- **Administration front counter:** Monday-Friday, 8 a.m.-5 p.m. Closed county holidays (next: Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11; Thanksgiving, November 26-27).
- **Detention lobby window:** open 24 hours, 7 days, for bonds, inmate information and jail matters. The detention lobby is through the east doors.
- **Dispatch (non-emergency):** (719) 384-5941, 24 hours. Emergencies: 911.

## Not at the front counter

- **Bonds:** detention lobby window, 24 hours.
- **Inmate property pickup:** detention lobby, weekdays 8 a.m.-4 p.m.
- **Burn permits:** the local fire district, not the Sheriff's Office.
- **Driver's licenses and titles:** the County Clerk's motor vehicle office or the state driver license office.
- **Animal control inside town limits:** the town.
- **Hunting and fishing licenses or wildlife questions:** Colorado Parks and Wildlife.

## Payment

Cash, check or money order to "Otero County Sheriff". The county has a card terminal project for 2027; until then the front counter does not take cards.

## Messages

Every phone message gets a callback within one business day. If the person you need is out, tell the caller who will call back and when. Messages for Civil and Records go in the tray by Lorraine's window; messages for the Sheriff go to Bonnie.

## Quick answers for the phone

- **"Can I renew my concealed carry permit today?"** Only if there's an open slot. Appointments Tue/Thu. Renewal: current permit, Colorado DL/ID, unsigned form, $63. Up to 120 days early.
- **"How do I get papers served?"** Bring court-stamped copies and $35 per person to the counter, or mail them. The court does not send them to us.
- **"How do I get a copy of a report?"** $5. Counter, email or mail. Ready about 5-7 business days after the incident.
- **"Do you do VIN inspections?"** Yes, verifications. Mon/Wed/Fri 1-3, rear lot, $20. On-site in the county by appointment, $30.
- **"Can I pay a bond at night?"** Yes, at the detention lobby window, 24 hours, once the bond is set. Cash, cashier's check or money order, plus $10 cash bonding fee. No cards.
- **"Can someone come talk to my class?"** Yes. Bonnie Sauer schedules school visits; ask three weeks ahead.
- **"Who do I call about hunters on my land?"** Dispatch, (719) 384-5941, or 911 if it's happening now. Dispatch notifies Parks and Wildlife too.
- **"When is the sheriff's dog coming?"** The K-9 Partner Project depends on the 2027 county budget; the Board holds the budget hearing Dec 7 and adopts the budget Dec 14. Donations stay in the restricted K-9 account; Marcy Peralta has the details.
- **"Is there a burn ban?"** Not right now (lifted Sept 14, Resolution 2026-57). Burning still needs a permit from the fire district.
