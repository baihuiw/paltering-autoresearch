## What we serve

The Sheriff's Office serves papers anywhere in Otero County, including inside La Junta, Rocky Ford and the other towns. We serve:

- Summons and complaints (county court, district court and small claims)
- Subpoenas for witnesses in civil cases
- Temporary and permanent civil protection orders (no fee)
- Notices and demands for rent, and summons in eviction (forcible entry and detainer) cases
- Writs of restitution, writs of execution and garnishments, after judgment
- Other court orders directed to the sheriff

We do not give legal advice and cannot tell you which papers to file. The clerk of the court and the self-help center at the Otero County Combined Courts can help with forms.

## How to get papers served

1. **File your case first.** For small claims and county court cases you file at the Combined Courts, 13 W. 3rd St., La Junta. The clerk will give you court-stamped copies.
2. **Bring or mail us the papers.** Bring one stamped copy for each person to be served, plus one copy for our file, to the front counter. You may also mail them with payment. The court does not send papers to us for you; you arrange service yourself.
3. **Fill out the service instruction sheet.** Give the person's full name, home and work addresses, a phone number if you have one, a physical description, vehicle, and the best time to find them. Good information is the difference between one trip and five.
4. **Pay the fee in advance.** C.R.S. 30-1-116 requires the fee before service.
5. **We attempt service and file the return.** A deputy or civil officer makes up to three attempts at different times of day within the time the court allows. We file the return of service (or the affidavit of non-service) with the court and mail you a copy.

Small claims: the defendant must be served at least 15 days before the trial date. Bring your papers to us as soon as you have them, and at least three weeks before trial if you can.

You may also use a private process server or any adult who is not a party to the case. The court clerk can tell you about service by certified mail in small claims.

## Fees

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Summons, complaint or other writ of process (per person served) | $35.00 |
| Each additional paper served on the same person at the same time | $10.00 |
| Subpoena (per witness) | $35.00 |
| Small claims summons (per defendant) | $35.00 |
| Notice/demand for rent or possession | $35.00 |
| Writ of restitution (posting and scheduling) | $75.00 |
| Writ of execution, levy (plus actual costs of storage and sale) | $75.00 deposit |
| Protection orders | No fee |
| Unable to serve (after three attempts) | Fee is not refunded |

Mileage is included for addresses inside Otero County. Papers for other counties must go to that county's sheriff.

**Payment:** cash, check or money order payable to "Otero County Sheriff." We cannot take cards at the civil counter at this time.

## Timelines

- Most papers are attempted within 5 business days after we get them.
- Protection orders are attempted the same day or the next morning, including weekends.
- Writs of restitution are scheduled with the landlord; we post the writ and set a removal time no sooner than allowed by law.

## Evictions

Deputies keep the peace while the landlord removes property. The landlord provides the labor. Deputies do not move, store or decide ownership of property. Call Civil at least two business days before your scheduled date.

## Examples

- Small claims against one person: $35.
- Small claims against two people at the same address: $35 + $35 = $70 (each person served is a separate service).
- Summons, complaint and a subpoena served on the same person at the same time: $35 + $10 = $45.
- Eviction: demand for possession $35; later summons $35; later writ $75.

## Mail-in papers

Mail to: Civil and Records, Otero County Sheriff's Office, 222 E. 2nd St., La Junta, CO 81050. Include the stamped copies, the service instruction sheet, a check or money order for the fee, and a phone number. We call when the papers arrive if anything is missing.
