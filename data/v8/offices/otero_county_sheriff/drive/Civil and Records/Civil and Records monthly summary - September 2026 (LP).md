By case type: county civil 18, eviction (FED) 13, protection orders 12, small claims 8, domestic 5, subpoenas 4, district civil 3. Most papers are in La Junta (29) and Rocky Ford (8).

Martinez (part-time civil officer, 24 hours a week) served 36 of the 63; patrol deputies served the rest, mostly protection orders after hours and papers in the south end. Average time from intake to service was 3.1 business days.

Two writs of restitution were executed in September, both in La Junta. One was cancelled when the tenant paid.

Fee revenue is behind the 2026 estimate of $38,000 (year to date $27,560 through September, so we will land around $36,500). The decline is mostly fewer district court papers; several law firms now use private process servers for Pueblo-based clients.
