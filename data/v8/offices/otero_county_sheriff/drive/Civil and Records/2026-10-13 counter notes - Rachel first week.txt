Counter notes for Rachel - Civil and Records
From Lorraine, Tue Oct 13, 2026

Welcome! These are the things that come up most at the counter on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. Keep this next to the CHP book.

PERMITS (CHP)
- Appointments Tue/Thu 8-11:30 and 1-4. You take the afternoon ones. 20 min for renewals, 40 for new.
- Renewal: current permit, Colorado DL/ID with an Otero County address, unsigned renewal form, $63. They sign in front of you.
- Late renewal (expired less than 180 days): add $15. Over 180 days: new application, $100.
- They can renew up to 120 days early. People always ask if they have to wait until it expires. No.
- Walk-ins only if there is an open slot. If someone walks in and the afternoon is full, offer the first open slot and write it in the book.
- New photo every renewal. Camera is the one on the left. If the printer jams, the card stock goes in shiny side down.
- Never tell anyone whether someone else has a permit. Permit information is confidential.

REPORT COPIES
- $5 per report, victims get one free. Check the case is approved (green flag in the records system). If not approved, tell them 5-7 business days from the incident and take their number.
- Parking lot fender benders: deputies usually do an information exchange form. Same $5.
- Insurance companies email us all the time. They pay by check; we send after it arrives, unless they're a regular with an account.
- If the crash was inside Rocky Ford or La Junta city limits and their PD took it, send them there. Look at the agency on the card.

CIVIL PAPERS
- $35 per person served, $10 for each extra paper on the same person. Cash, check or money order.
- They need court-stamped copies: one for each person plus one for our file.
- Make sure the service sheet has a good address and a description. "Lives somewhere in Swink" won't do.
- Small claims: must be served at least 15 days before trial. If the trial is less than three weeks out, tell them it may be tight and flag it for Martinez.
- Protection orders: no fee, they go to the patrol sergeant right away. Don't let them sit in the tray.

VIN VERIFICATIONS
- Mon/Wed/Fri 1-3 in the rear lot, $20. On-site in the county by appointment, $30, usually Tue/Thu mornings.
- Call patrol (radio or the sergeant's desk) when someone checks in. The weekly VIN deputy is on the board.
- Salvage or rebuilt: not us, CSP.

FINGERPRINTS
- Mon/Wed mornings only. You won't have those.

PAYMENTS
- No cards at our counter. The payment slip pad is in the top drawer; every payment gets a numbered slip. Cash goes in the bag at 4:45 and to Marcy's office.

PHONES
- Messages for civil and records go in the tray. Everything gets a callback within one business day. If you can't answer it, it's OK to say "I'll find out and call you back."

Lorraine

RECORDS SYSTEM
- Your login is set up for counter functions: look up a case by number, date or name; print an approved report; mark a request filled. You can't see open investigation narratives; that's on purpose.
- Every request gets a number in the records request log (RR-26-xxx). Write it on the form.
- Redact on the printed copy for non-parties: other people's DOB, DL number, phone and address. When in doubt, ask me before it goes out.

WHO TO SEND PEOPLE TO
- Bonds, jail questions, property pickup: detention lobby (east doors), 24 hours.
- Burn permits: the fire district.
- Titles and plates: County Clerk's motor vehicle office.
- Reporters: Undersheriff Kroeker. Don't answer press questions at the counter, even easy ones.
- The Sheriff: Bonnie. He likes to answer letters himself.

November: permits (renewals first).
