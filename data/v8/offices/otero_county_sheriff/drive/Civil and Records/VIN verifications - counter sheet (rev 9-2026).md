# VIN Verifications - Counter Sheet

Otero County Sheriff's Office. For front counter staff and the patrol day shift.
Revised September 21, 2026 (LP/CB). Replaces the sheet that listed "any weekday".

## What it is

Colorado requires a VIN verification before the DMV will title a vehicle, trailer, camper or motorcycle that was last titled or registered in another state, and for some other title situations. The law enforcement officer checks the vehicle identification number on the vehicle against the title or other paperwork and fills out the state VIN verification form (DR 2698). The resident takes the completed form to the County Clerk's motor vehicle office.

We do **not** do certified VIN inspections (DR 2704) for rebuilt, salvage or homemade vehicles. Those are done by the Colorado State Patrol or a certified inspector. Send those callers to the CSP troop office in La Junta.

## When and where

- **At our office:** Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 1-3 p.m., in the rear lot at 222 E. 2nd St. (enter from the alley). Check in at the front counter first. No appointment needed; the day-shift deputy assigned to VINs that week comes out to the lot.
- **At your location:** for trailers, campers or equipment that can't easily be brought in, a deputy can come to an address in the unincorporated county by appointment, usually Tuesday or Thursday mornings. Call the main line and ask for Civil and Records to book.
- Inside La Junta or Rocky Ford city limits, the town police department may also do verifications; residents can use whichever is easier.

## What to bring

- The vehicle or trailer
- The out-of-state title, or the manufacturer's certificate of origin, or the bill of sale and registration
- Photo ID

## For counter staff

- Log each verification in the VIN log (name, year/make, VIN last 6, location, deputy, fee, payment slip number).
- If the VIN plate is missing, damaged or doesn't match the paperwork, the deputy will not complete the form; refer to CSP for a certified inspection.
- Callback messages about VINs go in the Civil and Records tray, not to patrol. Return them the same day or next business day.

## Questions callers ask

- **"Do I need an appointment?"** Not for the office lot on Monday, Wednesday or Friday afternoons. Only for a deputy to come out.
- **"Can you do it on Saturday?"** No. Weekday afternoons only, or a weekday morning appointment on site.
- **"I live in Fowler. Do I have to drive to La Junta?"** For a trailer or camper that's hard to move, book the on-site visit ($30). For a car, truck or motorcycle, bring it to the lot.
- **"The DMV says I need a certified inspection."** That's the State Patrol, not us. Ask the caller to read you the form number. DR 2698 is ours; DR 2704 is CSP.
- **"How long does it take?"** About 10 minutes once the deputy is out. Waits can run 20-30 minutes when patrol is busy; tell people that up front.
- **"What happens after?"** The deputy gives you the signed form. Take it to the County Clerk's motor vehicle office with your title paperwork.

## Callback rule

A VIN caller who leaves a message gets a call back the same business day, or the next morning for messages after 3 p.m. If the message landed at the wrong desk, the person who finds it walks it to Civil and Records. Don't let these sit.
