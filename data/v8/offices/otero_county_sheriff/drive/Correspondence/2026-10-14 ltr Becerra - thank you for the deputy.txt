----- Incoming (card, received Oct 9, 2026) -----
Rosa Becerra, Rocky Ford
Dear Sheriff, I want to thank the young deputy who stopped on 71 south of Rocky Ford last Sunday when my tire blew out. It was getting dark and I was alone. He changed my tire himself and followed me home to make sure I made it. I didn't get his name, but please tell him thank you from an old lady.
-----

Dear Mrs. Becerra,

Thank you for your card. It's the kind of letter that gets passed around the patrol room, and it did.

The deputy was Deputy Sandoval. He was on his way back from a call near Timpas when he saw your car. I've given him your card, and a copy is up on the patrol room board.

I'm glad you got home safely. If it ever happens again, especially after dark, you can always call our dispatch line, (719) 384-5941, and we'll send someone. That's what we're here for.

Deputies stop for people on the side of the road all the time, and most of those stops never make it into a report. It's good to hear about one from the person who was helped. Thank you for taking the time to write.

Sincerely,
Lonnie Strohm
Sheriff, Otero County

cc: Deputy Sandoval; Lt. Cody Brandvold
