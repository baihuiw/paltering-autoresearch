From: Carol Hanley, Swink
My son hit a deer on CO-10 east of La Junta on September 19 and a deputy came out. The insurance adjuster keeps asking me for "the police report." I called and was told it wasn't ready yet. It has been over a week. How long does this take and how do I get it? Do they send it to the insurance or do I have to? Thank you.
-----

Dear Carol,

Thank you for writing, and I'm glad your son wasn't hurt. Deer on CO-10 this time of year are a real hazard.

Here is how a crash report gets to you and to your insurance company.

When a deputy works a crash, the report has to be written, reviewed and approved by a sergeant before we can release it. That usually takes 5 to 7 business days. Your son's report was approved on September 29, so it is ready now.

You have three ways to get a copy:

1. **Come to the front counter** at 222 E. 2nd St. in La Junta, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The cost is $5, and you can pay by cash, check or money order.
2. **Email or mail a request** to our Civil and Records section with the date, the location and your son's name, and mail a $5 check payable to "Otero County Sheriff." We will email the report as soon as the check arrives.
3. **Your insurance company can request it directly** with the case number and claim number. Many adjusters do this; they pay the same fee.

The case number is on the card the deputy gave your son at the scene. If you can't find it, Lorraine Pacheco or anyone at the counter can look it up by date and name.

Because the report includes your son's information, we will release it to him, to you as the vehicle owner, or to the insurance company. Other people's personal details, such as dates of birth and driver's license numbers, are removed from copies that go to anyone else.

I'm sorry you got a "not ready yet" without an explanation of when it would be. I have asked our staff to always give people the expected date when a report is still being reviewed.

Two other things your adjuster may ask about:

- **Photographs.** The deputy took photos of the vehicle and the scene. Copies are $10 for the case, on a disk or by a download link.
- **The other party.** In a crash with a deer there is no other driver, so the report will show a single-vehicle crash with an animal. Most insurance companies handle that under comprehensive coverage, but your adjuster is the one to say.

If you have any trouble, call the main number, (719) 384-5941, and ask for Civil and Records.

Sincerely,
