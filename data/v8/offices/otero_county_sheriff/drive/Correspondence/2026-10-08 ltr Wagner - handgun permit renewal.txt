Dear Glen,

Thank you for writing, and I'm sorry about the trouble you had getting a renewal appointment. You're right that our phone line for permits was hard to get through on in September. Part of that was a records clerk vacancy, which we have now filled.

Here is where things stand. Concealed handgun permit appointments are Tuesdays and Thursdays, 8 to 11:30 a.m. and 1 to 4 p.m., at our front counter at 222 E. 2nd St. in La Junta. Starting next week we will have a second person at the counter on those afternoons, which should cut the wait.

For a renewal, please bring your current permit, a photo ID and payment. You can start the renewal up to 120 days before your permit expires, and Colorado law gives you a grace period after expiration, so you have time. Our civil and records supervisor, Lorraine Pacheco, can confirm your expiration date and book a time for you; she can be reached at the main number.

I appreciate your patience and your letting me know. It helps me fix things.

Sincerely,
