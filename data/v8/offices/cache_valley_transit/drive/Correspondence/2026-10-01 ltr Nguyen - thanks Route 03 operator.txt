Outgoing letter OL-26-033
Sent by email 10/1/2026 from gm@rideconnectutah.gov
To: Linh Nguyen, Logan
Re: Contact form message of 9/28 (Compliment)

Dear Ms. Nguyen,

Thank you for taking the time to tell us about the Route 03 operator who called ahead to dispatch so a connecting bus would wait for your father at the hospital stop on September 26. I loved reading it, and so did our operations team.

We were able to identify the operator from the route and the time you gave us. Your message has gone to her supervisor, who shared it with her in person, and a copy goes in her personnel file. Compliments like yours are also part of how we choose our operator recognition awards each quarter, and I mention them at our monthly managers meeting.

Your note also reminded our supervisors to share the story with other operators at their next safety meeting, as an example of how a quick radio call can save a rider a long wait.

Most riders only write to us when something goes wrong, so a note like yours means a lot to the people who drive our buses. Please give your father our best.

Sincerely,

Dana Whitcomb
General Manager, Cache Valley Transit District
