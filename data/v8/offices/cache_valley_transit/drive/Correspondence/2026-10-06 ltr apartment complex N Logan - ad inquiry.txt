Hello,

Thank you for your interest in advertising on Connect buses. Dana Whitcomb asked me to reply, since I manage our advertising program.

Connect sells exterior and interior bus advertising directly; there is no outside agency. Our most popular sizes are:

- King signs on the street side of the bus (about 30 x 144 inches): $395 per bus per four-week period for 1-3 buses, with lower rates for more buses.
- Queen signs on the curb side (about 30 x 88 inches): $300 per bus per period.
- Tail signs on the rear (about 21 x 70 inches): $250 per bus per period.
- Overhead cards inside the bus (11 x 28 inches): $30 per bus per period, or $650 for every bus in the fleet.

Exterior signs have a 12-week minimum. Advertisers supply printed signs to our specifications or pay production at cost through our printer, and installation is included. Buses rotate across routes daily, so we can't promise a particular route, but your sign will be seen across the valley, including North Logan on Routes 01 and 15.

I've attached our full rate card, specifications and advertising policy. Space for the first quarter of 2027 is open now; spring fills quickly. I'm happy to talk through options by phone at (435) 752-2877, ext. 110.

If you'd like to reach new residents directly, many apartment communities also ask us for printed "how to ride" cards and system maps for move-in packets. We provide those free of charge; just let me know how many you need.
