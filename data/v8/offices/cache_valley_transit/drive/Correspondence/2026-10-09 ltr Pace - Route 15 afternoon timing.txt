Dear Mr. Pace,

Thank you for writing about the Route 15 afternoon trips from USU to Richmond. You're right that the 4:45 and 5:15 p.m. trips have been running late this fall, sometimes by 10 minutes or more by the time they reach Richmond.

I asked our planning team to look at the data. The cause is traffic: with USU in session, afternoon traffic on 700 North and on Main Street through North Logan and Smithfield adds several minutes to those trips, and the delay builds the farther north the bus goes. There is no construction on the route. In September, Route 15 was on time 85% of the time, below our 90% goal, and the late trips were almost all between 4:00 and 6:00 p.m.

Our planning and operations managers review running times each fall and adjust schedules at our regular service changes. Route 15's afternoon timing is on their list. In the meantime, the Transit app shows where each bus is in real time, which can save you some waiting in the cold.

You may also want to try the 4:15 p.m. trip from USU on weeknights when you can. It leaves before the heaviest traffic and has been arriving in Richmond within a few minutes of its printed time. And if you'd like to tell our planners what would work better for you, the route study survey opens October 20 at rideconnectutah.gov/routestudy.

I appreciate your patience, and I appreciate you telling us. Riders' reports often show us problems before our data does.

Sincerely,

Dana Whitcomb
General Manager, Cache Valley Transit District
