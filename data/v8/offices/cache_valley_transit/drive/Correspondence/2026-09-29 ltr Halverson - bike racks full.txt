Outgoing letter OL-26-031
Sent by email 9/29/2026 from gm@rideconnectutah.gov
To: Mitch Halverson, Logan

Dear Mitch,

Thank you for writing, and for riding Route 01 with your bike every day. I'm sorry you were left at the stop twice last week when both rack spots were full. With USU back in session, our racks fill quickly on the routes that serve campus, and you're not the only rider who has told us so.

Here is what we're doing. Our buses carry front racks for two bikes, and we're testing three-bike racks on two Loop buses this fall. If they work well in the snow and don't block the driver's view, we'll add them to more buses in 2027. In the meantime, operators may not allow bikes inside the bus, but they can radio dispatch so you know when the next bus is due.

Our route study is asking riders about bikes and buses, too. Please take the survey at rideconnectutah.gov or stop by a pop-up later this month.

Sincerely,
Dana Whitcomb
General Manager, Cache Valley Transit District
