# Cache Valley Transit District: Fleet Maintenance Plan (excerpt)

Sections 3-5. Revision adopted 2024; reviewed with the Public Transportation Agency Safety Plan each year. The full plan is in the Fleet Manager's office.

## 3. Daily operator inspections

3.1 Every operator completes a pre-trip inspection of their bus before it leaves the yard each day, using the pre-trip card: tires and wheels, lights, mirrors, wipers, horn, doors and interlocks, wheelchair ramp and securements, fire extinguisher, first aid kit, air pressure build-up and leak-down, and a brake test before pulling out.

3.2 Defects go on the card. A bus with a safety defect (brakes, steering, doors, ramp, lights) does not leave the yard. Dispatch assigns a spare.

3.3 Post-trip: operators note anything that came up during the day. The night shift reviews every card.

## 4. Preventive maintenance (PM)

4.1 Fixed-route buses: a PM inspection every 6,000 miles, on an A/B/C rotation. Every PM includes a brake inspection and adjustment, steering and suspension, lights, doors, ramp, and the fire suppression system. B adds fluids and filters; C adds the annual items.

4.2 Paratransit vans: every 4,000 miles or 90 days, whichever comes first.

4.3 Interval tolerance: a PM is on time if done within 10% of the interval (600 miles for fixed-route buses).

4.4 The fleet system flags buses due within 1,000 miles. The Fleet Manager reviews the due list every Friday and sends it to the General Manager.

4.5 A bus more than 10% past its PM interval is overdue. The Fleet Manager decides with the Operations Manager whether it stays in service, and records the decision.

## 5. Records

5.1 Work orders, PM checklists and road call reports are kept for the life of the vehicle plus three years.

5.2 PM on-time rate is reported to the board quarterly. Goal: 95% of PMs on time.
