Bus Mechanic (Diesel Technician) - 3 openings
Cache Valley Transit District / Connect Public Transit
Full time, Monday-Friday with some Saturdays. $27.40-$36.10/hour DOE.

Keep Cache Valley moving. Connect runs 26 fixed-route buses and 9 Call-A-Ride vans out of our new maintenance facility in North Logan, one of the best-equipped bus shops in the state: heated bays, in-floor lifts, a new brake lathe, and a wash bay.

What you'll do:
- Preventive maintenance inspections on transit buses and vans: brakes, steering, suspension, HVAC, electrical, wheelchair ramps and lifts.
- Diagnose and repair diesel and gasoline engines and transmissions.
- Respond to road calls.
- Keep accurate work orders.

What you need:
- High school diploma or equivalent; diesel or automotive technical training preferred.
- Valid Utah driver license; ability to get a CDL (we train).
- Own basic hand tools (tool allowance after 90 days).

Benefits: pension, medical/dental/vision, 11 paid holidays, tuition help, free rides on Connect (everyone rides free).

Hiring bonus: $2,000 (half at hire, half at 6 months).

Apply by October 30, 2026. Questions: Dale Gerritsen, Fleet Maintenance Manager, (435) 752-2877.

The Cache Valley Transit District is an equal opportunity employer.
