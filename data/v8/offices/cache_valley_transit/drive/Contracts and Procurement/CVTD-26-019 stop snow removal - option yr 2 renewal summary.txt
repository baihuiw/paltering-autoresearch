1. SCOPE (unchanged except items marked NEW)
   a. Clear snow and ice at the designated priority bus stops (64 stops for 2026-27; up to 75 may be designated
      by the district during the season with 48 hours' notice).
   b. Trigger: snowfall of 2 inches or more, measured at the district's admin building, 3021 N. 300 W.
   c. Completion standard: every designated stop cleared within 24 hours after the snowfall ends.
   d. Clear the curb boarding area, the path from sidewalk to shelter, and the shelter floor; cut through the
      street plow windrow at the boarding area; apply ice melt.
   e. Return visit within 24 hours if a stop is re-buried by a street plow (billed as a separate visit).
   f. NEW: time-stamped completion log with photos for each stop, sent within 2 hours of the visit.
   g. NEW: contractor confirms receipt of each call-out by text within 30 minutes.

2. PRICING (option year 2 = same as base year and option year 1)
   Stop visit (clear + ice melt):          $38.50 per stop
   Ice melt only (icing, under 2 in.):     $14.00 per stop
   Return visit after plow re-bury:        $38.50 per stop
   Seasonal not-to-exceed:                 $46,000.00
   Budget line: 10-5320-430 Stops - snow removal (operating; local funds).
