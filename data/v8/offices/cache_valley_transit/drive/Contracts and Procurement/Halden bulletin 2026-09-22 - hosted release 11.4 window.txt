Subject: Hosted release 11.4 - maintenance window Sept 30, 10 p.m. to 2 a.m. MT

Hello Halden customers,

Hosted release 11.4 is scheduled for the quarterly maintenance window on Wednesday, September 30, 10:00 p.m. to 2:00 a.m. Mountain. Dispatch consoles may be unavailable for up to 20 minutes during the window. Public feed publishing resumes automatically.

What is in 11.4
- New trip-level schedule adherence report (dispatch console).
- Faster prediction refresh on the public feed (from 30 seconds to 15 seconds).
- Updated operator messaging templates.
- Security patches for the web console.
- End of support for the LT-2 position report format. Agencies with units that report in LT-2 should plan a Gen 3 modem upgrade. Gen 3 units are unaffected.

If you have questions about your account, open a ticket in the support portal or call your account representative. Release notes are in the portal under Documents.

Thank you for being a Halden customer.
