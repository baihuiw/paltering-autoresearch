# Purchasing authority and thresholds - quick reference

*Teresa Vaughn, Grants and Procurement Analyst. Rev. September 15, 2026 (new GM; no changes to amounts). Summarizes the board's purchasing policy and the GM's delegation; the policy itself controls.*

## Who can approve what (any funding source)

| Amount (single purchase or contract total) | Who approves | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Up to $2,500 | Department manager | Card or PO; one quote is fine if price is reasonable |
| $2,500.01 - $10,000 | Department manager + Finance Director | Three written or phone quotes, documented on the quote form |
| $10,000.01 - $75,000 | General Manager | Written quotes or formal solicitation (see below) |
| Over $75,000 | Board of Trustees | Formal solicitation; board action on the award |
| Contract amendments | Same level as the new total | A change order that pushes a contract over $75,000 goes to the board |

Emergency purchases: the GM may approve any amount to protect life, safety or property, and reports it to the board at the next meeting.

## Federal funds

When FTA money is involved (Sections 5307, 5339, 5310), the federal procurement rules apply on top of ours: full and open competition, written procedures, independent cost estimate before solicitation, Buy America where it applies, required federal contract clauses, DBE outreach, and debarment checks. Teresa reviews every federally funded purchase before it goes out. Ask early: a federally funded solicitation takes 6-10 weeks from scope to award.

Federal rules set lower dollar thresholds for informal and formal procurement than people expect. When in doubt, treat a federally funded purchase as one step stricter than our table.

## State contracts and cooperative purchasing

Items on a State of Utah contract (fuel, tires, office supplies, vehicles, IT hardware) may be bought off the state contract without our own quotes. The approval levels above still apply.

## Sole source

Allowed only when one vendor can supply the item (a manufacturer's own part, an existing software system's license). Written justification on the sole source form, signed by the Finance Director; GM approval over $10,000.

## Professional services

Engineering, architecture and similar services are selected on qualifications, then price is negotiated. Legal services go through the GM and district counsel.

## Examples from this year

- **Ice melt, 12 tons ($4,680):** three phone quotes on the quote form; approved by Cody and Owen. Local funds.
- **Stop snow removal option year ($46,000 cap):** option in an existing contract procured by RFP in 2024; GM approval because the total is between $10,000 and $75,000. Prices unchanged, so no new solicitation.
- **Bus wash brush motor ($3,870):** sole source (manufacturer's part); justification form signed by Owen.
- **Scheduling software license ($18,600):** sole source (existing system); GM approval.
- **Route study paper surveys and postage ($3,960):** two quotes; the third printer declined to quote. Documented on the quote form.
- **Operator uniforms (two-year price agreement):** RFQ 26-07 with four quotes; award considered delivery and fitting as stated in the RFQ.

## What not to do

- Don't split a purchase to stay under a threshold.
- Don't sign a vendor's terms and conditions; send them to Teresa.
- Don't promise a vendor work before a PO or contract exists.
- Advertising sales are not purchases. Ad contracts are signed by the Communications Manager on the standard ad agreement; nonstandard terms go to Teresa.
