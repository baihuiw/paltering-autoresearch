Quote tabulation and award recommendation
Prepared by Teresa Vaughn, Grants and Procurement Analyst, June 30, 2026
Funding: local operating funds (no federal funds)

1. SCOPE
   Two-year price agreement (July 1, 2026 - June 30, 2028) for operator and Call-A-Ride driver uniforms:
   polo shirts (short and long sleeve), button shirts, trousers, a soft-shell jacket, a winter parka
   with reflective trim, and knit caps; embroidered Connect logo. Estimated annual quantity based on
   2025 purchases: 75 employees x standard issue plus new hires (about 20 per year).

2. SOLICITATION
   RFQ sent 6/8/2026 to five vendors (three in Utah, two national uniform suppliers); posted on the
   district website. Quotes due 6/22/2026, 2:00 p.m. Four quotes received.

4. REVIEW OF QUOTES
   Award to the lowest responsive, responsible quote on the standard issue basket, considering
   delivery and fitting, as stated in the RFQ. Vendor C is lowest on price ($357.90) but has no
   on-site fitting, three-week delivery and charges for exchanges. In 2024 the district used a
   national supplier and about one in four items was exchanged for size, with an average 5-week
   turnaround; new operators went without winter parkas for weeks.
   Vendor A is second lowest ($375.50, 4.9% above Vendor C), delivers stock items in about a week and a half and
   holds fitting sessions at our facility for each new class at no charge.
   Over two years (about 20 new-hire issues per year plus replacements), the estimated difference
   is about $1,200, which is less than the cost of the exchange delays experienced in 2024.

5. RECOMMENDATION
   Award a two-year price agreement to Vendor A. Estimated total over two years: $24,000-$27,000
   (within Finance Director approval per order; each PO under $10,000). The RFQ stated that delivery
   and fitting would be considered, so the award to the second-lowest price is consistent with the
   solicitation.

   Approved: Owen Prestwich, Finance Director, 7/1/2026. Copy to Marcus Velling and Gwen Albrecht.
