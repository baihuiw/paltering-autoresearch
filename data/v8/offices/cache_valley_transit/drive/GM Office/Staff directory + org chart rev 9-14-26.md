## Who handles what (front desk cheat sheet)

- Lost and found: front desk; items held 30 days.
- Detours, late buses, operator compliments or complaints: Operations.
- Call-A-Ride trips, eligibility questions: Paratransit.
- Bus stop requests (bench, shelter, snow, light): Capital Projects.
- Advertising on buses: Communications.
- Media, records requests (GRAMA), anything legal or threatening: Kendra Oberlin.
- Board meeting questions and public comment sign-up: Lacey Dunford.

## Board of Trustees

Nine trustees appointed by Cache County and the member cities. Chair: Lynette Jardine. Budget committee chair: Ross Danforth. Regular meetings fourth Wednesday, 5:30 p.m., Connect boardroom; agendas posted on the Utah Public Notice Website and at rideconnectutah.gov/board.
