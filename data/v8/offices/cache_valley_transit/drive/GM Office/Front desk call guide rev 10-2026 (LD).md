# Front desk call guide

*For front desk staff and anyone covering the main line. Rev. October 16, 2026 (LD). Updated: route study dates, Route 12 morning note, holiday hours, snow at stops, Sunday pilot (DW 10/15). Main line (435) 752-2877. Front desk hours Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.*

When you don't know, take a name and number and send it to the right person (see the staff directory). Log every rider comment in the customer comment tracker before you hang up.

## Service basics

- **Hours:** Loop 6:00 a.m.-8:45 p.m. weekdays; most numbered routes 6:30 a.m.-8:30 p.m. weekdays and 10:30 a.m.-6:30 p.m. Saturdays (check the route's schedule). No service on six holidays.
- **Fares:** none. Connect is zero fare for everyone, including Call-A-Ride. No pass or ID needed.
- **Live bus locations:** the Transit app, or call dispatch (option 2) for a bus that's very late.
- **Printed schedules:** on every bus, at the Transit Center and at the front desk.

## Common questions

**"My bus is late."** Ask route, stop, time. Check the AVL screen. If the bus is more than 10 minutes late, tell dispatch. Log it as a comment. *Route 12 mornings (6:50 and 7:20 from Hyrum):* running late because of school drop-off traffic between Hyrum and Providence. No construction or detour. Planning is moving the morning trips earlier; details coming in a rider alert.

**"Can I bring my bike?"** Front rack, two bikes, first come first served. No full-size bikes inside the bus, even when the rack is full. Folding bikes OK if folded. Three-bike racks are being tested on two Loop buses. Web page: rideconnectutah.gov/bikes.

**"I lost something."** See the lost and found procedure (Rider Communications folder). Search the log. Items from Friday and Saturday are logged Monday morning. Held for a month (30 calendar days). Pick up here with photo ID, 8 to 5. No mailing.

**"I want to thank my driver."** Get route, direction, time, stop and any description. Log it as a compliment. The supervisor identifies the operator and delivers it in person; a copy goes in the operator's file. The GM signs a reply to the rider.

**"I want to complain about a driver."** Same details. Log it. Supervisor follows up within 3 business days. Don't promise discipline or an outcome.

**"When is the board meeting?"** Fourth Wednesday, 5:30 p.m., boardroom here, except Nov 18 and Dec 9 this year. Public comment: sign up at the door before the meeting or email Lacey (ldunford@) by noon on the day. 3 minutes. In person only; written comments by email by noon. Livestream is watch-only.

**"Do the buses run on Sundays?"** Not yet. The Sunday pilot is part of the 2027 budget process; the board holds the budget hearing and adopts the 2027 budget Wed Dec 9. Updates: rideconnectutah.gov/sunday.

**"Who clears snow at my stop?"** Priority stops (shelters, busiest stops): contractor within 24 hours after a 2-inch snowfall ends. Others: our crew, as time allows. Take the stop location and send to Cody the same day.

**"I want to advertise on a bus."** Kendra Oberlin, ext. 110. Rate card in Rider Communications. Don't quote prices for multi-bus packages; let Kendra.

**"Are you hiring?" / "Do I need a CDL?"** No CDL needed to apply. Paid training (4 weeks). Minimum age twenty-one. Apply at rideconnectutah.gov/careers. Hiring fair Fri Oct 30, 10-2, training room. Next operator class starts 1/11. Questions to HR, ext. 180.

**"Where's the survey?"** Route study survey and map: rideconnectutah.gov/routestudy, Oct 20-Nov 15. Paper copies on buses and here. We can take it by phone (script in the drawer). Pop-up schedule in the news release.

**"Do you run on Thanksgiving?"** No buses or Call-A-Ride Thursday Nov 26. Normal weekday service Friday Nov 27 (our office is closed Friday, but buses run). Christmas Day and New Year's Day: no service.

**"Is Call-A-Ride available for me?"** Transfer to Call-A-Ride, ext. 155. Don't try to answer questions about a specific rider's trips or paperwork.

**"Will you ever charge fares?"** No plans to. Any fare would need a board vote after a public hearing, and nothing is proposed. Riders who want to share an opinion can use the contact form ("Opinion") or speak at a board meeting. Log it as an opinion; the GM reads them.

**"Can the GM come to our meeting?"** Take the group, date, time, place and topic, and send it to Lacey. Don't commit the GM's time.

**"Can my driver wear a costume?"** Yes on Halloween, as long as it doesn't cover the face or block vision.

**"Is there a bus to the new warehouses / my new job?"** Check the system map first. If there's no route, take the address and shift times, log it as a request, and point them to the route study survey and map (rideconnectutah.gov/routestudy), which is collecting exactly this. Don't promise a new route.

## Transfer, don't answer

- Reporters, TV, radio: Kendra (ext. 110). Say "Let me connect you with our communications manager."
- Records requests (GRAMA), lawyers, threats, anything about a lawsuit or injury: Kendra.
- Anyone asking for the GM: take a message for Lacey unless the GM has said to put them through.
- Trustees: put them through to Lacey or the GM.
