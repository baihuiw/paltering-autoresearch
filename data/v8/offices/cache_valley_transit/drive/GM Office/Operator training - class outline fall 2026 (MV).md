# Operator training class: outline and daily plan (fall 2026 class)

*Marcus Velling, Operations Manager. Class of six, Monday 9/28 - Friday 10/23, 2026. Training room and yard, 3021 N. 300 W. Same outline for the 1/11 class with dates shifted. Lead trainer plus one assistant trainer; Call-A-Ride lead trainer for the mobility device sessions.*

## Ground rules

- Paid from day one at the training wage. Report at 7:00 a.m. Out at 3:30 p.m. Lunch 30 minutes.
- Two absences in the four weeks and you move to the next class (unless medical or family emergency).
- Phones away in the classroom and always away behind the wheel.
- Trainees ride as passengers on at least four different routes on their own time in week 1 (free, of course). Write down three things you notice about how operators handle stops.

## Week 2: behind the wheel (10/5-10/9)

- Yard: straight-line backing, offset backing, alley dock, mirrors, reference points, tight turns around cones.
- Quiet streets in North Logan and Hyde Park in the late morning, then arterials after lunch.
- Pulling to the curb at stops: approach, the 6-inch rule, kneeling, leaving the stop.
- Daily pre-trip walk-around practice (each trainee leads one each day).
- GM visit Thursday 10/8.

## Week 3: routes and riders (10/12-10/16)

- Route training on every route, with an operator on board: Loop Blue and Green, then Routes 01-16. Note timepoints, turns, tricky stops, layover locations and restrooms.
- Wheelchair and scooter securement (two sessions with the Call-A-Ride lead trainer). Every trainee secures a manual chair, a power chair and a scooter, twice each.
- Bike rack: load, unload, three-bike rack on bus 2301.
- Winter: what we do in snow, chains, slick stops, when to call a supervisor.

## Week 4: skills test and revenue service (10/19-10/23)

- Mon-Tue: final skills practice. Skills test (pre-trip, basic control, road test) at the yard with the third-party tester; retest slots Wednesday.
- Wed-Thu: supervised revenue service. Each trainee drives at least four hours in service with a trainer on board.
- Fri 10/23: wrap-up; uniforms check; extra board orientation; run-cut and bidding explained. **Graduation 3:00 p.m.**, training room. Families welcome. Board chair and GM present certificates.

## After graduation

- First 30 days: extra board, daytime runs only, no Route 16 until day 30.
- 30-day and 90-day ride checks with a supervisor.
- Mentors: each new operator paired with a senior operator for the first 90 days.

## What trainers watch for

- Smooth starts and stops: riders standing with bags, kids and canes should not have to brace.
- Pulling all the way to the curb, every stop, so riders don't step into the gutter.
- Looking for riders running for the bus, and waiting when it is safe to.
- Calling out stops clearly when the automatic announcements fail.
- Treating every rider the same: students, seniors, riders with disabilities, people who don't speak English.
- Keeping calm with a difficult rider and calling dispatch early rather than late.
