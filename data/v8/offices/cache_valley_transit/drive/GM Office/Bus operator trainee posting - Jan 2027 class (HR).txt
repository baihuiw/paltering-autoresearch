NO CDL? NO PROBLEM. WE'LL TRAIN YOU, AND PAY YOU WHILE YOU LEARN.

About the job
Connect operators drive zero-fare buses on the Loop and 14 routes from Lewiston to Hyrum. You'll
greet riders, help people who use wheelchairs and walkers board safely, keep to the schedule, and be
the face of Connect for thousands of people a day. Most new operators start on the extra board,
covering open runs at varied times, and bid for regular runs as seniority allows.

Pay and benefits
- Training wage: $20.75 per hour, paid from your first day of class.
- Operator wage after graduation: $23.40 per hour, with step increases at 6 and 12 months.
- $2,000 hiring bonus: $1,000 at graduation and $1,000 after six months of service.
- Medical, dental and vision insurance; Utah Retirement Systems pension; paid holidays and paid time
  off; uniforms provided.
- Overtime available; shift differential for runs ending after 7:00 p.m.

Training (four weeks, full-time, Monday-Friday, about 7:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m.)
- Week 1: classroom; commercial learner permit (CLP) knowledge tests at the Utah Driver License
  Division (we schedule and pay the fees); district policies; customer service.
- Week 2: behind-the-wheel training in the yard and on quiet streets with a certified trainer.
- Week 3: route training on every Connect route; serving riders with disabilities; securing
  wheelchairs and mobility devices; radio and dispatch.
- Week 4: CDL skills test (Class B with passenger endorsement) at our yard; then supervised
  revenue service with a trainer on board. Graduation at the end of week 4.
Our classroom and driving training meet federal entry-level driver training requirements. Training
is paid. You do not repay training costs if you leave, though the second half of the hiring bonus is
paid only after six months.

Applicants who already hold a Class B or Class A CDL with a passenger endorsement may skip week 1 and
start at the operator wage after passing our road test.

Requirements
- At least twenty-one years old.
- Valid driver license (Utah, or able to get one within a month).
- Driving record for the past 3 years with no more than two moving violations and no DUI.
- Able to pass a DOT medical exam, a pre-employment drug and alcohol test, and a background check.
  Operators are subject to random federal drug and alcohol testing.
- Able to work early mornings, evenings and Saturday shifts.
- High school diploma or equivalent.

How to apply
Apply online at rideconnectutah.gov/careers. Applications received by December 1, 2026 will be
considered for the Jan. 11 class. We need at least eight trainees to run a class; if a class
doesn't fill, applicants are offered the next class (usually March).

Meet us: Hiring fair Friday, October 30, 10:00 a.m.-2:00 p.m., Connect training room, 3021 N. 300 W.,
North Logan. Sit in the driver's seat, meet our trainers, and apply on the spot.

Common questions
- Do I need a CDL to apply? No. Most of our operators had never driven anything bigger than a pickup
  before they started.
- Is training really paid? Yes, at the training wage for every hour of class, starting day one.
- What if I don't pass a test the first time? You get a retake. Most trainees who need one pass on
  the second try.
- Can I work part-time? New operators start full-time. Part-time positions open after 12 months.
- What are the hours like? New operators start on the extra board, which covers open runs early in
  the morning, in the middle of the day and in the evening. Regular runs are bid by seniority three times a year.
