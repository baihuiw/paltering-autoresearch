PAD DIMENSIONS
  Length along curb ............ 8'-0" min.
  Depth from back of curb ...... 5'-0" min.
  Thickness .................... 6" (4,000 psi, fiber reinforced) over 4" compacted
                                 untreated base course
  Joints ....................... tooled control joints at 4'-0" max. spacing;
                                 1/2" expansion joint at curb and at existing sidewalk

SLOPES
  Parallel to roadway .......... match roadway grade
  Perpendicular to roadway ..... 2.0% max. (1:48), draining to curb

SURFACE
  Medium broom finish perpendicular to curb. 
  Detectable warning surface NOT required (not a curb ramp).

CONNECTIONS
  Connect to existing sidewalk where present with an accessible route (36" min. width).
  Where no sidewalk exists, pad to stand alone; note on stop list.

SHELTER PADS (where shown on stop list)
  Extend pad 6'-0" beyond boarding area along curb for shelter footprint.
  Anchor bolts per shelter manufacturer.

NOTES
  1. Contractor to verify curb and gutter condition before forming; report damaged curb to CVTD.
  2. Stake each pad and call CVTD for form inspection before pour (24 hr notice).
  3. Protect from traffic 72 hours minimum.
  4. Restore park strip / landscaping disturbed by work with topsoil and sod or seed to match.
  5. Stop sign/pole relocation by CVTD.
