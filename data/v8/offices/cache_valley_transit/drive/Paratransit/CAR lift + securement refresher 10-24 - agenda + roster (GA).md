## Reminders for the session

- **Every wheelchair and scooter gets all four tie-downs and the occupant belt**, every trip, even short ones. If a rider declines the belt, note it on the manifest; you still secure the chair.
- **Never deploy a lift onto ice or a slope you wouldn't stand on.** Move the van. If there's no safe spot, call dispatch.
- **Packages:** operators may help with up to four bags, as the rider guide says. More than that, the rider needs to bring help.
- **Waiting time:** five minutes after the pickup window opens. Dispatch must try to call the rider before you leave.
- **Personal care attendants** ride free and must board and exit with the rider.
