# Call-A-Ride ADA eligibility: processing steps (staff)

Rev. March 2025. Owner: ADA Paratransit Coordinator. Backup: Paratransit Manager.

Call-A-Ride is our ADA complementary paratransit service. It is for people whose disability prevents them from using the fixed-route buses for some or all trips. Having a disability alone does not create eligibility; we look at whether the person can get to and from a stop, board and ride an accessible bus, and find their way on the system.

## 1. Intake

- Applications come in by mail, at the front desk, or by email to the eligibility inbox. Date-stamp the day it arrives and log it the same day (App ID PE-YY-####).
- Check every page. If anything is blank, mail the "incomplete application" letter within 3 business days and note it in the log.
- Send the professional verification request to the licensed professional the applicant lists (doctor, PT/OT, case manager, vision specialist). Second request at 14 days.

## 2. When an application is complete

An application is complete when we have the signed form and the professional verification. Enter the complete date in the log. The log calculates day 21.

Federal rule (49 CFR 37.125(c)): "If, by a date 21 days following the submission of a complete application, the entity has not made a determination of eligibility, the applicant shall be treated as eligible and provided service until and unless the entity denies the application."

In practice: if a determination will not be made by day 21, issue a temporary rider ID on day 21 and let the applicant book trips. Note "presumptive" in the log.

## 3. Determination

- Categories: full, conditional (list the conditions, e.g. snow and ice, stops without sidewalks), temporary (with an end date), or not eligible.
- Determinations are in writing. A "not eligible" letter states the reasons and includes the appeal form (appeals within 60 days).
- Full and conditional riders get a 4-year ID card. Mail the card and the rider guide together.

## 4. Phone line

The eligibility line rings to the coordinator's desk and rolls to reservations after 4 rings. Callers asking about status get the actual status from the log: received, waiting on verification, complete and in review, or determined.

## 5. Records

Applications and verifications are kept in the locked cabinet in the paratransit office, not on the shared drive. The log on the drive holds names, dates and status only. No medical information goes in the log or in email.
