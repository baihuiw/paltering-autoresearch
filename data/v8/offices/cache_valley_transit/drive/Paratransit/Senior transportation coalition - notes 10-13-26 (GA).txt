Bear River Health Department - Senior Transportation Coalition
Quarterly meeting, Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 3:00-4:30 p.m., Health Department conference room, Logan
Notes by Gwen Albrecht for CVTD (not the official minutes; the Health Dept. coordinator sends those)

Attending: Health Dept. aging services coordinator (chair); Dana Whitcomb and Gwen Albrecht (Connect);
senior center directors from Logan and Smithfield; a hospital social worker; two volunteer driver
program coordinators; a home health agency; the Area Agency on Aging (BRAG) transportation lead;
a USU gerontology student (observer). About 14 people.

1. Introductions. Dana introduced herself; the group welcomed her. She said she wants Connect at this
   table every quarter and that she or Gwen will attend.

2. Round robin.
   - Logan senior center: lunch attendance up about 10% since spring. Most riders who come by bus use
     the stop in front of the center on 100 East. They asked whether the shelter there could get a
     second bench. (Gwen: will pass to Cody Hales; benches are on the spring list.)
   - Smithfield senior center: wants a Connect visit to show seniors how to ride Route 15. Dana offered
     to come to the November 17 lunch; it's already on her calendar.
   - Hospital social worker: discharge trips home after 5 p.m. are the hardest. Call-A-Ride requires
     booking a day ahead; volunteer drivers can't always do same-day. Group discussed whether a short
     list of on-call volunteer drivers could cover evenings. BRAG will look into it.
   - Volunteer driver programs: 23 active volunteer drivers between them. Mileage reimbursement rates
     unchanged. One program needs drivers in Hyrum and Wellsville.
   - Home health agency: some clients don't know the buses are free. They'd like printed "how to ride"
     cards. (Gwen: Kendra can send 200.)

3. Connect update (Dana and Gwen).
   - Route study round 2 opens October 20. Pop-up at the Health Department October 28, 11:30-1:30.
     Dana asked members to share the survey link with clients and to help older riders fill out the
     paper version. Paper surveys and return envelopes available from Gwen.
   - Holiday service: no buses or Call-A-Ride on Thanksgiving Day; normal service the Friday after.
   - Winter: priority bus stops cleared within 24 hours after a 2-inch snowfall; members can report
     buried stops through the Connect website or front desk.
   - Call-A-Ride: zero fare; attendants ride free; trips booked 1 to 7 days ahead. Gwen reminded
     members that Call-A-Ride is limited to areas within 3/4 mile of a fixed route.
   - Travel training: Connect operators can do a one-on-one "ride with me" for seniors trying the bus
     for the first time. Call the front desk to set one up. (Several members didn't know about this.)

4. Coalition project: one-page "Getting around Cache Valley without a car" guide.
   - Draft by the Health Dept. coordinator by December. Connect to supply bus and Call-A-Ride
     sections; BRAG the volunteer driver section; senior centers the meal site list.
   - Spanish version needed. Rosa Ibarra may be able to review.

5. Next meeting: Wednesday, December 2, 11:00 a.m. (moved for the holidays).

6. Other business.
   - The Logan senior center asked whether buses could wait an extra minute at the center at lunch
     pickup time. Dana said she'd ask Operations about timing at that stop (it's a timepoint on two
     routes) and report back in December.
   - A volunteer driver coordinator asked whether Connect could share its holiday schedule earlier
     each year so volunteer programs can plan. Gwen will send the 2027 holiday dates once the board
     adopts its 2027 calendar.
   - Members thanked Connect for zero-fare service. Several said their clients don't believe it's
     really free until they try it.
