# Call-A-Ride: standing orders, cancellations, no-shows and service days

*Reservations and dispatch procedure. Rosa Ibarra, Reservations and Dispatch Lead. Rev. March 2025; holiday dates updated for 2026 on 9/30/26. Internal; the rider-facing version is in the Call-A-Ride rider guide.*

## 1. Booking

- Riders book by phone, ext. 155, 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m., Monday-Friday, and 8:00 a.m.-noon on Saturdays for Monday trips. Trips may be booked 1 to 7 days ahead. Same-day trips are not taken; if a rider calls same-day, offer the next available day and remind them of the fixed-route option if they use it for some trips.
- We may negotiate a pickup time up to one hour before or after the requested time. Record the requested time and the time we offered.
- Pickup window: 30 minutes. Operators wait 5 minutes after the window opens.

## 2. Standing orders (subscription trips)

- A standing order is a trip the rider takes at the same time, from the same place, to the same place, at least once a week (dialysis, work, school, day programs). Standing orders are booked automatically and don't need a weekly call.
- Standing orders may not use more than half of the available trips in any hour. When an hour is full, new standing orders go on the waitlist and the rider books those trips one at a time.
- **Holidays:** standing orders on a no-service day are cancelled automatically. Reservations calls every standing-order rider affected the week before the holiday. For Thanksgiving 2026: Thursday 11/26 trips cancelled; Friday 11/27 standing orders run as normal. Christmas Day (Friday 12/25) and New Year's Day (Friday 1/1/2027) standing orders cancelled; reminder calls the week of 12/14.
- Riders may suspend a standing order for up to 60 days (vacation, hospital stay) by calling. After 60 days the standing order is released.

## 3. Cancellations

- Riders should cancel as soon as they know, and at least 2 hours before the pickup window.
- **Late cancel:** cancelled less than 2 hours before the window. Logged, but not counted as a no-show unless the rider has a pattern (more than 4 late cancels in a month).
- When a rider cancels the first trip of the day, ask about the return trip. Most riders want both cancelled.

## 4. No-shows

- A no-show is when the van arrives within the pickup window, waits 5 minutes, dispatch tries to call the rider, and the rider doesn't take the trip.
- Operators never leave before dispatch has tried the call and released them.
- **No-show not counted** if the van was late (outside the window), or the cause was beyond the rider's control (a medical issue, a late appointment, a fixed-route bus that didn't connect, bad weather).
- **Suspensions** are possible only for a pattern of no-shows. Rosa reviews the no-show list monthly. A rider is sent a warning letter at 3 counted no-shows in a month **and** when those no-shows are more than 10% of their trips that month. A second month at that level brings a notice of a proposed suspension (one week for the first time), which the rider may dispute in writing or by phone before it takes effect. Gwen approves any suspension.
- September 2026: 96 no-shows on 3,412 trips (2.8%). Warning letters: 2. Suspensions: 0.

## 5. Weather days

- On snow days, dispatch may call riders to move trips earlier or later. No-shows on a declared weather day are never counted.
- If the fixed routes are delayed or pulled from a hilly segment, Call-A-Ride follows the same decision for that area. Communications posts the notice by 5:30 a.m.

## 6. Visitors

- Visitors certified by another paratransit system may ride as visitors for the limited period the federal rules allow each year (49 CFR 37.127) by showing their ID card or proof from their home system. Gwen handles visitors who need longer.

## 7. Comments

- Compliments and complaints from Call-A-Ride riders go into the customer comment tracker under "Call-A-Ride." Gwen or Rosa follows up within 3 business days. Compliments for drivers go in the driver's file, the same as for fixed-route operators.
