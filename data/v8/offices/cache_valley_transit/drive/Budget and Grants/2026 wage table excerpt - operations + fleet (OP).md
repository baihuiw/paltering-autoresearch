# 2026 wage table - excerpt (operations, Call-A-Ride, fleet)

*Finance, rev. Jan. 2026 (board-adopted with the 2026 budget). Excerpt for managers and HR; the full table includes administrative positions. Owen Prestwich. Wages per hour unless noted.*

## Fixed-route operators

| Step | When | Wage |
|---|---|---|
| Trainee | During the four-week training class | $20.75 |
| Step 1 | On graduation | $23.40 |
| Step 2 | After 6 months | $24.15 |
| Step 3 | After 12 months | $24.95 |
| Step 4 | After 2 years | $25.90 |
| Step 5 | After 4 years | $27.10 |
| Step 6 | After 7 years | $28.35 |
| Step 7 | After 10 years | $29.60 |

- Shift differential: $0.75 per hour for runs ending after 7:00 p.m.
- Trainer premium: $1.50 per hour while training.
- Hiring bonus (operators, mechanics, Call-A-Ride drivers): $2,000; half at graduation or after the first month, half at 6 months.
- Overtime: time and a half over 40 hours per week.

## Overtime and extra board (for managers)

- Overtime is approved by the supervisor on duty for open runs, the operations manager for planned overtime (special events, pop-ups, training), and the paratransit manager for Call-A-Ride.
- Extra board operators are guaranteed eight hours a day, five shifts a week, at their step.
- Holiday pay: operators who work a district holiday (for example the Friday after Thanksgiving) receive time and a half plus a floating holiday.
- Training premium applies to trainers in the operator class and to mentors during a new operator's first three months (mentors: $0.75 per hour while riding with the new operator).

## Notes

1. Step increases take effect on the first full pay period after the anniversary date. Steps 2 and 3 require a satisfactory ride check.
2. The board-adopted cost-of-living adjustment for 2026 was 3.0%, effective the first pay period of the year. Any 2027 adjustment is set with the 2027 budget in December.
3. Part-time operators (after 12 months full-time) are paid at their step; benefits are prorated.
4. Benefits for full-time employees: medical, dental and vision (district pays 90% of employee-only premium and 75% of family); Utah Retirement Systems (Tier 2) with district contribution; life insurance; 11 paid holidays (the six service holidays plus the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve afternoon, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day and Pioneer Day for administrative staff; operators who work a holiday receive holiday pay plus a floating holiday); paid time off accrual starting at 4.6 hours per pay period.
5. Uniforms, safety boots allowance ($150 per year) and CDL renewal fees are paid by the district.
