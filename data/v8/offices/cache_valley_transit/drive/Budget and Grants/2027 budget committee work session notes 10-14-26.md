# Budget Committee work session: 2027 budget

**Wednesday, October 14, 2026, 4:00 p.m., small conference room**
Notes: Owen Prestwich (not formal minutes; work sessions take no action)

Present: Trustee Ross Danforth (committee chair), Trustee Lynette Jardine (board chair), one other committee trustee by phone. Staff: Dana Whitcomb, Owen Prestwich, Teresa Vaughn, Janelle Ostler (item 4).

## 1. Revenue forecast

Owen presented the revised sales tax forecast. Third-quarter distributions came in under last year in four of the member cities, mostly building materials and vehicle sales. Revised 2027 forecast: $15.21 million, down from the $15.91 million used in the July planning numbers (-4.4%). Federal 5307 operating assistance assumed flat at the 2026 apportionment. Interest income lower as the facility reserve is drawn down.

## 2. Bus replacement match

Teresa: the state transit fund award for four replacement buses is expected in November. The 20% local match is about $520,000 in 2027 and has to be in the tentative budget for the award. Committee agreed it comes first.

## 3. Wages and staffing

Operator and mechanic wage step adjustment (3.5%) stays in. Committee asked for a one-page summary of vacancies and overtime for the Oct 28 board meeting.

## 4. Service

Janelle: the route study's fall pop-ups run Oct 20-31; preliminary service concepts in February.

Committee consensus: the Sunday service pilot ($640,000) comes out of the tentative 2027 budget, so it will not start in January. Revisit for mid-2027 or 2028 if third-quarter sales tax recovers and once the route study is done. The committee had not seen cost estimates before the Sept 24 announcement of a Jan. 10 start (the board chair asked on 9/23 that it see them first). Dana noted she had announced a January start and asked that the change be handled through the normal budget process.

No other service expansion in the tentative budget.

## 5. Next steps

- Owen to send the tentative budget (v3) to trustees by Oct 21 for the Oct 28 meeting.
- Tentative budget adopted Oct 28; public hearing and final adoption Dec 9.
- Teresa to confirm match timing with the state.
