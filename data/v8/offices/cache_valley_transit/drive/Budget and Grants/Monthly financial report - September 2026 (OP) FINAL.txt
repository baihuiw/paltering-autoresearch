CACHE VALLEY TRANSIT DISTRICT
MONTHLY FINANCIAL REPORT - SEPTEMBER 2026 (unaudited)
Prepared by Owen Prestwich, Finance Director, October 13, 2026
For the Board of Trustees, October 28, 2026 (third quarter financials, agenda item 5C)

Percent of year elapsed: 75.0%

1. SUMMARY
   - Operating revenue year to date is 76.4% of the annual budget; operating expenses are 72.0%.
   - Local sales tax receipts year to date are 1.7% above the same period of 2025. September's
     distribution (July sales) was 0.9% above September 2025, slower growth than the first half.
   - Fuel is under budget (diesel averaged $3.41/gal in Q3 against $3.65 budgeted).
   - Overtime is over budget in Operations and Call-A-Ride because of operator vacancies;
     partly offset by unfilled positions (wages under budget).
   - Fund balance (unassigned, general fund) at 9/30: $6.92 million, about 5.2 months of operating
     expense, above the board's 4-month minimum.
