CACHE VALLEY TRANSIT DISTRICT
DBA CONNECT PUBLIC TRANSIT
BOARD OF TRUSTEES - REGULAR MEETING MINUTES
Wednesday, September 23, 2026, 5:30 p.m.
Connect Boardroom, 3021 North 300 West, North Logan, Utah
DRAFT - for approval October 28, 2026

Present: Trustees Lynette Jardine (Chair), Ross Danforth, Garth Vollmer, and five other trustees.
Excused: One Cache County trustee.
Staff: Dana Whitcomb (General Manager), Owen Prestwich, Lacey Dunford, Janelle Ostler, Marcus Velling, Gwen Albrecht, Kendra Oberlin.
Public: Five members of the public.

1. Call to order. Chair Jardine called the meeting to order at 5:30 p.m. and welcomed General Manager Whitcomb to her first regular meeting.

2. Consent agenda. (A) Approval of agenda; (B) minutes of August 26 regular meeting and September 9 special meeting; (C) August check register; (D) next meeting October 28. Moved Danforth, seconded by a North Logan trustee. Vote 8-0.

3. Public comment.
   - A Providence resident asked for a bench at the Center Street stop. Staff will add it to the Phase 2 list.
   - A USU student asked about later evening service on the Loop. Chair Jardine referred her to the route study survey.
   - No other comments.

4. October 12 service changes. Janelle Ostler presented the October service change package: Route 02 timing for school bell times; Route 06 weekday midday trips (9:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.) from every 30 to every 60 minutes; Route 11 Saturday timing; a Route 15 deviation into the new Canyon Bench neighborhood in Hyde Park with four new stops; and moving the midday operator relief point to the Aquatic Center. The package is hours-neutral and needs no budget amendment. Rider notices go out September 28.
   Discussion: A Logan City trustee asked how many riders use Route 06 midday. Ms. Ostler: about 11 boardings per revenue hour, below the system midday average of 19. A trustee asked whether the Route 06 change could be revisited after the route study; GM Whitcomb said yes, everything will be looked at in the study.
   Trustee Vollmer moved to approve the October 12 service change package as presented. Seconded by a Logan City trustee. Vote 8-0.

5. Management report (GM Whitcomb).
   a. First two weeks: rode all routes; met each department; staff town hall September 16.
   b. Sunday service: she will announce a proposed 12-month Sunday pilot at the community open house tomorrow and bring it forward in the 2027 budget. Chair Jardine asked that the budget committee see cost estimates first; GM agreed.
   c. Operators: four fixed-route operator vacancies out of 64 positions; extra board covering all runs. A class of six starts CDL training September 28 and graduates October 23.
   d. Call-A-Ride: September on-time performance on track; the ADA paratransit coordinator position is posted and interviews are next week.
   e. Facility: final punch list items on the bus wash closed.

6. Finance (Owen Prestwich). August financials: sales tax receipts year to date 1.8% over 2025; operating expenses at 64% of budget at 67% of the year. 2027 budget calendar: budget committee work session October 14; tentative budget October 28; hearing and adoption December 9.

7. Route study update (Janelle Ostler). Phase 1 survey closed with 1,912 responses. Fall pop-ups October 20-31. Board one-on-ones with the consultant continue through October.

8. Chair report. Employee anniversaries: two operators at 10 years, one mechanic at 20 years. Chair Jardine will present to the Hyrum city council in October.

9. Public comment. None.

10. Adjourn. 6:41 p.m.

Lacey Dunford, Board Clerk
