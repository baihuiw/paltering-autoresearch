CACHE VALLEY TRANSIT DISTRICT - BOARD OF TRUSTEES
PROPOSED 2027 REGULAR MEETING SCHEDULE
For action October 28, 2026 (agenda item 5E). DRAFT - LD 10/9/26, reviewed by Chair Jardine 10/12.

All meetings 5:30 p.m., Connect Boardroom, 3021 North 300 West, North Logan, Utah, unless noted.
Rule: fourth Wednesday of the month, except November and December (holidays).

  Month       Date                       Notes
  ---------   ------------------------   ---------------------------------------------------
  Jan.        Wednesday, Jan. 27            Route study round 2 summary; annual officer election
  February    Wednesday, February 24     Bus stop list for spring work; annual audit status
  March       Wednesday, March 24
  April       Wednesday, April 28        Route study draft concepts (public comment period)
  May         Wednesday, May 26          Annual audit report
  June        Wednesday, June 23         Mid-year financials
  July        Wednesday, July 28
  August      Wednesday, August 25       Fall service plan (if any)
  September   Wednesday, September 22    Budget calendar
  October     Wednesday, October 27      Tentative budget; 2028 meeting schedule
  November    Wednesday, November 17     Third Wednesday (Thanksgiving is November 25)
  December    Wednesday, December 8      Second Wednesday; budget hearing and adoption

Budget committee work sessions: to be set by the committee chair (usually two in October and
November).

Remaining 2026 meetings for reference (already adopted):
  Wednesday, October 28, 2026 - regular meeting
  Wednesday, November 18, 2026 - regular meeting (third Wednesday)
  Wednesday, December 9, 2026 - regular meeting, budget hearing and adoption (second Wednesday)

Packet deadlines (staff): items to the Board Clerk by noon on the Wednesday one week before the
meeting; packet posted by Friday 5:00 p.m.; agenda posted on the Utah Public Notice Website at least
24 hours before.

Public comment: sign up at the door before the meeting starts or email ldunford@rideconnectutah.gov
by noon on the meeting day. Three minutes per speaker. In person; written comments accepted by email
by noon. Meetings livestreamed (watch only) at rideconnectutah.gov/board.

Notes for the chair:

  Owen confirmed that works with the state's budget notice requirements.

  and revisit in 2027. Not in this draft.
- Hyrum and Smithfield trustees asked whether one meeting a year could be held in the south or north
  end of the valley. Possible for July (no budget items). Would need an anchor location with

  May            Audit report from the independent auditor

Suggested motion: "I move to adopt the 2027 regular meeting schedule of the Board of Trustees as
