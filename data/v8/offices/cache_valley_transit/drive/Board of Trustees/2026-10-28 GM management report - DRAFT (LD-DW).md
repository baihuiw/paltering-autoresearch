## 6C. Winter operations readiness

**Bus stops.** Our snow removal contract for priority stops was renewed for its second option year at unchanged prices (seasonal cap $46,000; within GM authority). The contractor clears every priority stop, which is all 64 shelters and busiest stops, within 24 hours after a snowfall of 2 inches or more ends, including cutting through the plow ridge at the curb. New this year: time-stamped photos within two hours of each visit. Our facilities crew handles the Transit Center before the first bus and other stops as time allows. We have shared stop maps with each member city's streets division and asked their crews to avoid burying stops where they can.

**Facility and supplies.** Twelve tons of ice melt due 10/20; plow truck mounted by 10/26. The bus wash brush motor is due and set for installation 10/23.

**Rider information.** Winter riding tips and "who clears the stops" in the November Rider News and on social media. Riders report buried stops through the website contact form ("Bus stops") or the front desk; our standard is a reply within one business day.

**Weather days.** If conditions require detours or delays, we post at rideconnectutah.gov, on Facebook and in the Transit app by 5:30 a.m.

## Other updates (information)

**Route study.** Round 2 opens 10/20 with the online survey, interactive map and ten pop-ups through 10/31 (schedule in your packet). Janelle Ostler will report response counts under item 5D. Survey closes 11/15.

**Route 12 mornings.** Morning trips from Hyrum have been running late because of school drop-off traffic between Hyrum and Providence; September on-time performance on Route 12 was 81%. Beginning 11/2, the 6:50, 7:20 and 7:50 a.m. trips will leave Hyrum five minutes earlier, with no added cost. [Confirm after 10/19 managers meeting.]

**Three-bike racks.** The test on two Loop buses continues through February. In the first five weeks, the third slot was used on about one in nine trips and riders left behind because of full racks dropped sharply on those buses.

**Holiday service.** No service Thanksgiving Day (11/26) or Christmas Day (12/25); normal service the Friday after Thanksgiving.

**Community.** In my first six weeks I've met with city leaders in North Logan and Smithfield, the chamber's transportation committee, USU, the Bear River Health Department's senior transportation coalition and Logan Rotary. I'll speak at the Smithfield Senior Center on 11/17 and to Logan neighborhood councils this fall.

**Advertising.** Advertising revenue is on track to finish the year slightly above budget (about $85,000 against $84,000).

**Rider comments.** We logged 253 rider comments in September, up 20% from September 2025, including 17 compliments for operators, our highest monthly total this year. Late buses were the most common complaint (21), led by Route 12 mornings. Every compliment is delivered to the operator in person by a supervisor; I sign a reply to the rider.

**Front desk.** We now staff two people on the phones from 8:00 to 9:00 a.m. on Mondays, when calls peak. The front desk call guide was updated 10/12 with the route study, holiday, snow, bike and board meeting answers.
