CACHE VALLEY TRANSIT DISTRICT
DBA CONNECT PUBLIC TRANSIT
BOARD OF TRUSTEES - SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
Wednesday, September 9, 2026, 5:30 p.m.
Connect Boardroom, 3021 North 300 West, North Logan, Utah
(Approved September 23, 2026)

Present: Trustees Lynette Jardine (Chair), Ross Danforth, Garth Vollmer, and six other trustees (all nine present).
Staff: Owen Prestwich (Finance Director, Acting General Manager), Lacey Dunford (Board Clerk), Teresa Vaughn, Cody Hales.
Others: District legal counsel; two members of the public.

1. Call to order. Chair Jardine called the meeting to order at 5:31 p.m. Notice of the special meeting was posted on the Utah Public Notice Website and at the district office on September 4.

2. Public comment. One resident thanked the previous general manager for his years of service and asked the board to keep the system zero-fare. No other comments.

3. Appointment of general manager. Chair Jardine summarized the search: the position was advertised nationally in June; 31 applications; the search committee interviewed six and recommended two finalists; the full board interviewed both finalists in closed session on August 26 (closed for the character and professional competence of individuals, as allowed by state law). Under the Public Transit District Act the appointment requires the affirmative vote of a majority of all trustees.
   Trustee Danforth moved to appoint Dana Whitcomb as General Manager effective September 14, 2026, at the salary and terms in the employment agreement presented. Trustee Vollmer seconded. Vote: 9-0. Motion carried.
   Ms. Whitcomb, attending by video, thanked the board and said she looked forward to riding every route in her first month.

4. Employment agreement. Counsel reviewed the agreement: at-will (serves at the pleasure of the board), annual review by the board each September, relocation allowance. Moved Danforth, seconded by a Logan City trustee. Vote 9-0.

5. Bus Stop Improvements Phase 1 contract (over the GM purchasing limit). Cody Hales presented the bid results: four bids, low bid Tanner Creek Concrete LLC of Providence at $118,600 against an engineer's estimate of $126,000, for concrete boarding pads at 14 stops. FTA Section 5307 capital with 20% local match. Work to be complete before the end of concrete season. Moved Vollmer, seconded Danforth. Vote 9-0.

6. Acting general manager. The board thanked Owen Prestwich for serving as acting general manager from September 5.

7. Adjourn. 6:12 p.m.

Lacey Dunford, Board Clerk
