# Board of Trustees: public meetings and public comment

*Excerpt from the Board of Trustees Policies and Procedures Manual, Chapter 2 (Meetings), as amended by the board in 2024. Kept here for staff and front desk reference. Board Clerk: Lacey Dunford, ext. 102, ldunford@rideconnectutah.gov.*

## 2.1 Regular meetings

The board holds regular meetings on the fourth Wednesday of each month at 5:30 p.m. in the Connect boardroom, 3021 North 300 West, North Logan, unless the board adopts a different date in its annual meeting schedule. The board usually meets once in November and once in December on dates set to avoid the holidays. The annual schedule is adopted at the October meeting for the following year and posted on the Utah Public Notice Website and at rideconnectutah.gov/board.

## 2.2 Notice and agendas

Agendas are posted at least 24 hours before each meeting, as the Open and Public Meetings Act requires, on the Utah Public Notice Website, on the district website, and at the front entrance of the administration building. Staff aim to post agendas and the board packet by the Friday before the meeting.

## 2.3 How meetings are held

Meetings are held in person. Trustees may attend electronically under the board's electronic meeting rule, but the meeting has an anchor location in the boardroom, and the public attends there. Meetings are livestreamed on the district's YouTube channel (linked from rideconnectutah.gov/board) and recordings are posted within three business days. The livestream is for watching only; it is not a way to address the board.

## 2.4 Public comment

a. **When.** Every regular meeting has a public comment period near the start of the agenda for any topic, and a second period before adjournment. For a public hearing (for example, the budget hearing), comment on the hearing subject is taken during the hearing.

b. **Signing up.** Anyone wishing to speak signs the public comment sheet at the boardroom door before the meeting starts, or emails the Board Clerk at ldunford@rideconnectutah.gov **by noon on the day of the meeting** with their name and city of residence. Speakers are called in the order they signed up.

c. **Time.** Each speaker has up to three minutes. The chair may shorten the time for all speakers equally when many people sign up, or extend it. Speakers may not give their time to another speaker.

d. **In person only.** Comments are given in person at the anchor location. The board does not take comments by phone or video.

e. **Written comments.** Anyone who cannot attend may email written comments to the Board Clerk by noon on the day of the meeting. Written comments are given to every trustee before the meeting and included in the record. The chair may summarize them aloud but they are not read in full.

f. **Conduct.** Speakers address the chair and the board as a whole. Personal attacks, threats and disruptive behavior are not allowed. The board generally does not respond or deliberate during public comment; the chair may ask the General Manager to follow up with a speaker.

g. **Accommodations.** Anyone needing an accommodation to attend or speak (including a sign language interpreter or materials in another format) should contact the Board Clerk at (435) 752-2877 at least three working days before the meeting.

h. **Handouts.** Speakers may bring written materials for the board. Please bring 12 copies (nine trustees, the General Manager, the Board Clerk and the record), or email them to the Board Clerk by noon on the meeting day.

i. **Groups.** When several people want to speak on the same topic, the chair may ask a group to choose one or two speakers and may allow those speakers additional time.

## 2.5 Minutes

The Board Clerk prepares draft minutes for approval at the next regular meeting. Draft minutes are marked DRAFT until approved. Approved minutes and recordings are posted at rideconnectutah.gov/board and on the Utah Public Notice Website.

## 2.6 Committees

The board's budget committee (three trustees) meets as needed, usually in October and November. Committee meetings are public and noticed like board meetings, but public comment at committee meetings is at the committee chair's discretion.

## 2.7 Special meetings

The chair or any three trustees may call a special meeting with at least 24 hours' notice. Public comment is taken at special meetings on the items on the agenda.
