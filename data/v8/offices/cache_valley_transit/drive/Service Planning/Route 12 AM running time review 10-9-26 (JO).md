# Route 12 morning running time - review and recommendation

**To:** Marcus Velling, Operations Manager
**Cc:** Dana Whitcomb; Kendra Oberlin
**From:** Janelle Ostler, Planning and Scheduling Manager
**Date:** October 9, 2026

## Why I looked

Route 12 had the lowest on-time performance in the system in September (81.3%, against our 90% target; see "On-time performance by route - Sept 2026"). Customer comments about Route 12 being late went from 2 in August to 11 in September and 7 so far in October, nearly all about the 6:50 and 7:20 a.m. trips from Hyrum. Supervisor Ken Rasmussen rode both trips on 10/6 and 10/7.

## What's happening

- **It's the schools, not construction.** There is no road work or detour on the Route 12 alignment this fall. The 6:50 and 7:20 trips from Hyrum now run into school drop-off traffic at Mountain Crest High and South Cache Middle School. Both schools adjusted their start times this year, and the drop-off lines now back up onto the streets the bus uses between 7:05 and 7:45 a.m.
- **The delay compounds.** By the time the 7:20 trip reaches Providence (Center Street and 100 West), it is averaging 9.4 minutes late, and it reaches the Transit Center about 14 minutes late. Riders boarding in Providence and South Logan see the worst of it.
- **The schedule hasn't caught up.** Route 12's morning running time was last set in August 2023. Ken's ride notes show the bus needs about 6 more minutes between Hyrum and Providence from 6:50 to 7:50 a.m. on school days.
- **Layover is too short to recover.** The 7:20 trip has 4 minutes of layover at the Transit Center before its outbound trip, so lateness carries into the 8:20 departure from Logan.

## Options

1. **Add running time in the Jan. 2027 runboard.** Leave Hyrum 5 minutes earlier on the 6:50, 7:20 and 7:50 trips (6:45, 7:15, 7:45), keep Transit Center arrival times. No added service hours; uses existing layover. Needs a rider notice in December. *Recommended.*
2. **Interim timepoint adjustment from November 2.** Same as option 1 but earlier. The schedules are already printed through year end, and an off-cycle adjustment would need new printed schedules and a Transit app feed update. Possible, but Kendra would need two weeks.
3. **Reroute around the schools in the morning.** Would skip two stops near Mountain Crest used by about 40 students a day. Not recommended.
4. **Ask Hyrum City and the school district about signal timing and drop-off staging.** Worth doing whatever we choose. Dana could raise it at her Hyrum visit.

## What we tell riders now

Riders writing in about the 12 should hear that morning trips are running late because of school traffic between Hyrum and Providence, that there's no construction involved, and that we are adjusting the schedule. Until the adjustment, riders in Providence and South Logan can use the Transit app's live bus locations, and riders who need to arrive by a fixed time can take the trip before. Kendra will add a short note to the November Rider News if we go with option 2.

## Cost and effect

Option 1 or 2 uses existing layover at Hyrum and adds no revenue hours or operators. Riders boarding at Hyrum stops will leave five minutes earlier; riders at Providence and South Logan should see their bus arrive close to the printed time. We'll check AVL data for the first three weeks after the adjustment and report on-time performance in the December managers meeting. If the 7:20 trip is still more than 5 minutes late at Providence, the next step is a short-turn tripper on school days, which would need an operator and a bus.
