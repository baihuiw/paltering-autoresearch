# Route study, round 2 - fall outreach plan

*Internal. JO, v3, October 7, 2026 (v2 was before the consultant check-in). Staffing grid to Lacey by 10/14.*

## Purpose

Round 1 (spring/summer 2026) gathered where people go and what keeps them off the bus: 1,912 survey responses, 9 stakeholder interviews, and the board one-on-ones. Round 2 tests the trade-offs that came out of round 1 and asks for specific places riders want service. The consultant uses round 2 to build two draft network concepts for spring 2027.

## Round 2 materials

- **Survey** (10 min): coverage vs. frequency trade-off; evening service; travel to job centers (industrial areas on 1000 West, North Logan business park, the hospital campus); bikes on buses; how riders get to the stop; a few demographic questions. English and Spanish.
- **Interactive map:** "drop a pin where you'd like a bus to go" plus a comment box. Comments go straight into the study database; the consultant codes them weekly.
- **Paper survey** with postage-paid return envelope: 2,500 printed. 1,200 on buses (2 pockets per bus), 600 at the Transit Center, 300 front desk, 400 for pop-ups.
- **Phone option:** front desk takes surveys by phone; Lacey has the call script. Expect 20-40.
- **Gift card drawing:** five $50 cards, drawn November 20. Approved by Owen from the outreach line.
- **Web:** rideconnectutah.gov/routestudy goes live Tuesday 10/20, 6:00 a.m. Survey closes 11/15 at 11:59 p.m.. (Kendra: page and QR codes.)

## Talking points for staff at pop-ups

- **What is this?** A study of the whole bus network: where routes go, how often they run, and what hours. It's the first full look in more than ten years.
- **Will my route change?** Nothing from the study takes effect before spring 2027, and nothing happens without public comment and a board vote. Please tell us what you need from your route.
- **Can you add a bus to X?** Ask them to drop a pin on the map or write it on the survey, with the time of day they'd travel. Every pin is read by the study team.
- **Is the bus staying free?** Yes. Zero fare is not part of this study and nobody is proposing fares.
- **What about my bus being late?** Thank them, take the route and time, and log it in the comment tracker on the tablet. Route 12 mornings: school drop-off traffic, schedule being adjusted.
- **Snow at my stop?** Priority stops cleared within 24 hours after a 2-inch snowfall; report a buried stop on the website or by phone.
- **Hiring?** Hand them a hiring card: no CDL needed, paid training, fair 10/30.
- Don't promise anything about specific routes, dates or costs. If in doubt, take their contact information and Janelle will follow up.
