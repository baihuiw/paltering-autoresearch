# Sunday pilot: service plan and cost estimate

Janelle Ostler, Planning. Draft for the GM, September 21, 2026. For the September 24 open house talking points and the 2027 budget.

## Proposed service

| Service | Span | Frequency | Buses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loop (Blue + Green) | 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. | 30 min (weekdays 15) | 4 |
| Route 01 | 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. | 60 min | 1 |
| Route 02 | 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. | 60 min | 1 |
| Route 03 | 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. | 60 min | 1 |
| Route 07 | 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. | 60 min | 1 |
| Call-A-Ride | 9:00 a.m.-6:00 p.m. | Logan and North Logan only (ADA: same days and hours as the fixed routes it complements) | 2 vans |

Why these routes: highest Saturday ridership, and together they reach the hospital, both care centers on the east side, the Transit Center, USU housing and the big-box stores on Main.

## Cost (2027, 12 months)

- Fixed route: 8 buses x 9 hours x 51 Sundays = 3,672 revenue hours x $92.40 = $339,300
- Call-A-Ride: 2 vans x 9 hours x 51 Sundays = 918 hours x $71.80 = $65,900
- Operators at Sunday premium (time and a quarter on the base rate), dispatch, supervisor and shop coverage: $168,000
- Marketing, schedules, bus stop decals: $22,000
- Contingency (about 7%): $44,800
- **Total estimate: about $640,000**

No fare revenue (zero-fare). No federal operating assistance assumed for the pilot year.

## Staffing

Needs 12 operators on a Sunday rotation plus 2 Call-A-Ride drivers. Assumes the October and January operator classes fill current vacancies first. If not, the pilot would run with overtime, which is in the premium line above.

## Start date

Sunday, January 10, 2027 (after the holidays and the start of USU spring semester). Needs a board-adopted 2027 budget with the pilot in it, Transit app schedule load by December 18, and printed schedules by January 4.

## Measures

Boardings per revenue hour by route; Call-A-Ride trips; rider survey at month 3; report to the board at months 6 and 11. Continue only if boardings reach 12 per revenue hour systemwide.
