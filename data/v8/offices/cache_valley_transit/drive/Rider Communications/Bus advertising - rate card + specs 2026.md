# Advertise on Connect

*Rate card and specifications, effective Jan. 1, 2026. Communications (Kendra Oberlin). Rates reviewed annually; 2026 rates unchanged from 2025 except interior cards.*

Connect buses carry more than 2 million rides a year and cover Logan, North Logan, Hyde Park, Smithfield, Richmond, Providence, Hyrum and the USU campus. Our exterior signs are seen by drivers, pedestrians and riders throughout Cache Valley, six days a week.

## Terms

- **Minimum:** three four-week periods (12 weeks) for exterior signs; one period for interior cards.
- **Production:** the advertiser supplies printed signs to our specifications, or pays production at cost through our printer. Exterior signs must be 3M or equal cast vinyl rated for vehicle use. Proofs are due three weeks before the start date.
- **Installation:** included for the first installation. Replacements because of advertiser changes are $75 per sign.
- **Damage:** we replace signs damaged by weather or vandalism within two weeks at no charge, once per contract. Signs damaged in a collision are replaced at our cost.
- **Placement:** buses are assigned to routes daily, so we can't guarantee a sign on a particular route. We rotate advertising buses across the system.
- **Payment:** first period due at signing; later periods invoiced every four weeks, net 30. Nonprofit and government public service rate: 25% off.
- **Bus wraps:** full or partial wraps are not offered at this time.

## What we accept

Connect's advertising space is a nonpublic forum. Under the board's advertising policy (rev. 2023), we accept commercial and public service advertising only. We do not accept:

- Political or electoral advertising, or advertising about a ballot measure or a matter of public debate.
- Religious advertising, or advertising for or against any religion.
- Advertising for tobacco, vaping, alcohol, cannabis, firearms, or adult products or services.
- Ads that are false or misleading, that disparage any person or group, or that look like Connect's own signs or service information.

The Communications Manager reviews every ad before it is accepted. We may ask for changes, and the decision on any ad may be appealed to the General Manager.

## Getting started

1. Email or call Kendra with your preferred start date, budget and the sign types you're interested in.
2. We send an availability sheet and a quote within 48 hours (weekdays).
3. Sign the standard advertising agreement and pay the first period.
4. We send templates for each bus model. Your designer or our printer prepares the artwork; we approve a proof.
5. Signs go up within a week after we receive them. We send a photo of each installed sign.

Tips from our installers: big type and few words work best on a moving bus. A King sign is read in about three seconds. Keep it to a logo, one line and a web address or phone number.

## Common advertisers

Dental and medical offices, credit unions, car dealers, restaurants, apartment complexes, employers recruiting staff, USU events and nonprofits. We are happy to help first-time advertisers choose a package.
