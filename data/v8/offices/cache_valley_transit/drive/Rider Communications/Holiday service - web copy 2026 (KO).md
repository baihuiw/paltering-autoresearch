# Holiday service

*Web copy for rideconnectutah.gov/holidays. Updated September 30, 2026 (KO). Approved by Marcus Velling and Gwen Albrecht. Posted; also pinned on Facebook the week before each holiday.*

Connect buses and Call-A-Ride do not run on six major holidays. On all other days, including the day before and the day after each holiday, we run our normal schedule.

## No service on these holidays

| Holiday | 2026 date | Fixed-route buses | Call-A-Ride |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Year's Day | Thursday, Jan. 1 | No service | No service |
| Memorial Day | Monday, May 25 | No service | No service |
| Independence Day | Saturday, July 4 | No service | No service |
| Labor Day | Monday, September 7 | No service | No service |
| Thanksgiving Day | Thursday, November 26 | No service | No service |
| Christmas Day | Friday, December 25 | No service | No service |

New Year's Day 2027 falls on a Friday (Jan. 1). There will be no service that day, and normal Saturday service on Jan. 2.

## Thanksgiving week

- **Monday-Wednesday, November 23-25:** normal weekday service on all routes.
- **Thursday, November 26 (Thanksgiving Day):** no buses and no Call-A-Ride.
- **Friday, November 27:** normal weekday service on all routes, including the Loop from 6:00 a.m. Buses serving Cache Valley Mall, Walmart and the shopping areas on Main Street run their usual weekday trips.
- **Saturday, November 28:** normal Saturday service.

Call-A-Ride riders: book Friday, November 27 trips as usual. Trips you would normally schedule for Thanksgiving Day can't be booked. Reservations are open Wednesday, November 25, 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m.

## Christmas and New Year's

- **Thursday, December 24 (Christmas Eve):** normal weekday service.
- **Friday, December 25:** no service.
- **Saturday, December 26:** normal Saturday service.
- **Thursday, December 31:** normal weekday service.
- **Friday, Jan. 1, 2027:** no service.

## Other holidays

We run normal service on Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Pioneer Day (July 24), Columbus Day, Veterans Day and the day after Thanksgiving. USU breaks don't change our schedules, though buses near campus are much emptier.

## Holiday questions we hear a lot

**Will the buses run the day after Thanksgiving?** Yes. Friday, November 27 is a normal weekday on every route, from the first Loop trip at 6:00 a.m. to the last trips in the evening. Our office is closed that day, but the buses run.

**Do you run extra buses for holiday shopping?** We run the normal weekday schedule. Buses serving Main Street and the shopping areas can be busy in the afternoon. Leave a little extra time, and check the Transit app to see where your bus is.

**Will Call-A-Ride take me to a family dinner on Thanksgiving?** We're sorry, but Call-A-Ride does not run on Thanksgiving Day. Volunteer driver programs in the Bear River area sometimes offer holiday rides; ask our front desk for their contact information.

**What about the day before a holiday?** Normal service. On Christmas Eve, buses run the full weekday schedule even though our office closes at noon.

**What if it snows on a holiday?** Watch rideconnectutah.gov, Facebook and the Transit app for any weather changes.

## Our office

The administration office and front desk in North Logan are closed on the six service holidays and also on the Friday after Thanksgiving. Dispatch is staffed whenever buses are running. On Friday, November 27, call dispatch at (435) 752-2877 and follow the prompts for a bus that's running behind or something left on a bus.

## Bad weather

In rare severe weather we may run late or pull buses from hilly segments. We post changes at rideconnectutah.gov, on Facebook and in the Transit app by 5:30 a.m. whenever we can.
