# Connect to pilot Sunday bus service starting January 10

NORTH LOGAN, Utah - Connect Public Transit will run Sunday buses for the first time, General Manager Dana Whitcomb announced tonight at a community open house at the district's administration and maintenance facility.

Under the 12-month pilot, the Loop and Routes 01, 02, 03 and 07 would run on Sundays from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. starting **Sunday, January 10, 2027**, with Call-A-Ride available in Logan and North Logan during the same hours. Like all Connect service, Sunday rides would be zero-fare.

"The number one thing I've heard in my first two weeks is 'I can get to work Monday through Saturday, but not on Sunday,'" Whitcomb said. "Hospitals, care centers, restaurants and stores are open seven days a week, and a lot of the people who work there don't drive. A Sunday pilot lets us find out who rides and what it costs before we make it permanent."

Whitcomb will propose the pilot, estimated at $640,000 for 2027, in the district's 2027 budget. The board of trustees holds its budget hearing and adopts the 2027 budget on December 9.

Riders can sign up for Sunday service updates at rideconnectutah.gov/sunday.

About 150 residents attended the open house, which included facility tours, a bus "touch-a-truck" for kids, and a first look at the route study's fall survey.

About Connect: The Cache Valley Transit District, doing business as Connect Public Transit, provides zero-fare bus and paratransit service from Lewiston to Hyrum, funded mainly by a local sales tax.
