# Call-A-Ride: rider guide (web page copy)

Last updated by Communications, February 2026. Posted at rideconnectutah.gov/accessibility and printed in the rider guide mailed with each new ID card.

## What is Call-A-Ride?

Call-A-Ride is Connect's curb-to-curb paratransit service for people whose disability prevents them from riding our fixed-route buses. Like all Connect service, Call-A-Ride is zero-fare.

## Where and when

Call-A-Ride serves addresses within 3/4 mile of a Connect fixed route, during the same hours the fixed routes run in that area, Monday through Saturday. There is no Call-A-Ride service on Sundays or on the holidays when the buses don't run.

## How to apply

1. Pick up an application at our front desk (3021 N. 300 W., North Logan), download it from our website, or call us and we'll mail one.
2. Fill out every question. Missing answers will delay your application.
3. List a licensed professional who knows you (a doctor, therapist or case manager). We'll contact them to confirm how your disability affects your travel.
4. Return the application by mail, in person, or by email.

Once a complete application is received, it can take up to three weeks for a decision. We'll send you our decision in writing.

## After you're approved

You'll receive a Call-A-Ride ID card, good for four years. Book trips by phone from one to seven days ahead, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Your driver will wait up to five minutes after your pickup window opens.

## If you're not approved

You can appeal within 60 days. Our letter will explain why and include the appeal form.

## Questions

Call (435) 752-2877 and ask for Call-A-Ride, or stop by the front desk.
