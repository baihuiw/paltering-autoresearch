## Connect wants to know where you want to go: route study survey and pop-ups start October 20

NORTH LOGAN, Utah - Connect Public Transit invites riders and residents across Cache Valley to help shape the future of their bus system. The second round of Connect's route study opens Tuesday, October 20, with an online survey, an interactive map, and pop-up events at ten locations through October 31.

"This study will decide where our buses go for the next ten years, and the people who ride them should have the biggest say," said Dana Whitcomb, Connect's general manager. "If there's a place you can't get to by bus today, or a time of day when you wish we ran more often, we want to hear about it."

The first round of the study, held this spring and summer, drew 1,912 survey responses. The second round asks riders and non-riders to weigh in on the trade-offs that came out of that round: more frequent buses on fewer streets or less frequent buses covering more of the valley; later evening service; and new connections to growing job centers and neighborhoods.

Janelle Ostler, Connect's planning and scheduling manager, said the study team reads every comment. "Last round, riders told us loud and clear that they want buses later in the evening and better connections to where the jobs are," Ostler said. "Now we want to hear how people would weigh those choices, because we can't do everything at once."

**How to take part**

- **Online:** take the survey and drop pins on the interactive map at rideconnectutah.gov/routestudy, October 20 to November 15. It takes about 10 minutes. Available in English and Spanish.
- **On paper:** printed surveys and postage-paid return envelopes are on every Connect bus, at the Transit Center, and at the front desk at 3021 N. 300 W., North Logan.
- **In person:** visit a pop-up (below). Staff and the study team will have maps, stickers and free hot chocolate.
- **By phone:** call (435) 752-2877 and ask for the route study. We'll take your answers over the phone.
