# Transit app and website bus map: help page copy

*Web page rideconnectutah.gov/riding-tips. Rev. September 2026 (KO). Linked from the home page "Plan your trip" box.*

## Plan your trip

Connect is zero fare. You don't need a ticket, a pass or an app to ride. If you'd like to plan ahead, here are three ways.

**The Transit app.** Free for iPhone and Android. Search for "Cache Valley" or "Connect Public Transit." The app shows routes, stops and schedules, and shows when the next buses are expected at your stop. Tap a route to see the line on the map.

**The bus map on our website.** Go to rideconnectutah.gov/map to see routes and stops on a map, and look up your stop by name or number.

**Printed schedules.** On every bus, at the Transit Center, and at the front desk. Schedules are updated at each service change; the next change is October 12.

**Call us.** Main line (435) 752-2877. Dispatch is option 2. Dispatch can tell you where a bus is if it is very late.

## Tips for winter mornings

- Dress for the wait. Our shelters are not heated.
- Be at your stop a few minutes early. Operators cannot wait if the bus is early.
- Wave at the operator. In the dark, operators may not see someone standing outside the light at an unsheltered stop. Use your phone light.
- Check rideconnectutah.gov, Facebook and the Transit app for weather alerts. We post by 5:30 a.m. when we can.
- Snow at stops: see the snow page. Priority stops are cleared within 24 hours after a 2-inch snowfall ends.

## Questions about the app

The Transit app is made by a separate company, and we send it our schedule and bus information. If you have trouble with the app itself, contact the app's support in Settings. For questions about our service, use the contact form on our website or call the main line.

## Accessibility

The bus map page works with screen readers. If you can't use the app or the website, call and ask for a printed schedule by mail.
