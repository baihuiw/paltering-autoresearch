# Service changes starting Monday, October 12

*Posted September 28, 2026: website, Facebook, X, Transit app, and printed notices on affected buses and at the Transit Center.*

Connect's October service changes start **Monday, October 12**. Most riders will see no change. Here's what's different:

**Route 02:** Weekday trips run about 4 minutes later to match the new middle school bell times.

**Route 06:** Between 9:30 a.m. and 2:30 p.m. on weekdays, Route 06 will run every 60 minutes. Morning and afternoon trips stay every 30 minutes, and Saturday service doesn't change. Like transit systems across the country, Connect is still working to fill operator positions, and we are matching midday service to where riders need it most.

**Route 11:** Saturday trips leave 10 minutes earlier for a better connection to the Loop at the Transit Center.

**Route 15:** Every Route 15 trip will now serve the new Canyon Bench neighborhood in Hyde Park, with four new stops. Check the updated map for stop locations.

New printed schedules are on buses and at the Transit Center now, and the Transit app will update on October 12.

**We're hiring operators!** Paid CDL training, a $2,000 hiring bonus, and great benefits. Apply at rideconnectutah.gov/careers.

Questions? Call (435) 752-2877 or use the contact form at rideconnectutah.gov.
