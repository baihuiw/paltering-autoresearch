A few favorites: the Blue Loop operator who lowered the bus and waited for a rider with a cane and a heavy box; the Route 03 operator who called ahead so a connecting bus would wait at the hospital; and the Route 08 operator who walked a lost first-grader's backpack back to the school office. ## Winter is coming

- **Daylight saving time ends November 1.** It gets dark before 5:30 p.m. Wear something bright or carry a light at the stop so your operator can see you.
- **Snow at stops:** our contractor clears snow at shelters and our busiest stops within 24 hours after a snowfall of 2 inches or more. Report a buried stop with the contact form ("Bus stops") or at (435) 752-2877.
- **Running late?** On snowy mornings, buses may run behind. Check the Transit app for live bus locations.
- Please knock snow off your boots before you board. Wet floors are slippery.

## New to the bus? Ride with us

Connect operators and staff can meet you at your stop and ride with you the first time, showing you how to read a schedule, use the bike rack, pull the cord and transfer at the Transit Center. It's free, like everything else. Call (435) 752-2877 to set up a "ride with me" trip. Great for seniors, new residents, and anyone who's been meaning to try the bus.
