# Lost and found

*Two parts: (1) web copy for rideconnectutah.gov/lost-and-found, rev. September 2026 (KO); (2) front desk procedure, rev. 9-2026 (LD / front desk). Part 2 is internal.*

## Part 1: web copy

**Left something on a bus?** We'll do our best to get it back to you.

- Call us at **(435) 752-2877**, Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m., or use the contact form at rideconnectutah.gov and choose "Lost and found."
- Tell us the route, the direction you were traveling, about what time you got off, and a description of the item (color, brand, anything inside it).
- Items found on buses are turned in by operators at the end of their shift and logged by the front desk the next business morning. **An item lost on Friday afternoon or Saturday is usually in the log by Monday at 10:00 a.m.** Please allow that time before calling.
- Pick up items at the front desk of our administration building, **3021 N. 300 W., North Logan**, Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Bring a photo ID. For phones, laptops and tablets, be ready to unlock the device or describe the lock screen.
- We hold items for **30 days**. After that, clothing and household items are donated, and electronics are wiped and recycled. IDs, bank cards and passports go back to the issuing agency or the police.
- The Transit Center (Logan) does not keep lost items. Operators can't hand items back on the street once they've been turned in.
- Items left at a bus stop or shelter aren't collected by Connect. Try the Logan City Police non-emergency line.
- Call-A-Ride: items left in a Call-A-Ride van are handled the same way. Call reservations at ext. 155.

We can't mail items or deliver them, but you can send someone to pick up for you if they bring your written note and their own ID.

**Common situations**

- *"I left my backpack on the Loop this afternoon."* Operators turn items in at the end of their shift, which may be late in the evening. Call the next business morning after 10:00 a.m.
- *"My phone is on the bus right now."* Call (435) 752-2877 during office hours. Dispatch can ask the operator to set the item aside, but the operator can't stop the route to return it, and you'll pick it up at our front desk after it's turned in.
- *"Can you check the cameras?"* We don't review video to find lost items, but tell us the bus and time and we'll check whether anything was turned in.
- *"Someone else picked up my item."* We release items only to someone who can describe them, show ID, and (for devices) unlock them. If you think someone took your item, contact the police.

## Part 2: front desk procedure (internal)

1. **Turn-in.** Operators bag and tag each item at end of shift (tag: date, route, bus number, run, operator badge number, where found). Bags go in the lost and found bin in the operators' room. Supervisors bring the bin to the front desk by 8:30 a.m. each business day. Saturday's bin is logged Monday.
2. **Valuables.** Phones, laptops, tablets, wallets, purses, cash, keys, IDs, medication: log immediately and lock in the gray cabinet behind the front desk. Two people sign the cash count. Cash over $20 goes to Finance in a sealed envelope; the log shows the envelope number.
3. **Log.** Enter every item in the lost and found log (shared drive, front desk folder): log number LF-26-####, date in, route, bus, description, where stored, owner contacted, date out, claimed by, notes.
4. **Finding the owner.** If there's an ID, name or phone number in or on the item, call or email the owner the same day. For student IDs, USU's lost and found contact can usually reach the student. Do not open a laptop or guess a phone passcode.
5. **Callers.** Search the log by route and date. If you don't find a match, take a description and the caller's phone number and add a "looking for" note. Check the notes against new items for the next two weeks.
6. **Release.** Check photo ID against the description. For electronics, the owner unlocks the device in front of you or matches the lock screen. Record "claimed by" and initials. Medication is released only to the person whose name is on the label.
7. **Disposal.** On the first business day of each month, pull items over 30 days old. Clothing, umbrellas, water bottles, backpacks (emptied) go to the donation bin. Electronics go to IT (Bryce) for data wipe and the county e-waste day. Keys and glasses: hold two months, then discard. IDs to the issuing agency. Record disposal in the log.
8. **Food and wet items.** Toss perishables at intake. Don't log them.

Questions about this procedure: Lacey Dunford, ext. 102.
