# Rider News: October 2026

*Connect Public Transit's monthly rider newsletter. Posted at rideconnectutah.gov/news October 5, 2026; printed copies on buses and at the Transit Center; emailed to 3,940 subscribers.*

## A note from our new general manager

Hello, Cache Valley! I'm Dana Whitcomb, and I joined Connect as general manager on September 14. I've spent my first weeks riding every route, meeting our operators and mechanics, and listening to riders at stops and at our open house. Two things stand out: people here love their zero-fare buses, and they want more of them. I'm grateful to the board for this opportunity and to the previous general manager for a strong system to build on. My door is open: gm@rideconnectutah.gov.

## Sunday buses are coming

At our September 24 open house, we announced a 12-month pilot of Sunday service starting **January 10, 2027**: the Loop and Routes 01, 02, 03 and 07, from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., with Call-A-Ride in Logan and North Logan. Sign up for updates at rideconnectutah.gov/sunday.

## 14 new accessible bus stops

Our Phase 1 bus stop improvements are complete! Crews poured new concrete boarding pads at 14 stops in Logan, North Logan, Smithfield, Hyrum and Providence, so riders using wheelchairs, walkers and strollers have a solid, level place to board. New shelters with benches arrive at three of the busiest stops this month. Phase 2 starts next spring.

## Service changes start October 12

Route 02 runs a few minutes later on weekdays, Route 06 runs every 60 minutes from 9:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. on weekdays, Route 11's Saturday trips leave 10 minutes earlier, and Route 15 now serves the new Canyon Bench neighborhood in Hyde Park. Details at rideconnectutah.gov/service-changes.

## Tell us where you want to go

Our route study wants to hear from you. Look for our team at pop-ups October 20-31 at the Transit Center, USU, the library and Smith's Marketplace, or take the survey online. Your answers will shape service for years.

## We're hiring

Operators, mechanics and Call-A-Ride drivers: paid CDL training, a $2,000 hiring bonus and great benefits. Apply at rideconnectutah.gov/careers.

## Ride smart this fall

- Daylight saving time ends November 1. Wear something bright at the stop.
- Pull the cord one block before your stop.
- Lost something? Call (435) 752-2877. We hold lost items for 30 days.
