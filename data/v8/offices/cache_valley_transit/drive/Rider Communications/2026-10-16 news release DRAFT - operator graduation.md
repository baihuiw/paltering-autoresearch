## Six new bus operators join Connect; next paid training class starts early in 2027

NORTH LOGAN, Utah - Six new bus operators graduated today from Connect Public Transit's four-week paid training program and will begin driving routes across Cache Valley next week. None of the six held a commercial driver license when they applied.

The graduates earned their Class B commercial driver licenses with passenger endorsements during training, which covered classroom instruction, behind-the-wheel practice, route training on every Connect route, and serving riders who use wheelchairs and other mobility devices. [Names: list only with each graduate's written OK - Marcus has the forms.]

"Every one of these operators started with a regular driver license and a willingness to learn," said Dana Whitcomb, general manager of Connect. "Four weeks later, they're professional drivers responsible for thousands of riders a day. Our operators are the face of Connect, and we're proud to welcome them."

[Graduate quote - KO to get one at the ceremony. Ask: "Why did you apply?"]

Connect is hiring for its next class, which begins in the new year. Applicants do not need a commercial driver license or experience driving a bus. Trainees are paid from their first day of class, and new operators receive a $2,000 hiring bonus, medical, dental and vision coverage, and a state retirement pension. Applicants must be at least twenty-one years old, hold a valid driver license, and have a good driving record.

Connect will hold a hiring fair Friday, October 30, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at its facility at 3021 N. 300 W. in North Logan, for operators, mechanics and Call-A-Ride drivers. Visitors can sit in the driver's seat, meet trainers and apply on the spot. Or apply anytime at rideconnectutah.gov/careers.

"If you like people and you like to drive, this is a career with real stability," Marcus Velling, Connect's operations manager, said. "Some of our operators have been here more than 20 years."

Connect's fixed-route fleet of 26 buses runs Monday through Saturday on the Loop and 14 numbered routes, and its Call-A-Ride vans serve riders whose disabilities prevent them from using the fixed routes. New operators typically start on the "extra board," covering open shifts across the system, before bidding on regular routes.

**About Connect Public Transit**
Connect Public Transit (the Cache Valley Transit District) provides zero-fare bus service and Call-A-Ride paratransit service from Lewiston to Hyrum, funded by a local sales tax and federal and state grants. Learn more at rideconnectutah.gov.
