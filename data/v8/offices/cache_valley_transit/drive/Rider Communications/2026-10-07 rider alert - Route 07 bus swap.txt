Channels: website alerts banner, Facebook, X, Transit app message
Posted by: K. Oberlin

Route 07: bus swap this afternoon, service back on schedule

This afternoon at about 3:10 p.m., a Route 07 bus heading toward Willow Park was taken out of service on 200 West after the driver noticed a brake warning and an odor. Out of an abundance of caution, the driver pulled over safely and stopped the bus. No one was hurt.

All 17 passengers were transferred to a replacement bus within about 20 minutes, and Route 07 was back on its regular schedule by 4:00 p.m. Riders waiting at stops along 200 West may have seen delays of up to 25 minutes.

The bus is with our maintenance team and will not return to service until it has been fully inspected and repaired.

Thank you for your patience, and thank you to our operator for a quick, safe response.

Questions: (435) 752-2877 or rideconnectutah.gov/contact
