*Kendra Oberlin, Communications. From the customer comment tracker, all channels (website form, phone, email, letters, front desk, social media messages). Prepared 10/6/26 for the managers meeting; shared with supervisors. Comments about Call-A-Ride trips are summarized separately in Gwen's monthly report.*

September is always our busiest month: USU is back, schools are back, and new riders are learning the system. Volume was up 20% from September 2025. The open house (9/24) added 19 written comment cards, logged as requests and suggestions.

## Response time

- Acknowledged within 1 business day: 96% (goal 95%).
- Closed within one business week: 88% (goal 90%). Misses were mostly complaints waiting on AVL video review in the week of 9/21.

## Compliments (17)

Every compliment was delivered to the operator by a supervisor and placed in the operator's file. The GM signed reply letters for nine of them; the rest were thanked by email from Communications.

Themes: operators waiting for a running rider (5); helping riders with mobility devices, walkers or strollers (4); patience with new riders and students (3); calling ahead to hold a connection (2); returning a lost item directly to the rider at the Transit Center before turn-in (1); general courtesy (2).

Sample (shared with the rider's permission): "The driver on the Blue Loop saw me struggling with a box and a cane and lowered the bus and waited until I was sitting before she pulled away. Please tell her she made my week."

## Requests and suggestions (66) by theme

Later evening service (18); more frequent service on the Loop and Route 01 (9); service to job centers on the west side of Logan (7); benches and shelters (11); bike racks and bikes inside the bus (6); real-time signs at stops (4); Spanish-language information (3); other (8). All were added to the route study comment file or the stop request log.

## Questions (112)

Most common: how to ride for the first time and where routes go (31); lost items (24); whether Connect is really free (11); holiday schedules (9); board meetings and public comment (6); advertising (4); jobs (13); other (14).

## What we changed

- Front desk now has two people on the phones Monday mornings, 8:00-9:00 a.m.
- Operators reminded at the 9/28 safety meeting not to leave timepoints early, with the four confirmed early departures discussed (without names).
- Transit Center schedule rack restocked daily during the first month of the semester.
- Planning is reviewing Route 12 morning running times (memo due 10/9).
- Stops reported as dark or unsafe at night were sent to the cities for street lighting.

## October to date (10/1-10/16)

Logged so far: 131 (compliments 9, complaints 27, requests 34, questions 61). Late-bus complaints are again led by Route 12 mornings (7); the planning memo of 10/9 recommends moving the morning trips earlier, and a decision is expected 10/19. Bike rack complaints dropped on the Loop Green buses carrying three-bike racks. Questions about the holidays have started ("do buses run the Friday after Thanksgiving?": yes, normal weekday service).

## Notes for October

Expect a jump in route study questions after 10/20. The front desk call guide has been updated with the survey, holiday, snow, bike and board meeting answers so most can be handled on the first call.
