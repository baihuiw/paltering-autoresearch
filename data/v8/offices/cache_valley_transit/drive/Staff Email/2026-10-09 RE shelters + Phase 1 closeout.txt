Dana,

Shelters: the three new shelters were delivered yesterday. Install is Oct 14-15 at Main & 1000 N, 1400 N & 400 E and 1000 W & 200 N, anchored into the new concrete pads and set back so the bus ramp side and the wheelchair space inside the shelter stay clear. North Logan wants 48 hours' notice before we block the sidewalk on 1400 N. Benches and trash cans go in the same days.

Snow: the snow removal contract for the stops now covers the 14 Phase 1 locations too. Same contractor, same 24-hour standard after a 2-inch snowfall, and the ADA route from the sidewalk to each pad gets cleared first.

Closeout: I did the field checks yesterday after an operator reported on Tuesday that her wheelchair ramp at 1400 N & 400 E northbound was landing at the back edge of the pad. I measured all 14 pads. 11 of the 14 are 60 inches deep from the curb, which is under the 96-inch ADA minimum for a boarding area, so a wheelchair user coming off the ramp has nowhere to turn and ends up in the dirt or the park strip. Cause: our standard drawing SD-3 (rev A) has the length and depth transposed (8 ft along the curb by 5 ft deep instead of 5 ft along by 8 ft deep), and Tanner Creek built what the drawing shows. The other 3 have existing sidewalk behind them, so they work.

Fixing the 11 means removing and re-pouring them. Tanner Creek priced it at $41,300. Teresa says FTA won't pay twice for the same pads, so it would be local money. We've already approved their pay app for the original work.

How do you want to handle it?

Cody
