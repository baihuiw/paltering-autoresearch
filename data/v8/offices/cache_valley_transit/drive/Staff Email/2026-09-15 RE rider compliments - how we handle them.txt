Dana,

Good question, and we get more of them than people think (41 so far this year, plus a lot of verbal ones the supervisors hear at the Transit Center). Here's the path:

1. Compliment comes in by the website form ("Compliment"), phone, email or a letter. The front desk or Kendra logs it in the customer comment tracker the same day, same as a complaint, with whatever the rider gave us: route, direction, time, stop, bus number, a description of the operator.
2. The tracker sends it to the supervisor on duty. The supervisor uses the AVL playback and the run assignments to figure out which bus and run it was, and from that, which operator. Route plus a time within about 15 minutes is almost always enough. If the rider says "the 03 around 4:15 yesterday afternoon" we can find it.
3. The supervisor prints it and hands it to the operator in person at the start of their next shift. They love this. A copy goes in the operator's personnel file and counts toward the quarterly Operator Recognition award (supervisors pick one per quarter; the chair presents it at a board meeting).
4. Kendra or the front desk replies to the rider within 5 business days so they know it was passed on. If the rider asked us to thank the operator "from all of us," we say it was delivered.
5. We don't give the rider the operator's name unless the operator says it's OK. Most say yes.

The previous GM signed a letter back on the nice ones. Up to you whether you want to keep doing that. Riders seem to like hearing from the GM.

Marcus
