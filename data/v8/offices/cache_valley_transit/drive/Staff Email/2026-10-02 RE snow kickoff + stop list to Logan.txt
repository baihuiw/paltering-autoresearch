Winter status for stops:

1. Snow contract (Kestrel Grounds, CVTD-26-019) is renewed for option year 2, same prices. You signed 9/21. Season starts Nov 1, but they've agreed to respond to an October storm at contract rates.
2. Priority list is 64 stops this year (was 58). The six added are stops that got buried repeatedly last winter or that riders wrote about, including 600 W & 200 N (Ms. Jeppson's letter) and Center & 1000 W.
3. Standard: every priority stop cleared within 24 hours after a snowfall of 2 inches or more ends. Contractor now has to send time-stamped photos within 2 hours of each visit, so I can check rather than take their word for it.
4. Non-priority stops: our two facilities techs, after the Transit Center and the priority list. Realistically 2-3 days after a big storm.
5. Logan City asked for the list of corner stops where their plows bury us. Sent today: 18 stops, with photos from last winter. The streets division said they'll brief their plow drivers but can't promise anything on the arterials during a storm. I'll send the same list to North Logan, Smithfield, Hyrum and Providence next week.
