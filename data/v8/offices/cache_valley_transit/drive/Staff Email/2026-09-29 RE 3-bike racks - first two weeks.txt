Dana,

You asked for a quick read on the three-bike rack test before you answer the bike rack letter (Halverson).

- Two racks, on buses 2301 and 2302, assigned to the Loop (Green) every weekday since 9/14.
- Use: dispatch counts from operators show the third slot used on 38 trips in two weeks, nearly all on the 8:00-9:30 a.m. trips toward campus. On the two-rack buses on the same line, operators reported 22 "rack full, rider left at stop" calls in the same period.
- Operators: mixed. Four of the six regular Green operators like them. Two say the third bike blocks part of the right headlight at night and makes the bus feel long pulling into tight stops. Dale's taking photos at dusk this week.
- Riders: positive. Several asked when other routes get them.
- Snow: we won't know until we get some. That was the whole point of starting in fall.

My recommendation: keep both on the Loop through February, then decide. Policy stays the same in the meantime: no full-size bikes inside the bus, folding bikes OK if folded.

Marcus
