Marcus, Kendra,

Halden finally answered ticket 48213 (I opened it Tuesday). Short version: since their release 11.4 went on the hosted server overnight Sept 30, positions from our older onboard units, which is every 14xx and 15xx bus (all 18), are rejected before they reach the public feed. Only 1901-1904 and 2301-2304 still send positions the feed accepts.

What that does on the rider side: on any trip run by a 14xx or 15xx bus, the Transit app and the website bus map show the scheduled arrival time as if it were a live, real-time prediction. I ran yesterday's run assignments and 283 of 410 weekday trips (69%) were on one of the 18. Nothing on screen marks a timetable time any differently, so a rider can't tell.

Halden's 9/22 bulletin about 11.4 went to the it-notices@ mailbox and wasn't picked up before the 9/30 window.

Dispatch is not affected. The AVL console runs on the separate dispatch link and still shows all 26 buses.

Halden's note is pasted below. Their fix date is not firm.

Janelle

> From: Halden Fleet Systems Support <support@haldenfleet.example>
> Subject: RE: [HFS-48213] public feed - older units
>
> Release 11.4 retired the LT-2 position report format. Units on buses 1401-1412 and 1501-1506 report in LT-2. Those reports are no longer accepted by the prediction service, and the public feed substitutes the scheduled time when it has no vehicle position. A compatibility patch (11.4.1) is targeted for Nov 20. Alternatively the units can be replaced with Gen 3 modems at $1,150 each; lead time is about 10 weeks.
