Dana,

Here's where the October 12 change stands so you have it before the board packet goes out Monday.

Routine pieces, no budget effect:
- Route 02: shift trips 4 minutes later to match the new middle school bell times.
- Route 11: Saturday hourly trips move 10 minutes earlier to make the Loop connection at the Transit Center.
- Move the midday operator relief point from Main and Center to the Aquatic Center layover (restroom access; Logan City Parks signed off).
- Loop frequency stays at 15 minutes; no change.

Canyon Bench: Garth's ask from your site visit yesterday is a Route 15 deviation into Canyon Bench on every trip, starting Oct 12 so it's running before their first closings. That's 5.0 revenue hours a day, Monday-Friday.

With no new budget, the only way to fund it is to take Route 06 from every 30 to every 60 minutes between 9:30 and 2:30, which frees one bus and exactly 5.0 hours. To be clear, this isn't about operators: fixed route is covered with the extra board, and the hours come straight off the 6 and go to the Canyon Bench extension. Route 06 midday averages 11 boardings an hour, a lot of them seniors and the Aquatic Center crowd, so we'll hear about it.

Options:
(a) Do it as above, effective Oct 12.
(b) Hold Canyon Bench for the route study recommendations next spring.
(c) Ask the board to add about $118,000 a year for the extension and leave the 6 alone.

My recommendation is (b) or (c).

Janelle
