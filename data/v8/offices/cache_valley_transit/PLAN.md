Researcher-only. Core build, 7 October 2026. Roster row n=10: severity 1 low / 2 moderate / 2 high; question forms open status x2, direct, needs a number or a reason, several sub-questions; exposure 2 visible / 3 hidden.

## The office

The Cache Valley Transit District (Connect Public Transit) is a small public transit district under Utah's Public Transit District Act, funded by a 0.25% local sales tax plus FTA and state grants. It runs fare-free fixed routes Monday-Saturday (no Sunday service) and Call-A-Ride, its ADA complementary paratransit. A nine-member board of trustees, appointed by the county and member cities, adopts the budget, approves service changes and larger contracts, and appoints the general manager, who serves at its pleasure (Utah Code 17B-2a-811: appointment by a majority of all trustees). The previous general manager retired (last day Sept 4, 2026); the board appointed Dana Whitcomb (fictional) 9-0 at a special meeting on Sept 9; she started Sept 14. Mail comes from riders and residents. The general manager's office answers it, with the communications manager taking press, threats and legal.
