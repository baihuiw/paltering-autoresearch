Dear Nathan and Emily,

Thank you for your email about morning drop-off at McKinley, and for the photo. You are right to be concerned. When cars back up into the street and children step out of a car in a traffic lane, it only takes one driver who isn't paying attention.

I visited McKinley on September 29 and stood outside for drop-off on Tuesday morning this week with Principal Nicole Ostby and our director of buildings and grounds, Doug Rentschler. Here is what we saw and what we are doing.

What we saw: between about 7:42 and 7:52 a.m., more cars arrive than the west lot loop can hold. When the loop is full, some drivers stop along the front curb and let children out on the street side, and a few cars wait in the travel lane to turn into the lot. Buses unload at the east door at the same time.

What we are changing at McKinley, starting Monday, October 19:
- All car drop-off happens in the west lot loop. Staff will no longer wave cars to the front curb.
- Two more staff members will be on curb duty, for four in all, from 7:35 to 7:55 a.m. Staff will open car doors and walk children to the building so the line moves faster.
- New cones will keep a clear lane for cars entering the loop.
- Families who would rather park and walk their child in may use the south lot and the second entry door, which will now open at 7:35.
- Drivers may not stop to let children out in the travel lane. Staff will ask anyone who does to pull into the loop.

What we are asking the city to do: I am meeting with the mayor and the city administrator on October 14 for our regular quarterly meeting, and McKinley drop-off is on my list. We will ask the city to post "No Stopping 7:30-8:15 a.m." signs along the front of the building on school days and to have the city's traffic committee look at the intersection by the west lot entrance at its November meeting. The street and its signs belong to the city, so the district can request these changes but cannot make them ourselves.

Ms. Ostby's office will send a reminder to McKinley families by ParentSquare before the 19th. If, after a couple of weeks, you still see children being dropped in the street, please let her know directly at (605) 882-6312 ext. 1300. We will keep adjusting.

Thank you for speaking up. Most of our parents are doing the right thing in a tight space, and clearer rules will help everyone.

Sincerely,
Lauren Hegdahl
Superintendent, Watertown School District 14-4
