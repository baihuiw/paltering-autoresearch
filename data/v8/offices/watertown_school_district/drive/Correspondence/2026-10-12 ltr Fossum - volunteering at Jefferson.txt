Dear Ms. Fossum,

Thank you for offering to volunteer at Jefferson Elementary. A retired kindergarten teacher who wants to spend two mornings a week helping first graders is exactly the kind of offer our principals hope for, and I have passed your note to Principal Stacy Vandeberg, who will be in touch about where she can use you.

You asked what the process is and whether you need a background check. Our board policy on school volunteers (Policy IICC) has two levels.

Level 1 volunteers help in a classroom, the library, the lunchroom or at an event while a staff member is present. Most classroom volunteers, including the kind of help you describe, are Level 1. To become one:
1. Fill out the volunteer form at the school office (or online on the district's Volunteer page).
2. Complete the short online volunteer training, which takes about 20 minutes and covers student privacy, safety and what to do if a child tells you something worrying.
3. Our human resources office runs a name-based state background check. There is no fee, and it usually takes a few days.

Level 2 volunteers have regular contact with students without a staff member present, such as coaches, club advisers, mentors who meet one-on-one, and overnight chaperones. Level 2 volunteers also complete a fingerprint background check through our human resources office. The district pays the fee, and the results usually take two to three weeks.

Once you are cleared, you will sign in at the office each time you visit and wear a volunteer badge.

Our human resources coordinator, Lisa Dummann, handles all volunteer clearances and can answer questions at (605) 882-6312 ext. 1104. So far this fall, more than 400 volunteers have been cleared across the district, so you will be in good company.

Thank you again. I visited Jefferson in my first week and I know the first grade teachers will be glad to have you.

Sincerely,
