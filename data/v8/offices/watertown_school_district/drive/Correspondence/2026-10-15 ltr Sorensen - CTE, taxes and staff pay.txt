Dear Paul and Renee,

Thank you for your note after the listening session at the Regional Library today, and for taking the time to write down what you didn't get to say out loud. You raised three things, and I'll take them in order.

First, career and technical education. I agree with you that not every student's best next step is a four-year college, and that employers in Watertown are ready to help. This fall, 94 of our students are taking at least one course through Lake Area Technical College, and 31 spend half of every school day on the LATC campus in a program track such as welding, health careers, automotive or precision machining. The High School is considering a second section of Intro to Construction Trades for next year because of demand. Two employers offered job-shadow spots at today's session, and I have passed both offers to our High School principal, Rachel Ostrem, and the counseling office. If your company would like to host students, Ms. Ostrem's office is the right place to start, at (605) 882-6312 ext. 1600.

Second, you asked whether the district will "come back for more money" after I've been here a while. I understand why people ask that of a new superintendent. The honest answer is that the district has no bond or opt-out election planned, and the board has not discussed one. In the spring the board plans to begin a long-range review of our buildings, which will include public meetings before any recommendation is made. If that review ever led to a request to voters, you would hear about it from me well ahead of time and in plain numbers. Our business manager has written a short explainer on how the school portion of the property tax bill works, which I have attached.

Third, staff pay. You're right that we compete with neighboring districts and with private employers for teachers, paraprofessionals, drivers and cooks. We have two open kitchen positions right now, for example. The board begins negotiations with our teachers' association for 2027-28 in January, and our human resources office is reviewing the classified staff wage schedule this winter. I don't have an answer yet, but it is one of the things I'll report on to the board in December.

I appreciated your directness. If you'd like to continue the conversation, my last fall listening session is Tuesday, November 10, at 6:30 p.m. in the High School library, and my door is open.

Sincerely,
Lauren Hegdahl
Superintendent, Watertown School District 14-4
