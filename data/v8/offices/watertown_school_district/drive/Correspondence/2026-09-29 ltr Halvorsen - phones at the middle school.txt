Dear Ms. Halvorsen,

Thank you for writing about phones at the Middle School, and for being so specific. Your description of what your daughter sees in her afternoon classes, with students checking messages under their desks and filming one another in the hallway, matches what I have heard from several teachers in my first week of building visits.

Here is where things stand. Our current board policy on personal electronic devices, Policy JICJ, was last revised in 2019. It sets district-wide expectations about misuse (bullying, recording others without permission, cheating) but leaves the classroom rules to each building. At the Middle School today, phones are supposed to be "away during class," with teachers deciding whether students may use them for a classroom activity, and students may use them at lunch. As you point out, "away" means different things in different rooms, and that inconsistency is hard on teachers and students alike.

I don't think this is something I should decide alone in my second week, but I also don't think it should wait for a year of study. My plan is to form a Personal Device Review Committee this fall, with parents, teachers, students and principals from every level, chaired by our assistant superintendent, Craig Tollefsrud. The committee will look at what our own buildings do now, what has worked in other South Dakota and Minnesota districts, and what families think, and bring a recommendation to the school board. Mr. Kludt, the Middle School principal, has already told me he would welcome a stronger, clearer rule.

If you would be willing to serve on the committee, or would like to suggest another parent, please let my assistant, Tammy Rieck, know at (605) 882-6312 ext. 1100. We will also survey families in November.

I also hope you can come to one of my listening sessions: Tuesday, October 6, at 6:30 p.m. at the Intermediate School commons, or Thursday, October 15, at noon at the Regional Library. I expect phones will come up at both.

Thank you again for writing.

Sincerely,
Lauren Hegdahl
Superintendent, Watertown School District 14-4
