Dear Karen,

Thank you for writing about the attendance area proposal and for coming to Monday's hearing. I know how much your family loves Lincoln, and I heard the same thing from many of the parents who spoke.

I want to be clear about why the board is looking at this. Lincoln has 486 students in grades 1-4 this year in a building designed for 450. Music is taught from a cart, and some 3rd and 4th grade classes have 27 or 28 students. Jefferson, about a mile away, has room.

The options were drawn by Brightline Demographics, an independent enrollment planning firm, using the criteria in board policy: building capacity, safe walking routes, keeping neighborhoods together and moving as few students as possible. Your street would move to Jefferson under Option B-1. Your son, who is in 4th grade, would not move; your daughter would attend Jefferson starting in fall 2027 unless a transfer is approved.

Transfer requests for 2027-28 are due February 1. Assistant Superintendent Craig Tollefsrud can walk you through the process at (605) 882-6312.

The board expects to act on October 12. Thank you again for taking the time to write.

Sincerely,
Lauren Hegdahl
Superintendent, Watertown School District 14-4
