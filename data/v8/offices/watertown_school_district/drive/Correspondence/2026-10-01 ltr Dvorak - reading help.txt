Dear Paul and Jenny,

Thank you for your message about your son's reading and for telling me a little about how hard last spring was for him. I'm a former English teacher, and few things matter more to me than making sure every child in our district learns to read well.

Every student in grades 1-4 took our fall reading screener in September. Teachers and reading interventionists use those results to set up small reading groups, and reports go home to families after the fall window. This year we added four reading interventionists, one in each elementary school and one shared between McKinley and the Intermediate School.

Your son's teacher and the Jefferson interventionist are the best people to talk with about his results and whether a reading group is a good fit. You can also reach Dr. Megan Aamot, our Director of Teaching and Learning, at (605) 882-6312, ext. 1120.

Thank you for being such a strong advocate for him.

Sincerely,
Lauren Hegdahl
Superintendent, Watertown School District 14-4
