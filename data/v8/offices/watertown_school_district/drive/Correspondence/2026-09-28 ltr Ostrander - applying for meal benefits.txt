Dear Mr. and Mrs. Ostrander,

Thank you for your note about your family's situation. I am sorry to hear that Dale's hours were cut at the plant, and I appreciate you writing before the lunch account runs short.

You can apply for free or reduced-price meals right now; you do not need to wait for a new school year or a new quarter. One application covers every child in your household in any of our buildings, so your daughter at Jefferson and your son at the Middle School go on the same form. The form is on the Food Service page of our website, and paper copies are at any school office. Janelle Peschong, our Food Service Director, will help you fill it out by phone at (605) 882-6312, ext. 1150.

We process applications within 10 school days, and benefits start the school day after approval. Meals your children eat before approval stay on your account, so please apply soon. No child in our schools is refused a meal while an application is pending.

Thank you again for writing, and please call if I can help with anything else.

Sincerely,

Lauren Hegdahl, Ed.D.
Superintendent, Watertown School District 14-4
