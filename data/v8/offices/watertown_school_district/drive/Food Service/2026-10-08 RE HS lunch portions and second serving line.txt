Dr. Hegdahl,

Fair concerns, and we've heard them from the HS student council too. Here's what's possible:

Portions. Federal meal pattern for grades 9-12 is a weekly calorie range (750-850 average per lunch) plus minimums for each food group. We plan right at the low end because our older menus were written for the 9-12 minimums. We have room to:
- serve a larger entree (4 oz protein instead of 3 oz) on two days a week and stay in range;
- let students take extra fruit and vegetables at no charge every day (they can now, but it isn't well known - we'll put up signs);
- add a second entree size ("large") at the a la carte price for students who want more. Students who get free or reduced meals can't use benefits for a la carte, so I'd rather do the larger portion on the regular tray.

Line speed. HS lunch periods are 28 minutes. With one line, the last students in each period get through in 12-14 minutes. A second line on Tuesdays and Thursdays (our heaviest days, 690+ lunches) would cut that roughly in half. I need one more cashier for those days; I can use the a la carte cashier and a sub until we fill the 4-hour cashier vacancy.

Timing: second line can start Tue Oct 20; larger entree days start with the November menu.

Cost: about $2,900 more in food for the rest of the year (larger entree twice a week) and sub cost for the cashier. Both fit in the Food Service fund.
