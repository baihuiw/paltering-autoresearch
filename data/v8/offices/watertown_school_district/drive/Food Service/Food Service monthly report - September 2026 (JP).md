## 2. Free and reduced-price eligibility

As of Sept 30: 1,212 students approved free and 248 approved reduced, out of 3,926 enrolled (37.2% combined). Direct certification letters went out Aug 14. We processed 341 paper and online applications in August and September; 46 came in during September. The Oct 1 count by building is in "Free and reduced eligibility counts by building Oct 1 2026.csv".

## 4. Staffing

- 41 positions across 7 kitchens and the central bakery. Two vacancies (Middle School 5-hour cook, High School 4-hour cashier), both posted through HR. Substitutes covered 63 shifts in September; our sub list is down to 8 people.
- Food safety refresher training held Aug 19 for all staff. Two new cooks are scheduled for the state food safety course in Nov..

## 5. Equipment

- High School dish machine booster heater replaced Sept 10 (Northside Heating & Cooling, $3,180, Food Service fund).
- Intermediate School walk-in cooler door gasket replaced (work order through buildings and grounds).
- McKinley's convection oven is 22 years old; replacement request is in the 2027-28 equipment list.

## 6. Menu and family feedback

- The second entree at the Intermediate and Middle School is working; the most popular choices are chicken tortilla soup with a sandwich half, and the build-your-own rice bowl.
- At the Oct 6 listening session, parents raised portion sizes at the High School and how fast the lines move. We are adding a second serving line at the HS on Tuesdays and Thursdays (the busiest days) starting Oct 20, and a larger entree for grades 9-12 on two menu days per week, within federal calorie ranges.
- Several families asked for the menu in the ParentSquare app. Kayla Brende posts the monthly menu there now.

## 7. Health inspections

City/state inspections this fall: McKinley (Sept 16) and Middle School (Sept 23), no critical items. One non-critical note at the Middle School (thermometer missing from a reach-in cooler), corrected the same day.
