Breakfast is served at every building starting at 7:30 a.m. Lunch is served on each building's schedule. Menus are posted monthly on the Food Service page and in the ParentSquare app; November menus will be posted October 26.

Students approved for free meals pay nothing for breakfast or lunch. Preschool and junior kindergarten students in half-day sessions eat breakfast or a snack depending on their session.

## Who can apply

Any household can apply for free or reduced-price meals. Eligibility depends on household size and income, or on whether someone in the household receives SNAP or TANF benefits. Foster children and children who are homeless, migrant or runaway may qualify regardless of household income; call our office and we will help.

Some families are approved automatically ("direct certification") through state benefit records. If you received a letter from us in August saying your children are approved, you do not need to apply.

## One application per household

Fill out **one application for your whole household**, and list every child who lives with you and attends any Watertown school: McKinley, any elementary, the Intermediate School, the Middle School or the High School. You do not need a separate form for each school or each child. If your children attend different buildings, the approval covers all of them.

## You can apply any time during the school year

Applications are accepted all year. If your household income drops, someone loses a job, or your household gets bigger, you can apply at that time, even in the middle of the year. A family that was not eligible in August may be eligible now.

## How to apply

- **Online:** Food Service page at watertown.k12.sd.us (Families > Food Service > Apply for Meal Benefits). The online form takes about 10 minutes.
- **Paper:** pick up a form at any school office or the District Office, 200 9th St NE. Return it to any school office or mail it to Food Service, Watertown School District 14-4, 200 9th St NE, Watertown, SD 57201.

You will need the names of everyone in your household, each person's income and how often it is received (weekly, every two weeks, monthly), and the last four digits of an adult's Social Security number, or a SNAP or TANF case number instead of income.

## What happens next

- We process applications within 10 school days of receiving them, usually faster.
- You will get a letter telling you whether your children are approved for free meals, reduced-price meals, or not approved.
- Benefits start the school day after approval. Meals charged to your account before approval are not refunded, so please apply as soon as you can.
- A small number of applications are selected each fall for verification, as federal rules require. If yours is selected, we will write to you and ask for income documents.

## Your information is private

Meal benefit information is confidential. Teachers and other students do not know who receives free or reduced-price meals; every student uses the same lunch number at the register. With your written permission, we can share your approval with programs such as activity fee waivers or the high school's testing fee assistance.

## Meal accounts

Each family has one meal account shared by all children. You can add money online or send cash or a check to any school office. See "Meal account and charge procedure" for low-balance notices. No child is ever refused a meal.

## Special diets

A student who needs a meal change for a disability or a medical reason should turn in the Special Diet Statement, signed by a medical provider, to the school nurse or our office.
