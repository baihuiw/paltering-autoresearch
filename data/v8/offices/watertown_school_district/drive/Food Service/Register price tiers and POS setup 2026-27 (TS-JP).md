# Register Price Tiers and POS Setup, 2026-27

Watertown School District 14-4 | Food Service with Technology
Maintained by Tyler Stensland (Technology Director) and Janelle Peschong (Food Service Director). Last table change: Sept 3, 2026 (HS lunch corrected to $3.55).

## 1. How the registers price a meal

Each student has a lunch number. When a cashier enters the number, the register looks up the student's tier (free, reduced-price or paid) and the price for that tier and grade band in the price table. The student's eligibility is set on the student's record when Food Service approves an application, or when direct certification matches are loaded in August. The price table itself is edited by Technology at Food Service's request and is synced to all seven kitchens at 6:00 a.m. each school day.

## 4. Changes to the table

1. Food Service sends the request by email to Technology.
2. Technology changes the table and records the version in the sync log.
3. The next morning each kitchen manager rings one sample meal and confirms the price on the screen.
