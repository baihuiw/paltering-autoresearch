Menus to Kayla for the website and ParentSquare by Thu 10/22; posted Mon 10/26.

CYCLE
- Week 4 of the fall cycle runs Nov 2-6, then back to week 1 Nov 9. Thanksgiving week is short (Mon-Tue only; no school Wed Nov 25-Fri Nov 27).
- 17 serving days in November. Veterans Day Nov 11 is a regular school day; HS assembly at 9:30 may push HS first lunch 10 minutes. Confirm with Rachel Ostrem's office.

ELEMENTARY (McKinley, Jefferson, Lincoln, Fieldcrest)
- Keep one entree plus the daily sandwich/yogurt alternate. The alternate is chosen by about 11% of K-4 students; not worth a second hot entree yet.
- New in November: turkey and cheese sub (replaces the pre-packaged "uncrustable" day), cheesy chicken and rice bake, sweet potato fries on Fridays.
- Drop the fish sandwich on Nov 13 (Family Literacy Night is Nov 12 at the Intermediate - no connection, but Fieldcrest and Jefferson cooks asked for a lighter prep day after the Thursday event because two of them work it). Replace with chicken nuggets.

INTERMEDIATE AND MIDDLE SCHOOL
- Second entree choice continues every day. Top sellers in September: chicken tortilla soup with half sandwich (MS), rice bowl (Int), pizza (both, always).
- Rotate the rice bowl from weekly to twice a week at the Middle School per student council request (Ben Kludt forwarded, 10/2).
- Fewer pre-packaged items: we are down to two per week from five last year. Scratch cooking adds about 40 minutes of prep a day at the MS; the 5-hour cook vacancy hurts here. HR reposted 10/8.

HIGH SCHOOL
- Portion feedback from the Oct 6 listening session and the HS student council: students say lunches are too small. Our grade 9-12 lunches meet the federal calorie range (750-850 average for the week), but we are at the low end. Plan: a larger entree portion (4 oz protein instead of 3 oz) on Tuesdays and Thursdays, plus extra fruit and vegetable available every day at no charge.
- Second serving line Tue/Thu starts Oct 20. Need one more cashier; using the a la carte cashier and a sub until the 4-hour cashier position is filled.
- Breakfast to go ("grab and go" cart) by the commons entrance continues; average 152 per day in September.

THANKSGIVING MEAL - TUESDAY NOV 24
- All buildings: roast turkey, mashed potatoes and gravy, stuffing, green beans, cranberry sauce, dinner roll, pumpkin bar.
- Order turkey breast from Lake Region by Nov 9 (USDA Foods turkey roast allotment is 820 lb; we need about 1,050 lb, so buy the rest commercially).
- Adult guest meal $5.25. Elementary offices need guest counts by Nov 18.

SPECIAL DIETS

- Sub cards for every building must be updated with the November menu; Fieldcrest's binder had an October card missing (fixed 10/9).

MILK AND BREAD
