# McKinley Early Learning Center: Morning Drop-off and Afternoon Pickup Plan

Revised October 15, 2026 | Nicole Ostby, Principal, and Doug Rentschler, Director of Buildings and Grounds
Replaces the August 2026 version. Changes take effect **Monday, October 19, 2026**. Shared with McKinley staff 10/15 and with families by ParentSquare 10/15.

## Why we are changing it

McKinley serves about 318 preschool, junior kindergarten and kindergarten students, and most arrive by car. Between about 7:42 and 7:52 a.m., more cars arrive than the west lot loop holds. Since the last week of September, cars have backed up into the street, some drivers have let children out from the travel lane, and cars waiting to turn into the lot have blocked through traffic. Staff counted 6-11 street-side drop-offs per morning during the week of Oct 5. Several parents have written to the superintendent. Our goal is that no child gets out of a car anywhere except at the curb inside the west lot loop or in the south lot with a parent.

## Morning drop-off (7:35-7:55 a.m.)

1. **Car line: west lot loop only.** Enter from the west lot entrance, pull forward as far as possible, and stay in your car. Staff will open the door and walk your child to the building. Please have backpacks on and children ready to step out on the curb side.
2. **No drop-off on the street.** Do not stop on the front street or let children out in the travel lane, even if the line is long. Staff will ask drivers who stop in the street to pull into the loop.
3. **Park and walk in: south lot.** Families who want to walk their child in may park in the south lot and use the south entry door, which now opens at 7:35 a.m. (was 7:45). A staff member is at the south door.
4. **Buses** unload at the east door as before (changed in September). Cars may not use the east bus lane from 7:30 to 8:00 a.m.
5. **Staffing.** Four staff members on curb duty from 7:35 to 7:55 (was two): two at the loop curb, one at the south door, one at the west lot entrance directing cars. The principal or office manager will be outside most mornings.
6. **Cones.** 24 new cones mark a clear entry lane and the loop curb (WO 26-1874).
7. **Arrivals after 7:55** park and walk in through the main office.

Tip from staff: the line is shortest before 7:40 and after 7:50.

## Afternoon pickup

The afternoon is calmer because pickup is spread over two dismissals (the morning preschool session ends at 11:15 a.m.; full-day dismissal is 3:05 p.m.).
- Car pickup in the west lot loop with the family's name card on the dashboard. Staff bring children to the car.
- Walk-up pickup at the south door.
- No pickup from the street.

## Requests to the city

The street and its signs belong to the city. On October 14, Dr. Hegdahl raised McKinley drop-off with the mayor and the city administrator at their quarterly meeting. The district asked the city to:
1. Post "No Stopping 7:30-8:15 a.m. School Days" signs along the front of the building, on both sides of the entrance.
2. Repaint the crosswalk at the west lot entrance before winter.
3. Have the city traffic committee review the west lot entrance intersection (left-turn lane or time-limited turn restriction) at its November meeting.

The city agreed to place the request on the traffic committee's November agenda and to look at repainting before winter. Signs would require City Council approval after the committee's recommendation; staff expect December or January at the earliest. The district does not control the timing.

## Winter

On snow days and two-hour delay days, the south lot may be partly closed for snow piles. The car line moves to the west loop only, and the south door opens at the regular time. Riverside Lawn & Snow clears the McKinley lots under the district's snow removal contract (starting at 3:00 a.m. after 2 inches or more).

## Review

Doug and Nicole will count street-side drop-offs for two weeks after the change (Oct 19-30) and report at the admin team meeting on Nov 2. If street drop-offs continue, options include staggered arrival times by classroom and a second staffed curb at the south lot.
