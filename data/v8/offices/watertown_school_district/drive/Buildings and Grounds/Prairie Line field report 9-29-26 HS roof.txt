1. GENERAL
Membrane is installed over 100% of the roof area (main gym, auxiliary gym, commons). Crew of 4 on site finishing coping on the east elevation. The contractor stated the crew will move to another project the week of October 5 and return to complete the punch list "when weather allows."

2. SUBSTANTIAL COMPLETION
The work is not yet substantially complete. Outstanding items affect watertightness at the north parapet of the main gym and at two roof drains. A Certificate of Substantial Completion will not be issued until items 1-6 below are corrected and reinspected. Contractor's Pay Application No. 4 (marked final) was received by PLA on September 25 and has not been certified; PLA returned it to the contractor with this report.

3. PUNCH LIST (23 items; first 10 shown, full list attached)
1. Main gym north parapet: counterflashing not installed for approx. 120 LF; membrane termination exposed. Water entry likely in wind-driven rain.
2. Drain D-4 (main gym): clamping ring not seated; reset and test.
3. Drain D-7 (commons): drain body not sealed to deck; leak path.
4. Overflow scupper S-2: missing sheet metal collar.
5. Pipe penetration (exhaust fan EF-3): pre-molded boot missing.
6. Expansion joint, gym/commons: cover not fastened 18 LF.
7. Coping east elevation: 4 sections short; joint covers missing.
8. Walkway pads to RTU-1 and RTU-2 not installed.
9. Debris and fasteners on commons roof; magnet sweep required.
10. Closeout: as-builts, manufacturer's inspection request, lien waivers not received.

4. SCHEDULE
Original substantial completion August 28; contractor is 32 days behind. Liquidated damages provisions apply per the contract; PLA recommends the owner reserve its rights in writing.

5. ACTION REQUIRED
Contractor: correct items 1-6 before the next rain event and request reinspection.
Owner: no further payment until substantial completion is certified.
