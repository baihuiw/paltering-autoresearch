Project: Fieldcrest Elementary, lower level (1928 building and 1964 addition), 619 2nd St NW

SCOPE
Active sub-slab depressurization for the lower level of Fieldcrest Elementary:
- Diagnostic communication testing (pressure field extension) at 6 locations, 1 day.
- Two suction points with dedicated inline radon fans (one each for the 1928 and 1964 slabs), schedule 40 PVC risers routed through the existing chase to the roof, roof penetrations flashed by our roofing subcontractor.
- Seal accessible slab cracks, floor drains and pipe penetrations in Rooms 2-10, LL-12, LL-14 and the boiler room.
- System monitors (manometers) at each riser; fan failure alarm wired to the building automation system.
- Post-mitigation short-term test in every lower-level room 24 hours to 30 days after installation; results to the district in writing.
