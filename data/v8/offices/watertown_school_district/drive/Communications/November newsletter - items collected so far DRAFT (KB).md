## Winter weather: are you getting our messages?

Before the first snow, please take two minutes to check:
- **Is your phone number and email current?** If you've changed numbers, call your school office. Our messages go to every parent and guardian listed in Infinite Campus, and updates made by the office take effect the next morning.
- **Have you opted in to text messages?** Reply "Y" to the ParentSquare welcome text, or text "Y" to 67587. Only about two-thirds of our families get our texts right now.
- **Do you have the ParentSquare app?** It's free and lets you choose how you're notified.

When weather is a concern, we decide by 5:30 a.m. and send notices by 6:00 a.m. by phone call, text, email and app, and post them on our website, Facebook and local radio and TV. A two-hour delay means routes run two hours later, there is no morning preschool or junior kindergarten, and breakfast is not served. Three snow days are built into the calendar; makeup days, if needed, are February 15 and April 2. Full details: watertown.k12.sd.us/weather.

## Phones and personal devices: tell us what you think

A committee of parents, teachers, students and principals is reviewing the district's policy on student phones and other devices. Families will receive a short survey by ParentSquare **November 2-13**. The committee will share its progress with the school board on November 23 and bring a recommendation in January. Until then, each building's current rules stay in place.
