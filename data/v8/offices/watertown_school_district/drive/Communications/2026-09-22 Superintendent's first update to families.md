# From the Superintendent: first update to families

**Watertown School District 14-4 | Tuesday, September 22, 2026**
Sent by ParentSquare and email; posted at watertown.k12.sd.us/news

Dear Watertown families and staff,

Yesterday was my first day as your superintendent, and I want to start by saying thank you. Thank you to the school board for its trust, to Craig Tollefsrud for leading the district so steadily as acting superintendent these past four weeks, and to the teachers, bus drivers, custodians, cooks and paraprofessionals who had our schools running smoothly long before I arrived.

I spent my first morning riding a bus route out past Lake Kampeska and my first afternoon at Lincoln and the Intermediate School. Over the next two months I will visit every building at least twice, and I will hold three open listening sessions for parents and community members (dates below). I would much rather hear about a concern early than late.

## What's coming this fall

**Rural families: the late activity route returns.** Starting Monday, November 2, the late activity route returns for students who live in the country and stay after school for practices, rehearsals and clubs. Buses will leave the High School at 5:45 p.m. and the Middle School at 5:55 p.m., Monday through Friday, on three rural loops (north and Lake Kampeska, east, and south). The route was cut two years ago, and many of you have told us how hard that has made it for rural students to take part in winter sports and activities. If your student will ride, please fill out the sign-up form on the Transportation page by Friday, October 9 so we can plan stops.

**New in-town stop.** At parents' request, Kampeska Bus Lines added a morning stop at 14th Avenue SE and 4th Street SE on Route 6, starting September 28.

**Attendance area study.** Lincoln Elementary is well over its capacity and Jefferson has room. Our enrollment consultant, Brightline Demographics, will present options to the board at its work session on Monday, September 28, at 5:30 p.m. at the District Office. A public hearing will follow on Monday, October 5. Any change would take effect in fall 2027, not this year.

**Parent-teacher conferences** are Thursday, October 22 (3:30-7:30 p.m.) and Friday, October 23 (8:00 a.m.-noon) at all buildings. There is no school for students on Friday, October 23.

## Listening sessions

- Tuesday, October 6, 6:30 p.m., Intermediate School commons
- Thursday, October 15, 12:00 noon, Watertown Regional Library community room
- Tuesday, November 10, 6:30 p.m., High School library

Bring questions. No sign-up needed.

## A few housekeeping notes

- Picture retakes are October 14 at the elementary schools and October 15 at the Intermediate and Middle School.
- Homecoming week at the High School is October 5-9; the parade is Friday, October 9, at 2:00 p.m. downtown.
- Free and reduced-price meal applications are accepted all year. Apply online through the Food Service page or pick up a paper form at any school office.

I'm proud to be here. See you around our schools.

Lauren Hegdahl, Ed.D.
Superintendent
