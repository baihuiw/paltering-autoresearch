# Winter Weather: School Closings, Late Starts and Early Dismissals

Web page text | watertown.k12.sd.us/weather | Revised September 2026 (KB) for the 2026-27 school year. Reviewed by Dr. Hegdahl 9/29.

## How we decide

When snow, ice, extreme cold or blowing snow could make travel unsafe, the superintendent makes the call by 5:30 a.m. The decision is based on a road check that starts at 4:30 a.m. by our transportation coordinator, the buildings and grounds staff and the transportation contractor's dispatcher, along with National Weather Service forecasts and conversations with neighboring districts and the city street department.

We look at:
- whether routes can safely run, including gravel and township roads;
- whether parking lots, sidewalks and building entrances can be cleared in time;
- wind chill (we consider closing or a late start at forecast wind chills of about -35 degrees or colder during the morning pickup window);
- whether conditions are expected to get better or worse during the day.

Every family knows its own situation best. If you believe it is unsafe for your child to travel, keep them home and call the school office; the absence will be excused.

## How you will be notified

Notices go out **by 6:00 a.m.** whenever possible:

1. **ParentSquare**: a phone call, text message, email and app notification to every parent and guardian contact in our student information system (Infinite Campus).
2. **District website** (banner at the top of every page) and the district Facebook page.
3. **Local radio and TV stations** in Watertown and Sioux Falls.

**You do not need to sign up.** Every parent or guardian listed for a student in Infinite Campus receives ParentSquare messages automatically. To make sure you get them:
- Keep your phone number and email current with your school office (new families: please check the information on your enrollment form with the office).
- To receive text messages, reply "Y" to the ParentSquare welcome text, or text "Y" to 67587.
- Download the free ParentSquare app and choose how you want to be notified.

If you are not getting messages, call your building office or the District Office at (605) 882-6312.

## What each notice means

**Two-hour late start**
- School starts two hours later than normal. Every route runs two hours after its usual pickup time.
- No morning preschool or junior kindergarten sessions; afternoon sessions run as usual.
- Breakfast is not served.
- Before-school activities and practices are cancelled.

**Early dismissal**
- We will announce the dismissal time by ParentSquare at least one hour ahead whenever possible. Please make sure your child knows where to go if you are not home.
- After-school and evening activities are cancelled.

**School closed**
- No school for students. Activities, practices and community use of buildings are cancelled unless the activities director announces otherwise by 2:00 p.m.
- Staff should follow the employee weather procedure.

**Hard-surface routes only**
Some mornings the main roads are fine but gravel roads are not. On those days some stops on gravel roads will not be served and families will be asked to bring students to the nearest hard-surface stop or to school. Families on affected stops are notified by ParentSquare.

## Makeup days

Three snow days are built into the 2026-27 calendar. If we use more than three, the first makeup days are **Monday, February 15, 2027** and **Friday, April 2, 2027**. Any other makeup days will be set by the school board.
