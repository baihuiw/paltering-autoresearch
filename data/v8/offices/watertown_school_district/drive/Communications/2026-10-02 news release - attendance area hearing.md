# Public hearing Monday on elementary attendance areas for 2027-28

WATERTOWN - The Watertown School Board will hold a public hearing on proposed changes to elementary attendance areas at 6:30 p.m. Monday, October 5, in the Watertown High School auditorium.

Lincoln Elementary enrolls 486 students in grades 1-4 this fall in a building designed for 450. Jefferson Elementary enrolls 392 in a building designed for 475. The board hired Brightline Demographics in April to study options for balancing enrollment among the three elementary schools.

Two options are posted today at watertown.k12.sd.us/attendance-areas:

- **Option A** would move about 72 students in the northeast corner of the Lincoln area to Jefferson.
- **Option B-1** would move about 109 students in the area between 14th and 20th Streets NE and 4th and 9th Avenues NE to Jefferson.

Under either option, current 4th graders would not move, and changes would take effect in fall 2027.

"Our Lincoln teachers and families have been making do with a building that is too small for a long time," said Superintendent Lauren Hegdahl. "We want to hear from every family before the board decides. Please come Monday or send us your comments."

Written comments may be sent to attendance@k12.sd.us or mailed to the District Office, 200 9th St NE, by Friday, October 9. The board may act at its regular meeting on October 12.
