# 2027-28 Elementary Attendance Areas: Frequently Asked Questions

Posted at watertown.k12.sd.us/attendance-areas | Updated October 13, 2026 (after board action)

**What did the board decide?**
On October 12, 2026, the Board of Education voted 5-0 to adopt Option B-1 of the attendance area study. Starting in fall 2027, the area roughly between 14th and 20th Streets NE and 4th and 9th Avenues NE moves from Lincoln Elementary to Jefferson Elementary. About 109 students in grades 1-4 are affected, based on this year's enrollment.

**Why change anything?**
Lincoln has 486 students in grades 1-4 in a building designed for 450, and new housing north of 20th Street NE will add more. Jefferson has room. Under Option B-1, Lincoln drops to about 84% of capacity and Jefferson rises to about 92% in fall 2027.

**Who drew the lines?**
The board hired Brightline Demographics, an independent enrollment planning firm, in April 2026. Brightline used current enrollment, housing projections and the board's criteria in Policy JC: building capacity, safe walking routes, keeping neighborhoods together and moving as few students as possible.

**What was the public process?**
The options were presented at the board's September 28 work session and posted online October 2. The board held a public hearing on October 5 (64 people attended; 14 spoke) and accepted written comments through October 9. The board acted at its regular meeting on October 12.

**Why does the line step up to 17th Street between 6th and 9th Avenues?**
The adopted map includes minor adjustments made between the September 28 draft and the October 5 hearing. The full study and both maps are on this page.

**My child is in 4th grade now. Does he move?**
No. Current 4th graders go on to the Intermediate School as usual. Current 1st-3rd graders in the area attend Jefferson starting fall 2027.

**Can my child stay at Lincoln?**
You may request an intradistrict transfer under Regulation JC-R by February 1, 2027. Transfers are approved when the receiving school has space in that grade. Lincoln is expected to remain near capacity in some grades, so not every request can be approved. A younger sibling of a student finishing 4th grade at Lincoln may request a transfer to stay with the older sibling.

**Will there be busing?**
Most students in the area live within the walk zone for Jefferson. The crossing guard at 7th Avenue NE and 14th Street NE stays in place.

**When will we know our child's school for 2027-28?**
Families will receive a letter in January 2027.
