## School meals at Watertown

Breakfast is served at every building starting at 7:30 a.m., and lunch is served on each building's schedule. Menus are posted monthly on this page and in the ParentSquare app. November menus will be posted October 26.

## Prices 2026-27

- Breakfast: $2.10 (McKinley and grades 1-4), $2.35 (grades 5-12)
- Lunch: $3.15 (McKinley and grades 1-4), $3.40 (grades 5-8), $3.55 (grades 9-12)
- Reduced-price breakfast $0.30; reduced-price lunch $0.40
- Free meals: no charge for breakfast or lunch
- Extra milk $0.55; adult lunch $5.25

## Apply any time

Any household can apply for free or reduced-price meals at any time of year. One application covers every child in the household at any Watertown school. Applications are processed within 10 school days, and benefits start the school day after approval. Apply online (Families > Food Service > Apply for Meal Benefits) or pick up a paper form at any school office or the District Office.

## Your family's meal account

Each family has one meal account for all of its children. You can add money online, or send cash or a check made out to Watertown School District to any school office. No child is refused a meal. A weekly email reminds families when the account is below $10.00.

## Second serving line at the High School

Starting Tuesday, Oct 20, the High School adds a second serving line on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Larger entrees on two days a week begin with the November menu.
