# The Arrow Update: October 2026

Watertown School District 14-4 family newsletter | Sent Tuesday, October 13, 2026 by ParentSquare and email

## From Dr. Hegdahl

Three weeks in, I've been in every one of our buildings, and I've eaten lunch in four cafeterias (the chicken tortilla soup at the Middle School is a highlight). Thank you to the 38 parents who came to the first listening session on October 6. I heard a lot about class sizes, phones in classrooms and the new lunch menu, and I'm taking all of it back to our team. Two more sessions are coming up: Thursday, October 15, at noon at the Watertown Regional Library, and Tuesday, November 10, at 6:30 p.m. in the High School library.

## Reading: a strong start

More than 1,000 students in grades 1-4 completed fall reading screening in September, and our four new reading interventionists are working in all three elementary schools and at McKinley and the Intermediate School. Our K-2 classrooms are in their first fall with the new structured literacy curriculum, and teachers tell us kids are already decoding words they couldn't in August. Family Literacy Night is Thursday, November 12, 5:30-7:00 p.m. at the Intermediate School, with a free book for every child.

## Conferences October 22-23

Parent-teacher conferences are Thursday, October 22, 3:30-7:30 p.m., and Friday, October 23, 8:00 a.m.-noon. There is no school for students on Friday. Sign up through ParentSquare by October 20.

## Elementary attendance areas for 2027-28

On October 12 the school board adopted new elementary attendance areas that move part of the Lincoln area to Jefferson starting in fall 2027. Lincoln has 486 students in a building designed for 450. Current 4th graders are not affected. Families in the affected area will get a letter in January. Answers to common questions are at watertown.k12.sd.us/attendance-areas.
