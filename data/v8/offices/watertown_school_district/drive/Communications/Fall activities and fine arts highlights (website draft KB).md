# Arrows Fall Highlights: Activities and Fine Arts

DRAFT for the website "Arrows Pride" page and the November newsletter | Kayla Brende | started 10/12, updated 10/16
Results through Oct 15. Check scores with Curt Henrichs before posting. Photos from the yearbook staff.

## Marching band: a season to remember

The Arrows Marching Band, about 140 students strong this year, finished its competitive season on Saturday, October 3, with a **second-place finish in its class at the regional marching festival in Aberdeen**, the band's best result in several years. Judges singled out the brass section and the drumline.

This year's show, "Prairie Skies," was performed at every home football game and at the homecoming game on October 9, where the band also led the homecoming parade downtown. The season wraps up with the **Marching Band Showcase on Saturday, October 17, at 2:00 p.m. at Watertown Stadium**, with the Middle School band joining for the final number. The showcase is free.

"These students started in July in 90-degree heat and never let up," said Director of Bands **Andrew Fiechtner**. "They earned every point in Aberdeen." Assistant Director **Kelsey Mohr**, who teaches the Middle School band and works with the high school percussion section, wrote the drumline features.

The band moves inside for concert season. The High School fall band concert is **Tuesday, November 17, at 7:00 p.m.** in the High School auditorium. The Middle School fall concert is **Tuesday, November 3, at 7:00 p.m.**

Thank you to the band parents who fed, fixed and hauled all fall. The equipment truck crew logged more than 600 miles.

## Volleyball

The Arrows opened the season **15-4**, including a five-set win over Aberdeen Central on October 8. The team hosts its final regular-season home match on October 20. Regional play begins in early November.

## Football

The Arrows are 4-3 after the homecoming game against Pierre on October 9 (a 28-21 win). The regular season closes October 16 at Mitchell. Playoff seedings are announced the following week.

## Cross country

Both teams ran at the Eastern South Dakota Conference meet on October 8. The girls placed fourth and the boys sixth. Five runners qualified for the state meet, which is October 24.

## Soccer and golf

Girls soccer finished 6-6-1. Boys soccer reached the first round of the playoffs. Girls golf completed its season in early October with two players at the state tournament.

## Theater and speech

The High School one-act play, "The Secret in the Wings," opens November 13 and 14 in the auditorium (7:00 p.m.). Fall speech practice is under way; the first competition is in early December.

## Intermediate and Middle School

- The Intermediate School fall concert (5th and 6th grade choir and band) is **Tuesday, October 27, at 7:00 p.m.** in the Intermediate gym.
- Middle School volleyball and football seasons ended the week of October 12. Thank you to our seventh and eighth grade coaches.
- The Intermediate School student council collected 1,140 food items for the Watertown food pantry.

## FFA

The High School FFA chapter's fruit sale runs through **November 6**. Oranges, grapefruit, apples and pears; delivery the first week of December. Order from any FFA member.

## Winter season

Winter activities start in November: girls and boys basketball, gymnastics, competitive cheer and dance, debate, and the winter musical. Practice schedules are on the Activities page. Questions: Activities Director Curt Henrichs, (605) 882-6312 ext. 1610.
