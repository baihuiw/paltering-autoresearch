## Snow days

Three snow days are built into the calendar. If more than three are used, the first two makeup days are Monday, February 15, and Friday, April 2. The board will set any additional makeup days. See "Winter Weather: School Closings, Late Starts and Early Dismissals" on the website for how closings are announced.

## School board meetings

Regular meetings: second Monday, 5:30 p.m. (Oct 12, Nov 9, Dec 14, Jan 11, Feb 8, Mar 8, Apr 12, May 10, Jun 14). Work sessions: fourth Monday, 5:30 p.m. (Oct 26, Nov 23, Jan 25, Feb 22, Mar 22, Apr 26, May 24); no work session in December. District Office Board Room, 200 9th St NE.
