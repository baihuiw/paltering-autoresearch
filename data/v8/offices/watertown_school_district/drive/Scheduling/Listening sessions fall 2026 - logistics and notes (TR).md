# Superintendent's Listening Sessions, Fall 2026: Logistics and Notes

Tammy Rieck | working file | last updated October 16, 2026
Purpose (from the entry plan): three open sessions in the first 60 days, at different times and places, so parents, staff and taxpayers can talk with Dr. Hegdahl directly. Notes summarize themes only; speakers are not named.

## Format (all three)

- 90 minutes. Dr. H opens with 5 minutes on who she is and what she's doing in her first 90 days, then mostly listens.
- Small tables for the first 30 minutes with one of our staff at each table taking notes on chart paper; then whole-group open mic.
- Comment cards for anyone who doesn't want to speak. Online comment form on the superintendent's page stays open through Nov 30.
- Staff at every session: Dr. H, Craig Tollefsrud, Kayla Brende (photos, notes), Tammy Rieck (sign-in). Principals of the host building invited.
- No agenda items from the board; directors may attend but sit with the public (Kayla checks that fewer than three attend, or we post a notice).

## Session #1 - Tue Oct 6, 6:30 p.m., Intermediate School commons

- Attendance: 38 signed in (about 30 parents, 4 staff, 4 other residents). Heather Sundvold hosted.
- Set-up: 6 tables. Custodian unlocked at 5:45; we needed the extra extension cord for the projector (bring one next time).
- Themes, by how often they came up:
  1. Class sizes, especially grades 3 and 5 (11 comments)
  2. Phones and personal devices, mostly Middle School (9 comments; several asked for "away for the day")
  3. New lunch menu: portions at the HS, line speed, more choices (7 comments)
  4. Other topics: sports schedules, parking at the HS during events, communication from teachers (ParentSquare overload), recess length (2-3 comments each)
- Follow-up: phone comments to Craig for the Personal Device Review Committee; lunch comments to Janelle Peschong (she has already added the HS second line on Tue/Thu starting Oct 20).

## Session #2 - Thu Oct 15, 12:00 noon, Watertown Regional Library community room

- Attendance: 22 (mostly retirees and business people; 5 parents; 2 staff on lunch break).
- Room reserved through the library, no fee. Library asked that we be out by 1:45 for a 2:00 program.
- Themes:
  1. Property taxes and how the school budget works (6 comments). Sheri's explainer handed out; three people asked for copies by mail (sent 10/16).
  2. Career and technical education, the trades, and Lake Area Tech dual credit (5 comments; two employers offered job-shadow spots)
  3. Phones (4 comments)
  4. School lunch (3 comments)
  5. Staff pay and keeping good teachers and paras (3 comments)
  6. Other: parking at the HS during events, the condition of the Watertown Stadium bleachers (city-owned; passed to the mayor's office)
- Follow-up: employer job-shadow offers to Rachel Ostrem and the HS counseling office. Dr. H to write a short "what I'm hearing" note for the November newsletter.
