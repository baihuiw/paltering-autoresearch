Dr. Hegdahl,

Short answer: please keep saying yes. Here's where we are as of this morning.

Cleared for 2026-27: 412
- Level 1 (supervised; name-based state check, no fee): 361
- Level 2 (unsupervised or regular contact - coaches, club advisers, mentors, overnight chaperones; fingerprints): 51

Pending: 38 (31 Level 1, 7 Level 2)

By building (cleared): McKinley 58, Jefferson 49, Lincoln 61, Fieldcrest Elementary 44, Intermediate 52, MS 47, HS 101 (includes 33 volunteer assistant coaches and booster club workers).

Timing:
- Level 1: 2-4 business days once the form and online training are both done. Most delays are people who fill out the form but don't do the 20-minute training; I send a reminder after 5 days.
- Level 2: fingerprints are taken here at the District Office Tuesdays and Thursdays 8:00-11:00 a.m. or by appointment. State and FBI results usually come back in 2-3 weeks. The district pays the fee. Level 2 is good for three years as long as the person volunteers at least once a year.

Things to know:
1. The robotics volunteer will be Level 2 (he'll be with students after school without a teacher present some of the time). Please have him contact me directly; I'll get him fingerprinted this week.
2. Anyone who wants to start a new club needs a staff sponsor and the principal's OK before the club meets (Policy IICC). The volunteer can't be the only adult in charge until Level 2 clears.
3. Overnight chaperones for winter trips (band, debate, speech) need to be Level 2 by Dec 1. I'll remind Curt and Andrew.
4. We've declined 2 applicants so far this year based on records. Those stay confidential.

The volunteer page on the website has the form and the training link. If someone calls you, the easiest thing is to give them my extension, 1104.
