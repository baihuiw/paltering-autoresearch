Before the first snow, here's the weather-morning routine we've used the last few winters, updated with Dr. Hegdahl's name and cell. Please check your piece and reply with corrections by Friday.

4:00 AM  Lori checks NWS Aberdeen forecast, road reports and wind chill.
4:30 AM  Road check: Lori drives the north and west routes; the contractor's dispatcher (Rick or his night lead) drives east and south including gravel; Doug's night lead (Kevin) checks lots, walks and entrances at all 7 buildings plus the District Office.
5:00 AM  Lori calls Dr. Hegdahl with a recommendation (on time / 2-hour late start / close / hard-surface only). Dr. H may call the Codington County superintendents and the city street superintendent.

5:35 AM  Kayla sends the ParentSquare message (call, text, email, app), posts the website banner and Facebook, and emails local radio and TV. Target: everything out by 6:00 AM.
5:40 AM  Tyler checks that ParentSquare delivery started and watches for failures.
5:45 AM  Lori confirms the decision with dispatch; Doug confirms with the snow contractor (Riverside Lawn & Snow starts on lots at 3:00 AM when 2"+ falls, under the contract).
6:00 AM  Building principals notify their own staff (each building has its own phone tree).

Early dismissal decisions: by 10:30 AM if at all possible, with at least an hour's notice to families.
