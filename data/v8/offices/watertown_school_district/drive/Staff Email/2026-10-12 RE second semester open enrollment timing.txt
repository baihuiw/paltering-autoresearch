Jodi - good idea. Here's a script. Keep it handy and send any detailed questions to me.

"Yes, students who live in another district can apply to attend Watertown schools through South Dakota open enrollment, including starting in the middle of the year. Here's how it works:

- The parent fills out the state open enrollment application (on our Enrollment page, or we can mail one) and turns it in to the Watertown district, at the District Office, attention Craig Tollefsrud. It's a good idea to let your current district know too; we'll send them a copy of our decision.
- For a student to start second semester, the application has to be filed before the last Friday in January, which this year is January 29. If the family wants to start right after winter break on January 4, they should get it to us by December 1 so the school board can act at its December 14 meeting.
- We decide based on space in the grade and building. We'll tell you which building your child will attend. We let you know the decision in writing within five days.
- There's no tuition.
- Families provide transportation. A student can ride from an existing in-town stop if there's room on the bus, but we don't run routes outside the district.
- High school students who want to play a sport should talk to our activities director first, because state association transfer rules apply.

Questions: Craig Tollefsrud, Assistant Superintendent, ext. 1110, craig.tollefsrud@k12.sd.us."

A few notes for you, not for callers:
- Applications after Jan 29 can still be approved but don't take effect until next fall.
- For 4th graders right now, I would place a student wherever the grade has room (currently Jefferson or Fieldcrest Elementary). Don't promise a building on the phone.
- Special education: take a name and number and I'll call them myself.

This is our policy JFBB if anyone asks. Thanks for asking - the log is in my folder if you want to see the numbers.

Craig
