Thank you for a warm first three days. I'm learning a lot, and so far mostly that this team knows what it's doing.

Starting Monday, Sept 28, here's how I'd like our weekly admin team meeting to run. I'm open to changing it after a month if it isn't working.

When: Mondays, 9:00-10:30 a.m., District Office Board Room. On Mondays after a no-school Friday or a holiday, we'll meet Tuesday at the same time.
Who: Craig, Sheri, Megan, Doug, Lori, Kayla, Lisa, Tyler, Janelle, Tammy (notes). Principals join the first Monday of each month (the full principals' meeting is the third Thursday at 8:00 a.m.).

Agenda (Tammy sends it by Friday noon; add items by Thursday at 3:00):
1. Calendar for the next two weeks (5 min) - everyone
2. Board items coming up (10 min)
3. Anything families or staff are upset about this week, and who owns the answer (15 min) - this is the part I care most about
4. One or two topics in depth (45 min)
5. Decisions and who's doing what (5 min) - Tammy reads back

Two habits I'd like us to build:
- If a family writes to me about something in your area, I'll usually copy you on my reply. If you already answered them, tell me so I don't send a different answer.
- If something goes sideways in your area, I'd like to hear it from you before I hear it from a parent or the paper. I won't shoot the messenger.

Upcoming in-depth topics (tentative): Oct 5 - listening session plan; Oct 12 - winter weather routine; Oct 19 - personal device committee; Oct 26 - food service changes and the HS lunch line; Nov 2 - 2027-28 calendar.
