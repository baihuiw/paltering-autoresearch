For this first year I want to say yes to community groups as much as I can. Getting known by employers, service clubs and churches is part of the job right now. Rule of thumb:

- Yes to community groups, employers, PTOs and service clubs if the calendar is open, up to three evenings a week. Short talks are fine; I don't need a speech written, just the audience, the time, how long they want me to talk, and what they want to hear about.
- Never on board or work session nights or the nights before them if I can help it (board prep).

- Church youth group: yes, happy to.
- PTOs: yes to both.

Please always send the inviter a confirmation with the time and the person I should look for when I arrive, and put the address in the calendar entry.
