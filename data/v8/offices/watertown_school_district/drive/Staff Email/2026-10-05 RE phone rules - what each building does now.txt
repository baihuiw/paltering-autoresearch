Craig,

1. "Phones are away during class unless the teacher allows use for a class activity. Phones may be used at lunch." 
2. Not consistently. About a third of my teachers collect phones in a caddy at the door; the rest say "put it away" and it ends up in laps. Lunch is fine. Hallways and bathrooms are where most of the trouble starts (videos, group chats).
3. I'd go "away for the day" for 7-8: phones off and in lockers from first bell to last bell, including lunch. Parents can call the office. I know some parents will push back on lunch, but I'd rather have one clear rule.
4. 212 phone confiscations logged Aug 20-Oct 2. Eleven discipline referrals where a phone was involved (recording, social media). Lockers would need new locks on about 40 that don't latch.
