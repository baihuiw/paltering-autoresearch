Heather (and copying the other building offices, since the elementary schools will get the same calls from the Oct 14 retakes),

I checked with Prairie Light this afternoon. The answer:

- Retake days at school are done for the fall: Oct 14 (McKinley and elementary) and Oct 15 (Intermediate and MS). The HS retake day is Wed Oct 21 at the HS - that one is for HS students only.
- Students who missed both picture day and retake day can get their picture taken at Prairie Light's studio by appointment, through Friday, Nov 6, at no charge. Families call the studio to book (number is on the order envelope and on the picture day page of our website). The photo goes into the yearbook and the student ID system like any other school picture.
- After Nov 6, students who don't have a fall picture will show "picture not available" in the yearbook. They can still be photographed on spring picture day.
- Packages ordered at the studio are the same prices as picture day.

I'll post this on the picture day page of the website tomorrow and send a short ParentSquare post to Intermediate and MS families Monday. Feel free to give families this info now.
