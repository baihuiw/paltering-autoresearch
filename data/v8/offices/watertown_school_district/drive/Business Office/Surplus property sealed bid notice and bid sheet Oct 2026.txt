NOTICE OF SALE OF SURPLUS PROPERTY BY SEALED BID
WATERTOWN SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 14-4

(To run in the Watertown Public Opinion, the district's official newspaper, Wednesday, October 21 and Wednesday, October 28, 2026. Sent to the paper 10/14 by S. Voegeli.)

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Board of Education of the Watertown School District No. 14-4, Codington County, South Dakota, having declared the property listed below surplus on October 12, 2026 (ACTION 27041), will receive sealed bids for its sale at the District Office, 200 9th St NE, Watertown, SD 57201, until 2:00 p.m. Friday, Nov. 6, 2026. Bids will be opened publicly at that time in the District Office conference room. Results will be presented to the Board at its regular meeting on Monday, Nov. 9, 2026.

ITEMS
Lot 1. Fourteen (14) student classroom desks, steel frame with laminate top, adjustable height, approx. 20 years old. Sold as one lot.
Lot 2. One (1) walk-behind automatic floor scrubber, 20-inch, battery powered, batteries do not hold a charge.
Lot 3. One (1) walk-behind automatic floor scrubber, 26-inch, battery powered, runs; squeegee assembly worn.
Lot 4. One (1) 1998 Chevrolet 2500 pickup, 2WD, regular cab, 8-foot box, approx. 162,400 miles, gasoline, automatic. Runs and drives. Rust on rocker panels and box; heater core needs replacement; no plow. Title clean. VIN available on request.

TERMS
1. All property is sold as is, where is, with no warranty of any kind.
2. Bids must be submitted in a sealed envelope marked "Surplus Bid - Lot __" on the outside, with the bidder's name, address and phone number. A separate envelope is required for each lot.
3. Minimum bids: Lot 1 $50; Lot 2 $50; Lot 3 $150; Lot 4 $1,200.
4. Payment in full by cash, cashier's check or money order within five business days of notice of award. Personal checks are not accepted.
5. Successful bidders must remove property by Friday, Nov. 20, 2026, at their own expense, during the hours arranged with the district. Property not removed by that date may be awarded to the next highest bidder.
6. The Board reserves the right to reject any and all bids and to waive any irregularities.
7. District employees and school board directors may bid on the same terms as the public.

INSPECTION
Items may be inspected at the district maintenance shop by appointment with Doug Rentschler, Director of Buildings and Grounds, (605) 882-6312 ext. 1130, weekdays 8:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m. through Nov. 5.

Sheri Voegeli, Business Manager
