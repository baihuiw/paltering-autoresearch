# 2026-27 Budget Summary: All Funds

Watertown School District 14-4 | Business Office
Budget adopted by the school board at the annual meeting, July 13, 2026, after the June 8 budget hearing. Summary prepared for the new superintendent's budget overview, Sept 22, 2026 (SV). Updated 10/2 to add September actuals reference.

Fiscal year: July 1, 2026 - June 30, 2027 ("FY27").

## 1. Expenditure budget by fund

| Fund | FY27 budget | FY26 budget | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| General Fund (10) | $46,800,000 | $45,310,000 | +3.3% |
| Capital Outlay Fund (21) | $7,950,000 | $8,420,000 | -5.6% |
| Special Education Fund (22) | $9,640,000 | $9,180,000 | +5.0% |
| Pension Fund (23) | $1,210,000 | $1,190,000 | +1.7% |
| Food Service Fund (51) | $3,480,000 | $3,365,000 | +3.4% |
| **Total** | **$69,080,000** | **$67,465,000** | **+2.4%** |

Capital outlay is lower than last year because large one-time summer projects were budgeted in FY26 and paid across the two years.

## 2. General Fund revenue

| Source | FY27 budget | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Local property taxes (general fund levy) | $17,320,000 | 37.0% |
| State aid to education (formula) | $24,140,000 | 51.6% |
| Other local (tuition, fees, interest, rentals) | $1,410,000 | 3.0% |
| Federal (Title I, II, IV, Medicaid reimbursement) | $2,860,000 | 6.1% |
| Other state (dual credit, other grants) | $420,000 | 0.9% |
| Transfer from fund balance (planned) | $650,000 | 1.4% |
| **Total** | **$46,800,000** | **100%** |

State aid assumes the formula's per-student allocation set by the 2026 Legislature and fall enrollment about level with fall 2025. The Oct 1 count came in slightly higher than budgeted; the effect on aid is small and is settled by the state in the spring.

**The district has no opt-out in effect for 2026-27 and no bond debt outstanding in the General Fund.** The general fund levy is the rate state law sets for each class of property; the district does not choose a higher rate.

## 4. Capital Outlay Fund highlights

The Capital Outlay Fund pays for buildings, equipment, technology, textbooks and buses (the district owns no buses; the contractor's buses are covered under the transportation contract in the General Fund). FY27 budget includes:
- Technology: student devices for grades 5 and 9 ($612,000), network switches ($148,000)
- Curriculum materials: K-4 literacy materials, year 2 ($286,000)
- Equipment and furniture ($340,000)
- Boiler and HVAC preventive maintenance contract ($118,000)
- Building repairs and summer projects (balance)

## 5. Fund balance

Board policy DCB calls for an unassigned General Fund balance of at least 15% of expenditures. Projected June 30, 2027 unassigned balance: $8.1 million (17.3%), after the planned $650,000 transfer.

## 6. Year to date

September general fund expenditures were 9.1% of budget, in line with past years (most of the year's spending on supplies happens in the summer and first quarter; salaries run evenly from September through August). See "Financial report - September 2026 (to board 10-12)".
