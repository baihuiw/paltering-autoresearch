# High School gym and commons roof replacement: contract key terms

Summary prepared by the business office from the signed owner-contractor agreement (AIA A101 with district supplementary conditions) and the project manual. The signed documents are in the vault binder "HS Roof 2026."

| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| Owner | Watertown School District 14-4 |
| Contractor | Dakota Ridge Roofing, Inc., Sioux Falls, SD |
| Architect | Prairie Line Architects (project architect Nate Lunstra) |
| Work | Tear-off and replacement of approximately 41,000 sq ft of roof over the High School main gym, auxiliary gym and commons; new tapered insulation, fully adhered 60-mil TPO membrane, metal coping and parapet flashing, 9 roof drains and overflow scuppers |
| Bid and award | Bids opened April 22, 2026 (4 bidders); awarded by the board May 11, 2026 |
| Contract sum | $642,800.00 |
| Contract time | Start June 8, 2026; substantial completion August 28, 2026; final completion September 25, 2026 |
| Liquidated damages | $250 per calendar day after substantial completion date |
| Progress payments | Monthly, on the contractor's pay application certified by the architect |
| Retainage | 5% of each progress payment, held until the architect issues the Certificate of Substantial Completion; then reduced to 150% of the value of open punch-list items; released in full at final completion and receipt of closeout documents (warranty, lien waivers, as-builts) |
| Bonds | Performance bond and payment bond, each 100% of the contract sum |
| Warranty | Contractor's 2-year workmanship warranty; manufacturer's 20-year no-dollar-limit membrane warranty, issued after the manufacturer's final inspection |
| Insurance | Contractor's general liability $2M aggregate; district named additional insured |
