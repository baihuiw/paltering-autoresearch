STANDING COMMITTEES
Committees meet as needed, make recommendations only, and report at regular meetings. Two directors per committee (less than a quorum).

1. Policy Committee
   Assigned: Jill Schwebach (chair), Melissa Fergen
   Staff: Superintendent; Tammy Rieck
   Work this year: GBEB, IKF, KF (second readings Nov 9); JICJ review after the Personal Device Review Committee reports; annual review of the J-series in spring.

2. Finance and Audit Committee
   Assigned: Kevin Duerre (chair), Todd Bjornstad
   Staff: Sheri Voegeli
   Work this year: FY2026 audit exit conference (early December); 2027-28 budget calendar; quarterly investment report; review of the fee schedule.

3. Negotiations Committee
   Assigned: Todd Bjornstad, Brad Ihnen
   Staff: Superintendent; Lisa Dummann
   Work this year: Negotiations with the Watertown Education Association for 2027-28 begin in January. Classified staff wage schedule review in February.

4. Facilities Committee
   Assigned: Brad Ihnen (chair), Kevin Duerre
   Staff: Doug Rentschler; Sheri Voegeli
   Work this year: Capital outlay plan; scope of a long-range facilities review to begin in spring 2027; surplus property; Policy KF fee schedule.

5. Activities and Fine Arts Liaison
   Assigned: Melissa Fergen
   Staff: Curt Henrichs, Activities Director
   Attends an activities council meeting each season; recognitions at regular meetings.

6. Teaching and Learning Liaison
