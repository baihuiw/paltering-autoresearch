SCHOOL BOARD MINUTES
WATERTOWN SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 14-4
REGULAR MEETING - SEPTEMBER 14, 2026

The Board of Education of the Watertown School District No. 14-4 met in regular session on Monday, September 14, 2026, at 5:30 p.m. in the District Office Board Room. President Todd Bjornstad called the meeting to order. Members present: Bjornstad, Brad Ihnen, Melissa Fergen, Jill Schwebach, Kevin Duerre. Also present: Acting Superintendent Craig Tollefsrud, Business Manager Sheri Voegeli.

ACTION 27014: Kevin Duerre moved to approve the agenda as presented. Melissa Fergen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27015: Jill Schwebach moved to approve the minutes of August 10 and August 24, 2026. Brad Ihnen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27016: Brad Ihnen moved approval of the verified claims and salaries for the month of August 2026 as presented. Total verified claims $2,212,640.18; salaries $4,488,915.02. Kevin Duerre seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27017: Melissa Fergen moved to approve the financial report for August 2026 as presented. Jill Schwebach seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

Public comment: None.

EMPLOYMENT OF SUPERINTENDENT
President Bjornstad reported that the board interviewed three finalists in executive session on September 8, 9 and 10 under SDCL 1-25-2(1) and that the board's search consultant checked references for all three.

ACTION 27018: Kevin Duerre moved to employ Lauren Hegdahl, Ed.D., as Superintendent of Schools effective September 21, 2026, under a contract through June 30, 2028, at an annual salary of $172,500 prorated for 2026-27, with benefits as provided to administrative staff, and to authorize the President to sign the contract. Jill Schwebach seconded. Roll call: Bjornstad yes, Ihnen yes, Fergen yes, Schwebach yes, Duerre yes. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

President Bjornstad thanked Craig Tollefsrud for serving as acting superintendent since August 24.

ACTION 27019: Jill Schwebach moved to approve the 2026-27 tuition and fee schedule for driver education and adult education as presented. Melissa Fergen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27020: Melissa Fergen moved to approve the agreement with Lake Area Technical College for dual-credit courses for 2026-27 as presented. Brad Ihnen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27021: Brad Ihnen moved to approve the resignations and new hires on the personnel list as presented. Kevin Duerre seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27022: Kevin Duerre moved to approve the co-curricular assignments for 2026-27 as presented. Jill Schwebach seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27023: Melissa Fergen moved to approve the snow removal contract with Riverside Lawn & Snow for November 1, 2026 through April 30, 2027 at the rates quoted. Jill Schwebach seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTING SUPERINTENDENT'S REPORT
- Fall enrollment is up slightly from last year; the official Oct 1 count will be reported in October.
- The attendance area study will come to the board's September 28 work session.
- Fall testing calendar is posted; conferences are October 22-23.

BUSINESS MANAGER'S REPORT
- Capital outlay projects: summer projects closed out except the High School roof; the Jefferson boiler controls and Intermediate School flooring are complete.

ACTION 27024: Jill Schwebach moved to adjourn at 6:41 p.m. Kevin Duerre seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

Todd Bjornstad, President
Sheri Voegeli, Business Manager
