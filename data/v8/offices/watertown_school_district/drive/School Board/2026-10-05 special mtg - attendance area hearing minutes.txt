SCHOOL BOARD MINUTES
WATERTOWN SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 14-4
SPECIAL MEETING AND PUBLIC HEARING - OCTOBER 5, 2026

The Board of Education of the Watertown School District No. 14-4 met in special session on Monday, October 5, 2026, at 6:30 p.m. in the Watertown High School auditorium. President Todd Bjornstad called the meeting to order. Members present: Bjornstad, Brad Ihnen, Melissa Fergen, Jill Schwebach, Kevin Duerre. Also present: Superintendent Lauren Hegdahl, Assistant Superintendent Craig Tollefsrud, Business Manager Sheri Voegeli, and 64 members of the public who signed in.

ACTION 27031: Kevin Duerre moved to approve the agenda as presented. Jill Schwebach seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

PUBLIC HEARING: ELEMENTARY ATTENDANCE AREAS FOR 2027-28
President Bjornstad opened the public hearing at 6:34 p.m.

Alison Peery of Brightline Demographics presented the study and two options. Option A moves 72 students from Lincoln to Jefferson; Option B-1 moves 109. Option B-1 is the Option B shown at the September 28 work session with minor adjustments to follow walking routes. Under both options current 4th graders are not moved, and siblings may request a transfer under Policy JC.

Public comment (3 minutes each; 14 speakers):
- Six Lincoln parents in planning area L-4 spoke against moving their children, citing friendships, a longer walk and before-school care at Lincoln.
- Two Jefferson parents spoke in favor of either option, asking that Jefferson get a second kindergarten-to-grade-1 transition program if enrollment grows.
- A parent on 6th Ave NE asked why the B-1 line steps up to 17th St NE between 6th and 9th Avenues. Ms. Peery answered that the posted changes from Option B were minor adjustments to follow walking routes and that the full study is on the website.
- A grandparent asked that crossing guards at 14th St NE be kept. Superintendent Hegdahl said they will be.
- A Lincoln teacher spoke in support of Option B-1, citing class sizes of 26-28 in grades 3 and 4 this year.
- Three speakers asked the board to wait a year. Two others asked the board to decide this fall so families can plan.

President Bjornstad closed the public hearing at 7:41 p.m. He said the board will consider action at its regular meeting on October 12 and that written comments received by October 9 will be included in the board packet.

ACTION 27032: Melissa Fergen moved to adjourn at 7:43 p.m. Kevin Duerre seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

Todd Bjornstad, President
Sheri Voegeli, Business Manager
