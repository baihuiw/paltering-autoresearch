# Policy KF: Community Use of School Facilities

Watertown School District 14-4 | Board Policy
NEPN/NSBA Code: KF | Adopted: 1996 | Last revised: April 11, 2022
**Proposed revision: first reading October 12, 2026 (ACTION 27038); second reading scheduled November 9, 2026.** Changes from the 2022 text are marked [NEW] or [CHANGED]. Prepared by Doug Rentschler and Sheri Voegeli; clean copy to Tammy by Oct 28.

## 1. Purpose

School buildings and grounds belong to the community. When they are not needed for school programs, the board makes them available to community groups for educational, civic, cultural, recreational and other lawful purposes, in a way that protects students, keeps the district's costs covered and does not interfere with school activities.

## 2. Priority of use

1. District instructional programs and school-sponsored activities
2. School-affiliated groups (booster clubs, PTOs, employee groups)
3. Youth nonprofit organizations serving district-age children
4. Other nonprofit, civic and governmental groups
5. Adult community recreation groups
6. Commercial and private users (limited; see section 5)

Within a category, requests are handled in the order received. School activities may displace a scheduled community use with at least 7 days' notice, except in emergencies.

## 3. How to request

- Requests go to the Buildings and Grounds office on the district's Facility Use Request form at least 14 days before the first use. [CHANGED: was 10 days]
- Recurring uses (weekly practices, meetings) may be approved for up to one semester at a time.
- The Director of Buildings and Grounds approves requests after checking the building calendar with the principal; High School gym, auditorium and stadium-area requests also need the Activities Director's approval.
- Approved users receive a written permit listing the space, times, fees, contact person and rules.

## 4. Conditions of use

1. An adult (21 or older) named on the permit must be present for the entire use and is responsible for the group's conduct.
2. Groups serving minors must certify that their adult leaders have had background checks under the group's own policy. [NEW]
3. Groups other than school-affiliated groups must provide a certificate of liability insurance naming the district as additional insured, $1,000,000 per occurrence. Youth nonprofits without insurance may apply for a waiver through the business manager. [CHANGED: amount was $500,000]
4. No alcohol, tobacco, vaping products, cannabis, firearms (except licensed law enforcement) or gambling on district property.
5. Users stay in the approved space. Kitchens, science labs, shops, and technology rooms are not available except under a separate agreement with district staff present.
6. Users leave the space as they found it. Damage is billed at cost.
7. Political events: candidate forums and meetings open to the public may be held in the Board Room or High School auditorium on the same terms as other civic groups. Campaign events that collect money are not permitted. [CHANGED: clarifies forums]
8. When school is closed or dismissed early for weather, all community use that day is cancelled unless the Activities Director or Director of Buildings and Grounds approves by 2:00 p.m.
9. Private parties (birthday parties, family reunions) are not an allowed use.

## 5. Commercial use

Businesses may rent the High School auditorium, gyms and commons for events open to the public (recitals, clinics, trade shows) at the commercial rate, with a written agreement signed by the business manager. Commercial use may not occur during the school day.

## 6. Fees

The fee schedule is adopted by the board with this policy and reviewed every two years.

| Category | Room/classroom | Gym or commons | Auditorium | Custodial (outside staffed hours) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| School-affiliated groups | No charge | No charge | No charge | No charge |
| Youth nonprofit | No charge | No charge weekdays | $25/hr | Weekends **$28/hr** [CHANGED: first reading listed $32/hr; staff recommends $28/hr for second reading] |
| Other nonprofit/civic | $25 flat | $40/hr | $60/hr | $32/hr |
| Adult community recreation | $15/hr | $15/hr | n/a | $32/hr after 9:00 p.m. |
| Commercial | $50/hr | $75/hr | $85/hr + technician $30/hr | $32/hr |

Custodial fees apply whenever a custodian must open, supervise or clean outside the building's regular staffed hours (generally weeknights after 10:00 p.m. and all weekend hours). The superintendent may waive fees for district partners (for example, Lake Area Technical College under the dual-credit agreement) or for one-time community events that benefit students.

## 7. Revocation

The district may revoke a permit for violation of this policy, damage, unpaid fees, or conduct that endangers students or property.

## 8. Administration

The superintendent will maintain procedures (KF-R) and an annual report to the board of community use hours and fees collected. In 2025-26, community groups used district facilities about 6,400 hours; fees collected were $21,880.

Legal references: state law on community use of school buildings (citation to be confirmed by the business manager before second reading). Cross references: IICC (volunteers), KFA (public conduct on school property).
