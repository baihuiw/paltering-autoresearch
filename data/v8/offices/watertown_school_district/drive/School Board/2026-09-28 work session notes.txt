1. ELEMENTARY ATTENDANCE AREA STUDY (2027-28)
Alison Peery presented the study the board commissioned in April. Key points:
- Oct 1 projections: Lincoln 486 students in grades 1-4 against a capacity of 450 (108%); Jefferson 392 of 475 (83%); Fieldcrest 361 of 400 (90%).
- Lincoln is projected to reach 512 by fall 2028 with the new housing north of 20th St NE.
- Criteria used: keep walkers walking (no new crossings of US-212 or the railroad); keep neighborhoods whole; bring every building under 95% of capacity; minimize the number of students moved.
- Option A moves planning areas L-6 and L-7 (north of 20th St NE, east of 9th Ave NE) to Jefferson: 74 students. Lincoln ends at 412 (92%), close to the line again by 2028.
- Option B moves planning area L-4 (14th to 20th St NE, 4th to 9th Ave NE) to Jefferson: 118 students. Lincoln 368 (82%), Jefferson 510... corrected on screen to Jefferson 445 of 475 (94%) after removing the current 4th graders, who would not move.
- Under either option, current 4th graders finish at their school and go on to the Intermediate School as planned. Siblings of a student staying may request a transfer under Policy JC-R.

Board discussion: Mr. Duerre asked whether Option B splits any neighborhood; Ms. Peery said L-4 is one neighborhood bounded by arterials. Ms. Fergen asked about walking routes for the L-4 children to Jefferson; staff to check the 7th Ave NE crossing at 14th St NE. Mr. Ihnen asked for updated Fieldcrest numbers. Mr. Bjornstad said the board leans toward Option B because it solves Lincoln for more than one year.

Next steps: Brightline to post maps and the study summary on the website by Oct 2. Public hearing Monday, Oct 5, 6:30 p.m., High School auditorium. Possible action Oct 12.
