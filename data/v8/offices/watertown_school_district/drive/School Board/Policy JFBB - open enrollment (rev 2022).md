# Policy JFBB: Open Enrollment

Watertown School District 14-4 | Board Policy
NEPN/NSBA Code: JFBB | Adopted: 1998 | Revised: March 14, 2022 | Reviewed: August 2026 (no changes)
See also: JC, JFBB-R (Open Enrollment Procedure), JFBB-E (State application form)

## 1. Purpose

The board recognizes the right of South Dakota families to apply to enroll a student in a public school district other than the district where the family resides, as provided by state law. This policy sets out how the Watertown School District receives, decides and reports open enrollment applications, both for students who live elsewhere and wish to attend Watertown schools ("open enrollment in") and for Watertown residents who wish to attend another district ("open enrollment out").

## 2. Applications

1. A parent or guardian applies on the application form provided by the South Dakota Department of Education. The completed form is submitted to the district where the student wishes to enroll (the admitting district). Families applying to Watertown submit the form to the office of the Assistant Superintendent, District Office, 200 9th St NE.
2. Applications are accepted throughout the year. State rules set the timing:
   - an application filed before the last Friday in September may take effect during the first semester;
   - an application filed before the last Friday in January may take effect for the second semester;
   - an application approved after those dates takes effect at the start of the following semester or school year, as state rules provide.
3. The family should also notify the district where the student lives. The district will send that district a copy of its decision.

## 3. Decisions

1. The board, or the superintendent or the superintendent's designee acting for the board between meetings, approves or denies each application. Decisions made by the designee are reported to the board at its next regular meeting, and the board's approval is recorded in the minutes.
2. The district notifies the applicant and the resident district of the decision within five days of making it.
3. An application may be denied only for reasons permitted by state law and this policy, including:
   - lack of space in the requested grade, program or building, based on the capacity guidelines the board has adopted for each building;
   - a student's current expulsion from another district, for the length of the expulsion;
   - the student's need for a special education program the district does not offer, after the district has reviewed the student's individualized education program with the family.
4. Applications are decided in the order received. Siblings of students already enrolled under open enrollment are given priority when space is limited.
5. The superintendent or designee assigns an approved student to a building based on space. An open-enrolled student does not choose a building, but the district will consider the family's request.

## 4. After enrollment

1. An approved student is enrolled on the same terms as a resident student. No tuition is charged.
2. The parent or guardian is responsible for transportation to and from school. An open-enrolled student may ride a district bus from an existing in-district stop where there is room on the bus, but the district does not add stops or routes outside the district.
3. Students in grades 9-12 are subject to the eligibility rules of the South Dakota High School Activities Association for athletics and fine arts. Families should contact the Activities Director before applying.
4. A student who enrolls under this policy may remain enrolled in the district through graduation without reapplying, as long as the student remains continuously enrolled, unless the family chooses to return to the resident district.
5. Students with disabilities are served under their individualized education programs, consistent with state rules for open-enrolled students.

## 5. Reporting

The assistant superintendent keeps a log of all applications in and out and reports the number approved to the board each month on the consent agenda. Enrollment counts follow state reporting rules.
