# Policy JC: School Attendance Areas

Watertown School District 14-4 | Adopted 2009 | Revised August 2023 | Regulation JC-R attached

## Policy

The Board of Education establishes attendance areas for each elementary school. Students in grades 1-4 attend the elementary school serving the attendance area in which their parent or guardian resides. Students in preschool, junior kindergarten and kindergarten attend McKinley Early Learning Center; students in grades 5-6, 7-8 and 9-12 attend the district's single Intermediate, Middle and High School.

In setting or changing attendance areas, the board considers:

1. the capacity of each building and projected enrollment;
2. safe walking routes and the cost of transportation;
3. keeping neighborhoods together;
4. the number of students affected; and
5. public comment received at a public hearing held before the board acts.

Changes to attendance areas take effect at the start of a school year. Families affected are notified in writing.

## Regulation JC-R: Intradistrict transfers

1. A parent may request that a student in grades 1-4 attend an elementary school other than the one serving the family's attendance area.
2. Requests for the following school year are due to the Assistant Superintendent by February 1. Requests received later are considered if space allows.
3. Requests are approved when the receiving school has space in the grade, as determined by the Assistant Superintendent. A school above 95% of its functional capacity in a grade is closed to transfers in that grade.
4. When attendance areas change, a student already enrolled in grade 4 may finish grade 4 at his or her current school. A younger sibling may request a transfer to the same school under item 3.
5. The district does not provide transportation for a transfer student unless the student lives on an existing route.
6. Decisions may be appealed in writing to the Superintendent within 10 days; the Superintendent's decision is final.

Cross-references: SDCL 13-28-40 et seq. (open enrollment between districts) is handled under Policy JFBB, not this policy.
