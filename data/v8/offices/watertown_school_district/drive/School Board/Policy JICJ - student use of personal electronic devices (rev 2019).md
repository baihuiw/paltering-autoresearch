# Policy JICJ: Student Use of Personal Electronic Devices

Watertown School District 14-4 | Board Policy
NEPN/NSBA Code: JICJ | Adopted: 2009 | Revised: May 13, 2019

> **Note added October 2026 (TR):** Under review in 2026-27. See the Personal Device Review Committee charge (Teaching and Learning). Any revision is expected to come to the board for first reading in January 2027. Until then, this policy and each building's current handbook rules remain in effect.

## 1. Purpose

Students may bring cell phones, smart watches, tablets and other personal electronic devices to school. The board recognizes that devices can help families stay in touch and can support learning, and that they can also distract from instruction and be misused. This policy sets district-wide limits and directs each building to set classroom rules.

## 2. District-wide rules

1. Students may not use any personal device to:
   - bully, harass, threaten or intimidate any person;
   - record audio, photos or video of another student or staff member without that person's permission, except at public events;
   - record or send images in locker rooms, restrooms or nurse's offices, under any circumstances;
   - cheat on any test or assignment;
   - access content that is obscene or violates the district's acceptable use policy.
2. Devices must be silenced during instructional time.
3. The district is not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged personal devices.

## 3. Building rules

Each principal, with staff, sets rules for when and where students may use devices during the school day, and publishes them in the student handbook. Building rules must be consistent with this policy and appropriate to students' ages.

*(Building handbook rules for 2026-27, for reference: elementary and Intermediate - phones off and in backpacks during the school day; Middle School - phones away during class unless the teacher permits use, allowed at lunch; High School - "off and away" during class, allowed during passing time and lunch.)*

## 4. Confiscation and discipline

1. A staff member may take a device that is used in violation of this policy or building rules. The device is returned to the student at the end of the day for a first offense, and to a parent or guardian for later offenses.
2. Repeated or serious violations are handled under the student discipline policy.
3. Staff may search the contents of a device only as permitted by law and policy JIH.

## 5. Exceptions

A principal may allow a student to keep and use a device for a documented health need (for example, glucose monitoring) or under an IEP or Section 504 plan.

## 6. Emergencies

During a building emergency, students should follow staff directions. Families should rely on district messages sent by ParentSquare for accurate information.

Legal references: state law requiring a district bullying policy. Cross references: IJNDB (acceptable use), JIH (searches), JICFB (bullying).
