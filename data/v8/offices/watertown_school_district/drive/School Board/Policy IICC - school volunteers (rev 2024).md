# Policy IICC: School Volunteers

Watertown School District 14-4 | Board Policy
NEPN/NSBA Code: IICC | Adopted: 2006 | Revised: June 10, 2024 | Administrative procedure IICC-R revised August 2026
See also: GCDB (Criminal Background Investigations), KF (Community Use of Facilities), JRA (Student Records)

## 1. Purpose

The board welcomes parents, grandparents and neighbors who give their time to Watertown's schools. Volunteers extend what teachers and staff can do for students. This policy sets expectations so that volunteering is safe for students and rewarding for volunteers.

## 2. Who is a volunteer

A volunteer is any person, not employed by the district for the activity, who helps with a school program or event under the direction of district staff without pay. Parents who attend a public event, chaperone a class party in a classroom with the teacher present, or visit for lunch are visitors, not volunteers, and follow the building's visitor rules.

## 3. Levels of volunteers

**Level 1.** Volunteers who work with students only while a district employee is present, such as classroom helpers, library and lunchroom helpers, day field trip chaperones assigned to a group within sight of staff, and event workers.
- Complete the volunteer form at the building office or online.
- Complete the district's online volunteer training (about 20 minutes).
- Name-based state criminal history check run by the human resources office. No fee.

**Level 2.** Volunteers who have regular or unsupervised contact with students, including volunteer coaches, club advisers or co-advisers, mentors who meet one-on-one, and overnight chaperones.
- All Level 1 requirements, and
- a fingerprint-based background check through the South Dakota Division of Criminal Investigation and the FBI, processed by the human resources office. The district pays the fee. Results usually take two to three weeks.
- A Level 2 clearance is valid for three years if the volunteer serves at least once each school year.

A volunteer may not begin the role until the required clearance is complete.

## 4. Approval and supervision

1. The building principal approves each volunteer and each volunteer role. The principal may decline or end any volunteer's service.
2. Each volunteer is supervised by a named district employee.
3. A new student club that relies on a volunteer must have a district staff sponsor and the principal's approval before it meets. Clubs at the High School also need the Activities Director's approval for scheduling and space.
4. Volunteers sign in at the building office each visit and wear the district volunteer badge.

## 5. Expectations

Volunteers:
- keep confidential anything they learn about students, including grades, behavior, health and family information;
- do not discipline students; they tell the supervising employee;
- do not transport students in private vehicles unless the principal has approved it in writing;
- do not take or post photos or video of students other than their own children;
- report to the supervising employee or principal any concern about a student's safety. Volunteers are not mandatory reporters under state law, but the district expects them to report any concern right away.

## 6. Disqualification

The human resources coordinator reviews any record returned by a background check. A person with a conviction for a crime involving a child, a violent felony, or another offense the district determines makes service unsafe will not be approved. Decisions are confidential.

## 7. Records

The human resources office keeps volunteer clearance records and reports the number of active volunteers to the board each fall.
