SCHOOL BOARD MINUTES - DRAFT (for approval November 9)
WATERTOWN SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 14-4
REGULAR MEETING - OCTOBER 12, 2026

The Board of Education of the Watertown School District No. 14-4 met in regular session on Monday, October 12, 2026, at 5:30 p.m. in the District Office Board Room. President Todd Bjornstad called the meeting to order. Members present: Bjornstad, Brad Ihnen, Melissa Fergen, Jill Schwebach, Kevin Duerre. Also present: Superintendent Lauren Hegdahl, Assistant Superintendent Craig Tollefsrud, Business Manager Sheri Voegeli.

ACTION 27033: Jill Schwebach moved to approve the agenda as presented. Kevin Duerre seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27034: Melissa Fergen moved to approve the minutes of September 14 and October 5, 2026. Brad Ihnen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27035: Kevin Duerre moved approval of the verified claims and salaries for the month of September 2026 as presented. Total verified claims $2,684,118.37; salaries $4,912,406.55. Jill Schwebach seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27036: Brad Ihnen moved to approve the financial report for September 2026 as presented. Melissa Fergen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

Public comment: Two Lincoln parents spoke on the attendance area item. One asked the board to delay a year.

ACTION 27037: Melissa Fergen moved to adopt Option B-1 of the Brightline Demographics study as the elementary attendance areas effective with the 2027-28 school year, as presented. Kevin Duerre seconded. Discussion: Mr. Duerre said Lincoln's class sizes make this necessary. Ms. Schwebach asked that families receive their 2027-28 school assignment by letter in January. Roll call: Bjornstad yes, Ihnen yes, Fergen yes, Schwebach yes, Duerre yes. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27038: Jill Schwebach moved to approve the first reading of Policies GBEB, IKF and KF as presented. Brad Ihnen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27039: Kevin Duerre moved to approve the open enrollment applications received under SDCL 13-28-40 (4 in, 2 out) as presented. Melissa Fergen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27040: Brad Ihnen moved to approve the resignations and new hires on the personnel list as presented. Jill Schwebach seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

ACTION 27041: Melissa Fergen moved to approve the surplus property list (14 classroom desks, 2 floor scrubbers, 1 1998 Chevrolet pickup) for sale by sealed bid. Kevin Duerre seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

SUPERINTENDENT'S REPORT
- Visited every building at least once; second round of visits under way.
- Listening session Oct 6: 38 attended; topics were class sizes, phones in class and the new lunch menu.
- High school roof replacement: the new membrane is installed over the gym and commons; remaining items are on the contractor's list for this fall.
- Parent-teacher conferences Oct 22-23. No school Friday, Oct 23.
- Homecoming week went well; thanks to the student council.

BUSINESS MANAGER'S REPORT
- September general fund expenditures 9.1% of budget; on track.
- Fall property tax settlement expected in early November.

ACTION 27042: Kevin Duerre moved to enter executive session at 6:48 p.m. for personnel matters per SDCL 1-25-2(1). Jill Schwebach seconded. Five votes yes. The board came out of executive session at 7:15 p.m. No action taken.

ACTION 27043: Jill Schwebach moved to adjourn at 7:16 p.m. Melissa Fergen seconded. Five votes yes. Motion carried.

Todd Bjornstad, President
Sheri Voegeli, Business Manager
