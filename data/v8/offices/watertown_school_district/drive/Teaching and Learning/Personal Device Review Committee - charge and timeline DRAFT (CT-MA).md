# Personal Device Review Committee: Charge and Timeline

DRAFT 2 | Craig Tollefsrud (chair) with Megan Aamot | October 12, 2026
Approved in concept by Dr. Hegdahl, Oct 9. Committee roster to the board for information Nov 9.

## Why now

Student phones and smart watches were among the top three topics at the superintendent's first listening session (Oct 6) and came up again at the second (Oct 15). Teachers and principals report that the current rules work at the elementary and Intermediate levels but are applied unevenly at the Middle School and, to a lesser degree, the High School. Board Policy JICJ was last revised in 2019 and leaves classroom rules to each building.

Building data, Aug 20-Oct 2, 2026:
| Building | Current rule | Confiscations | Referrals involving a device |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elementary (3) and McKinley | Off and in backpacks | Few; not tracked | 0 |
| Intermediate | Off and in backpacks; watches not addressed | 18 | 1 |
| Middle School | Away during class at teacher discretion; allowed at lunch | 212 | 11 |
| High School | "Off and away" in class; passing time and lunch allowed | 96 | 4 |

## Charge

The committee will:
1. Review what each of our buildings does now and how well it works, using building data and staff, student and family input.
2. Review approaches used by other districts in South Dakota and neighboring states (bell-to-bell "away for the day," lockable pouches, classroom caddies, locker storage, building-level rules), and what those districts report about results and costs.
3. Survey families, staff and students in grades 7-12 (Nov 2-13).
4. Recommend to the superintendent:
   - a revised Policy JICJ, including smart watches and earbuds;
   - a district-wide rule for each level (elementary/Intermediate, Middle School, High School) or a clear statement of what buildings may decide;
   - how and when to start any change (second semester or fall 2027), including cost and how families will be told;
   - exceptions for health needs, IEPs and 504 plans, and emergencies.

The committee advises; the superintendent brings a recommendation to the school board, which adopts policy at two readings.

## Cost notes (preliminary)

- Lockable pouches for grades 7-8: about 650 at $25 = $16,250, plus unlocking stations.
- Locker storage at the MS: about 40 lockers need new latches (Doug: about $1,800).
- Classroom caddies/pocket organizers: the HS bought these in August for about $3,100.
- The business manager says any of these fits within capital outlay this year.

## What to tell families who ask

"The district is reviewing its policy on student phones and personal devices. A committee of parents, teachers, students and principals meets in November, and families will be surveyed. A recommendation will go to the school board in January. Until then, each building's current rules stay in place." (Please don't promise a start date; the committee will recommend one.)
