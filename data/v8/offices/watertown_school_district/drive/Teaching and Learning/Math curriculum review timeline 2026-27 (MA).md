# K-8 Mathematics Curriculum Review: Timeline and Process, 2026-27

Megan Aamot, Ed.D., Director of Teaching and Learning | October 5, 2026
For the admin team and the Teaching and Learning board liaison (Jill Schwebach). The district's six-year curriculum cycle puts K-8 math in review this year, with new materials in classrooms for fall 2027.

## Why we're reviewing

- Our current K-5 and 6-8 math materials were adopted in 2019 and the publisher has stopped updating the online platform for the 6-8 edition after 2027-28.
- Teachers report gaps in fluency practice in grades 3-5 and too little support for students who need extra time.
- The committee will check every option against the current state mathematics standards.
- Our own spring assessment data show stronger results in grades 3-5 than in grades 7-8; the committee will look at the building-level detail.

## Committee

- Chair: Megan Aamot
- Teachers (14): two from each grade band at each level (K-1, 2-4 from all three elementary schools; Intermediate 5-6; Middle School 7-8), plus one special education teacher and one EL teacher
- Principals: Heather Sundvold (Intermediate), one elementary principal

Teachers are paid the curriculum work rate ($30/hour) for meetings outside the contract day. Budget: $14,800 (Title II and general fund).

## Criteria (draft)

1. Alignment with current state standards (required)
2. Strong fluency and problem-solving practice; clear progression across grades
3. Supports for students who need more time and for students ready for more challenge
4. Usable by a new teacher with the teacher's guide alone
5. Print materials available (families asked for fewer screens at the listening session)
6. Assessment tools that show teachers what to reteach
7. Six-year cost, including online licenses and consumables
