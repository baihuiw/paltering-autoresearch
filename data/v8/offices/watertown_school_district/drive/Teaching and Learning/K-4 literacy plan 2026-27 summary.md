# K-4 Literacy Plan, 2026-27: summary for families and the board

Watertown School District 14-4 | Teaching and Learning | Rev. September 2026

## Our goal

Every Watertown student reading at grade level by the end of 3rd grade. In spring 2026, 71% of our 3rd graders met the end-of-year grade-level target on the district's reading screener. Our goal for spring 2027 is 76%.

## What's new this year

- **Structured literacy curriculum in K-2.** Daily explicit phonics, phonemic awareness and decodable texts, adopted by the board in March 2026 and in every K-2 classroom this fall. Grades 3-4 add the knowledge-building units in January.
- **Four new reading interventionists.** One each at Jefferson, Lincoln and Fieldcrest and one shared between McKinley and the Intermediate School, funded by federal Title I and the general fund. That brings the district to nine.
- **Training.** All K-4 teachers completed 24 hours of summer training in the science of reading; 31 more complete the second course this year.
- **Family Literacy Night** each semester, with free books for every child (fall: Thursday, November 12).

## How we know who needs help

- **Every student in grades K-4 is screened three times a year** (fall, winter, spring) with a short reading screener given by the classroom teacher. It takes about 20 minutes per student.
- Students below the grade-level target get a closer diagnostic look from the interventionist.
- **Tier 2 groups:** small groups of 3-5 students, 30 minutes a day, four or five days a week, in addition to classroom reading. Groups are reset after each screening window.
- **Tier 3:** individual or pairs, for students who need more than Tier 2 after 8-10 weeks.
- Progress is checked every two weeks for every student in a group.

## How families hear about it

- Screening reports go home after each window (fall reports at or after fall conferences).
- If your child is placed in a reading group, the interventionist sends a letter and a home practice packet.
- Questions: your child's teacher or building principal, or Megan Aamot, Director of Teaching and Learning, (605) 882-6312, ext. 1120.

## Board reporting

The Director of Teaching and Learning reports screening results to the board after the winter and spring windows (February and June).
