## Overview

Dual-credit courses with Lake Area Technical College (LATC) are included in registration under the 2026-27 agreement the board approved September 14 (ACTION 27020). Counselors meet with every student; 8th graders also have a family night.

## Dual credit with Lake Area Tech

- Students in grades 11-12 may take LATC courses for both high school and college credit, on the LATC campus or (for some general education courses) at the High School.
- Fall 2026: 94 Watertown students enrolled in at least one LATC course; 31 attend LATC for half the school day in a program track (welding, automotive, health careers, precision machining, ag and diesel).
- Tuition is set by the state's dual-credit program and the LATC agreement; families pay the student share per credit hour. Students approved for free or reduced-price meals may apply for help with the student share through the counseling office.
- Transportation between the HS and LATC: students drive or use the noon shuttle (Route 5). Students who leave for LATC must sign out at the HS office.
- Students who attend LATC part of the day keep full eligibility for HS activities.

## Graduation requirements

Policy IKF (graduation requirements) is on its second reading Nov 9; the update changes only the state reference, not the credits required. Counselors check every student's plan against the credit requirements in Policy IKF at each registration meeting; seniors short of credits are flagged to the principal by Feb 26.

## Changes under consideration for 2027-28

1. A second section of Intro to Construction Trades (demand from the listening session and employers; the HS shop has space for 16 per section).
2. Moving AP Chemistry to every year instead of alternate years (requests: 22 this fall).
3. Adding a semester course in Computer Science Principles (one teacher interested; needs a summer training course).
4. Retiring Keyboarding (3 requests).

Department heads send proposed changes to Rachel Ostrem by Nov 13. Any course that needs new materials goes through Teaching and Learning first.
