# Kampeska Bus Lines: student transportation contract, 2026-27 (summary)

Prepared by Lori Steffenhagen, Transportation Coordinator, for the business office and new administrators. Summary only; the signed contract (third year of a three-year agreement, approved by the board in May 2024) is in the Business Office contract file.

## Scope

- Regular routes: 6 in-town, 5 rural, 3 special education (vans).
- Shuttle between buildings (Route 5).
- Field trips and athletic trips at the charter rate.
- Added routes or runs: by written change order from the district, at the added-route rate below, subject to the contractor's driver availability.

## Rates (2026-27)

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Regular route, per route per day | $318.40 |
| Special education van, per route per day | $244.10 |
| Added route or run, per route-hour | $54.50 |
| Charter (field and athletic trips), per bus-hour | $61.00 |
| Charter mileage over 150 miles | $2.15 per mile |
| Fuel adjustment | +/- 2% for each $0.25 change from $3.40/gal diesel base, reviewed quarterly |

## Budget (2026-27 transportation, general fund)

| Line | Budget |
|---|---|
| Regular routes (11 x 175 days) | $612,920 |
| Special education vans (3 x 175 days) | $128,150 |
| Added runs, including the rural evening loops (about 495 route-hours) | $27,000 |
| Field and athletic trips | $96,000 |
| Fuel adjustment reserve | $18,000 |
| **Total** | **$882,070** |

## Driver requirements

Drivers are contractor employees. The contractor is responsible for CDL and school bus endorsements, drug and alcohol testing and training. The district receives a monthly compliance letter from the contractor.
