# Field Trip and Athletic Trip Transportation: Procedure and Rates, 2026-27

Watertown School District 14-4 | Transportation | Lori Steffenhagen, Transportation Coordinator
Revised August 2026. Rates from the transportation contract (third year), effective July 1, 2026.

## 1. Who this is for

Teachers, coaches, advisers and building secretaries arranging a trip that needs district-contracted transportation: class field trips, athletic and fine arts trips, and other school-sponsored trips. Regular daily routes are not covered here.

## 2. How to request a trip

1. Get the principal's (or for athletics and fine arts, the Activities Director's) approval first.
2. Submit the Trip Request form in the district's online forms (Transportation > Trip Request) **at least 10 school days before the trip**. Athletic schedules for the season should be submitted as one batch at least 3 weeks before the season starts.
3. Lori confirms with the contractor's dispatch and replies by email with the confirmation number, driver assignment (when available) and estimated cost.
4. Changes or cancellations: email Lori and call dispatch at (605) 886-4410. Cancellations less than 24 hours ahead are billed a 2-hour minimum.

Out-of-state trips and overnight trips also need board approval (consent agenda) at least one meeting ahead.

## 3. Rates for 2026-27

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Field, athletic and fine arts trips (charter rate) | $61.00 per bus-hour, yard to yard, 2-hour minimum |
| Charter mileage | $2.15 per mile for miles over 150 on a trip |
| Fuel adjustment | As set quarterly under the contract (+/- 2% steps) |
| Motorcoach | Quoted per trip; usually for trips over 150 miles one way |
| Driver meals on trips over 6 hours | $15 per meal, billed at cost |
| Overnight driver lodging | Actual cost, single room |

Example: a class trip to Sioux Falls (about 100 miles each way), leaving the yard at 8:15 a.m. and back at 2:15 p.m. Time 6 hours x $61.00 = $366.00; mileage 205 miles, so 55 miles over 150 x $2.15 = $118.25; total about $484.25 per bus before any fuel adjustment.

## 4. Who pays

- **Field trips:** building budgets (each building has a field trip line). PTO-funded trips are billed to the building, and the building collects from the PTO.
- **Athletics and fine arts:** Activities budget.
- **Special education community instruction:** Special Education Fund.

The business office pays the contractor monthly from Lori's trip log; please don't sign or pay any trip invoice directly.

## 5. Capacity and safety

- Standard route vehicles seat 71 (three small students to a seat) or about 48 high school students. Plan two vehicles for a group of more than 48 high schoolers with equipment.
- Students ride to and from school-sponsored events on district transportation unless a parent signs the release form to drive their own child. Students may not drive other students.
- A staff member rides on every trip. Chaperone ratios are set by the principal.
- Every vehicle carries a first aid kit and a roster; the trip leader keeps the emergency medical forms.
- The driver has final say on safety, including weather. If the driver and trip leader disagree, call Lori, then the principal.

## 6. Weather

If school is closed or dismissed early for weather, trips that day are cancelled unless the Activities Director (athletics/fine arts) or Lori (field trips) approves in writing. When roads are poor on the return trip, the trip leader and driver decide whether to wait; call Lori and the principal, and post a message to families through the building's ParentSquare.

## 7. Small-group trips

For groups of 10 or fewer, the district's two passenger vans (7 passengers plus driver) may be reserved through the activities office. Van drivers must be district employees approved by HR, age 21+, with a clean driving record check on file.
