# Sanitation and Recycling - Holiday Collection Schedule, FY2027

City of Cullman Sanitation and Recycling. (256) 734-2185, Monday-Friday 7:00 a.m. - 3:30 p.m.
Superintendent: Gary Lofton (glofton@cullmanal.gov)
Issued October 13, 2026. Posted at cullmanal.gov and printed in City Notes before each holiday.

## The basic rule

Residential garbage is collected **once a week on your regular day**. Carts must be at the curb by **6:00 a.m.** and back off the street by the end of the next day. Crews start at 6:00 a.m. on regular days and at **5:00 a.m. on any day that carries extra routes** because of a holiday.

City Hall holidays are not always Sanitation holidays. Sanitation works on several days when City Hall is closed so that routes don't stack up.

## Recycling drop-off center

Closed on every City Hall holiday listed above. Open regular hours (Mon-Fri 7:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m., Sat 8:00 a.m. - noon) on the Saturdays when garbage routes are being made up.

## Recycling drop-off center - what it takes

The City's recycling drop-off center is for residents of the city. Bring materials loose (no bags), rinsed and sorted into the marked bins.

| Accepted | Not accepted |
|---|---|
| Cardboard (flattened), paperboard, newspaper, magazines, office paper | Plastic bags and film (return to grocery stores) |
| Aluminum and steel cans | Glass (not accepted at this time) |
| Plastic bottles and jugs #1 and #2 | Styrofoam, clamshells, plastic #3-#7 |
| Used motor oil (sealed jugs, up to 5 gallons) | Paint, chemicals, pesticides |
| Household batteries (bin inside the office) | Tires, appliances with refrigerant (call for bulk pickup) |
| Scrap metal (small items) | Electronics (watch for the spring collection day) |

Contamination is the biggest cost to the program. A bin with garbage or plastic bags in it can be rejected by the processor, and then the whole load goes to the landfill. When in doubt, leave it out.

## Bulk and limb pickup

Bulk item and limb pickup do not run during a holiday week that has Saturday make-up routes (Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's). Calls taken during those weeks are scheduled for the following week.

## Leaf season

Leaf collection is run by the Street Department, not Sanitation: November 2, 2026 - January 29, 2027. See the Street Department's leaf collection plan or call (256) 734-2180.

## If your cart was not picked up

Call (256) 734-2185 by 3:00 p.m. the day after your collection day. Leave the cart at the curb. On holiday weeks, wait until after the make-up day before calling.
