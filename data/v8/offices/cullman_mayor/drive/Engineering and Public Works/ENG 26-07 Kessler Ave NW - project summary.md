# ENG 26-07: Kessler Avenue NW drainage improvements - project summary

City of Cullman Engineering Department. Summary sheet for the project file; updated October 12, 2026.

## Background

Kessler Avenue NW crosses a small branch at its low point near Pine Hollow Court NW. The old 30-inch corrugated metal cross-drain under the road had rusted through at the invert, and the roadside ditches had filled in. In heavy rains, water ponded on Kessler Avenue and ran into driveways on the low side of the street. Residents reported water in garages and crawlspaces in 2023 and 2025.

## Scope (as designed, May 2026)

- Replace the 30-inch CMP cross-drain under Kessler Ave NW with **48-inch reinforced concrete pipe**, 64 feet, with concrete headwalls.
- Design storm: **25-year** (the crossing carries the 25-year storm without overtopping the road).
- Regrade about 600 feet of roadside ditch on both sides, with rip-rap at the outlet.
- Replace 7 driveway pipes (Kessler Ave NW and Pine Hollow Ct NW) with 18-inch HDPE.
- Repave the trench area and driveway aprons.
