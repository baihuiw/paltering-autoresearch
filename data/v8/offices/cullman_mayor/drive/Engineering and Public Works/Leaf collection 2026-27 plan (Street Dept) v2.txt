CITY OF CULLMAN - STREET DEPARTMENT
LEAF COLLECTION PLAN, 2026-27 SEASON
Version 2, October 9, 2026 (v1 had the zone order reversed; east side goes first this year)
Prepared by: Wade Lovelady, Street Superintendent
For: Mayor's Office (Bryce for City Notes, website and Facebook); Sanitation; Police dispatch

1. DATES
- Season starts: Monday, November 2, 2026.
- Season ends: Friday, January 29, 2027 (last pass west side). After that, leaves go out with regular yard waste.
- Holidays: crews work on Veterans Day (Wed Nov 11). No leaf collection on Thanksgiving Thu-Fri Nov 26-27; Christmas Thu-Fri Dec 24-25; New Year's Day Fri Jan 1. Bad weather days are made up at the end of the pass, not the next day.

2. ZONES AND ORDER
Zones follow the railroad and 2nd Avenue, same as the limb routes:
  Zone 1  NE (east of the CSX tracks, north of 2nd Ave / Main St dividing line)
  Zone 2  SE (east of the tracks, south)
  Zone 3  SW (west of the tracks, south)
  Zone 4  NW (west of the tracks, north)
Order: Zone 1, Zone 2, Zone 3, Zone 4, then repeat. A full pass of the city takes about 10 working days with two vac trucks and the knuckleboom. Expect a truck on your street about every two weeks during the season. Peak weeks (late November) may stretch to 12-13 working days.

East side (Zones 1 and 2): first pass week of Nov 2.
West side (Zones 3 and 4): first pass week of Nov 9.

3. HOW RESIDENTS SET OUT LEAVES
- Loose leaves: rake to the edge of the yard, right behind the curb or the edge of the pavement. Do not put leaves in the street, the gutter or over a storm drain. The vacuum trucks pick up loose piles.
- Bagged leaves: also fine. Any bag (paper yard bags or plastic), tied or folded closed, under 40 pounds each. Set bags next to the curb, separate from loose piles. The crew behind the vac truck picks up bags by hand on the same pass.
- Either way works. Loose is faster for us; bags are fine if that's what the resident prefers.
- No limbs over 4 feet long or thicker than 4 inches in leaf piles; limbs jam the vacuum. Limbs go out separately for the regular limb pickup.
- No grass clippings in large amounts, no rocks, bricks, trash, pumpkins, or pine straw mixed with dirt.
- Keep piles away from mailboxes, fire hydrants, water meters and parked cars.
- Contractors and lawn services must haul away leaves from properties they service. Crews will not pick up leaf piles that are clearly from commercial lots.

4. EQUIPMENT AND STAFF
- Vac truck 1 (2019), vac truck 2 (new, delivered Oct 2026; training week of Oct 12 with the dealer rep).
- Knuckleboom (unit 27) for bags and large piles.
- Two 3-person crews, 7:00 a.m. - 3:30 p.m.; Saturday overtime in the last two weeks of November if we fall behind.
- Leaves go to the city compost site, then to farmers and residents free in the spring (first come, first served, bring your own trailer).

5. CALLS AND MISSED STREETS
- Residents call the Street Department, (256) 734-2180, 7:00 a.m. - 3:30 p.m. The office keeps a running list.
- "Missed" calls during the first pass are usually streets the trucks haven't reached yet. Front desk: check the zone and the week before logging it.
- The website map will show which zone the trucks are working in each day (Bryce to post the map by Oct 30).

6. NOTES FROM LAST SEASON
- 1,940 tons collected (2025-26). The old vac truck was down 9 days in December; that is why some west side streets waited three weeks. The second truck should prevent that.
- Most calls were about piles in the street and piles mixed with limbs. Repeat the "behind the curb, no limbs" message in every post.

6a. COMPOST GIVEAWAY (SPRING 2027)
- Leaf compost available free to City residents starting the first Saturday in April 2027, 8:00 a.m. - noon, at the compost site; weekdays by appointment.
- Residents load their own (shovels and buckets) or bring a pickup or trailer; the Street Department loader will load pickups on Saturdays only.
- No commercial landscapers.

7. MESSAGE FOR CITY NOTES AND FACEBOOK (short)
"Leaf collection begins Monday, November 2, east side first. Rake leaves to the curb, loose or bagged, but keep them out of the street and away from storm drains. No limbs in leaf piles. Trucks come by about every two weeks through January 29. Questions: Street Department, (256) 734-2180."
