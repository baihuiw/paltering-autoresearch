RE: Street Department yard (ENG 26-19) - offer to purchase 0.40 acre

Dear Mrs. Sartain,

Thank you for meeting with me and Superintendent Lovelady at your home on October 6.

As we discussed, the City is planning a new Street Department yard on the former nursery property next to you. To build a safe entrance for trucks, the City needs a strip of land about 50 feet wide along your north line, approximately 0.40 acre, shown on the enclosed drawing. Your house, garage, well and garden are not affected, and the City would replace your fence along the new line at no cost to you.

The City had the strip appraised by an independent state-certified appraiser. The appraisal, which is enclosed, values the strip, including any effect on the rest of your property, at $38,000. The City's offer is the appraised value, $38,000. The City pays appraised value for the land it buys from property owners, and I'm not able to offer more than that amount.

If you accept, please sign and return the enclosed acceptance form by November 9, 2026. The City would pay your reasonable closing costs. If you have questions, or would like to have your own appraisal done, please call me at (256) 734-2150 ext. 230.

Sincerely,
