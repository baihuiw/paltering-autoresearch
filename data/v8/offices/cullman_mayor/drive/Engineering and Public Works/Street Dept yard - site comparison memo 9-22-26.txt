RE:      Street Department yard relocation (ENG 26-19) - site comparison

PURPOSE

The FY2027 capital budget adopted September 14 includes $625,000 for land and access for a new Street Department yard. This memo summarizes why the yard needs to move and how we compared the candidate sites.

WHY THE YARD NEEDS TO MOVE

The current yard on 4th Street SW sits in the low corner of the old industrial block next to the branch. It has flooded three times in two years. Each time, crews lose a day or more moving equipment, salt and sand to high ground, and the salt barn floor has been undermined at the north wall. The site is also too small: the two newest dump trucks are parked outside the fence, and there is no room to stage brine equipment for winter. Moving the yard was first recommended in the 2023 facilities study.

SITE CRITERIA

1. At least 7 acres of usable land outside the flood zone
2. Access to a collector street without sending trucks through neighborhoods
3. Utilities available (water, sewer, three-phase power)
4. Within 2 miles of the downtown core for response time
5. Cost of land plus site work within the capital budget and the FY2028 build-out estimate

CANDIDATE SITES

| Site | Acres | Flood zone | Access | Utilities | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Former nursery, 1900 block Brockmann Rd NE | 9.2 | Outside | Brockmann Rd (collector); needs a short access drive | All at the road | Old greenhouse frames and a block office to demolish; mostly level |
| B. Industrial park lot 14 | 6.1 | Outside | Good | All | Too small; owner (industrial board) prefers job-creating use |
| C. Farmland off the bypass, south | 18.0 | Partly in | Fair | Water only | Sewer extension about $300,000; farther from downtown |
| D. Expand in place (4th St SW + adjacent lot) | 5.5 | In | Fair | All | Does not solve flooding |

RECOMMENDATION

Site A ranks first on every criterion. The access drive needs a strip of about 0.4 acre from the adjoining residential lot at 1921 Brockmann Rd NE; the rest of the frontage is too steep for trucks. We recommend the City authorize negotiations with the owners of Site A and the strip. Site work (demolition, grading, gravel, fencing) is estimated at $480,000 and would be budgeted in FY2028, with the salt barn and shop building to follow.

The owner of record of Site A is Ruhrmann Holdings LLC. An independent appraisal of Site A and the strip, by the same certified appraiser the City used for the Main Ave SW turn lane, was completed September 15 and is in the project file.
