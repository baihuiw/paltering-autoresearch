# Eastside Community Safe Room - Public Information Sheet

City of Cullman / Cullman Fire Rescue. Revised September 2026 (posted at the entrance and on cullmanal.gov).

**Address:** 1450 3rd Street SE, Cullman, AL 35055 (next to Pine Ridge Mobile Home Park)
**Capacity:** 300 people
**Built:** 2026, with a FEMA Hazard Mitigation Grant and City funds, to FEMA P-361 safe room standards (designed for EF-5 winds)

## When is it open?

The safe room opens whenever Cullman County EMA activates community shelters for a **tornado watch or tornado warning** that includes the City of Cullman, **day or night**. **The doors unlock automatically when shelters are activated.** It stays open until EMA ends the activation.

The safe room is not a place to stay overnight after a storm and is not a cooling or warming center.

## How do I know when shelters are open?

- Cullman County EMA posts activations on its social media and website.
- Local radio stations announce activations.
- Sign up for emergency alerts through the county's alert system.
- Do not wait for the sirens: if a warning is issued for your area, go.

## What can I bring?

- Yourself and your family, shoes, a flashlight, medications, a phone and charger.
- **Pets:** small pets in carriers or crates are allowed in the separate pet room. Dogs must be crated. Service animals are welcome in the main room.
- No weapons, alcohol, smoking or vaping.

## What to expect

- There is no staff on site. Please be considerate of others, keep children with you, and leave the room as clean as you found it.
- Restrooms and bench seating are inside. There is no food or water service; bring water if you can.
- Cullman Fire Rescue checks the safe room during activations.
- Parking is in the lot on 3rd Street SE. Walk in if you can.
