CULLMAN FIRE RESCUE
OFFICE OF THE FIRE CHIEF

SPECIAL ORDER 26-14
Date:     October 1, 2026
To:       All shifts; Station 3 officers
From:     Chief Ronnie Stidham
Subject:  Eastside Community Safe Room (1450 3rd St SE) - opening on activations

1. Until the replacement door controller is installed (vendor ship date the week of November 16), the Eastside safe room doors will not unlock on their own when Cullman County EMA activates the community shelters. The doors open only with the Fire Rescue keypad code.

2. Assignment. On every EMA shelter activation (tornado watch or warning that includes the city), the Station 3 company officer will send the first available unit to the Eastside safe room to open the doors, turn on the lights and check the restrooms and pet room. The unit radios dispatch when the doors are open.

3. If Station 3 is committed to a call when the activation comes, the unit goes to the safe room as soon as it clears. The shift battalion officer may send another unit if one is available, but no unit is to be taken off an emergency call for this assignment.

4. The unit does not stay at the safe room. Doors are left unlocked until EMA ends the activation; Station 3 locks up afterward and reports the number of people sheltered.

5. Keypad code: posted on the Station 3 board and in the apparatus binders. Do not give the code to the public.

6. Questions from the public about the safe room go to the Mayor's office (Bryce Templeton). Do not post about it on personal or station social media.

7. This order expires when Capt. Burleson confirms that the new controller has received a test activation from EMA.

R. Stidham
Fire Chief

Distribution: A, B, C shifts; Station 3 board; Capt. Burleson; Mayor's office
