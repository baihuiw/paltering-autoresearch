# City of Cullman Special Events Policy

Adopted by resolution of the City Council; revised January 2024. Administered by the Office of the City Clerk with the Police, Fire Rescue, Street and Parks departments.

## 1. Purpose

The City welcomes festivals, races, parades, concerts and other events that bring people to Cullman. This policy sets out how events on public streets, sidewalks and City property are permitted, staffed and paid for, so that every organizer is treated the same way and residents' safety and access are protected.

## 2. When a permit is required

A special event permit is required for any event that:
- closes or partly closes a public street, alley or City parking lot;
- uses a City park or the downtown square for more than 100 people;
- sells food or alcohol on public property; or
- uses amplified sound outdoors after 9:00 p.m.

Applications are filed with the City Clerk at least 30 days before the event (60 days for events expecting more than 2,000 people).

## 3. Review

The City Clerk circulates each application to Police, Fire Rescue, Street and Parks. Police decide how many officers are needed for traffic control and crowd safety, based on the expected attendance, the streets closed and whether alcohol is served. Fire Rescue may require a standby unit for large events.

## 4. Fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Permit application | $50 |
| Police officers (traffic control and security) | $45 per officer per hour, 4-hour minimum per officer |
| Street closure and barricades | $250 per day |
| Fire Rescue standby unit | $90 per hour |
| Park pavilion or square | Per Parks fee schedule |
| Extra trash pickup and cleanup | Actual cost |

Police and Fire Rescue charges are billed by the department after the event and are due within 30 days. Organizers who have not paid for a past event may not receive a new permit.

## 5. Waivers

**The Mayor may waive or reduce fees for events sponsored by nonprofit organizations that serve a public purpose.** Waivers must be in writing and filed with the City Clerk. Events organized by the City, by City schools under the joint-use agreement, and memorial services for public safety personnel are not charged.

Requests for a waiver should be included with the application and should say what public purpose the event serves (for example, a charity race whose proceeds go to a local cause).

## 6. Insurance and cleanup

Organizers provide a certificate of general liability insurance naming the City as additional insured ($1,000,000 per occurrence) and leave the site clean by 8:00 a.m. the next day.

## 7. Alcohol

Events serving alcohol must have the required state license and the City's approval, and must use wristbands and a fenced service area. Police staffing for events with alcohol is at least one officer per 500 expected attendees.
