Citations are down from last year mainly because of the two patrol vacancies (filled; recruits start the academy Nov 2). The state highway safety overtime grant ($24,000, Res. 2026-89) pays for extra traffic details from October through September; first details are school zones and Main Ave SW.

Speed trailer program: 7 deployments in September, including 5th Ave SW (1600 block) after the resident's comment at the Sept 14 Council meeting. That trailer showed an 85th-percentile speed of 34 mph in a 25 zone, mostly 6:45 to 8:00 a.m. Directed morning enforcement on 5th Ave SW is scheduled Oct 5-16 and the trailer goes back the week of Oct 19 for a comparison. Full log: "Speed trailer deployments Aug-Oct 2026 (Traffic Unit)".

Traffic Unit notes (Sgt. Travis Whitt):
- School zones: 34 citations and 51 warnings in September across the five City school zones. Most were in the first two weeks. Crossing guards report fewer close calls at 3rd St SW since the new sidewalk section opened.
- Main Ave SW corridor: 22 citations in the August directed patrol; the repeat trailer in late September showed the 85th-percentile speed down from 40 to 39. The grant overtime details will continue there in October.
- Residential requests: 11 speed trailer requests received in August-September (phone, email, Council, Facebook). Wait time for a trailer slot is about four weeks. We are asking requesters for the block and the worst times of day so the trailer goes where it's useful.
- Requesters get a mailed summary of the trailer data. Callbacks have been slower than they should be while the unit was short one officer; Sgt. Whitt is now assigning each request to an officer by name.
