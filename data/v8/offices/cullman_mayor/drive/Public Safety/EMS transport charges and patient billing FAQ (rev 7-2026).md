# Cullman Fire Rescue - Ambulance Charges and Patient Billing FAQ

Revised July 2026. Questions about a statement: Hallbrook Revenue Cycle patient line (855) 555-0142, Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., or Fire Rescue administration at (256) 734-2170.

## What does an ambulance trip cost?

Charges are set by Ordinance 2023-31 and have not changed since January 2023.

| Level of service | Base charge |
|---|---|
| Basic life support (BLS) | $950.00 |
| Advanced life support, level 1 (ALS1) | $1,150.00 |
| Advanced life support, level 2 (ALS2) | $1,400.00 |
| Mileage, per loaded mile | $16.50 |

The level of service is set by the care the crew gave on the trip, not by how serious the call seemed.

## Who sends the bill?

Since July 1, 2026, the City's billing company is Hallbrook Revenue Cycle LLC. Before that it was Teague Medical Billing, Inc. Statements come from Hallbrook with the City's name on them.

## Who pays?

We bill your health plan, Medicare or Medicaid first when you give us the information on the trip form. You are responsible for any part your plan does not pay, including deductibles and copays. If you have no insurance, the statement is for the full charge.

## Can I pay over time?

Yes. Payment plans are interest-free for up to 12 months. Call the patient line to set one up. Card, check and online payments are accepted; online payment is at cullmanal.gov under Pay a Bill.

## What if I cannot afford the bill?

You may ask for a hardship review. Send the one-page application (available at City Hall, 204 2nd Avenue NE, and from the patient line) with proof of household income. The Treasurer decides each review; balances can be reduced or waived.

## Do city residents get a discount?

Residents are not charged a separate trip fee. Mileage is charged only for loaded miles to the hospital.

## I disagree with my statement. What do I do?

Call the patient line and ask for an itemized statement. Billing questions are reviewed within ten business days.
