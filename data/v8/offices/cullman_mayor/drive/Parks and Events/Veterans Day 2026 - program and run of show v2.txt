VETERANS DAY CEREMONY 2026 - PROGRAM AND RUN OF SHOW
Version 2, October 14, 2026 (Tonya Haskew). v1 had the wrong choir and no rain plan.

WHEN: Wednesday, November 11, 2026, 11:00 a.m. (about 30-35 minutes)
WHERE: City Hall flagpole, 204 2nd Avenue NE
RAIN LOCATION: City Hall auditorium (decision by 9:00 a.m.; Bryce posts on Facebook)
CITY OFFICES: closed for Veterans Day. Mayor's office staff, Parks crew and the custodian are working the event.
PUBLIC: Open to everyone. Veterans are asked to sit in the reserved rows (about 60 chairs). Others stand or bring a chair.

LEAD: Mayor's Office (Tonya). Setup: Parks (chairs, podium, sound, tent for the choir). Flags: Street Dept lowers and raises per the program.

RUN OF SHOW
10:15  Parks setup complete; sound check
10:30  Veterans arrive; ushers (Council members volunteered) seat veterans in reserved rows
10:55  Color guard in position
11:00  Welcome - Mayor Kreuzer (2 min)
11:02  Invocation - local pastor (TBD; Russell has the ministerial association list)
11:04  Presentation of the colors - color guard (NOT YET CONFIRMED, see notes)
11:06  Pledge of Allegiance - led by a Scout troop
11:07  National anthem - high school chamber choir
11:10  Recognition of veterans by branch (each branch's song played; veterans stand)
11:16  Remarks - guest speaker, a Cullman veteran (Mayor's choice; invitation out)
11:26  Laying of the wreath at the flagpole - Mayor and the oldest veteran present
11:28  Moment of silence
11:29  Taps - high school band trumpeter
11:31  Retiring of the colors
11:33  Closing - Mayor; invitation to coffee and cookies in the City Hall lobby

AFTER: Lobby reception until 12:30 (Tonya ordering cookies; coffee from the lobby urns).

RECOGNITION BY BRANCH (11:10) - SCRIPT FOR THE MAYOR
"In a moment we will play the song of each branch of our armed forces. When you hear your branch's song, if you served in that branch, please stand, or raise your hand if standing is hard, and let us thank you." Order: Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard. Then: "Would all Gold Star families please stand or raise a hand." Then: "And would all spouses and family members of veterans stand. You served too."
- Sound: Parks has the medley file (about 4 minutes); test at 10:15.
- Pause the music between branches for applause; the Mayor will nod to the sound tech.

WREATH (11:26)
- Mayor and the oldest veteran present carry the wreath to the stand at the base of the flagpole. Tonya will find out at check-in who that is and ask whether they'd like to; if not, the Mayor carries it with a veteran of their choosing.
- Flag at half-staff is NOT correct for Veterans Day; the flag stays at full staff. (Last year someone asked.)

VETERANS CHECK-IN TABLE (10:15 - 11:00)
- Two volunteers (Council members offered); sign-in sheet with name, branch and years of service (optional).
- Name tags; a printed program for each veteran; a small lapel flag from the Mayor's office supply (order 100).
- Ask whether the veteran would like to be recognized by name in next year's ceremony program.

LOBBY RECEPTION
- Cookies (bakery, 20 dozen, $180), coffee (two urns plus decaf), lemonade for children.
- High school choir invited to stay; Tonya to thank the choir director by letter.
