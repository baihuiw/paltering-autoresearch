# Parks and Recreation - Facility Rental Rates and Rules, 2026-27

Effective October 1, 2026. Reserve at the Parks and Recreation office or call (256) 734-2195, Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m. - 4:30 p.m. Reservations open 90 days ahead for residents and 60 days ahead for non-residents. Payment is due when you reserve.

"Resident" means you live inside the city limits (bring a driver's license or utility bill with a city address).

## Pavilions

| Facility | Capacity | Resident (4 hrs) | Non-resident (4 hrs) | Each added hour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small pavilion (picnic tables, grill) | 40 | $35 | $55 | $10 |
| Large pavilion (tables, grills, power, restrooms nearby) | 120 | $75 | $110 | $20 |
| Amphitheater lawn (no stage power) | 300 | $100 | $150 | $25 |
| Amphitheater with stage power and lights | 300 | $175 | $250 | $40 |

Unreserved pavilions are first come, first served. A reservation tag is posted on reserved pavilions by 9:00 a.m. that day.

Deposits are refunded within 15 business days if the room is clean, trash is bagged, and nothing is damaged.

## Athletic fields (tournaments and private use)

- Field rental with lights, per field per day: $250; without lights: $175.
- Field prep (lining, bases, drag): $40 per prep.
- Tournament directors must show proof of liability insurance ($1 million) naming the City as additional insured.
- City leagues and City school teams are scheduled first; rentals fill around them.

## Downtown square

The downtown square is reserved through the Parks office for gatherings, weddings, photos and small concerts. Gatherings that close a street or use City power or barricades also need a permit from the City Clerk's office; Parks will tell you if yours does.

## Rules for all rentals

1. No alcohol in parks or City buildings unless the City Clerk has issued an approval for a specific event.
2. No inflatables, dunk tanks or trackless trains without the Parks director's approval and the vendor's proof of insurance.
3. Decorations: tape only (no nails, staples or tacks). Remove everything when you leave.
4. Confetti, glitter, rice and birdseed are not allowed (they can't be cleaned out of the grass).
5. Music must be family-friendly and turned down by 10:00 p.m.
6. Parks close at 10:00 p.m.
7. Cancellations: full refund with 14 days' notice; 50% with less than 14 days; no refund on the day. Weather cancellations by the City are refunded in full.

## Holiday season notes

- The downtown square is busy from the tree lighting (Friday, November 27) through December. Square reservations for Saturdays in December are first-come; two December Saturdays are already taken.
- The community room is popular for December parties. Book early; the City employee Christmas lunch has the room on Friday, December 18.
- Pavilions stay open all winter but water to the pavilion sinks is shut off from mid-November to mid-March to keep pipes from freezing. Restrooms near the large pavilion stay open with heat tape.

## Insurance and liability

Private renters of pavilions and rooms do not need their own insurance for ordinary family gatherings. Commercial uses (vendors selling goods, fitness classes charging a fee, paid entertainment) need proof of liability insurance naming the City as additional insured, and the vendor needs a City business license.

## Booking tips

- The most-requested dates are Saturdays in April, May, September and October, and the weekends around Easter, Mother's Day and graduation. Book those at 90 days.
- Pavilion reservations can be made by phone and paid by card. Room reservations need a signed rental agreement and deposit; email is fine.
- A rental may be moved once to another available date at no charge with 14 days' notice.
- Groups that rent the same pavilion weekly (walking clubs, homeschool groups) may book up to 12 weeks at a time at the resident rate.

## Free use

Pavilions are free for City-sponsored events, City schools' events during the school day, and nonprofit youth sports leagues that play in City leagues. Ask the office to confirm before you book.
