## Cost to play

| | City resident | Non-resident |
|---|---|---|
| Early (through Nov 6) | $55 | $80 |
| Regular (Nov 7-13) | $65 | $90 |
| Each additional child, same household | $10 off | $10 off |

The fee includes a jersey, end-of-season award for ages 5-8, and gym use for practices and games. Scholarships are available for families who qualify for free or reduced school lunch; ask at the Parks office. No child is turned away for inability to pay, but please ask before the deadline so we can plan.

"Resident" means the child lives inside the Cullman city limits. Bring a utility bill or lease to the office if registering in person.

## What to bring or upload

- Copy of the child's birth certificate (first-year players only).
- Signed medical release and code of conduct (part of the online form).
- Payment.

## Coaches

All coaches are volunteers. Each coach must complete a background check (paid for by the City), the online concussion awareness course, and the coaches meeting. Parents who want to coach should check the box on the registration form. We need about 40 coaches; divisions with too few coaches will be capped.

## Practices and games

- Practices: one hour, once a week, at the City gym or a school gym (assigned by division). Practice times are 5:00-8:00 p.m. weekdays.
- Games: 8:00 a.m. - 6:00 p.m. Saturdays at the City gym; weeknight games for older divisions 6:00-9:00 p.m.
- Schedules are posted online by December 4.
- Weather: if City schools close for weather, all practices and games that day are cancelled.

## Behavior

The league is for fun and skill-building. Parents and spectators who yell at officials, coaches or players will be asked to leave the gym. Officials for ages 9 and up are certified; ages 5-8 games are run by a Parks staff member.

## Volunteers

Scorekeepers and clock operators are needed for every Saturday game. High school students can earn service hours. Contact the Parks office.

## Concessions

The concession stand is run by the youth sports booster club; proceeds pay for scholarships and equipment.

## Gym schedule changes for the holidays

There are no practices December 21 through January 1. The City gym is closed Thanksgiving Day and the day after, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. Open gym for adults (Tuesday and Thursday 6:00-9:00 p.m.) moves to the auxiliary court while youth practices are running in December.

## Refunds

Full refund before the November 13 deadline. After that, refunds only for a medical reason with a doctor's note, minus $15 for the jersey. No refunds after the first game.

## Last season

587 players registered in 2025-26 (up from 548 the year before). Divisions for 9-10 and 11-12 filled before the final deadline, so register early.
