2. FACILITY RENTALS

Pavilion rentals: 41 (Sept 2025: 37). Community room rentals: 18. Ballfield tournament rentals: 3 weekends (two travel baseball, one softball). Rental revenue for the month: $14,385 (budget for the month $12,500).

Tournament weekends filled the hotels both weekends (per the tourism office). The Sept 19-20 baseball tournament had 44 teams.

3. MAINTENANCE

- Fields 3-4 LED lighting: state grant awarded ($150,000); bid documents done; advertise Oct 20, open bids Nov 10, recommend at the Nov 23 Council meeting. Installation in January-February so the lights are up for spring.
- Playground replacement (one neighborhood park, FY2027 capital): staff recommends the park on the north side whose equipment is from 1998. Community meeting to choose equipment in November.
- Aquatic center: pool closed for the season Sept 7; drained and inspected. Heater replacement needed before May ($23,000, will come from the capital contingency request in the spring).
- Greenway: two benches replaced after vandalism; one trail light pole knocked down by a mower (claim filed with Accounting).
- Mowing contract crews finished the last full mow of the season Sept 30. In-house crews switch to leaf and limb help for the Street Department in November and to holiday decorations.

4. EVENTS (City-run)

- Movie in the Park, Sept 5: about 450 people. Concession revenue went to the youth soccer league.
- Mayor's oath reception, Sept 21: chairs and setup for the auditorium and lobby.
- Fall fishing rodeo, Oct 17: 120 kids registered (limit 150). Rods and bait provided; Fire Rescue bringing an engine for the kids to see.
- Downtown trick-or-treat, Oct 31: Parks provides barricades and trash cans; merchants hand out candy.
- Veterans Day ceremony, Nov 11, 11:00 a.m., City Hall flagpole: Parks sets up chairs, podium, sound and the choir tent.
- Christmas tree lighting, Fri Nov 27, 5:30 p.m., downtown square.
- Christmas parade, Sat Dec 5, 6:00 p.m.: entry form goes public Oct 9 (release and website); due Nov 20. 23 entries are already in from last year's groups, who got the form by email Oct 1.

4a. RESIDENT REQUESTS (September)
- Dog park: petition with 212 signatures delivered to the Mayor; Mayor's reply Sept 29. Parks will list two or three City-owned sites with costs by spring for the FY2028 budget discussion. Rough estimate $60,000-$90,000 for about an acre with separate small and large dog areas.
- Pickleball: requests for lights on the two new courts. Not budgeted; add to the FY2028 list.
- Restrooms at the greenway trailhead: open 7:00 a.m. - 8:00 p.m. through October, then 7:00 a.m. - 6:00 p.m. A resident asked for year-round late hours; the restrooms aren't winterized for overnight use below freezing, so no.
- Playground surfacing at two parks topped off with 60 cubic yards of mulch after a parent's photos showed bare spots under swings.

5. STAFF
