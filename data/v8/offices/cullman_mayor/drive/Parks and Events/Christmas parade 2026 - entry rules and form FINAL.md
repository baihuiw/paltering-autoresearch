# 2026 Cullman Christmas Parade - Entry Rules and Form

**Saturday, December 5, 2026, 6:00 p.m.** Downtown route. Theme: **"Home for the Holidays."**
Presented by the City of Cullman Parks and Recreation Department.

**Entry forms due Friday, November 20, 2026, by 4:30 p.m.** No entry fee.
Return to: Parks and Recreation office, or email parksevents@cullmanal.gov, or drop off at the City Hall front desk (204 2nd Avenue NE) in an envelope marked "Parade."
Questions: Jill, Parks events coordinator, (256) 734-2195.

## Who may enter

Churches, schools, dance and cheer studios, scout troops, civic clubs, businesses, nonprofits, bands, antique car clubs, horse groups and families. Entries are accepted in the order received until the parade is full (limit **110 units**). Last year the parade filled on November 16, so earlier is better.

Political campaign entries are not accepted (no candidate signs or campaign materials on any unit).

## Rules

1. **Lineup** begins at **4:30 p.m.** in the staging area east of downtown. Your lineup number and a staging map are emailed to the contact on the form by **December 1**. Units not in place by 5:30 p.m. may lose their spot.
2. **Candy is handed out, not thrown.** Walkers may hand candy to children at the curb. Nothing may be thrown from any vehicle or float.
3. Every float and vehicle needs a licensed driver with proof of insurance. Trailers must have safety chains and working lights.
4. **Walkers** stay with their unit and on the street side of the barricades. Children under 12 who walk must have at least one adult for every 8 children.
5. **Lights** are strongly encouraged; the parade is after dark. Generators must be secured and have a fire extinguisher on board.
6. **Horses and animals**: a cleanup person must walk behind each animal unit with a shovel and bag.
7. **Sound**: music is welcome; keep it family-friendly and stop amplified sound while passing the judges' stand for announcements.
8. **Santa rides last.** Please do not include Santa Claus on any other unit.
9. No alcohol, no fireworks, no open flame, no confetti cannons, no glitter.
10. Dance and cheer groups: performances are on the move. Stopping to perform holds up the units behind you; you may do one short routine at the judges' stand.
11. Units should be no wider than 12 feet and no taller than 13 feet 6 inches (traffic signals and banners).

## Judging

Trophies in four categories: Best Overall, Best Use of Theme, Best Lighted Float, and Best Youth Group. Judges are at the reviewing stand downtown. Winners announced on the City's Facebook page Monday, December 7.

## Weather

If the parade is cancelled for weather, an announcement will be posted on Facebook and cullmanal.gov by 2:00 p.m. on December 5. There is no rain date.

## Questions we hear every year

- **Can we use a golf cart or side-by-side?** Yes, if it's decorated and driven by a licensed driver 16 or older. It counts as a vehicle.
- **Can our group march with a banner?** Yes. Banners must be carried, not attached to the front of a moving vehicle where they block the driver's view.
- **Where do we park the trucks and trailers after the parade?** The parade ends west of downtown; marshals will direct vehicles to the disband area. Pick up walkers there, not along the route.
- **Can a business hand out coupons or flyers?** Small items handed to adults are fine. No flyers to children, and nothing thrown.
- **Our float has a generator. Is that OK?** Yes, secured, with a fire extinguisher on board and fuel stored in an approved can.
- **We entered last year. Do we need a new form?** Yes, every year.
- **Can we add walkers after we send the form?** Yes, email the updated count by December 1.
