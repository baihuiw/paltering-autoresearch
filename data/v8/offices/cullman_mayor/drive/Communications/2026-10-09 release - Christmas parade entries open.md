# Cullman Christmas Parade set for December 5; entries now open

CULLMAN, Ala. - The City of Cullman's annual Christmas Parade will roll through downtown on **Saturday, December 5, at 6:00 p.m.**, and the City is now taking entries from churches, schools, businesses, dance and cheer groups, scout troops, civic clubs, car clubs and families.

This year's theme is "Home for the Holidays."

"The Christmas parade is one of the nights when the whole town comes downtown," said Mayor Denise Kreuzer. "I've watched it from the curb for years. This year I get to wave from the street, and I hope to see a lot of homemade floats and a lot of lights."

**How to enter**
- Entry forms are on cullmanal.gov (Parks and Events) and at the Parks and Recreation office.
- **Forms are due Friday, November 20, by 4:30 p.m.** Email them to parksevents@cullmanal.gov or drop them off at the City Hall front desk, 204 2nd Avenue NE, in an envelope marked "Parade."
- **There is no entry fee.**
- The parade is limited to 110 units and fills in the order forms are received. Last year it was full by mid-November.

**Rules to know**
- Lineup begins at 4:30 p.m. Each entry will receive its lineup number and a staging map by email by December 1.
- Candy must be handed out by walkers, not thrown from floats or vehicles.
- Lighted floats are encouraged, since the parade is after dark.
- Santa Claus rides at the end of the parade, so please don't include a Santa on your entry.

Trophies will be awarded for Best Overall, Best Use of Theme, Best Lighted Float and Best Youth Group.

The City's Christmas tree lighting will be held the week before, on Friday, November 27, at 5:30 p.m. on the downtown square.

Spectators can expect downtown streets on the parade route to close around 5:00 p.m. on December 5. The best viewing is along 2nd Avenue; families are asked to stay behind the barricades and not to run into the street for candy. Free parking is available in the public lots on 1st Avenue.

Questions about parade entries can go to the Parks and Recreation office at (256) 734-2195.
