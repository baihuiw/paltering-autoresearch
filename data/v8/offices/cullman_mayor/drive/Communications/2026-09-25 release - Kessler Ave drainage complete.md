# Kessler Avenue drainage project complete; road fully open

CULLMAN, Ala. - Kessler Avenue NW is fully open again after the City finished a drainage project designed to keep stormwater off the road and out of yards on Kessler Avenue and Pine Hollow Court.

Crews replaced the rusted-out pipe that carries the branch under Kessler Avenue, regraded about 600 feet of roadside ditch, and replaced seven driveway pipes. The road, trench area and driveway aprons have been repaved. Final inspection was completed Thursday.

For years, heavy rains have backed water up at the low point on Kessler Avenue, where it ran across the road and into driveways and garages. The new crossing was designed to carry a 25-year storm without water going over the road.

"Families on Kessler Avenue have been dealing with this for a long time, and I'm glad we could get it done before winter," said Mayor Denise Kreuzer, who visited the site Thursday in her first week in office. "Thank you to our Engineering and Street departments, and to the neighbors for their patience with the lane closures."

The project was designed by the City Engineering Department and built by Winsted Site Works under a $286,400 contract.

Residents with questions about drainage on their street can call the Engineering Department at (256) 734-2150 ext. 230.
