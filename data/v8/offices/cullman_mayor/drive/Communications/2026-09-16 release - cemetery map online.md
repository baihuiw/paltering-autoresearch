# City Cemetery records now searchable online

CULLMAN, Ala. - Families looking for a loved one's grave, or wanting to see which spaces are available in City Cemetery, can now do it from home. Starting today, the City's cemetery map is searchable on cullmanal.gov.

The online map shows every section of City Cemetery, with burial records for more than 9,000 people going back to the 1890s. Visitors can search by name, see where a grave is located, and print a map to bring with them. Open spaces are shown in green.

The records were digitized from the City's original paper ledger cards in 2019, and the City Clerk's office has used the digital map at the counter since then. Today's launch puts the same map online for the public.

"People call us every week asking where a grandparent is buried, or whether there's still room next to a husband or wife," said City Clerk Russell Ingle. "Now they can look it up themselves, any time, and come in knowing exactly what they want."

Council President Denise Kreuzer, who visited the Clerk's office for a demonstration Tuesday, said the project is the kind of everyday service residents notice. "This is about families," she said. "Making it easier to find and care for the people we've lost is a good use of the City's time."

Spaces are still purchased in person at the City Clerk's office at City Hall, 204 2nd Avenue NE, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Certificates are mailed within 30 days of purchase. Burials are scheduled through the Cemetery Sexton at the Street Department, (256) 734-2180.

Corrections: if you find an error in a record, please call the City Clerk's office at (256) 734-2150 ext. 212.
