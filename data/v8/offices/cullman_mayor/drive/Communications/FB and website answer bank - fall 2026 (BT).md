# Facebook and Website Answer Bank - Fall 2026

For whoever is answering comments and messages on the City's Facebook page and the website contact form (Bryce, Tonya, and the front desk when covering). Last updated October 15, 2026. Use these answers as written, add a "thank you for asking," and point people to the right phone number. If a question isn't here, or it's a complaint about a specific employee, a legal matter, or something angry, don't answer publicly; send it to Bryce.

General rules:
- Answer within one business day on weekdays.
- Don't argue in the comments. One factual answer, then move it to a message or a phone call.
- Never discuss a resident's account, ticket, or case publicly.
- Don't delete comments unless they're obscene, threats, spam or personal information. Hide, don't delete, if unsure, and tell Bryce.

**Coffee with the Mayor - when is the next one?**
Every third Thursday at 7:30 a.m. in the City Hall lobby, 204 2nd Avenue NE. Next: Thursday, November 19, and Thursday, December 17. No RSVP needed. Bring a friend.

**How do I invite the Mayor to speak or come to your group?**
Contact Tonya Haskew in the Mayor's office, (256) 734-2150 ext. 201, or thaskew@cullmanal.gov. Include the group, date, time, place and what you'd like the Mayor to do. Tonya will check the calendar and confirm in writing.

**When does leaf pickup start? Bags or loose?**
November 2 through January 29. East side first (week of Nov 2), then west side (week of Nov 9); trucks come by about every two weeks. Loose or bagged both work. Keep leaves behind the curb, out of the street and storm drains, and no limbs in the piles. Street Department: (256) 734-2180.

**Is garbage pickup changing for the holidays?**
Veterans Day (Wed Nov 11): no change. Thanksgiving week: Mon-Wed normal; Thursday's route on Friday Nov 27; Friday's route on Saturday Nov 28. Full schedule on cullmanal.gov or call Sanitation, (256) 734-2185.

**Is the City putting in parking meters downtown?**
No. The Mayor has said she does not support parking meters downtown and will not propose them. Downtown street parking stays free with the posted two-hour limit, and new signs will point drivers to the free public lots on 1st Avenue.

**Can you stop the trains from blowing horns at night?**
Federal law requires train crews to sound horns at public crossings day and night. The only exception is an approved quiet zone, which requires safety improvements at several crossings, railroad and federal review, and Council funding. The City Engineer is studying a quiet zone at the 5th Street crossing and will report to the Council this winter. It usually takes a year and a half or more once a city decides to go forward.

**Who do I call about a building permit?**
Building Inspections, City Hall, (256) 734-2150 ext. 240, Mon-Fri 8:00-4:30. The fee schedule and the "what to bring" checklist are on the Building Inspections page. Most garages and accessory buildings need a simple site plan and floor plan; no engineer's seal under 1,000 sq ft for wood frame.

**When do I renew my business license?**
All City business licenses expire December 31, no matter when they were issued, including new businesses. Notices mail November 2. Renew by January 31, 2027 to avoid the 15% penalty. Business license desk: ext. 211.

**Cemetery: when will my certificate come? Can I buy a space in Section D or E?**
The City Clerk's office and the sexton are checking the original ledger cards for Sections D and E against the new online map, with a target of the week of November 16. Sales in D and E are paused and some certificates in those sections are on hold until the check is done; Section F spaces are available now. For a specific purchase, call Paula Rutledge, ext. 212.

**Can I pay a traffic ticket online?**
Most Cullman Police traffic tickets can be paid on the Municipal Court page of cullmanal.gov about five business days after the ticket is written (3.5% card fee). Some tickets require a court appearance. Court clerk: (256) 734-2165, Mon-Fri 8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m. Do not discuss the ticket itself.

**When is the Christmas parade? How do we enter?**
Saturday, December 5, 6:00 p.m., downtown. Entry forms on the Parks page, due Friday, November 20, 4:30 p.m. No entry fee. Parks: (256) 734-2195.

**Veterans Day ceremony?**
Wednesday, November 11, 11:00 a.m., City Hall flagpole (rain: City Hall auditorium). Veterans' organizations that want to take part can call Tonya, ext. 201.

**Someone is speeding on my street.**
Thank you for telling us. Please send the street and block, and the times it's worst, in a message. We'll pass it to the Police Traffic Unit, which can place a speed trailer and schedule enforcement. Engineering's traffic calming procedure explains when speed humps are considered. Police non-emergency: (256) 734-2160.

**Pothole / street sign down / limb in the road?**
Street Department, (256) 734-2180. If it's dangerous after hours, call police non-emergency.

**Water leak or no water?**
Water/Sewer, (256) 734-2190; after hours through police dispatch, (256) 734-2160.

**Question about my water or garbage bill?**
Utility Billing, ext. 220, Mon-Fri 8:00-4:30. The billing supervisor reviews any penalty the City caused (for example, a bill mailed late).

**Can I burn my leaves?**
No, open burning of leaves and yard waste isn't allowed inside the city limits. The City picks them up. Fire Rescue: (256) 734-2170.

**When does City Council meet?**
Second and fourth Mondays at 6:00 p.m. in the City Hall auditorium; work session at 5:30. Agendas are posted the Thursday before. Anyone may speak during public comments; sign in before 6:00.
