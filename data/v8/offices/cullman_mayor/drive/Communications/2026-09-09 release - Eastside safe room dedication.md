# New Eastside Community Safe Room dedicated

CULLMAN, Ala. - The City of Cullman dedicated its new Eastside Community Safe Room this morning, giving residents on the city's east side, including the families at Pine Ridge Mobile Home Park next door, a place built to withstand the strongest tornadoes.

The reinforced concrete safe room at 1450 3rd Street SE holds up to 300 people and is built to FEMA standards for EF-5 winds. It was funded by a FEMA Hazard Mitigation Grant with a City match.

"Too many people in this part of town live in mobile homes or houses without basements," said Fire Chief Ronnie Stidham. "Now they have somewhere to go that is close, that is strong, and that will be open when they need it."

The safe room's doors unlock automatically when Cullman County EMA activates community shelters for a tornado watch or warning, day or night, so no one has to wait for someone to arrive with a key. A separate pet room allows small pets in carriers or crates.

Councilmember Denise Kreuzer, who attended the dedication, thanked the Fire Rescue staff who wrote the grant. "This building is about peace of mind," she said. "When the sky turns dark, families on the east side won't have to wonder where to go."

Residents can find the safe room rules and a list of other community shelters on cullmanal.gov.
