# City Notes - October 2026

News from the City of Cullman. Printed and mailed with utility bills; also on cullmanal.gov. Sign up for the email edition on the City website.

## A note from Mayor Denise Kreuzer

Dear neighbors,

On September 21, I took the oath as your mayor. I never expected to be writing this letter, and I don't take it lightly. When our previous mayor resigned, my colleagues on the City Council chose me as Council President, and under state law that meant stepping into this office for the rest of the term.

I spent 31 years in our schools, and I learned there that people want three things from the folks in charge: show up, tell them the truth, and do what you said you'd do. That's my plan.

Starting October 15, I'll hold "Coffee with the Mayor" every month at 7:30 a.m. in the City Hall lobby. Come tell me what's on your mind.

Denise Kreuzer

## New safe room open on the east side

The Eastside Community Safe Room at 1450 3rd Street SE is open. It holds 300 people and is built to withstand EF-5 winds. **Its doors unlock automatically when Cullman County EMA activates shelters for a tornado watch or warning, day or night.** Small pets in carriers or crates may use the separate pet room. Fall can bring severe weather to north Alabama, so make your plan now.

## Kessler Avenue drainage work finished

The City has finished replacing the failed pipe under Kessler Avenue NW, regrading the ditches and replacing seven driveway pipes. The new crossing is designed to keep a 25-year storm off the road. Thank you to the neighbors for their patience.

## Find a loved one at City Cemetery - online

The City Cemetery map is now on cullmanal.gov. Search by name, see where a grave is, and see which spaces are open. Spaces are purchased at the City Clerk's office, and certificates are mailed within 30 days.

## Smoke on Second - Saturday, September 26

Barbecue, live music and a kids' area on 2nd Avenue downtown, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. 2nd Avenue will be closed from 1st Street to 3rd Street from 6 a.m. Saturday until 7 a.m. Sunday.

## Your FY2027 budget

The City Council adopted the budget for October 2026 through September 2027 on September 14. Highlights: four new police vehicles, Phase 1 of the 2027 street resurfacing program (14 streets), new sidewalks on 3rd Street SW near the school, and money to move the Street Department out of its flood-prone yard. The full budget is on cullmanal.gov.

## Leaf collection starts November 2

Rake leaves to the curb, loose or in bags, but keep them out of the street and away from storm drains. No limbs longer than 4 feet in leaf piles. East side first, then west.
