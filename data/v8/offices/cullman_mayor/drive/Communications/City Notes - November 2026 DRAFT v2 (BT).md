## A note from Mayor Denise Kreuzer

Dear neighbors,

Thank you to the 41 of you who came to the first Coffee with the Mayor on October 15, and to the people who brought their questions. I heard about train horns, leaves, speeding, parking, sidewalks, holiday garbage pickup, and a dog park. Every one of those is on a list in my office with a name next to it, and you'll hear back.

We'll keep doing this every month, on the third Thursday at 7:30 a.m. in the City Hall lobby. The next two are **Thursday, November 19** and **Thursday, December 17**. No need to RSVP. Just come.

One thing I want to say clearly, because I've heard the rumor: **I am not in favor of parking meters downtown, and I won't propose them.** Free parking is part of what makes downtown Cullman work. Instead, we're putting up signs to the free public lots on 1st Avenue and asking downtown employees to park there, so the spaces on 2nd Avenue are open for shoppers.

Denise Kreuzer

## Leaf collection starts November 2

Rake leaves to the curb, **loose or bagged**, and the Street Department will pick them up. Keep them out of the street and away from storm drains, and keep limbs out of the piles. Crews start on the east side the week of November 2, then the west side the week of November 9, and come by about every two weeks through January 29. A map of where crews are working is on cullmanal.gov. Questions: (256) 734-2180.

## Train horns at the 5th Street crossing

We've heard you. Federal law requires train crews to sound their horns at every public crossing, day and night, unless the crossing is part of an approved "quiet zone." The City Engineer is looking at what a quiet zone at 5th Street would take. It would include the crossings on either side and would need safety improvements, railroad approval and Council funding, a process that usually takes a year and a half or more. The City Engineer will report to the Council this winter.

## Building something?

A detached garage, carport, deck more than 30 inches high, or an addition needs a building permit. Bring a simple site plan showing distances to your property lines. Building Inspections, City Hall, ext. 240. Fee schedule and checklist on cullmanal.gov.
