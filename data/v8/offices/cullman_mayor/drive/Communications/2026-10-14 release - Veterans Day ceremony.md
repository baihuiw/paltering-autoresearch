# City to honor veterans at November 11 ceremony at City Hall

CULLMAN, Ala. - The City of Cullman will hold its annual Veterans Day ceremony on **Wednesday, November 11, at 11:00 a.m.** at the City Hall flagpole, 204 2nd Avenue NE. The public is invited. In case of rain, the ceremony will move inside to the City Hall auditorium.

The ceremony will include the presentation of the colors, the national anthem by the high school chamber choir, recognition of veterans of each branch of service, the laying of a wreath at the flagpole and the playing of Taps. Color guard and program details will be announced.

"Every year this ceremony reminds me how many families in Cullman have given a son, a daughter, a husband or a wife to the service of this country," said Mayor Denise Kreuzer. "I hope veterans will come and let us say thank you in person, and I hope parents will bring their children."

Reserved seating will be available for veterans. Others are welcome to bring a folding chair. Parking at City Hall will be reserved for veterans and people with disabilities; others may park in the public lots on 1st Avenue. A reception with coffee and cookies will follow in the City Hall lobby.

City offices will be closed on Veterans Day. **Garbage collection will run on the normal schedule, including Wednesday routes.**

The community Veterans Day parade, organized by the local veterans council, is Saturday, November 7, at 9:00 a.m. downtown.

The Council will also recognize Veterans Day with a proclamation at its regular meeting on Monday, November 9, at 6:00 p.m. in the City Hall auditorium.

Schools, scout troops and families are welcome to bring handmade cards or letters for veterans, which will be handed out at the reception. Cards can also be dropped off at the Mayor's office before November 11.

Veterans organizations or individuals who would like to take part in the ceremony may contact Tonya Haskew in the Mayor's office at (256) 734-2150, ext. 201.
