# Leaf collection begins November 2, east side first

CULLMAN, Ala. - The City of Cullman Street Department will begin its annual leaf collection on **Monday, November 2**, and continue through **Friday, January 29, 2027**.

Crews will start on the east side of the railroad (northeast, then southeast) during the week of November 2 and move to the west side (southwest, then northwest) the week of November 9. Trucks will come by each street about every two weeks during the season. A map showing where crews are working each day will be posted on cullmanal.gov by October 30.

**How to set out leaves**
- Rake leaves to the curb or the edge of the pavement, **loose or in bags**. Both are picked up. The vacuum trucks collect loose piles, and the crew behind them picks up bags.
- Keep leaves **out of the street and away from storm drains**. Leaves in the street clog drains and can make the road slick.
- **No limbs** in leaf piles. Limbs jam the vacuum; put them out separately for limb pickup.
- Keep piles away from mailboxes, fire hydrants, water meters and parked cars.
- Lawn services and contractors must haul away leaves from the properties they maintain.

The City added a second leaf vacuum truck this fall, which should shorten the time between passes, especially in the heavy weeks of late November.

"Last year some streets waited three weeks when a truck broke down," said Street Superintendent Wade Lovelady. "With two trucks, we can keep both sides of town moving."

Collected leaves are composted, and compost is available free to residents in the spring.

There will be no leaf collection on Thanksgiving (November 26-27), Christmas (December 24-25) or New Year's Day.

Residents who rake leaves before November 2 may set them out now; crews will collect them on the first pass. Please do not blow or rake leaves into the street while you wait.

Limb pickup continues on its regular schedule during leaf season. Limbs should be cut to 8 feet or shorter and stacked at the curb with the cut ends facing the street, separate from leaf piles. Burning leaves or limbs inside the city limits is not allowed.
