# Smoke on Second draws about 6,000 to downtown Cullman

CULLMAN, Ala. - Downtown Cullman was packed Saturday for the first Smoke on Second BBQ & Music Festival, which drew an estimated 6,000 people to 2nd Avenue for barbecue from 22 pitmasters, four live bands and a kids' area.

Downtown merchants reported one of their busiest Saturdays of the year. Several restaurants and shops stayed open late to serve festival-goers, and parking lots around downtown were full by mid-afternoon.

"This is what a downtown is for," Mayor Denise Kreuzer said at the evening welcome. "When people come to Cullman for a day like this, they eat in our restaurants, shop in our stores and come back. Every one of those sales helps pay for the services our residents count on."

The festival was produced by Cullman-based Ashmore Hospitality. The Cullman Police Department, Cullman Fire Rescue, and the Street and Sanitation departments supported the event. Police reported no arrests and no serious injuries, and Street and Sanitation crews had 2nd Avenue clean and open by 7 a.m. Sunday.

"Our officers did a great job keeping traffic moving and people safe," said Police Chief Allen Wakefield.

The Mayor thanked City employees who worked the event and the neighbors and businesses on 2nd Avenue for their patience with the street closure.

Organizers hope to make Smoke on Second an annual event.
