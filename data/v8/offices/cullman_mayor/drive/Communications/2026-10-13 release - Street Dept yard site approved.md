# Council approves site for new Street Department yard

CULLMAN, Ala. - The Cullman City Council voted 5-0 Monday night to buy a 9.2-acre site on Brockmann Road NE for a new Street Department yard, ending years of flooding problems at the department's current home on 4th Street SW.

The current yard has flooded three times in the last two years, forcing crews to move trucks, salt and equipment to high ground and costing days of work each time. It is also too small for the department's equipment and winter supplies.

"Our street crews are the people who clear the roads when it ices over and fix the potholes on your way to work," said Mayor Denise Kreuzer. "They need a yard that doesn't go under water every time it rains hard. This site is out of the flood zone, it has good access for trucks, and it lets us plan for the next 30 years."

The site, a former nursery, was ranked first of four sites evaluated by the City Engineering Department. The purchase is funded from the FY2027 capital budget, which set aside $625,000 for land and access for the new yard. Site work and buildings will be budgeted in future years.

Council also appointed Dwayne Pickard, a retired banker and Planning Commission member, to the Place 3 Council seat, and approved the road salt and sand contract for the coming winter.

The next Council meeting is Monday, October 26, at 6 p.m. in the City Hall auditorium.
