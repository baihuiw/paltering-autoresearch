MEMORANDUM

To: All Employees
From: Human Resources
Approved: Mayor Denise Kreuzer
Date: September 30, 2026
Re: City holidays, fiscal year 2027 (October 1, 2026 - September 30, 2027)

The following days are paid holidays for full-time employees. City Hall and most offices are closed. Departments that operate every day (Police, Fire Rescue, dispatch, water and sewer plant operators) and crews scheduled for holiday collection or events are paid under the holiday pay provisions of the personnel policy.

  Veterans Day ..................... Wednesday, November 11, 2026
  Thanksgiving ..................... Thursday, November 26, 2026
  Day after Thanksgiving ........... Friday, November 27, 2026
  Christmas Eve .................... Thursday, December 24, 2026
  Christmas Day .................... Friday, December 25, 2026
  New Year's Day ................... Friday, January 1, 2027
  Martin Luther King Jr. Day ....... Monday, January 18, 2027
  Memorial Day ..................... Monday, May 31, 2027
  Independence Day (observed) ...... Monday, July 5, 2027
  Labor Day ........................ Monday, September 6, 2027
  Floating holiday ................. one day, scheduled with your supervisor (use by September 30, 2027)

NOTES
1. Sanitation works on some City holidays so that routes don't stack up (Veterans Day and MLK Day: normal routes). Sanitation will publish its own holiday collection schedule in October. Front desk staff: please don't guess at garbage schedules; use the Sanitation schedule or call (256) 734-2185.
2. The Street Department works Veterans Day during leaf season (holiday pay or comp time, employee's choice).
3. Parks staff working the Veterans Day ceremony (Nov 11), the Christmas tree lighting (Fri Nov 27) and the Christmas parade (Sat Dec 5) receive holiday pay or overtime as applicable.
4. Municipal Court does not hold sessions on City holidays. Cases set on a holiday are moved to the next session automatically; defendants receive a notice.
5. The Council does not meet on City holidays. No regular meeting falls on a holiday this fiscal year.
6. Holiday pay for part-time and seasonal employees: see section 7.4 of the personnel policy.
7. Open enrollment for health insurance closes October 30. Questions: HR, ext. 250.

LEAVE REMINDERS FOR THE HOLIDAY SEASON
8. Vacation requests for the weeks of Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's must be approved by your supervisor by November 13. Departments must keep minimum staffing; requests are approved in order of receipt, with seniority breaking ties on the same day.
9. Employees must work the scheduled workday before and after a holiday to be paid for the holiday, unless on approved leave or out sick with a doctor's note.
10. Comp time earned for working a holiday must be used within 90 days or it is paid out at the end of that period.
11. Annual leave carryover: up to 240 hours carries into the next calendar year. Hours over 240 on December 31 are converted to sick leave. Check your balance on your pay stub or ask HR.
12. City Hall will close at noon on Wednesday, November 25 only if the Mayor approves it in a separate notice. Do not tell the public City Hall is closing early unless that notice goes out.
13. Inclement weather: if City schools close for snow or ice, City Hall opens at 10:00 a.m. unless the Mayor announces otherwise. Essential services (Police, Fire Rescue, Water/Sewer, Street, Sanitation) report as scheduled.

14. Public notices: departments that change hours or services around a holiday must send the notice to Bryce Templeton at least 7 days ahead so it can go on the website and Facebook. Don't post holiday changes on department pages without checking with Bryce; last year two departments posted conflicting information.

EMPLOYEE EVENTS

- Employee Christmas lunch: Friday, December 18, 11:30 a.m. - 1:00 p.m., community room. Departments rotate so phones stay covered.
