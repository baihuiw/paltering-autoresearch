# First 60 Days - Priorities and Working List

Draft for the Mayor, September 24, 2026 (Bryce). Updated October 14 with status notes in brackets. Not for distribution; this is our working list, not a public plan. The Mayor's own words from the Sept 21 dept heads meeting are at the top because everything else should line up with them.

> "Show up, tell people the truth, and do what you said you'd do. And no surprises."

## 1. How the office runs

- **Response standard.** Every resident who writes, emails, calls or speaks at Council gets an answer within five business days, even if the answer is "we're working on it, here's who has it." Tonya keeps the log and forwards to departments; departments answer Tonya within three business days. [Oct 14: running about 85% on time. Police and Engineering slowest; both now copy Tonya on replies.]
- **Weekly rhythm.** Monday 8:30 dept heads; Monday afternoon the Mayor and Bryce go through the week; Thursday 8:00 talking points for the Mayor, Tonya and the front desk. Council weeks: agenda review with Russell on the Wednesday before.
- **Calendar rules.** Tonya owns the calendar. No one books the Mayor directly, including Council members. Protect Friday afternoons for reading and letters. Rotary Wednesdays at noon. [Mayor added: no evening events two nights in a row unless it's Council.]
- **Letters.** The Mayor signs every reply letter herself. Tonya drafts routine ones; Bryce drafts anything sensitive. Initials "DK/th" on Tonya's drafts.
- **One-on-ones** with every department head in the first three weeks. [Done Oct 9 except HR (Oct 20).]

## 6. Communications

- Facebook: five posts a week max; departments send by 2:00 p.m. the day before.
- Press: Bryce takes all calls from the paper and TV. The Mayor does interviews with Bryce present or on the line.
- Website cleanup: Building Inspections fee page, Municipal Court payment page, leaf map, holiday schedule. [Court page and holiday schedule done; building fee example Oct 20.]

## 7. Things not to do in the first 60 days

- Don't reorganize departments.
- Don't promise anything that needs a Council vote until the Council has heard it.
- Don't announce new events. Get through the fall calendar (Veterans Day, tree lighting, parade) first.
