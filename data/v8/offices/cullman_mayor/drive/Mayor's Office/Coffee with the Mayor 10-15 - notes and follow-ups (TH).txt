COFFEE WITH THE MAYOR - NOTES AND FOLLOW-UPS
Thursday, October 15, 2026, 7:30 - 9:00 a.m., City Hall lobby
Notes: Tonya Haskew. Also present: Bryce Templeton; Wade Lovelady and Ken Matthis stopped by.

Attendance: 41 signed in (30 RSVPs; about a dozen walked in). Coffee: 3 urns was not enough; get 4 next time plus decaf. Donuts from the bakery on 1st Ave SE, $64 (Mayor paid personally).

FORMAT: Mayor welcomed everyone (about 4 minutes), then went table to table. Bryce and I took notes at each table. People liked that she sat down at each table instead of standing at a podium. Several asked if it would continue.

NEXT DATES (announced at the end): every third Thursday, 7:30 a.m., City Hall lobby. **Thursday, November 19** and **Thursday, December 17.** No RSVP needed; RSVPs just help with the coffee count. Bryce to post both dates on Facebook and in City Notes November.

WHAT PEOPLE RAISED (by topic, with follow-up owner)

1. Train horns at the 5th Street crossing (5 people, all east side). Mayor said Matt is pricing a quiet zone. Follow-up: Matt's memo came this afternoon; Bryce to draft a short answer for callers from it. -> Bryce
2. Leaf collection (4). Starts Nov 2, east side first, loose or bagged at the curb, no limbs. Wade answered at the table. One man asked for the zone map; Wade will have it on the website by Oct 30. -> Wade/Bryce
3. Downtown parking (4). Two merchants and two residents. Someone had heard "the City is putting in parking meters." Mayor said plainly she is not in favor of meters and no one has brought her a proposal; she wants free downtown parking to stay and better signs to the 1st Avenue lots. She asked Bryce to say that in City Notes. -> Bryce
4. Speeding on residential streets (3): 5th Ave SW (the same resident from the Sept 14 Council meeting; she has not heard back from Police), 11th St SW, 4th Ave NE. Mayor apologized for the delay on 5th Ave SW. -> Tonya to Chief Wakefield today (done, 10:15 a.m.)
5. Christmas parade (2). One church and one scout troop asking about entry. Forms are on the website and at the Parks office, due Nov 20, no fee. -> answered at the table
6. Building permit for a carport (1). Ken talked with him at the table; carports need a permit; he'll come in Monday. -> Ken
7. Garbage on holidays (2). Both asked if Thanksgiving week changes their day. Gave out Gary's schedule (copies at the sign-in table were gone by 8:15; print more). -> Tonya
8. Sidewalks near schools (2). Mayor mentioned 3rd St SW; another parent asked about the north side. -> Matt
9. Dog park (1). Same person who wrote in September; Mayor said Parks is looking at a site in the FY2028 plan. -> Parks director
10. Business license (1). New restaurant owner asked when to renew; told all licenses expire Dec 31, renewal notices Nov 2, due by Jan 31. -> answered
11. Veterans Day (1). Asked if the ceremony is still at the flagpole; yes, Nov 11, 11:00 a.m. Offered help with chairs. -> answered

WHAT WORKED / WHAT TO CHANGE (Mayor and Bryce, after)
- Sitting at each table worked. Keep it. Two staff note-takers is the minimum; three would be better if 50 come.
- Name tags helped the Mayor remember names. Keep them.
- Several people wanted to ask questions in front of everyone. Next time: 10 minutes of open questions at 8:30 for anyone who wants to stay.
- Parking: the City Hall lot was full by 7:35. Put a sign at the entrance pointing to the 1st Avenue lots.
- Two people asked for an evening version for working parents. Mayor wants to try one evening session in the spring (Thursday 6:00 p.m.) and see who comes.
- Spanish: one resident needed help with a business license question in Spanish. Ask HR which employees speak Spanish and would be willing to help at the next coffee.
