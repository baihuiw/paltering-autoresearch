CULLMAN CITY COUNCIL
REGULAR MEETING
MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 28, 2026, 6:00 P.M.
CITY HALL AUDITORIUM, 204 2ND AVENUE NE

The Cullman City Council met in regular session following a 5:30 p.m. work session at which no action was taken.

CALL TO ORDER: Council President Lori Stansell called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. The invocation was given by a member of the ministerial association, followed by the Pledge of Allegiance led by Boy Scout Troop members.

ROLL CALL: Present: Councilmembers Mark Steadman (Place 1), Lori Stansell (Place 2, President), Steve Uptain (Place 4) and Ron Graveman (Place 5). Place 3 is vacant. Also present: Mayor Denise Kreuzer; City Clerk Russell Ingle; City Attorney Hal Bevis; department heads.

1. STATEMENT ON OFFICERS: City Attorney Bevis stated for the record that Council President Denise Kreuzer succeeded to the office of Mayor at 5:00 p.m. on September 18, 2026 under Section 11-43-42 of the Code of Alabama, vacating her seat in Place 3, and that under the same section the President Pro Tem, Councilmember Stansell, became Council President for the unexpired term.

2. ELECTION OF PRESIDENT PRO TEM: Councilmember Graveman nominated Councilmember Uptain. No other nominations. Vote 3-0, Uptain abstaining.

3. DECLARATION OF VACANCY, PLACE 3: Motion by Steadman, second by Graveman, to declare a vacancy in Place 3 and to receive letters of interest from qualified electors residing in the City at the City Clerk's office through 4:30 p.m. Wednesday, October 7. City Attorney Bevis advised that under Section 11-43-41 the vacancy may be filled at the next regular meeting, October 12, or any later meeting. Vote 4-0.

4. MINUTES: Minutes of September 14, 2026, approved as presented. Motion by Uptain, second by Steadman. Vote 4-0.

5. MAYOR'S REPORT: Mayor Kreuzer thanked City employees for a smooth transition and for their work at Saturday's Smoke on Second festival, which brought an estimated 6,000 people downtown with no arrests. She announced a monthly "Coffee with the Mayor" starting October 15 at 7:30 a.m. in the City Hall lobby and asked residents to sign up for City Notes by email on cullmanal.gov.

6. Res. 2026-86: Bid award, 2027 street resurfacing program, Phase 1 (14 streets, list attached), to the low bidder, $1,486,302.50. Street Superintendent Lovelady said work would start in April. Motion by Graveman, second by Uptain. Vote 4-0.

7. Res. 2026-87: Agreement with the Alabama Department of Transportation for traffic signal maintenance on state routes inside the city, FY2027. Motion by Steadman, second by Stansell. Vote 4-0.

8. Res. 2026-88: Approving the annual renewal of the City's employee assistance program contract, $11,400. Motion by Uptain, second by Graveman. Vote 4-0.

9. Res. 2026-89: Authorizing the Mayor to sign the grant agreement for a state highway safety grant for overtime traffic enforcement, $24,000, no local match. Motion by Steadman, second by Uptain. Vote 4-0.

10. FIRST READING, Ord. 2026-31: Amending the City's sign ordinance to allow temporary banners on light poles downtown for City-approved events. No action; second reading October 12.

11. PUBLIC COMMENTS: Two residents spoke in support of more sidewalks near schools. One resident asked when leaf collection begins; Superintendent Lovelady said the first week of November.

12. COUNCIL COMMENTS: Councilmembers welcomed Mayor Kreuzer and thanked Council President Stansell.

ADJOURNMENT: 6:41 p.m. on motion by Uptain, second by Graveman. Vote 4-0.

Approved October 12, 2026.
