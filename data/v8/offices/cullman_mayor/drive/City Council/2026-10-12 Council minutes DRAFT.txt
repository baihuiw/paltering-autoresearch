CULLMAN CITY COUNCIL
REGULAR MEETING
MONDAY, OCTOBER 12, 2026, 6:00 P.M.
CITY HALL AUDITORIUM, 204 2ND AVENUE NE

DRAFT - for approval October 26, 2026

The Cullman City Council met in regular session following a 5:30 p.m. work session at which no action was taken.

CALL TO ORDER: Council President Lori Stansell called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. Invocation and Pledge of Allegiance.

ROLL CALL: Present: Councilmembers Mark Steadman (Place 1), Lori Stansell (Place 2, President), Steve Uptain (Place 4, President Pro Tem) and Ron Graveman (Place 5). Place 3 vacant at the call to order. Also present: Mayor Denise Kreuzer; City Clerk Russell Ingle; City Attorney Hal Bevis; department heads. About 25 members of the public.

1. MINUTES: Minutes of September 28, 2026, approved as presented. Motion by Steadman, second by Graveman. Vote 4-0.

2. Res. 2026-90, APPOINTMENT TO PLACE 3: Three letters of interest were received by October 7 and each applicant spoke at the work session. Councilmember Graveman nominated Dwayne Pickard, a retired banker and Planning Commission member. No other nominations. Vote 4-0. Mr. Pickard was sworn in by the City Clerk and took his seat at 6:09 p.m. for the remainder of the term.

3. PROCLAMATION: Mayor Kreuzer read a proclamation declaring October 2026 Domestic Violence Awareness Month.

4. Res. 2026-91: Bid award, road salt and sand for the 2026-27 winter season, low bidder, $84,250.00. Motion by Uptain, second by Steadman. Vote 5-0.

5. Res. 2026-92: Renewal of landscaping maintenance for City Hall and the downtown square, one year, $46,800.00. Motion by Graveman, second by Uptain. Vote 5-0.

6. Res. 2026-93: Interlocal agreement with Cullman County for animal shelter services, FY2027, $96,000. Motion by Steadman, second by Pickard. Vote 5-0.

7. Res. 2026-94: Authorizing the purchase of approximately 9.2 acres on Brockmann Road NE from Ruhrmann Holdings LLC for a new Street Department yard, at a negotiated price of $565,000.00, from the FY2027 capital budget, and authorizing the Mayor to sign the closing documents. Mayor Kreuzer presented the item, noting that the current yard on 4th Street SW has flooded three times in two years and that the Engineering Department's site comparison ranked the Brockmann Road site first of four. Councilmember Uptain asked when the yard would be built; City Engineer Coggins said site work would be budgeted in FY2028. No public comments. Motion by Uptain, second by Steadman. Vote 5-0.

8. Res. 2026-95: Declaring Police units 14 and 22 surplus for sale by online auction. Motion by Graveman, second by Pickard. Vote 5-0.

9. SECOND READING, Ord. 2026-31: Sign ordinance amendment, temporary event banners on downtown light poles. Motion to adopt by Uptain, second by Stansell. Roll call: Steadman yes, Stansell yes, Pickard yes, Uptain yes, Graveman yes. Adopted 5-0.

10. MAYOR'S REPORT: Mayor Kreuzer welcomed Councilmember Pickard, reminded residents of Coffee with the Mayor on October 15, and said leaf collection begins November 2.

11. PUBLIC COMMENTS: A resident asked about the train horn noise at the 5th Street crossing; the Mayor said the City Engineer is looking at the cost of a quiet zone. A downtown merchant asked about Halloween; downtown trick-or-treat is Saturday, October 31, 4:00 to 6:00 p.m.

12. COUNCIL COMMENTS: Councilmembers welcomed Mr. Pickard.

ADJOURNMENT: 6:48 p.m. on motion by Uptain, second by Pickard. Vote 5-0.
