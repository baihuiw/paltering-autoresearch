CULLMAN CITY COUNCIL
REGULAR MEETING
MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 14, 2026, 6:00 P.M.
CITY HALL AUDITORIUM, 204 2ND AVENUE NE

The Cullman City Council met in regular session. A work session was held at 5:30 p.m. in the same room; no action was taken at the work session.

CALL TO ORDER: Council President Ron Graveman called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. The invocation was given by a local pastor, followed by the Pledge of Allegiance.

ROLL CALL: Present: Councilmembers Mark Steadman (Place 1), Lori Stansell (Place 2, President Pro Tem), Denise Kreuzer (Place 3), Steve Uptain (Place 4) and Ron Graveman (Place 5, President). Also present: the Mayor; City Clerk Russell Ingle; City Attorney Hal Bevis; department heads. Approximately 40 members of the public.

1. MINUTES: Minutes of the August 24, 2026 regular meeting were approved as presented on motion by Uptain, second by Steadman. Vote 5-0.

2. MAYOR'S REPORT: The Mayor noted that the resignation delivered to the City Clerk on September 2 takes effect at 5:00 p.m. on Friday, September 18, thanked department heads and employees, and asked the Council for an orderly transition. City Attorney Bevis reviewed Section 11-43-42 of the Code of Alabama: in a city of more than 12,000 inhabitants, the president of the Council succeeds to the office of mayor for the unexpired term when the office becomes vacant, and the president's Council seat is vacated.

3. OFFICE OF COUNCIL PRESIDENT: Council President Graveman stated that, after discussion with his family and his business partners, he is not able to leave his Council seat and his business to serve as mayor for the remainder of the term, and he resigned the office of Council President effective immediately, keeping his seat as Councilmember for Place 5. He thanked the Council for the honor of serving as president. President Pro Tem Stansell assumed the chair.

The chair opened nominations for Council President. Councilmember Steadman nominated Councilmember Denise Kreuzer. There were no other nominations. Councilmember Kreuzer said she understood that the person elected would succeed to the office of mayor on September 18 and was willing to serve. City Attorney Bevis confirmed that a councilmember may be elected president at any regular meeting by majority vote.

Vote to elect Denise Kreuzer as Council President: Steadman yes; Stansell yes; Uptain yes; Graveman yes; Kreuzer abstained. Elected 4-0. President Kreuzer took the chair for the remainder of the meeting.

4. FY2027 BUDGET - Res. 2026-81: City Clerk Ingle presented the FY2027 budget for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2026. General Fund revenues are estimated at $57,940,000, with sales tax essentially flat compared with FY2026. The budget includes a 3% cost-of-living increase for full-time employees, the City's share of a health insurance increase, local funding for City schools, and the FY2027 capital budget (separate schedule). There were no public comments.

Motion by Uptain, second by Stansell, to adopt Res. 2026-81 adopting the FY2027 budget and capital budget. Vote 5-0.

5. Res. 2026-82: Bid award, four Police patrol vehicles (2027 model year, pursuit-rated SUVs) through the state contract, $214,880.00. Motion by Steadman, second by Graveman. Vote 5-0.

6. Res. 2026-83: Restaurant retail liquor license recommendation for a new restaurant on 1st Avenue SE (state license required). No objections from Police. Motion by Uptain, second by Steadman. Vote 4-1 (Graveman no).

7. Res. 2026-84: Change order no. 1, 3rd Street SW sidewalk project (ENG 26-11), add 140 feet of sidewalk at the school crossing, $18,620.00. Motion by Stansell, second by Uptain. Vote 5-0.

8. Res. 2026-85: Declaring a 2014 tandem dump truck (Street, unit 31) surplus for sale by online auction. Motion by Graveman, second by Steadman. Vote 5-0.

9. PUBLIC COMMENTS: A resident of 5th Avenue SW asked about speeding on her street; President Kreuzer asked the Police Chief to follow up. A downtown business owner thanked the Council for supporting events on 2nd Avenue.

10. COUNCIL COMMENTS: Councilmembers thanked the outgoing Mayor and wished President Kreuzer well. Councilmember Graveman thanked her for stepping forward.

ADJOURNMENT: Motion by Uptain, second by Steadman, to adjourn. Vote 5-0. Adjourned at 6:52 p.m.

Approved September 28, 2026.
