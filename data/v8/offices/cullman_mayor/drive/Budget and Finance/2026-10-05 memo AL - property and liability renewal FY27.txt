2. WHY PROPERTY WENT UP

- The pool raised property rates statewide after two bad hail years in north and central Alabama.
- Our insured building values went up 4.1% when the pool's valuation consultant updated replacement costs in June. The biggest changes were City Hall, the Police Department and the two newest fire stations.
- New buildings added since last year's statement of values are on page 3 of the statement. Please initial each page.

Deductibles are unchanged: $10,000 per property claim, $2,500 per auto physical damage claim, none on liability.

4. THINGS THAT WOULD HELP NEXT YEAR

a. Building inventories. Fire Rescue and Parks have each added storage buildings that were not on the statement until the pool's valuation consultant found them. Please ask department heads to send any new building, large shed or generator to Accounting when it is installed, not at renewal.
b. Driver checks. The pool gives a 3% auto credit if every employee who drives a City vehicle has a motor vehicle record check on file each year. We were at 88% in August. HR is chasing the rest.
c. Sidewalk inspections. The pool's loss control rep suggested a written sidewalk inspection schedule downtown. Matt says Engineering can do that this winter.

4a. HOW CLAIMS FROM RESIDENTS ARE HANDLED

Residents sometimes call the Mayor's office about damage they think the City caused (a mower throws a rock, a garbage truck clips a mailbox, a sewer backs up). For your office and the front desk:
- Take the name, address, phone and a short description, and send it to Accounting (ext. 215). Don't say whether the City will pay.
- Accounting sends the resident a claim form and forwards it to the pool's adjuster. The adjuster decides liability under state law, not the department.
- Mailboxes damaged by a City truck are handled by the department directly (Sanitation or Street replaces a standard mailbox and post). Anything beyond that goes through the claim process.
- Small claims under the $2,500 auto deductible are paid from the department's budget once the adjuster confirms them.
