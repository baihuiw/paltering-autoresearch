# Utility Billing - Garbage and Water/Sewer Rates, FY2027

Counter reference for Utility Billing (City Hall, 1st floor, ext. 220), the front desk and the Mayor's office. Effective October 1, 2026. Rates are set by Council ordinance; this sheet summarizes them. Prepared by Accounting.

**FY2027: no change to residential garbage rates. Water and sewer rates increase 3% on the first bill after January 1, 2027 (Ord. 2025-44, the third of three annual steps adopted in 2025).**

## Billing basics

- Water, sewer and garbage appear on one monthly bill. Bills mail around the 1st; due the 20th.
- A 10% penalty is added to the unpaid balance after the 20th. Service may be disconnected for nonpayment after a written notice and 10 days.
- Pay online (cullmanal.gov > Pay My Bill), by bank draft, by mail, at the drop box on the City Hall parking lot side, or at the counter Mon-Fri 8:00 a.m. - 4:30 p.m.
- Properties outside the city limits that get City water are billed water only at the outside-city rate; garbage service is for inside-city customers.

## Residential garbage (inside city limits)

| Service | Monthly |
|---|---|
| One 96-gallon cart, weekly pickup (standard) | $17.50 |
| Second cart | $8.00 |
| Senior/disability rate (age 65+ or disability, owner-occupied, application required) | $12.25 |
| Backdoor service (medical need, physician's letter) | No added charge |

Garbage rates include weekly cart pickup, monthly bulk pickup by request (up to 3 items), limb pickup, leaf collection in season, and use of the recycling drop-off center. Replacement for a cart that is stolen or damaged by the truck is free; a cart damaged by the customer is $65.

Holiday schedule: see "Sanitation holiday collection schedule FY2027" or call Sanitation, (256) 734-2185.

## Commercial garbage

Dumpster service (2, 4, 6 or 8 yard) is billed by size and pickups per week; see the commercial rate table in the ordinance. Small businesses using carts pay $24.00 per cart per month.

## New service and transfers

- New account: $50 deposit for owners, $150 for renters (refunded after 12 months of on-time payment, or at closing). Bring ID and a lease or deed.
- Connect/transfer fee: $25. Same-day service if requested by noon.
- Landlords may sign a standing agreement so service stays on between tenants.

## Leak adjustments

One adjustment per 12 months for a repaired leak: the customer sends a plumber's invoice or receipt for parts; the bill is adjusted to the average of the prior six months for sewer and half the excess for water. Pool fills: call before filling for a sewer credit.

## Senior and disability garbage rate - how to apply

Bring proof of age (65 or older) or a disability award letter, and proof that you own and live in the home (deed or property tax bill with homestead exemption). The rate starts on the next bill after approval and is reviewed every three years. Renters do not qualify unless the account is in their name and the landlord confirms in writing.

## Who handles what

- Bills, deposits, adjustments: Utility Billing, ext. 220
- Leaks at the meter, low pressure, water quality: Water/Sewer, (256) 734-2190 (after hours through police dispatch)
- Missed garbage, bulk pickup, carts: Sanitation, (256) 734-2185
- Leaves and limbs: Street Department, (256) 734-2180

Billing questions belong with Utility Billing; please refer callers there rather than promising anything. The billing supervisor can review a penalty if the City caused the delay (for example, a bill mailed late by the City's print vendor).
