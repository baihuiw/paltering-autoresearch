Adopted by the City Council September 14, 2026 (Res. 2026-81), for October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027. Prepared by the City Clerk and the City Treasurer for the budget book. The capital budget is a separate schedule (see "FY2027 capital budget summary"). Water/Sewer is an enterprise fund with its own budget.

Notes on revenue:
- Sales tax is budgeted essentially flat against FY2026 actual receipts (preliminary $32,388,541). Receipts have run within about 2% of the prior year all year.
- Building permit revenue is lower because two large commercial projects permitted in FY2026 will not repeat.
- Interest is lower because rates on the City's CDs are expected to fall through the year.
- "Other" includes a one-time reimbursement from the county for shared radio tower work ($260,000).

## What changed

1. **Pay.** 3% cost-of-living increase for full-time employees effective the first full pay period in October. Step increases continue for police and fire under the existing pay plans. No new positions in the General Fund except one Municipal Court clerk position converted from part-time to full-time.
2. **Health insurance.** The City's share of the premium rises 7.5% (employee share unchanged). Open enrollment runs through October 30.
3. **Police.** Two officer positions that were vacant for most of FY2026 are funded for the full year; two recruits start the academy November 2.
4. **Street.** One-time money for a leaf vacuum truck was spent in FY2026 (delivered October 2026); FY2027 adds overtime for leaf season and the brine operation.
5. **Sanitation.** Landfill tipping charges increase $1.50 per ton January 1; the residential garbage rate is unchanged for FY2027.
6. **Parks.** Seasonal staff wages rise to match the private market; the Christmas parade, tree lighting and Veterans Day ceremony are budgeted in the Parks programs line ($38,000) and Administration ($6,500).
7. **Outside agencies.** The animal shelter interlocal with Cullman County is $96,000 (Res. 2026-93, approved October 12).

## Reserve

The unassigned General Fund balance at September 30, 2026 is estimated at $19.6 million before audit, or about 124 days of operating spending. The Council's reserve policy calls for at least 90 days. The FY2027 transfer to reserve keeps the City above that target.

## Budget calendar for the year

- Monthly financial reports to the Mayor and Council by the fourth Friday of the following month (first FY2027 report: October 23).
- Carryover resolution for open FY2026 capital projects: November 9 Council meeting.
- Mid-year review: April 2027.
- FY2028 budget requests from department heads: due June 2027.
