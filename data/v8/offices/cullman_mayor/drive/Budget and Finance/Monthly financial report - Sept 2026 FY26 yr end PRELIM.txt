PRELIMINARY. Accounts stay open through November 13 for September invoices and accruals. Final FY2026 figures will be in the audited statements (field work in January). This report replaces the "September flash" numbers I gave at the October 5 department heads meeting; the payroll accrual for the last week of September was not in the flash.

1. SUMMARY

- General Fund revenue for FY2026 is $57,312,406.18, or 100.8% of the $56,880,000 budget.
- General Fund expenditures are $55,604,219.77, or 97.8% of appropriations.
- Revenue over expenditures for the year: $1,708,186.41, before year-end accruals. I expect that figure to drop by about $250,000 once September invoices are posted.
- Sales tax: September receipts (August sales) were $2,671,904.19, down 0.8% from September 2025. For the full year, sales tax was $32,388,541.06, up 0.5% from FY2025 and $288,541.06 over budget. Detail is in "Sales tax receipts by month FY25-FY27 (AL)".
- Cash and investments, all funds, at September 30: $48,206,733.52.
