CITY OF CULLMAN
OFFICE OF THE CITY CLERK

PUBLIC NOTICE
VACANCY ON THE CITY COUNCIL, PLACE 3

Posted September 29, 2026 at City Hall and on cullmanal.gov. Published in the newspaper October 1, 2026.

At its regular meeting on September 28, 2026, the Cullman City Council declared a vacancy in City Council Place 3. The seat became vacant when Councilmember Denise Kreuzer, then President of the Council, succeeded to the office of Mayor on September 18, 2026, under Section 11-43-42 of the Code of Alabama.

Under Section 11-43-41 of the Code of Alabama, the Council will fill the vacancy by appointment for the remainder of the term, which ends in November 2029. The Council expects to make the appointment at its regular meeting on Monday, October 12, 2026, at 6:00 p.m.

WHO MAY APPLY
Any person who:
- is a qualified elector (registered voter) of the City of Cullman;
- has lived inside the city limits for at least 90 days; and
- meets the other qualifications for municipal office under state law.
Council seats are elected at large by place, so an applicant may live anywhere in the city.

HOW TO APPLY
Submit a letter of interest to the City Clerk by 4:30 p.m. on Wednesday, October 7, 2026. The letter should include:
1. your name, home address, phone and email;
2. how long you have lived in Cullman;
3. your work, civic and volunteer experience;
4. why you want to serve; and
5. a statement that you are a registered voter of the City of Cullman.
Letters may be delivered to the City Clerk's office, City Hall, 204 2nd Avenue NE (1st floor), mailed (must be received by the deadline), or emailed to ringle@cullmanal.gov. The Clerk will confirm receipt by email or phone within one business day.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
- Letters of interest are public records and will be included in the Council's packet for October 12.
- Each applicant will be invited to speak for up to five minutes at the 5:30 p.m. work session on October 12.
- At the regular meeting, a Councilmember may nominate an applicant; the appointment requires a majority vote of the Council members present.
- The person appointed will be sworn in by the City Clerk at the meeting and take the seat immediately.

The Mayor does not vote on the appointment.

FREQUENTLY ASKED
- Will there be a special election? No. State law provides for the Council to fill a Council vacancy by appointment for the rest of the term. The seat is next on the ballot at the regular municipal election in August 2029.
- Can the person appointed run in 2029? Yes, like any other qualified candidate.
- Does the appointee have to live in a particular part of town? No. Places are citywide.
- Is the pay the same? Yes. Council members are paid under the existing ordinance; the appointee is paid from the date of the oath.
- What will the appointee need to file? A statement of economic interests with the state ethics commission within the time the law requires, and the City's oath of office. The Clerk will provide the forms and an orientation on the agenda process and the budget.
