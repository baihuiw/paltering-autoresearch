# 2027 Business License Renewal - Notice Text and Counter FAQ

City Clerk's Office. Renewal notices mail **Monday, November 2, 2026**, to every business with a 2026 City of Cullman business license. Rates are unchanged from 2026 (no Council action this year). This file is the text of the notice plus the answers the counter gives most often. Business license desk: City Hall 1st floor, (256) 734-2150 ext. 211; licenses@cullmanal.gov.

---

## Text of the notice

Dear Business Owner:

Your City of Cullman business license expires **December 31, 2026**. All City business licenses run on the calendar year, January 1 through December 31, no matter what month the license was first issued.

**Renew between November 2, 2026 and January 31, 2027.** Renewals received or postmarked after **January 31, 2027** are delinquent and will be charged a penalty of **15% of the license amount**, plus interest of 1% per month after February. Doing business without a current license is a violation of City ordinance.

How to renew:
1. **Online** at cullmanal.gov > Business License > Renew (card or e-check; convenience fee applies). Online renewal opens November 2.
2. **By mail** with the enclosed form and a check payable to "City of Cullman."
3. **In person** at the City Clerk's office, City Hall, 204 2nd Avenue NE, Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Please avoid the last week of January if you can; lines are long.

The license fee for most businesses is based on **gross receipts for calendar year 2026** from business done in the city. Report your gross receipts on the form. Please have your 2026 figures ready; we may ask for supporting records.

Thank you for doing business in Cullman.

Russell Ingle, City Clerk

## Counter FAQ (for staff)

**I opened in August (or September, or October). Is my license good for a year?**
No. Every license expires December 31. A business that opened during 2026 renews in January like everyone else. On the renewal, report gross receipts from your opening date through December 31, 2026. There is no penalty as long as the renewal is in by January 31, 2027. (New businesses paid a prorated or minimum first-year amount; the renewal is based on actual receipts.)

**I opened in November or December. Do I renew right away?**
Yes; licenses issued after October 1 are still 2026 licenses. Businesses that open in November or December can buy their 2027 license at the same visit to avoid coming back in January. The counter will offer it.

**What if I didn't get a notice?**
Renew anyway. The notice is a courtesy; the deadline is the same. Updated addresses help: check the mailing address on file when renewing.

**What is the minimum license?**
The minimum is $50 to $100 depending on the type of business (see "Business license schedule - common classes and rates"). Some classifications (contractors, vending, alcohol, lodging) have flat or separate schedules; see the license schedule at the counter.

**I closed my business.**
Email or write the Clerk's office with the closing date so we can close the account and stop notices. A final gross receipts report may be requested.

**I sell at the farmers market / festivals only.**
Vendors at City-permitted markets and events need a license for each calendar year they sell in the city. The market manager can tell you whether the market's license covers you.

**Home-based businesses?**
Yes, they need a license. Zoning sign-off (home occupation) is done at the counter the first year only.

**Contractors from outside Cullman?**
A contractor working inside the city limits needs a City license (contractor schedule) before a building permit is issued. Proof of state licensing is required for trades that require it.

**What if my renewal was on time but the penalty was charged anyway?**
Bring the receipt or bank record. The Clerk corrects a penalty when the City made the mistake (for example, a payment received on time but posted late). Otherwise penalties are set by ordinance and apply to everyone.

**Do I need a separate license for each location?**
Yes. A business with two locations in the city needs two licenses.

**I sold my business.**
Licenses don't transfer to a new owner. The new owner applies for a new license; the old owner closes the account.

**Where does license money go?**
The General Fund (about $3.9 million in FY2027), which pays for police, fire, streets and parks.
