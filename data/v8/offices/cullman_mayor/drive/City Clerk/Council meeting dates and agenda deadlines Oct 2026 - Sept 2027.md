# City Council Meeting Dates and Agenda Deadlines, October 2026 - September 2027

City Clerk's Office. Regular meetings are the **second and fourth Mondays at 6:00 p.m.** in the City Hall auditorium, 204 2nd Avenue NE, with a work session at 5:30 p.m. in the same room. Meetings are open to the public and streamed on the City's Facebook page (new audio and streaming equipment in the FY2027 capital budget, installation in January).

## How agenda items get on the agenda

1. Department heads send the item request form, a draft resolution or ordinance, and backup (bids, quotes, maps, contracts) to the City Clerk **by noon on the Tuesday 13 days before the meeting** (see the table).
2. The Clerk and the city attorney review drafts Wednesday through Friday.
3. The Mayor reviews the draft agenda with the Clerk on the **Wednesday before** the meeting.
4. The packet goes to Council members by email and paper on **Thursday at 3:00 p.m.**; the agenda is posted at City Hall and on cullmanal.gov the same afternoon.
5. Late items: only with the Mayor's and the Council President's approval, and only for time-sensitive matters (bid deadlines, emergencies). A late item is added at the start of the meeting by motion.

Ordinances are read at two meetings unless the Council votes unanimously to suspend the rules. Resolutions are adopted at one meeting.

## Public comments

Anyone may speak during Public Comments at the end of each regular meeting. Sign in with the Clerk before 6:00 p.m.; three minutes per speaker. The Council does not take action on public comments; the Mayor or Council President usually asks the right department to follow up, and the Clerk notes the request in the minutes.

## Minutes

Draft minutes are posted with the next agenda packet and become official when approved. Approved minutes, resolutions and ordinances are on cullmanal.gov and in the Clerk's office. Copies: $0.25 per page; certified copies $5.00.
