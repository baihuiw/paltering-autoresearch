# City Cemetery: Rules, Fees and Procedures

City of Cullman, Office of the City Clerk
Revised January 2024 (fee schedule adopted by the City Council). Counter copy; the binder copy at the front desk is the same.

## 1. Who to contact

- **Purchases, certificates, transfers and records:** Office of the City Clerk, City Hall, 204 2nd Avenue NE, (256) 734-2150 ext. 212. Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
- **Burials, grave locations, markers on the ground:** Cemetery Sexton, Street Department, (256) 734-2180.
- Funeral homes schedule burials through the Sexton at least 24 hours in advance (48 hours for Saturday services).

## 2. Buying a space

1. Spaces are sold by the City Clerk's office from the cemetery map. Ask at the counter or look up open spaces on the online map before you come in.
2. The purchaser pays in full at the time of purchase. We accept cash, check or card (card fee applies).
3. A receipt is issued at the counter. **Certificates of Interment Rights are mailed to the purchaser within 30 days of payment.** The certificate is the family's proof of the right to burial in the space; keep it with your important papers. Monument companies usually ask for a copy.
4. Purchase of a space conveys a right of interment only. The City retains ownership of the land.

## 4. Returns, exchanges and transfers

- **Unused spaces may be returned to the City for a refund of the purchase price less a $50.00 processing fee, or exchanged for available spaces of equal value.**
- A certificate holder may transfer an unused space to another person by filing a transfer form with the City Clerk and paying the transfer fee. Both parties sign.
- The City does not buy back spaces at current prices.

## 5. Markers and monuments

- One upright monument per two adjoining spaces, or flat markers on each space. Double (companion) monuments must sit on the line between two spaces owned by the same family.
- All monuments must be set on a concrete foundation inspected by the Sexton before setting. Monument companies must show a copy of the certificate for each space the monument covers.
- No curbing, fencing, glass containers or shepherd's hooks.

## 6. Decorations and upkeep

- Fresh flowers are removed when wilted. Artificial arrangements are removed on the first Monday of each month, March through November, and after the January cleanup.
- The City mows and trims on a two-week cycle from April through October.

## 7. Records

The City Clerk keeps the official record of every space sold and every interment. Historic records from the original ledger cards were entered into the City's digital cemetery map in 2019. If you believe the map shows a space incorrectly, please tell the City Clerk's office so it can be checked against the original records.
