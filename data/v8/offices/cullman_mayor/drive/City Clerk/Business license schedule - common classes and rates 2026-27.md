# Business License Schedule - Common Classifications and Rates (2026 and 2027 license years)

City Clerk's Office, business license desk, (256) 734-2150 ext. 211. This is a counter summary of the most-used classifications in the business license ordinance. Rates for 2027 are unchanged from 2026. The ordinance controls if anything here differs. Less common classifications (utilities, banks, insurance companies, liquor, lodging, pawn, peddlers) have separate schedules in the ordinance binder at the counter.

## How the license year works

- **Every City business license runs January 1 - December 31.** It expires December 31 regardless of the date issued.
- Renewals are due January 1 and are delinquent after **January 31**. Penalty: 15% of the license amount, plus 1% per month after February.
- **New businesses** pay for the rest of the current year when they open: the minimum for their class if they open after June 30, otherwise the full minimum plus the gross receipts schedule as estimated. In January they renew on actual gross receipts from their opening date through December 31.
- A separate license is needed for each location. Businesses with more than one classification pay under the highest one plus any separately required class.

## Gross receipts schedule (Schedule A) - most retail, restaurant, service and professional businesses

Based on gross receipts from business done inside the city limits during the prior calendar year.

| Prior-year gross receipts | Annual license |
|---|---|
| $0 - $25,000 | $50 (minimum) |
| $25,001 - $100,000 | $50 plus $1.50 per $1,000 over $25,000 |
| $100,001 - $500,000 | $162.50 plus $1.25 per $1,000 over $100,000 |
| $500,001 - $2,000,000 | $662.50 plus $1.00 per $1,000 over $500,000 |
| Over $2,000,000 | $2,162.50 plus $0.75 per $1,000 over $2,000,000 |

Example: a bakery with $180,000 in receipts for its first full year: $162.50 + (80 x $1.25) = **$262.50**.
Example: a new business that opened August 1 and had $60,000 in receipts August-December: $50 + (35 x $1.50) = **$102.50** for 2027.

Minimums by type (Schedule A businesses never pay less than this):
- Retail, services, personal services (barber, salon, nails, massage): $75
- Restaurants and food service: $100
- Home-based businesses: $50
- Professional offices (accounting, engineering, real estate brokers, attorneys): $75
- Child care centers: $100

## Contractors (Schedule C)

Contractors working inside the city limits need a City license whether or not they have an office here.

| Class | Annual license |
|---|---|
| General contractor (residential or commercial) | $150 plus $0.50 per $1,000 of city contracts over $100,000 |
| Trade contractor (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing) | $150 |
| Subcontractor (non-trade: painting, flooring, landscaping over $25,000/yr) | $75 |
| Lawn care and landscape services | $50 |

Proof of the state license is required for trades that require one. Building Inspections will not issue a permit to a contractor without a current City license.

## Mobile and temporary (Schedule M)

| Class | License |
|---|---|
| Mobile food vendor (food truck, cart) | $100 per year, plus Fire Rescue inspection and county health permit |
| Farmers market vendor | $50 per year (or covered under the market's license if the market holds one) |
| Temporary vendor at a City-permitted event | $25 per event, unless covered by the event organizer's license |
| Transient merchant / door-to-door sales | $100 per year plus solicitor registration with Police |

## Other common classes

| Class | License |
|---|---|
| Vape, tobacco and smoke shops | $150 minimum (Schedule A applies above the minimum) |
| Short-term rental operators | New class proposed (Ord. 2026-32, first reading Oct 26): $75 per unit per year |
| Vending machines | $10 per machine |
| Coin-operated amusement devices | $50 per device |

## Exemptions

- Nonprofit organizations (with IRS letter) are exempt from the license fee but must register.
- Disabled veterans: exemption for certain classes under state law; bring the VA letter.
- Farmers selling their own produce: exempt under state law.

## Counter notes

- Gross receipts are confidential. Do not discuss a business's figures with anyone other than the owner or the owner's accountant.
- Penalties are set by ordinance. The Clerk corrects a penalty only when it was caused by City error.
- Renewal notices for 2027 mail November 2. Online renewal opens the same day.
