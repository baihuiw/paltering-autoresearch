Dear Mrs. Dodd,

Thank you for your card. It was a bright spot on my desk this week. You wrote to thank the Street Department crew who picked up the limbs from the oak in your front yard and who, you said, "raked up behind themselves better than my grandsons do." I passed your card along to Wade Lovelady, our Street Superintendent, and he read it to the crew at their morning meeting. They don't hear thank-you often enough.

You also asked two questions, and I want to answer both.

First, when does leaf season start, and does the City take bags? Leaf collection starts Monday, November 2, and the east side, including your street, is first. You can rake leaves to the curb loose or put them in bags; the crews take both. The vacuum truck picks up the loose piles and the crew behind it picks up the bags. Please keep the leaves behind the curb and out of the street and away from storm drains, and keep limbs out of the leaf piles, because they jam the vacuum. The trucks come by about every two weeks through the end of January.

Second, why did the limb pickup take two weeks this time? Honestly, because one of the Street Department's mowers broke down in September and the crew that normally runs limbs was covering mowing. Limb pickup is back on its normal schedule now. If you ever have limbs at the curb for more than two weeks, please call the Street Department at (256) 734-2180 and ask them to check.

And yes, the oak was a good one. Our Street Department tells me it was likely more than 80 years old. If you would like a replacement tree for your front yard, the City's Tree Commission gives away a limited number of young trees each spring on Arbor Day; I'll make sure your name is on the list.

Thank you for writing, and for 52 years on 7th Street. I hope to see you at Coffee with the Mayor sometime; it's every month at City Hall.

Sincerely,

Denise Kreuzer
Mayor, City of Cullman
