Dear Mr. Gentry,

Thank you for calling my office on Saturday evening, and I am so sorry about the water in your garage and the damage to your freezer and your tools. I know how upsetting it is to watch water come up the driveway after you were told the work on your street was finished.

Saturday's storm was extraordinary. Our rain gauge at the Street Department recorded 3.4 inches of rain in about an hour and a half, the heaviest burst it has recorded since it was installed. Many streets across the city had water over them for a time, and our crews were out until after midnight clearing inlets.

Our City Engineer, Matt Coggins, and Street Superintendent Wade Lovelady have been out to Kessler Avenue and Pine Hollow Court to look at how the new crossing and ditches handled the storm, and they will keep watching it. If you see anything new, such as erosion at the ditch or the pipe outlet, please call the Engineering Department at (256) 734-2150 ext. 230.

For your own losses, your homeowner's insurance agent is the best first call. The Street Department can also pick up damaged items at the curb at no charge this week; call (256) 734-2180.

Thank you for your patience, and please don't hesitate to call my office again.

Sincerely,

Denise Kreuzer
Mayor, City of Cullman
