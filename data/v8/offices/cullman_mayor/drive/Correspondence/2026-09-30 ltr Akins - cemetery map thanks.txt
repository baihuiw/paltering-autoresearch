Dear Mrs. Akins,

Thank you for your kind note about the new online cemetery map, and for taking the time to write by hand. I passed it along to Russell Ingle and Paula Rutledge in the City Clerk's office, who did the work, and they were glad to hear it.

I was touched to read that you found your grandparents' graves in Section A after looking for years, and that your granddaughter in Huntsville was able to find them too. That is exactly why we put the map online.

You asked whether the City can help with the leaning headstone you found. The stones belong to the families, so the City can't reset them, but our Cemetery Sexton, Earl Bratcher, can tell you which monument companies do resetting work and what the foundation rules are. You can reach him at the Street Department, (256) 734-2180.

Thank you again for writing. It has been a busy first few weeks, and letters like yours are a good reminder of why we do this work.

Sincerely,

Denise Kreuzer
Mayor, City of Cullman
