Dear Ms. Lambert,

Thank you for your note about parking downtown. You said you had heard that the City was "about to put in parking meters" on 2nd Avenue, and that you'd stop shopping downtown if it did.

Let me answer that directly: I do not support parking meters downtown, and no proposal to install them has come to me or to the City Council. Free parking is one of the things that makes downtown Cullman work. People drive here from all over north Alabama because they can park, walk, shop and eat without feeding a meter, and our merchants depend on that.

I think I know where the rumor started. This summer, a working group of downtown merchants and the Chamber looked at parking during busy weekends. Their draft listed a number of ideas, from better signs to the public lots to paid parking, as things other cities have tried. Listing an idea isn't the same as recommending it, and the City hasn't adopted any of it.

What I would like to do instead:
- Keep downtown street parking free, with the two-hour limit on 2nd Avenue that is already posted, so spaces turn over for shoppers. Police will enforce the limit more consistently, starting with warnings.
- Put up clear signs pointing drivers to the free public lots on 1st Avenue. Many visitors don't know those lots are there.
- Ask employees of downtown businesses to park in the lots rather than on 2nd Avenue, so the front spaces are open for customers. The merchants I've talked with are already working on this.

Our Street Department will have the new lot signs up before the Christmas shopping season.

You also asked about handicapped parking. There are accessible spaces on every block of 2nd Avenue downtown and in each of the 1st Avenue lots. If you find one blocked by a vehicle without a placard, please call Police non-emergency at (256) 734-2160.

Thank you for shopping downtown, and for telling me what you think. Please keep doing both.

Sincerely,

Denise Kreuzer
Mayor, City of Cullman
