Dear Mr. Treadaway,

Thank you for inviting me to speak to your club. I'm glad to accept, and I'm looking forward to it.

To confirm the details as I understand them:

  Date:      Tuesday, November 17, 2026
  Time:      12:00 noon (I'll arrive by 11:50)
  Program:   about 20 minutes on my first two months as mayor and what's ahead for the City, followed by questions
  Contact:   Tonya Haskew in my office, (256) 734-2150 ext. 201, thaskew@cullmanal.gov

If your club would like me to cover particular topics, please let Tonya know by November 10 and we'll build them in. Groups have been asking most about downtown, streets and sidewalks, leaf season, and the budget. I'm happy to take any question, and if I don't know the answer, I'll find out and get back to whoever asked.

I don't use slides for civic club talks unless the room is set up for them, so there's nothing you need to prepare except a spot near a microphone.

I'm saying yes to as many civic clubs, schools, churches and neighborhood groups as my calendar allows this fall. Tuesday lunches are often open, and I'd be glad if you'd pass that along to other program chairs you know. They can reach Tonya directly.

If it's helpful for your newsletter, you're welcome to use this short introduction: "Denise Kreuzer became Mayor of Cullman on September 18, 2026. A retired elementary school principal, she served on the City Council in Place 3 and was elected Council President in September, which under state law made her the successor to the office of mayor for the remainder of the term. She holds Coffee with the Mayor on the third Thursday of each month at City Hall."

Thank you again, and please give my regards to your members.

Sincerely,

Denise Kreuzer
Mayor, City of Cullman
