Dear Mr. Bowers,

Thank you for your letter about events downtown, and for your kind words about the first few weeks.

You asked whether the City plans to keep bringing festivals to 2nd Avenue, and whether I'd consider more of them on Saturdays instead of Sundays. The answer to the first is yes. Downtown events bring people from all over north Alabama, and when they come, they spend money in shops like yours. The sales tax they pay helps fund our police, fire, streets and parks. I want Cullman to be the easiest city in north Alabama to hold an event in.

On Saturdays versus Sundays: the City doesn't pick event dates; organizers apply for the dates they want, and the City Clerk's office reviews each permit with Police, Fire Rescue and the Street Department. I've passed your suggestion to City Clerk Russell Ingle so it's part of the conversation when organizers call.

I also heard your concern about parking for your regular customers on event days. Our Street Department will look at signs pointing people to the lots on 1st Avenue during the next big event.

Thank you for writing, and for 30 years of business downtown.

Sincerely,

Denise Kreuzer
Mayor, City of Cullman
