Amber,

I finished the quarter-end tie-out of our transport counts against what the billing company shows as submitted. July and September tie. June and August do not, and the gap is big.

What happened: when Teague wound down in June they stopped taking new trips as of Monday, June 8. Hallbrook's connection to our run reporting system did not go live until Monday, August 3. Every transport in between (June 8 through August 2) was sitting in our export folder and never went to either company. I count 231 trips. I checked three of them by hand against the run reports and they are real transports with signed patient forms.

Of the 231, 14 are self-pay with no insurance on file, so those get a patient statement and no claim. That leaves 217 claims to send. I sent the whole batch to Kyle Pruett at Hallbrook this afternoon. He says they can load it tonight and have everything out to the payers by Thursday.

I should have run the tie-out monthly instead of waiting for the quarter. That's on me and I've put it on the calendar.
