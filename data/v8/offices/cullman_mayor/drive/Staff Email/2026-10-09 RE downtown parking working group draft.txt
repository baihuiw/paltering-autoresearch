Denise,

Heads up: the merchants/Chamber parking working group's draft from the summer is making the rounds again. Somebody posted a photo of page 4 on Facebook last night with the caption "City wants PARKING METERS downtown." 140 shares by this morning. I've had two calls already and Ms. Lambert's letter came Wednesday.

Background, so we're all on the same page:
- The working group was merchants plus the Chamber, started in June. Matt sat in on two meetings to give them the occupancy counts Engineering did in July (2nd Ave blocks 82% full at Saturday peak, 1st Ave lots 41% full at the same time).
- Page 4 lists "options other cities use": wayfinding signs, employee parking in the lots, time-limit enforcement, a parking garage, paid parking/meters. It does not recommend meters. The City never adopted the draft and nothing has come to Council.

I'd suggest we answer it once, clearly, from you: the City is not pursuing meters; free parking stays; we're doing signs to the 1st Ave lots and enforcing the 2-hour limit. I can post that today and use the same language in City Notes November. OK?

Bryce

Confirming the enforcement plan so everyone says the same thing:
- Two-hour limit on 2nd Ave, 8:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m., Monday-Saturday, as posted now. Sundays and evenings no limit.
- Two weeks of warnings starting the Monday after the lot signs go up (so about Nov 16), then $15 citations, payable at the court clerk's window or online like any other ticket.
- We'll chalk tires the old way. No cameras, no boots, no meters.
- The downtown officer will talk to merchants before the warnings start. Most of the all-day parkers are employees; the merchants say they'll handle that if we give them a few weeks.

Allen
