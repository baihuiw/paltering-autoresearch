Yes on both.

Mayor and Council: I'll put them in unit 3, right behind the color guard and the high school band, in the white convertible the car club loans us every year (they offered again). If all five Council members come, I'll add the second convertible as unit 4. Please get me a yes/no from each Council member by Nov 20 so I can tell the car club.

Dance groups: one routine of 60 seconds or less at the judges' stand, keep moving everywhere else. The stand is where the announcer is, so that's where they'll want to perform anyway. Last year one studio stopped three times and we had a 10-minute gap behind them. I put it in the rules this year in bold.

Other things people are asking that you may get:
- Can we throw candy? No, handed out by walkers only.
- Can we bring a golf cart? Yes, if it's decorated and the driver is 16 or older with a license. Counts as a vehicle.
- Can our horses be in the middle instead of the end? Horses are grouped near the end, before Santa, so the bands and walkers aren't behind them.
- Can a candidate ride? No campaign entries, no candidate signs. A sitting official may ride with a group but not with campaign signs.

Jill
