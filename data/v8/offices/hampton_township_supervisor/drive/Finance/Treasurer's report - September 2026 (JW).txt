3. SUMMER 2026 TAX COLLECTION

Summer taxes were due September 14 without penalty. As of September 30:
- Summer levy (all units): $9,846,207.33
- Collected: $8,965,380.11 (91.1%)
- Uncollected: $880,827.22. Most of the balance is escrowed parcels whose mortgage companies pay in the last week and a small number of commercial parcels.

Summer taxes not paid by September 14 carry 1% interest per month, and are added to the winter bill if not paid by then.

4. WINTER 2026 TAXES

The township's L-4029 millage request was approved by the board on October 5 (Res. 2026-32) and sent to the county. The winter tax roll will be printed in late November and bills mailed by December 1. Winter taxes are due February 14, 2027 without penalty. Escrow companies that have requested bill data will receive it electronically the first week of December; homeowners get a copy of the bill marked "for your records."
