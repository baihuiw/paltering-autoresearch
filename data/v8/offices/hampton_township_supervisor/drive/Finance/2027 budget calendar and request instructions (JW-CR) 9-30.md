# 2027 budget: calendar and instructions for department requests

To: Department heads and elected officials
From: Jean Wiltse, Treasurer, and Carla Rheaume, Deputy Supervisor
Date: September 30, 2026
cc: Supervisor Seidlitz; Deb Szostak

The township's fiscal year is the calendar year. The board must adopt the 2027 budget and the general appropriations act before January 1, after a public hearing. With the change in supervisors this fall the schedule is tighter than usual, so please stick to these dates.

## Calendar

| Date | Step |
|---|---|
| Wed Sept 30 | Request forms and 2026 year-to-date actuals (through 9/30) sent to departments |
| **Fri Oct 9** | **Department requests due to Deb** (electronic spreadsheet + one-page narrative) |
| Wed Oct 14 | Treasurer's draft General Fund revenue estimate and first draft of expenditures to the Supervisor |
| Fri Oct 16 | Supervisor meets with department heads on requests (boardroom, 9 a.m.) |
| Fri Oct 23 | Supervisor's recommended budget to the Clerk for the board packet |
| Thu Oct 22 | Hearing notice to the newspaper (published at least one week before the hearing) |
| Mon Oct 26 | Board budget workshop, 1:00 p.m., boardroom (open to the public) |
| Mon Nov 2 | Public hearing on the proposed 2027 budget and property tax millage, 7:00 p.m.; adoption the same night if the board is ready, otherwise at a special meeting before December 1 |
| Dec 2026 | Amendments to the 2026 budget for year-end, as needed |

## What to send

1. **Spreadsheet**: every line in your department, with the 2025 actual, 2026 amended budget, 2026 actual through September 30, your 2026 year-end estimate, and your 2027 request. Use the template Deb sent; don't change the account numbers.
2. **Narrative** (one page): what changes from 2026 and why. If you're asking for a new position, a raise outside the wage schedule, or a purchase over $5,000, explain it here with quotes if you have them.
3. **Capital requests** (vehicles, equipment, building work over $5,000) on the separate capital sheet for Fund 245. Include the age and condition of whatever it replaces.

## Assumptions to use

- Wages: 3.0% on the wage schedule for non-union staff, pending the board's decision. Contract assessor: per agreement (2027 is the second year, $120,400).
- Health insurance: plan renewal not in yet. Use a 9% increase; we'll true up when the quote comes in November.
- Retirement: MERS employer contribution per the actuarial letter Deb has.
- Fuel: $3.40/gallon gasoline, $3.90 diesel.
- Utilities: 4% increase.
- Revenue: Jean will estimate taxes, state revenue sharing and interest. Departments estimate their own fees (permits, rentals, reports).

## Funds in the 2027 budget

| Fund | What it pays for | Main revenue |
|---|---|---|
| 101 General Fund | Board, Supervisor, Clerk, Treasurer, assessing, elections, building and zoning, DPW, parks, public safety, township hall | Township operating millage, state revenue sharing, permits and fees, interest |
| 226 Sanitation special assessment | Collection contract payments | Special assessment on the winter bill |
| 245 Capital improvement | Vehicles, equipment, building projects | Transfers from the General Fund, interest |
| 590 Sewer / 591 Water | Operations by Bay County DWS under the agreement; meter replacements; system repairs | User charges billed by BCDWS |
| 703 Tax collection | Pass-through of taxes collected for other units | Taxes collected |

Departments only prepare requests for the General Fund lines they manage and their capital requests. The water and sewer budgets are prepared by Jean with BCDWS's numbers, which they send by October 15.

## General direction from the Supervisor

Hold 2027 requests at or below 2026 amended levels unless there is a specific reason. The General Fund is in balance for 2026 but with very little room, and interest income will be lower next year. If something has to grow, show where it can come from.
