# Contract assessing agreement: summary

Prepared by the Treasurer's office for the Supervisor's orientation binder, September 2026. The signed agreement is in the Clerk's contract file (approved by the Township Board, December 2025). FOIA F-26-074 released a copy on September 24.

## Parties and term

- Charter Township of Hampton and Strasz Assessing Services (Renee Strasz, MAAO, Michigan Advanced Assessing Officer).
- Term: January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2028. Either party may end it with 120 days' written notice, but not between January 1 and the close of the March Board of Review.
- Renewal: by board action; the board must approve any new agreement before the 2029 roll work starts.

## Who is responsible

The Supervisor is the township's assessing officer under state law. Because the Supervisor is not a certified assessor, the township contracts with a certified assessor of the level required by the State Tax Commission for a township of our size and class mix. Renee signs the assessment roll as the certified assessor; the Supervisor works with her and attends the Boards of Review.

## Services included

1. Prepare the annual assessment roll for real and personal property, including the sales studies, land value maps, economic condition factors and the annual 20% field review.
2. Process principal residence exemptions, property transfer affidavits, uncapping, land divisions and combinations (after Building and Zoning approval), and new construction from building permits.
3. Prepare and mail assessment change notices in February.
4. Staff the March, July and December Boards of Review, prepare their records, and send changes to the county and the Treasurer.
5. Represent the township in small claims cases at the Michigan Tax Tribunal. Full tribunal cases are handled with the township attorney and are billed separately at the hourly rate below.
6. Office hours at the township hall on **Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9 a.m.-3 p.m.** (more days in February and March as needed, no extra charge).
7. Answer resident questions by phone and email within two business days.
8. Prepare the poverty exemption guidelines resolution and the annual State Tax Commission filings.
9. Keep the property records in the township's assessing software, which the township owns and licenses.

## Not included

- Appraisals for township purchases or sales of land.
- Full Tax Tribunal appeals (hourly).
- Special assessment district rolls (the Supervisor's office and the township attorney prepare these; the assessor provides parcel data at no charge).

Paid monthly by check on the first check run of the month, account 101-209.

## Performance and audit

- The State Tax Commission audits the township's assessing (the "AMAR" review) on a five-year cycle. The agreement requires the assessor to correct any findings at her own cost if they come from her work.
- The assessor carries professional liability insurance ($1 million) and names the township as additional insured. Certificate on file with the Clerk; renewed each January.
- The assessor reports monthly to the Supervisor (monthly report in the board packet, information only).

## Things to know

- Renee also has contracts with two other townships in the county. Her Hampton days are fixed (Tue/Thu); urgent questions on other days go to her by email.
- The 2027 roll work runs from October through February: field review in the fall, sales study and land values in December and January, notices in February, March Board of Review.
