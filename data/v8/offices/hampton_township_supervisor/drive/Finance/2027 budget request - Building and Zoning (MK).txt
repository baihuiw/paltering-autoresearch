NARRATIVE

The 2027 request is $220,130, up $7,530 (3.5%) from the 2026 amended budget. Almost all of the change is the 3% wage step and the health insurance assumption in the budget memo. Non-wage lines are flat or down.

Contract inspectors. Building, electrical, mechanical and plumbing inspections are done by state-registered contract inspectors paid per inspection; we pass the cost through in permit fees. 2026 is running above budget because of the spring storm roof season (61 roof permits through August against 40 in a normal year), but fee revenue ran ahead by about the same amount. I'm requesting $54,000 for 2027 assuming a normal year.

Planner. The township's planning consultant is helping the Planning Commission with the master plan update (resident survey this fall, draft plan in 2027). The $14,000 assumes four attended meetings and the draft chapters. The master plan has to be reviewed at least every five years under the Planning Enabling Act, and ours is due.

Rental program. 212 registered rental units as of October 1 (up from 198 a year ago). Registrations are on a three-year cycle at $60 per unit; about one-third are due each year. I do the rental inspector visits myself. No new cost.

Blight. 47 blight cases opened January through September; 41 closed, most after the first letter. Six went to a second letter and two to a civil infraction ticket. One township cleanup (Scheurmann Rd, June) was billed to the owner and added to the winter tax roll ($1,140). I'd like to keep the $5,000 legal line because the next ticket that gets contested will use it.

Permit software. Small increase per the vendor's renewal letter (7%).

What I'm not asking for: a second vehicle or a part-time code officer. I'd like to talk about the code officer for 2028 if the rental count keeps climbing.
