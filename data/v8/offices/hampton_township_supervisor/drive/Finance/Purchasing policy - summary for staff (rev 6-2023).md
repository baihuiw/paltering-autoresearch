# Purchasing policy: summary for staff

Charter Township of Hampton. Summary of the purchasing policy adopted by the Township Board (most recently amended June 2023). Prepared by the Treasurer's office for department heads and new staff. The adopted policy controls; copies are in the Clerk's office.

## Who can approve what

| Amount (per purchase, not per invoice) | Approval | Quotes |
|---|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | Department head | None required; use judgment |
| $1,000 to $2,500 | Department head, with a purchase order signed by the Treasurer before ordering | One written quote or catalog price |
| $2,500.01 to $5,000 | Supervisor | Three written quotes, attached to the PO |
| $5,000.01 to $25,000 | Township Board | Three written quotes; recommendation memo in the board packet |
| Over $25,000 | Township Board | Sealed bids, advertised at least 10 days; bid opening by the Clerk |

Splitting one purchase into smaller pieces to stay under a limit is not allowed.

## Purchase orders

- Get a PO number from Deb (ext. 214) before ordering anything over $1,000. Give her the vendor, amount, account number and a short description.
- The Treasurer signs POs. Anything over $1,000 that arrives without a PO goes back to the department head for an explanation before it is paid. The Treasurer may ask the Supervisor to approve it after the fact.
- POs are not needed for utilities, payroll and benefits, debt payments, or payments under an approved contract (for example the Road Commission agreements or the contract assessor). The contract itself is the authority; invoices are checked against it.

## Exceptions

- **Emergencies.** When there is an immediate threat to people or property (a water heater failure in the township hall, storm damage, a vehicle breakdown during snow season), the department head may order what is needed and notify the Supervisor the same day. Purchases over $5,000 are reported to the board at the next meeting.
- **Sole source.** When only one vendor can supply the item (a replacement part from the manufacturer, software renewals), note "sole source" and why on the PO. Over $5,000 still goes to the board.
- **Cooperative purchasing.** State of Michigan contracts (MiDEAL) and other public cooperative contracts count as competitive bidding.
- **Professional services** (attorney, auditor, engineer, planner) are selected by the board by qualifications, not low bid.

## Paying invoices

- Invoices go to Deb with the PO number and the department head's initials showing the goods or work were received.
- Checks run every other Friday (October 2, October 16 and October 30 this fall). Invoices received by Tuesday noon make that Friday's run.
- The board approves the bills list at each regular meeting (consent agenda). Checks are signed by two of the Supervisor, Clerk and Treasurer.
- Account coding: use the Michigan uniform chart of accounts numbers (101-xxx-xxx). If you're not sure which line, ask Deb before you code it, not after.

## Credit cards

- Township cards are issued to the Supervisor, Clerk, Treasurer, DPW Superintendent and Director of Public Safety, $2,500 limit each.
- Itemized receipts within 5 days. No personal purchases, ever, even if repaid.
- Monthly statements are reviewed by the Treasurer and available by FOIA.

## Things that come up

- **Gift cards** are not purchased with township money except for the board-approved employee recognition program.
- **Food** for meetings is allowed for board workshops, volunteer events and day-long trainings. Keep receipts with a list of who attended.
- **Local vendors**: the policy allows the township to choose a local Bay County vendor whose quote is within 5% of the lowest quote, if the department head notes it on the PO.
- **Conflicts of interest**: an employee or official who has a financial interest in a vendor must disclose it in writing and stay out of the decision.
- **Surplus property** (old vehicles, equipment) is sold by sealed bid or online auction with board approval if the value is over $1,000.
