## How residents can pay

1. **In person** at the Treasurer's window in the township hall, Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Cash, check or money order. Exact change helps. Receipts are given at the window.
2. **Drop box** on the east side of the building, any time. Put the bill stub with the check. Receipts are mailed only if the payer includes a stamped envelope.
3. **By mail** to the Treasurer at 801 W. Center Rd., Essexville, MI 48732. We go by the postmark only for payments mailed on or before the due date and received within the following week; to be safe, mail early.
4. **Online** by card or e-check through the payment link on the township website. The card processor charges a convenience fee; the township doesn't receive it.

We do not take card payments at the window.

## Escrow and refinance questions

Most mortgaged homes pay through escrow. The mortgage company pays the township directly.

- If someone has refinanced or changed lenders this year, the **new lender** should request the bill. Lenders request tax data through their tax service; we send it to any servicer that asks. A homeowner can also bring or mail their copy of the bill to the new lender.
- We mail the bill to the owner of record either way, marked "for your records" if a servicer has requested it. That copy is not a second bill.
- If both the homeowner and the bank pay, we refund the second payment to whoever paid second, usually within two weeks.
- Owners can check whether a payment has posted by calling the Treasurer's office or on the online tax lookup after the first week of December.

## What's on the winter bill

County operating, township operating (charter township millage), public safety and road millages, the library and special assessments certified by the board. Questions about **the amount of a special assessment** go to the Supervisor's office; questions about **assessed or taxable value** go to Assessing (Tuesdays and Thursdays). We can explain which line is which.

## Deferments

Seniors and certain others with household income under the state limit can defer summer taxes, not winter. If someone asks about winter hardship, give them the poverty exemption application from Assessing and the Board of Review dates.
