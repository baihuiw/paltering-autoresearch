Dear Mrs. Kroetsch,

Thank you for your call to the office on October 12, and I'm sorry it took a couple of days to get back to you. I checked with our Treasurer, Jean Wiltse, who collects the taxes, and here is what she told me.

**When the winter bills come.** Winter tax bills will be mailed by December 1. They are due by February 14, 2027. Because February 14 falls on a Sunday and the township office is closed Monday, February 15, for Presidents Day, the Treasurer will accept payments without penalty through Tuesday, February 16. After that a 3% penalty is added through the end of February, and on March 1 unpaid taxes go to the Bay County Treasurer as delinquent.

**Your new mortgage company.** Since you refinanced this summer, your new lender's tax service should request your bill from the township. The Treasurer's office sends bill information to any mortgage servicer that asks, usually the first week of December. You will still get a copy of the bill in the mail, marked "for your records." If you want to be certain, call the Treasurer's office after December 7 and they can tell you whether your new lender has requested the bill, or you can send your copy to the lender yourself. If both you and the bank pay, the township refunds the second payment.

**Paying in person.** If you pay the taxes yourself, you are always welcome to pay at the Treasurer's window in the township hall, Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. The Treasurer will also have extended hours December 28-31 for people who want to pay before the end of the year. There is a drop box on the east side of the building, and you can pay by mail or online.

The Treasurer's office can be reached at ext. 211.
