Dear Mr. and Mrs. Rogalski,

Thank you for your card about the brush chipping crew. I read it at our Monday department heads meeting and then took it out to the DPW garage so Jeff Bousson and Nate Zelt could see it themselves. Rick Haske tells me it is now taped to the break room refrigerator, which I understand is the highest honor the DPW gives.

You wrote that after the windstorm in July the crew came back for a second load from your yard without being asked, and that Nate helped you drag the last of the limbs to the road. That's the kind of thing that never shows up on a work order, and I'm glad you took the time to tell us.

You also asked about this fall's leaf pickup. It begins Monday, October 26, and runs weekly through Friday, November 20, weather permitting. Avondale is on the west side of Ledyard Road, so the leaf truck usually comes through early in the week, Monday or Tuesday. Please rake your leaves loose to the edge of your yard next to the road by Monday morning. Don't bag them; the vacuum truck can't take bags. And keep the piles out of the street and away from the storm drain at the end of your block, which Rick says tends to plug up every fall.

The last brush chipping of the year will be November 3 and 4. After that, chipping stops until April.

Thank you again for your kind words. Hearing from residents when things go right means a great deal to the people who do the work.

Sincerely,

Tom Seidlitz
Supervisor, Hampton Charter Township
