Dear Mr. Nowicki,

Thank you for your call to the office on September 30 and for your patience. I know you have been asking about the ditch in front of your home for some time, and I'm sorry it has taken as long as it has.

I checked with our DPW Superintendent, Rick Haske. Ridge Road from Pine to Farley is on the township's 2026 roadside ditching list. The crew had it scheduled for September 28, but it was too wet to work that day, and it has been moved into October. Brush chipping takes the crew the first Tuesday and Wednesday of each month, so the ditching is planned for the week after. Rick or his foreman will knock on your door before they start so you can move anything near the road.

As you may know, the ditches along Ridge Road are in the county road right-of-way. The township pays for our own crew to clean them because we think it is worth doing, and we work through the list in order each season. Our crew cleaned more than four miles of ditch this year on Ledyard, Heavenridge and Fillmore Place, and we are adding roads to next year's list now.

If water is standing in your yard before the crew gets there, please call Pat Gwizdala at the office and we will have Rick take a look.

Sincerely,

Tom Seidlitz
