Dear Mrs. Domke,

Thank you for calling the office on September 15 about the pothole on Pine near your mailbox. Pat Gwizdala passed along your message and the photo your grandson emailed.

All of the roads in Hampton Township, including Pine, are county roads. The Bay County Road Commission maintains them: patching, plowing, signs, shoulders and trees in the road right-of-way. The township doesn't have its own road crew or patching equipment. Each year the township board does put money toward road projects that the Road Commission carries out, such as chip seal and resurfacing, under agreements with the Road Commission, but day-to-day repairs like potholes are theirs.

We reported the pothole to the Road Commission on September 16 with the location and your photo. They told us their patch truck works through reports by area and that Pine should be reached within two weeks, sooner if it is a hazard. If it is still there after that, or it gets worse, please call us again and we will follow up. You can also report road problems directly to the Road Commission by phone or on its website; residents tell us that is often faster.

If a pothole damages your car, the Road Commission has a claim process. Keep your repair receipts and a photo of the hole.

Thank you for taking the time to let us know. Residents' calls are how most of these get found.

Sincerely,

Carla Rheaume
