Dear Mr. Zawacki,

Thank you for your letter. Elections in the township are run by our Clerk, Andrea Pohlod, so I asked her office to make sure I gave you the right answers. I've sent her a copy of this letter.

**Returning your absentee ballot.** You don't have to mail it. There is a secure drop box on the east side of the township hall, next to the flagpole, at 801 W. Center Rd. It is open 24 hours a day, is watched by camera, and is emptied every weekday by two election workers together. On Election Day it closes at 8:00 p.m. sharp. You can also hand your ballot to the Clerk's office at the counter during office hours, Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., or on Saturday, October 31, from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.

If you do mail it, the Clerk recommends mailing by Friday, October 30. A ballot must be **received** by 8:00 p.m. on Election Day to be counted; a postmark doesn't count.

Please remember to sign the back of the return envelope. That signature is how the Clerk's office confirms the ballot is yours.

You can check that your ballot has been received on the state's voter information website, or call the Clerk's office at ext. 206.

**Election Day.** The polls are open from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3. Anyone in line at 8:00 p.m. will be allowed to vote. You asked about your precinct: Heavenridge Road votes in Precinct 3, at the church fellowship hall on Ledyard Road. Your voter ID card shows it too.

You also mentioned you weren't sure your absentee ballot for the August primary was counted. The Clerk's records show it was received on July 29 and counted on August 4. If you ever want to check, the state's voter information website keeps a history.

Thank you for voting.

Sincerely,
