Dear Mr. and Mrs. Trombley,

Thank you for stopping in on September 30. I'm sorry you had to make two trips, and I appreciate your patience while Matt Krawczak, our Building and Zoning Administrator, looked into your questions. Here are the answers in writing, as I promised.

**The prebuilt shed (8 x 10).** Under the state building code, a one-story detached accessory building of 200 sq. ft. or less does not need a building permit. That's true whether you build it yourself or have it delivered already built. Township zoning still applies, though, so every shed needs a **zoning permit** from the township before it goes in. The zoning permit is a one-page form with a simple sketch showing the shed and its distances from the lot lines and the house. The fee is $40, and Matt usually turns them around in two or three business days.

The zoning rules for a shed in your district (R-1):
- In the rear yard, or in the side yard behind the front line of the house. Not in the front yard.
- At least 5 ft. from the side and rear lot lines, measured to the closest part of the building (including roof overhang).
- At least 10 ft. from the house.
- No more than 14 ft. tall.
- Not on top of a recorded easement. Your survey or mortgage plot plan will show any easements; the utility easement along the rear of most Kulaw lots is 6 ft. wide, so in practice a shed there sits at least 6 ft. off the back line.

**The detached garage (24 x 30).** This one needs both a zoning permit and a building permit, plus an electrical permit if you run power to it. The same setbacks apply, with a 17-ft. height limit for detached garages. Matt will need two sets of drawings showing the foundation, framing and roof, and the building permit fee is based on the square footage. He can walk you through it.

Before anything is anchored or a footing is dug, please call 811 (MISS DIG) at least three business days ahead so the utilities can mark their lines. It's free.

Forms are at the front counter and on the website under Building and Zoning. If you'd like to go over your sketch first, Matt is in the office most mornings, or I'm at the township hall Tuesdays from 3 to 5 p.m. for resident office hours.
