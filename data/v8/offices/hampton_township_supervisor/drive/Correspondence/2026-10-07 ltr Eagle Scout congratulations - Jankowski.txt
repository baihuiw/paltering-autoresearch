Dear Nathan,

On behalf of the Township Board and the residents of Hampton Township, congratulations on earning the rank of Eagle Scout.

Your troop leader sent us a description of your Eagle project: the twelve wooden benches you designed, built and installed along the trail at Pondside Park, with the help of your troop and a lot of donated lumber. Our DPW Superintendent, Rick Haske, tells me they are well built and level, which he says is not something he can say about every bench he has inherited. Walkers have already noticed them; one called the office to ask who put them there.

Only a small share of Scouts reach Eagle. It takes years of steady work, and the project takes leadership: planning, raising the money, organizing volunteers and seeing it through. Those are the same skills that keep a community running, and I hope you will use them here for a long time.

I'm told your court of honor is on October 24. I have a conflict that afternoon, but Deputy Supervisor Carla Rheaume will attend on behalf of the township and present a certificate from the board.

Congratulations again, and thank you for what you have given the township.

Sincerely,

Tom Seidlitz
Supervisor, Hampton Charter Township
