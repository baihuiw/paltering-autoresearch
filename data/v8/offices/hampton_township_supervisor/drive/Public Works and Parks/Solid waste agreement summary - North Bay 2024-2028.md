# Residential solid waste collection: agreement summary

Charter Township of Hampton and North Bay Refuse, Inc. Agreement dated January 1, 2024; term January 1, 2024 - December 31, 2028. Staff summary for the front counter and board packets (CR, updated Jan 2026). The signed agreement is in the clerk's vault.

## Who is covered

- Every single-family home, duplex unit and mobile home in the township, and multi-family buildings of four units or fewer. Larger complexes contract on their own.
- 3,940 residential units on the 2026 roll (3,912 in 2025).
- Paid by a special assessment per residential unit on the winter (December) tax bill, collected by the treasurer into the Solid Waste (Trash Collection) Fund. The December assessment pays for collection in the following calendar year.

## Services

| Service | Frequency | Container |
|---|---|---|
| Trash | Weekly, on your zone's day | One 96-gal. gray cart (second cart available from North Bay for a fee billed directly) |
| Single-stream curbside recycling | Every other week, same day as trash | One 96-gal. blue cart |
| Yard waste | Weekly, April 1 - November 30 | Paper bags or marked can, up to 10 per week |
| Bulk item | One item per week with trash | At curb |

Collection zones: Monday (west of Ledyard), Tuesday (Center Rd corridor and subdivisions north), Wednesday (east of Ledyard and south of Center). Holiday weeks slide one day.

## Unit rate (per residential unit per month)

| Component | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trash and bulk | $11.40 | $11.75 | $12.10 |
| Recycling | $2.00 | $2.05 | $2.15 |
| Yard waste (seasonal, spread over 12 months) | $1.95 | $2.00 | $2.05 |
| Total per month | $15.35 | $15.80 | $16.30 |

The township adds a small amount for administration, cart replacement reserve and the household hazardous waste day. The annual assessment has been held at $214.00 per unit since the 2024 roll.

## Sections staff get asked about

- **5.2 Missed collection.** A cart that was out by 7 a.m. and missed is picked up within one business day of the call to North Bay. Repeated misses are reported to the township, which can assess a $50 per-incident charge.
- **6.1 Carts.** Carts belong to North Bay. Damaged carts are replaced free once per year.
- **7.4 Rate adjustment.** Annual adjustment each January 1, capped at 3 percent, except as provided in 9.3.
- **9.3 Recycling processor.** If the contractor's recycling processor stops accepting material and no comparable processor is available, the contractor may give the township written notice and the township chooses either to discontinue recycling collection with a monthly credit for the recycling component, or to continue at an adjusted rate for the added hauling and processing cost.
- **11.1 Term and renewal.** Five years; may be renewed for one three-year period by mutual agreement.
