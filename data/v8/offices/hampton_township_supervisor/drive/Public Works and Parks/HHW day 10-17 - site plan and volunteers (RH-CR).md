# Household hazardous waste day, Saturday October 17: site plan and volunteers

Rick Haske and Carla Rheaume. October 12, 2026. Final for Saturday.

## Basics

- 9:00 a.m.-1:00 p.m. Gates close at 1:00; anyone in line at 1:00 gets served.
- Township hall parking lot. Township residents only; proof of address (license or tax bill).
- Licensed hazardous waste contractor runs the collection, packing and shipping. Their crew: 6 people, 2 box trucks, arrive 7:00 a.m.
- Township cost: contractor's flat setup fee plus per-pound disposal. Budgeted $14,500 in 101-441 (2026). Last year's total was $13,280 for 312 vehicles.

## Traffic plan

- Enter from Center Road at the east drive. Cones run cars along the east side of the building, past the drop box and the flagpole, to the back lot.
- **Check-in station** at the northeast corner: volunteers check address and hand out the survey card (zip code and what they brought).
- **Unload stations** (3) in the back lot under the contractor's tents. Residents stay in their cars; the contractor's workers unload.
- **Rejected items** table: latex paint (we hand out paint hardener packets, 200 on hand), tires, electronics, medicines. A sign with where to take each.
- Exit by the west drive back onto Center Road. No left turn sign out.
- If the line backs up onto Center Road: two volunteers walk the line with check-in cards to speed things up; Public Safety will send a car to watch the turn lane.
