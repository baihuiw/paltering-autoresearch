# Pondside Park and Finn Road Park: fall closing and 2027 pavilion rentals

Kelly Fruchey, Recreation and Parks Coordinator. October 13, 2026. To: Rick Haske; cc Carla Rheaume (for the website and newsletter).

## Fall closing

**Finn Road Park** (boat launch, canoe/kayak launch, campground area)
- Restroom winterized October 8 (WO-26-149). Portable toilet stays through November 15 for hunters and late-season boaters.
- Boat launch stays open year-round, conditions permitting. Launch fee box removed for the season October 31; daily passes not required November 1-March 31.
- Courtesy dock: Rick wants it out by October 30 depending on water levels. If the bay drops early, earlier.
- Camping area closed for the season October 15. Last 2026 reservation was the weekend of October 9-11.

**Pondside Park** (ball diamonds, trail, pavilion, sledding hill)
- Dock sections pulled and restroom winterized October 15 (WO-26-154).
- Ball diamonds: fall league ended October 4. Diamonds dragged and closed for the season; bases stored.
- Trail stays open year-round. The township doesn't plow the trail; it's open for walking, snowshoeing and cross-country skiing when there is snow.
- Sledding hill: open when there is snow, daylight only. Hay bales at the bottom of the hill go in by December 1 (DPW).
- The twelve new benches along the trail (Eagle project, installed in September) are a hit. Congratulations letter went out from Tom on 10/7.
