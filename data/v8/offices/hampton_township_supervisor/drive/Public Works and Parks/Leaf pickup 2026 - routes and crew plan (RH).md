# Leaf pickup 2026: routes and crew plan

DPW, Charter Township of Hampton. Rick Haske, Superintendent. October 8, 2026. For the crew, the front counter and the Supervisor's office.

## Dates

- **Start: Monday, October 26.** Last pass: week of November 16 (ends Friday, November 20). Weather permitting; if we lose days to rain or an early snow we'll post it on Facebook and the website. We do not add a week after Thanksgiving.
- Brush chipping on November 3-4 (the last chipping of the season) takes the chipper crew those two days; the leaf truck keeps running with the seasonal laborers.

## How residents put leaves out

- Loose piles at the edge of the yard, next to the road (not in the road, not over a storm drain).
- **No bags.** The vacuum can't take them. Bagged leaves are left. Residents who bag can take them to a yard waste drop-off or compost them.
- Out by 7 a.m. Monday of the week. We go down every township road once a week.
- No branches, rocks, pumpkins, decorations or pet waste in the piles.

## Routes

The township is split at Ledyard Road.

| Days | Area | Approx. road miles |
|---|---|---|
| Mon-Tue | West of Ledyard: Woodside Ave area, Kulaw, Pine, Avondale, 2nd and 4th Streets, Center Rd frontage west | 31 |
| Wed-Thu | East of Ledyard: Heavenridge, Scheurmann, Finn, Ridge, Farley, Center Rd frontage east | 34 |
| Fri | Catch-up, call-ins logged by the front counter, township hall and park grounds | - |

Subdivision streets on the west side are done first each week because that's where most of the leaf volume is.

## Equipment

- Leaf vac #1 (trailer unit) with truck #3 and the 20-yd dump box. New hose installed 10/9 (WO-26-150).
- Leaf vac #2 (older) as backup; needs the impeller housing looked at before 10/26. Parts quote from the equipment dealer: $640. If it fails mid-season, we run one unit and add Saturdays.
- Front loader for piles that are too wet to vacuum (rarely; it tears up lawns).
- Leaves go to the compost area at the DPW yard. The farmer who takes the finished compost will haul in the spring as usual.

## Crew

- Two-person vac crew per truck: Jeff Bousson leads; Nate Zelt; two seasonal laborers (hired from the September posting; start October 19 for orientation and safety training).
- Andy Kuch covers buildings and the township hall, and runs supplies on Election Day (November 3).
- Overtime: Saturdays only if we fall a full day behind. Budget for leaf season overtime is in 101-446.

## Front counter

- Callers who ask "when are you coming to my street": tell them which half of the township they're in and the days above. We can't give a time.
- Piles the truck passed by or left: put it on the Friday catch-up list with the address. Ask if the pile had bags or branches in it.
- Leaves in the road or blocking a drain: log it; Rick will have the crew stop.

## Weather and delays

- Rain: the vacuum can't take soaked, matted piles. We skip the wettest streets and come back on Friday if they've dried.
- Snow: if snow covers the piles we stop and post it. Leaves under snow wait until it melts or until spring (residents may also mow them into their lawns).
- Equipment breakdown: one vac unit runs while the other is fixed; the schedule moves back a day and we post it.
- Election Day (November 3): leaf crew runs as normal. Brush chipping crew starts on the east side.

## Phone script for the counter

"Leaf pickup runs weekly from October 26 to November 20. Rake your leaves loose to the edge of your yard next to the road by Monday morning - no bags. West of Ledyard is usually Monday and Tuesday; east of Ledyard Wednesday and Thursday. If we pass your pile, call us and we'll put you on the Friday list." 

## Last year

2025: 2,430 cubic yards collected over five weeks; one week lost to rain; 63 call-ins. Most call-ins were bags (we leave them) or piles put out after the truck went by.
