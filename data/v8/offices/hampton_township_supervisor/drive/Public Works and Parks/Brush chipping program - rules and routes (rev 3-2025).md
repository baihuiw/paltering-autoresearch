# Brush chipping program: rules and routes

Department of Public Works, Charter Township of Hampton. Revised March 2025 (RH). Posted on the website and given out at the counter.

## Schedule

- **First Tuesday and Wednesday of each month, April through November.** Tuesday: west of Ledyard Road. Wednesday: east of Ledyard Road and Center Rd east.
- 2026 remaining dates: November 3-4 (last of the season). No chipping December through March; storm damage pickups are announced separately.
- When the first Tuesday is a holiday or Election Day falls on it, the crew still runs; on Election Day (November 3) the crew starts on the east side because the township hall area is busy with voters.

## How to put brush out

- Brush must be **at the road by 7:00 a.m.** on Tuesday. The crew passes each road once and doesn't come back for brush put out later.
- Stack it at the edge of the yard, **cut ends facing the road**, parallel to the road. Don't put it in the road or under power lines.
- **Branches 4 inches in diameter or smaller**, and no longer than 10 ft. Larger logs and stumps are the owner's responsibility.
- **No** root balls, stumps, lumber, fence pieces, vines tangled with wire, rose bushes with tags and wire, leaves, grass clippings or yard waste in bags.
- Up to a pile about 10 ft. long, 5 ft. wide and 4 ft. high per household per month. Larger loads from tree contractors are not picked up; the contractor must haul it.

## Why piles get skipped

- Out after 7 a.m. on Tuesday.
- Mixed with stumps, lumber, leaves or other material.
- Placed in the road, blocking a mailbox or behind a parked car.
- Branches too big for the chipper (over 4 inches).
- Contractor-generated brush.

When a pile is skipped, the crew leaves a door hanger saying why.

Chips go to the DPW yard and are available free to residents (load it yourself, Monday-Friday 7 a.m.-3 p.m., call ahead).

## Equipment and crew

- Two chippers (#1, 2019 drum chipper; #2, 2011 disc chipper, backup). Belts and knives checked the Friday before each route.
- Two-person crew: foreman plus one, with the chip truck. A third person flags on Center Rd and other busy roads.
- Safety: chaps, face shields and hearing protection on the chipper at all times; no one feeds the chipper alone.
