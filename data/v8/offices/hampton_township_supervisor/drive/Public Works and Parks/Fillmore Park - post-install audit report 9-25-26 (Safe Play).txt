POST-INSTALLATION PLAYGROUND SAFETY AUDIT

Owner: Charter Township of Hampton (attn: Kelly Fruchey, Recreation and Parks Coordinator)
Site: Fillmore Park, Fillmore Place, Hampton Township, Bay County
Equipment: Composite play structure (2-5 and 5-12 age groups), 8 ft. tower deck with two slides (straight and spiral), ramp-accessible upper deck, transfer station, suspension bridge; freestanding 2-bay swing (belt and ADA seat); spring riders (2)
Installer: Northern Play Systems (installation completed 09/18/2026)
Protective surfacing: engineered wood fiber (EWF) over drainage fabric; poured-in-place rubber accessible route and ramp landing
Date of audit: Friday, September 25, 2026, 9:00-11:40 a.m.
Inspector: Linda Oszust, Certified Playground Safety Inspector (CPSI)

SUMMARY
The equipment is installed plumb and secure, hardware is complete and tight, and the accessible route and ramp meet the plans. Two high-priority hazards were found. Do not open the play structure to the public until Items 1 and 2 are corrected and re-inspected. Items 3-5 may be corrected after opening.

FINDINGS

Item 1 - HIGH PRIORITY - Protective surfacing depth.
EWF depth measured at 14 points in the use zones of the 8 ft. tower deck, both slide exits and the transfer station. Depth ranged from 5 to 6.5 inches (average 5.6 in.). The surfacing manufacturer's specification for the fall height of this structure, and the plan sheet (L-2), call for 12 inches of EWF after compaction. At the measured depth the surfacing does not provide the impact attenuation required for the 8 ft. fall height. Recommendation: add EWF to bring all use zones to 12 inches compacted (estimated 55-60 cubic yards), then re-measure.

Item 2 - HIGH PRIORITY - Head entrapment.
The opening between the suspension bridge side panel and the tower post at the north end of the bridge measures approximately 5 inches at the narrowest point, within the range in which a child's head can pass through and become trapped. Recommendation: install the manufacturer's filler panel or replace the side panel so no opening falls within the entrapment range; re-inspect.

Item 3 - MEDIUM - Swing use zone.
EWF under the swings has been displaced by foot traffic to about 7 in. at the ADA seat. Rake and top up with the Item 1 material.

Item 4 - LOW - Signage.
Age-appropriate use signs for the 2-5 and 5-12 areas not yet installed (in the shipping crate). Install.

Item 5 - LOW - Maintenance.
Establish a written daily and monthly inspection routine; raking of EWF under slide exits and swings at least weekly during heavy use.

RE-INSPECTION
Call to schedule a re-inspection when Items 1 and 2 are complete. There is no charge for the first re-inspection.

Report prepared September 25, 2026. Photos (22) and measurement sheet sent separately by email.
