# Hampton Township DPW: roadside ditching and drainage

Department of Public Works, Charter Township of Hampton. Revised March 2025 (RH). Approved by the Township Board as part of the DPW service policy.

## Who owns what

- All public roads in the township are county roads. The **Bay County Road Commission** owns the road right-of-way, including the roadside ditches and the cross culverts under the road.
- The township has an agreement with the Road Commission that lets the township DPW clean roadside ditches on county roads at township expense, on a schedule the township sets. This is a service the township chooses to pay for; it is not a Road Commission program.
- Storm sewers and catch basins in the platted subdivisions are part of the township's storm system, maintained by the DPW and Bay County DWS under the township's stormwater permit.

## Roadside ditching program

- The DPW keeps a **ditching list**: roads where standing water, washouts or blocked flow have been reported or seen. The list is reviewed each spring by the DPW Superintendent.
- Roads are cleaned in list order, weather permitting, from May through November. Brush chipping (first Tuesday and Wednesday of each month) and leaf pickup take priority over ditching on those days.
- A typical season covers 3 to 5 miles of ditch. Spoils go to the DPW yard; disturbed banks are seeded and strawed.
- Residents can ask to be added to the list by calling the township office or using the website form. We will tell you where your road is on the list.

## Driveway culverts

- A driveway culvert is the pipe under a private driveway where it crosses the roadside ditch. It belongs to the property owner.
- Installing, replacing or extending a driveway culvert in the road right-of-way requires a **driveway permit from the Bay County Road Commission**. The Road Commission sets the pipe size and length.
- **The township DPW does not install, replace or pay for private driveway culverts.** Owners hire a contractor or do the work themselves under the Road Commission permit.
- When the DPW cleans a ditch, it will clear debris from the ends of existing driveway culverts at no charge. If a driveway culvert is crushed or too small and is backing up the ditch, we will tell the owner and the Road Commission.

## Who to call

- Ditching list, brush and leaf pickup: township office, (989) 893-7541.
- Driveway permits, road surface, potholes, snowplowing, signs on county roads: Bay County Road Commission.
- Sewer backups and storm drains in subdivisions: Bay County Department of Water and Sewer.
