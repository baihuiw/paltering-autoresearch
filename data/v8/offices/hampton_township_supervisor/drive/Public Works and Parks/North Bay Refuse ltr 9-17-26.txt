Re: Residential Solid Waste Collection Agreement dated January 1, 2024 (the "Agreement") - Notice under Section 9.3

Dear Supervisor:

We understand the township is between supervisors at the moment, so I am also sending a copy to Ms. Rheaume.

This letter is formal notice under Section 9.3 that North Bay Refuse will discontinue curbside recycling collection in Hampton Township effective December 31, 2026. The materials recovery facility in Saginaw County that processes our single-stream loads has told its haulers it will stop accepting material at the end of this year. We have looked for another processor within the distance the Agreement contemplates and have not found one that will take our volume at a cost anywhere near the current contract rate.

Weekly trash collection and seasonal yard waste collection are not affected and will continue under the Agreement through its term (December 31, 2028).

Section 9.3 gives the township two options. Please let us know which the township chooses by October 30, 2026:

  Option A - Discontinue. Curbside recycling collection ends with the last scheduled route on December 31, 2026. North Bay credits the township $2.15 per residential unit per month for 2027 (the recycling portion of the current unit rate). We will pick up the blue carts during January 2027, or residents may keep them.

  Option B - Haul to an alternate processor. We can continue every-other-week collection by hauling to a facility outside the region. Our estimate is an increase of $3.40 per residential unit per month over the current rate, beginning January 1, 2027, subject to that facility's acceptance terms.

Either way, we recommend the township tell residents in November so they are not surprised in January. We can provide a mailer insert or door hanger text at no charge.

Separately, and so the office is not caught off guard by calls: for the next several weeks some every-other-week routes may run late or be skipped while we move one of our recycling trucks to cover a commercial account. Any missed carts will be picked up on the next scheduled date. Trash routes are not affected.

I am happy to meet with the new supervisor once one is in place. My cell is (989) 553-0417.
