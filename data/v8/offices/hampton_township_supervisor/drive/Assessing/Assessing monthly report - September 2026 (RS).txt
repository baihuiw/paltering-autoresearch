5a. PRINCIPAL RESIDENCE EXEMPTION REVIEW
Each fall I compare the PRE list against the Secretary of State and voter records and against out-of-state addresses on the tax roll, as the state recommends. This year's review (finished 9/29):
- 3,412 parcels with a PRE on file.
- 27 flagged for a second look (owner's mailing address outside the township, or a homestead claimed elsewhere in Michigan).
- 19 cleared after a phone call or letter (snowbirds who keep their Michigan home as their principal residence; one military family).
- 6 letters sent asking the owner to confirm or rescind (reply due November 6).
- 2 already rescinded by the owners after they moved.
Where an owner claimed an exemption they weren't entitled to, the state rules allow the township to deny it for up to three prior years with interest. I don't expect any of these six to go that far.
