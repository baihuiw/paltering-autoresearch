# December 2026 Board of Review: notice text and staff notes

Assessing office, Charter Township of Hampton. Draft October 8, 2026 (RS) for the Clerk to publish in November and post at the hall and on the website. Dates confirmed with the board members on October 6.

## NOTICE: DECEMBER BOARD OF REVIEW

The Charter Township of Hampton Board of Review will meet on **Tuesday, December 8, 2026, at 9:00 a.m.**, in the township hall boardroom, 801 W. Center Rd., Essexville.

Under Michigan law, the December Board of Review may:
- correct clerical errors and mutual mistakes of fact on the 2026 assessment roll;
- hear appeals of the principal residence exemption (homestead) denials and late filings for the current year and up to three prior years;
- hear qualified agricultural property exemption matters;
- hear poverty exemption requests for the 2026 tax year; and
- hear disabled veterans exemption requests for the 2026 tax year.

**The December Board of Review cannot hear appeals of property values.** Value appeals for 2027 are heard by the March Board of Review (dates will be published in February).

Appointments are encouraged. Call the Assessing office at (989) 893-7541 ext. 220 on Tuesdays or Thursdays, or email assessing@hamptonmi.net. Written petitions received by December 7 will be considered without an appearance.

Tentative ratios and multipliers for 2027 will be posted in February.

Americans with Disabilities Act: the township will provide reasonable auxiliary aids to people with disabilities with seven days' notice. Contact the Clerk's office.

Andrea Pohlod, Clerk
