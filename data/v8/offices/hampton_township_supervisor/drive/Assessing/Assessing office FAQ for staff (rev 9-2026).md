# Assessing office: answers to common questions

Charter Township of Hampton, Assessing office. For front counter staff. Contract assessor Renee Strasz, MAAO, is in the office Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9 a.m.-3 p.m. Revised September 2026 (RS).

## Who does what

- **Assessing** sets each property's assessed value and taxable value, administers exemptions and keeps the property records and maps. The Supervisor is the township's chief assessing officer; the contract assessor does the work day to day.
- **The Treasurer** sends the tax bills and collects the taxes (summer bills in July, winter bills in December).
- **The county** handles equalization and delinquent taxes after March 1.

## Common questions

**"Why did my taxable value go up when my house didn't change?"** Taxable value can rise each year by the inflation rate set by the state, or 5 percent, whichever is less, until the property sells. After a sale, taxable value "uncaps" to the assessed value the next year. Refer detailed questions to the assessor.

**Principal residence exemption (homestead).** Owners who live in the home as their principal residence should have a Principal Residence Exemption affidavit on file; it exempts the home from part of the school operating millage. New owners file it with the Assessing office. If a resident thinks theirs is missing, take the parcel number and have the assessor call back.

**Disabled veterans exemption.** Applications and the VA letter go to the Assessing office. Renewals each year.

**Poverty exemption.** The township board adopts income and asset guidelines each year. Applications are available at the counter and are heard by the Board of Review. Applications are confidential; give them only to the assessor.

**Business personal property.** Small businesses that qualify can claim an exemption; the assessor has the forms.

## Board of Review

- **March Board of Review** hears appeals of assessed and taxable values for the current year.
- **July and December Board of Review** sessions can correct clerical errors and mutual mistakes of fact and hear certain exemption matters (such as principal residence, poverty and veterans exemptions). They cannot hear ordinary value appeals.
- The December 2026 session dates will be posted on the website and at the hall in November.

## Land divisions and parcel splits

Requests go to Building and Zoning first, then to Assessing for new parcel numbers on the next year's roll. Parcel maps are updated in our mapping system after approval.

## What not to give out

Applications for poverty exemptions and anything with a Social Security number. Ask the assessor if unsure. Property record cards and sales are public and can be printed for anyone.
