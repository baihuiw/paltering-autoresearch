# Phones and callbacks: counter standard

Supervisor's office, Charter Township of Hampton. Pat Gwizdala, Office Coordinator. Revised September 2026 for the front counter and for anyone covering the phones. Replaces the one-page sheet in the desk binder.

## How the main line works

- The main number, (989) 893-7541, rings at Pat's desk (ext. 200) from 9:00 to 3:00, Monday through Friday. If Pat is on another call or away from the desk, it rings six times and rolls to the Supervisor's office overflow box.
- Callers who press 0 during business hours go to the front counter. After 3:00 p.m. and on weekends the recording gives the office hours and the Public Safety non-emergency line.
- Each department has its own extension and its own box; see the staff directory.

## Callbacks

- **Messages left on the overflow box are returned the same or next business day.** Pat checks the box when she gets back to her desk and again at 8:45 each morning.
- When Pat is out (jury duty, vacation, sick), Carla covers the counter and the box.
- A caller who has to leave a message should leave a name, a number and one sentence about what they need. Pat writes down the details in the resident requests log, gives it an RR number and sends it to whoever handles it.
- If Pat takes the call live, she writes down the request, gives the caller an estimate of when someone will call back, and puts it in the log the same day.
- Questions that belong to another office (the Road Commission, Bay County Water and Sewer, Consumers Energy, the Clerk) get the right number. Pat will report a pothole or a streetlight for a caller who asks.

## Things not to do

- Don't promise a date for a permit, a ditch, or a decision by the Supervisor or the board.
- Don't give out anyone's cell number.
- Anything from a lawyer, a reporter or anything threatening goes to Carla before anyone calls back.

## Busy times

The weeks before a tax due date, absentee ballot mailing and Election Day bring the most calls. In September the main line and counter logged 527 contacts for the Supervisor's office (see the September tally). During those weeks Pat will shorten voicemail greetings and make two callback passes a day.

## Closings

Holiday closings are on the posted schedule. A snow-day recording is changed by Carla.
