# Township office hours and holiday closings, 2026-2027

Charter Township of Hampton, 801 W. Center Rd., Essexville. Posted at the front door and on the website, October 2026. Approved holiday list per the personnel policy (Township Board, rev. January 2024).

## Regular hours

- **Township offices** (Supervisor, Clerk, Treasurer, Building and Zoning): Monday-Friday, 9:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m.
- **Assessing**: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 9:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m. Other days, leave a message at ext. 220.
- **Supervisor's resident office hours**: Tuesdays, 3:00-5:00 p.m. (beginning October 13, 2026). Walk-in. Appointments at other times through the Supervisor's office, ext. 200.
- **Public Safety** lobby: Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-4:00 p.m. Officers on patrol 24 hours; call 911 in an emergency.
- **Drop box** (tax payments, permit applications, forms): east side of the building, checked each business day at 9 a.m.

The front counter closes for lunch only when staffing is short; a sign will be posted.

When the township board's regular first-Monday meeting falls on a holiday, it moves to the next day (Tuesday) at 7 p.m.

DPW: brush chipping and leaf pickup days that fall on a holiday shift to the next working day. Brush chipping ends for the season after the November route (Tuesday-Wednesday, November 3-4).

## Weather closings

- The township offices close for weather only when the Supervisor decides that travel is unsafe for staff and residents, usually when Bay County government offices close. A decision is made by 7:00 a.m. and posted on the township Facebook page and website, and a message is put on the main line.
- When the offices close, payments may still be left in the drop box. Any deadline that falls on a closed day (for example a permit or tax deadline) moves to the next business day.
- Board and commission meetings cancelled for weather are rescheduled with new public notice.
- Public Safety and DPW snow operations continue regardless of office closings. Roads are plowed by the Bay County Road Commission, not the township; the DPW plows township parking lots and the township hall sidewalks.

## Elections and tax deadlines

The Clerk's office and the Treasurer's office set their own extra hours around elections and tax due dates; those are posted separately and announced on Facebook. When in doubt, call the main line.
