# Personnel policy excerpt: work hours, leave and holidays

Charter Township of Hampton. Excerpt of Sections 4-7 of the personnel policy adopted by the Township Board (rev. January 2024), for posting in the staff break room. Applies to non-union, full-time and regular part-time employees. Elected officials are not covered by these sections. Public Safety officers are covered by their own agreement.

## Section 4. Work hours

4.1 Township offices are open to the public Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m., except holidays. Office staff normally work 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. with a one-hour unpaid lunch, or another schedule approved by the Supervisor, so that the front counter is staffed during public hours.

4.2 DPW employees normally work 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., Monday through Friday. During snow events and leaf season the Superintendent may change hours and assign overtime.

4.3 Flexible schedules. With the department head's approval, an employee may flex start and end times within the same day (for example, to cover an evening meeting or extended office hours) without overtime, as long as the employee works no more than 40 hours in the week.

4.4 Overtime. Non-exempt employees are paid time and one-half for hours worked over 40 in a work week. Overtime must be approved in advance except in emergencies. Comp time may be taken instead of overtime pay if the employee and department head agree, up to 40 hours banked.

4.5 Evening meetings. Staff who attend board, Planning Commission or ZBA meetings as part of their job are paid for the time or flex it the same week.

## Section 5. Holidays

5.1 Paid holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Good Friday (half day, afternoon), Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day and the day after, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

5.2 A holiday falling on Saturday is observed on Friday; on Sunday, on Monday.

5.3 Employees required to work on a holiday (DPW snow events, election workers) receive holiday pay plus time and one-half for hours worked.

## Section 6. Paid time off

6.1 Vacation accrues by years of service:

| Years of service | Vacation days per year |
|---|---|
| 0-4 | 10 |
| 5-9 | 15 |
| 10-19 | 20 |
| 20 or more | 25 |

6.2 Vacation requests go to the department head at least two weeks ahead. The Clerk's and Treasurer's offices limit vacation in the weeks around elections and tax due dates (see each office's posted blackout dates).

6.3 Up to 10 unused vacation days may be carried into the next year.

6.4 Sick leave: 12 days per year, accumulating to 90. May be used for the employee's or an immediate family member's illness or medical appointments.

6.5 Personal days: 2 per year, not carried over.

## Section 7. Other leave

7.1 Jury duty. Employees called for jury duty receive regular pay for the days served and turn in the juror fee (mileage may be kept). Notify the department head as soon as the summons arrives so coverage can be arranged.

7.2 Bereavement. Up to 3 days for an immediate family member; 1 day for other relatives.

7.3 Military leave per federal and state law.

7.4 Election day. Employees who are registered voters will be given time to vote if their work schedule doesn't allow it.

7.5 Family and medical leave: the township follows the federal Family and Medical Leave Act where it applies and treats eligible employees the same way where it does not.

Questions: Deputy Supervisor (ext. 202) or Bookkeeper (ext. 214).
