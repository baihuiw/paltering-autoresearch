# Website contact form: who answers what, and how fast

Supervisor's office, Charter Township of Hampton. Carla Rheaume, revised September 28, 2026 for the new Supervisor. Applies to messages that come in through the "Contact the Supervisor" form on hamptontownshipmi.gov, which land in the hamptonsupervisor@hamptonmi.net inbox (Tom, Carla and Pat all see it).

## Reply standards

- **Acknowledge within one business day.** Even if we don't have the answer yet, tell the person who has it and when they'll hear back.
- **Answer within five business days** for most questions. If it will take longer (a site visit, a records search), say so in the first reply.
- **Use plain language.** Short paragraphs. Give the phone extension or email of the person who can help.
- **Don't guess.** If the answer depends on a department (taxes, zoning, elections), check with them or forward it and copy the resident.
- **Opinions** ("I'd like the Supervisor to support...") get a reply from the Supervisor or Carla that thanks them, says where the issue stands and whether it's coming before the board. We don't promise a vote.
- **Invitations** go to Pat for the calendar. Tom decides; Pat replies.
- **Anything from a lawyer, a reporter, or anything threatening** goes to Carla before anyone replies.
- Log every message in the resident requests log (RR number), even thank-you notes.

## Standard wording we reuse

**Water bills:** "The township still owns the water and sewer system, but since January 2024 the Bay County Department of Water and Sewer runs it and handles all billing. Please call BCDWS customer service at the number on your current bill. If your bill is unusually high, ask them for a re-read and a leak check."

**Potholes:** "All roads in Hampton Township are county roads maintained by the Bay County Road Commission. We have reported the location to them [or: You can report it directly to the Road Commission by phone or on its website]."

**Office hours:** "The township office is open Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. The Supervisor holds resident office hours on Tuesdays from 3 to 5 p.m.; no appointment is needed, or call ext. 200 to set up another time."

**Blight:** "Thank you for letting us know. Our Building and Zoning Administrator will visit the property. If it's a violation, the owner gets a written notice with a deadline to clean it up, followed by a second visit; if it isn't corrected, the owner can be ticketed and the township can clean it up and bill the owner."

## Volume

The form received 214 messages from January through August 2026 (about 27 a month), plus 61 in September. The September spike was the supervisor vacancy and the new appointment.
