## Not the township (most common referrals)

**Water and sewer bills, high bills, meter readings, shutoffs, water main breaks, sewer backups.** The township still owns the water and sewer systems, but since January 2024 the **Bay County Department of Water and Sewer (BCDWS)** runs them, reads the meters and sends the bills. We can't look up an account or adjust a bill. Give residents the BCDWS customer service number printed on the current bill (not the old township bill; the old number now rings at an unstaffed line). For a high bill, tell them to ask BCDWS for a re-read and a leak check, and to check toilets first: a running toilet is the usual cause.

**Potholes, road surface, snowplowing on roads, road signs, tree limbs over the road, speed limits.** All roads in the township are county roads maintained by the **Bay County Road Commission** (Center Rd is a state highway the Road Commission maintains for the state). The township pays for some local road projects by agreement, but we don't patch or plow roads. Residents can report potholes to the Road Commission by phone or through its website. If they'd rather, take the location (road, nearest cross street, which side) and Pat will report it and log it.

**Dog licenses** are sold at the Treasurer's window (county license), but **animal control** is Bay County Animal Services.

**County drains** (the large named drains) are the Bay County Drain Commissioner.

**Streetlights out**: Consumers Energy, reported online with the pole number if possible.

**Ambulance bills**: the county ambulance service.

## Tips

- Don't guess at a tax amount or a zoning answer. Take the question and route it.
- Write down the parcel number or address on every request.
- If someone is upset, let them finish, then tell them who will call back and by when. Put it in the log the same day.
