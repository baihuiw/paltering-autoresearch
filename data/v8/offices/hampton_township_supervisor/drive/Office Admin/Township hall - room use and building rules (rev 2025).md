# Township hall: room use and building rules

Charter Township of Hampton, 801 W. Center Rd. Adopted by the Township Board as part of the facilities policy, revised May 2025. Maintained by the Supervisor's office (Pat Gwizdala keeps the room calendar).

## Rooms

| Room | Seats | Who can use it | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boardroom | 80 (theater) / 30 (tables) | Township boards and commissions; township-sponsored events; elections (Precinct 1 and early voting) | None |
| Conference room (upstairs) | 12 | Staff meetings, interviews, small committee meetings | None |
| Community room (lower level) | 50 | Township use first; then nonprofit groups based in the township (scouts, homeowner associations, senior club, service clubs) | None for township nonprofits; $25 cleaning deposit |
| Lobby display case | - | Township history and community displays, by arrangement with the Clerk | None |

The township hall is not rented to private parties, businesses or for political campaign events. Candidates and parties may not use rooms for campaign meetings; this applies to all candidates equally.

## Booking

- Requests to Pat (ext. 200) at least two weeks ahead. Township meetings and elections always take priority; nonprofit bookings may be moved, with notice, if the room is needed for a township purpose.
- Bookings for October 23-November 3, 2026 are closed (early voting and Election Day).
- Evening and weekend use requires a township staff member or official to unlock and lock the building, or a key holder from the group approved by the Supervisor.

## Rules for users

1. Leave the room as you found it: tables and chairs back, garbage bagged and taken to the dumpster, lights off.
2. No alcohol. No open flames or candles. No smoking or vaping anywhere on township property.
3. Children must be supervised.
4. Do not move or unplug the boardroom audio/video equipment. Staff will set up the microphones and projector on request.
5. The township is not responsible for items left behind. Lost and found is at the front counter for 30 days.
6. Groups serving food must clean the kitchenette and take leftovers home.
7. Nothing is attached to walls except with the blue painter's tape in the supply closet.
8. Groups must follow the township's nondiscrimination policy for their event.

## Building hours and access

- Public hours: Monday-Friday, 9:00 a.m.-3:00 p.m. (Supervisor's office open until 5:00 p.m. on Tuesdays for resident office hours.)
- Board, Planning Commission and ZBA meetings: doors open 30 minutes before the start.
- Alarm codes are issued by the Supervisor's office. Codes are changed when a key holder leaves.
- The Public Safety lobby has its own entrance and hours.

## Emergencies

- Call 911 first. Then the Supervisor's office (or after hours, the Public Safety non-emergency line).
- Exits are marked. The meeting point is the flagpole on the east side.
- First aid kit and AED are in the main hallway by the restrooms; staff are trained to use the AED.
- Severe weather: shelter in the lower-level hallway away from windows.

## Damage

Groups are responsible for damage beyond normal wear. The cleaning deposit is kept if the room isn't cleaned up; repair costs over the deposit are billed to the group.
