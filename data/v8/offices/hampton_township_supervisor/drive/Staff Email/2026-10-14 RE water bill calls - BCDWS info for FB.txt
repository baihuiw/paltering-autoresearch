Pat,

I asked the phone vendor to forward the old billing line to BCDWS customer service. They said 3-5 business days and a one-time $35 change fee. In the meantime I recorded a greeting on that line: "You've reached the former Hampton Township water billing line. Water and sewer billing is now handled by the Bay County Department of Water and Sewer. Please call the customer service number on your current bill." 

Facebook post goes up tomorrow morning (graphic with BCDWS contact info). Text:

"Questions about your water or sewer bill? Since January 2024, the Bay County Department of Water and Sewer (BCDWS) operates Hampton Township's water and sewer systems and handles all billing, meter readings, high-bill questions and leaks. The township office can't look up or adjust water accounts. Please call BCDWS customer service at the number on your current bill. Water main break or sewer backup? Call BCDWS any time."

Tom - FYI. This is the most common referral we get after potholes.
