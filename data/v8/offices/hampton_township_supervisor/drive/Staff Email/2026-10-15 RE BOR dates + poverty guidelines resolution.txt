2. Poverty exemption guidelines. The board has to adopt income and asset guidelines by resolution each year (MCL 211.7u). The draft for 2026-27 keeps last year's method:
   - Household income at or below the federal poverty guidelines for the household size (we use the current HHS table, which I'll attach),
   - Assets other than the home and one vehicle no more than $15,000,
   - The applicant must own and live in the home and have a PRE on file,
   - Applications due by the day before the Board of Review session that hears them.
   
   The only change: I'd like to add that applicants who were granted a full exemption the prior year and whose circumstances haven't changed may file the short renewal form (the state allows it). That cuts paperwork for the seniors who qualify every year.
