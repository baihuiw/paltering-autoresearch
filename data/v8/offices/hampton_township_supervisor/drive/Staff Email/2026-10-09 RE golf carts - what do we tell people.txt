Carla, Tom,

For the counter: right now golf carts are not allowed on any road in the township. We don't have a resolution under 257.657a. If someone is driving one on the street my officers will stop them and usually give a warning the first time. Kids driving them is the bigger worry - we've had two calls this summer about 12- and 13-year-olds driving carts in the Avondale/Kulaw area.

My thoughts if the board takes it up:

1. It's workable in the subdivisions. The subdivision streets are 25 mph. Anything on Center Rd (M-25), Ledyard, Scheurmann and the other section-line roads is 45-55, so carts could cross those but not drive on them. That means in practice a cart could get around inside a subdivision and maybe to Pondside Park, but not to the stores on Center.
2. State law already sets the main rules (16 and licensed, 15 mph, daylight only). The township can add more. Most townships that allow it add: a township registration sticker ($25 or so), proof of insurance, headlights/taillights/mirror/slow-moving-vehicle sign, no carts on sidewalks or the park paths.
3. Enforcement load is small. A registration sticker makes it easier because officers can tell at a glance.
4. The county board can disapprove a township resolution, and the Road Commission will have an opinion since they're county roads. I'd ask both before it goes to a vote.

I'm neutral. If the board wants it, I'd rather have rules and stickers than what we have now, which is people doing it anyway.

Steve
