Re: Special Land Use 26-03, Ledgewood Materials Recycling LLC, Hotchkiss Rd - adequacy of notice for the September 10, 2026 Planning Commission hearing

Dear Matt:

You asked me to review the file after your office received calls from residents of Plover Lane and Teal Court when equipment began arriving at the site. I have reviewed the application, the August 25 published notice, the radius list and mailing record you sent, the parcel map, and the draft minutes of September 10.

The Michigan Zoning Enabling Act requires that notice of a special land use request be published in a newspaper of general circulation at least 15 days before the hearing and be sent by mail or personal delivery to the owners of property assessed within 300 feet of the subject property and to the occupants of structures within 300 feet (MCL 125.3103 and 125.3502). Publication was timely. The posted agenda was also proper.

The mailing was not. The radius list run on August 24 was drawn from parcel 090-026-300-010-00, which was the parent parcel before the July 2026 land division. The approved site is the east part of that parcel, now 090-026-300-020-00. Drawn from the correct parcel, the 300-foot radius reaches 23 additional parcels in the Killdeer Acres No. 2 subdivision, all of them on Plover Lane and Teal Court. Based on the parcel map you sent, those 23 homes on Plover Lane and Teal Court are within 300 feet of the approved site, and none of them was on the mailing list, so they did not receive the mailed notice the statute requires.

In my opinion, the approval is vulnerable. An owner or occupant who did not receive required notice has a reasonable argument that the Planning Commission's decision was made without the notice the Act requires. If the decision were challenged in circuit court, I would expect the court to look hard at the defect, particularly because the subdivision is the nearest residential use and noise was discussed at the meeting.

My recommendation:
1. Send corrected notice to all owners and occupants within 300 feet of 090-026-300-020-00 and re-hear SLU 26-03 at the November 12 Planning Commission meeting. The commission may reaffirm, modify or deny.
2. Tell the applicant in writing now, so it can decide whether to keep operating at its own risk until the re-hearing.
3. Correct the radius-list procedure so lists are drawn from the current parcel number after any land division.

The cost to re-notice is small (postage and publication, under $300). The cost of defending a challenge is not.

Please call me if you or the Supervisor want to discuss.

Very truly yours,
