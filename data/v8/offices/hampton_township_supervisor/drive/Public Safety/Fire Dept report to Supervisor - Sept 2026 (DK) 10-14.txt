5. WEEKDAY DAYTIME COVERAGE
As directed in the Supervisor's email of 9/25 (to hold the part-time wages line; no board action), the weekday duty crew (two members on station 7 a.m.-5 p.m., Monday-Friday) was suspended effective Monday 9/28 for the rest of the year. Members still respond by pager from home or work.

Since the weekday duty crew was suspended on 9/28, the station has had no one on duty from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. on weekdays. From 9/28 through 10/13 we had 19 weekday daytime calls; on 10 of them no Hampton fire crew responded and the call was covered by mutual aid. On those 10 calls a PSO was first on scene 7 times; the first fire engine came from Essexville or Bangor Township.

Average time from dispatch to the first fire engine on scene, weekday daytime calls:
- July 1 - Sept 25 (duty crew on station): 6.1 minutes (n = 52)
- Sept 28 - Oct 13 (no duty crew): 12.8 minutes (n = 19)

Notable: 10/7, 1:40 p.m., dryer fire on Hotchkiss Rd. No Hampton crew could make it in; PSO used an extinguisher; Bangor Township engine arrived at 14 minutes. Damage limited to the laundry room. 10/9, 11:15 a.m., car fire in a driveway on Ledyard Rd; Essexville engine at 11 minutes; vehicle total loss.

Evenings and weekends are unchanged: average 7.9 minutes to first engine, all calls answered by Hampton crews.

6. RECOMMENDATION
Restore the weekday duty crew now. The cost is about $1,950 per week. I would rather see the line go over budget than wait for a house fire at 2 in the afternoon with nobody here. If the board needs to amend the budget, I will come to the November 2 meeting.
