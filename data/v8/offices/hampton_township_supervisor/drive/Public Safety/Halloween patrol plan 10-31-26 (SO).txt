HAMPTON TOWNSHIP PUBLIC SAFETY - OPERATIONS PLAN
Trick-or-treat, Saturday, October 31, 2026, 5:00-7:00 p.m.
Prepared by Director Steve Okerlund, October 14, 2026. Distribution: all officers; Supervisor; DPW; Clerk (polling place prep is the same weekend).

HOURS
Township trick-or-treat hours are 5:00-7:00 p.m. on Saturday, October 31, per the Halloween proclamation adopted by the Township Board on October 5. Essexville set the same hours (confirmed with Essexville PD 10/13), which helps families who cross the line.

STAFFING
- Shift: 4 officers on patrol 4:00 p.m.-midnight (normal Saturday evening is 2). Overtime for 2 officers, 8 hours each.
- Director on call; in the office 4:30-7:30 p.m.
- One car assigned to each of: west subdivisions (Woodside, Kulaw, Pine, Avondale); 2nd and 4th Street neighborhood; Center Rd corridor and the township hall; east side (Heavenridge, Scheurmann, Finn, Ridge).

PRIORITIES
1. Visible presence in the subdivisions, slow patrol with lights on, 5:00-7:30 p.m.
2. Traffic: enforce speed on the subdivision feeder streets. No stops for minor equipment violations during trick-or-treat hours unless needed; focus on speed and impaired drivers after 7.
3. Township hall: staff and board members will be handing out candy at the front entrance, 5-7 p.m. One officer stops by every 30 minutes. Note the Clerk's office is open 8-4 that day for absentee voting; election materials will be locked in the vault by 4:15.
4. After 7 p.m.: vandalism calls (pumpkins, eggs, mailboxes) are typical. Document; most are handled as juvenile contacts with parents.

GLOW STICKS
1,000 glow bracelets on hand (donated by the Hampton-Essexville business association). Officers hand them out from the cars. Leftovers go to the township hall table.

MESSAGES FOR FACEBOOK (Carla posting 10/30)
- Walk facing traffic where there is no sidewalk.
- Carry a flashlight; wear something reflective.
- Kids under 12 with an adult.
- Check candy before eating. Anything suspicious, call us.
- Drivers: slow down in neighborhoods, watch between parked cars, no texting.
- Porch light on means trick-or-treaters welcome.

LAST YEAR
Halloween 2025 (Friday): 23 calls between 5 p.m. and midnight; 3 traffic crashes, no injuries; 6 vandalism reports; 1 lost child reunited with parents in 10 minutes.

COORDINATION
- DPW: Rick will leave the leaf piles alone that week on the subdivision streets with the most trick-or-treaters only if there is a scheduling conflict; otherwise leaf pickup runs as normal Monday-Thursday. Ask residents on Facebook to keep piles out of the road so kids don't walk around them into traffic.
- Clerk: the township hall lot will have voters until 4 p.m. and candy table from 5. The front lot stays open for parents; the back lot is closed after 4 so the tabulator and supplies can be loaded into the vault without traffic.
- Essexville PD: we will monitor their channel and back each other up if either side gets busy.
- County ambulance: no special staffing requested.

AFTER-ACTION
Sergeant on duty writes a one-page summary for the Monday department heads meeting (11/2 notes): calls, crashes, anything to change for next year.

S. Okerlund
