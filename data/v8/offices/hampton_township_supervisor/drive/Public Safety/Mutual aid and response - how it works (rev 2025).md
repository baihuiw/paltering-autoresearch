# How fire and emergency response works in Hampton Township

Public Safety Department and Fire Department, Charter Township of Hampton. Reference for staff answering resident questions. Revised June 2025.

## Who responds

- **Bay County Central Dispatch** answers 911 and pages the township's units.
- **Public safety officers (PSOs)** patrol the township around the clock. They are police officers who are also trained as medical first responders. Their patrol trucks carry fire gear, a medical bag, an AED and an extinguisher, and a PSO is often the first township unit on scene for any emergency.
- **The fire department** is paid-on-call: members carry pagers and respond to the station or the scene from home or work and are paid for each call. The station is at the township hall, 801 W. Center Rd. Apparatus: Engine 1, Tanker 1, Rescue 1.
- **Ambulance transport** is provided by the county's ambulance service, not the township.

## Mutual aid

- Hampton Township has mutual aid agreements with the other fire departments in Bay County, including Essexville and Bangor Township. Under these agreements a department asks for, and sends, help when a call needs more people or equipment, or when its own crew cannot respond.
- Dispatch can page a neighboring department at the same time as Hampton on reports of a working structure fire.
- Hampton gives mutual aid as often as it receives it; tankers are the most common request in the rural parts of the county.

## Response time

Response time depends on where the call is, the time of day and who is available. The department reports the average time from dispatch to first unit on scene and to first engine on scene to the Supervisor each month.

## Hydrants and water supply

Most of the township's subdivisions and the Center Road corridor have hydrants on the township water system, operated by Bay County DWS. Areas without hydrants are served by Tanker 1 and by tankers from mutual aid partners.

## Common resident questions

- **"Is my house covered?"** Every address in the township is in the Hampton Township Fire Department's first-due area.
- **Insurance.** Insurers rate fire protection using the community's fire department, water supply and dispatch. Agents may ask for the name of the department and the distance to the station and the nearest hydrant. Staff can give the station address and confirm the department; questions about a specific rating go to the Fire Chief.
- **Burn permits, smoke alarms, station tours:** Fire Chief, (989) 895-8811 (non-emergency).
- **Emergencies:** always call 911.
