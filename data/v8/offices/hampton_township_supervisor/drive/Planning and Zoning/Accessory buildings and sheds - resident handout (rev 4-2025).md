# Sheds, garages and other accessory buildings: what you need to know

Charter Township of Hampton, Building and Zoning. Handout for residents. Revised April 2025 (MK). This is a summary; the zoning ordinance controls. Questions: Building and Zoning, (989) 893-7541 ext. 230.

## Do I need a permit?

**Every accessory building needs a township zoning permit**, including sheds that are delivered already built, kit sheds, and portable or skid-mounted buildings. The zoning permit makes sure the building is in an allowed location on your lot.

Whether you **also** need a **building permit** depends on size:

| Building | Zoning permit | Building permit |
|---|---|---|
| Shed or accessory building, one story, 200 sq. ft. or less (for example 10 x 12 = 120 sq. ft.; 12 x 16 = 192 sq. ft.) | Yes | No (exempt under the Michigan Residential Code) |
| Accessory building over 200 sq. ft. | Yes | Yes |
| Detached garage or pole barn (any size) | Yes | Yes |
| Any accessory building with electrical, plumbing or heat | Yes | Yes, plus trade permits |
| Fabric or "portable garage" structures left up more than 180 days a year | Yes | Over 200 sq. ft., yes |

A building exempt from a building permit still has to meet the zoning rules below.

## Where can it go? (Residential districts R-1 and R-2)

- **Yard:** Rear yard, or side yard behind the front building line of the house. Not in the front yard. On corner lots, both street sides count as front yards.
- **Distance from lot lines:** At least **5 ft.** from side and rear lot lines, measured to the nearest part of the building, including eaves and overhangs.
- **Distance from the house:** At least **10 ft.**
- **Height:** 14 ft. maximum for sheds and accessory buildings; 17 ft. for detached garages (measured to the midpoint of the roof).
- **Size:** All accessory buildings together may not cover more than 25% of the rear yard. Detached garages and pole barns on lots under one acre: 900 sq. ft. maximum.
- **Easements:** No building on a recorded utility or drainage easement. Check your survey or mortgage plot plan.
- **Number:** Two accessory buildings per lot on lots under one acre, plus an attached or detached garage.

Agricultural (A-1) and larger rural lots have different rules; call us.

## How to apply

1. Fill out the one-page zoning permit application (front counter or website).
2. Attach a simple sketch of your lot (a copy of your survey is best) showing the house, the new building, and the distances to the lot lines and the house.
3. For a building permit, also attach construction drawings (foundation, framing, roof) or the manufacturer's plans.
4. Fees: zoning permit $40. Building permit fee by square footage (see the township fee schedule). Fees are paid when the permit is issued.

Most zoning permits are issued in two to three business days. Building permits take about a week.

## Before you dig or anchor

Call **811 (MISS DIG)** at least three business days before you dig footings, set posts or drive ground anchors. It's free, and the utilities will mark their lines.

## Common questions

**My dealer says I don't need a permit.** Dealers are usually talking about the state building permit. A shed 200 sq. ft. or less doesn't need one, but it still needs the township zoning permit.

**Can I put the shed right on the property line if my neighbor agrees?** No. The 5-ft. setback applies even with a neighbor's permission. A variance from the Zoning Board of Appeals is the only way to change it, and the ZBA can only grant one for a practical difficulty with the lot itself.

**What happens if I put it up without a permit?** We may ask you to apply after the fact (double fee) and to move the building if it doesn't meet the setbacks.

**Does a shed raise my taxes?** Accessory buildings are part of your property's assessed value. A small shed usually makes a very small difference. The Assessing office can tell you more.
