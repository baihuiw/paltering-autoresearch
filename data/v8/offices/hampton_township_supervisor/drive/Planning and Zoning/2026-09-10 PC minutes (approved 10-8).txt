PUBLIC HEARING: SLU 26-03, Ledgewood Materials Recycling LLC, special land use for a concrete and asphalt crushing and recycling yard, parcel 090-026-300-020-00, north side of Hotchkiss Rd east of Ledyard Rd, zoned I-2 General Industrial.

Chair Tessin opened the public hearing at 7:06 p.m.

Mr. Krawczak reported that notice of the hearing was published in the Bay City Times on August 25 and mailed to owners and occupants within 300 feet of the property, and that the agenda was posted at the township hall and on the website. No written comments were received.

Darren Huebner, owner of Ledgewood Materials Recycling, presented the application. The company takes broken concrete and asphalt from road and demolition jobs, crushes and screens it, and sells it as base material. A portable crusher would run on site up to about 60 working days a year, in campaigns of one to three weeks. Stockpiles would be limited to 25 feet in height. Access is from Hotchkiss Rd only. He estimated 20 to 40 truck trips on crushing days.

Public comment:
- Dan Rousseau, owner of an auto repair shop on Hotchkiss Rd west of the site, asked about truck traffic and mud on the road. Mr. Huebner said a wheel-wash pad and a paved apron are part of the site plan.
- A resident of Ledyard Rd asked about dust. Mr. Huebner described the water spray bars on the crusher and a water truck for stockpiles and the drive.
- No one else wished to speak.

Chair Tessin closed the public hearing at 7:31 p.m.

Commission discussion: Commissioner Behmlander said she was concerned about noise reaching the Killdeer Acres subdivision to the east and asked whether a sound study had been done. Mr. Krawczak said the ordinance does not require one for I-2 but the commission may set conditions. Commissioner Kosnik noted that the parcel has been zoned I-2 since 1998 and that the use is listed as a special land use in that district. Trustee Janowiak asked about hours. The applicant agreed to the hours below.

Motion Kosnik, second Vanderhoof, to approve SLU 26-03 with the following conditions:
1. Crushing and screening only Monday-Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.; no crushing on Saturdays, Sundays or holidays.
2. Water spray on the crusher and conveyors at all times while operating; water truck on the drive and stockpiles as needed to keep dust on site.
3. A 10-foot landscaped earth berm along the east property line, completed before crushing begins.
4. Stockpiles no higher than 25 feet.
5. Wheel-wash pad and 100 feet of paved apron at the Hotchkiss Rd drive.
6. Compliance with EGLE air permit requirements for the portable crusher; copy of permit to the township before operation.
7. Approval lapses if operations do not begin within 12 months.
Roll call: Yes - Kosnik, Vanderhoof, Tessin, Sobczak, Janowiak. No - Behmlander. Motion carried 5-1.
