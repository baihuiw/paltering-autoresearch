Dear Mr. Huebner:

At its regular meeting on September 10, 2026, following a public hearing, the Hampton Township Planning Commission approved your special land use application by a vote of 5-1, subject to the conditions below. This letter is the township's written record of the approval. Please keep it with your site plan.

Conditions of approval:

1. Hours. Crushing and screening only Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. No crushing or screening on Saturdays, Sundays or the holidays listed in the township's personnel policy. Loading of trucks from stockpiles is permitted during the same hours.
2. Dust. Water spray on the crusher, screen and conveyors at all times while operating. A water truck shall be available on site and used on the drive and stockpiles as needed so that dust does not leave the property.
3. Screening. A 10-foot landscaped earth berm along the entire east property line, completed before crushing begins, and maintained.
4. Stockpiles. No stockpile higher than 25 feet above grade.
5. Tracking. A wheel-wash pad and a 100-foot paved apron at the Hotchkiss Rd drive. Mud tracked onto Hotchkiss Rd shall be cleaned the same day.
6. Air permit. Operation of the portable crusher under a valid permit from the Michigan Department of Environment, Great Lakes, and Energy (EGLE). A copy shall be provided to the township before operation.
7. Lapse. This approval lapses if operations do not begin within 12 months.

The Building and Zoning Administrator may inspect the site at any reasonable time to verify compliance. Violation of any condition may result in enforcement under the zoning ordinance, including a stop-work order, and may be grounds for the Planning Commission to consider revoking the special land use after notice and a hearing.

Before you begin crushing, please call me to schedule a site inspection of the berm and wheel-wash pad, and send the EGLE permit.

If you have questions, please contact me.

Sincerely,
