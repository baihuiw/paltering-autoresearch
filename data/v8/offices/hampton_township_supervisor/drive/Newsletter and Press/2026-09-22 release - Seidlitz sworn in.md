# Tom Seidlitz sworn in as Hampton Township Supervisor

ESSEXVILLE - Tom Seidlitz took the oath of office as Supervisor of the Charter Township of Hampton this morning at the township hall. Clerk Andrea Pohlod administered the oath, with Mr. Seidlitz's wife Diane, members of the Township Board and township staff present.

The Township Board appointed Mr. Seidlitz on September 21, by a 5-1 vote, after interviewing four applicants at a special meeting. The office became vacant when the board accepted the previous supervisor's resignation on September 8. Under Michigan law, the township board fills a vacancy in a township office by appointment for the rest of the term. Mr. Seidlitz will serve until the term elected in November 2028 begins.

Mr. Seidlitz retired after 34 years as a maintenance planner at the power plant on the bay. He served on the Hampton Township Planning Commission from 2019 to 2024, the last three years as chair. He and Diane have lived on Nebobish Road for 27 years and have two grown children.

"I've lived in this township most of my life, and I know how much people here count on the township to do the basics well," Supervisor Seidlitz said. "My priorities are simple: keep the budget balanced, finish the projects we've started, and answer every resident who calls or writes. My door is open."

The supervisor presides over the seven-member Township Board and serves as the township's chief administrative officer. Supervisor Seidlitz will hold resident office hours on Tuesday afternoons beginning in October.

Residents can reach the Supervisor at hamptonsupervisor@hamptonmi.net or (989) 893-7541.
