## Public Works

**Brush chipping** continues on the first Tuesday of each month through November. Brush must be at the road by 7 a.m., cut ends facing the road, nothing larger than 4 inches. No root balls or building materials.

**Roadside ditching.** The DPW crew cleaned more than four miles of roadside ditch this year, including Ledyard Road, Heavenridge Road and Fillmore Place, with Ridge Road next. The ditches are in the county right-of-way; the township pays for our own crew to clean them on a rotating list. Call the office to have your road added.

**Leaf pickup** runs weekly from October 26 through November 20, weather permitting. Rake leaves to the edge of the road, not into the road or the ditch.

---

## Trash and recycling

Trash is picked up weekly on your zone's day. **Curbside recycling is collected every other week on your trash day** in the blue cart: paper, cardboard, plastic bottles and jugs, cans and cartons. No glass and no plastic bags. Last year township households recycled 1,140 tons. Thank you!

Yard waste in paper bags is picked up weekly through November 30. A missed cart? Call North Bay Refuse at (989) 684-2290.

The solid waste assessment on the winter tax bill has been held at $214 per home for the third year in a row.

---

## Fire Department

Our paid-on-call firefighters answered 38 calls in August. **The station has a weekday daytime duty crew from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m.**, so an engine can roll in minutes during the hours when most members are at work. Evenings and weekends, members respond by pager. Our public safety officers carry fire gear and are trained medical first responders, and they are often first on scene.

Fire Prevention Week is October 4-10. Test your smoke and carbon monoxide alarms monthly and change the batteries when you change your clocks on November 1. Free smoke alarms are available for township homeowners; call (989) 895-8811.

The fire department is always looking for paid-on-call members. If you live in the township and are 18 or older, call the Chief.

## Public Safety

Leaving for the winter? Our public safety officers offer house checks while you are away. Stop at the Public Safety office to fill out the form. And remember: nobody legitimate asks to be paid in gift cards.
