# Hampton Township sets fall leaf pickup and trick-or-treat hours

ESSEXVILLE - Hampton Charter Township's Department of Public Works will begin its annual fall leaf pickup on Monday, October 26, and the Township Board has set trick-or-treat hours for Saturday, October 31, from 5:00 to 7:00 p.m.

**Leaf pickup**

The DPW leaf crew will make one pass down every township road each week from October 26 through November 20, weather permitting. Residents should:

- Rake leaves into a loose pile at the edge of their yard, next to the road, by 7:00 a.m. on Monday of each week. The truck works west of Ledyard Road early in the week and east of Ledyard Road later in the week.
- **Not bag leaves.** The leaf vacuum can't pick up bags, and bagged leaves are left behind.
- Keep leaves out of the road itself and away from storm drains, mailboxes and hydrants, and keep piles free of branches, rocks, pumpkins and yard decorations, which can damage the vacuum.
- Not park cars on top of or right next to leaf piles.

Branches go out for brush chipping instead, on the first Tuesday of the month (the final chipping of the season is November 3-4).

"Last year the crew picked up more than 2,400 cubic yards of leaves," said DPW Superintendent Rick Haske. "The biggest thing residents can do to help us is keep the piles loose and out of the street. When it rains, leaves in the road wash straight into the drains."

Schedules can slip after heavy rain or an early snow. Updates will be posted on the township's Facebook page and website.

**Trick-or-treat**

At its October 5 meeting the Township Board adopted a proclamation setting township trick-or-treat hours on Halloween, Saturday, October 31, from 5:00 to 7:00 p.m. Public Safety officers will be on extra patrol in the subdivisions during those hours.

Public Safety Director Steve Okerlund offered a few reminders: walk on the left side of the road facing traffic where there are no sidewalks, carry a flashlight or glow stick, check treats before eating, and drivers please slow down and watch for children between parked cars. Residents who want trick-or-treaters should leave a porch light on.

Township officials will be handing out candy at the township hall, 801 W. Center Rd., during trick-or-treat hours.

"Halloween on a Saturday should bring out a lot of families," said Supervisor Tom Seidlitz. "Let's keep it safe and fun for the kids."

**Other dates**

- 2027 budget workshop: Monday, October 26, 1:00 p.m., township boardroom.
- General election: Tuesday, November 3, polls open 7 a.m.-8 p.m.
