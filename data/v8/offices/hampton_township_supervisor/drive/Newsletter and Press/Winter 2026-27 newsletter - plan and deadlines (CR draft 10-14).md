## Draft text: "Who to call" box (Pat's version, 10/14)

**Water or sewer bill, high bill, leak, main break, sewer backup:** Bay County Department of Water and Sewer (BCDWS), customer service number on your current bill. Emergencies any time.
**Pothole, plowing, road signs, trees in the road:** Bay County Road Commission.
**Property taxes, dog licenses:** Township Treasurer, ext. 211.
**Assessments and exemptions:** Assessing, ext. 220 (Tuesdays and Thursdays).
**Elections and voter registration:** Township Clerk, ext. 206.
**Permits, sheds, fences, blight, rentals:** Building and Zoning, ext. 230.
**Brush chipping, leaf pickup, township buildings:** DPW, ext. 240.
**Police non-emergency, house checks:** Public Safety, ext. 250.
**Everything else, or not sure:** Supervisor's office, ext. 200. Office hours Monday-Friday 9-3; Supervisor's resident hours Tuesdays 3-5 p.m.
**Emergencies:** 911.

## Draft text: winter taxes (Jean, to be checked against the final roll)

Winter tax bills will be mailed by December 1. Taxes are due February 14, 2027; because the 14th is a Sunday and the 15th is Presidents Day, payments will be accepted without penalty through Tuesday, February 16. Pay at the Treasurer's window (Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m.), in the drop box on the east side of the township hall, by mail, or online (card fees apply). If you refinanced this year, make sure your new lender has requested your bill. Extended hours December 28-31 for year-end payments.
