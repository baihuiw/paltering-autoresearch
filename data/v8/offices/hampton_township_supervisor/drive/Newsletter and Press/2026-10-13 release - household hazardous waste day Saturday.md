# Household hazardous waste day is Saturday at the Hampton Township hall

ESSEXVILLE - Hampton Charter Township residents can drop off household hazardous waste for free on Saturday, October 17, from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., in the parking lot of the township hall, 801 W. Center Rd.

The collection is open to Hampton Township residents only. Please bring a driver's license or a recent tax bill showing a township address.

**Drive-through and stay in your car.** Enter from Center Road, follow the cones, and pop your trunk. Workers from the township's licensed hazardous waste contractor will unload for you. Please don't get out of your vehicle.

**Accepted:**
- Oil-based paint, stain, varnish and paint thinner
- Solvents, degreasers and gasoline (in closed containers, 5 gallons or less)
- Pesticides, herbicides, weed killers and fertilizer with weed killer
- Pool and spa chemicals
- Household cleaners, drain openers, bleach and ammonia
- Fluorescent tubes and CFL bulbs
- Car and household batteries (alkaline, rechargeable, lithium)
- Mercury thermometers and thermostats
- Small propane cylinders (camping size; no 20-lb grill tanks)
- Aerosol cans that are not empty

**Not accepted:**
- **Latex (water-based) paint.** It isn't hazardous. Let it dry out with kitty litter or a paint hardener and put the can out with your household garbage with the lid off.
- Tires, appliances, electronics, medicines and sharps, ammunition and explosives, 20-lb propane tanks, and business waste.

Keep products in their original containers if you can, and don't mix anything. Containers should be no larger than 5 gallons and the total load should fit in a car trunk or the bed of a pickup.

The township holds the collection once a year in the fall. Last year 312 vehicles came through and the contractor shipped out more than 11 tons of material.

"Most of us have a shelf in the garage full of half-empty cans we don't know what to do with," said Supervisor Tom Seidlitz. "This is the day to clean it out. Please don't pour it down the drain or put it in the garbage."

The township thanks the DPW crew and the volunteers from the Planning Commission and the township board who help direct traffic.

**Other ways to dispose of items not accepted Saturday**

- **Electronics** (TVs, computers, monitors): many electronics retailers take them back; check before you go. Some charge a fee for TVs.
- **Medicines:** take-back boxes are in the Public Safety lobby at the township hall (pills only; no liquids or needles) during lobby hours, Monday-Friday, 8 a.m.-4 p.m.
- **Sharps:** sealed in a hard plastic container (a laundry detergent bottle with the cap taped shut), labeled "sharps," and put out with household garbage.
- **Tires:** tire dealers take old tires for a fee when you buy new ones.
- **Appliances:** scrap metal dealers take most appliances. Refrigerators, freezers and air conditioners must have the refrigerant removed by a certified technician first.
- **Latex paint:** dry it out (open the lid, or mix in kitty litter or a hardener) and put the can out with household garbage, lid off, so the hauler can see it's dry.
