# Hampton Township Clerk: early voting starts October 24; absentee ballots can go in the drop box

ESSEXVILLE - Hampton Charter Township Clerk Andrea Pohlod reminds voters of the key dates for the Tuesday, November 3 general election.

**Polls open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Election Day.** Voters in line at 8:00 p.m. will be allowed to vote. Hampton Township's four precincts vote at the township hall (Precincts 1 and 2), the church fellowship hall on Ledyard Road (Precinct 3) and the church gym on Center Road east (Precinct 4). Voters can find their precinct on their voter ID card or the state's voter information website.

**Early in-person voting** runs Saturday, October 24 through Sunday, November 1, in the township hall boardroom, 801 W. Center Rd., for voters in all four Hampton precincts. Hours are 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. daily, except Thursday, October 29, when early voting is open from noon to 8 p.m.

**Absentee ballots.** As of October 15, the Clerk's office had issued 1,994 absentee ballots for the November election and received 1,102 back. Voters who have an absentee ballot at home are encouraged to return it as soon as possible:

- **Drop box:** The secure drop box on the east side of the township hall, next to the flagpole, is open 24 hours a day until 8:00 p.m. on Election Day. It is monitored by camera and emptied every weekday by two election workers.
- **In person:** at the Clerk's office counter, Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., and Saturday, October 31, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.
- **By mail:** postage is prepaid, but the Clerk recommends mailing no later than Friday, October 30. Ballots must be received by 8:00 p.m. on Election Day to be counted.

"Please sign the back of your return envelope," Pohlod said. "It's the most common reason we have to call a voter. If we can't match a signature, we'll contact you so you can fix it, but it's easier to get it right the first time."

**Registration.** Monday, October 19 is the last day to register online or by mail. After that, eligible residents may register in person at the Clerk's office with proof of residency, through 8:00 p.m. on Election Day.

**Election workers.** The Clerk's office thanks the more than 30 residents who are serving as election inspectors this fall. Residents interested in serving in future elections can sign up at the Clerk's office.

Voters can track their absentee ballot and see a sample ballot on the state's voter information website.
