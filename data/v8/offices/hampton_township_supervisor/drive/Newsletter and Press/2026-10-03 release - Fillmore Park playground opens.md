# Hampton Township opens new accessible playground at Fillmore Park

ESSEXVILLE - Hampton Township families have a new place to play. Supervisor Tom Seidlitz cut the ribbon this morning on a new accessible playground at Fillmore Park, joined by members of the Township Board, a representative of the state grant program, neighbors and about 120 children and parents.

The playground includes a composite play structure for ages 2 to 5 and 5 to 12 with a ramp to the upper deck, a transfer station, a suspension bridge and two slides, plus a two-bay swing set with an accessible seat and two spring riders. A poured rubber path connects the parking lot, the new accessible parking spaces and the play area, so children and parents who use wheelchairs or walkers can reach the equipment.

"This playground is for every kid in Hampton Township," Supervisor Seidlitz said. "A lot of people worked hard to get here, and I'm proud to open it in my first weeks on the job. Come out and use it."

The $186,400 project was funded by a $139,800 state recreation grant and a $46,600 township match. The equipment was installed by Northern Play Systems, a certified installer, in September. The township's DPW crew checks the playground every day, and the surfacing is raked and topped up as part of regular park maintenance.

Fillmore Park is off Fillmore Place. The park is open dawn to dusk. Two new benches and a trash receptacle were added this fall, along with striped parking and two accessible spaces.

The township thanks the Township Board, the DPW crew, Recreation and Parks Coordinator Kelly Fruchey, and the neighbors who wrote letters of support for the grant application.
