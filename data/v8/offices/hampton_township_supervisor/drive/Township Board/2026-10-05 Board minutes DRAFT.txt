CHARTER TOWNSHIP OF HAMPTON
TOWNSHIP BOARD - REGULAR MEETING MINUTES
Monday, October 5, 2026 - 7:00 p.m.
Township Hall Boardroom, 801 W. Center Rd.

DRAFT - subject to approval November 2, 2026.

CALL TO ORDER: Supervisor Seidlitz called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance. It was his first regular meeting as Supervisor.

ROLL CALL: Present: Supervisor Tom Seidlitz, Clerk Andrea Pohlod, Treasurer Jean Wiltse, Trustees Ed Borowicz, Sharon Pfund, Kurt Janowiak, Denise LaBair. Also present: Deputy Supervisor Carla Rheaume, Director of Public Safety Steve Okerlund, DPW Superintendent Rick Haske. Audience: 17.

AGENDA: Motion Pfund, second Borowicz, to approve the agenda as presented. Carried 7-0.

PUBLIC COMMENT: A Woodside Ave resident asked when leaf pickup begins (October 26). A Center Rd business owner thanked the DDA for the new banners.

CONSENT AGENDA (Motion Janowiak, second Wiltse; carried 7-0 by roll call):
a. Minutes of the September 8 regular meeting and September 21 special meeting.
b. Bills payable: General Fund $201,448.62; Solid Waste Fund $63,210.00; Water $39,560.18; Sewer $40,002.71; Parks grant $83,880.00.
c. Treasurer's report for September.
d. Resolution 2026-31, confirming the 2026 Solid Waste Collection special assessment roll at $214.00 per residential unit (3,940 units, $843,160.00) for collection services in 2027, and directing the Clerk to certify it for the December tax roll.
e. Resolution 2026-32, 2026 millage certification (L-4029) as presented by the Treasurer.
f. Halloween proclamation.

SUPERVISOR'S REPORT:
- Supervisor Seidlitz thanked staff for a smooth transition and said he will hold resident office hours Tuesdays 3-5 p.m. starting October 13.
- He thanked everyone who came to the Fillmore Park ribbon cutting on Saturday, about 120 people, and the DPW crew and Kelly Fruchey for the work on the grant project.
- In response to a question from Trustee LaBair about the fire department, the Supervisor said the fire department continues to respond to every call, with mutual aid from neighboring departments when needed, and that fire department staffing will be part of the 2027 budget discussion.
- The 2027 budget workshop is set for Monday, October 26.

NEW BUSINESS:
1. Deputy Supervisor appointment. The Supervisor reported that he has reappointed Carla Rheaume as Deputy Supervisor. Information only.
2. Budget workshop. Motion Pohlod, second Pfund, to hold a budget workshop on Monday, October 26 (time to be set by the Clerk) and a public hearing on the 2027 budget on Monday, November 2 at 7:00 p.m. Carried 7-0.
3. Bay County Road Commission - 2027 local road program. The Supervisor reported that Road Commission staff will present the 2027 township road options at the November meeting. Trustee Janowiak asked that Hotchkiss Rd be considered for chip seal. Information only.
4. Snow removal at township facilities. Motion Borowicz, second LaBair, to renew the township hall and parking lot plowing contract with the same vendor at the 2025-26 rate. Carried 7-0.

REPORTS:
- Clerk: 1,862 absentee ballots issued for November 3 as of today. Early in-person voting begins October 24. Election inspector training October 13 and 15.
- Treasurer: Winter tax bills will be mailed by December 1.
- Public Safety: 198 calls for service in September.
- DPW: Brush chipping October 6-7; leaf pickup from October 26.
- Zoning: 38 permits in September.

BOARD COMMENTS: Trustee LaBair asked that the supervisor's office hours be posted on Facebook. Trustee Pfund welcomed the Supervisor.

PUBLIC COMMENT: None.

ADJOURNMENT: Motion Wiltse, second Pfund, to adjourn at 7:51 p.m. Carried.

Andrea Pohlod, Clerk
