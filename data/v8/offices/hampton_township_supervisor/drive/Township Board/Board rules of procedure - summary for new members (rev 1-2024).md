# Township Board rules of procedure: summary

Charter Township of Hampton. Summary of the rules of procedure adopted by the Township Board (revised January 2024), prepared by the Clerk for new board members and the public. The adopted rules and the Open Meetings Act control.

## Meetings

- **Regular meetings**: first Monday of each month, 7:00 p.m., township hall boardroom, 801 W. Center Rd. When the first Monday is a legal holiday, the meeting is the next day (Tuesday) at 7:00 p.m.
- **Special meetings**: called by the Supervisor or by any two board members, with at least 18 hours' public notice under the Open Meetings Act, and notice to each member.
- **Work sessions / workshops**: posted like special meetings. The board may discuss but normally does not take final action at a workshop.
- **Closed sessions**: only for purposes allowed by the Open Meetings Act (for example, pending litigation with the attorney, or a written legal opinion), by a roll call vote (two-thirds for most purposes). No decisions are made in closed session.
- The annual meeting schedule is adopted each December and posted at the hall and on the website.

## Quorum and voting

- The board has seven members: Supervisor, Clerk, Treasurer and four Trustees. A quorum is four.
- Most actions pass with a majority of members present. Some actions need a majority of the full board (four votes) regardless of how many are present, including adopting ordinances, the budget and appropriations, and filling a board vacancy.
- Members vote on every question unless they have a conflict of interest, which they state on the record before abstaining.
- Roll call votes are taken on ordinances, resolutions, the budget, bills payable and any question a member asks to have recorded.

## The chair

- The Supervisor presides. In the Supervisor's absence (or during a vacancy) the board chooses a president pro tem from among its members.
- The chair votes on all questions as a member.
- The chair keeps order, recognizes speakers and rules on points of order. A ruling can be appealed to the board by a majority vote.

## Order of business

1. Call to order and Pledge of Allegiance
2. Roll call
3. Approval of the agenda
4. Public comment (agenda items)
5. Public hearings, when scheduled
6. Consent agenda
7. Unfinished business
8. New business
9. Reports (Supervisor, Clerk, Treasurer, departments)
10. Board comments
11. Public comment (any topic)
12. Adjournment

The **consent agenda** groups routine items (minutes, bills, the Treasurer's report, routine resolutions and proclamations) into one motion. Any member may ask to pull an item for separate discussion; no second is needed to pull an item.

## Agenda and packet

- Items for the agenda go to the Clerk and the Deputy Supervisor by noon on the Wednesday before the meeting, with backup.
- The packet goes to members and is posted on the website the Thursday before the meeting.
- An item may be added at the meeting only by a majority vote approving the amended agenda.

## Public comment

- Three minutes per person, at the two public comment times. The chair may extend time.
- Speakers state their name and, if they wish, their address.
- Public comment is a time for the board to listen. Members generally don't debate with speakers; the Supervisor may ask staff to follow up.
- Written comments received by the Clerk before the meeting are summarized in the minutes.

## Minutes

- The Clerk takes minutes. Draft minutes are available to the public within eight business days after the meeting, and approved minutes within five business days after approval, as required by the Open Meetings Act.
- Minutes record actions and votes, not a transcript of discussion.

## Conduct

- Members address one another through the chair.
- Members don't direct staff individually; requests for staff work go through the Supervisor.
- Email among a quorum about board business should be avoided, so that deliberations happen at public meetings.
