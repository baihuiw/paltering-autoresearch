CHARTER TOWNSHIP OF HAMPTON
TOWNSHIP BOARD - SPECIAL MEETING MINUTES
Monday, September 21, 2026 - 6:00 p.m.
Township Hall Boardroom, 801 W. Center Rd.

Approved October 5, 2026.

PURPOSE: To interview applicants for the vacant office of Supervisor and to make an appointment for the remainder of the term. Notice of the special meeting was posted September 9 in accordance with the Open Meetings Act.

CALL TO ORDER: President Pro Tem Borowicz called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m.

ROLL CALL: Present: Pohlod, Wiltse, Borowicz, Pfund, Janowiak, LaBair. Audience: 41.

PUBLIC COMMENT: Six residents spoke. Two urged the board to appoint someone with township experience; one asked the board to leave the seat open for a special election; three spoke in support of individual applicants.

APPLICANTS: The Clerk reported four letters of interest received by the September 16 deadline, all from qualified electors of the township: Thomas Seidlitz, Nebobish Rd; Karen Mielke, Woodside Ave; Bruce Halberg, Center Rd; Angela Pyrzynski, Ledyard Rd. Each applicant gave a five-minute statement and answered the same four questions from the board (budget priorities, experience supervising staff, time available, and plans for the 2028 election).

Summary of statements:
- Mr. Seidlitz: retired after 34 years as a maintenance planner at the power plant; Planning Commission 2019-2024, chair 2021-2024. Priorities: hold the line on the budget, finish the park grant projects, improve communication with residents. Available full-time. Undecided about 2028.
- Ms. Mielke: small-business owner; DDA board member. Priorities: Center Road corridor, business attraction. Available part-time until January.
- Mr. Halberg: retired teacher; ZBA member. Priorities: transparency, recorded meetings. Available full-time.
- Ms. Pyrzynski: accountant; no prior township service. Priorities: finances, audit follow-up. Available evenings and Fridays.

BOARD DISCUSSION: Trustees noted the township needs a full-time supervisor during the 2027 budget process and the park and sewer grant closeouts. Trustee LaBair said she favored Mr. Halberg for his commitment to open meetings.

MOTION: Motion Wiltse, second Janowiak, to appoint Thomas Seidlitz as Supervisor of the Charter Township of Hampton for the remainder of the term, effective upon taking the oath of office and filing the required bond, at the salary set in the 2026 budget for the office. Roll call: Yes - Wiltse, Janowiak, Pohlod, Borowicz, Pfund. No - LaBair. Motion carried 5-1.

The Clerk will administer the oath on Tuesday, September 22, at 9:00 a.m. at the township hall. The Treasurer will arrange the bond.

The board thanked all four applicants. Trustee Borowicz thanked Deputy Supervisor Rheaume and the staff for keeping the office running during the vacancy.

PUBLIC COMMENT: Two residents congratulated Mr. Seidlitz. One asked that the new supervisor hold regular office hours for residents.

ADJOURNMENT: Motion Pfund, second Wiltse, to adjourn at 7:34 p.m. Carried.

Andrea Pohlod, Clerk
