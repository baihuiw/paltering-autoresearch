CHARTER TOWNSHIP OF HAMPTON
TOWNSHIP BOARD - REGULAR MEETING MINUTES
Tuesday, September 8, 2026 - 7:00 p.m. (moved from Monday, September 7, Labor Day)
Township Hall Boardroom, 801 W. Center Rd.

Approved October 5, 2026.

CALL TO ORDER: Clerk Pohlod called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led the Pledge of Allegiance.

ROLL CALL: Present: Clerk Andrea Pohlod, Treasurer Jean Wiltse, Trustees Ed Borowicz, Sharon Pfund, Kurt Janowiak, Denise LaBair. Absent: none. Also present: Deputy Supervisor Carla Rheaume, Building and Zoning Administrator Matt Krawczak, DPW Superintendent Rick Haske, Director of Public Safety Steve Okerlund. Audience: 22.

ELECTION OF PRESIDENT PRO TEM: The Clerk reported that the Supervisor's chair is vacant pending action on item 1 below and that the Charter Township Act provides for the board to appoint one of its members president pro tem in the Supervisor's absence. Motion Pfund, second Janowiak, to appoint Trustee Borowicz president pro tem. Carried 6-0. Trustee Borowicz presided for the rest of the meeting.

AGENDA: Motion LaBair, second Wiltse, to approve the agenda as presented. Carried 6-0.

PUBLIC COMMENT: Two residents thanked the previous supervisor for years of service. One resident asked how the vacancy would be filled; the Clerk explained the process under state law (see item 1).

CONSENT AGENDA (Motion Pfund, second Janowiak; carried 6-0 by roll call):
a. Minutes of the August 3, 2026 regular meeting.
b. Bills payable, General Fund $184,226.17; Solid Waste Fund $63,210.00; Water $41,882.50; Sewer $38,117.04.
c. Treasurer's report for August.
d. Reappointment of Lori Behmlander to the Planning Commission, term to 9/30/2029.

NEW BUSINESS:

1. Resignation of the Supervisor. The Clerk reported receiving a written resignation from the Supervisor on September 3, effective upon acceptance by the board. Under state law the board fills a vacancy in a township office by appointment for the remainder of the term; if no appointment is made within 45 days, the county clerk calls a special election. The appointee must be a qualified elector of the township. Motion Wiltse, second Borowicz, to accept the resignation with thanks for years of service, effective tonight. Carried 6-0.

   Motion Janowiak, second Pfund, to post the vacancy and accept letters of interest and resumes at the Clerk's office until 3:00 p.m. Wednesday, September 16, and to hold a special meeting on Monday, September 21, at 6:00 p.m. to interview applicants and make an appointment. Carried 6-0. Deputy Supervisor Rheaume will continue to manage day-to-day administration in the interim, with the Clerk and Treasurer signing checks.

2. Fillmore Park playground - progress payment. Recreation and Parks Coordinator Kelly Fruchey (by memo) reported that Northern Play Systems began installation on September 8 and expects to finish by September 18. Motion Pfund, second LaBair, to approve progress payment #1 of $83,880 to Northern Play Systems, to be reimbursed in part by the state grant. Carried 6-0.

3. DPW pickup truck replacement. Superintendent Haske presented three quotes for a 3/4-ton pickup with plow prep. Motion Janowiak, second Wiltse, to purchase from the low bidder under the state cooperative purchasing program at $54,915, budgeted in the 2026 equipment line. Carried 6-0.

4. Trick-or-treat hours. Motion LaBair, second Pfund, to set township trick-or-treat hours for Saturday, October 31, 5:00-7:00 p.m. Carried 6-0.

5. Household hazardous waste day. Deputy Supervisor Rheaume reported the annual HHW collection is set for Saturday, October 17, 9 a.m.-1 p.m. at the township hall, using the county's contractor. Information only.

REPORTS:
- Clerk: Absentee ballots for the November 3 general election will be mailed beginning September 24. Election inspector training October 13 and 15.
- Treasurer: Summer tax collections are 91% of the levy. Investments renewed at 4.1%.
- Public Safety: 212 calls for service in August; two new PSOs completed field training.
- DPW: Brush chipping done 9/1-9/2; ditching on Ledyard Rd this week.
- Zoning: Planning Commission hearing on SLU 26-03 (Hotchkiss Rd) Thursday, September 10.

BOARD COMMENTS: Trustee LaBair asked that the vacancy notice be posted on Facebook as well as the website. The Deputy Supervisor will do so.

PUBLIC COMMENT: None.

ADJOURNMENT: Motion Wiltse, second Pfund, to adjourn at 7:58 p.m. Carried.

Andrea Pohlod, Clerk
