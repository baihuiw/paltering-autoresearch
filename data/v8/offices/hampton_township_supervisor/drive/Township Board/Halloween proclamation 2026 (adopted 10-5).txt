CHARTER TOWNSHIP OF HAMPTON
BAY COUNTY, MICHIGAN

PROCLAMATION
HALLOWEEN TRICK-OR-TREAT HOURS, 2026

Adopted by the Township Board at its regular meeting of October 5, 2026 (consent agenda item f)

WHEREAS, Halloween is a tradition enjoyed by the children and families of Hampton Charter Township; and

WHEREAS, Halloween falls on Saturday, October 31, in 2026; and

WHEREAS, setting common hours for trick-or-treating helps residents know when to expect visitors, allows the Department of Public Safety to schedule additional patrols, and helps keep children safe on township roads; and

WHEREAS, the City of Essexville has set the same hours, and many families visit friends and relatives on both sides of the city line;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Township Board of the Charter Township of Hampton proclaims that:

1. Trick-or-treating in Hampton Charter Township shall be observed on **Saturday, October 31, 2026, from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m.**

2. Residents who wish to welcome trick-or-treaters are asked to turn on a porch light. Residents who do not wish to participate are asked to leave porch lights off, and trick-or-treaters are asked to respect that.

3. Motorists are urged to drive slowly and with extra care in residential areas during and after these hours.

4. Parents and guardians are encouraged to accompany young children, to have children wear reflective clothing or carry a light, and to check all treats before they are eaten.

5. The Department of Public Safety will provide additional patrols during trick-or-treat hours.

PROCLAIMED this 5th day of October, 2026.

Tom Seidlitz, Supervisor

ATTEST:
Andrea Pohlod, Clerk

CERTIFICATION
I certify that the foregoing is a true copy of a proclamation adopted by the Township Board of the Charter Township of Hampton at a regular meeting held October 5, 2026, by a vote of 7-0 (consent agenda).

Andrea Pohlod, Clerk
