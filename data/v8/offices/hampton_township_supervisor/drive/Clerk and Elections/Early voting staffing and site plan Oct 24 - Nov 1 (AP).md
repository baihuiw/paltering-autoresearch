# Early in-person voting, October 24 - November 1: site and staffing plan

Clerk's office, Charter Township of Hampton. Andrea Pohlod, Clerk. Draft v3, October 7, 2026.

## Site and hours

- **Site:** Township hall boardroom, 801 W. Center Rd. Voters from all four Hampton precincts vote here.
- **Days:** Saturday, October 24 through Sunday, November 1 (nine consecutive days).
- **Hours:** 9:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. every day, except Thursday, October 29, 12:00-8:00 p.m. (one evening day for people who work days).
- Parking: front lot and north lot. Accessible entrance at the front door; voting at the car available by calling the number on the sign.
- The boardroom is not available for other meetings October 23 (setup) through November 2 (Board meeting is that evening; Precinct 1 sets up for Election Day after the meeting).

## How it works

Early voting works like Election Day. The voter checks in at the e-pollbook, gets a ballot, marks it at a booth and puts it into the tabulator. Early voting ballots are not counted until Election Day; the tabulator's results stay sealed. The tabulator, ballots and pollbook laptops are locked in the vault every night, with numbered seals recorded on the seal log by two workers of different parties.

Voters who already have an absentee ballot can bring it and have it spoiled, or drop it in the drop box outside.

## Estimated turnout

Based on the August primary (early voting accounted for about 6% of ballots cast) and the size of this November ballot, I expect 500-700 early voters over the nine days, with a third of them on the last weekend.

## Supplies (checked 10/7)

- Ballots: 1,200 early voting ballots (all four precinct styles) from the county; more available on 24 hours' notice.
- E-pollbook laptops (2) and the backup paper list.
- Tabulator, ballot box and auxiliary bin; seals (numbered, 60).
- Signs: "Vote Here," "Early Voting Today," hours sign for the front door and the road sign on Center Rd (DPW sets it out 10/23).
- Privacy booths (8), one accessible booth with the ballot marking device.

## Coordination

- **Halloween, Saturday 10/31:** trick-or-treat is 5-7 p.m., after early voting closes. The Clerk's office counter is also open 8-4 that day for absentee ballots and same-day registration, at the counter, separate from early voting in the boardroom.
- **DPW:** Andy Kuch sets up booths on Friday 10/23 and moves everything for Precinct 1 on the night of 11/2.
- **Public Safety:** a patrol car checks the lot at closing each night when the tabulator goes to the vault.
- **Supervisor's office:** Pat answers the main line with "early voting is in the boardroom" and directs walk-ins. The Supervisor's Tuesday office hours on 10/27 will be in his office as usual; voters in the hall are directed past.

## Reporting

Daily counts of early voters by precinct are reported to the county clerk by 9 p.m. and posted on the township Facebook page the next morning (count only, never results).
