# Absentee voting, the drop box and Election Day: website text

Clerk's page, hamptontownshipmi.gov. Posted September 24, 2026 (AP); early voting paragraph updated October 1. Copy the wording exactly when answering resident emails.

## November 3, 2026 General Election

**Polls are open 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3.** Anyone in line at 8:00 p.m. may vote. Hampton Township has four precincts; your precinct is printed on your voter ID card and on the state's voter information website.

### Voting absentee

Any registered voter in Michigan can vote absentee without giving a reason. Ballots were mailed September 24 to everyone on the permanent absentee voter list and everyone with an application on file. You can still apply online through the state's voter information website, by mail, or in person at the Clerk's office.

**Returning your ballot.** Sign the outside of the return envelope. Your signature is checked against your registration record; an unsigned envelope can't be counted until you sign it. Then return it one of three ways:

1. **The township drop box**, on the east side of the township hall by the flagpole, 801 W. Center Rd. The drop box is open 24 hours a day and is under video surveillance. It is emptied by two election workers every weekday, and on Election Day it closes at exactly 8:00 p.m.
2. **In person** at the Clerk's office during office hours (Monday-Friday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m.) or on Saturday, October 31, 8 a.m.-4 p.m.
3. **By mail.** Postage is prepaid. We recommend mailing it by Friday, October 30. A ballot that arrives after 8:00 p.m. on Election Day can't be counted, no matter when it was postmarked.

Only you, a member of your immediate family or a person living in your household may return your ballot for you. If you need help, call the Clerk's office.

**Track your ballot** on the state's voter information website. It shows when your ballot was mailed and when we received it.

**Changed your mind?** If you have an absentee ballot but want to vote at the polls, bring the ballot with you and the precinct will spoil it and issue a new one. If you lost it, you can sign a statement at the polls.

### Early in-person voting

Early voting runs Saturday, October 24 through Sunday, November 1. Location and daily hours are posted on this page and at the township hall. Early voting works like Election Day: you feed your ballot into the tabulator yourself.

### Registering to vote

Through Monday, October 19, register online, by mail or at any Secretary of State office. From October 20 through Election Day, register in person at the Clerk's office with proof of residency, and you may vote an absentee ballot on the spot.
