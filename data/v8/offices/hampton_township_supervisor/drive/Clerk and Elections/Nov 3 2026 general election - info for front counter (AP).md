# November 3, 2026 general election: front counter information

Clerk's office, Charter Township of Hampton. For staff answering resident questions. Updated October 1, 2026 (AP). Questions the counter can't answer go to the Clerk.

Voters can look up their precinct on the state's voter information website or call the Clerk's office.

## Absentee ballots

- Any registered voter may vote absentee. Apply online through the state's website, by mail, or at the Clerk's office.
- Drop box: east side of the township hall by the flagpole, open 24 hours until 8 p.m. on Election Day.
- Ballot status can be tracked on the state's voter information website.
- If a voter received a damaged ballot or made a mistake, they can ask the Clerk's office for a replacement (spoil and reissue).

## Things staff should not do

- Don't tell anyone who or what to vote for, and don't wear campaign items at work.
- Don't accept a completed ballot at the front counter yourself. Send the person to the Clerk's window or the drop box.

## Election inspectors

Training October 13 and 15, 6 p.m. Election inspectors are paid $15/hour. Interested residents can sign up with the Clerk for future elections.
