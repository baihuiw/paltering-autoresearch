# Fischer Creek Park Master Plan Update (2026) - Draft Summary

Manitowoc County Planning, Parks & Land Information Department. Public review draft, September 8, 2026.

## The park today

Fischer Creek Park covers 109 acres on the Lake Michigan shore near the Village of Cleveland, with access from CTH LS. It has a main parking lot and a lower lot, a picnic shelter (reservable), vault toilets, a beach access stairway, about 3.5 miles of trails along the bluff and Fischer Creek, and a fishing area at the creek mouth. The park is open 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. for day use. Annual visits are estimated at 85,000, about two-thirds of them May through September.

The last master plan was adopted in 2009. Most of its projects (shelter, stairway, parking) are done.

## What the 2026 draft proposes

### 1. North loop campground (2028 at the earliest)
- 60 sites with 30/50-amp electric service; 6 of them accessible; 8 walk-in tent sites as an option.
- Shower and restroom building with a dump station; camp host site.
- Gravel loop road on about 14 acres of mostly former farm field and young woods north of the main lot.
- A right-turn taper on CTH LS.
- Estimated cost: $2.4 million, paid from camping fees and the parks fund (borrowed internally and repaid over 12 years). No new levy.

### 2. Accessible kayak and canoe launch (2027)
- A hardened path from the lower lot and an accessible launch at the creek mouth.
- Estimated cost: $185,000; county parks fund plus donations already pledged by a local paddling club ($40,000).

### 3. Trail resurfacing and overlooks (2027-2028)
- Resurface 2.1 miles of the bluff trail and creek trail with crushed limestone.
- Two new overlooks with benches on the bluff.
- Estimated cost: $320,000.

## Natural resources

A wetland delineation was completed in July 2026. The campground loop is laid out to stay at least 75 feet from the delineated wetland. The plan keeps the bluff and creek corridor as natural area and adds invasive species control (buckthorn and honeysuckle) on 20 acres.

## Operations

Camping season May 1 to October 15. Quiet hours 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. Reservations online. Proposed fees: $35 per night (electric), $22 (tent), with a $5 discount for county residents. The park would remain open to day users at no charge.
