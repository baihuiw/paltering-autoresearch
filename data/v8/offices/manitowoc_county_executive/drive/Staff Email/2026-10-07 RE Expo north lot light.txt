Update on the north lot light, since we've had a couple of calls about it from the rummage sale crowd and Kelly said one came in to your office too.

- The county electrician was out Oct 2. Failed LED driver in the fixture, and when he opened the handhole he found the base connection damaged, probably from water getting in over the years. He made the base safe and capped it. Pole itself is fine.
- Replacement fixture ordered Oct 5 through the county's electrical supplier. They're quoting the week of Oct 26 for delivery. Electrician will reinstall the same day or the next once it's here, and redo the base connection then.

The rest of the north lot is lit, but N-4 is the one that covers the walk from the back rows to the rear gate, and that's the dark corner. Weekends we're fine (rummage sale is daytime, gun show is mostly daytime). My worry is the Ice Center opening on Friday the 23rd, then public skating Friday nights and youth hockey every weeknight. Parents park in the back rows when the front fills up.
