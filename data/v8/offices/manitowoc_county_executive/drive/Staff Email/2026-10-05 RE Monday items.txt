Joel,

First the bad one. The Tamarack Creek box culvert on CTH E was set Friday about 1.3 feet too high, because our survey crew tied the grades to the wrong survey control point, so it has to be lifted and reset, and with frost coming we won't be done until spring. Realistically the road stays closed until about May.

What happened: the crew picked up an old control point on the headwall of the culvert we were replacing instead of the project control point the designer set. The old headwall had settled. Nick's crew caught it this morning when they shot the inlet before backfilling. The DNR permit requires the bottom of the box buried below the streambed so fish can get through, and right now it sits above it. We can't leave it.

To fix it we have to pull the segments, dig out and rebuild the base, and reset them. The precast supplier's crane is booked into November, and once the ground freezes we can't get the base right. Nick and I agree the safe plan is to stabilize the site for winter, keep the road closed and redo it first thing in spring. I'll have a cost estimate for you by Wednesday and I'll report it to the Highway Committee on the 12th.

Other items:
- Salt: 9,200 tons in the dome as of Friday, about 80% of a normal winter. The rest is on order through the state contract.
- Winter crew: two LTE plow operators hired, start Nov 16.
- State highway maintenance (I-43 and the state routes): September billing to WisDOT went out, $412,600.
- Mailbox replacement policy for plow damage is unchanged from last year (we replace with a standard post and box, or pay $50 toward the owner's).

Tom
