Hi Kelly and Expo team,

Pinning down details for our walk-in flu clinic at the Expo safety day on Saturday, Oct 24, 9:00 a.m. to noon.

What we're bringing:
- 6 nurses plus 2 clerical staff for registration and insurance cards
- 350 adult doses and 150 pediatric doses, plus FluMist (nasal spray, ages 2-49) while supply lasts. Last year at safety day we gave 241 shots and nearly a third were kids, so I'm loading heavier on pediatric this time.
- Vaccines for Children stock for kids 18 and under on BadgerCare/Medicaid, uninsured or underinsured. No cost to them. Everyone else we bill to insurance. No one is turned away.

Reminders for anything you post:
- Everyone 6 months and older.
- A parent or guardian must come with children under 18.
- Children under 9 getting a flu shot for the first time need two doses four weeks apart. They can get the first one at safety day and the second at our Nov 12 clinic at Public Health (3:00-6:00 p.m.) or a Wednesday appointment (1:00-4:00 p.m., call (920) 683-4155).

Expo: we'd like the same spot as last year, the east end of the main exhibit building next to the restrooms, with 8 tables and 16 chairs, plus 2 outlets for the vaccine fridges. We'll arrive at 7:30 to set up. Can we have 4 parking spaces by the east door for our van and the cooler drop-off?

Thanks,
