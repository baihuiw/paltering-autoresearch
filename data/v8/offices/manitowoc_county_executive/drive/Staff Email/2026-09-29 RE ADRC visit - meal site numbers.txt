Joel,

Thank you for spending the morning with us and for staying through lunch at the Manitowoc site. The folks at your table are still talking about the hockey stories. As promised, here are the numbers you asked for. These are average meals served per day the site was open, January through August 2026, with the same months of 2025 for comparison.

Congregate meal sites (Manitowoc County)
- Manitowoc, Mon-Fri: 58 (2025: 55)
- Two Rivers, Mon-Fri: 41 (2025: 42)
- Kiel, Mon/Wed/Fri: 19 (2025: 14)
- Mishicot, Tue/Thu: 16 (2025: 15)
- Reedsville, Tue/Thu: 14 (2025: 13)
- Valders, Wed: 12 (2025: 12)

Home-delivered meals: 312 per weekday on average, up from 287. That's where most of our growth is, and it's where the volunteer driver shortage hurts the most. We have 9 routes that depend on volunteers and two of them are covered by staff more weeks than not.

Kiel is the one people ask about. It dipped to 11 a day in 2023 and there was talk then about cutting it to two days. The site manager and a core group of volunteers started calling every regular who missed a week, and it came back. I'd hate for anyone to read the 19 next to Manitowoc's 58 and draw the wrong conclusion.

Cost per congregate meal is about $11.40 all in (food, site staff, rent where we pay it). Participants give a suggested contribution of $4; the average actually given is $2.65. Federal Older Americans Act money and the county share cover the rest.

You asked what would help most. Honestly: volunteer drivers, and keeping the sites where they are. If there's ever a moment in a County Update or a talk where you can put in a word for volunteer drivers, we'd take it.
