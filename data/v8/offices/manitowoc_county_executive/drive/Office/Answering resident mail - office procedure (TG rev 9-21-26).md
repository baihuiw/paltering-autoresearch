# Answering resident mail - CE office procedure

Office of the County Executive | Rev. September 21, 2026 (TG) | replaces rev. 3-2025

*Updated for Joel's start. Main changes: Joel signs his own replies to opinion letters, and the referral list now has the fall items (flu clinics, energy assistance, Ice Center). Kelly, flag anything here that doesn't match how you actually do it and I'll fix it. -Tanya*

## 1. Where mail comes in

- **countyexecutive@manitowoccountywi.gov** - Kelly and Tanya both watch it. Kelly logs.
- **Website contact form** (County Executive page) - lands in the same mailbox with the dropdown topic in the subject line.
- **Letters** - opened by Kelly, date-stamped, scanned to the log folder. Originals in the green file until the item is closed.
- **Phone** - whoever answers takes name, municipality, callback number and the question. If it's a quick referral, give the number and log it anyway.
- **Mail addressed to Joel's own county email** - he forwards anything from residents to the office mailbox so it gets logged. He does not answer resident casework himself.

## 2. Timelines

- **Acknowledge within two business days.** Every resident gets a reply within two business days, even if the answer is "we've sent this to Highway and they'll be in touch."
- **Refer with a copy to the resident.** When we send something to a department, the resident is copied (or told by phone who will call and when).
- **Follow up at five business days** on anything still open. If the department hasn't answered, Kelly calls them, then tells the resident where it stands.
- **Close it in the log** with the date and one line on how it ended.

## 4. Standard phrases

- Acknowledgment: "Thank you for contacting the County Executive's office. I've shared your message with [department], and [name/staff] will be in touch with you by [day]. I've copied you on that message so you have it."
- Not a county matter: "That is handled by [office], not the county. You can reach them at [number]."
- Elected offices: "The [County Clerk/Treasurer/Register of Deeds] is a separately elected office. Their staff can help you directly at [number]."
- Opinions: "Thank you for sharing your views. County Executive Stahlberg reads these letters himself, and your comments have been shared with [department/your supervisor]."

Don't promise outcomes, dates or money on behalf of a department. Quote what the department says, not what we think it will do.

## 6. Things the office does not do

- **The office does not take positions on the special election** or on any candidate, and county email, letterhead, the newsletter and Facebook are not used for anything to do with it. If someone writes about the election, the reply is: "Questions about the November 17 special election should go to your municipal clerk or the County Clerk." Log it as "Other" and close it.
- We don't process passports, licenses or tax payments.
- We don't give legal advice or say whether someone has a claim.
- We don't share one resident's information with another resident.

## 7. The log

File: Office / Constituent mail log (KB working). One row per contact. Use first initial and surname only. Topic uses the website dropdown values. "Status" says Open or Closed and who has it; "Date closed" is blank until it's done. Kelly sends Tanya the open items every Friday by noon.
