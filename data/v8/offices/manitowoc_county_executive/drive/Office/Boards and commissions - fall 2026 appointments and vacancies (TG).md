## How appointments work

- The County Executive appoints members of most county boards and commissions. Appointments are **subject to confirmation by the County Board** (Wis. Stat. 59.17(2)(c) and the county code). Supervisor seats are appointed by the CE from among supervisors; citizen seats are open to any county resident unless the statute requires a qualification.
- **Applications to Tanya.** Residents fill out the one-page citizen application (on the County Executive page of the website, or we mail one) and return it to tanyagerlach@manitowoccountywi.gov or the office. Keep applications on file two years.
- Tanya sends Joel the applications for an opening with any recommendation from the department head and board chair. Joel interviews by phone if he wants.
- Tanya drafts the confirmation resolution; it goes to the County Board on the consent agenda. New members start at the next meeting after confirmation.
- Terms below are as set by statute or ordinance. Members serve until a successor is confirmed.
