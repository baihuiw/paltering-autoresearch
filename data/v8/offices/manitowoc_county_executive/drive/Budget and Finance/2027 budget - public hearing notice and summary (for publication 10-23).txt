NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING
MANITOWOC COUNTY 2027 PROPOSED BUDGET

NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Manitowoc County Board of Supervisors will hold a public hearing
on the proposed 2027 budget of Manitowoc County on MONDAY, NOVEMBER 9, 2026, at 6:00 P.M., in the
County Board Meeting Room of the Heritage Center, 1701 Michigan Avenue, Manitowoc, Wisconsin.

The annual meeting of the County Board will follow the public hearing. The County Board may amend
and adopt the 2027 budget and set the 2027 property tax levy at that meeting.

This notice is published under Section 65.90 of the Wisconsin Statutes. The summary below shows
the proposed budget as recommended by the County Executive on October 1, 2026, with changes
made by the Finance Committee during its review of October 5-7, 2026 (none affecting the totals).

A detailed copy of the proposed budget is available for public inspection:
- on the county website, manitowoccountywi.gov (Finance page);
- at the office of the County Clerk, Courthouse, 1010 S. 8th Street, Manitowoc, Monday through
  Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.;
- at the Manitowoc Public Library, the Lester Public Library in Two Rivers and the Kiel Public
  Library during regular hours.

Any resident or taxpayer of Manitowoc County may speak at the public hearing. Speakers are asked
to fill out a speaker card at the door before 6:00 p.m. Written comments may be sent to the
Manitowoc County Clerk, 1010 S. 8th Street, Manitowoc, WI 54220, or to
countyclerk@manitowoccountywi.gov, and must be received by Friday, November 6, 2026, at
4:30 p.m. Written comments will be provided to all members of the County Board.

The proposed levy is within the limit under Section 66.0602 of the Wisconsin Statutes. The
proposed budget does not use General Fund balance. General obligation debt outstanding on
January 1, 2027 is estimated at $24.6 million. Planned 2027 borrowing for the highway program is
included in the Debt Service and Highway figures above.

On a home with an equalized value of $200,000, the estimated county portion of the property tax
bill would be about $818 in 2027, compared with about $836 in 2026. Actual tax bills depend on
each municipality's assessment ratio and on the levies of the school district, technical
college, municipality and other taxing units.
