MANITOWOC COUNTY BOARD OF HEALTH
MINUTES - THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 10, 2026, 4:00 P.M.
Public Health, Conference Room A, 1028 S. 9th St., Manitowoc

DRAFT - not yet approved by the Board

Present: Supervisor Sue Kinjerski (Chair), Supervisor Dawn Kronforst, Supervisor Bill Rusch, Dr. Karen Lindstrom (physician member), Julie Strohmeyer, RN, Tom Wiegand (citizen member)
Excused: none
Also present: Nicole Fessenden, Health Officer; the Environmental Health Supervisor; the Public Health Nursing Supervisor; one member of the public

1. Call to order. Chair Kinjerski called the meeting to order at 4:01 p.m. Notice was posted September 4.

2. Minutes of August 13, 2026. Motion by Rusch, second by Wiegand, to approve as printed. Carried 6-0.

3. Public comment. A Town of Gibson resident thanked staff for help with a well sample after flooding in July and asked whether the county could offer free nitrate screening again next spring. Fessenden said staff would look at a spring screening day and report back.

4. Health Officer's report. Fessenden reported:
   a. Staffing. The department has 31.6 FTE. One public health nurse position is vacant (posted August 31). The summer beach monitoring LTE finished September 4.
   b. County Board Chair Zastrow is acting county executive; his appointment of Joel Stahlberg goes to the County Board for confirmation on September 15. The 2027 budget request went to Finance on August 28 on the normal schedule.
   c. The department completed its annual emergency preparedness drill with Emergency Management on August 20 (point-of-dispensing setup at the Expo grounds, 48 minutes to open).
   d. School-entry immunizations: staff held two catch-up clinics in August (71 children seen). School compliance reports are due to the state in November.
   Received and placed on file.

5. Environmental health: food, pool and lodging inspections, June-August.
   Restaurants and retail food: 247 routine inspections, 31 re-inspections, 2 pre-opening inspections. Three establishments needed a second re-inspection; all are now in compliance.
   Public pools and water attractions: 38 inspections at 21 facilities; two pools closed temporarily for chlorine levels and reopened the same week.
   Lodging and tourist rooming houses: 29 inspections.
   Complaints received (food and housing): 18, all investigated.
   Supervisor Kronforst asked about the backlog of new tourist rooming house licenses. The Environmental Health Supervisor said there are 6 pending, down from 14 in June.

6. Private well water testing, June-August.
   Samples analyzed for residents: 168
   Total coliform bacteria present: 19 (11%)
   E. coli present: 3 (all three wells disinfected and resampled clear)
   Nitrate above 10 mg/L (drinking water standard): 12
   Staff mailed a fact sheet with each unsafe result and followed up by phone. Most nitrate results above the standard were in the southwest part of the county, the same pattern as past years. Dr. Ebert asked that the fall County Update carry a reminder to have private wells sampled once a year; Fessenden will send the item to the County Executive's office.

7. Beach monitoring, 2026 season wrap-up (Memorial Day weekend to Labor Day).
   Beaches monitored: 9 (6 Lake Michigan, 3 inland)
   Samples collected: 214
   Advisories posted (E. coli above the advisory level): 17
   Closures (above the closure level): 2, both after heavy rain in July, each lasting one day
   Funding from the state beach grant covered the LTE and lab costs. The 2027 grant application is due in January. Received and placed on file.

8. Fall flu clinic plan. The Nursing Supervisor presented the draft schedule: early clinics at Public Health on October 6 and 13; community walk-in clinics October 22 through November 12, including Two Rivers, Kiel, Mishicot and the Expo safety day on October 24; and Wednesday afternoon appointments at Public Health. Children 18 and under are covered through Vaccines for Children if on BadgerCare, uninsured or underinsured. Supervisor Rusch asked whether Kiel could be held at city hall instead of the library for parking; staff will check. Final schedule to be posted by October 2.

9. 2027 budget request. Fessenden reviewed the request submitted August 28: total expenditures $3,551,000 (2026: $3,462,000), levy request $1,768,000 (2026: $1,714,000). Increases are wages and benefits under the pay plan and the state lab fee increase for water samples; grant revenue is flat. No new positions. Motion by Kronforst, second by Strohmeyer, to support the request as submitted. Carried 6-0.

10. Communicable disease report, second quarter. Received and placed on file (general categories only; no case details).

11. Next meeting: Thursday, October 8, 2026, 4:00 p.m.

12. Adjourn at 5:12 p.m. Motion by Wiegand, second by Rusch. Carried 6-0.

Recorded by Public Health administrative staff.
