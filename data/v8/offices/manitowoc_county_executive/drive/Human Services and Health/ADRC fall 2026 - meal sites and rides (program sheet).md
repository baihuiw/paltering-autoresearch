# ADRC of the Lakeshore - Manitowoc County Programs, Fall 2026

**Meal sites, home-delivered meals, rides and help with benefits.** Program sheet for staff, partners and the public. Updated September 2026.

ADRC of the Lakeshore (Aging and Disability Resource Center), serving Manitowoc and Kewaunee counties.
**Call (877) 416-7083** (toll-free), Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. After hours, leave a message; we call back the next business day.
Manitowoc County office: 926 S. 8th St., Manitowoc (Human Services building, first floor).

## Congregate meal sites (Manitowoc County)

Hot noon meal for adults 60 and older and their spouses. Meals are served at 11:30 a.m. unless noted.
**Reserve by noon the business day before** by calling the site manager or the ADRC.

| Community | Site | Days |
|---|---|---|
| Manitowoc | Senior center dining room | Monday-Friday |
| Two Rivers | Community center | Monday-Friday |
| Kiel | Community room, apartments for seniors | Monday, Wednesday, Friday |
| Mishicot | Village hall lower level | Tuesday, Thursday |
| Valders | Village community room | Wednesday (11:45 a.m.) |
| Reedsville | Legion hall | Tuesday, Thursday |

- **Suggested contribution:** $4.00 per meal for people 60 and older. No one 60+ is turned away if they can't contribute, and contributions are confidential.
- **Guests under 60:** $11.50 (about the full cost of the meal, which runs $11.40 with food, site staff and rent).
- All sites stay open in 2027 on their current days (the County Executive's recommended budget keeps every congregate site open).
- Sites close when Manitowoc County schools close for weather. Listen for the school closing announcements or call the ADRC.

## Home-delivered meals

- For people 60+ who are homebound and can't prepare meals. An ADRC staff person does a short home visit first to set it up.
- Hot meals delivered at midday Monday-Friday in Manitowoc, Two Rivers and Kiel; frozen meals delivered once a week in the rest of the county.
- Weekend frozen meals available on request.
- Same suggested contribution, $4.00 per meal.
- Special diets: diabetic and soft-texture options. Tell us about allergies when you sign up.
- **Volunteer drivers needed.** Nine delivery routes depend on volunteers. An hour or two, one day a week; mileage is reimbursed. Call (877) 416-7083.

## Rides (specialized transportation)

For adults 60+ and people with disabilities of any age. Wheelchair-accessible vans and volunteer drivers.

| Trip | Fare (each way) |
|---|---|
| Within Manitowoc or Two Rivers | $3.00 |
| Elsewhere in Manitowoc County | $5.00 |
| Medical appointments out of county (Green Bay, Sheboygan, Appleton) | $12.00 |
| Medical appointments, Milwaukee VA (Tuesdays and Thursdays only) | $15.00 |

- **Call at least 2 business days ahead** to schedule; out-of-county trips need 5 business days.
- Rides run Monday-Friday, 7:30 a.m.-4:30 p.m. No weekend rides.
- A personal care attendant rides free.
- Veterans: the Veterans Service Office, (920) 683-4056, can help with VA travel questions.

## Options counseling

ADRC specialists help older adults, people with disabilities and families sort out choices: in-home help, assisted living, nursing homes, paying for care, Family Care and IRIS enrollment, caregiver support. Free and confidential. Home visits available.

## Benefit specialists

- **Elder benefit specialist** (60+): Medicare, Medicare Part D, Medicaid, Social Security, FoodShare, appeals.
- **Disability benefit specialist** (18-59): SSI, SSDI, Medicaid, work incentives.
- **Medicare open enrollment is October 15 to December 7.** Make an appointment early for a Part D plan review; the October slots fill fast.

## Energy assistance help

The ADRC helps older adults and people with disabilities apply for energy assistance (WHEAP), which opened October 1. We can do the application with you by phone, help gather the income and bill copies, and mail them to Human Services. Or call the county energy assistance line directly at (920) 683-4237. Crisis help (disconnect notice or out of fuel) is available year-round.
