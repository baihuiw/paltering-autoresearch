# Peer recovery coach program - program sheet and 2026 numbers

Manitowoc County Human Services, Behavioral Health unit. Prepared for the Human Services Board and the front desk. Rev. October 2, 2026 (Sandra Lohrey, Director).

## What it is
Peer recovery coaches are people in long-term recovery who have completed state peer specialist training. In Manitowoc County they meet people at the hospital after an overdose or a crisis and keep working with them for up to six months: rides to appointments, help getting into treatment, help with housing and job applications, and a regular check-in. Coaching is voluntary and free to the person.

The county started the program in March 2025. Coaches are employed by Wolf Hollow Peer Alliance, a nonprofit, under an agreement with the county. The agreement is paid from Fund 275 (Opioid Settlement Fund), under Resolution 2025/2026-17.

## Referral and contact
- Hospital emergency department staff, the Joint Dispatch Center's co-responder follow-up list, the jail, and self-referral.
- Intake line: (920) 683-4230, ask for Behavioral Health.
- Coaches work Monday through Saturday. They carry a case load of about 20 each.

## The agreement
- Parties: Manitowoc County and Wolf Hollow Peer Alliance (director Rob Dettmann).
- Term: January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026. A renewal for 2027 would be by written agreement of both parties.
- Annual amount: $198,000, billed monthly ($16,500).
- Reporting: monthly numbers to Human Services; quarterly to the Human Services Board.
