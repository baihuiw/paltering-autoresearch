Manitowoc County Executive's Office
Received September 14, 2026 (handwritten letter, scanned)

Dear County Executive,

I am writing to the county because I don't know who else to thank. In June my brother Alan was taken to the hospital in Manitowoc after an overdose. A woman named Marcy Teske, who works for the county's recovery coach program, was in the waiting room when I got there. She has been with him since, driving him to appointments in Green Bay and sitting with him the nights it got bad. He is 91 days clean as of last Saturday.

I did not know the county had a program like this. I only learned about it because a nurse mentioned it. I would like more people to know. If it costs money, it is worth every dollar.

Sincerely,
Janet Geiger
Two Rivers
