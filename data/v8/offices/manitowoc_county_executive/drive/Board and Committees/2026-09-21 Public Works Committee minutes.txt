MANITOWOC COUNTY PUBLIC WORKS COMMITTEE
MINUTES - MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 2026, 8:30 A.M.
Administration Building, Room 2A, 1110 S. 9th St., Manitowoc

Present: Supervisors Dennis Oswald (Chair), Carol Ziemer, Jeff Hupfer, Pat Rehrauer, Lyle Brockman
Also present: Brian Hornacek, Public Works Director; Gary Nehls, Recycling Center Manager; Linda Kuehl, Public Works; Tanya Gerlach, County Executive's office; two members of the public

1. Call to order. Chair Oswald called the meeting to order at 8:30 a.m. Notice of the meeting was posted in compliance with the open meetings law.

2. Minutes of September 9, 2026. Motion by Ziemer, second by Rehrauer, to approve as presented. Carried 5-0.

3. English Lake Management District request. The district asked to use the county-owned landing on the north shore of English Lake to launch its weed harvester three days a week through October 15. Hornacek said the landing is not otherwise used in the fall and the district carries its own insurance. Motion by Brockman, second by Hupfer, to approve a no-cost use agreement through October 15, 2026, with the district to repair any damage to the landing. Carried 5-0.

4. Recycling Center report (August). Nehls reviewed tonnage and revenue for August. Inbound 871 tons, residue 7.6%. Clean Sweep is set for Saturday, September 19 at the Expo grounds. Received and placed on file.

5. Tax-deeded property: fall sealed-bid list. Kuehl presented seven tax-deeded parcels recommended for the fall sealed-bid sale, with appraisals as minimum bids, bids due October 26. The list includes four vacant lots in the City of Two Rivers, a former tavern building in the Town of Cooperstown, a wooded parcel in the Town of Rockland and a 0.2-acre strip without frontage in the Town of Newton. Motion by Rehrauer, second by Ziemer, to approve the list and authorize advertising. Carried 5-0.

6. Tax-deeded parcel 010-114-003-02, 7311 English Lake Rd, Town of Newton. Hornacek said the parcel, a vacant lakefront lot of about 0.6 acre taken by tax deed in 2025, was appraised on September 10, 2026, and that the County Executive's office had asked that it be handled as a direct sale to a buyer who had approached the county rather than placed on the fall list. Delinquent taxes, interest and costs on the parcel total $9,860. Motion by Hupfer, second by Brockman, to authorize the direct sale of parcel 010-114-003-02 to DK Lake Properties LLC for $38,000 without advertising for bids, and to authorize the Public Works Director to sign the closing documents. Supervisor Ziemer asked why the parcel was not on the sealed-bid list and what it was appraised at. Hornacek said the appraisal was $71,500 and that the request came from the County Executive's office. Supervisor Rehrauer said lakefront lots usually draw several bids and that adjoining owners should have a chance to bid. Ms. Gerlach said the County Executive wanted the parcel back on the tax roll this year. Roll call: Oswald yes, Hupfer yes, Brockman yes, Ziemer no, Rehrauer no. Carried 3-2.

7. Courthouse elevator modernization. Hornacek reported the contractor expects the state inspection the week of September 28. One car remains in service.

8. Expo grounds. The fall rummage sale (Oct 3) and the Lakeshore Gun Show (Nov 7-8) are booked. Ice Center refrigeration startup is scheduled for October 12 with opening October 23. The Expo-Ice Center Board meets October 13.

9. Snow removal contract, county buildings. Discussion of renewing the current contract for the Courthouse, Administration Building, Human Services and Public Health lots at the same rates for 2026-27. Motion by Ziemer, second by Oswald, to recommend renewal. Carried 5-0.

10. 2027 Public Works budget. Hornacek reviewed the department's requests as submitted to the County Executive: building maintenance, the recycling center skid steer and tip floor roof panels, and Expo parking lot sealcoating. The County Executive's recommended budget will be released October 1.

11. Public comment. None.

12. Next meeting: Monday, October 5, 2026, 8:30 a.m.

13. Adjourn. Motion by Rehrauer, second by Hupfer, to adjourn at 9:42 a.m. Carried 5-0.

Recorded by Linda Kuehl, Public Works Department
Minutes are not final until approved by the committee.
