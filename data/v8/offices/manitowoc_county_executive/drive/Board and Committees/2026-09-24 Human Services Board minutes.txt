MANITOWOC COUNTY HUMAN SERVICES BOARD
MINUTES - THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 24, 2026, 4:00 P.M.
Human Services, Lower Level Training Room, 926 S. 8th St., Manitowoc

Present: Supervisor Janet Duvall (Chair), Supervisor Mike Tesch, Supervisor Tom Rappel, Mary Pfeffer (citizen member), Greg Schroeder (citizen member), Lisa Wachowiak (citizen member)
Excused: Paul Hennig (citizen member)
Also present: Sandra Lohrey, Human Services Director; the Children and Families Manager; the Behavioral Health Manager; the Economic Support Supervisor; Renee Stoeger, Economic Support; Kelly Brandl, County Executive's office

1. Call to order. Chair Duvall called the meeting to order at 4:00 p.m. Notice posted September 18.

2. Minutes of August 27, 2026. Supervisor Tesch noted that item 6 should read "eleven emergency detentions," not "seven." Motion by Tesch, second by Pfeffer, to approve as corrected. Carried 6-0.

3. Public comment. None.

4. Director's report. Lohrey presented the September monthly report (through September 20). Highlights:
   a. Economic Support: 4,812 FoodShare households; 9,436 health care cases. Applications and renewals processed: 1,128.
   b. Children and Families: 142 CPS reports, 38 screened in and 104 screened out. 72 children in out-of-home care. Nine initial assessments are behind the state timeline because of the two vacant initial assessment positions.
   c. Behavioral Health: outpatient waitlist down to 37 from 46; average wait 24 days. 211 crisis contacts.
   d. Adult Protective Services: 36 reports, 21 investigations opened.
   Supervisor Rappel asked whether the outpatient waitlist would keep falling. The Behavioral Health Manager said it depends on filling the therapist vacancy; the contracted evening hours run through December. Citizen member Wachowiak asked about the screened-out CPS reports; the Children and Families Manager explained that 47 of the 104 were referred to community services such as parenting programs or school social workers. Received and placed on file.

5. Energy assistance season. Stoeger reviewed the plans for the season opening Thursday, October 1 (through May 15, 2027): phone appointments on the energy line, (920) 683-4237; walk-in intake Monday-Thursday, 8:30-11:30 a.m.; online applications through the state; and phone-and-mail applications for older adults and homebound residents with ADRC help. Crisis help for disconnect notices and fuel emergencies is year-round. Two LTE intake workers start September 28 if the Personnel Committee approves them that afternoon. Chair Duvall asked that the County Executive's office include the energy assistance number in the next County Update. Brandl said it would go in the October 1 issue.

6. Staffing. Seven vacancies (two CPS initial assessment, one ES specialist with a start date of October 19, one outpatient therapist, one crisis worker, one psychiatric nurse, one APS social worker effective September 30). Lohrey said the department is trying a signing incentive for the psychiatric nurse position, which needs Personnel Committee approval. Supervisor Tesch, who also sits on Personnel, said he expected that to be on the October 27 agenda.

7. Human Services building roof. Lohrey reported the September 11 leak over the second-floor records room. No records were damaged. Public Works has the roof replacement in its 2027 request at an estimated $485,000. Supervisor Rappel asked whether the work could be done with staff in the building. Lohrey said Public Works plans a summer 2027 project in sections, with records moved out of the second floor beforehand.

8. 2027 budget. Lohrey said the department's request is 3.1% above 2026, mostly children's long-term support and crisis services, with most of the increase covered by state and federal revenue. The County Executive's recommended budget will be released October 1; the Finance Committee will review Human Services on October 6. Members were invited to attend.

9. Children's Long-Term Support update. Enrollment 318 with no waitlist. The state's new rate schedule for CLTS providers takes effect January 1; the department will send provider letters in November.

10. Contracts. Motion by Rappel, second by Schroeder, to approve the renewal of the agreement for crisis bed services with a regional provider for 2027 at the same daily rate as 2026. Carried 6-0.

11. Board appointments. Citizen member Paul Hennig's term ends in October; he has asked to be reappointed. The County Executive's office will forward the reappointment to the County Board for October 20.

12. Next meeting: Thursday, October 22, 2026, 4:00 p.m.

13. Adjourn at 5:21 p.m. Motion by Pfeffer, second by Tesch. Carried 6-0.

Recorded by Human Services administrative staff.
