MANITOWOC COUNTY FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES - 2027 BUDGET REVIEW, DAY 3
WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 7, 2026, 6:00 P.M.
Heritage Center, County Board Meeting Room, 1701 Michigan Ave., Manitowoc

Present: Supervisors Pat Rehrauer (Chair), Gary Pritzl, Ann Leiterman, Rick Gerds, Bill Rusch
Also present: Joel Stahlberg, County Executive; Mark Esslinger, Finance Director; Tanya Gerlach, County Executive's office; department heads and staff as listed under each item; one member of the public

1. Call to order at 6:00 p.m. by Chair Rehrauer.

2. Public comment. None.

3. Sheriff. A Sheriff's Office captain and the jail administrator.
   Patrol and investigations: 2026 adopted levy $5,989,000; 2027 recommended $6,027,000 (+0.6%). The traffic safety grant overtime is not on the levy. Jail and transport: $5,390,000 to $5,430,000 (+0.7%), based on an average daily population of 190. The jail medical contract rises $322,400 under the new agreement. Esslinger said the recommended budget offsets $320,000 of that with a transfer from Fund 275, shown on the Fund 275 schedule handed out tonight (a copy is on file with the Finance Department). Supervisor Gerds asked whether the average daily population of 190 was realistic; the jail administrator said it has run between 181 and 196 this year. No amendments proposed.

4. Public Works and Facilities. Brian Hornacek, Public Works Director.
   Facilities: Courthouse and Administration Building levy $1,405,000 to $1,420,000. Courthouse window replacement phase 2 and the Human Services building roof ($485,000) are in the capital plan, not the levy. Supervisor Rusch asked whether the roof could be bid in the winter; Hornacek said yes, with work in June and July. No amendments proposed.

5. Highway. Tom Kracht, Highway Commissioner.
   Levy $4,820,000 to $4,850,000 (+0.6%). Resurfacing of about 14 miles, a deck overlay on the CTH Q bridge and two tandem plow trucks. Kracht noted that the department trimmed $40,000 from its request after the County Executive's review. No amendments proposed.

6. Expo and Ice Center. The Expo facility manager.
   Levy held at $198,000. Parking lot sealcoating and the dasher board system are paid from Expo revenues and the youth hockey association's $60,000 pledge. No amendments proposed.

7. General government and debt service. Esslinger reviewed the County Board, County Executive, Finance, Corporation Counsel, Personnel and information systems lines and debt service, which rises $190,000 with the first payment on the 2026 highway notes. No amendments proposed.

8. Summary. Esslinger said the recommended levy of $42.31 million stands as presented. The committee will report to the County Board that it reviewed all departments on October 5, 6 and 7 and proposed no amendments. Amendments may still be offered at the November 9 hearing and annual meeting.

9. Adjourned at 8:31 p.m. Motion by Pritzl, second by Rusch. Carried 5-0.

Recorded by Finance Department staff.
