MANITOWOC COUNTY EXPO-ICE CENTER BOARD
MINUTES - TUESDAY, OCTOBER 13, 2026, 6:00 P.M.
Expo Office, 4921 Expo Dr., Manitowoc

DRAFT - subject to approval at the November meeting

Present: Supervisor Jeff Hupfer (Chair), Supervisor Dawn Kronforst, Supervisor Ken Wilda, Gail Ruppel (citizen member), Scott Hammen (citizen member), Brad Lensmire (youth hockey association representative)
Also present: Joel Stahlberg, County Executive; Brian Hornacek, Public Works Director; the Expo facility manager; the Ice Center rink supervisor

1. Call to order at 6:00 p.m. by Chair Hupfer. Chair Hupfer welcomed County Executive Stahlberg, attending his first meeting of the board.

2. Minutes of September 8, 2026. Motion by Ruppel, second by Wilda, to approve. Carried 6-0.

3. Public comment. A figure skating club parent asked whether the club could get one more weekday morning slot in January. Referred to the rink supervisor.

4. Ice Center opening. The rink supervisor reported that the ice was ready on October 6 and staff skated it that afternoon. Youth hockey fall tryouts are October 14-15 and practices begin the following week. Public skating opens Friday, October 23. Opening night will include a youth game at 6:00 p.m., before the public session.

   2026-27 public skating schedule, from October 23:
   - Fridays 7:00-8:30 p.m.
   - Saturdays 1:00-2:30 p.m.
   - Sundays 1:00-2:30 p.m. and 3:00-4:30 p.m.
   - Adult skate Wednesdays 11:30 a.m.-1:00 p.m.
   - Extra holiday sessions December 26 through January 3 (times to be posted by December 1).
   - Learn-to-skate Saturdays 10:00-10:45 a.m., six weeks starting November 7, $60. The first session is full at 60; the Expo office is keeping a waiting list for the January session.
   The schedule can change for tournaments; changes are posted on the Expo page and the Expo office takes calls at (920) 683-4378.

5. Fees reminder. The fees adopted by this board in April 2026 take effect with this season, the first change since 2019 (previously $5 adults, $3 children):
   - Adults 13-61: $6
   - Children 12 and under: $4
   - Seniors 62 and older: $4
   - Children 3 and under: free with a paying adult
   - Skate rental: $4
   - 10-visit punch card: $50
   Hammen asked that the new fees be posted at the window and on the website before opening night so there are no surprises at the counter. The facility manager said the signs are printed and the website was updated October 9. Old punch cards will be honored through the end of the season.

6. Dasher board replacement. Hornacek reported that the new dasher board and glazing system is in the 2027 recommended budget. Estimated project cost $168,000: the youth hockey association's pledge of $60,000 and $108,000 from Expo revenues. No levy. If the budget is adopted November 9, Public Works will bid it in January and install in June and July 2027, when there is no ice. Lensmire said the association has $41,500 of the pledge in hand and expects the rest by March. Supervisor Kronforst asked that the pledge be documented in a written agreement before bidding; Hornacek said Corporation Counsel is preparing one for the board's December meeting. County Executive Stahlberg said he had coached in the association for years and would leave the agreement to the board and Public Works.

7. Expo events. The facility manager reported:
   - Indoor rummage sale Saturday, October 3: 64 vendors, 1,340 shoppers counted at the door.
   - Fall gun and sportsman's show October 10-11: 212 tables, about 3,400 paid admissions.
   - Indoor rummage sale Saturday, October 17: 58 vendors booked.
   - Expo safety day Saturday, October 24, 9:00 a.m.-noon, with the Public Health flu clinic, car seat checks and Emergency Management. Free.
   - Holiday craft fair November 7-8: 150 vendors, sold out. Learn-to-skate that Saturday will use the Ice Center entrance.
   Rental revenue through September is 4% ahead of 2025.

8. North parking lot lighting. The facility manager reported that light pole N-4, at the north end of the lot by the rear gate, has been out since about September 30. The county's maintenance electrician found a failed LED driver and a damaged base connection on October 2. A replacement fixture was ordered October 5 and is expected the week of October 26. Until it is installed, the Highway Department will set a portable light tower near the rear gate for evening events, starting Friday, October 23. Supervisor Wilda asked that staff check the other north lot poles while the electrician is on site; the facility manager said all other poles were checked October 2 and are working.

9. Parking lot sealcoating. Hornacek said sealcoating and restriping of the main and north lots is in the 2027 recommended budget, paid from Expo revenues, for summer 2027.

10. Ice plant. The compressor overhaul done in August is holding temperature well; brine readings normal.

11. Next meeting: Tuesday, November 10, 2026, 6:00 p.m.

12. Adjourn at 7:04 p.m. Motion by Kronforst, second by Ruppel. Carried 6-0.

Recorded by Expo office staff.
