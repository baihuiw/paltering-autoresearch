# CTH E at Tamarack Creek - culvert replacement

Manitowoc County Highway Department. Project sheet for the Highway Committee, the County Executive's office and the front desk. Rev. September 2026.

## Location
CTH E between Hillcrest Road and Kassel Road, Town of Kossuth. Tamarack Creek crosses under the road about 0.4 mile west of Kassel Road.

## Why
- Two 72-inch corrugated metal pipes, installed in 1971, are rusted through at the bottom and the road above has settled.
- The pipes are too small. CTH E flooded at Tamarack Creek and was closed four times in the past ten years.
- The pipes are perched above the streambed at the outlet, so fish can't move upstream.

## What
- Remove both pipes and replace them with one 12-foot by 6-foot precast concrete box culvert, 56 feet long, with wingwalls.
- Box bottom buried below the natural streambed with a stone bed inside, as required by the state waterway permit, so fish can pass.
- Raise the road about 1 foot over the culvert; new guardrail; new pavement for 300 feet.

## Who
- Design: county Highway engineering technician with a consulting engineer for the structure.
- Construction: Highway Department crews (county forces). Precast box segments from a supplier, set by the supplier's crane.
- Gas line relocation: the gas utility, before excavation.

## Budget
$286,000 (county highway fund): precast box and wingwalls $142,000; stone, pavement and guardrail $61,000; crane $18,000; county labor and equipment $55,000; design and permit $10,000.

## Schedule (as of Sept 25)
- Road closed: Monday, August 24.
- Gas line relocation: planned Sept 8-11; finished Sept 18.
- Base preparation: Sept 21-30.
- Box set: early October.
- Backfill, road and guardrail: October.
- Reopen: early November, weather permitting.

## Detour
Posted detour via the county and town roads to the north, about 9 miles. School buses and milk trucks have been notified. Local traffic only up to the closure from each side.
