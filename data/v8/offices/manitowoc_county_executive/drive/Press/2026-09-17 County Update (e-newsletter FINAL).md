## Stahlberg sworn in

Joel Stahlberg was sworn in as Manitowoc County Executive on Wednesday, September 16, at the Courthouse. The County Clerk administered the oath before family, supervisors and county staff. County Board Chair Ron Zastrow appointed Stahlberg on September 8, and the County Board confirmed the appointment on September 15. Chair Zastrow served as acting county executive from September 1 until the oath.

Stahlberg retired last year after 31 years as a plant controller for a Manitowoc manufacturer and has coached youth hockey at the county Ice Center for more than 15 years. He lives in the Town of Manitowoc with his wife, Lynn.

The County Executive's office is on the first floor of the Courthouse, 1010 S. 8th St., open Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. Executive Assistant Tanya Gerlach and Communications Coordinator Kelly Brandl continue in the office.

## Clean Sweep is this Saturday

The fall Clean Sweep household hazardous waste collection is **Saturday, September 19, 8:00 a.m.-noon, at the Expo grounds, 4921 Expo Dr.** It's free for Manitowoc County households. Stay in your vehicle; staff will unload for you.

**Accepted:** oil-based paint and stain, solvents and thinners, pesticides and herbicides, pool chemicals, household cleaners, aerosol cans, fluorescent bulbs, mercury thermometers, household batteries and rechargeable batteries, gasoline and fuel mixtures.

**Not accepted:** latex paint (dry it out and put it in the trash), electronics, tires, appliances, medications, ammunition and explosives, business waste. Keep products in their original containers if possible, and don't mix chemicals.

Questions: Recycling Center, (920) 683-4333.

## ADRC: help for older adults and caregivers

The Aging and Disability Resource Center (ADRC) of the Lakeshore is the place to start for older adults, people with disabilities and the people who care for them. Staff can help with Medicare questions (open enrollment starts October 15), home-delivered and congregate meals, transportation to medical appointments, caregiver support and finding in-home help. Call **(877) 416-7083**. The service is free.

## Holiday travel? Plan ahead for passports

If you're traveling abroad over the holidays, apply for your passport now. The County Clerk's office in the Courthouse accepts passport applications by appointment, Monday-Friday, 9:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m. Call **(920) 683-4004** to schedule. Routine processing takes about 4-6 weeks; expedited service is available for an extra fee.

What to bring: proof of citizenship (a certified birth certificate or old passport), a photo ID, the federal fee by check to the U.S. Department of State, and the county's $35 acceptance fee. Photos can be taken at the Clerk's office for $15. Children under 16 need both parents present (or one parent with the other's notarized consent form).
