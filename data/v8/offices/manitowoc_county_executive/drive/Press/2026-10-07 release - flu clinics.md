## Public Health announces fall flu shot clinics around the county

MANITOWOC - Manitowoc County Public Health will hold walk-in flu shot clinics in Manitowoc, Two Rivers, Kiel and Mishicot this fall, including a Saturday morning clinic at the Expo grounds during the Expo safety day on October 24.

The clinics are open to everyone 6 months and older. No appointment is needed.

"The best time to get a flu shot is before flu starts spreading in our community, and that usually means by the end of October," said Health Officer Nicole Fessenden. "We've added evening and Saturday clinics so working families can come after school or on the weekend."

Flu shots are also available **by appointment on Wednesdays, 1:00-4:00 p.m.**, at Public Health. Call (920) 683-4155 to schedule.

**Cost**

- Children 18 and under who are on BadgerCare Plus or Medicaid, have no health insurance, or whose insurance does not cover vaccines can get a flu shot at no cost through the Vaccines for Children program.
- Adults and children with insurance: Public Health will bill your insurance, including Medicare Part B and most private plans. Please bring your insurance card.
- No one will be turned away because they cannot pay.

**What to know before you come**

- A parent or legal guardian must come with children under 18.
- Children under 9 who are getting a flu shot for the first time need two doses, given four weeks apart. Parents can schedule the second dose at the first visit.
- FluMist, the nasal spray flu vaccine, is available for healthy people ages 2 through 49 while supply lasts. Ask the nurse whether it is right for you or your child.
- Wear a short-sleeved shirt or one with sleeves that roll up easily.
- People who are sick with a fever should wait until they are well.
- If you have had a severe allergic reaction to a flu vaccine in the past, talk with your doctor first.

The Expo safety day on October 24 runs from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and includes fire and rescue equipment displays, car seat checks and home safety information. The flu clinic will be in the Main Hall.

Flu shots are also available at many pharmacies and clinics. People who get their shot elsewhere do not need to tell Public Health.

For more information, visit the Public Health page at manitowoccountywi.gov or call (920) 683-4155, Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m.
