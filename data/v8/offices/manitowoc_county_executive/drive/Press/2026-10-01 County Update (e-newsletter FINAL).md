## 2027 recommended budget released

The County Executive's 2027 recommended budget totals $131.9 million, up 2.7% from 2026. The property tax levy would rise 1.7%, from $41.62 million to $42.31 million, but because property values grew faster, the estimated county tax rate drops from $4.18 to $4.09 per $1,000 of equalized value. On a $200,000 home, the county portion of the tax bill would be about $818, roughly $18 less than this year.

The budget adds no new levy-funded positions and keeps the general fund balance at about 22% of spending. It includes road resurfacing on about 14 miles of county highways and two new plow trucks, the Courthouse window project (phase 2), a new roof on the Human Services building, and parking lot and dasher board work at the Expo grounds and Ice Center.

The full budget and the budget message are on the county website. Printed copies are at the County Clerk's office and the Manitowoc, Two Rivers and Kiel libraries. The Finance Committee reviews department budgets October 5-7 at 6:00 p.m. at the Heritage Center, and those meetings are open to the public.

**Public hearing:** Monday, November 9, 6:00 p.m., Heritage Center, 1701 Michigan Ave., Manitowoc.

## Energy assistance applications open today

Applications for help with this winter's heating bills open today, October 1, and run through May 15, 2027. The Wisconsin Home Energy Assistance Program (WHEAP) pays a one-time benefit directly to your utility or fuel supplier. Households facing a disconnect notice or running out of fuel can get crisis help year-round.

To apply, call Manitowoc County Human Services at **(920) 683-4237** for a phone appointment, or stop in at 926 S. 8th St. (walk-in hours Monday-Thursday, 8:30-11:30 a.m.). Older adults and people who are homebound can do the whole application by phone and mail. Have your last three months of income, a recent heating bill and electric bill, and Social Security numbers for your household ready.

## Ice Center opens October 23

Public skating at the county Ice Center starts Friday, October 23. Regular sessions are Fridays 7:00-8:30 p.m., Saturdays 1:00-2:30 p.m., Sundays 1:00-2:30 and 3:00-4:30 p.m., and adult skate Wednesdays 11:30 a.m.-1:00 p.m. Admission is $6 for adults, $4 for children 12 and under and seniors 62 and older, and free for children 3 and under with a paying adult. Skate rental is $4, and a 10-visit punch card is $50. Learn-to-skate classes start November 7 (six Saturdays, $60). Register with the Expo office at (920) 683-4378.

## Veterans: we're here to help

The county Veterans Service Office in the Courthouse (Room 105) helps veterans and their families with VA disability claims, pension, health care enrollment, state benefits and more, free of charge. Walk-ins Tuesdays and Thursdays 8:30-11:30 a.m., or call (920) 683-4056 for an appointment. Bring your DD-214 if you have it.
