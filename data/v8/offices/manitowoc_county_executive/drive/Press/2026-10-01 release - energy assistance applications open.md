# NEWS RELEASE - Manitowoc County Human Services

**For immediate release: Thursday, October 1, 2026**
**Contact:** Sandra Lohrey, Director, Manitowoc County Human Services, (920) 683-4230

## Energy assistance applications open for the 2026-27 heating season

MANITOWOC - Manitowoc County households that need help paying for heat this winter can apply for energy assistance starting today. Manitowoc County Human Services takes applications for the Wisconsin Home Energy Assistance Program (WHEAP) from **October 1, 2026, through May 15, 2027**.

WHEAP provides a one-time payment toward heating costs, paid directly to the household's utility or fuel supplier. Households may also qualify for help with electric bills. The program is open to homeowners and renters, including renters whose heat is included in their rent.

"Last winter we helped more than 3,200 households in Manitowoc County," said Human Services Director Sandra Lohrey. "Many of them were older adults on fixed incomes and working families who had never applied for help before. If you're not sure whether you qualify, call us. It takes a few minutes to find out."

**How to apply**

- **By phone:** call the energy assistance line at **(920) 683-4237** to schedule a phone appointment. Staff will take the application over the phone and tell you what to mail or drop off.
- **In person:** walk in at Manitowoc County Human Services, 926 S. 8th St., Manitowoc, **Monday-Thursday, 8:30-11:30 a.m.** Expect a wait during the first few weeks of the season.
- **Online:** apply through the State of Wisconsin's energy assistance website. A Human Services worker will follow up.

**Older adults and people who are homebound** can complete the entire application by phone and mail; no visit is required. The ADRC of the Lakeshore, (877) 416-7083, can also help older adults and people with disabilities apply.

**What to have ready**

- Proof of income for everyone in the household for the **last three months** (pay stubs, unemployment, pension statements; for Social Security, the annual benefit letter is enough).
- A recent **heating bill** and a recent **electric bill**, or the name of your fuel supplier and account number if you heat with propane, fuel oil or wood.
- **Social Security numbers** for everyone in the household.

Income limits depend on household size. Staff will check eligibility by phone; callers do not need to know the limits before calling.

**Crisis help**

Households that have received a **disconnect notice**, are **out of fuel or nearly out**, or have a broken furnace may qualify for crisis assistance **year-round**, not only during the heating season. Call (920) 683-4237 right away and say it is an emergency. Wisconsin's cold-weather rule generally prevents utilities from disconnecting heating service for nonpayment from November 1 through April 15, but customers should still apply and keep making whatever payments they can.

**Other help**

Energy assistance applicants may also be eligible for FoodShare, BadgerCare and other programs. Staff will screen for these during the application if the household asks. Weatherization services to make homes more energy efficient are available through the state program; Human Services refers eligible households.

Manitowoc County Human Services is open Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-4:30 p.m. More information is on the Human Services page at manitowoccountywi.gov.
