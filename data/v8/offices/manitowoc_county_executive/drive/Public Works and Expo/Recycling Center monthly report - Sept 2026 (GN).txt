1. OPERATIONS SUMMARY

The line ran 21 days (Labor Day closed). No unplanned downtime longer than two hours. The baler hydraulic hose replaced 9/10 took the line down 1 hr 40 min.

Drop-off sites: all six sites (Valders, Reedsville, Mishicot, Kiel, Francis Creek, Whitelaw) open on schedule, Tuesdays 2-6 p.m. and Saturdays 8 a.m.-noon. Attendants report Saturday counts up about 10% from August at Valders and Kiel. Glass bins at Valders and Kiel are now being pulled twice a week (Tue/Sat) because of volume; the other four sites stay weekly. The Francis Creek site needs gravel before winter; quote requested.

Glass: our processor in De Pere stopped taking our glass as of 9/14 (their notice is in Brian's email). Since then all glass from the drop-off sites and the line has gone to the landfill as daily cover, 104.3 tons from 9/14 through 9/30. Before 9/14 we shipped 62.5 tons to De Pere at the usual $0 net. Landfill cover tonnage is at our standard cover rate, not the full tipping fee. Brian is preparing an RFP for glass processing.

Market notes: cardboard is holding; mixed paper softened $4. Aluminum is strong. The steel buyer will pick up only full trailers, so steel will ship every other week.
