1. PURPOSE

Manitowoc County ("the County") requests proposals from qualified firms to receive and process container glass collected through the County's six drop-off sites and recovered on the Recycling Center's processing line, for a term of three years beginning on or about January 1, 2027, with two optional one-year renewals.

2. BACKGROUND

The County operates a Recycling Center that processes single-stream recyclables from the cities of Manitowoc, Two Rivers and Kiel, eight villages and contracted towns, and source-separated materials from six rural drop-off sites. The County expects to ship approximately 1,900 tons of mixed-color container glass per year. Glass from the drop-off sites is collected separately; glass from the line is mixed with some ceramics and fines.

Recent annual glass tonnage (approximate):
   2023 ........ 1,810 tons
   2024 ........ 1,860 tons
   2025 ........ 1,940 tons

3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

The proposer shall:
a) Accept mixed-color glass delivered by the County in its own trailers, or provide pickup (state which, and the price of each);
b) Accept a minimum of 120 tons per month and up to 200 tons per month;
c) Process the glass for use as cullet, fiberglass feedstock, abrasives or another end use other than disposal, and state the end uses and the share of glass going to each;
d) Provide monthly reports of tons received and the end use of the material;
e) State any contamination limits and the charges or rejections that apply when a load exceeds them.

4. PRICING

Proposers shall state a price per ton (charge or rebate) for glass delivered by the County and for glass picked up by the proposer, and any fuel surcharge formula. Prices shall be firm for the first contract year; later years may be adjusted by an index stated in the proposal.

5. SCORING

   Price .............................................. 40 points
   End use (share kept out of disposal) ............... 25 points
   Capacity and reliability ............................ 20 points
   References .......................................... 15 points

The County may award to the proposal it considers most advantageous, reject any or all proposals, and waive informalities. Award is subject to approval by the Public Works Committee.

Submit one signed original and one electronic copy (PDF on a flash drive or by email to the contact above) in a sealed envelope marked "RFP 2026-14 Glass." Late proposals will not be opened.
