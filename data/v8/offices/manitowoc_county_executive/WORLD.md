**Highway**
- Mailbox damage by county plows (Highway policy rev Oct 2025, unchanged for 2026-27): Highway pays only when a county plow or wing physically struck the mailbox or post on a road the county plows (county trunk highways, state highways, and town roads in towns that contract with the county). Snow thrown from the plow is not covered, and mailboxes not installed to the county standard (Code 15.11) may be denied. Remedy: Highway installs a standard post and box, or reimburses up to $50 toward the owner's replacement with a receipt. Report to Highway, (920) 683-4345, within 30 days and no later than April 30 for the winter; past winters' claims are reviewed case by case. Town plow damage: contact the town.
