## Purpose

The county emergency manager's office raised a joint January tabletop with Kyle in August and called the City Manager's office on Oct 14 to get this first meeting on the calendar. The county needs a discussion-based exercise for its grant-funded exercise schedule; the city's emergency operations plan annexes for hazardous materials and evacuation haven't been exercised since 2022. One exercise covers both.

## Agreed so far

- **Format:** Tabletop (discussion-based), facilitated, about 3.5 hours with a working lunch. No field deployment.
- **Date:** Thursday, January 14, 2027, 8:30 a.m.-12:30 p.m. Backup: Thursday, January 21.
- **Location:** Irving Community Center main hall (city booking; Kyle to confirm with the Parks office - the hall seats 120 at tables, we expect 45-55).
- **Incident concept:** A train derailment inside the city limits with a tank car leaking an inhalation hazard (anhydrous ammonia selected as the most realistic for our rail traffic). Weekday morning, school in session, light north wind.
- **Exercise objectives (draft):**
  1. Establish unified command between city fire, county EM and the railroad within the first hour.
  2. Make and communicate a shelter-in-place vs. evacuation decision for a defined area, including schools, the hospital and a nursing home.
  3. Identify public information responsibilities: who speaks, who posts, how city and county messages are kept consistent.
  4. Identify resource gaps: hazmat team response time from outside the county, air monitoring equipment, and bus availability for evacuation.
  5. Review procedures for traffic control and road closures around the scene, including state highways.
- **Facilitator:** the county will ask the state's regional exercise coordinator to facilitate so that no local agency both plays and evaluates.

## Discussion points

- The fire chief said the department's hazmat capability is operations level (defensive: isolate, deny entry, identify). Any offensive action would need a regional hazmat team; expected arrival time should be a central question in the exercise rather than assumed.
- The patrol lieutenant asked that the exercise include the first 30 minutes in detail, because patrol units will be first on scene and need to know isolation distances. Kyle will build an inject around the first officer's report.
- Public Works noted that a closure of the main rail crossings divides the city; the exercise map should show which crossings would be closed and the detour routes, and where Public Works would place barricades.
- The hospital coordinator asked to test decontamination: whether walk-in patients from the affected area would be decontaminated before entering the emergency department, and by whom.
- ECU campus police asked that the evacuation area discussion include the university, since several residence halls are within two miles of the tracks.
- Public information: Renee Calloway (Assistant City Manager) and Brooke Tanner (PIO) to be invited as players. The county's public information officer will also play. We'll test a joint news release and a joint social media post.
