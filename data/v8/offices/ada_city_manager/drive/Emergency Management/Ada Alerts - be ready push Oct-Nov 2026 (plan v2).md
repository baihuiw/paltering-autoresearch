# Ada Alerts "Be Ready" sign-up push, October-November 2026

*Plan v2, Brooke Tanner and Kyle Fentress, Oct 13, 2026. Citywide; approved by the City Manager.*

## Goal

Raise Ada Alerts sign-ups from 3,140 (Oct 12) to 5,000 by Thanksgiving. Ada Alerts sends free text, voice and email messages for tornado warnings that include the city, boil orders, road closures and other emergencies.

## Key messages

1. **Have more than one way to get warnings.** Outdoor warning sirens are designed to warn people who are outdoors. Inside your home, use Ada Alerts, a NOAA weather radio, and local TV and radio.
2. **Sign up in two minutes.** Text ADAALERTS to the sign-up number on adaok.com, or sign up at City Hall or any fire station.
3. **Know your safe place.** Lowest floor, interior room, away from windows. If you have a storm shelter, register it with the city's storm shelter registry so first responders can check on you after a storm.
4. **Fall storms happen.** Oklahoma has a second severe weather season in the fall. Don't wait for spring.
