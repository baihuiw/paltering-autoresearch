# Ada CERT Basic Training: Fall 2026 Class

*Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) program, City of Ada Emergency Management. Coordinator: Kyle Fentress. Updated October 13, 2026.*

## Class at a glance

- **Dates:** Tuesdays, October 20 through December 8, 2026, 6:00-9:00 p.m. (eight sessions), plus a Saturday final exercise December 12, 8:00 a.m.-noon.
- **Location:** Fire Station 1 training room. Final exercise at the Service Center yard and the old maintenance building.
- **Cost:** Free. Students receive a CERT backpack, vest, helmet, gloves, goggles and a field guide (purchased in September, $1,210).
- **Who:** Ada residents and people who work in Ada, 18 and over (16-17 with a parent enrolled). Background check required before graduation.
- **Instructors:** Kyle Fentress (lead); Ada Fire Department instructors for fire suppression and medical sessions; a licensed counselor from the hospital's behavioral health unit for the disaster psychology session; two returning CERT members as assistant instructors.

## Requirements to graduate

- Attend at least seven of the eight sessions (makeups available in the spring class).
- Complete the online introductory course (about two hours) before Session 2. Kyle will send the link Oct 21.
- Pass the final exercise.
- Background check through the Police Department (authorization form collected at Session 1).

Graduates are invited to join the Ada CERT team (currently 41 active members), which meets the second Thursday of each month and helps with large events, traffic control at the household hazardous waste day, search exercises and the annual preparedness booth at the farmers market.

## Notes from the spring 2026 class (what we changed)

- Spring feedback forms said Session 1 was "too much lecture." This fall it opens with a 20-minute hands-on go-kit exercise: each table builds a kit from a pile of household items and explains what they left out and why.
- Medical sessions now run as stations (bleeding control, splinting, assessment) with students rotating every 25 minutes, so nobody sits for three hours.
- Graduates asked for a refresher option. Returning CERT members may sit in on any session at no cost, and two have signed up for the medical sessions.
- Attendance dropped in spring when the class ran into spring break. This schedule skips Thanksgiving week and ends before the holidays.
