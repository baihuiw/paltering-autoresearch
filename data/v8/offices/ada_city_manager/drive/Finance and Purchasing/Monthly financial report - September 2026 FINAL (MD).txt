CITY OF ADA
MONTHLY FINANCIAL REPORT - GENERAL FUND
Period ending September 30, 2026 (FY2027, month 3 of 12; 25.0% of year elapsed)
Prepared by: Marcy Dellinger, Finance Director/City Clerk
Distributed: October 14, 2026 (council packet for October 19)

SUMMARY

General Fund revenues through September total $5,741,000, or 24.9% of the
FY27 budget of $23,020,000. Expenditures total $5,556,200, or 24.2% of the
budget of $23,000,000. Revenues exceeded expenditures by $184,800 for the
first quarter. Both are on track; nothing in the first quarter calls for a
budget amendment in the General Fund.

Sales tax received in September (July sales) was $1,266,590, up 2.4% from
September 2025. Use tax was $189,220, up 7.0%. Year to date, sales tax is up
2.7% and use tax is up 6.5% over the first quarter of FY26. The FY27 budget
assumed 2.0% growth in sales tax, so we are slightly ahead. One quarter is
not a trend; I'd hold off on reading too much into it until the December and
January receipts, which carry the holiday season.

Notes on revenues:
- Licenses and permits are ahead of pace because new-home permits in August
  and September were the strongest in three years.
- Fines and forfeitures (municipal court) are slightly behind. Court
  collections in September were $38,900, down from $41,200 last September.
  The court clerk position was vacant two weeks in August.
- Intergovernmental is low because the county's share of the dispatch
  agreement is billed quarterly; the first quarter invoice went out Oct 2.
- Interest is ahead of budget; rates on our pooled cash have held up better
  than we assumed in the spring.

Notes on expenditures:
- General government is above 25% because the annual property and liability
  insurance premium is paid in July. It levels out by midyear.
- Parks and recreation is above 25% because the pool season (seasonal
  wages, chemicals) falls entirely in the first quarter.
- Streets is below pace because the crack seal contract ($86,240) was
  approved Sept 21 and its first pay application has not come in. The FY27
  overlay contract ($412,870, awarded Oct 5) is budgeted in the street
  capital account and is not in these figures.
- Police personnel costs reflect three vacancies (two officers, one
  dispatcher) for most of the quarter. Two recruits started the academy
  Oct 12; their academy costs will show in October.
- Transfers out are made quarterly beginning in October.

FUND BALANCE

Unassigned General Fund balance at July 1, 2026 (unaudited): $4,860,000,
or 21.1% of budgeted expenditures. Council's reserve policy minimum is 15%.
The FY26 audit fieldwork is scheduled for October 19-23; final balances will
be reported when the audit is issued (target December).

CASH AND INVESTMENTS (all city funds, pooled)
