## What I'm asking for

**Start from a flat operating base.** Your FY28 operating request (supplies, contractual services, utilities, small equipment) should equal your FY27 adopted operating budget. Anything above that goes on a separate "decision package" sheet with a one-paragraph justification and the cost. Don't bury increases inside existing lines; I would rather see them clearly and say yes to the good ones.

**Personnel.** Finance will load current salaries and benefits for every filled and budgeted position. You don't need to calculate step increases or health insurance; we'll do that centrally once the health plan renewal comes in (expected February). New positions and reclassifications need a personnel request form by November 25 with the duties, the workload numbers that justify it and the proposed funding source.

**Capital.** Update your lines in the five-year capital plan. For each item: description, cost estimate and its source, the year you need it, what happens if it's deferred a year, and whether it has a grant or other funding. Vehicles and heavy equipment: include the unit number, mileage or hours, and the last 24 months of repair costs from the fleet report.

**Revenue ideas.** If your department collects any charge for service that hasn't been reviewed in three years or more, tell us. Cemetery fees just went up for the first time since 2018; I'd rather review on a regular schedule than make large jumps.

**Performance numbers.** Each department page in the budget book will carry four to six measures. Pick ones you already track and would be glad to have council ask about: response times, work orders closed, permits issued, participation, calls for service. Send last year's actuals and this year's target with your operating request.

## A few ground rules

1. Sales tax is up about 2.7% in the first quarter. That's good, but our FY28 base revenue assumption will be conservative (about 2%) until we see the holiday months.
2. The General Fund's unassigned balance is above the council's 15% minimum. It is not a source for ongoing costs. One-time capital, maybe; new positions, no.
3. Housing is one of my priorities, and I expect some FY28 requests from Community Development and Public Works will relate to it. Treat those like any other request: costs, benefits, and what we stop doing to pay for them if needed.
4. Grants: if a capital request depends on a grant, list the grant, the match and the application date. Don't assume the grant in the base.
