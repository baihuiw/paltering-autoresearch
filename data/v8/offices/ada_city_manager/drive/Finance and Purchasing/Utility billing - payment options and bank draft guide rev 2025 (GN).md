# Utility Billing: Payment Options and Bank Draft (Automatic Payment)

*Utility Office, City Hall, 231 S. Townsend. Counter hours Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Phone (580) 436-6300 (ask for utility billing). Revised August 2025 by Gloria Nix, Utility Office Supervisor. Staff use this sheet to answer calls and counter questions; the customer handout is the second page of the new-service packet.*

## The bill

- One bill covers water, wastewater and residential trash service.
- Bills are mailed around the 1st of the month and are due on the 15th.
- A 10% penalty is added to any balance not paid by the 15th (if the 15th falls on a weekend or holiday, the next business day).
- A disconnect notice is mailed on the 20th for unpaid balances. Service may be disconnected for nonpayment on the 28th after the notice.
- Customers can see their usage history, balance and past bills on the online customer portal at adaok.com (Utility Billing > Pay My Bill).

## Ways to pay

1. **Bank draft (automatic payment).** The bill amount is drafted from a checking or savings account on the 15th of each month. No charge. See below.
2. **Online portal.** Credit card, debit card or e-check through the portal at adaok.com. The portal also allows a saved card with **recurring automatic payment** on the due date. The card payment vendor charges a convenience charge on card payments (shown before you confirm); e-check payments have no charge.
3. **By phone.** Card payments through the automated line, 24 hours, (580) 436-6300, option 2.
4. **In person.** Cash, check, money order or card at the Utility Office counter.
5. **Drop box.** Checks or money orders only, in the drop box at the Townsend Street entrance. Emptied every business morning at 8:00. Don't put cash in the drop box.
6. **Mail.** City of Ada Utility Billing, 231 S. Townsend, Ada, OK 74820. Allow mail time; payments are credited the day they arrive.

## Bank draft: how to sign up

- Fill out the **Bank Draft Authorization** form. It's at the Utility Office counter and on adaok.com under Utility Billing > Forms. It can be mailed, dropped in the drop box or emailed as a scan to utilitybilling@adaok.com.
- Attach a voided check (checking) or a letter from the bank with the routing and account number (savings).
- The account holder on the utility account must sign. If the bank account is in another person's name, that person must sign too.
- Forms received by the 1st of the month start with that month's bill. Forms received after the 1st start the following month. **Until the draft starts, pay the bill the usual way.** The bill will say "DO NOT PAY - BANK DRAFT" once it is active.
- To stop or change the bank account, send a new form at least 10 days before the 15th.
- A draft returned by the bank (closed account, insufficient funds) is treated as a returned payment: a returned-item charge is added and the account comes off bank draft after two returns in 12 months.

## Customers away for an extended time

We get this every fall from residents who winter out of state, and every spring from ECU faculty who travel in the summer.

- Bank draft or recurring card payment is the best way to make sure the bill is paid while they are away. Suggest they sign up at least a month before they leave so the first draft has gone through.
- Suggest paperless billing (e-bill by email) through the portal so they can see the bill wherever they are. Or they can give us a temporary mailing address; update it in the account notes with the return date.
- Water service stays on unless they ask us to turn it off. If they want it turned off at the meter while the house is empty, Public Works can do that at no charge with two days' notice; the trash charge and base charges continue unless the account is closed. They should call before they want it back on.
- Remind them to have someone check the house during hard freezes. A broken pipe in an empty house can run for days before anyone notices, and the leak adjustment policy only covers part of the cost.

## Leak adjustments (summary)

Customers with an unusually high bill from a repaired leak on their side of the meter may apply once in 12 months for an adjustment of the wastewater portion above their average usage. Requires proof of repair (receipt or plumber's invoice) within 60 days of the bill. Water used is not adjusted. Forms at the counter. Gloria approves up to $500; above that goes to the Finance Director.
