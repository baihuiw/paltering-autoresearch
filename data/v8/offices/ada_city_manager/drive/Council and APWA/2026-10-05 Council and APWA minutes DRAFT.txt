DRAFT - for approval October 19, 2026

MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING OF THE ADA CITY COUNCIL
and the ADA PUBLIC WORKS AUTHORITY
Monday, October 5, 2026, 5:45 p.m.
Council Chamber, Ada City Hall, 231 S. Townsend

PRESENT: Mayor Gail Hensley; Vice Mayor Rick Tisdale; Council Members Marcus Bledsoe, Dana Whitworth, Ray Medrano
STAFF: Neil Hartsock, City Manager; Renee Calloway, Assistant City Manager; Lane Whitfield, City Attorney; Marcy Dellinger, Finance Director/City Clerk; Russell Gaines, Public Works Director; Shawna Rippetoe, Parks and Recreation Director; Dean Sizemore, Community Development Director

CITY COUNCIL

1. CALL TO ORDER at 5:45 p.m. by Mayor Hensley. Invocation by Council Member Medrano.

2. WELCOME. Mayor Hensley welcomed City Manager Neil Hartsock to his first regular meeting. Mr. Hartsock thanked the council and staff and said his early priorities are housing, keeping basic services reliable, and "answering every question we get from residents."

3. CONSENT AGENDA. Motion by Tisdale, second by Whitworth: (a) minutes of Sept 21; (b) September claims; (c) agreement with the county for election-day use of the Irving Community Center; (d) Fall Fest at Kestrel street closure and noise permit, Oct 24. Vote 5-0.

4. CITY MANAGER'S REPORT. Mr. Hartsock reported on the Oct 7 outdoor warning siren test (noon, weather permitting), Ada Alerts sign-ups, the start of the FY28 budget calendar, and the Nov 7 household hazardous waste day.

5. CEMETERY FEES. Resolution 2026-22 raising cemetery lot and opening fees an average of 9%, the first change since 2018. Motion Whitworth, second Medrano. Vote 5-0.

6. E. 14TH STREET SPEED HUMPS. Two residents spoke in favor. Staff recommended a traffic count before installing. Council directed staff to complete the count and return Nov 2. No vote.

7. STREET OVERLAY. Award of the FY27 overlay contract (Oak Ave from 8th to 14th; N. Rennie from Main to 6th) to the low bidder, $412,870, 4% under estimate. Motion Bledsoe, second Tisdale. Vote 5-0.

8. CITIZENS TO BE HEARD. A resident asked about stray dogs near Glenwood; referred to Animal Welfare.

9. ADJOURN at 6:38 p.m.

ADA PUBLIC WORKS AUTHORITY (trustees: the council members)

1. CALL TO ORDER at 6:40 p.m. by Chair Hensley.

2. CONSENT. Minutes of Sept 21 APWA meeting; September claims. Motion Tisdale, second Medrano. Vote 5-0.

3. LANDFILL GATE RATES. Resolution APWA 2026-07 amending the landfill gate rate schedule, effective Oct 15: $56.00 per ton (from $38.00) and a $20.00 minimum (from $15.00) for commercial, private hauler and self-haul customers. Public Works Director Gaines said the change reflects increased disposal and transportation costs while Cell 4 is in state permitting, and that residential trash rates are not affected. Trustee Bledsoe asked when the rate would come back down; Mr. Gaines said staff would review the rate when Cell 4 opens. Trustee Bledsoe said contractors he had talked to were unhappy about a 47% increase with ten days' notice. Motion Medrano, second Whitworth. Vote: Aye: Hensley, Tisdale, Whitworth, Medrano. Nay: Bledsoe. Adopted 4-1.

4. WATER PLANT CHEMICAL FEED PUMP. Sole-source purchase of a replacement chemical feed pump, $18,900. Motion Whitworth, second Tisdale. Vote 5-0.

5. ADJOURN at 6:58 p.m.

Attest: Marcy Dellinger, City Clerk
