MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING OF THE ADA CITY COUNCIL
Monday, September 21, 2026, 5:45 p.m.
Council Chamber, Ada City Hall, 231 S. Townsend
(Approved as presented October 5, 2026)

PRESENT: Mayor Gail Hensley; Vice Mayor Rick Tisdale; Council Members Marcus Bledsoe, Dana Whitworth, Ray Medrano
STAFF: Renee Calloway, Acting City Manager; Lane Whitfield, City Attorney; Marcy Dellinger, Finance Director/City Clerk; department heads as listed on the sign-in sheet

1. CALL TO ORDER. Mayor Hensley called the meeting to order at 5:45 p.m. Invocation by Vice Mayor Tisdale; Pledge of Allegiance.

2. CONSENT AGENDA. Motion by Whitworth, second by Medrano, to approve the consent agenda: (a) minutes of Sept 1 special meeting and Sept 8 regular meeting; (b) claims and payroll for August; (c) declare two 2011 police patrol units surplus for sale by online auction; (d) reappoint two members of the Library Board. Vote: 5-0. Approved.

3. PROCLAMATION. Mayor Hensley read a proclamation for National Preparedness Month and recognized Emergency Management Coordinator Kyle Fentress and the volunteers who staffed the preparedness booth at the farmers market.

4. EXECUTIVE SESSION. Motion by Tisdale, second by Bledsoe, to enter executive session under 25 O.S. Section 307(B)(1) to discuss the employment of a city manager. Vote: 5-0. Council entered executive session at 5:58 p.m. and returned to open session at 6:41 p.m. Mayor Hensley stated that no action was taken in executive session.

5. APPOINTMENT OF CITY MANAGER. Motion by Whitworth, second by Medrano, to appoint Neil Hartsock as City Manager for an indefinite term under Section 3-1 of the City Charter, effective September 23, 2026. Council Member Bledsoe said he respected Mr. Hartsock's experience but would have preferred a longer search with more local applicants. Vote: Aye: Hensley, Tisdale, Whitworth, Medrano. Nay: Bledsoe. Approved 4-1.

6. EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT. Motion by Tisdale, second by Whitworth, to approve the employment agreement with Neil Hartsock (annual salary $138,500; standard city benefits; vehicle allowance $450 per month; six-month review). Vote: 5-0. Approved.

7. RECOGNITION. Council thanked Renee Calloway for serving as acting city manager since Sept 1. Ms. Calloway returns to the position of Assistant City Manager on Sept 23.

8. FINAL PLAT, TIMBER BEND ADDITION. Community Development Director Dean Sizemore presented the final plat of a 28-lot single-family subdivision north of Kings Road (Tennant Homes LLC). The Planning Commission recommended approval 5-0. Council Member Medrano asked about traffic on Kings Road; Mr. Sizemore said 28 homes would add about 260 trips a day, within the road's capacity. Motion by Medrano, second by Whitworth, to approve the final plat subject to staff conditions. Vote: 5-0. Approved.

9. STREETS. Council awarded the FY27 crack seal contract to the low of three bidders, $86,240 (bid tab in the packet) (motion Tisdale, second Bledsoe, 5-0).

10. CITIZENS TO BE HEARD. Two residents spoke: one asked about speed humps on E. 14th Street (referred to staff); one thanked the Parks Department for the summer concert series.

11. ADJOURN. The council adjourned at 7:22 p.m. and convened as the Ada Public Works Authority (see separate APWA minutes).

Attest: Marcy Dellinger, City Clerk
