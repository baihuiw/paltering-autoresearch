   Information only; no action. Fire Station 1 open house (Oct 24); FY26 audit

5. RESOLUTION SUPPORTING COUNTY RURAL FIRE EQUIPMENT GRANT
   Consideration and possible action on Resolution 2026-23 supporting the
   Pontotoc County application for a rural fire equipment grant that
   includes mutual-aid equipment for the Ada Fire Department.
   Presenter: Lonnie Breedlove, Fire Chief.

6. ANIMAL SHELTER SERVICES AGREEMENT - FY27 RENEWAL
   Consideration and possible action on renewal of the agreement for animal
   shelter services for impounded animals, November 1, 2026 through October
   31, 2027, at $6,450 per month (up from $6,250).
   Presenter: Renee Calloway, Assistant City Manager.

7. PLANNING COMMISSION APPOINTMENTS
   Consideration and possible action on the Mayor's appointment, with council
   consent, of two members to the Planning Commission for terms ending June
   30, 2029, to fill the seats of members who moved away.

8. CITIZENS TO BE HEARD
   Members of the public may address the council on matters not on this
   agenda. Please complete a Request to Speak card (available on the table
   at the back of the chamber) and give it to the City Clerk before the
   meeting is called to order. Each speaker is limited to three minutes.
   Under the Open Meeting Act, the council may not discuss or take action on
   a matter raised under this item; the council may refer it to the City
   Manager or place it on a future agenda.

9. COUNCIL COMMENTS
   Announcements and requests for future agenda items. No discussion or
   action.

10. NEW BUSINESS
    Any matter not known about or which could not have been reasonably

STAFF NOTES FOR ITEMS (attached to council packet; not part of posted agenda)

Item 3d. The merchants' group requested the closure Sept 30. Police will place
barricades at Mississippi, Broadway, Rennie and Townsend at 3:30 p.m. and
remove them at 6:30 p.m. Merchants hand out candy from storefronts 4:00-6:00
p.m. Fire will stage a unit at Main and Broadway. Same plan as 2025.

Item 6. The shelter provider has held the contract since 2021. The increase
($200/month, 3.2%) reflects food and veterinary costs. Impounds in FY26 were
611 dogs and 288 cats; 71% of impounded dogs were returned to owners,
adopted or transferred to rescues. Animal Welfare officers remain city
employees in the Police Department; this agreement covers only housing,
feeding and basic veterinary care of impounded animals. Funds are budgeted.

Item 7. Two seats have been vacant since August. Mayor Hensley will announce
her nominees at the meeting; both have submitted the board application and
have been confirmed as residents of the city.

Item 8. Request to Speak cards ask for name, address and topic. Speakers who
arrive after the meeting has started may still hand a card to the Clerk;
the Mayor usually allows them if time permits. There is no advance sign-up
required.
