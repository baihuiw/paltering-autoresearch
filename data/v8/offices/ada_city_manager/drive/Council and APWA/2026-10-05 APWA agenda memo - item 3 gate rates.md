# Ada Public Works Authority: Agenda Item Memo

**Meeting:** October 5, 2026 (APWA convenes after the City Council meeting)
**Item 3:** Resolution APWA 2026-07, amending the landfill gate rate schedule
**From:** Russell Gaines, Public Works Director
**Through:** Neil Hartsock, City Manager
**Fiscal impact:** About $175,000 in additional gate revenue through June 30, 2027

## Background

The City of Ada Landfill serves city collection routes, private haulers, contractors and residents who haul their own loads. Gate rates for commercial, private hauler and self-haul customers were last changed in July 2019. City residential trash service is billed through the utility bill and is not part of the gate rate schedule.

## Discussion

The proposed rate reflects increased disposal and transportation costs. Cell 3, the active disposal cell, is expected to reach capacity in mid-November. Cell 4 is in state permitting. Until Cell 4 opens, waste received at the landfill will be loaded into transfer trailers and hauled to a permitted private landfill. Staff solicited prices from two facilities within 70 miles; Red Hill Disposal offered the lower combined disposal and hauling price.

| Rate | Current | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial, private hauler and self-haul, per ton | $38.00 | $56.00 |
| Minimum charge per load | $15.00 | $20.00 |
| Tires (passenger), each | $4.00 | $4.00 |
| Appliances with freon, each | $15.00 | $15.00 |
| Brush and yard waste (separate brush site) | No charge | No charge |

Even at $56.00 per ton, the Ada Landfill rate remains below the average of the regional facilities staff surveyed ($61.75 per ton; survey attached). Residential trash rates on utility bills are not affected by this change.

Staff will review the gate rate when Cell 4 opens and recommend an adjustment at that time.

## Notice

If adopted, the new rates take effect October 15, 2026. Notice will be posted at the scale house and on the city's website and Facebook page.

## Recommendation

Adopt Resolution APWA 2026-07 amending the landfill gate rate schedule effective October 15, 2026.
