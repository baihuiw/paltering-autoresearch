MINUTES OF A SPECIAL MEETING OF THE ADA CITY COUNCIL
Tuesday, September 1, 2026, 12:00 noon
Council Chamber, Ada City Hall, 231 S. Townsend
(Approved September 21, 2026)

Notice of this special meeting was posted at City Hall and on the city website at 10:30 a.m. on Monday, August 31, 2026, more than 24 hours before the meeting.

PRESENT: Mayor Gail Hensley; Vice Mayor Rick Tisdale; Council Members Marcus Bledsoe, Dana Whitworth, Ray Medrano
STAFF: Renee Calloway, Assistant City Manager; Lane Whitfield, City Attorney; Marcy Dellinger, Finance Director/City Clerk

1. CALL TO ORDER. Mayor Hensley called the meeting to order at 12:02 p.m. All members present.

2. RESIGNATION OF CITY MANAGER. Mayor Hensley reported that the city manager had submitted a letter of resignation effective September 1, 2026. Motion by Tisdale, second by Whitworth, to accept the resignation effective today. Vote: 5-0. Accepted. Mayor Hensley thanked the previous city manager for his service to the city.

3. ACTING CITY MANAGER. Under Charter Section 3-2, the council may appoint an acting city manager when no designation has been made. Motion by Medrano, second by Bledsoe, to appoint Assistant City Manager Renee Calloway as acting city manager effective immediately until a city manager is appointed and takes office, with an acting pay supplement of 10%. Vote: 5-0. Approved.

4. SEARCH PROCESS. Council discussed how to fill the position. Council Member Bledsoe favored a national search through a recruiting firm (four to six months). Vice Mayor Tisdale and Council Member Whitworth favored an expedited process, noting the FY28 budget calendar and several large projects. By consensus, council directed the city attorney and acting city manager to (a) post the position on the city website and through state and regional municipal job boards for two weeks, closing Sept 11, and (b) schedule interviews of up to three finalists in executive session the week of Sept 14. No vote was required.

5. EXECUTIVE SESSION. None.

6. ADJOURN. Meeting adjourned at 12:41 p.m.

Attest: Marcy Dellinger, City Clerk
