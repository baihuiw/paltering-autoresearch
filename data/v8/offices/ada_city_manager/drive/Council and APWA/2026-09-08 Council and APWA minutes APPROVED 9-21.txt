APPROVED September 21, 2026

MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING OF THE ADA CITY COUNCIL
and the ADA PUBLIC WORKS AUTHORITY
Tuesday, September 8, 2026, 5:45 p.m. (moved from Monday for Labor Day)
Council Chamber, Ada City Hall, 231 S. Townsend

PRESENT: Mayor Gail Hensley; Vice Mayor Rick Tisdale; Council Members Marcus
Bledsoe, Dana Whitworth, Ray Medrano
STAFF: Renee Calloway, Acting City Manager; Lane Whitfield, City Attorney;
Marcy Dellinger, Finance Director/City Clerk; Russell Gaines, Public Works
Director; Dean Sizemore, Community Development Director; Lonnie Breedlove,
Fire Chief

CITY COUNCIL

1. CALL TO ORDER at 5:46 p.m. by Mayor Hensley. Invocation by Vice Mayor
Tisdale. All members present.

2. PROCLAMATION. Mayor Hensley proclaimed October 4-10, 2026 as Fire
Prevention Week in Ada. Chief Breedlove thanked the schools for again
scheduling station visits for every second- and fourth-grade class.

3. CONSENT AGENDA. Motion by Whitworth, second by Medrano, to approve: (a)
minutes of the August 17 regular meeting; (b) August claims; (c) acceptance of the July financial report; (d)
renewal of the annual agreement with the county for dispatch services for
the rural fire districts. Vote 5-0.

4. ACTING CITY MANAGER'S REPORT. Ms. Calloway reported that the city manager
position closed Sept 11 for applications and that 19 applications had been
received as of that afternoon. Council scheduled interviews in executive
session for Sept 14 and 16. She reported that the municipal pool closed for
the season Sept 7 with attendance of 21,640, the highest since 2019; that
August sales tax was up 3.1%; and that two police officers had resigned to
take positions in larger departments, bringing police vacancies to three.

5. ORDINANCE 2026-09, ALLEY CLOSING. Consideration of an ordinance closing
the unimproved alley in Block 14 of the original townsite between W. 13th
and W. 14th, requested by both adjoining owners. Mr. Sizemore said the
Planning Commission recommended approval 6-0 and that the city retains a
utility easement. Motion Bledsoe, second Tisdale. Vote 5-0. Emergency clause
not attached.

6. REZONING, 1700 BLOCK N. STONECIPHER. Consideration of Ordinance 2026-10
rezoning 2.3 acres from R-1 to C-2 for a medical office building. Two
neighbors spoke about drainage and lighting. Mr. Sizemore said the site plan
must meet the drainage ordinance and that lighting must be shielded and
directed away from residential lots. Council Member Whitworth asked that
the building permit not be issued until the drainage plan was approved by
the city engineer; Mr. Sizemore said that is the standard practice. Motion
Medrano, second Whitworth. Vote 5-0.

7. NUISANCE ABATEMENT LIENS. Resolution 2026-19 confirming the costs of
abating nuisances (mowing and debris removal) at 11 properties, totaling
$6,418.50, and directing the Clerk to certify the liens to the county
treasurer. Mr. Sizemore said owners were notified by certified mail and
posting as the ordinance requires. Motion Tisdale, second Bledsoe. Vote 5-0.

8. CRACK SEAL PROJECT. Mr. Gaines reported that bids for the FY27 crack seal
contract would be opened Sept 15 and the award brought to council Sept 21.

9. CITIZENS TO BE HEARD.
   - A resident of W. 22nd St. asked whether the city could do something about
     a vacant house next door that had been open to the weather for a year.
     Mayor Hensley referred her to Community Development; Mr. Sizemore met
     with her after the meeting.
   - A resident of E. 6th St. thanked the Street Department for patching the
     alley behind his house.

10. COUNCIL COMMENTS. Council Member Bledsoe said he had heard from several
Ward 1 residents about speeding on E. 14th Street and asked that it be on a
future agenda. Council Member Medrano congratulated the Ada High band.

11. ADJOURN at 6:41 p.m.

ADA PUBLIC WORKS AUTHORITY (trustees: the council members)

1. CALL TO ORDER at 6:43 p.m. by Chair Hensley.

2. CONSENT. Minutes of the August 17 APWA meeting; August claims. Motion
Medrano, second Whitworth. Vote 5-0.

3. WATER LINE PROJECT, PAY APPLICATION 5 AND CHANGE ORDER 2. Mr. Gaines
presented change order 2 for the W. 9th Street water line replacement
(additional 140 feet of 8-inch line where an old 4-inch line was found to
cross under the street, plus $8,950) and pay application 5. Motion Tisdale,
second Bledsoe. Vote 5-0.

4. ADJOURN at 6:52 p.m.

Attest: Marcy Dellinger, City Clerk
