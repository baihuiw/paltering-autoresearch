Renee,

Good timing. The part-time parking enforcement officer, Rhonda Spurgeon, started this morning. That position was vacant from June 12 until today (the last one left for a full-time job at the hospital). Patrol covered downtown when they could, but with our vacancies it was mostly complaint-driven, and honestly not much. People noticed.

Current rules (city code, unchanged):
- 2-hour limit, Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. (not Saturdays or holidays).
- Main Street from Mississippi to Townsend, both sides, plus the first block north and south of Main on Broadway, Rennie and Townsend. Signs are up on every block; two on Rennie are faded and on the sign shop list.
- Public lots one block north and south of Main have no time limit. That's where employees and all-day parkers should be.
- Overtime parking citation: $20 first offense. Repeat within 30 days: $35. Citations go to municipal court and can be paid online like any other citation.

Plan, which the chief approved: Rhonda marks tires and logs plates starting today. For October she'll leave a printed warning with the lot map on the windshield of anyone over two hours. Citations start Monday, Nov 2. She works 8:00-1:00 Monday through Friday, and she'll vary the order she walks the blocks so it's not predictable.

A couple of other things that come up: handicap-placard vehicles are exempt from the 2-hour limit but not from fire lanes or loading zones. Loading zones at Main/Broadway and Main/Rennie are 20 minutes, deliveries only. And people sometimes move their car a space or two to reset the clock; the code counts that as the same block, and Rhonda logs plates, not just tire chalk, so that won't work anymore.

If merchants see repeat offenders, the best thing is to call the non-emergency line, (580) 332-4466, and ask for parking enforcement. Rhonda carries a radio.

Dale
Lt. Dale Brixey
Patrol Division, Ada Police Department
