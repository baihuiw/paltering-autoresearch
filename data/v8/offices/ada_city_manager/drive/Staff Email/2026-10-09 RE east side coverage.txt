Neil,

Following up on Wednesday's test (report attached). Until the replacement controllers come in, which SWS now says is Dec 7-14, the east and southeast side has no working outdoor warning siren: sites 11, 14, 17 and 19 cannot be set off from the EOC, from Dispatch or by hand. That is Country Club Estates, Sandy Creek, the Kings Road area, S. Mississippi south of 24th and Richardson Loop, roughly 2,300 homes.

We are in the fall severe weather window now, and November tornado warnings are not rare here. My recommendations:

1. Public notice to the affected area: door hangers (Parks and CERT can cover 2,300 homes in two Saturdays), a Facebook post with a map, and a line in the November Ada Update.
2. Use it to push Ada Alerts sign-ups and NOAA weather radios in those neighborhoods specifically.
3. Interim procedure: on a tornado warning that includes the city, Fire Station 3 and two patrol units run sirens-and-PA routes through the four coverage areas. Chief Breedlove is OK with it; I'm checking with PD.
4. Ask SWS to expedite (they say they've asked; no promises).

I can have door hangers printed Monday if you approve.
