5. CELL CAPACITY AND CELL 4
  Cell 3 remaining airspace (per 9/30 survey): about 7,600 cubic yards, roughly 4,900 tons at our compaction. At current intake Cell 3 is full between Nov 12 and Nov 18.

  Cell 4 permit status: DEQ's letter of Oct 2 states the permit modification application is considered withdrawn because the city's response to DEQ's Sept 2 deficiency letter was not received by the Sept 30 deadline. Hollis & Brandt had the response ready Sept 22; it was signed Oct 5 and has been refiled as a new application. DEQ's review time for a new application is 6-9 months, then about 4 months of construction.

  Because our response missed DEQ's Sept 30 deadline, Cell 4 will not open before July 2027, and the hauling to Red Hill that this forces is what the Oct 5 gate rate increase pays for. Starting about Nov 16, everything that comes across our scale goes into transfer trailers to Red Hill Disposal (about 58 miles one way) at $31.50/ton disposal plus hauling of about $18.40/ton.

  Had the response gone in on time, Cell 4 was on track to open in early January and the overlap with Cell 3 would have been covered by the vertical expansion on the west slope.
