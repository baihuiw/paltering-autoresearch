## Crack seal: zones and order of work

**North zone (Oct 12-23, rev 3).** Everything north of Main Street between Mississippi Ave and Country Club Rd: the numbered residential streets W. 1st through W. 6th, the Stonecipher Blvd neighborhood streets, N. Broadway side streets, and the Northside Park area. North zone finished ahead of schedule on Oct 16 except two blocks of N. Constant Ave that were too wet Thursday.

**South zone (Oct 19-Nov 6).** South of Main: W. and E. 9th through 24th Streets, S. Hope Ave, S. Townsend, the Glenwood Park area, Scenic Dr, and Countryside Dr and its cul-de-sacs.

**What residents see.** Crews blow out cracks, fill them with hot rubberized sealant, and dust the sealant with sand. Streets stay open; crews work one lane at a time with flaggers. Residents should avoid driving on fresh sealant for about 30 minutes. No door hangers needed for crack seal (no closures).

**N. Rennie, Main to 6th Street.**

- Downtown loading zones on the Main end will be kept open in the morning.

## Things we've been asked

- **"Why isn't my street on the list?"** The overlay list comes from the pavement condition survey (2024). Streets are ranked by condition and traffic. The full ranked list is at the Service Center and will be updated for the FY28 budget. Crack sealing is cheaper and keeps fair streets from becoming poor ones, which is why it covers far more of the city.
- **"Will the pothole on my street be fixed first?"** Potholes on overlay streets (Oak Ave) are patched before milling (done Oct 13-15). On other streets, potholes are handled through the normal repair request process.
- **"Why do you crack seal in the fall?"** Cracks are widest when the pavement is cool, so the sealant fills more of them.

## Lessons from FY26

- Last year's overlay on W. 15th ran two weeks long because valve boxes weren't raised before paving. This year the utility crew raises them ahead of the contractor (see Coordination).
- Door hangers went out late on two blocks last year and residents were blocked in. The contractor now gives Public Works the next day's blocks by 2:00 p.m., and our crew hangs the notices that afternoon.
- Residents asked for the schedule in one place. Brooke now posts it every Friday, and the City Manager's office has a copy for callers.
