1. WATER PRODUCTION AND DISTRIBUTION

                                   Sept 2026     Sept 2025     Change

Average day (MGD)                       3.95          4.14
Peak day (MGD) / date            5.21 (Sept 4) 5.48 (Sept 9)

Unaccounted-for (est.)                16.0%         18.8%

Production was down from last September because of the rain in the last
week of the month and cooler weather. Unaccounted-for water continues to
improve after the W. 9th line replacement and the leak survey in the summer.

Treatment: all compliance samples met state and federal standards. 62
routine bacteriological samples in the distribution system, all negative.
Chlorine residuals within range at all sites. Monthly operating report
submitted to the state Oct 7.

Chemical feed: the sodium hypochlorite feed pump at the high service
building failed Sept 18 and was replaced temporarily with the standby unit.
The standby is the last spare. A replacement pump (sole-source, matching
the existing control panel) was approved by the APWA trustees Oct 5,
$18,900; delivery expected late October.

3. WASTEWATER COLLECTION AND TREATMENT

                                   Sept 2026     Sept 2025
Influent (million gallons)             79.4          71.0
Average day (MGD)                       2.65          2.37
Peak day (MGD) / date            6.10 (Sept 27) 3.02

The peak on Sept 27 followed the heavy rain the night of Sept 26. Inflow
and infiltration on the east and south trunk lines remains the biggest
reason for wet-weather peaks. Plant effluent met all permit limits for the
month; discharge monitoring report submitted Oct 9.

Sanitary sewer overflows: 1 (Sept 27, manhole on the south trunk line
behind the 1900 block of S. Broadway, est. 2,500 gallons, rain-related).
Reported to the state within 24 hours; area cleaned and limed.

Line cleaning (jetting): 18,400 feet. Televised (camera): 3,100 feet.
Lift stations: all 9 in service. Generator exercise completed at all
stations with generators (6).
Grease trap program: 14 food service site visits; 2 follow-up notices.
