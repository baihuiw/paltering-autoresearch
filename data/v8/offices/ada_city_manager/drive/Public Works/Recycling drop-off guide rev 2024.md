# City of Ada Recycling Drop-Off Guide

*Public Works Department - Sanitation Division. Handout and web page text. Revised March 2024.*

## Where to recycle

Recycling trailers are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week at five locations:

| Site | Location note |
|---|---|
| Service Center | Public Works yard entrance, inside the front gate |
| Kestrel north lot | North parking lot, by the tennis courts |
| Fire Station 3 | East side of the station lot |
| Arlington Street lot | Behind the shopping center |
| Mississippi Ave lot | South end of the lot |

Each site has a mixed-recycling trailer. The Service Center and Arlington sites also have a cardboard-only trailer.

## What goes in

- Paper: newspaper, office paper, mail, magazines, cereal and cracker boxes
- Cardboard: flattened boxes only (please break them down so they fit)
- Plastic bottles and jugs marked #1 and #2, rinsed, lids on
- Aluminum and steel (tin) cans, rinsed

## What stays out

- Plastic bags and plastic film of any kind. Don't bag your recycling; empty it loose into the trailer.
- Glass (no glass recycling at this time)
- Food, liquids, diapers, yard waste
- Styrofoam, garden hoses, cords, clothing
- Household trash

If a trailer is full, please use another site or come back after the next pull. Leaving material on the ground is littering and may be cited.

## How the program works

The Sanitation Division pulls and empties the trailers on a regular schedule (currently twice a week). Everything collected in the trailers is taken to a materials recovery facility, where it is sorted, baled and sold to make new products. Clean material keeps the program affordable: loads with too much trash in them cost the city more and can be rejected.

Residential curbside trash service is not affected by recycling. There is no separate recycling charge on your utility bill.

## Questions

Sanitation Division, (580) 436-8100, weekdays 7:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.

Household hazardous waste, electronics and tires are collected at special events; watch the city's Facebook page and the Ada Update newsletter for dates.
