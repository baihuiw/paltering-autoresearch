Re: City of Ada Landfill
    Permit Modification Application - Cell 4 Construction and Operation
    Notice of Deficiency dated September 2, 2026

Dear City Manager:

On September 2, 2026, the Department issued a Notice of Deficiency identifying technical deficiencies in the above application, including (1) the subgrade settlement analysis for the Cell 4 liner, (2) the leachate collection pipe calculations for the north sump, and (3) the updated closure cost estimate and financial assurance mechanism. The Notice required the applicant to submit a complete response within 28 days, by September 30, 2026, and stated that failure to respond within that time would result in the application being considered withdrawn.

As of the date of this letter, the Department has not received a response. Accordingly, the application is considered withdrawn, and the Department has closed its review.

The applicant may submit a new application. A new application will be reviewed in the order received and is subject to the applicable fees and public notice requirements. Information submitted with the withdrawn application may be incorporated by reference where it remains current.

Nothing in this letter authorizes construction of Cell 4 or the placement of waste outside the currently permitted disposal area. Please contact the Department before the permitted capacity of Cell 3 is reached to discuss the facility's plans for managing waste after that date.

If you have questions, please contact the permit writer assigned to this facility at the Land Protection Division.
