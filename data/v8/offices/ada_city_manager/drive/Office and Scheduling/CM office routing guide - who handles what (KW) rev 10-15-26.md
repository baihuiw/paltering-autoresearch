# City Manager's Office: Who Handles What

*Kristi Wofford's working guide for routing calls, walk-ins and website contact-form messages that come to the City Manager's office. Updated October 15, 2026 (rev. 4: added downtown parking, streetlight ownership, Animal Welfare follow-up, equipment operator closing date).*

## How we answer

- Every message to citymanager@adaok.com or the contact form gets a reply, usually within two business days. Simple information questions (hours, phone numbers, meeting times) can be answered the same day.
- If the answer needs a department, forward it, copy the department head and log it. We answer the resident in Neil's name unless the department head has already called them.
- **Media questions, legal matters, threats, anything about a lawsuit or claim, and personnel complaints go to Renee Calloway.** Do not answer those ourselves.
