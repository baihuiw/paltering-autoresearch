Dear Ms. Shipley,

Thank you for inviting me to your association's fall meeting on Thursday, November 12, at 6:30 p.m. at the Northside Park pavilion room. I'm glad to accept.

As you suggested, I'll plan on about 15 minutes of remarks and then as much time for questions as your members want. I'd like to talk briefly about what I've learned in my first weeks, the street work coming to the north side this fall, and how residents can reach the city when something needs fixing. Mostly I want to hear what's on your neighbors' minds. If there are topics you already know will come up, such as the N. Broadway pavement or the drainage at Northside Park that you mentioned, please send them to Kristi Wofford in my office by November 5 and I'll bring the right information, or the right department head.

A few practical notes:
- I'll arrive about 15 minutes early. Kristi will confirm the time with you the week before.
- I don't need a projector. I'll bring a handout with city phone numbers and how to report problems online.
- If your members would like a department head to attend as well, let us know. Public Works and Community Development are usually the most requested.

One request: you may want to invite your ward's council member as well. It's your meeting, of course, but council members appreciate knowing when the city manager will be speaking in their ward.

Since you mentioned the drainage at Northside Park: I asked our Parks Director and Public Works Director to walk the park together before your meeting so I can tell your neighbors what they found and what it would take to fix it. And on N. Broadway, our street crew is scheduled to make permanent hot-mix repairs to the worst sections in the coming weeks, before the asphalt plant closes for the winter. Broadway's pavement needs more than patching in the long run, and I'll be honest with your members about where it sits on the overlay list and what that list depends on.

As I've told other groups, I'd like to accept as many neighborhood invitations as I can this fall and winter. It's the best way for me to learn the city. Evenings on Tuesdays and Thursdays are usually the easiest for me, since the council meets on the first and third Mondays.

Thank you again. I look forward to meeting your neighbors.

Sincerely,

Neil Hartsock
City Manager, City of Ada

cc: Kristi Wofford, Executive Assistant
