Dear Dale,

Thank you for your note and for the photo of the cardboard trailer at the Arlington Street lot. I agree it is frustrating to drive over with a car full of boxes and find the trailer full.

You asked whether the city could add a sixth trailer site on the east side, closer to your neighborhood. Our five trailers are emptied twice a week, on Tuesdays and Fridays, and the Arlington cardboard trailer fills fastest on weekends. Before we add a site, I have asked our Public Works staff to look at the whole recycling program, including our processing contract and how often each site is serviced, so that any change we make is one we can keep up. I'll keep your request on that list.

In the meantime, a couple of tips from our sanitation crew: Monday mornings are the most likely time to find the Arlington trailer full, and the Service Center trailer, inside the Public Works front gate, has the most room. Please break boxes down flat so they fit, and keep plastic bags out of the trailers.

I'm new to the job, and one of the things I have enjoyed most in my first week is hearing from people like you who care about how the city runs. Thank you for recycling and for taking the time to write.

Sincerely,

Neil Hartsock
City Manager, City of Ada
