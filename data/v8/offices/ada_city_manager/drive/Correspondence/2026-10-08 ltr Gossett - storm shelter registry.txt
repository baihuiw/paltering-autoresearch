Dear Loretta,

Thank you for your note, and congratulations on the new storm shelter in your garage. You asked how to let the city know it's there and whether the fire department will check on you after a storm.

Yes. The city keeps a storm shelter registry for exactly this purpose. After a tornado or major storm, firefighters and our emergency management volunteers use the registry to check registered shelters in damaged areas, in case anyone is trapped. You can register at adaok.com (Emergency Management page) or by calling Emergency Management at City Hall. You'll need your address, where the shelter is (garage floor, yard, inside the house), and a phone number for someone who is usually away from home. There is no charge, and the list is used only by first responders.

You also asked whether you will be able to hear the outdoor warning sirens from inside the shelter. Honestly, probably not. Our sirens are designed to warn people who are outdoors, and they are tested at noon on the first Wednesday of each month. Inside your home, the best way to get a warning is Ada Alerts, the city's free text and phone alert service, together with a NOAA weather radio. Our Emergency Management Coordinator, Kyle Fentress, would be glad to help you set up either one.

Thank you for planning ahead. It makes a real difference.
