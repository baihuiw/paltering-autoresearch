## From City Hall

As many of you have read, our city manager resigned effective September 1. The City Council has asked me to serve as acting city manager while it fills the position, and I'm glad to do it. City services continue as usual: your trash will be picked up, your water will run, and the people you're used to calling at City Hall are still here. The council plans to move quickly and has posted the position through September 11. - *Renee Calloway, Acting City Manager*

## Labor Day schedule

City offices are closed Monday, Sept 7. Monday trash routes will be picked up Tuesday, Sept 8, and Tuesday routes on Wednesday; the rest of the week runs one day late, with Friday routes picked up Saturday. The **City Council meets Tuesday, Sept 8 at 5:45 p.m.** instead of Monday.

## Last splash

The municipal pool's last day is Labor Day, Monday, Sept 7, 1-6 p.m. Thank you for a record-setting summer. Season passes for 2027 go on sale in April.

## Pay your utility bill automatically

Going on a trip, or just tired of remembering the 15th? Sign up for **bank draft** and your utility bill is paid from your checking or savings account on the due date, at no charge. Pick up a form at the Utility Office in City Hall or download it at adaok.com (Utility Billing > Forms) and return it with a voided check. Forms received by the 1st start that month. You can also set up recurring card payments on the online customer portal. Questions? Call the Utility Office at (580) 436-6300.

## Municipal court payments online

Did you know you can pay most traffic tickets online before your court date? Go to adaok.com > Municipal Court > Pay a Citation and have your citation number ready. If your ticket says "Mandatory Appearance," you'll need to come to court. The court clerk's window is inside the north entrance of City Hall, open weekdays 8 a.m.-5 p.m.

## We're hiring

The City of Ada is hiring **police officers** (lateral and entry level), a **dispatcher** and a **utility billing clerk**. City jobs come with health insurance, a state pension plan and paid leave. See current openings and apply online at adaok.com/jobs, or pick up an application at Human Resources on the second floor of City Hall.

## Book the Irving Community Center for the holidays

Planning a holiday party, shower or family gathering? Weekend dates at the Irving Community Center for November and December are already being reserved. The main hall seats up to 120 at tables and comes with tables, chairs and a kitchen. Call the Parks office at (580) 436-8101.

## Water line work on W. 9th Street

The W. 9th Street water line replacement is nearly done. Crews will be restoring yards, driveways and the street over the next three weeks. Thank you for your patience. Questions: Public Works, (580) 436-8100.

## Report it

See a pothole, a streetlight out, a stop sign down or tall grass at a vacant house? Use the contact form at adaok.com or call:
- Streets, potholes, streetlights, water leaks: Public Works, (580) 436-8100
- Tall grass, vacant buildings, junk vehicles: Community Development, (580) 436-6300 ext. 236
- Barking dogs, loose animals: Animal Welfare, through police non-emergency, (580) 332-4466
