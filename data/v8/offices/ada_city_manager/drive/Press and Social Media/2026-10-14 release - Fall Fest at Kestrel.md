# Fall Fest at Kestrel returns Saturday, October 24

ADA, Okla. - The City of Ada Parks and Recreation Department invites families to Fall Fest at Kestrel on Saturday, October 24, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. at Kestrel Park. Admission is free.

This year's Fall Fest features more than 40 local vendors along the lodge drive, live music at the amphitheater all afternoon, seven food trucks, and a free pumpkin patch for kids 12 and under. The kids' zone on the lawn by the lodge will have face painting, a hay maze, an inflatable obstacle course and sack races at 1 p.m. The Ada Public Library will hold story time at 10:30 a.m. and 1:30 p.m.

City departments will be on hand too. Ada Fire will bring a fire truck and the smoke house. Ada Police will make free child ID kits. Emergency Management will help families sign up for Ada Alerts, the city's free emergency text and phone alerts, and will give away 40 NOAA weather radios in a drawing.

"Kestrel is the heart of our park system, and Fall Fest is one of the best days of the year to enjoy it," said City Manager Neil Hartsock. "My family and I are looking forward to our first Fall Fest, and I hope to see a lot of Ada families out there."

"Our crews have been getting the park ready for weeks," said Parks and Recreation Director Shawna Rippetoe. "Bring a blanket, grab lunch from a food truck and stay for the music."

Scenic Drive will be closed to through traffic from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Parking is available in the north and south lots and along 18th Street. Accessible parking is at the lodge circle.

Vendor spots are full. Volunteers are still welcome; call the Parks office at (580) 436-8101.

For updates, follow the City of Ada on Facebook.
