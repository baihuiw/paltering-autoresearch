## Street work this month

- **Overlay:** Oak Ave from 8th to 14th (milling the week of Oct 26, paving the week of Nov 2) and N. Rennie from Main to 6th (about Nov 9-20). Driveways close for about a day during milling and a day during paving; you'll get a door hanger 48 hours ahead.
- **Crack seal:** finishing on the south side in early November. Streets stay open.
- **Potholes:** report them to Public Works at (580) 436-8100 or on the contact form at adaok.com. Our hot-mix crew is making permanent repairs until the asphalt plant closes for the winter in late November; after that, repairs are cold patch until spring.

## Downtown parking: 2-hour limit enforced again

The two-hour parking limit on Main Street (Mississippi to Townsend) and the first block of the cross streets is enforced Monday-Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Our new parking enforcement officer has been leaving warnings in October; **citations begin Monday, Nov 2.** Need to park all day? The public lots one block north and south of Main have no time limit. [CONFIRM wording with Lt. Brixey]

## Never miss a utility bill

Traveling this winter? Sign up for bank draft and your bill is paid automatically on the 15th. Forms at the Utility Office or adaok.com (Utility Billing > Forms); bring a voided check. Forms received by Dec 1 start with the December bill. Prefer a card? Set up recurring payments on the online customer portal. Questions: (580) 436-6300.

## Pay your ticket online

Most traffic and ordinance tickets can be paid online before your court date at adaok.com > Municipal Court. Have your citation number ready. Tickets marked "Mandatory Appearance" require a court appearance. The court clerk's window is at the north entrance of City Hall, weekdays 8-5.

## Barking dogs

Is a neighbor's dog barking all night? Call Animal Welfare through the police non-emergency line, (580) 332-4466, or use the contact form at adaok.com. If you call at night, ask dispatch to send it to Animal Welfare for follow-up the next business day. An officer will contact the owner, and if the barking continues, a log of dates and times helps the officer take the next step.

## Household hazardous waste day: Saturday, Nov 7

Old paint, chemicals, batteries, electronics and tires (limit 8) at the Service Center, 8 a.m. to noon. Ada residents only; bring a utility bill.

## Leaves

Bag leaves in paper or plastic bags and set them out on your regular trash day. Please don't blow leaves into the street; they clog drains and cause street flooding.

## Holidays

City Hall is closed **Wednesday, Nov 11 (Veterans Day)** and **Thursday-Friday, Nov 26-27 (Thanksgiving)**. Trash routes: Thanksgiving week, Thursday routes are picked up Friday and Friday routes on Saturday. Veterans Day has no change to trash pickup. [CONFIRM with Jody]
