# Ada City Council names Neil Hartsock city manager

ADA, Okla. - The Ada City Council on Monday voted 4-1 to appoint Neil Hartsock as city manager. Hartsock starts Wednesday, September 23.

Under the City Charter, the council appoints the city manager by a majority of all its members, choosing on the basis of executive and administrative qualifications. The city manager is the city's chief administrative officer, oversees all city departments and prepares the annual budget.

Hartsock has spent his career in city government, most recently as city manager of a small city in Kansas, where he led the city through a water plant expansion and a downtown streetscape project. He holds a master's degree in public administration. He and his wife, Carla, an Ada native, have two children and have moved back to Ada.

"Ada has been home for our family for years, even when we lived somewhere else," Hartsock said. "I'm grateful to the council for this opportunity. My early priorities are simple: help get more homes built so people who work here can live here, keep the basic services people count on running well, and make sure residents get straight answers from City Hall."

Mayor Gail Hensley said the council was impressed with Hartsock's experience and his ties to the community. "Neil knows how a city runs, and he knows Ada. We look forward to working with him."

The council thanked Assistant City Manager Renee Calloway, who has served as acting city manager since the previous city manager resigned on September 1. Calloway returns to her role as assistant city manager.

Residents can reach the city manager's office at (580) 436-6300 or citymanager@adaok.com, or through the contact form at adaok.com.
