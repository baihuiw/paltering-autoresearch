# City of Ada is hiring: equipment operator, police officers, utility billing clerk and seasonal recreation staff

ADA, Okla. - The City of Ada is accepting applications for several full-time and part-time positions, City Manager Neil Hartsock announced Monday.

"The people who plow our streets, answer our 911 calls and help residents at the utility counter are the city to most of our residents," Hartsock said. "If you want steady work that makes a difference in your hometown, we'd like to hear from you."

## Open positions

**Equipment Operator I - Street Division (Public Works)** - full time
- Operates dump trucks, a backhoe, a loader, a motor grader and street maintenance equipment; patches potholes, maintains ditches and alleys, and helps with water line and utility repairs as assigned.
- Requirements: high school diploma or GED; valid Oklahoma Class B commercial driver's license (CDL) or the ability to get one within six months of hire; experience operating heavy equipment preferred. Must pass a pre-employment physical, drug screen and driving record check. Some on-call, overtime and weekend work.
- Pay: $18.40-$20.10 per hour depending on experience, plus overtime.
- **Applications close Friday, October 30, 2026 at 5:00 p.m.**

**Police Officer (entry level and lateral)** - full time (2 positions)
- Entry-level applicants must be at least 21 and able to complete the state police academy; the city pays academy costs. Lateral applicants with current state certification may start at a higher step.
- Open until filled. Testing dates are scheduled as applications are received.

**Utility Billing Clerk** - full time
- Customer service at the Utility Office counter and phones, payment processing, new service setup. Cash handling and computer experience required.
- Pay: $15.25-$16.40 per hour. Open until filled.

**Seasonal Recreation Aide and Facility Attendant** - part time
- Youth basketball league aides (evenings and Saturdays, December-March) and a weekend facility attendant at the Irving Community Center. $12.50 per hour. Must be 16 or older. Open until filled.

## What happens after you apply

Human Resources reviews applications after the closing date (or as they arrive for positions open until filled) and contacts qualified applicants by phone or email to schedule an interview. Equipment operator interviews include a practical skills test at the Service Center yard. Finalists complete a pre-employment physical, drug screen and background check before a job offer is final. Most positions are filled within four to six weeks of the closing date.

Current city employees and seasonal workers are encouraged to apply and go through the same process.

## Benefits

Full-time city employees receive health, dental and life insurance (the city pays the employee's health premium), a state retirement plan, paid vacation and sick leave, 11 paid holidays, and uniforms for field positions.

## How to apply

- **Online:** adaok.com/jobs. Click the position, then "Apply." A resume can be attached but the application must be completed.
- **In person:** pick up a paper application at Human Resources, second floor of City Hall, 231 S. Townsend, Monday-Friday, 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m., and return it to the same office.
- **Questions:** Human Resources, (580) 436-6300, ext. 230.

Applicants for positions that require a CDL should bring or upload a copy of their license and a current driving record (available from the state online). Veterans' preference applies as provided by state law.

The City of Ada is an equal opportunity employer.
