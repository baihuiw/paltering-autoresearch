## A note from your new city manager

Hello, Ada. I started as your city manager on September 23, and I've spent my first week meeting the people who keep this city running: the crews who pick up your trash before sunrise, the operators at the water plant, our firefighters and police officers, and the staff at City Hall who answer your calls.

My priorities are straightforward. First, housing: Ada's employers tell us new hires can't find homes, and I want to help get more built. Second, the basics: water, streets, trash, parks and public safety should work every day. Third, straight answers: if you write to me, you'll get a reply.

My family is glad to be home. I hope to see you at Fall Fest. - *Neil Hartsock, City Manager*

## Fall Fest at Kestrel: Saturday, Oct 24

Vendors, live music, food trucks and a free pumpkin patch for kids, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. at Kestrel Park. Free admission.

## Sign up for Ada Alerts

Fall storms happen in Oklahoma. Ada Alerts sends free text, voice and email warnings when a tornado warning includes the city, plus boil orders and road closures. Our outdoor warning sirens are tested at noon on the first Wednesday of each month (next test: Oct 7). Remember: sirens are designed to warn people who are outdoors. Sign up for Ada Alerts at adaok.com.

## Recycling reminder

Ada's five recycling trailers are open 24/7 at the Service Center, the Kestrel north lot, Fire Station 3, the Arlington Street lot and the Mississippi Ave lot. Please break boxes down flat and keep plastic bags out. Bagged trash in the trailers makes the whole load harder to recycle. Thank you for recycling!

## New homes on the way

Council approved the final plat for the Timber Bend Addition, 28 new single-family homes on the east side. It's the first new subdivision platted in Ada in several years.

## Household hazardous waste day: Saturday, Nov 7

Bring old paint, chemicals, batteries, electronics and tires (limit 8) to the Service Center, 8 a.m. to noon. Ada residents only; bring a utility bill.

## Leaf season

Bag leaves in paper or plastic bags and set them out on your regular trash day, or take them to the brush site for free. Please don't blow leaves into the street; it clogs storm drains.

## Meetings

City Council and the Ada Public Works Authority meet the first and third Mondays at 5:45 p.m. at City Hall: Oct 5 and Oct 19. Agendas are posted at adaok.com the Friday before.
