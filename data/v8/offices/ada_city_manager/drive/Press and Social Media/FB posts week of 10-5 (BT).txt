LANDFILL GATE RATES: The Ada Public Works Authority approved new landfill gate rates for commercial, private hauler and self-haul customers, effective Thursday, Oct 15: $56 per ton and a $20 minimum per load. Gate rates were last changed in 2019. Residential trash service rates on your utility bill are NOT changing. Brush and yard waste drop-off at the brush site remains free. Questions: Public Works, (580) 436-8100.
