# Fall Fest at Kestrel 2026: event plan v3

*Parks and Recreation, updated Oct 9, 2026 (SR). Saturday, October 24, 10 a.m.-4 p.m. Rain date: none (indoor items move to the lodge).*

## Site layout

- **Vendor row:** 41 booths along the lodge drive from the north lot to the lodge circle. Booths 10x10; vendors set up 7-9:30 a.m.; no vehicles on the drive after 9:30.
- **Stage:** amphitheater. Music 11 a.m.-3:30 p.m. (four local acts; schedule below).
- **Kids' zone:** the lawn between the lodge and the playground: face painting, inflatable obstacle course (vendor-supplied, with attendant), hay maze, sack races at 1 p.m.
- **Pumpkin patch:** north lot, 600 pumpkins (donated by a local grocery; pick-your-own, free for kids 12 and under, one each).
- **Food trucks:** 7, in the south lot. Fire marshal and food truck inspection Fri Oct 23, 2 p.m.
- **City booths:** Emergency Management (Ada Alerts sign-ups, weather radio drawing), Police (child ID kits), Fire (truck and smoke house), Library (story time at 10:30 and 1:30), Parks (fall program sign-ups).
