# Irving Community Center: Rental Rates and Policies

*Ada Parks and Recreation Department. Irving Community Center, 530 W. 5th St. Reservations through the Parks office, (580) 436-8101, Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. (Jolie Harmon). Rates adopted with the FY26 budget; effective July 1, 2025. This sheet is the same as the handout at the front desk and the PDF on adaok.com.*

## Spaces

| Space | Seating | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| **Main hall** | 120 at tables; 175 in rows of chairs (posted occupancy limit 175) | 16 six-foot rectangular tables, 10 round tables (60-inch, seat 8), 140 folding chairs, two serving tables, warming kitchen |
| **Meeting Room B** | 20 at tables | 4 tables, 20 chairs, wall-mounted screen with HDMI |
| **Gym** | n/a | Basketball and volleyball; rented by the hour for practices only |

**Warming kitchen:** two refrigerators, a range and oven, two microwaves, an ice machine, a coffee maker and a sink. No dishes, utensils, cookware or linens are provided. The kitchen is included with the main hall at no extra charge.

**Tables and chairs come with the room.** Renters set up and put away their own tables and chairs (a diagram on the storage room door shows how they're stored). If you'd rather have staff set up, ask for the setup service when you book.

## Rates

| | Ada residents | Non-residents |
|---|---|---|
| Main hall, Monday-Thursday | $25 per hour | $40 per hour |
| Main hall, Friday-Sunday and holidays | $35 per hour (3-hour minimum) | $50 per hour (3-hour minimum) |
| Meeting Room B, any day | $15 per hour | $20 per hour |
| Gym (practices) | $20 per hour | $30 per hour |
| Setup service (staff set tables and chairs to your diagram) | $25 flat | $25 flat |
| Refundable damage and cleaning deposit (main hall) | $100 | $150 |

- Rental time must include your setup and cleanup time. If your party is 1:00-4:00, book at least 12:30-4:30.
- Nonprofit organizations based in Ada pay the resident weekday rate any day of the week for meetings that are free and open to the public.
- City-sponsored programs, the county's election-day use and other uses under city agreements are not charged.

## Reserving

1. Call or visit the Parks office to check the date. We can hold a date for 3 business days.
2. Complete the rental agreement (front desk, Parks office or adaok.com) and pay the rental in full plus the deposit to confirm. Cash, check or card.
3. Reservations are taken up to 12 months ahead. Weekend dates in November and December fill early, so call as soon as you know your date.
4. Pick up a door code at the Parks office on the last business day before your rental (codes change every rental).

**Cancellations:** more than 14 days ahead, full refund less a $10 processing charge. 14 days or less, the rental is not refunded unless we can rebook the date. The deposit is always refunded if the rental is canceled.

## Rules

- Hours: rentals between 8:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m. The building must be cleared and locked by 10:30 p.m.
- No alcohol on the property.
- No smoking or vaping anywhere in the building.
- Decorations: use the hanging hooks along the walls and the ceiling grid clips. No nails, tacks, pins or adhesive on walls or doors. No confetti, glitter or open flames (battery candles are fine).
- Children must be supervised at all times, including in the gym and outside.
- Clean up before you leave: wipe tables, sweep, take trash to the dumpster by the north door, put tables and chairs back, clean the kitchen. A checklist is posted in the kitchen.
- Parking: the lot holds 48 cars; overflow parking on W. 5th St.

## Deposits

Parks staff do a walk-through the next business day. Deposits are refunded within 14 days if the hall is clean and undamaged. Charges for extra cleaning ($25 per hour) or damage are taken from the deposit, and anything above the deposit is billed.

## Frequently asked

- **Can we bring our own food and caterer?** Yes. Caterers must clean up the kitchen like any renter.
- **Can we come in early to decorate?** Only during your booked time. If you need an hour to decorate, book it; weekday hours are less expensive if your event is on a weekday.
- **Is there a sound system?** A portable speaker with one wireless microphone can be checked out at no charge. DJs and bands are welcome; music must be turned down by 9:30 p.m.
- **Is the building accessible?** Yes: ramp at the main entrance, accessible restrooms, and accessible parking spaces by the north door.
- **Can children use the gym during a rental?** Only if the gym is reserved with the hall. It is often rented separately for practices.
