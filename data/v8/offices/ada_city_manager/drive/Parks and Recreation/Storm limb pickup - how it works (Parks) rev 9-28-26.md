# Storm limb pickup: how it works

*Parks and Recreation Department, Parks Maintenance. Prepared by Terry Gandy, Parks Maintenance Supervisor; approved by Shawna Rippetoe, Director. Issued Monday, September 28, 2026, after the September 26 storm. For the City Manager's office, the Public Works front desk and the Parks office, so everyone gives residents the same information.*

## What it is

The Parks tree crew will pick up storm-damaged limbs and branches from residential property inside the city limits, at no charge, until the work is done (we expect into early November). The crew cuts, chips and hauls. Chips go to the brush site. We do not remove stumps, whole trees that were already dead before the storm, or anything touching a power line (call the power company first).

## How to request

- Call Public Works at (580) 436-8100, 7:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m., or use the contact form at adaok.com (topic "Streets and traffic" is fine; we sort them).
- Give the address, what came down, how big it is, and whether it is blocking a driveway, a sidewalk, a street or a structure.
- Every request gets a ticket number (ST-0001 and up). Residents can call back with the ticket number or their address for a status.

## What to tell residents about timing

- Tickets are worked in the order received. The only exception is a hazard (a limb on a house, across a street or sidewalk, or hanging over a place people use), which goes to the front of the list.
- The crew is one lead and three workers with one chipper truck (T-2) and one dump truck (D-4). They average about three tickets a day, depending on size.
- Plan on **7 to 10 working days** from the call for an ordinary ticket. When the list is long, say so and give the scheduled day once it is set. The crew leader posts the schedule a week out.
- Limbs larger than 12 inches across, or anything needing a bucket truck, may take longer or be referred to a private tree service. The crew will tell the resident when it visits.

## What residents should do

- Cut limbs to 6-foot lengths if they can and pile them at the curb or by the alley. Please don't wait at the curb all day; the crew will still come if the pile is reachable.
- Do not pay a tree service unless they want faster service. The city does not reimburse private tree removal for storm limbs.
- Keep children and pets away from hanging limbs.
