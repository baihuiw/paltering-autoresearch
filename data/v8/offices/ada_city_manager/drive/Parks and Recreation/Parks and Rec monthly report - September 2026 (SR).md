# Parks and Recreation Department: Monthly Report, September 2026

*Shawna Rippetoe, Parks and Recreation Director. Submitted to the City Manager October 7, 2026. Figures from the Parks office (Jolie Harmon), the aquatics supervisor and the maintenance crews.*

## Highlights

- The municipal pool closed for the season on Labor Day, Sept 7. Season attendance was **21,640**, the best since 2019 (2025: 19,870). Swim lesson enrollment was 486 children across six sessions. Season revenue (admissions, passes, lessons, rentals) was $96,310 against a budget of $88,000.
- Fall youth soccer opened Sept 12 with **412 players** on 38 teams (2025: 380). Games Saturdays at the Glenwood fields through Nov 7.
- Fall adult softball started Sept 21 at Glenwood with 14 teams (2025: 12).
- Families are using the **inclusive swing at Kiwanis Park** (installed in August) a great deal; the Facebook post about it on Oct 8 had the highest engagement of any Parks post this year.

## Irving Community Center

- Main hall rentals in September: **19** (14 resident, 3 non-resident, 2 nonprofit). Revenue $3,465 (Sept 2025: $2,980).
- Room B rentals: 7.
- November and December weekends are booking fast. As of Oct 6, only 4 Saturday main-hall time slots remain open between Nov 1 and Dec 31. Jolie is telling callers to book as soon as they know their date.
- The county election board confirmed use of the main hall as a polling place Nov 3 (agreement approved by council Oct 5). The building is closed to other use that day.
- Kitchen range repaired Sept 24 ($485). The ice machine is making noise; maintenance is watching it.
- Basketball nets replaced Oct 2.

## Maintenance

- Mowing: 11 full mowing cycles in September on parks and city properties, plus medians on the contract route. Mowing drops to every three weeks in October.
- Glenwood ballfields: irrigation repairs (two broken heads, one valve), infield mix added before softball.
- Northside Park: restroom building closed Sept 14-18 for plumbing repair; portable restroom placed meanwhile.
- Tree program: 6 dead trees removed (Glenwood 3, Hope Street Park 2, Northside 1). Fall planting of 20 trees scheduled for November with the Tree Board.
- Vandalism: graffiti on the Hope Street Park pavilion (removed same day); two picnic tables damaged at Northside.
- Work orders: 64 opened, 58 closed in September.

## Cemetery

- 14 burials (Sept 2025: 12). 9 lots sold.
- New cemetery fee schedule (Resolution 2026-22, average 9%) adopted Oct 5; effective Nov 1. Notices to funeral homes went out Oct 7.
- Fall cleanup of decorations scheduled Oct 26-30; notice posted at the gates.

## Special events

- Summer concert series closed Sept 3 with the largest crowd of the season (estimated 900). A resident thanked the department at the Sept 21 council meeting.
- October event planning is covered in the separate event plan.

## Customer comments

- 23 compliments and 9 complaints logged by the Parks office in September. Most compliments were about the pool staff and the summer concerts. Complaints: restroom closure at Northside (3), mowing clippings blown onto a sidewalk (2), a rental deposit that took longer than 14 days to refund (1; refunded Sept 22 with an apology), dogs off leash at Glenwood (2), and a request for more shade at the Hope Street Park pavilion (1).
- Two residents asked about adding pickleball lines to the Northside tennis courts. If the courts are resurfaced in FY28, we can add lines at little extra cost. I'd like to survey court users first.

## Staffing

- Seasonal aquatics staff (38) finished Sept 7.
- Hiring for **seasonal winter staff**: 2 part-time recreation aides for youth basketball (evenings and Saturdays, $12.50/hour) and 1 part-time facility attendant for Irving CC weekend rentals ($12.50/hour). Posted on adaok.com through HR; open until filled.
- Maintenance crew: fully staffed (11).

## FY28 budget preview

Items I expect to request: replace the 2009 mower at Glenwood; resurface the Northside tennis courts; a second part-time facility attendant at Irving so weekend rentals can be supervised; a five-year park equipment replacement schedule. I'll have cost estimates by the December capital deadline.

Shawna
