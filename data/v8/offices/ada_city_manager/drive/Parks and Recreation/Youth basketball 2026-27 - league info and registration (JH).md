# Ada Youth Basketball League 2026-27

*Ada Parks and Recreation. League information sheet for parents, coaches and front-desk staff. Prepared by Jolie Harmon with the recreation supervisor, September 30, 2026. Registration opened October 1.*

## The basics

- **Who:** boys and girls in grades 1-8 (2026-27 school year). Divisions: 1st-2nd (coed), 3rd-4th, 5th-6th and 7th-8th (separate boys' and girls' divisions in grades 3-8 if enrollment allows).
- **Cost:** $35 per player, which includes a reversible jersey. Families with three or more children pay $90 total.
- **Scholarships:** available for any family that asks. Fill out the one-page form at registration; no documents are required. No child is turned away because of cost.
- **Season:** practices begin the week of **December 7**; games **January 9 through February 27**; end-of-season tournament **March 5-6**.
- **Where:** practices at the Irving Community Center gym and the Ada High auxiliary gym (shared-use agreement). Games on Saturdays at both gyms.

## How to register

1. **Online:** adaok.com > Parks and Recreation > Youth Basketball. Pay by card.
2. **In person:** Parks office, Monday-Friday 8:00 a.m.-5:00 p.m. Cash, check or card. Bring the player's birth certificate the first time a child plays in the league.
3. **Deadline: Friday, November 20.** Late registration after Nov 20 is $45 and only if there's room on a team.

Players are placed on teams by grade and school. Parents may make one teammate request (another player or a coach). We'll do our best but can't guarantee it.

## Volunteer coaches

We need about 40 volunteer coaches. Coaches get a free registration for one child.
- Must be 18 or older and pass a background check (free, through the Police Department).
- Mandatory coaches' meeting: **Thursday, November 12, 6:00 p.m.,** Irving Community Center main hall.
- Draft night (grades 3-8): **Thursday, December 10, 6:00 p.m.,** Irving Community Center.
- Each team practices once a week for one hour (weeknights between 5:30 and 8:30 p.m.); coaches pick their slot at draft night.

## Rules that parents ask about most

- **Equal playing time:** in grades 1-6, every player plays at least half of each game. In grades 7-8, every player who attends practice plays at least one full quarter.
- **No scores kept in grades 1-2.** Lower goals (8 feet) and a smaller ball in grades 1-4.
- **Officials:** grades 3-8 games have paid officials (many are high school and ECU students). Parents, please be kind; we have trouble finding enough of them.
- **Weather:** if Ada schools cancel classes for winter weather on a Friday, Saturday games are canceled. We'll post on the City of Ada Facebook page by 7:00 a.m. Saturday for anything else.
- **Sportsmanship:** a spectator who is asked to leave a game may not attend the next game.

## Gym schedule notes

The Irving gym is available for league practices Monday through Thursday, 5:30-8:30 p.m., starting December 7. Saturday games run 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at both gyms. The Ada High auxiliary gym is available only when the school has no home event; the athletic office sends us its calendar in November, and we'll schedule around it. Open gym at Irving moves to Friday evenings and Sunday afternoons during the league season.
