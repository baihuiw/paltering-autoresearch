# City Manager's Weekly Report to Council

**Friday, October 16, 2026** | Neil Hartsock, City Manager
*Informational only. Not for distribution outside council and department heads.*

Mayor and Council,

## Monday's agenda (Oct 19)

- Council: minutes of Oct 5; September claims; a resolution supporting the county's application for a rural fire equipment grant; annual renewal of the animal shelter services agreement; appointment of two Planning Commission members.
- APWA: annual water meter testing contract; Service Center fuel island repair ($11,400).
- Executive session: none.

## Departments

- **Police:** 421 calls for service. Homecoming traffic plan for Ada High went smoothly.
- **Fire:** 66 runs. Fire Prevention Week school visits reached 1,450 students.
- **Public Works:** Crack seal crews finished the north side; moving south next week. New landfill gate rate took effect Thursday. DEQ logged the Cell 4 refile Oct 7; its completeness review is due Nov 6. Service Center striping Saturday.
- **Parks:** Fall Fest is next Saturday. Weather looks good so far. Volunteers still welcome.
- **Community Development:** 11 permits this week. Code enforcement opened 23 cases (mostly tall grass and junk vehicles), closed 19.
- **Emergency Management:** "Be Ready" campaign launched Monday; Ada Alerts at 3,388 subscribers (up 248 in a week). Utility bill insert prints Oct 23.
- **Finance:** September sales tax up 2.4%. Auditors on site Oct 19-23. FY28 budget calendar out to departments.
- **Human Resources:** Open positions: 2 police officers, 1 equipment operator, 1 utility billing clerk, seasonal parks staff.

## Around town

- I spoke at Rotary on Tuesday about housing; good questions about infill lots.
- ECU community breakfast this morning. The university is interested in partnering on a homebuyer program for new employees.

## Coming up

- Oct 19: council and APWA
- Oct 24: Fall Fest at Kestrel, 10-4; Station 1 open house that morning
- Nov 2: council and APWA
- Nov 4: siren test, noon
- Nov 7: household hazardous waste day

Neil
