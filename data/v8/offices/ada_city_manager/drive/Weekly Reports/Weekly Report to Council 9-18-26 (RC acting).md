# City Manager's Weekly Report to Council

**Friday, September 18, 2026** | Renee Calloway, Acting City Manager
*Informational only. Not for distribution outside council and department heads.*

Mayor and Council,

Thank you for the long evenings this week with the interviews. Lane will have the draft employment agreement ready for your packet Friday afternoon so it can be on Monday's agenda with the appointment item, if that's where you land.

## Monday's agenda (Sept 21)

- Consent: minutes of Sept 1 and Sept 8; August claims and payroll; two 2011 patrol units declared surplus; two Library Board reappointments.
- Proclamation: National Preparedness Month.
- Executive session and possible action: city manager appointment and employment agreement.
- Final plat on the east side (Planning Commission recommended approval 5-0; staff report in the packet).
- Award of the FY27 crack seal contract. Three bids opened Tuesday; the low bid is $86,240, about 6% under the engineer's estimate.

## Departments

- **Police:** 382 calls for service. Still three officer vacancies; the chief has two candidates in background checks for the October academy class. Dispatch is down one position and covering with overtime.
- **Fire:** 61 runs, including a kitchen fire on W. 16th with no injuries. Engine 2 is out of service for a pump repair; Engine 4 (reserve) is covering at Station 2. Fire Prevention Week school visits are scheduled for Oct 5-9.
- **Public Works:** W. 9th water line project is in final restoration; sod and driveway approaches next week. Street crews patched 64 potholes this week, most of them on the north side ahead of crack seal. Bulk pickup for the north half of town finished Wednesday.
- **Parks:** Pool closed for the season Sept 7. Fall youth soccer has 412 players, up from 380. The fall adult softball league starts Monday at Glenwood.
- **Community Development:** 9 permits this week, including 1 new home. Code enforcement opened 27 cases (tall grass, junk vehicles, two open structures) and closed 22.
- **Finance:** August sales tax was up 3.1% and use tax up 7.4%. FY26 audit fieldwork is set for Oct 19-23. Year-end closing entries are done.
- **Human Resources:** Postings open for two police officers, a dispatcher and a utility billing clerk. The equipment operator posting will go up in early October, after Public Works finishes the job description update.

## Items for your awareness

- **E. 14th Street:** Council Member Bledsoe asked on Sept 8 about speeding on E. 14th. Public Works has scheduled a traffic count for mid-October so council has numbers before any decision on speed humps. Two residents have called the office in support.
- **Vacant house on W. 22nd:** Dean met with the resident who spoke on Sept 8. The house has been posted as an open, unsecured structure; the owner (out of state) has 10 days to secure it before the city boards it and liens the cost.
- **Medical office on N. Stonecipher:** drainage plan submitted; under review by the city engineer.

I'll walk the new manager through the full set on their first afternoon. Kristi has also pulled the last six months of council minutes and the FY27 budget book.
