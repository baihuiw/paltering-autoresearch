# City Manager's Weekly Report to Council

**Friday, September 11, 2026** | Renee Calloway, Acting City Manager
*Informational only. Not for distribution outside council and department heads.*

Mayor and Council,

My first full week as acting manager. Thank you for your patience and for the calls of support. Department heads have been terrific; the city is running normally.

## City manager position

- The posting closed today at 5:00 p.m. We received **23 applications**: 9 from Oklahoma, 14 from other states. Lane and I screened them against the criteria you set on Sept 1 (city manager or assistant manager experience in a council-manager city, budget responsibility, a degree in public administration or a related field).
- **Seven** meet all criteria. Their applications and our one-page summaries are in a sealed packet for each of you at the Clerk's office; please pick them up Saturday or Sunday. Do not forward or copy them; they are confidential personnel records until finalists are named.
- Executive sessions are set for **Monday, Sept 14 and Wednesday, Sept 16** at 5:30 p.m. to choose and interview finalists. Lane has posted both special meeting notices.
- Kristi has blocked out the first week of October for a new manager's orientation, assuming you make an appointment by Sept 21.

## Departments

- **Police:** 376 calls for service. Two officers resigned effective Sept 4 and Sept 18 (both to larger departments); vacancies are now three. The chief is working with HR to get two recruits into the October 12 academy class.
- **Fire:** 54 runs. Engine 2 out for pump repair since Sept 3; parts on order. Reserve Engine 4 covering Station 2.
- **Public Works:** W. 9th water line: final tie-ins done Sept 9; restoration next. Crack seal bids open Sept 15. Street crews patched 58 potholes. Bulk pickup (north half) starts Monday.
- **Parks:** Pool closed Sept 7 with record attendance (21,640). Youth soccer opens Saturday with 412 players. Fall softball team registration closes Monday.
- **Community Development:** 8 permits this week (1 new home). Code enforcement opened 31 cases (tall grass at the end of the growing season) and closed 26. The Planning Commission meets Tuesday, Sept 15 (moved from Sept 8 because of Tuesday's council meeting).
- **Finance:** August sales tax up 3.1%, use tax up 7.4%. Year-end closing is on schedule; FY26 audit fieldwork Oct 19-23.
- **Human Resources:** Postings open for police officers (2, soon 3), a dispatcher and a utility billing clerk.

## Items for your awareness

- **Council Member Bledsoe's request** about speeding on E. 14th Street: Russell is scheduling a traffic count for October so council has numbers before deciding anything. Staff will bring it back after the count.
- **Resident on W. 22nd** (spoke Tuesday about the vacant house): Dean visited the property Wednesday. It's open and unsecured; the owner lives out of state. Notice was posted and mailed Thursday; the owner has 10 days to secure it before the city boards it.
- **Ada High** asked whether the city could provide traffic control for homecoming on Oct 9. PD said yes.

## Budget and finance

- Marcy closed FY26 books on Sept 9 (unaudited). General Fund revenues came in about 1.5% over budget, driven by use tax and interest; expenditures were about 3% under budget, mostly from police vacancies. The unassigned fund balance at June 30 is above the council's 15% minimum. Final numbers will come with the audit.
- The FY27 budget is on track for the first two months. No amendments are needed at this time.
- The new city manager will receive the FY28 budget calendar draft in their first two weeks.

Renee
