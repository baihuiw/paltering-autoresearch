# City Manager's Weekly Report to Council

**Friday, September 25, 2026** | Neil Hartsock, City Manager
*Informational only. Not for distribution outside council and department heads.*

Mayor and Council,

My first report, after three days on the job. Thank you again for your confidence, and thank you to Renee for running the city for three weeks and handing me an organized set of open items. I'll keep these reports short and send them every Friday by 3:00 p.m. If there's something you want to see in them regularly, tell me.

## First three days

- Wednesday: department head meeting and an open-items briefing with Renee.
- Thursday: water plant, wastewater plant and the Service Center with Russell. The plants are well run; the Service Center needs a long-term plan for the fleet, which I'll bring up in the FY28 budget.
- Friday: all three fire stations with Chief Breedlove, then lunch with Mayor Hensley.
- Next week: the hospital, the Chamber, ECU and individual meetings with each of you. Kristi is scheduling those; please let her know what works.

## Departments

- **Police:** 401 calls for service. Two candidates cleared background checks and will start the academy Oct 12.
- **Fire:** 57 runs. Engine 2 pump repair is done; back in service after testing next week.
- **Public Works:** Crack seal contract signed; the contractor starts on the north side the week of Oct 12. W. 9th water line restoration finished. Streets patched 51 potholes.
- **Parks:** Fall softball started. Irving Community Center bookings for November and December are coming in early; the county will use it as a polling place on Nov 3.
- **Community Development:** 10 permits this week, including 1 new home. The vacant house on W. 22nd was secured by the owner's contractor on Wednesday, so the city did not need to board it.
- **Finance:** Marcy is preparing the FY28 budget calendar. I'll review it with her Oct 6.
- **Human Resources:** The equipment operator job description is updated; the posting goes up Oct 5.

## What I've heard so far

A few themes from my first conversations with staff, which I'll keep testing over the next month:

- **Vacancies are the biggest operational strain.** Police is down three officers and dispatch one; the wastewater plant is down an operator; the utility office is down a clerk. Overtime is covering, but people are tired. I've asked HR for a list of every open position, how long it's been open and what we've heard from applicants who turned us down.
- **Equipment is old in places.** The Service Center has a dump truck that barely passed its annual DOT check and a side loader that is in the shop more than on the route. I'll ask Russell for a ranked fleet list for the FY28 budget so you can see the whole picture at once rather than one emergency at a time.
- **Residents mostly want to know who to call.** Kristi tells me a good share of the contact-form mail is people who don't know which department handles what. She's updating the office's routing list, and I'd like the November newsletter to carry a plain "who to call" section.
- **Staff are proud of the basics.** The water plant operators, the street crews and the firefighters all walked me through what they do with obvious pride. I want to make sure our budget and our public communication give them credit.

## Housing

Every one of you mentioned housing in my interviews. I'm going to spend some of October learning what tools the city has now and what has and hasn't worked, and I'd like to bring that to you in a work session in November. More on that in a couple of weeks.

## How I'll work with council

- I'll answer every council email within one business day, even if the answer is "I'm finding out."
- If a resident contacts you about a city service, forward it to me or to Kristi and we'll track it to the end and let you know how it was resolved.
- Under the charter, council works with staff through the manager. I'd ask that requests for staff work come through me so I can keep priorities straight; I'm always glad to have department heads talk with you directly for information.

Neil
