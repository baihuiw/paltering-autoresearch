# City Manager's Weekly Report to Council

**Friday, October 2, 2026** | Neil Hartsock, City Manager
*Informational only. Not for distribution outside council and department heads.*

Mayor and Council,

Thank you again for the welcome. My first full week is done. Highlights below; call me any time.

## My first week

- Met one-on-one with every department head and toured the water plant, wastewater plant, landfill, Service Center and all three fire stations.
- Met with the hospital administrator, the ECU president's chief of staff, and the Chamber director. Housing came up in every meeting.
- Signed 31 purchase orders and 9 contracts under my authority.

## Monday's agenda (Oct 5)

- Council: cemetery fee update (first since 2018), E. 14th Street speed humps (staff recommends a traffic count first), FY27 overlay award (low bid 4% under estimate).
- APWA: landfill gate rate resolution (memo in your packet) and a sole-source chemical feed pump for the water plant.

## Departments

- **Police:** 412 calls for service this week. Two officers start the academy Oct 12.
- **Fire:** 63 runs, 41 medical. Station 1 open house Oct 24 morning.
- **Public Works:** Crack seal contractor starts Oct 13 on the north side. Two water main breaks (W. 9th, N. Johnston), both repaired same day.
- **Parks:** Fall Fest at Kestrel is Oct 24; 41 vendors. The inclusive swing at Kiwanis is getting a lot of use.
- **Community Development:** 9 building permits issued; the first Timber Bend permits are expected Monday. Average residential plan review: 6 business days.
- **Finance:** August sales tax was up 3.1% over last year; use tax up 7.4%. FY28 budget calendar goes to department heads Oct 15.
- **Emergency Management:** Monthly siren test Wed Oct 7 at noon. Ada Alerts at 3,071 subscribers.
