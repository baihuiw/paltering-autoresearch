# City Manager's Weekly Report to Council

**Friday, October 9, 2026** | Neil Hartsock, City Manager
*Informational only. Not for distribution outside council and department heads.*

Mayor and Council,

Thank you for a productive meeting Monday. A few follow-ups and the week's highlights.

## Follow-ups from Monday

- **E. 14th Street:** Traffic counters go down Oct 12 for one week. Results to council Nov 2.
- **Landfill:** The new gate rate takes effect Thursday, Oct 15. Notice is posted at the scale house and on Facebook. Cell 4 is in state permitting; Public Works is finalizing transfer and hauling arrangements for when Cell 3 fills in mid-November. Marcy will bring a solid waste fund budget amendment on Nov 16.
- **Overlay:** Contract signed. Work on Oak Ave starts after the crack seal crew finishes, about Oct 26.

## Departments

- **Police:** 398 calls for service. Two officers started the academy.
- **Fire:** 58 runs. Engine 2 back in service after pump repair.
- **Public Works:** Crack seal starts Monday. Service Center parking lot striping Oct 17. Sanitation is reviewing recycling processor options; I've asked for written quotes.
- **Parks:** Fall Fest prep on schedule. Lodge rentals up 12% over last year.
- **Community Development:** First five Timber Bend permits issued this week. 12 permits total.
- **Emergency Management:** Wednesday's siren test went off on schedule with CERT volunteers and fire crews observing. Kyle and Brooke are starting a citywide "Be Ready" Ada Alerts push next week with a goal of 5,000 subscribers by Thanksgiving. Fire and PD are setting up a sirens-and-PA procedure for tornado warnings as an extra layer.
- **Finance:** September sales tax report next week. Auditors on site Oct 19-23 for FY26 fieldwork.

## Housing

I'd like to hold a council work session on housing in November (Nov 9 or Nov 16). I'll bring numbers on permits, lot inventory and our current incentive tools, and some ideas for a clear incentive that applies to every builder. Kristi will poll you for dates.
