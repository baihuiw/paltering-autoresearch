# Water and Sewer Connection Fees: Fee Schedule Excerpt

*Ada Public Works Authority / City of Ada. Resolution 2025-11, Section 4 (connections). Effective July 1, 2025. Excerpt kept at the utility office counter and in the permit packet. For other fees (meter deposits, reconnects, after-hours calls) see the full schedule in Finance.*

## 4.1 When fees are due

Tap and capacity fees are due when the building permit is issued, before a meter is set. The utility office collects them. A permit may be paid in up to two installments if the second is paid before the meter is set.

## 4.2 Tap fees (cost of making the connection)

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Water tap, 3/4 in. meter | $1,150 |
| Water tap, 1 in. meter | $1,475 |
| Water tap, 1.5 in. and larger | Actual cost, estimated by Public Works |
| Sewer tap, 4 in. or 6 in. service | $900 |
| Irrigation or fire line only | Water tap only |

## 4.3 Capacity fees (share of plant and main capacity)

New connections pay a one-time capacity fee for their share of the water plant, wells, transmission mains and the wastewater plant.

| Use | Capacity fee |
|---|---|
| Single-family dwelling unit | $3,300 (water $1,800, sewer $1,500) |
| Each unit of a duplex or multifamily building | $3,300 |
| Commercial, institutional, by meter size | 3/4 in. $3,300; 1 in. $5,600; 1.5 in. $11,200; 2 in. $17,900 |
| Meter upsize | Difference between the two sizes |

Additions and remodels that do not add a dwelling unit or a larger meter pay no capacity fee.

## 4.4 Waivers and reductions

(a) To encourage construction of workforce housing, the City Council may waive or reduce capacity fees for a qualifying project under the Housing Incentive Policy.

(b) Capacity fee waivers or reductions are granted only by resolution of the City Council. Staff may not waive or reduce a capacity fee administratively.

(c) Tap fees cover the direct cost of the connection and are not waived.

## 4.5 Use of capacity fee revenue

Capacity fees are deposited to the APWA capital improvement account and used only for capacity-related projects (current commitments: the north water tower rehabilitation and the wastewater plant headworks screen). They are not used for operating costs.
