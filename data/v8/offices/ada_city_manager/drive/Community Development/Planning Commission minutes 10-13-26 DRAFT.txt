MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING OF THE ADA PLANNING COMMISSION
Tuesday, October 13, 2026, 5:30 p.m.
Council Chamber, Ada City Hall, 231 S. Townsend

MEMBERS PRESENT: Arturo Quintero (Chair); Carol Dupree (Vice Chair); four
other members. Two seats are vacant (appointments on the Oct 19 council
agenda). One member absent (excused).
STAFF: Dean Sizemore, Community Development Director; planning technician;
Lane Whitfield, City Attorney (items 4 and 5)

1. CALL TO ORDER. Chair Quintero called the meeting to order at 5:31 p.m. A
quorum was present (6 of 7 seated members).

2. MINUTES. Minutes of the September 15 meeting approved as presented.
Motion Dupree, second by a member. Vote 6-0.

3. LOT SPLIT, 1100 BLOCK W. 19th ST. Request by the owner to divide one
140-foot lot into two 70-foot lots for single-family homes. Mr. Sizemore said
both lots meet the R-1 minimum lot width (60 feet) and area, water and
sewer mains are in the street, and the existing garage on the east half will
be removed. No one spoke. Motion to approve, second; vote 6-0. (Lot splits
are final at the Commission level unless appealed to council within 10
days.)

4. REZONING, 600 BLOCK E. 6th ST, R-1 TO R-2. Request to rezone two vacant
lots (combined 0.4 acre) to R-2 to allow two duplexes. The lots have been
vacant since the houses were demolished under the dilapidated building
process in 2019 and 2021. Mr. Sizemore said the request fits the
comprehensive plan's infill housing goals; the block already has two
duplexes built before the 1978 zoning map; parking can be met on site with
access from the alley.

Public comment:
- A resident of the 800 block of E. 6th spoke in favor, saying the empty
  lots attract dumping and that new homes would help the block. She asked
  about the abandoned house next to her own; Mr. Sizemore said it is an open
  code case and he would call her this week.
- A resident of the 700 block asked about drainage. Mr. Sizemore said the
  site plan must hold rainwater runoff to existing levels.

Commissioner discussion: Vice Chair Dupree asked whether the duplexes would
be rentals. The applicant said yes, two-bedroom units aimed at hospital and
ECU staff. Motion to recommend approval to council, second; vote 5-1. The
item goes to council Nov 2.

5. VARIANCE REQUEST, 1418 W. 18th ST (Board of Adjustment matter heard by
the Commission sitting as the Board). Request to reduce the front setback
from 25 feet to 18 feet for a covered front porch. Mr. Lane Whitfield
explained the hardship standard. The owner said most houses on the block
were built at 20 feet before zoning and the porch would line up with them.
Staff measured six neighboring houses: setbacks 17 to 22 feet. Motion to
grant, second; vote 6-0.

6. ZONING CODE UPDATE - ACCESSORY DWELLING UNITS (discussion only). Mr.
Sizemore presented a first look at allowing one accessory dwelling unit
(garage apartment or backyard cottage) on single-family lots, with limits on
size (800 sq. ft. or 40% of the main house), height, parking and owner
occupancy. He said the City Manager has asked for housing ideas for a
council work session in November and that ADUs are one tool that costs the
city little. Commissioners asked about utility services for a second unit
and about short-term rentals. Staff will bring draft language in December.
No action.

7. STAFF REPORT. Mr. Sizemore reported:
- Building permits: 9 issued the week of Sept 28 and 12 the week of Oct 5.
  Year to date (since July 1), new single-family permits are more than double
  the same period last year.
- Code enforcement: 21 cases opened and 18 closed in the week of Oct 5,
  mostly tall grass and inoperable vehicles.
- Two open structures on the west side were secured in September without
  city boarding.

   Chair Quintero asked whether staff could bring a list of vacant city-owned
   and tax-resale lots that might be suitable for infill homes. Mr. Sizemore
   said he is preparing that list for the council housing work session in
   November and will share it with the Commission at the same time.
   Commissioner Dupree asked that future agendas include a standing item on
   housing so the Commission can track progress through the year. Staff will
   add it beginning in December.

8. PUBLIC COMMENTS (not on agenda). None.

9. ADJOURN at 6:47 p.m.

Next regular meeting: Tuesday, November 10, 2026, 5:30 p.m.

Recorded by: planning technician. To be approved November 10, 2026.
