# Staff Report: Final Plat, Timber Bend Addition

**To:** Mayor and City Council
**From:** Dean Sizemore, Community Development Director
**Through:** Renee Calloway, Acting City Manager
**Meeting:** September 21, 2026, Agenda Item 8
**Planning Commission:** Recommended approval 5-0, September 10, 2026

## Request

Tennant Homes LLC (Burl Tennant, managing member) requests approval of the final plat of the Timber Bend Addition, a 28-lot single-family subdivision on 9.6 acres on the east side of Ada, north of Kings Road. The property is zoned R-1 (single-family residential). The preliminary plat was approved by the Planning Commission on July 9, 2026.

## Description

- 28 single-family lots, 60 to 75 feet wide, average 10,900 sq. ft.
- One new public street (Timber Bend Drive) with a cul-de-sac, 31 ft. back-to-back curb and gutter
- 8 in. water main looped to the existing main on Kings Road; 8 in. gravity sewer to the existing manhole at the northeast corner
- Detention pond in Reserve A, maintained by a homeowners association
- 5 ft. sidewalk on both sides of Timber Bend Drive

The applicant expects homes in the range of 1,350 to 1,700 sq. ft., priced about $215,000 to $265,000. Construction would start with Lots 1-6 this fall.

## Staff review

Engineering reviewed the construction plans for the street, water, sewer and drainage. All comments from the August 14 review letter have been addressed. Public improvements will be built by the developer and accepted by the city after inspection; the developer posted a performance bond of $612,400 on September 15.

The Fire Department confirmed hydrant spacing (no lot more than 500 ft. from a hydrant) and turnaround dimensions.

Fees: platting fees ($850) are paid. Water and sewer tap and capacity fees are due lot by lot at building permit under Resolution 2025-11.

## Housing context

Ada has had few new single-family subdivisions in recent years, and employers, East Central University and the hospital regularly tell staff that new hires have trouble finding homes to buy. Timber Bend adds 28 homes in a price range that is in short supply.

## Recommendation

Staff recommends approval of the final plat of the Timber Bend Addition, subject to (1) recording within 90 days and (2) acceptance of public improvements before any certificate of occupancy is issued.
