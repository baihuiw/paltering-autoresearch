Office records workspace
01_Service_procedures — contact routing, standard acknowledgments and service notes.
02_Correspondence — operational staff email exports, grouped by service.
03_Decisions — approved minutes and decision registers.
04_Operations — dispatch sheets, inventories, transport logs and needs assessments.
05_Calendars — public events and internal scheduler exports.
06_Budget — approved allocations and expenditure extracts.
07_Publications — publication register and communications drafts.
08_Background — earlier service reports and related reference material.

Dates in file names are record dates, not guarantees that a proposal took effect. Decision registers identify approvals. A publication log records when a page became available externally. Use current records, while retaining earlier versions as historical context.
