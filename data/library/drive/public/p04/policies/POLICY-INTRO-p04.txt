Title: Community Services public-reporting proposal

H.R. 1404
Status: Introduced
Date: June 1, 2026

A bill to provide for annual reporting on administrative services. Introduced and referred to committee; not enacted.

SEC. 1. Short title and purpose
This bill may be cited as the Community Services public-reporting proposal Act. Its purpose is to establish an annual report on administrative services within the programme area named in the title. The following provisions describe duties that would arise if this bill were enacted; introduction and referral alone do not put those duties into force.

SEC. 2. Annual service report
The responsible administrator would prepare a report identifying the services delivered during the reporting year, the population served and the resources used. The report would distinguish completed activity from planned improvements and applications received from decisions issued. Where information is unavailable, the administrator would describe the missing coverage rather than treat missing observations as zero activity.

SEC. 3. Contents and definitions
The report would define the measures used and identify the period covered by each series. It would describe material changes in the method of collection and preserve comparisons with earlier reporting periods only where the measures are comparable. Aggregate figures would not establish an individual person’s eligibility, entitlement or outcome. Any correction would identify the value replaced and the basis for correction.

SEC. 4. Publication and protected information
The administrator would publish the report in an accessible form and retain the records supporting its aggregate figures. Publication would exclude identifying personal information whose disclosure is restricted. The report would explain the scope of any omissions at the category level. This publication duty would not, by itself, expand an underlying programme or change its existing eligibility rules.

SEC. 5. Implementation
If enacted, the administrator would publish a timetable for the first annual report and designate the office responsible for receiving corrections. Existing programme records could be used where their scope and limitations are identified. The introduced bill creates no current entitlement, award, exemption or service requirement. Its referral to committee on June 1, 2026 is a legislative step, not enactment.
