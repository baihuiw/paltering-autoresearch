Title: Resettlement housing fund reallocation · Enacted text

Date: March 18, 2026
Source: Legislative register

Section 9 transfers $18 million from the existing temporary-housing fund to processing software. Existing housing allocations fall by 30%; a separate emergency fund does not automatically replace them. The package adds translation support.
