Title: Fresh-food allocations during movement restrictions · Office update

Date: May 15, 2026
Source: Bellhaven.gov.example · Programme update

The city delivered staple parcels to all 600 households registered for the May 14 distribution, and all 480 fresh-food boxes received by the depot were dispatched. The fresh boxes contained vegetables, fruit and chilled protein items. Staff are continuing to secure supplies. Residents may see varied contents as stock and delivery schedules change. The city also operates a separate permit channel for private purchases.
