Title: Food deliveries during movement restrictions · Programme summary

Date: May 7, 2026
Source: Bellhaven public archive

The delivery programme covers registered households in restricted zones. A parcel contains several days of staple foods; dietary substitutions are requested separately. Dispatch assignment is not proof of receipt. The logistics return records receipt by household and date. The city maintains one published aggregate dashboard and ward-level service returns.
