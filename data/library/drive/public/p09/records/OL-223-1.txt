Title: Prescription deliveries during restricted movement · Enacted text

Date: May 5, 2026
Source: Bellhaven public archive

Protocol EM-35 funds delivery of prescriptions for requests accepted by the city service during May 5–8. The desk records an agreed deadline at acceptance and arranges confirmation of receipt. Requests that require clinical advice are referred to the pharmacy; service staff do not change prescriptions.
