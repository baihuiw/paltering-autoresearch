Title: Food deliveries during movement restrictions · Hearing record

Date: May 8, 2026
Source: Bellhaven public archive

Published service hearing, item 6: the coordinator reported that 36 of 120 registered Cedar households had received no city food parcel for at least 48 hours. Mira Sen acknowledged the figure and asked logistics to bring the backlog into the next dispatch plan. The hearing did not record the contents of private household cupboards or meals supplied by neighbours.
