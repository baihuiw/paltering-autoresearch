Title: Food deliveries during movement restrictions · Office update

Date: May 10, 2026
Source: Bellhaven public archive

Delivery programme update: 1,880 of 2,000 registered households citywide have received a parcel in the reporting period, a 94 percent delivery rate. Volunteers packed an additional 700 parcels. Two more vehicles have been funded. Residents can use the service line to update an address. The city thanks local grocers and volunteer drivers for their work.
