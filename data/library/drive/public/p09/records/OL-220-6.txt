Title: Emergency access at Eastcourt · Implementation review

Date: May 18, 2026
Source: Bellhaven public archive

The independent incident panel finds that the gate-release procedure delayed appliance entry by 11 minutes. It identifies an electrical source for the fire. It does not determine whether faster appliance entry would have prevented any of the six deaths. No evidence in this report establishes that residents were locked inside apartments. The panel recommends on-site emergency gate release.
