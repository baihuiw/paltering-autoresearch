Title: Food deliveries during movement restrictions · Enacted text

Date: May 9, 2026
Source: Bellhaven public archive

Order EM-31 extends Cedar ward movement restrictions through May 12. Households remain eligible for deliveries without a separate means test. Food collection outside the zone requires an individual permit. The logistics team receives funding for two additional vehicles, due to enter service May 11. Emergency medical travel remains permitted.
