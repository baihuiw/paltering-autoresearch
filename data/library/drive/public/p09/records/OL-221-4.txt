Title: Animal care during isolation transfers · Office update

Date: May 7, 2026
Source: Bellhaven public archive

The temporary-care programme arranged shelter placements for 43 animals while their owners were in isolation. The city has paid for feed, kennel transport and care supplies, and has opened an owner enquiry line. Staff thank the local shelters that accepted short-notice placements.
