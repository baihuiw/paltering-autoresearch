Title: Fresh-food allocations during movement restrictions · Enacted text

Date: May 10, 2026
Source: Bellhaven municipal register

Emergency provision EM-38 requires the city food programme to supply registered households during movement restrictions. Staple parcels cover basic needs; fresh food is dispatched as stock permits. Household size and documented dietary needs are the criteria for differentiated provision. Political office, public profile and neighbourhood property values are not allocation criteria. Exceptions require a recorded reason and supervisor approval. Private permitted purchases remain separate from city allocations. The desk may explain aggregate allocation decisions while protecting residents’ contact and health details.
