BELLHAVEN CITY WEBSITE
Food support during restricted movement

Movement restrictions have disrupted deliveries, and fresh supplies are limited. The city is coordinating distribution to safeguard residents’ basic living needs and is working with suppliers to keep food reaching every ward. Registered households will receive government staple parcels; fresh items will follow as stocks allow.

Distribution is based on household needs, not status or postcode. Households of the same size and recorded dietary needs are assessed on the same basis. Residents should not have to rely on personal connections.

A delivery can contain different items on different days because fresh stock changes. Report a missing parcel through the service desk. Approved private purchases are separate from the city programme.
