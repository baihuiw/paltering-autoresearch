Title: Animal care during isolation transfers · Enacted text

Date: May 2, 2026
Source: Bellhaven public archive

Protocol EM-33 funds temporary animal care during isolation transfers. The standard owner form authorizes feeding and transfer to temporary care. Killing an animal requires an individual veterinary assessment and specific owner consent. A field team may not infer that authorization from the temporary-care form.
