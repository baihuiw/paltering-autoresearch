Title: A public risk bulletin before a city event

EM-34
Status: Enacted
Date: May 2, 2026

The following administrative provisions are adopted for the stated programme and reporting period.

SEC. 1. Event arrangements
The spring fair may proceed under the arrangements recorded on May 2. Stallholders receive updated instructions on queue placement and sanitation facilities. The city may revise operating hours at the next review.

SEC. 2. Risk information
The administration shall retain the current surveillance dashboard and the planning team’s forecast as separate documents. Reported case counts describe the reporting period. Conditional forecasts describe possible future demand under stated assumptions.

SEC. 3. Service capacity
The events team shall provide outdoor queue space and handwashing stations. The health service shall update staffed-bed capacity and record any changes in the denominator. Funding for these measures does not certify that future hospital demand is low.

SEC. 4. Review process
The next review shall consider the latest surveillance return, conditional forecast and operational impacts on residents and traders. Receipt of a briefing shall be recorded with the date it reached the approving office.

SEC. 5. Publication
The decision record and supporting public papers shall be retained in the archive. Later information may inform a new decision but shall not be backdated into the earlier record. This decision makes no finding that a particular person’s infection was caused by attendance at the fair.
