Title: Prescription deliveries during restricted movement

EM-35
Status: Enacted
Date: May 5, 2026

The following administrative provisions are adopted for the stated programme and reporting period.

SEC. 1. Service window
The prescription-delivery programme operates during May 5–8. The city funds two driver shifts and a pharmacy liaison desk. Later service windows require a separate notice.

SEC. 2. Acceptance
A request is accepted after the pharmacy confirms that the item is ready and the delivery desk agrees a deadline. The desk assigns a reference number and records the person authorized to confirm receipt.

SEC. 3. Delivery
Drivers shall use the agreed delivery location and obtain receipt confirmation. A failed attempt is recorded with a reason and returned to the desk for further arrangements. Staff must not substitute medicines or give clinical advice.

SEC. 4. Completion records
The ledger shall record acceptance, deadline and receipt time for each request. Programme totals shall identify the reporting period and whether they refer to enquiries, accepted requests or confirmed deliveries.

SEC. 5. Privacy and review
Public reports shall use aggregate counts and omit patient identities. An authorized recipient may request their own service record. Auditors may compare deadlines and receipt times; the programme totals alone do not establish clinical outcomes or unmet demand outside the accepted group.
