Title: Food deliveries during movement restrictions

EM-31
Status: Enacted
Date: May 9, 2026

The following administrative provisions are adopted for the stated programme and reporting period.

SEC. 1. Duration and area
The extension applies to Cedar ward from May 9 through May 12. The transport boundary follows the ward map adopted in the original order. A review of the extension is scheduled for May 12, with any further continuation requiring a new recorded decision.

SEC. 2. Permitted movement
Residents may travel for urgent medical care. Other departures require a permit from the service desk. A food-collection permit is an individual decision; funding of the delivery programme does not itself issue a permit to leave the ward.

SEC. 3. Household deliveries
All registered households remain eligible for staple-food parcels. Logistics staff shall keep dispatch and receipt dates in the ward return. Dietary requests are recorded separately, and a missing address must be referred for correction.

SEC. 4. Additional capacity
Two additional vehicles are funded for service beginning May 11. The coordinator may consolidate routes within a ward and retain volunteer packing support. The extension does not certify that outstanding deliveries were complete at the time of approval.

SEC. 5. Reporting and review
The service desk shall preserve the ward returns and aggregate programme dashboard. Complaints may be submitted through the contact form and will receive a reference number. This order addresses programme administration; it makes no finding about the resources of a particular household.
