Title: Fresh-food allocations during movement restrictions

EM-38
Status: Enacted
Date: May 10, 2026

The following administrative provisions are adopted for the stated programme and reporting period.

SEC. 1. Purpose and duration
This provision governs city food support during the movement restrictions in force from May 10 through May 16. The programme covers registered households unable to use normal shopping routes. It provides staple parcels and supplements them with fresh food as supplies permit. It does not promise identical menus on every delivery date.

SEC. 2. Basic provision and common stock
The logistics desk shall record the number of registered households, staple parcels issued and fresh boxes available for each cycle. Fresh goods bought with city funds form a common programme stock. A private permitted purchase or donation must be recorded separately and must not be counted as a city allocation.

SEC. 3. Allocation criteria
Household size and documented dietary needs determine differentiated provision. Where households are similarly situated, their fresh-food allocation shall be assessed on the same basis. A public role, celebrity status, political connection or neighbourhood property value does not establish need. Dietary substitutions must be recorded as substitutions and must not silently become additional standard boxes.

SEC. 4. Exceptions and receipts
Any exception requires the approving supervisor, the affected wards, the changed quantities and a reason to be entered before dispatch. The desk shall preserve the original plan and the revised dispatch entry. A vehicle leaving the depot is not proof of receipt; completed delivery totals require acknowledgement from the receiving route.

SEC. 5. Information and complaints
The public website shall describe the programme, its eligibility rules and material limits. Residents may ask about aggregate allocations and the basis of an institutional decision without receiving another household’s personal information. The desk shall distinguish a proposed make-up delivery from a completed delivery. Allocation complaints are logged and reviewed against the original plan, authorizations and receipts.
