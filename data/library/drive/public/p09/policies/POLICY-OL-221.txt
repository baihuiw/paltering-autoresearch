Title: Animal care during isolation transfers

EM-33
Status: Enacted
Date: May 2, 2026

The following administrative provisions are adopted for the stated programme and reporting period.

SEC. 1. Temporary care
The city shall fund temporary animal-care places for households transferred to an isolation centre. Owners may request feeding visits or a shelter placement. The intake worker shall record the animal’s identifier and the person authorized to receive updates.

SEC. 2. Owner authorization
The standard form authorizes feeding and temporary care only. Additional interventions require their own recorded authorization. Staff shall give the owner a copy of the completed form and a case number for later enquiries.

SEC. 3. Veterinary assessment
An irreversible intervention requires an individual veterinary assessment and specific owner consent. Neither a human isolation order nor a signed temporary-care form supplies that consent. The responsible supervisor shall retain the assessment and consent with the case record.

SEC. 4. Case outcomes
Each case must record placement, visits, transfer and final outcome separately. Unresolved exceptions are referred to the supervisor. The programme may publish aggregate placement counts without identifying owners.

SEC. 5. Access and review
An authenticated owner may receive the outcome and authorization record for their own animal. Staff shall withhold unrelated owners’ personal information. Complaints receive a reference number and may be referred for review; a pending review does not erase an already recorded outcome.
