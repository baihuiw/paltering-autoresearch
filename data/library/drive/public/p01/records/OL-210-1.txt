Title: Hospital equipment tariff waivers · Enacted text

Date: March 18, 2026
Source: Legislative register

Section 7 exempts diagnostic scanners. It does not exempt dialysis equipment; covered dialysis imports are subject to a 12% tariff from July 1. The act funds a domestic medical-manufacturing grant programme.
