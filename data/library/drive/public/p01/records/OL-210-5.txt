Title: Hospital equipment tariff waivers · Programme summary

Date: May 1, 2026
Source: Programme administration

The act funds a domestic medical-manufacturing grant programme. Diagnostic scanners have a medical exemption. Small clinics may apply for a grant; awards are competitive, not automatic. This summary describes these provisions only. Consult the enacted text and implementation record for scope.
