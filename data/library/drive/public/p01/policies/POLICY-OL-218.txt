Title: Postal opening-hours pilot

S. 1320
Status: Enacted
Date: March 18, 2026

Be it enacted that the following provisions govern the programme described in this measure.

SEC. 1. Short title and purpose
This Act may be cited as the Participating Branch Saturday Service Pilot Act. It establishes funded Saturday opening hours at the branches listed in the pilot schedule and provides for evaluation of the service. The pilot is a defined programme for participating branches and does not amend the opening hours of every branch in the postal network.

SEC. 2. Participating branches
The pilot schedule shall contain 12 branches. The administering authority shall publish that list with the branch addresses and the applicable Saturday hours. A branch shall not be represented as participating solely because it is in the same city or service district as a listed branch. Correspondence about eligibility shall refer to the published schedule.

SEC. 3. Saturday opening hours
(a) Saturday opening hours at all 12 participating branches shall be funded from July 1, 2026. The authority shall arrange the staffing required to provide the scheduled service.

(b) The Saturday service shall be additional to the participating branches’ existing weekday service. This Act does not reduce weekday hours.

(c) The funding and service provisions in this section are enacted requirements of the pilot, rather than a proposal awaiting adoption.

SEC. 4. Staffing agreements
The authority shall retain the staffing agreement for each participating branch and identify the hours covered by it. All 12 participating branches shall have signed arrangements before the service begins. Where an operational adjustment is required, the authority shall publish the revised hours and identify the branch affected. An adjustment at one branch shall not silently change the schedule for another.

SEC. 5. Public information
Each participating branch shall display its Saturday schedule and the date on which service begins. Programme information shall identify the participating branches and distinguish them from branches outside the pilot. The authority may explain how members of the public can check their branch’s status. Information about a future expansion shall be labelled as a proposal unless separately adopted.

SEC. 6. Evaluation
A service evaluation shall be published after six months of operation. It shall report use of Saturday service, staffing arrangements and any material interruptions. Reports shall preserve the distinction between participating and nonparticipating branches. Evaluation of an enacted pilot shall not be described as a condition that must be satisfied before the Saturday hours authorised by section 3 may begin.

SEC. 7. Implementation records
The authority shall retain the branch schedule, staffing agreements and readiness review. Those records shall distinguish planned service, readiness to begin and service actually delivered after July 1. Readiness documentation does not establish the attendance of a particular customer or the opening hours of a branch outside the pilot. Any subsequent expansion shall identify the added branches and its effective date.
