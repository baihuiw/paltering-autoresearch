Approved by the Chief of Staff. Signature block updated September 2026 for the new mayor; no other changes. --- ## 1. Scope This procedure covers how the Mayor's Office receives, logs, routes and closes contacts from residents and others. It applies to the Constituent Services Coordinator, the front desk, the Scheduler, the Communications Assistant, the Administrative Assistant and anyone covering for them. It is a workflow document. It does not cover the content of replies. ## 2. Logging Every contact that asks a question, requests service or expresses an opinion is logged on the day it is received (or the next business day if it arrives after hours). **Log number:** MO-YY-NNNN, assigned in order (for example, MO-26-0412). Numbers are not reused. A contact that turns out to be a duplicate keeps its number and is marked "Duplicate of MO-26-____." **Log fields:** - Log number - Date and time received - Channel - Name, address and ward (look up the ward on the GIS ward map if not given) - Contact information - Topic (see the topic list in Section 4) - One-line summary - Category: Opinion / Information request / Casework / Invitation / Press / Other - Routed to (department and person) - Response due date - Date response sent and who signed it - Date closed - Staff initials Spam, sales solicitations and newsletters from other organizations are deleted and not logged. Phone messages are logged from the contact slip. The front desk puts slips in the Coordinator's tray by 11:30 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. ## 4. FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE October 16, 2026 Contact: Megan Ostrander, Chief of Staff mostrander@cityofdefiance.com | (419) 784-2101, ext. 201 Collection questions: Street Department, (419) 784-2252 CURBSIDE LEAF COLLECTION BEGINS MONDAY, OCTOBER 26 DEFIANCE, Ohio - The City of Defiance Street Department will begin curbside leaf collection on Monday, October 26. Crews will make three passes through the city, ending Friday, December 4, weather permitting. Each pass works ward by ward in the same order: First Ward, then Second, Third and Fourth. Crews spend about two to three working days in each ward per pass. Pass 1: Monday, October 26 - Friday, November 6 Pass 2: Monday, November 9 - Friday, November 20 Pass 3: Monday, November 23 - Friday, December 4 (no collection Thanksgiving Day or the day after) There is no collection on Wednesday, November 11, Veterans Day, when city offices are closed. Crews go street by street within each ward, so the city cannot give a specific day for each street. A ward map is posted on the city website and at the City Building. Residents who aren't sure of their ward can call the Mayor's Office at (419) 784-2101, ext. 201. HOW TO PREPARE YOUR LEAVES - Rake leaves to the tree lawn (the strip between the sidewalk and the curb), within about 3 feet of the curb. - Do not rake leaves into the street. Leaves in the street wash into storm drains and can cause flooding and street ponding. - Leaves only. Sticks, brush, rocks, grass clippings, pumpkins and trash damage the vacuum equipment. Crews will skip piles with other material in them. - No bags. Bagged leaves will not be picked up at the curb. - Do not park on or next to leaf piles. Hot exhaust can start a fire, and parked cars block the vacuum trucks. - Keep piles away from fire hydrants, mailboxes and utility poles. BAGGED LEAVES AND BRUSH Bagged leaves and brush can be taken to the city's compost site on Island Park Avenue on Saturdays from 8 AM to noon through November 21. The site is for City of Defiance residents only. Bring a utility bill or driver's license. Branches must be under 4 inches across. No stumps, lumber or trash. "Leaf season is the Street Department's busiest time of year," said Safety-Service Director Tom Wiechers. "This year we've repaired our second leaf vacuum and brought on seasonal help so we can keep the passes on schedule. If everyone keeps the leaves out of the street and the piles clean, the crews can move a lot faster." The Board of Control approved the repair of the city's No. 2 leaf vacuum ($9,860) and the hiring of up to four seasonal laborers at its October 12 meeting. Weather delays and progress updates will be posted on the city website and the City of Defiance Facebook page. Questions about leaf collection can go to the Street Department at (419) 784-2252, Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 3:30 PM. ###
